- **Implementation:** PROVED.
- **Proof artifact:** Deficient.

- **Defect:** The public `# Safety` contract is sufficient, but the unsafe operation has no adjacent local `SAFETY` proof connecting that caller contract to `get_unchecked`.
- **Counterexample:** None established or required.
- **Minimal repair:**

```rust

// exactly the in-bounds condition required by `[u8]::get_unchecked`.
```
