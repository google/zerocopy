## TCB `m2q8-TCB-1`

- **AX-U8:** the Reference gives `u8` minimum `0` and maximum `2^8-1`; hence `0` and `1` are valid values. [Numeric types](https://doc.rust-lang.org/1.80.0/reference/types/numeric.html#integer-types).

- **AX-MATCH:** the “first arm with a matching pattern is chosen as the branch target.” [Match expressions](https://doc.rust-lang.org/1.80.0/reference/expressions/match-expr.html).
