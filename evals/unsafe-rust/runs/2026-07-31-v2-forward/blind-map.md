## Normalization

The immutable originals remain byte-for-byte in `reports/rNNN.md`. Blind
copies replace the runtime root, opaque package/target labels, and run-number
tokens with neutral placeholders. This prevents incidental path text from
revealing or clustering conditions; no substantive report prose is changed.
