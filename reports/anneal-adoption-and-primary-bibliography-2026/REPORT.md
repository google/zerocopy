# Conditional adoption paths, primary bibliography, and distribution boundaries

## Summary

The evidence favors testing a mixed architecture: keep the existing pure proofs useful, bind the consumer's claim independently, and certify selected extraction/resource steps before claiming that their implementations have left the trusted boundary. Lean-Iris and Rocq-Iris/RefinedRust offer different resource-proof foundations; Verus and Creusot offer different automation interfaces. Neither adopting a tool nor agreeing with its output supplies the missing Rust-source relation.

This is a conditional research judgment and experiment plan. It does not choose Anneal's final architecture, certify a combined software distribution, or declare all 364 questions complete.

### F2. New primary work strengthens workflow evidence without closing extraction trust

Basis: documentation + source; author observations are not local replay.

The [Aeneas/SymCrypt draft](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15648v1), §§3/5/8 and Appendix C, reports 16,680 Rust lines and 237,160 Lean lines, including 124,777 proof lines. Its API example proves a modeled decapsulation call terminates without panic under key well-formedness, characterizing success, length errors and allocation failure. The authors report a seven-hard-proof agent comparison and an estimated few dollars per Rust line with high variance. These are workload-specific author measurements. They explicitly trust rustc/backends, Charon, Aeneas, Lean/native computation, standard-library/intrinsic models, suitable callers and specifications; leakage resistance is excluded. `decompose` proves equality of reshaped Lean definitions, making that simplification depend on a checked equality proof. It does not certify the preceding extractor. The study therefore supports proof-workflow and scaling feasibility, not an Anneal source-safety certificate or transferable cost forecast.

The [Verus thesis](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/avigad/Students/rong_ms_thesis.pdf), Theorem 4.2 and §§4.4/5, proposes: AIR equivalence in all relevant models satisfying generated axioms, with coherent valuations, implies equal denotations of two typed SST expressions. Its expression fragment omits statements and substantial memory/array/bitvector interpretation. The thesis explicitly leaves the main mechanization unfinished. At source revision `e939579e65643456084cba9ca7c1dc3c81369118`, `LeanVerus/trans/soundness.lean:trans_sound` ends in `by sorry`; encoding and result-equivalence helpers also contain active admissions. This is a concrete encoding/interface lead, not a completed verified Verus pass. No scaling or checking-cost measurement was obtained here. Its Lean 4.29.0-rc6 pin is distinct from the current Verus toolchain.

The [VerusBelt paper](https://iris-project.org/pdfs/2026-pldi-verusbelt.pdf), Theorem 6.1, proves safety of a closed, semantically typed no-argument/unit program in its modeled language. Its Leaf lifetime logic connects generic sharing to Rust-style borrows; its proof-oriented types include pointer permissions, cells, invariants, resource algebras and storage protocols. Prophecy-independent ghost functions restrict how future values can be used. Section 6 leaves actual verification-condition generation, translation of specifications and ghost erasure outside the formalization. It also simplifies layout/provenance and omits two-phase borrowing and standard container axioms. This is a materially stronger resource foundation than the 2023 core; it does not prove the current verifier or Rust execution safe merely because they accept corresponding syntax.

At public source revision `b9b14b2cb47b6a6307611578782adc3481627aac`, [typing/soundness.v](https://github.com/verus-lang/verus-belt/blob/b9b14b2cb47b6a6307611578782adc3481627aac/src/lambda_verus/typing/soundness.v) contains `type_soundness_closed`: semantic typing under every fresh `typeG typeΣ` instance implies nonracing threads and value-or-reducible after each finite `erased_step` execution from the empty state. The proof initializes lifetime, prophecy, unique-borrow and invariant-context resources and invokes `lrust_adequacy`; this is a model safety endpoint, not termination. The relation's name does not supply a theorem about real ghost-code erasure. The selected theorem ends in `Qed`; its compiled transitive assumptions were not checked here.

The [PPtr rule](https://github.com/verus-lang/verus-belt/blob/b9b14b2cb47b6a6307611578782adc3481627aac/src/lambda_verus/typing/pptr.v) `typed_pptr_borrow_mut` requires a live lifetime, pointer and unique `PointsTo` borrow, with current/future location equalities; an address alone does not grant authority. The active `typed_ra_join_shared_to_target` and `typed_sp_join_shared_to_target` rules require equal resource identities and an explicit target below every valid common upper bound of the two fragments. Earlier, stronger join declarations are commented out, so they are not treated as proved interfaces. `typed_local_inv_open`/`close` track `InvCtx`, a closer and mask changes around restoring the invariant predicate. These exact source endpoints offer reusable interpretation obligations, with changed primitive APIs rather than automatic parity with native Verus.

The source README records its correspondence differences, including initialized `PPtr` permissions, sequentially consistent modeled atomic operations, and an omitted actual-Verus atomic-block restriction. It says the historical invariant-context issue was fixed upstream in PRs 2476/2501; that paper issue is not evidence of a present defect in current Verus. It declares dependent function extensionality, reflexive-equality substitution and excluded middle as Rocq axioms. Its example's documented assumption output is not a fresh executed audit of the entire development. Its README links artifact 19080059, while the inspected primary record 19613067 describes a 2.35 GB archive; archive contents were not downloaded and are not asserted identical to the GitHub tree. `verusbelt-evidence-identities.json` separates these identities and inspection roles.

All three publications were available before this observation date. Their exact PDF hashes bind the examined versions; neither title nor publication date establishes artifact compatibility or a fresh assumption audit.

### F6. A mixed design can retain a small, explicit initial frontier

Basis: derived from the comparison and corpus certificate endpoints.

For P139, a conservative source import can initially remain trusted while later normalization is certified. Its language must retain all behavior relevant to the advertised claim, and its exclusions must reject explicitly. “Minimal” means a declared semantic fragment and small replaceable boundary, not permission to erase hard operations. An importer that already removes a guard or destructor cannot be repaired by checking only its output.

For P140, useful seams are imported ULLBC → structured LLBC, selected borrow/update translation, pure rewrites, and emitted-definition denotation. Existing actual validity-module and pair-update specimens provide bounded seeds. Each milestone states its own semantic relation and remaining import/capture premise. Verifying all future passes is not needed before using a first checked seam, but a partial chain cannot be advertised as full source correspondence.

For P141, a standalone certifier separates producer releases, search implementations and process authority from acceptance. It can consume versioned source/model data and exact semantic definitions. Portability costs reconstructing source identity, types, dictionaries, effects and layouts without trusting the original producer's descriptions. A shared canonical program/claim format helps; it does not prove its parser or Rust meaning.

For P142, UB-prefix certificates can be narrower than full behavioral equivalence. They must still cover all admitted entries and bad prefixes. They do not automatically preserve termination, external traces, cost or relational security properties. Preserve an extensible outcome/observation interface if those are future goals; do not declare a narrow safety theorem to be a universal refinement relation.

For P143, assign repetitive syntactic transforms to proved algorithms or checked relations, semantic rules to proved interpretation libraries, witness generation to untrusted search, and executable loading/verdict publication to an explicitly accounted wrapper. A solver certificate removes only the solver proposition it actually checks. Moving an unchanged primitive axiom upstream changes ownership, not the accepted theorem's assumptions.

### F7. Compiled definitions and independent expectations control correlated mistakes

Basis: source/observations retained in corpus + derived acceptance interface.

T092 needs more than two matching manifests. The consumer fixes the semantic definitions and expected proposition independently; binds source/configuration, imported definitions, helper implementations and proof closure; and checks the actual loaded terms against those selections. If model code and its generated expected assertion are both changed to `True`, their agreement is evidence of correlation, not correspondence. A changed `implemented_by` body, native-computation path, or stale imported object needs its own executable/artifact policy.

The corpus's theorem/definition substitution, weakened guard/narrowed decoder, hidden assumption, native evaluation, stale import and failure-protocol controls provide concrete rejection workloads. Their success applies only to their specimens and policies. The known Lean 4.31 nested-inductive kernel defect makes old successful checks conditional; a clean axiom list does not establish robust acceptance of arbitrary producer artifacts. Checker selection and migration require independently justified versions, source/dependency closure and replay of relevant definitions. The existing 4.34 source fix is not a universal soundness theorem, and the pure storage library has an observed elaboration difference across these pins.

T119 should therefore record a vector, not one “TCB size”: acceptance-relevant implementation, Rust/primitive/environment assumptions, bypass privilege, parser/import/runtime closure, update coupling, and generation/check/reuse cost. For each claimed reduction name the old unchecked proposition, replacement theorem and actual checking endpoint. Count it only when the accepted result ceases to depend on the old proposition. This wave performed no new runtime measurement. The ninth-publication delta supplies bounded executed costs: the composed two-target packet model took about 1.20 seconds to build and its small target receipts about 0.33–0.37 seconds to check; their source/import binding remains manual. An actual array-frame Alethe experiment reports median generation/check/elaboration/recheck times of about 5.65/2.95/3.00/2.87 ms, three samples, on one host. Its equivalent `distinct` encoding yields `holey` with exit zero, demonstrating why status interpretation is an acceptance obligation. These are local workload measurements under explicit native/runtime assumptions, not a matched comparison of resource foundations or a production TCB reduction. See `zerocopy-packet-api-target-certificates-and-reuse-costs`, Findings F2–F4, and `smt-frame-diagnostics-elaboration-and-checking-costs`, Findings F1/F4, at the delta revision.
