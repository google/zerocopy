### 2. Wait-Level DAG & Linear Obligation Preservation (`Anneal.DeadlockLogic`)

`Basis: execution (Lean kernel)`

`TypedStmt prec held ambient s held'` enforces five invariants simultaneously:
1. **Strict wait-order (`AllBelow prec l held` + `ambient`):** Blocking on synchronization object `o` at level `l` requires `prec l h` for every level `h` carried in `held` or `ambient`.
2. **Linear obligation conservation (`LinearToken`):** Guards and obligations (`.mutexGuard`, `.rwReadGuard`, `.rwWriteGuard`, `.rwUpgradeGuard`, `.asyncMutexGuard`, `.condvarSignalOb`, `.onceInitOb`, `.joinHandleOb`, `.barrierPartyTicket`, `.chanSendOb`, `.chanRecvOb`, `.chanCapSlot`) cannot be silently dropped (`stmt.skip` preserves `held` unchanged; `topology1a_forget_deadlock_witness` demonstrates deadlock when linearity is violated).
3. **Carrier-tagged ambient frames (`CarrierBinding.{osBound, taskMovable}`):** Entering `onceCall` pushes `(lvl_once, .osBound)` onto `ambient`, while `.await` (`asyncAwaitPoint`, `asyncMutexLockAwait`) and `rayonJoin` enforce `HasNoOsBound` on both `held` and `ambient` (`topology4_once_containing_await_rejected`, `topology4_once_containing_rayon_rejected`, `topology7_sync_mutex_across_await_single_worker_deadlock`).
4. **Symmetric fork-join & mode-disjoint bounded channels:** `rayonJoin` checks `HasNoOsBound` on all four pre/post lists `(held_f, held_g, held_f', held_g')` (`topology6_rayon_asymmetric_returned_guard_deadlock_rejected`), and `ChannelMode` partitions bounded channels into `.creditManaged` (`creditChannel_slot_conservation` over `CreditChannelState`) vs. `.levelOrdered` (`topology5_mixed_mode_send_cannot_steal_credit_slot`).
5. **Lock-guarded `Condvar` notification:** `condvarNotify` requires holding `.mutexGuard` on the coupled mutex (`condvarNotify_preserves_coupledInv`, `topology3_unheld_lock_condvar_notify_rejected`, `topology3_unguarded_condvar_lost_wakeup_rejected`).

`worldStep_preserves_wellTypedWorld` proves that for any state `w` satisfying `TokenConservingWorld prec w` and any transition `WorldStep w w'`, `WellTypedWorld prec (mapExecUnits w'.units)` and `∀ S, ¬ IsClosedBlockedSet (mapExecUnits w'.units) S` hold in `w'`.

### 3. `std::sync::Mutex` Safe Guard-Forget Invariant Bypass, Optimistic `SeqLock`, `RelLAT`, & `RatLevel` (`Anneal.ConcurrentSpecs`)

`Basis: source + execution (Lean kernel)`

1. **Why Custom `std::sync::Mutex<T>` Invariants Require Linear `MustRelease` (`library/std/src/sync/poison/mutex.rs:610-667`):**
   - `Mutex::clear_poison(&self)` (`mutex.rs:610`) and nested-panic drop (`!guard.panicking && thread::panicking()`, `poison.rs:126`) transition a mutex holding broken state back to unpoisoned `Ok`, refuting split `(OkInv, PoisonInv)` contracts (`safe_rust_refutes_distinct_poisonInv`).
   - Furthermore, `Mutex::into_inner(self)` (`mutex.rs:631`) and `Mutex::get_mut(&mut self)` (`mutex.rs:664`) take ownership or `&mut self` without acquiring the underlying OS lock. Once a caller's shared borrow `'a` ends via safe `core::mem::forget(guard)`, safe code can immediately call `get_mut()` or `into_inner()` and observe the mid-critical-section broken `T` (`safe_forget_then_get_mut_breaks_unrestored_inv`). Enforcing unified `Inv` across normal and unwind exits together with linear `MustRelease` guard consumption (or wrapper-private guard confinement) restores soundness (`linear_mustRelease_and_unified_inv_sound_for_realStdMutex`).
2. **Even/Odd `SeqLock` & Physical Prophecy Erasure:**
   - `seqlockTryRead` accepts `(s₁, s₂)` iff `(s₁.version & 1) == 0 && s₁.version == s₂.version`, rejecting odd in-progress writer versions (`seqlock_odd_version_rejected`) and concurrent writer increments (`seqlock_concurrent_write_retry_required`) while linearizing clean reads (`seqlock_validated_read_linearizes_at_clean_snapshot`).
   - `ghost_erasure_invariant` and `whole_program_future_lp_from_erased_physTrace` prove inductively over `n : Nat` that auxiliary ghost state does not alter physical traces (`(runGhost ... n).1 = runPhys ... n`) while witnessing future-dependent linearization points over the whole execution.
3. **Coupled-Ghost `OMO -> RelLAT` & Dense Unbounded `RatLevel`:**
   - `omo_refinement_implies_coupled_ghost_concurrent_relLAT` proves modular logically atomic triple composition for arbitrary caller ghost state `CallerGhost` when `CallerInv` and `CallerPost res` are `RelyStable`; `non_relyStable_callerPost_fails_concurrent_relLAT` demonstrates failure when `CallerPost` is non-stable.
   - `cantor_no_unstratified_ghost_predicate_heap` (`0` axioms) rules out unstratified `G -> (G -> Prop)` ghost heaps.
   - `RatLevel` (`numMinus1, denMinus1 : Nat` encoding `(numMinus1 + 1) / (denMinus1 + 1) > 0` ordered by `ratNum a * ratDen b < ratNum b * ratDen a`) forms a dense, minimum-free, maximum-free strict weak order (`StrictDAG`) supporting arbitrary below/between/above cross-crate level interposition (`ratLevel_no_minimum`, `ratLevel_no_maximum`, `ratMediant_strictly_between`, `downstream_interposes_below_between_and_above_upstream`).

## Boundaries

1. **Standalone `requireMirWp: false` Audit Scope:** `support/verify_deadlock_and_specs.py` audits `Trusted.Concurrency` (`-- anneal-trust-tier: candidate:mir_semantics`) and `Anneal.{ConcurrencySeparation, DeadlockLogic, ConcurrentSpecs}` with `"requireMirWp": False` in `TrustedBoundary.lean`, because these modules model synchronization/scheduling semantics independently of sequential `Trusted.MirAst`/`Trusted.MirSemantics`/`Anneal.MirWp` and are not yet wired into `Trusted.MirSemantics.Crate`.
2. **Abstract `ConcSystem L` vs. Concrete `ConcInstr` Retry Loops:** `ConcSystem L` and `subsystemDeadlockFreeWaitEpisode_implies_globalDeadlockFree` quantify over arbitrary local state types `L`. The concrete `ConcInstr` bytecode interpreter exercises fault-free synchronization/scheduling steps (`WellFormedFutexProg` checks syntactic 2-PC `futexWaitEnter`/`futexRetryAcquire` pairing with unbounded retry-counter updates, rather than MIR memory-safety `WellFormed`).
3. **Single-Step `WorldStep` Coverage & Surface Lowering Obligations in `DeadlockLogic`:** `worldStep_preserves_wellTypedWorld` proves 1-step preservation `TokenConservingWorld prec w -> WorldStep w w' -> WellTypedWorld prec (mapExecUnits w'.units)` across the 20 modeled `WorldStep` constructors (rotation, `seqSkip`, 4 unlock/notify/credit-send wake transitions, 2 `onceCall`/`rayonJoin` entry transitions, and 12 contended blocking transitions); obligation-consuming block steps (`blockCondvarWait`, `blockChan*`, `blockBarrierWait`) strip the consumed token from `u.held` without rewriting `u.stmt` to a post-wait continuation. Surface MIR lowering owns binding each `Condvar` to a single coupled `Mutex` `ObjId` across `wait` and `notify`.
4. **Unwind-Safety Under `MustRelease` & Tear-Free `SeqLock` Payload Reads:** `RealStdMutexNoForgetStep` requires `Inv v` on both normal unlock (`unlockCleanOk`, `unlockKeepPoison`) and unwind unlock (`unlockUnwindFreshPanic`, `unlockUnwindAlreadyPanicking`); callers mutating `*guard` to temporarily violate `Inv` must prove intermediate calls cannot unwind before `Inv` is restored. Lowering `SeqLock` from SC snapshot pairs `(s₁, s₂)` to Rust MIR requires atomic (e.g., `Relaxed` byte/chunk) payload loads rather than non-atomic reads to avoid data-race UB prior to version validation.
5. **`RatLevel` Unreduced Representation:** `RatLevel` represents unreduced positive integer pairs (`⟨0, 0⟩` and `⟨1, 1⟩` both denote $1$ and are `ratPrec`-incomparable), forming a dense unbounded strict weak order (`StrictDAG`: irreflexive and transitive) directly without quotienting (`0` `Quot.sound` required for `ratPrec_irrefl`, `ratLevel_no_minimum`, `ratLevel_no_maximum`).
