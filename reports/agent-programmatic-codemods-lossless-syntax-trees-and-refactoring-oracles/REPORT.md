# Programmatic Codemods, Lossless Syntax Trees, and Two-Sided Refactoring Oracles

## Summary

Primary-source synthesis across 12 peer-reviewed and preprint software-refactoring studies (2013–2026), line-level source audits of 9 pinned upstream repositories (`openrewrite/rewrite`, `Instagram/LibCST`, `benjamn/recast`, `facebook/jscodeshift`, `comby-tools/comby`, `semgrep/semgrep`, `tsantalis/RefactoringMiner`, `microsoft/RefactorBench`, `squaresLab/pldi-artifact-2019`), and deterministic Python 3.10+ verification (`scripts/verify_codemods_cst_refactoring.py`) establishes four structural invariants governing programmatic code transformation, formatting fidelity, and refactoring verification:

1. **Trivia-Owning CSTs & Type-Attributed LSTs (`OpenRewrite`, `LibCST`) vs. Shadow-AST Diff Patchers (`Recast` / `jscodeshift`) vs. Unparsed ASTs (`ast.unparse`):**
   - Naive AST round-tripping (`ast.parse` $\to$ `ast.unparse`) discards **`100%` of comments** (`0/7` retained in Probe 1) and normalizes all untouched parenthesized wrapping, mirroring the **`73.3%` comment line destruction** (`0.60 -> 0.16` comments/program) observed under direct LLM rewriting (`Shirafuji et al., APSEC 2023`, Table V).
   - **Prefix/Line-Trivia CSTs** (`OpenRewrite` `Space` prefix with comment-free flyweight interning up to `50` characters at `Space.java:74–82`; `LibCST` `SimpleStatementLine.leading_lines` + `ParenthesizedWhitespace` at `statement.py:432` & `whitespace.py:243`) guarantee `0`-byte formatting drift on untouched subtrees without shadow-tree diffing. However, attaching standalone comments to the *following* statement introduces a systematic deletion hazard: removing statement $S_k$ (`cst.RemoveFromParent()`) silently deletes preceding section-banner comments owned by `S_k.leading_lines` (`6/7` [`85.7%`] comments retained in Probe 1) unless `S_k.leading_lines` is explicitly spliced onto $S_{k+1}$.
   - **Shadow-AST `.original` Patching** (`benjamn/recast@b6377759` `lib/parser.ts:163–225` & `lib/patcher.ts:290–569`) preserves untouched slices during leaf `Identifier` renames (`3/3` multi-line breaks kept in Probe 1), but suffers abrupt **enclosing-node pretty-print cascades** (`0/3` line breaks kept) whenever: (a) a newly constructed AST node lacks `.original` (`patcher.ts:176–181, 299–301`), (b) **any** AST array changes length (`oldNode.length !== len` at `patcher.ts:345`, e.g., appending `1` call argument or import specifier), (c) an `ImportSpecifier` flips shorthand status or has overlapping child spans (`patcher.ts:481–483, 521–526`), or (d) **any** descendant inside a `ReturnStatement` changes (`patcher.ts:531–536`, blanket ASI fallback).

2. **Scannerless Combinator Matchers (`Comby`) & Flat Byte-Span Autofix Engines (`Semgrep`):**
   - `Comby` (`PLDI 2019`; `comby-tools/comby@a49be8ef`) outperforms regular expressions on nested balanced calls containing unbalanced delimiters inside strings and scopes enclosed holes such as `foo(:[x])` via `delimiters left right` (`matcher_engine.ml:379–382, 580–584, 727–741`) to `[("(", ")")]` exclusively (allowing unbalanced `[` or `}` inside `foo([1, 2)` and `foo("}"[0], 1)` while enforcing all three Dyck pairs `()`, `[]`, `{}` on unenclosed holes). However, `Comby` faces an inescapable **scannerless quote-vs-lifetime dilemma** (`languages.ml:110, 499–502, 533–535`; `omega_string_literals.ml:6–42`): `.generic` includes `'` in `escapable_string_literals` (failing with `None` on single Rust lifetimes `fn demo(x: &'a str)` and silently swallowing closing `)` across paired lifetimes in `outer(inner(x: &'a str), y: &'b str)`), whereas `.rs` and `.ml` drop `'` entirely—causing valid character literals `'('` and `'"'` to corrupt delimiter/string state—and `.rs` specifies `raw_string_literals = [("r#", "#")]` (`languages.ml:500`), which breaks both interior `#` characters (`r#"a#b"#`) and **all Rust 2018 raw identifiers** (`foo(r#type, 1)`).
   - `Semgrep` (`semgrep/semgrep@c4dbe9ee` `libs/commons/Textedit.ml` & `src/fixing/Autofix.ml`) exhibits **four live source-verified autofix defects**: (i) **Bug S1** (`Textedit.ml:57–60, 150–159`), where `overlaps` treats a zero-length point insertion `[5, 5)` and a co-located byte replacement `[5, 8)` as non-overlapping (`5 <= 5`) and `List.sort` sorts descending **only by `start`**, applying `[5, 5) -> "HELLO"` *before* `[5, 8) -> "XYZ"` on `"01234foo89"` to emit corrupted **`Success "01234XYZLOfoo89"`** with `0` reported conflicts (fixed by a point-collision guard plus `(-start, -end_)` tie-breaking $\to$ `"01234HELLOXYZ89"`); (ii) **Bug S2** (`Autofix.ml:149–175`, `Textedit.ml:180–187`), where `validate_fix` parses each edit only in isolation, writes `partial_result` to disk on `Overlap`, and requires **$d$ sequential passes** on $d$-deep nested calls `old^d(x)` vs. `1` bottom-up CST pass; (iii) **Bug S3** (`Textedit.ml:95–104, 177` vs. `Autofix.ml:379–387`), where production `apply_edits` strips `\r?\n` on whole-line empty replacements (`2` newlines) while test harness `apply_fixes_to_file_exn` skips `remove_newline_for_empty_replacement` (`3` newlines); and (iv) **Bug S4** (`Autofix.ml:303–315`), where `replace_first_n` retains legacy `pcre-ocaml` `replace_first` `[count]` semantics (`L303–306`) re-scanning from byte `0` on each iteration (`count=2` on `"x + x"` with `x -> safe(x)` yields `"safe(safe(x)) + x"`).

3. **Two-Sided Refactoring Oracles (`SWE-Refactor` + `RefactoringMiner` + `SafeRefactor` vs. `RefactorBench`):**
   - Neither unit tests alone nor static AST structural checks alone are sound refactoring oracles. Even industrial IDE refactoring engines produced **`120` unique bugs** (`57` compilation errors + `63` silent behavioral changes across `153,444` generated programs in `Soares et al., TSE 2013`).
   - Across `SWE-Refactor`'s `1,099` JaCoCo-covered Java tasks (`arXiv:2602.03712v1`, Table 2), **`17.51%` (`97/554`, `DeepSeek-V3`) and `18.44%` (`99/537`, `gpt-4o-mini`)** of `Compile&Test`-passing outputs fail `RefactoringMiner` AST verification (including multi-agent test-overfitting via empty stub methods), while **`32.20%` (`217/674`) and `31.13%` (`198/636`)** of AST-verified refactorings fail compilation or tests. Conversely, `microsoft/RefactorBench@210b2d15` executes **`0 / 100` (`0.0%`)** target repositories, inspects `ImportFrom.level` in **`0 / 100`** test suites across `73` `ImportFrom`-checking files (`63` reading `.module`), restricts every `Call.func` type check to `ast.Name` in **`24 / 100`** test files (`36 / 100` having at least one `isinstance(x.func, ast.Name)` site; `20 / 100` never mentioning `Attribute` anywhere in the file), and contains live false-positive/false-negative AST-predicate inversions in `adapt_method_mode.py:39` and `add-log-parameter-disconnect-all.py:27,30`.
   - At the same time, `RefactoringMiner` (`tsantalis/RefactoringMiner@485889d8` `documentation/accuracy.md`, `102` Java Benchmark 1 refactoring types, `P = 0.9990`, `R = 0.9837`) exhibits an asymmetric **`29.95%` false-negative rate (`R = 0.7005`, `138` TP / `59` FN) on compound `Extract And Move Method`**—meaning conjunctive `Compile&Test && RefactoringMiner` benchmarks achieve near-zero false-positive reward hacking (`FP = 0`) at the cost of underscoring up to `~30%` of valid non-canonical compound refactorings.

4. **Direct-Patch vs. Codemod Synthesis & Hybrid Proposer-Verifier Crossover:**
   - Raw per-site LLM edits incur independent per-site semantic/precondition failure rates ($\epsilon_{\text{unsafe}} = 7.03\%$ combined across GPT-4 [`7.39%`] and Gemini-1.0 Pro [`6.57%`] in `Liu et al., ASE 2025`; $\epsilon_{\text{io}} = 8.89\%$ in `Shirafuji et al., APSEC 2023`; `57.4%` precondition-invalid raw `Extract Method` suggestions in `Pomian et al., ICSME 2024`; `0%` baseline `SWE-agent` resolution above `6` files in `RefactorBench`; `82.63% -> 39.39%` atomic-to-compound cliff in `GPT-5.1-Codex`, two-sided Fisher $p = 1.0732 \times 10^{-6}$).
   - Under an `[illustrative analytical model]` (`Basis: derived`) anchored to these empirical error rates and `Refazer`'s `83.05%` (`49/59`) synthesis accuracy, $O(1)$ CST codemod synthesis crosses direct patching at **$N_{\text{cost}}^* = 4$ sites** in token cost ($k = 3$ iterations) and **$N_{\text{rel}}^* \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ sites** in end-to-end reliability **for uniform structural transformations** (`PyCraft` `58.0` mean variants/CPAT, `13.61x` aggregate / `14.1x` mean per-CPAT site expansion, `83%` [`72/86`] PR merge rate). However, on **non-uniform edits governed by local data-flow invariants**, purely syntactic codemod synthesis over-generalizes in **`16.95%` (`10/59`)** of scenarios (`Refazer` Scenario 2 modifying `200` sites instead of `4`), requiring **LLM-as-intent-proposer + deterministic IDE/CST precondition & test+AST verification** (`EM-Assist` `53.4%` `Recall@5`, `81.3%` / `94.4%` developer approval; `RefactoringMirror` `22/22` [`100%`] bug elimination with `32/35` [`91.4%`] end-to-end reapplication).

### 2. Architectural Taxonomy of Programmatic Codemod & CST Engines

`Basis: source + execution`

Programmatic refactoring engines divide along two orthogonal axes: **trivia representation** (prefix/line CST ownership vs. shadow-AST diff patching vs. trivia-stripping ASTs) and **semantic attribution** (compiler-resolved type/scope graphs vs. purely syntactic trees).

#### 2.1 Type-Attributed Prefix LSTs (`openrewrite/rewrite@5c471ca885f60943772d66c18de2f7be94c1a18c`)
- **Prefix `Space` & Flyweight Interning (`Space.java:34–82`):** Every `J` syntax node stores its preceding comments and whitespace in a `Space` prefix (`List<Comment> comments` + `@Nullable String whitespace`). To prevent heap blowup across multi-million-LOC repositories, `Space.build` (`L60–68`) delegates comment-free prefixes (`comments.isEmpty()`) to `Space.shared` (`L74–82`), interning strings of length `1..50` inside `ConcurrentHashMap<String, Space> flyweights` (`L48`, returning `Space.EMPTY` for `""` and `null`—forcing a fresh `new Space`—for `whitespace.length() > 50`). Probe 1 confirms `ws50_interned=True`, `ws51_interned=False`, and `comment_space_interned=False`.
- **Typed Template Substitution (`JavaTemplate.java:120–145`):** Unlike text-splice templates, `JavaTemplate.doApply` substitutes AST parameters into context-stubbed Java source, invokes `substitutions.bindTypeVariables` (`L136`), and parses the fragment so newly synthesized `J` nodes carry resolved `JavaType` attribution into downstream visitors in the same pipeline.

#### 3.2 Four Live Source Defects & Traps in `Semgrep` Autofix (`semgrep/semgrep@c4dbe9ee2fc6d0022ba02ef6b97c5954ed9549e3`)
Direct inspection of `libs/commons/Textedit.ml` and `src/fixing/Autofix.ml`—reproduced bit-for-bit in Probe 2—establishes four live autofix hazards:
- **Bug S1 — Co-Located Point-Insertion + Span Replacement Corruption (`Textedit.ml:57–60, 150–159`):**
  - `overlaps e1 e2` (`L57–60`) evaluates `not (e1.end_ <= e2.start || e2.end_ <= e1.start)`. For a zero-length point insertion `e1 = (5, 5, "HELLO")` followed by a span replacement `e2 = (5, 8, "XYZ")` on `"01234foo89"`, `e1.end_ <= e2.start` (`5 <= 5`) is `true`, so `overlaps` returns **`false`** (`conflicts=0`).
  - `apply_edits_to_text` (`L150–152`) then sorts descending **solely by `start`**: `List.sort (fun a b -> Int.compare b.start a.start) applicable_edits`. Because `Int.compare 5 5 = 0` and `List.sort` is stable, `e1` (`[5, 5)`) is applied **before** `e2` (`[5, 8)`): Step 1 produces `"01234HELLOfoo89"`, and Step 2 overwrites stale bytes `[5, 8)` (`"HEL" -> "XYZ"`), returning **`Success "01234XYZLOfoo89"`**—corrupting `"HELLO"` into `"XYZLO"` and failing to delete `"foo"`! Likewise, dual point insertions `[(5, 5, "A"), (5, 5, "B")]` bypass `overlaps` and splice in reversed rule-precedence order `"01234BA5"`.
  - **Verified Fix:** Adding `(e1.start = e1.end_ && e2.start = e2.end_ && e1.start = e2.start)` to `overlaps` (`Overlap` on `[(5, 5, "A"), (5, 5, "B")]`) and sorting `applicable_edits` by `(-start, -end_)` applies `[5, 8) -> "XYZ"` before `[5, 5) -> "HELLO"`, yielding exact **`"01234HELLOXYZ89"`**.
- **Bug S2 — Isolated Per-Edit Parse Validation, Partial-Overlap Disk Writes, & $O(d)$ Nesting Passes (`Autofix.ml:149–175`, `Textedit.ml:180–187`):** `validate_fix` (`Autofix.ml:156`) applies only a single `edit` to `target_contents` (accepting broken parses whenever `List.length fix_skipped_tokens <= List.length orig_skipped_tokens`, `L166`), and `Textedit.apply_edits` (`L180–187`) never re-parses the multi-edit result—writing `partial_result` directly to disk even on `Overlap`! On $d$-deep nested matches `old^d(x)`, `remove_overlapping_edits` discards inner matches on each pass, requiring **`[1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6]` passes** for $d = 1..6$ vs. **`1` pass** for bottom-up CST post-order rewriting.
- **Bug S3 — CLI vs. `--test` Whole-Line Deletion Divergence (`Textedit.ml:95–104, 177` vs. `Autofix.ml:379–387`):** Production `Textedit.apply_edits` (`L177`) maps `remove_newline_for_empty_replacement` before `apply_edits_to_text` (`"a = 1\nb = 2\nc = 3\n"` $\to$ `"a = 1\nc = 3\n"`, `cli_newlines=2`), whereas `Autofix.apply_fixes_to_file_exn` (`Autofix.ml:381`) calls `apply_edits_to_text` directly (`"a = 1\n\nc = 3\n"`, `test_exn_newlines=3`).
- **Bug S4 — Retained Legacy PCRE Non-Idempotent `replace_first_n` Re-Scanning (`Autofix.ml:303–320`):** Upstream `Autofix.ml:303–306` explicitly documents `replace_first_n` (`L311–315`) as retained legacy `pcre-ocaml` `replace_first` `[count]` compatibility semantics: each iteration `1..n` calls `Pcre2_.replace ~limit:1` from byte `0` of the **already-rewritten** string rather than advancing past the prior replacement. ### 5. Direct-Patch vs. Codemod Synthesis Crossover & Hybrid Proposer-Verifier Design

#### 5.1 Empirical Failure Modes of Direct LLM Patching Across $N$ Sites
- **Silent Functional Breakage (`Liu et al., ASE 2025`, §4.2, Table 4):** `13 / 176` (`7.39%`) of GPT-4 and `9 / 137` (`6.57%`) of Gemini-1.0 Pro patches are unsafe (**`22 / 313 = 7.03%` combined**), with **`18 / 22` (`81.82%`)** introducing silent semantic bugs vs. `4 / 22` (`18.18%`) syntax errors. Similarly, `Shirafuji et al. (APSEC 2023)` Table II measures **`8.89%`** single-shot behavioral failure (`91.11%` `Pass@1` despite `99.93%` syntactic compilability) alongside **`73.3%`** mean comment erasure (`0.60 -> 0.16`, Table V).
- **Precondition Hallucination (`Pomian et al., ICSME 2024`, §I, §III-B, & §IV-C):** `17,356 / 22,741` (**`76.32%`**) of raw LLM `Extract Method` proposals are hallucinations (`57.4%` invalid preconditions defined under §III-B + `18.9%` non-useful in the §I formative breakdown).
- **Multi-File Context & Edit-Loop Collapse (`RefactorBench`, §4.2, §5, §6.1):** Baseline `SWE-agent` (`GPT-4`) solves **`0%`** of tasks requiring `> 6` edited files and **`0%`** of 3-task combined pseudotasks (`78.4%` of trajectories encounter edit-step errors, burning `1,466` `o200k_base` tokens per lint loop), whereas a human developer with IDE refactoring tools solves **`87%`** within `5` minutes/task.

#### 5.2 Analytical Crossover Model (`[illustrative analytical model]`, `Basis: derived`)
For $N$ **uniform, type/scope-attributable target sites**, Probe 5 evaluates independent per-site direct patching $P_{\text{direct}}(N) = (1 - \epsilon)^N$ and $C_{\text{direct}}(N) = N \cdot c_{\text{patch}}$ against $O(1)$ CST codemod synthesis $C_{\text{codemod}}(N) = k_{\text{iter}} c_{\text{synth}} + N \cdot c_{\text{cst}}$ ($c_{\text{cst}} = 0$ LLM tokens):
- At $\epsilon = 7.03\%$ (`Liu et al.` combined unsafe rate), direct-patch safety decays across `{1: 0.9297, 3: 0.8036, 5: 0.6946, 10: 0.4825, 25: 0.1617, 50: 0.0261}`.
- Under representative parameterization ($c_{\text{patch}} = 1,800$ tokens/site, $k_{\text{iter}} = 3$, $c_{\text{synth}} = 2,400$ tokens/iteration), **token cost breaks even at $N_{\text{cost}}^* = 4$ sites** ($k \in [2, 5] \implies N_{\text{cost}}^* \in [3, 7]$).
- Benchmarked against `Refazer`'s empirical synthesis accuracy $p_{\text{synth}} = 49 / 59 = 83.05\%$, **end-to-end reliability crosses over at $N_{\text{rel}}^* = 3$ sites** for $\epsilon = 7.03\%$ (`0.8036 < 0.8305`), **$N_{\text{rel}}^* = 2$ sites** for $\epsilon = 8.89\%$, and **$N_{\text{rel}}^* = 1$ site** for precondition-heavy `Extract Method` (`57.4%` raw invalidity).

#### 5.3 Scope Qualification: Where Blind Codemod Synthesis Fails & Hybrid Engines Dominate
Programmatic codemod synthesis **does not** unconditionally dominate direct edits when target sites are semantically heterogeneous:
1. **Data-Flow Over-Generalization (`Refazer`, `ICSE 2017`, Table IV):** In **`10 / 59` (`16.95%`)** C# scenarios, syntactic AST version-space synthesis over-generalized (`Scenario 2`: `2` examples modified `200` locations instead of `4`; `Scenario 4`: failed because skipping reassigned locals required intra-procedural data-flow analysis).
