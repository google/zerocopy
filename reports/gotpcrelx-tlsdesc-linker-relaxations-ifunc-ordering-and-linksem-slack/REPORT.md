# Post-Codegen ELF Linker Instruction Relaxations, `STT_GNU_IFUNC` Startup Ordering, and `(Frame, Slack)` ABI Separation

## Summary

Compiler correctness proofs that terminate at assembly or relocatable object (`.o`) emission do not establish the semantics of the executed binary: static ELF linkers (`lld`, `mold`, `wild`, GNU `ld`) routinely mutate machine opcodes, addressing modes, memory-access traces, and CPU condition flags after code generation, while the dynamic loader (`glibc` `ld.so`) executes arbitrary `STT_GNU_IFUNC` resolver code mid-relocation before Global Offset Tables (GOT/PLT) finish initializing. Furthermore, `GOTPCRELX` and TLS relaxations are **not merely optional optimizations**: both `mold` (`elf/arch/x86_64.rs:389–391`, relaxing `GOTPCRELX` even under `--no-relax`) and `wild` (`libwild/src/elf_x86_64.rs:1–4`) document that static-PIE C runtime startup (`_start`) relies on `MOV -> LEA` relaxation for correctness because the GOT is unrelocated prior to self-relocation, and GNU `ld 2.47` unconditionally applies `TLSDESC GD->LE` and `TLS IE->LE` rewrites even when passed `-Wl,--no-relax`.

Direct inspection of `llvm/llvm-project` (`4f6781e188aaae89290d963375341d3886f90945`), `rui314/mold` (`331ac663668dc0b1f4949403854613b9b1913025`, now implemented in Rust under `elf/arch/`), and `wild-linker/wild` (`e15f1f9077812d657ee3a3237b6206c430f82eef`), corroborated by live `x86-64` hardware execution on GCC 15.2.0 / GNU `ld 2.47`, uncovers three linker-induced semantic divergences invisible at `.o` level: (1) on `x86-64` `R_X86_64_GOTTPOFF` (`IE -> LE`), `lld`, `wild`, and GNU `ld` rewrite `addq x@gottpoff(%rip), %reg` into `leaq tpoff(%reg), %reg` (**preserving `EFLAGS`**) for 14 general-purpose registers, **except** `%rsp` and `%r12` (where x86-64 ModR/M `rm=100b` would mandate an 8-byte SIB encoding) and APX `%r16`–`%r31`, which are rewritten into `addq $tpoff, %reg` (**clobbering `EFLAGS`**)—verified live on x86-64 hardware (`ZF(rax)=1` preserved vs. `ZF(r12)=0` cleared); (2) on `x86-64` `R_X86_64_TLSGD` (`GD -> LE`), `lld` emits `lea tpoff(%rax), %rax` (`EFLAGS`-preserving) whereas `mold` emits `add $tp_offset, %rax` (`EFLAGS`-clobbering); and (3) on `AArch64` `TLSDESC` (`GD -> LE` / `GD -> IE`), `lld` places two trailing NOPs (`movz; movk; nop; nop`), whereas `mold` and `wild` place two leading NOPs (`nop; nop; movz; movk`). Across all examined x86-64 and AArch64 relaxations, instruction byte lengths are strictly preserved (via `0x67` `addr32` prefixes, trailing `0x90`, or fixed 4-byte AArch64 NOPs), leaving `.eh_frame` DWARF CFA offsets unchanged—verified byte-identically on `got_relax_pie` vs. `got_norelax_pie`.

In `bminor/glibc` (`04e750e75b73957cf1c791535a3f4319534a52fc`, `elf/do-rel.h` and `elf/dynamic-link.h`), `R_*_IRELATIVE` entries are deferred only to the end of their own relocation range, while `R_X86_64_64` / `R_X86_64_GLOB_DAT` relocations targeting `STT_GNU_IFUNC` symbols are **not** deferred past `.rela.plt` (`ranges[1]`). Live execution on Debian GLIBC 2.43 reproduces deterministic startup `SIGSEGV` (`exit=139`) under **both** `-Wl,-z,now` and `-Wl,-z,lazy` whenever a shared object's `.rela.dyn` references an `STT_GNU_IFUNC` symbol whose resolver invokes an external helper via `.rela.plt`—even with explicit `DT_NEEDED` ordering—while succeeding under `-fno-plt` (only because `R_X86_64_GLOB_DAT` happened to precede `R_X86_64_64` in `.rela.dyn`) or when `static` linkage turns the relocation into `R_X86_64_IRELATIVE` inside `.rela.plt`.

Finally, `rems-project/linksem` (`446412810661b91a39c86425c1e924e35661098c`) models ELF binary parsing, layout, and relocation arithmetic up to `R_X86_64_IRELATIVE` (37), but omits `R_X86_64_GOTPCRELX` (41), `R_X86_64_REX_GOTPCRELX` (42), all `TLSDESC` relocation execution (explicit `failwith "... not implemented"`), all instruction relaxations, and all calling-convention state. Separating cross-ABI state into preserved semantic `Frame` versus unpreserved `Slack` cleanly accounts for caller-saved GPRs, `x86-64` AVX/AVX-512 upper lanes (`vzeroupper`), and tail-call stack reuse, **provided** the partition is indexed by **call-site ABI class** rather than fixed globally per ISA—because System V AMD64's `128`-byte red zone sits in `Frame` across user-space POSIX signals (verified live: canary intact, `sp_gap = 1,440 B`) yet in `Slack` across `call *x@tlsdesc(%rax)` (`glibc` `_dl_tlsdesc_dynamic` clobbers `-8(%rsp)`..`-24(%rsp)` directly!) and kernel interrupts (`-mno-red-zone`), and AAPCS64 shifts `z8`–`z23` and `p4`–`p15` between `Slack` and `Frame` depending on whether a callee uses the base PCS or SVE PCS, while `glibc` AArch64 `_dl_tlsdesc_dynamic` saves only 128-bit `q0`–`q31` (`SAVE_Q_REGISTERS`), leaving SVE upper lanes outside its saved set.

## Applicability

These findings govern post-codegen binary verification, static/dynamic ELF linkers, foreign-function interface (FFI) contracts, and calling-convention formalizations targeting Linux `x86-64` and `AArch64`.

- **Upstream systems and their relationships:**
  - `rui314/mold` (`331ac663668dc0b1f4949403854613b9b1913025`), `wild-linker/wild` (`e15f1f9077812d657ee3a3237b6206c430f82eef`, formerly `davidlattimore/wild`), and `llvm/llvm-project` (`4f6781e188aaae89290d963375341d3886f90945`, `lld/ELF`) are compared directly on byte-level `x86-64` and `AArch64` GOT and TLS relaxation implementations. Note that at commit `331ac663668dc0b1f4949403854613b9b1913025`, `mold` has been rewritten in Rust (`elf/arch/{x86_64,arm64}.rs` rather than the historical C++ `src/arch-*.cc` layout).
  - `bminor/glibc` (`04e750e75b73957cf1c791535a3f4319534a52fc`) defines the dynamic loader relocation sequencing (`elf/dl-reloc.c`, `elf/dynamic-link.h`, `elf/do-rel.h`, `elf/rtld.c`) and runtime `TLSDESC` trampolines (`sysdeps/{x86_64,aarch64}/dl-tlsdesc*.{S,h}`).
  - `rems-project/linksem` (`446412810661b91a39c86425c1e924e35661098c`) and Kell, Mulligan & Sewell (OOPSLA 2016) provide the formal ELF linking baseline against which post-codegen relaxation and `(Frame, Slack)` calling-convention models are evaluated.
  - Local execution probes ran on Linux `x86-64` using GCC 15.2.0 (`-14`), GNU `ld 2.47`, and Debian GLIBC 2.43 (`-7+gl1`), directly confirming linker byte rewrites, `EFLAGS` divergence, `STT_GNU_IFUNC` startup `SIGSEGV`, `.eh_frame` invariance, and Linux user-space red-zone preservation across `SIGUSR1`.
- **Scope limits:** Findings apply to ELF static/dynamic linking under System V AMD64 psABI and Arm IHI 0055/0056 (`AAPCS64`/`AAELF64`). Mach-O (`ld64`/`ld_prime`), PE/COFF (`lld-link`), and RISC-V **length-shrinking** linker relaxations (`R_RISCV_RELAX` deleting instructions and rewriting `.eh_frame`/symbols) exhibit materially different DWARF/CFA invariants and are noted under Boundaries.

#### 1.3 DWARF `.eh_frame` Unwind Table Preservation

Basis: execution (GCC 15.2.0 + GNU `ld 2.47` `readelf -wf` diff on `got_relax_pie` vs. `got_norelax_pie`).

Because every x86-64 and AArch64 GOT/TLS relaxation above is **strictly byte-length-preserving** (`7→7`, `6→6`, `9→9`, `16→16`), FDE start/end PC ranges and `DW_CFA_advance_loc` deltas in `.eh_frame` remain **byte-identical** between relaxed and unrelaxed binaries (`norm_call` FDE `pc=0x1190..0x11a2`, `DW_CFA_advance_loc: 4 to 0x1194`, `DW_CFA_def_cfa_offset: 16`, `DW_CFA_advance_loc: 10 to 0x119e`, `DW_CFA_def_cfa_offset: 8`). The linker does *not* need to rewrite `.eh_frame` CFA bytecode for x86-64/AArch64 GOT or TLS relaxations—in sharp contrast to instruction-deleting relaxations (e.g., RISC-V `R_RISCV_RELAX` shrinking `auipc+jalr` to `jal`), which mutate section offsets and require full DWARF/symbol address recomputation.

### 2. `glibc` Dynamic Relocation Ordering and `STT_GNU_IFUNC` Resolver Failures

Basis: source (`bminor/glibc@04e750e75b73957cf1c791535a3f4319534a52fc`: `elf/dl-reloc.c`, `elf/dynamic-link.h`, `elf/do-rel.h`, `elf/rtld.c`, `sysdeps/x86_64/dl-machine.h`) + execution (live Debian GLIBC 2.43 reproducer matrix).

#### 2.1 Exact Relocation Pass Structure in `glibc` (`04e750e7`)

1. **Inter-DSO ordering (`elf/rtld.c`):** `ld.so` relocates loaded shared objects in reverse `l_initfini` dependency order (dependencies before dependents), skipping IFUNC execution only during diagnostic `LD_TRACE_LOADED_OBJECTS`/`LD_WARN` scans (`__RTLD_NOIFUNC`, `rtld.c:2042, 2131`). If two shared objects have a **cyclic dependency** or a **missing `DT_NEEDED`** edge (or `LD_PRELOAD` interposition reverses lookup), DSO `B` can be relocated while DSO `A`'s `.data.rel.ro` (`R_X86_64_RELATIVE`) and `.got` are still unrelocated.
2. **Intra-DSO range splitting & `DT_BIND_NOW` merging (`elf/dl-reloc.c:246–248, 296`, `elf/dynamic-link.h:81–133`):**
   - When `DT_BIND_NOW` (`-Wl,-z,now`) is set (and profiling is inactive), `dl-reloc.c:247–248` clears `lazy = 0`.
   - `_ELF_DYNAMIC_DO_RELOC` (`dynamic-link.h:81–133`) assigns `ranges[0]` = `DT_RELA` (`.rela.dyn`) and `ranges[1]` = `DT_JMPREL` (`.rela.plt`).
   - **Critical `-z,now` range-merge asymmetry (`dynamic-link.h:111–116`):** Only when `!do_lazy` **and** `ranges[0].start + ranges[0].size == start` (`.rela.dyn` and `.rela.plt` are contiguous) does `glibc` merge `ranges[1]` into `ranges[0]`. Under lazy binding (`do_lazy != 0`), `ranges[0]` runs completely to completion *before* `ranges[1]` even rebases `.got.plt` entries via `elf_machine_lazy_rel` (`dl-machine.h:513–522`).
3. **Asymmetric deferral inside `elf/do-rel.h` (lines 85–100, 129–167):**
   - `do-rel.h` records the first and last `ELF_MACHINE_IRELATIVE` (`R_X86_64_IRELATIVE`) within a range (`r2..end2`) and defers them to a second sub-pass at the end of *that range*.
   - **CRITICAL GAP:** Relocations of type **`R_X86_64_64`** or **`R_X86_64_GLOB_DAT`** whose target symbol has `ELF64_ST_TYPE(sym->st_info) == STT_GNU_IFUNC` are **NOT** `R_X86_64_IRELATIVE` and are **NOT deferred**! They execute the IFUNC resolver **immediately inline inside `ranges[0]` (`.rela.dyn`)**, strictly before `ranges[1]` (`.rela.plt`) has run—even when `DT_NEEDED` is completely acyclic and valid!
4. **Eager `TLSDESC` initialization (`sysdeps/x86_64/dl-machine.h:523–540`):** Even under `RTLD_LAZY`, `elf_machine_lazy_rel` eagerly calls `elf_machine_rela` for `R_X86_64_TLSDESC` (comment at line 537: *"Always initialize TLS descriptors completely at load time, in case static TLS is allocated for it that requires locking"*), invoking `TRY_STATIC_TLS` (`dl-machine.h:377`) to assign `_dl_tlsdesc_return` (fast static-TLS path) or `_dl_make_tlsdesc_dynamic` + `_dl_tlsdesc_dynamic` (slow DTV path).

### 5. Human Comprehensibility and Conceptual Simplicity / Model Refinement (C1–C2)

Basis: derived from primary source analysis and empirical probes.

#### 5.1 (C1) Human Comprehensibility & Mental-Model Ergonomics

- **Hidden post-codegen mutation breaks local source-to-assembly audit:** Working systems engineers—and automated verifiers reasoning over compiler-emitted `.s`/`.o` files—naturally assume the linker only fills in numeric displacement immediates (`S + A - P`). The discovery that GNU `ld` silently rewrites `addq` into `leaq` for `%rax` (preserving `ZF`) while rewriting the identical instruction into `addq $imm` for `%r12` (clearing `ZF`)—**even when the user explicitly passes `-Wl,--no-relax`**—violates local mental models and produces bugs impossible to diagnose from `.o` disassembly alone.
- **Opaque `IFUNC` startup diagnostics:** When `t_now_plt` and `t_lazy_plt` segfaulted (`exit=139`) inside `ld.so` before `main()`, the dynamic loader emitted **zero** diagnostic explaining that `.rela.dyn` `R_X86_64_64` triggered `resolve_b()` prior to `.rela.plt` relocation; diagnosing it required `readelf -r` inspection combined with reading `glibc/elf/do-rel.h` and `dynamic-link.h`. Even senior engineers commonly misbelieve that adding explicit `DT_NEEDED` or switching from `-z,now` to `-z,lazy` eliminates IFUNC ordering crashes (both still crashed in our live glibc 2.43 reproducer!).
- **Subtle calling-convention traps:** Three footguns consistently evade human inspection: (1) assuming a `TLSDESC` access preserves the x86-64 red zone (it does *not*: `_dl_tlsdesc_dynamic` immediately overwrites `-8(%rsp)..-24(%rsp)`); (2) assuming `-mred-zone` is safe in bare-metal/kernel/interrupt-handler code (hardware interrupts push frames onto `%rsp` without a 128-byte skip); and (3) assuming AArch64 `v8–v15` preservation covers 128-bit NEON or SVE upper lanes (base AAPCS64 preserves only the bottom 64 bits `d8–d15`, and glibc's AArch64 `_dl_tlsdesc_dynamic` preserves only 128-bit `q0–q31`).

#### 5.2 (C2) Simplicity, Orthogonality, and Universal Model Refinement

- **Separating architectural invariants from historical ABI accidents:**
  - *Instruction relaxation as an explicit verified pass vs. ad-hoc byte patching:* Today, every linker reimplements dozens of brittle byte-pattern tables (`mold` 16-entry `match` arms for `r0–r15` + 16 for APX `r16–r31`, `lld` raw `memcmp(inst, "\x48\x03\x25", 3)`, `wild` `RelaxationKind` enums) because ELF `.o` files encode relaxation permissions implicitly in relocation type numbers (`GOTPCRELX`, `GOTTPOFF`, `TLSDESC_CALL`) rather than in a typed relocation-transition contract. Worse, static-PIE `_start` conflates *mandatory semantic lowering* (eliminating pre-relocation GOT reads) with *optional optimization* (`--no-relax`).
  - *Universal `(Frame_κ, Slack_κ)` refinement:* Rather than maintaining separate ad-hoc formalisms for C calls, `TLSDESC`, POSIX signals, kernel IRQs, SVE calls, and tail calls, a single **boundary-indexed bit-slice partition** `(Frame_κ, Slack_κ, OwnTransfer_κ)` over machine state `(RegBitSlices ∪ StackRanges ∪ Flags)` makes every exception an ordinary instance of one deep module:
    - Each ISA relaxation rule `R : insn_seq → insn_seq'` is sound iff it preserves `(Frame_κ, ReturnValue)` up to `Slack_κ` (automatically explaining why `lld`'s `lea` vs. `mold`'s `add` in `TLSGD → LE` are both sound—`EFLAGS ∈ Slack_std`—and revealing the exact side condition for `GOTTPOFF IE → LE`: `EFLAGS` must be **dead** immediately after `addq x@gottpoff(%rip), %reg`, an unwritten psABI precondition!).
    - Separating **mandatory lowering** (static-PIE self-relocation, undefined-weak TLS) from **optional peephole relaxation** (`--no-relax`) restores a clean semantic contract where `--no-relax` actually guarantees zero opcode mutation outside explicitly flagged mandatory transitions.
  - *Eliminating `IFUNC` startup ordering complexity:* The root defect of `STT_GNU_IFUNC` is executing Turing-complete user code *inside* the dynamic relocation loop before any module reaches a relocated steady state. Replacing arbitrary IFUNC resolver execution with either (a) declarative hardware-capability dispatch tables evaluated solely from `auxv`/`AT_HWCAP` bitmasks by `ld.so` (zero user code during relocation), or (b) two-phase loader relocation where *all* non-IFUNC relocations across *all* DSOs (both `.rela.dyn` and `.rela.plt`) complete before *any* `STT_GNU_IFUNC`/`IRELATIVE` resolver runs, eliminates the entire class of unrelocated-GOT crashes without shifting complexity to every library author.

### 6. End-to-End Solution and Implementation Validation (V1–V4)

Basis: source (`wild-linker/wild`, `rems-project/linksem`, `bminor/glibc`) + documentation (Kell et al. OOPSLA 2016) + execution.

- **(V1) Specification Adequacy, Mutation & Non-Vacuity Validation:**
  - *Linksem's wild-binary & proof co-validation (OOPSLA 2016 §4):* Differential testing against `readelf` and `hexdump` byte-for-byte roundtripping on **7,054 real-world binaries** exposed undocumented ELF conventions (`DT_GNU_PRELINKED`) missing from specs, while attempting Isabelle/HOL proofs uncovered an 8-byte endianness decoding bug that all 7,054 test binaries missed—demonstrating that neither wild-corpus testing nor proof alone suffices.
  - *Spec-mutation on relaxation contracts:* Mutating a linker relaxation table (e.g., forgetting the `%rsp`/`%r12` SIB exception in `GOTTPOFF`, dropping the `rd == rn == rt` guard in AArch64 `ADRP+LDR`, or relaxing `R_AARCH64_AUTH_TLSDESC_*`) must be caught by targeted semantic tests checking both disassembly bytes and live register/flag post-states.
- **(V2) Compiler, Lowering & Linker Soundness Fuzzing:**
  - **`wild`'s built-in `linker-diff` (`libwild/src/diff.rs` & `linker-utils/src/x86_64.rs:32–35`):** `wild` actively validates its ELF output by differential binary comparison against GNU `ld` and `lld`, even implementing `RexAddIndirectToLea` specifically so `linker-diff` recognizes GNU `ld`'s relaxation patterns alongside its own, and includes explicit fault-injection hooks (`malfunction_point_ret!("no-mov-indirect-to-absolute", None)` at `elf_x86_64.rs:275`) for deterministic negative testing!
  - *Post-link translation validation:* Running alive/SMT-backed instruction-sequence equivalence checks over every relocated/relaxed site extracted via `--emit-relocs` (asserting preservation of `Frame_κ` and architectural semantics up to `Slack_κ`) directly closes the compiler-to-binary verification gap left open by `linksem`.
- **(V3) Concurrency, Crash, Silicon & Physical Leakage Validation:**
  - *Concurrent lazy `TLSDESC` & `dlopen` races:* In `glibc`, `_dl_tlsdesc_dynamic` reads `dtv[0].counter` and `dtv[modid]` lock-free on the fast path while concurrent `dlopen`/`pthread_create` updates DTV generations; validating memory-ordering fences (`acquire`/`release` on AArch64 weak memory) requires stateless weak-memory model checking or litmus stress testing across concurrent `dlopen` + thread-spawn workloads.
  - *Asynchronous signal & hardware-interrupt red-zone stress testing:* Validating red-zone safety requires firing high-frequency asynchronous `SIGPROF`/`SIGALRM` signals (and on kernels/bare-metal, timer IRQs) while leaf functions actively read/write `[-128, 0)` below `%rsp` with known canaries, verifying zero silent corruption under `-mred-zone` in user space and verifying zero negative-`%rsp` writes under `-mno-red-zone` in kernel/bare-metal binaries (checkable statically via binary disassembly audit).
  - *Indirect-branch & speculative-execution microarchitectural side effects:* Relaxing `call *foo@GOTPCREL(%rip)` (`ff 15`) to direct `addr32 call foo` (`67 e8`) eliminates an indirect branch target (reducing BTB/Spectre-v2 attack surface and CET `ENDBR64` reliance); conversely, unrelaxed `TLSDESC` `call *(%rax)` remains an indirect call requiring `_CET_ENDBR` (`dl-tlsdesc.S:52`) / AArch64 `bti c` (`dl-tlsdesc.S:77`) and `AUTIASP` (`dl-tlsdesc.S:176`) validation on CET/BTI/PAC silicon.
- **(V4) Operational Replay & Human Comprehension Validation:**
  - *Startup relocation-order replay (`LD_DEBUG=reloc,bindings`):* Production DSO graphs (especially with `LD_PRELOAD` allocators like `tcmalloc`/`jemalloc` and `-Wl,-z,now` hardening) can be systematically audited by replaying startup under randomized/permuted `DT_NEEDED` and `LD_PRELOAD` load orders, plus static binary lints rejecting any `STT_GNU_IFUNC` resolver function whose call graph reaches `.plt`, `.got`, or TLS relocations.
  - *Human mental-model checks:* Verify via minimal C/assembly litmus tests that engineers auditing ABI boundaries distinguish (a) user-space signal red-zone safety from kernel IRQ/TLSDESC red-zone clobbering, and (b) base AAPCS64 64-bit `d8–d15` preservation from SVE full-width `z8–z23`/`p4–p15` preservation.

## Boundaries

- **Native AArch64 hardware execution (`not examined`):** All live execution probes (`GOTPCRELX`, `TLSDESC`, `GOTTPOFF` `EFLAGS`, `STT_GNU_IFUNC` `SIGSEGV`, red-zone `SIGUSR1`) ran on `x86_64-linux-gnu` (GCC 15.2.0, GNU `ld 2.47`, Debian GLIBC 2.43). AArch64 `ADRP+LDR`, `TLSDESC`, `PAUTH`, and SVE findings are grounded in direct primary-source inspection of `lld`, `mold`, `wild`, `glibc`, and Arm `IHI 0055`/`IHI 0056`, not live AArch64 silicon execution.
- **Direct local execution of `mold`, `wild`, and `lld` binaries (`not examined`):** Only GNU `ld 2.47` was executed locally on this host (`mold`, `wild`, and `lld` were not installed locally); `mold`, `wild`, and `lld` byte-level rewrites and benchmark numbers are sourced directly from their pinned repository sources (`331ac663`, `e15f1f90`, `4f6781e1`) and published README benchmarks.
- **Length-shrinking linker relaxations on RISC-V / LoongArch (`known not to apply` to the `.eh_frame`-invariance claim):** The finding that linker relaxation leaves `.eh_frame` DWARF CFA offsets byte-identical holds for `x86-64` and `AArch64` GOT/TLS relaxations because they preserve instruction byte length. It is **known not to apply** to length-shrinking relaxations (e.g., RISC-V `R_RISCV_RELAX` deleting instructions), which shift PCs and require full `.eh_frame` / debug-line / symbol rewriting (`mold/elf/shrink_sections.rs`).
- **Incremental linking performance in `wild` (`unsupported` at pinned revision):** Although `wild` is architected for future incremental linking, `wild-linker/wild@e15f1f9077812d657ee3a3237b6206c430f82eef` (`README.md`) explicitly states incremental linking is not yet implemented; all reported `wild` timings are full non-incremental links.
- **`linksem` support for `GOTPCRELX`, `TLSDESC`, instruction relaxation, and calling conventions (`unsupported` at pinned revision):** Direct inspection of `rems-project/linksem@446412810661b91a39c86425c1e924e35661098c` confirms `R_X86_64_GOTPCRELX` and `R_X86_64_REX_GOTPCRELX` are absent, `TLSDESC` relocations call `failwith "... not implemented"`, and neither opcode relaxation nor register/stack calling conventions are modeled.
- **`glibc` cross-DSO `IRELATIVE` deferral beyond `04e750e7` (`unknown` for unexamined future releases):** At `bminor/glibc@04e750e75b73957cf1c791535a3f4319534a52fc` and local Debian GLIBC 2.43, `R_X86_64_64` targeting `STT_GNU_IFUNC` in `.rela.dyn` still executes before `.rela.plt` and crashes under both `-z,now` and `-z,lazy`. Whether subsequent upstream glibc releases defer non-`IRELATIVE` `STT_GNU_IFUNC` symbol relocations across `.rela.dyn`/`.rela.plt` or across DSOs was not examined beyond `04e750e7`.
