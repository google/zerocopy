# Source and specification synthesis for 23 #3731 architecture investigations

## Summary

This is a source-reading synthesis of the 40 #3732 architecture reports added at `e2692485d` and the later LCF report added at `0c504d98f`: 41 packages in all. Their exact files and SHA-256 values are frozen in the [source census](support/source-census.json); the [ID map](support/id-map.json) names the packages used for each of 23 locally actionable #3731 investigations. Nineteen IDs gain bounded design or specification context. Four—**I057, I059, I061 and I062**—gain no new ID-specific evidence from these reports. None gains an Anneal product run, implementation, completed gate, or status change.

The most useful combined conclusion is an operation-specific contract: identify the compilation subject and complete semantic input closure; keep reusable realized work separate from the authority to publish it as current; identify the exact generation of every answer; and let only a checked path grant verification success. A small coarse scheduler can enforce those boundaries while the external tools retain their own internal incremental engines. Finer reuse needs demonstrated dependency completeness and a fresh-result oracle, not merely smaller keys.

## Cross-source judgments

- **Compiler authority versus editor latency.** The [RLS/rust-analyzer](../rls-rust-analyzer-architecture-evolution-2015-2026/REPORT.md) lineage shows that sharing `rustc` did not solve whole-crate refresh cost; rust-analyzer accepted a separate, sometimes approximate interactive engine. [Kotlin/Swift](../kotlin-swift-compiler-backed-ide-boundaries-2021-2026/REPORT.md) and [TypeScript](../typescript-host-owned-language-world-2014-2026/REPORT.md) show a different viable split: compiler-owned semantics under host-owned project, file, version and build context. This is a design tension for I002/I157, not evidence that either model fits Anneal's latency or semantic-acceptance requirements.
- **Graph granularity versus complete dependencies.** [Buck2](../buck-to-buck2-one-graph-rewrite-2013-2026/REPORT.md), [Salsa/rustc](../salsa-rustc-query-granularity-2016-2026/REPORT.md), [Adapton](../adapton-names-identity-freshness-2014-2018/REPORT.md) and [early cutoff](../early-cutoff-equality-relations-2010-2026/REPORT.md) show how named computations, tracked reads and consumer-relative equality can save work. [Build Systems à la Carte](../build-systems-a-la-carte-anneal-orchestration-2018-2026/REPORT.md) distinguishes dependency discovery, scheduling, rebuild tests and retained traces. Opaque Charon/Aeneas/Lake stages do not inherit these completeness guarantees. The source-backed baseline for I005/I015/I056 is a coarse DAG with explicit input receipts and a fresh oracle, then finer reuse only where the owner can enumerate semantic dependencies.
- **Immutable values versus mutable authority.** [Roslyn](../roslyn-immutable-snapshots-workspace-boundaries-2012-2026/REPORT.md), [Nix/Guix](../nix-guix-immutable-closures-mutable-roots-2004-2026/REPORT.md), [Theseus](../theseus-ownership-global-invariants-2017-2024/REPORT.md) and [MVCC](../mvcc-snapshot-isolation-proof-work-consistency-1981-2026/REPORT.md) distinguish a retained snapshot or object from the mutable root that selects current work and the references that keep old work alive. They do not make arbitrary filesystem capture atomic or hidden subprocess state pure. [Functional-core/effect](../functional-core-effects-hidden-state-2005-2026/REPORT.md) and [Cargo/rustc cache](../cargo-rustc-cache-reuse-extraction-boundaries-2016-2026/REPORT.md) make the missing semantic-output receipt especially concrete for I009–I011 and I145.
- **Proof authority versus useful repair state.** [LCF](../lcf-abstract-theorem-values-trust-boundaries-1978-2026/REPORT.md) makes checked theorem construction the authority boundary; [F*](../fstar-extensible-proof-automation-trust-boundaries-2019-2026/REPORT.md) shows that an abstract tactic API does not remove an SMT oracle from the trust base. [Why3](../why3-durable-proof-sessions-2011-2025/REPORT.md) retains matched old attempts as repair state while marking changed tasks obsolete. [Dafny/Viper](../dafny-boogie-viper-obligation-boundaries-2005-2026/REPORT.md) keeps source obligation and provenance distinct from backend-specific verification conditions. These support I001/I144/I145 contracts, but no source report proves an Anneal obligation or determines its final theorem strategy.
- **Events, diagnostics and edits need separate freshness rules.** [clangd](../clangd-authority-freshness-2017-2026/REPORT.md) deliberately uses different snapshot policies for completion, diagnostics and index queries. [Reconciliation loops](../reconciliation-event-hints-repair-loops-2005-2026/REPORT.md) treat notifications as hints to reread authority after missed or coalesced events. [Optimistic semantic edits](../optimistic-edits-semantic-transactions-1981-2026/REPORT.md) require the query's semantic basis to be validated at mutation, beyond text range matching. These are design constraints for I058/I060/I063/I064, not editor feature or recovery results.

## Boundaries and revalidation

The source reports are literature and pinned-source analyses; their performance numbers are often first-party and their mechanisms depend on their own languages and tool contracts. This synthesis does not infer Anneal product validation, new issue state, a universally minimal identity tuple, a complete effect list, or a winning architecture. The v67 audit leaves all 333 statuses, gates, residuals and prerequisites unchanged. Run `python3 -B support/check.py` here and the v67 audit checker offline.

After any future remote advance or rebase, regenerate the audit validation and source inventory against the **actual commit parent**, then regenerate the catalog and check that `REPORT.md` lineage language agrees with Git. For server readiness studies, keep invalid-manifest waits bounded and guarded, and report `result: null` with missing quiescence as that bounded observation rather than proof of permanent goal absence.
