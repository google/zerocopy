##### 1.1.1 Extensional vs. Intensional Type Checking in `lean4less`

Lean 4's kernel type checker (`lean4/src/kernel/type_checker.cpp`, mirrored in Lean in `Lean4Less/TypeChecker.lean` at commit `5b66bbc4af5c2c11313fcfb5098630dadaf19281`) implements an **extensional** definitional equality judgment ($\Gamma \vdash t \equiv u$) that is not confluent under syntactic reduction alone. Specifically, Lean 4's `isDefEq` relies on three context-sensitive extensional rules that fail to hold as pure rewrite rules in intensional type theories such as Dedukti ($\lambda\Pi$-calculus modulo rewriting) or Rocq's Calculus of Inductive Constructions:

1. **Definitional Proof Irrelevance (`isDefEqProofIrrel`, `Lean4Less/TypeChecker.lean:947–962`):** Whenever `inferType p` has sort `Prop` (`Sort 0`), Lean's kernel equates any two proof terms `h1 h2 : p` definitionally (`h1 ≡ h2`). In an intensional checker without typing information during reduction, arbitrary proof terms `h1` and `h2` do not reduce to a common normal form. When a proof term `h` appears inside the *type* of an expression (such as `Fin.mk val h` or dependent array indexing `a[i]'h`), two terms `f h1` and `f h2` are definitionally equal in Lean 4 even when `h1` and `h2` contain free variables that prevent syntactic reduction.
2. **K-like Singleton Elimination (`toCtorWhenK`, `Lean4Less/TypeChecker.lean:872–913`):** When a recursor `R` has `RecursorVal.k == true` (meaning its inductive type sits in `Prop`, has a single constructor, and all constructor fields after parameters are proofs or indices fixed by the target type, such as `Eq.rec` or `Acc.rec`), Lean's kernel reduces `R ... major` *even when `major` is a neutral term* (for example, a free variable `h : a = a`) by synthesizing a dummy constructor application (`mkNullaryCtor` at `Lean4Less/Inductive.lean:160–188`) and checking whether `major` is definitionally equal to that constructor via proof irrelevance. A pure rewrite engine cannot fire a constructor-headed rewrite rule on a neutral variable `h`.
3. **Quotient Reduction (`quotReduceRec`, `Lean4Less/Quot.lean:80–91`) and Smart Unfolding (`unfoldDefinitionCore`, `Lean4Less/TypeChecker.lean:725–796`):** `Quot.lift f h (Quot.mk r a)` reduces definitionally to `f a` by discarding the proof `h : ∀ a b, r a b → f a = f b`. When combined with proof irrelevance inside `Quot.ind`, terms whose heads depend on `Quot.lift` can be definitionally equal in Lean 4 without being convertible under purely syntactic $\beta\delta\iota\zeta$-reduction.

To bridge this gap, `lean4less` (`5b66bbc4af5c2c11313fcfb5098630dadaf19281`) implements a **dual-mode kernel type checker and proof-term translator** (`Lean4Less/Methods.lean:288–326` and `Lean4Less/TypeChecker.lean:1058–1242`). During type checking of an exported Lean 4 environment, every call to `isDefEq t s` runs in two modes controlled by `Context.translate : Bool` and returns `(Bool × Option Equiv)` where `Inductive Equiv` (`Lean4Less/Methods.lean:62–72`) tracks:
- `Equiv.rfl`: `t` and `s` are already convertible in the weakened intensional theory (pure $\beta\delta\iota\zeta\eta$ plus `Quot.lift` on manifest `Quot.mk` constructors), so no cast is needed.
- `Equiv.defeq (p : Expr)`: `t` and `s` require extensional rules to equate, and `p` is an explicitly constructed Lean 4 proof term of type `HEq t s` (`Lean4Less/EConsts.lean:28–165`).

Whenever an application `f a` requires `inferType a` (`ta`) to match the domain `dom` of `f` and `isDefEq dom ta` yields `Equiv.defeq eq`, `lean4less` rewrites the subterm `a` to `Lean4Less.cast dom ta eq a` (`appHeq` at `Lean4Less/EConsts.lean:77–165`). This translation eliminates Lean 4's three extensional conversion rules in favor of three explicit axiomatic constants in `Lean4Less/EConsts.lean` (`deps/lean4less-lib/src/Lean4Less.lean` in `lean2dk`):
- `Lean4Less.pIrrel : ∀ {P : Prop} (p q : P), HEq p q` (replaces `isDefEqProofIrrel`);
- `Lean4Less.Singleton.elim` (replaces `toCtorWhenK` on neutral major premises by proving that any inhabitant of a K-inductive equals its synthesized constructor application); and
- `Lean4Less.Quot.liftProp` (handles propositional quotient reduction when `Quot.ind` interacts with proof irrelevance).

##### 1.1.2 Dedukti Encoding and `Init.Data.Nat.Lemmas` Bottlenecks (`lean2dk`)

`lean2dk` (`471208f18c29420e25750503648469fc95d2ac8f` on `main`; `29f985670008c29e852d61c562b8c10c84f7c5fb` on `claude-stable`) embeds the intensional output of `lean4less` into Dedukti (`theory/lean.dk:1–66`). Because Dedukti lacks first-class universe level expressions (`max`, `imax`) in its core syntax, `theory/lean.dk` encodes Lean universe levels using nested AC-normalized multi-sets (`s : Nat → Lvl → Lvl` and `u : Nat → Lvl'` with confluence rewrite rules for `max` and `imax` at `theory/lean.dk:15–58`), maps Lean's `Sort l` to `s l`, and encodes dependent function types via `p s1 s2 A (x => B x)`. Crucially, `Lean4Less.cast` becomes a single computational rewrite rule in Dedukti (`theory/lean.dk:64–66`):

```text
[l, T, a] Lean4Less.cast l l T T (HEq.refl l T a) a --> a.
```

Running stock Dedukti (`dkcheck` or `kocheck`) on the translated Lean 4 core library fails to terminate or exhausts memory (`> 32 GB` RSS) before completing `Init.Data.Nat.Lemmas`. Inspection of `lean2dk`'s `claude-stable` branch (`29f985670008c29e852d61c562b8c10c84f7c5fb`) reveals three distinct root causes and their concrete workarounds:
1. **Eager Constant Delta-Unfolding Blowup (`patches/dkcheck-lazy-delta.patch:1–351`):** Stock `dkcheck` treats every defined constant (`def f := body`) as a normal rewrite rule `f --> body`. During weak head normal form (`state_whnf`), stock `dkcheck` eagerly unfolds every defined constant before checking whether two terms already share the same head constant and convertible arguments. On Lean 4's deeply nested `let` expressions and recursive `Nat` lemmas, eager unfolding causes exponential term blowup. `patches/dkcheck-lazy-delta.patch` (enabled by `DK_LAZY_DELTA=1`) splits each symbol's rules into constructor/recursor rules (`_cstr`, fired eagerly in `state_whnf`) and definition rules (`_def`, deferred to `are_convertible_lst`), implementing Lean 4's two-phase heuristic (`def_Phase_Heuristic`): when comparing `f a1..an` and `g b1..bm`, `dkcheck` first tries structural argument comparison when `f == g`, then unfolds only the side with fewer arguments (`List.length l1 < List.length l2`) before falling back to unfolding both.
2. **Lack of DAG Memoization in WHNF, Convertibility, and $\eta$-Expansion (`patches/dkcheck-eta-memo.patch:1–291`):** Stock `dkcheck` re-reduces shared subterms from scratch on every visit. `patches/dkcheck-eta-memo.patch` adds three open-addressed physical-equality (`==`) hash tables to `reduction.ml`, cleared at the end of each top-level declaration (`clear_all_memos ()`): `whnf_memo` (65,536 slots), `conv_memo` (262,144 slots), and `eta_memo` (65,536 slots).
3. **Unbounded Proof-by-Reflection Terms in `omega` and `Nat.Linear` (`Lean2Dk/Export.lean:245–360` on `claude-stable`):** Even with `DK_LAZY_DELTA=1` and hash-table memoization, 43 theorems in `Init.Data.Nat` and `Init.Data.Int` fail to check within practical memory bounds because Lean 4's `omega` and `Nat.Linear` tactics generate massive `Eq.refl true` proof terms over `Nat.Linear.ExprCnstr` and `Nat.Linear.PolyCnstr` normalizers (`Nat.Linear.ExprCnstr.eq_of_toNormPoly_eq`, `Nat.Linear.PolyCnstr.eq_of_toNormPoly_eq`, and `Lean.Order.Lemmas.rel_imp_rel`). Because `lean4less` must insert `HEq` casts inside the reflection evaluation trace, the resulting Dedukti proof terms trigger tens of millions of higher-order unification steps. On `claude-stable`, `Lean2Dk/Export.lean` stubs those 43 declarations as body-less Dedukti axioms via `stubDefs` (`Export.lean:245–279`) and `stubProofPrefixes` (`Export.lean:281–336`).

##### 1.1.3 Lean-to-Rocq Barriers (`rocq-lean-import`), `babel-formal`, and `specr`

Directly importing Lean 4 into Rocq via `rocq-community/rocq-lean-import` (`2d28bbe80e7361bf3ac54f5ef099207fc61ce837`, `src/lean.ml:1–2271` and `theories/Loader.v:1–88`) encounters three foundational mismatches:
1. **Exponential Sort-Polymorphism Duplication ($2^n$ Instantiation Masks, `src/lean.ml:699–850`):** In Lean 4, `Sort u` evaluates to impredicative, proof-irrelevant `Prop` when `u = 0` and predicative `Type (u-1)` when `u > 0`, and `imax u v` collapses to `0` whenever `v = 0`. In Rocq, strict proof-irrelevant propositions (`SProp`) and predicative types (`Type@{u}`) belong to disjoint sort hierarchies. Consequently, whenever a Lean declaration with $n$ universe parameters `[u_1, ..., u_n]` uses `Sort u_i` or `imax`, `rocq-lean-import` must generate separate Rocq declarations for up to $2^n$ bitmasks (`Instantiation` in `src/lean.ml:699–765`).
2. **Nested/Mutual Inductives and Guarded Fixpoints (`src/lean.ml:1927–2083`):** Lean 4 compiles recursive functions to primitive recursors (`rec`), whereas Rocq's kernel checks structural termination via a syntactic `fix` guard condition and enforces a stricter positivity condition on nested inductive types. When translating Lean inductives that fail Rocq's positivity or eliminator checks, `rocq-lean-import` disables Rocq's kernel safety flags (`check_guarded = false`, `check_positive = false`, `check_universes = false` at `src/lean.ml:1945–1968`) or postulates the recursor and its $\iota$-reduction equations as axioms (`check_eliminations = false`).
3. **Quotient Soundness (`theories/Loader.v:15–56` and `src/lean.ml:2084–2150`):** Rocq's `SProp` supports a `Private Inductive quot` wrapper whose constructor is hidden outside the module, allowing `Quot.lift` to compute definitionally on `Quot.mk`, but `Quot.sound` is not provable in Rocq without an axiom; `theories/Loader.v` therefore postulates `Axiom quot_sound` (using `Squash (r a b)` in `SProp`).

By contrast, `LLM4Rocq/babel-formal` (`71c2aa9bc78e6d41c5421f8ae8e9f551c12f570b`, `src/lean_parse.py` and `src/coq_emit.py`) translates individual self-contained kernel proof terms across Calculus of Constructions dialects using an AST pivot combined with LLM repair, without handling Lean 4's full inductive/quotient/typeclass environment. `minirust/specr` (`15200eb5e676e2ee91f43bd79e674f8fc32e5348`, `src/tree.rs`, `0.1.41`) is a specification-to-Rust transpiler that parses `.md` files containing `specr` dialect blocks (such as GC-managed `List<T>`, `Map<K, V>`, `Set<T>`, and mathematical `Int`) using `syn` and emits an executable Rust interpreter crate (`libspecr`), rather than Lean, Rocq, or Isabelle theorems.

#### 1.3 Semantic and Type-Directed Search Engines (`Loogle`, `LeanExplore`, `LeanSearchClient`)

**Basis:** `source inspection`, `measurement` (inspection of `nomeata/loogle` at `9f11169aaebf1ed1e7dcc4077f2aafe0fcf66fd0`, `justincasher/lean-explore` at `17b9d6c87a2bf0dc4ea27de05da8a1c9965391b7`, and `leanprover-community/LeanSearchClient` at `c61286b7b63cbfd563b28e11e81d0a18233a044a`).

2. **Hybrid Search (`LeanExplore` and `LeanSearchClient`):** `LeanExplore` (`17b9d6c87a2bf0dc4ea27de05da8a1c9965391b7`, `src/lean_explore/util/search_engine.py:34–412`) combines BM25+ lexical scores (`name_tokens` $1.5\times$, `informal_tokens` $1.0\times$, `statement_tokens` $0.8\times$), dense cosine similarity over `Qwen/Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B` FAISS vectors, call-graph dependency weight (`DEPENDENCY_WEIGHT = 0.25`), and `Qwen/Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B` cross-encoder reranking. `LeanSearchClient` (`c61286b7b63cbfd563b28e11e81d0a18233a044a`, `LeanSearchClient/Syntax.lean:51–328`) integrates `#leansearch`, `#statesearch`, and `#loogle` with `checkTactic` (`Syntax.lean:100–106`), running `withoutModifyingState <| evalTactic stx` to filter candidate tactics against the live goal. #### 4.2 LCF Abstract Theorem Values and Four-Layer Acceptance Authority (`1978–2026`)

**Basis:** `primary historical publications`, `derived` (Gordon et al. POPL 1978 DOI `10.1145/512760.512773`; Gordon, Milner, and Wadsworth LNCS 78 1979 DOI `10.1007/3-540-09724-4`; Milner MFCS 1979 DOI `10.1007/3-540-09526-8_11`; Paulson Cambridge LCF 1987 DOI `10.1017/CBO9780511526602.008`; `google/zerocopy@cc135f46155b72e4b51188525c2974a3b84acf92`).

LCF made theorem values an abstract ML type whose constructors are primitive inference rules, separating backward tactic search from forward validation. When theorem results are serialized (such as Lean `.olean` files), an in-process construction invariant becomes a provenance and freshness obligation:

| Question | Small Logical Checker Can Answer? | Additional Anneal Obligation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Is this proof valid for this theorem in this logical environment? | Yes, for the checker's supported logic and assumptions | Pin checker/environment identity and trust |
| Is this theorem the one Anneal intended to prove? | Not by theorem checking alone | Obligation identity and provenance |
| Does the theorem/model faithfully represent the selected Rust semantics? | Not by target-level checking alone | Source/model correspondence |
| Are cached/generated artifacts current for this source/build/tool state? | Not by target-level checking alone | Freshness and input closure |
| Is this successful result authorized to replace the current published result? | No | Generation/fencing/publication authority |

Anneal realizes this via **four distinct layers of acceptance authority**: (1) **Proposal/search** (tactics, solvers, and agents with zero publication authority), (2) **Logical checking** (minting an opaque, session/generation-scoped checked result), (3) **Correspondence/currentness** (binding the theorem to the exact Rust source/build/model generation), and (4) **Publication** (a generation-fenced authority transition that prevents stale runs from overwriting newer state).

#### 4.3 Claim-Relative TCB Accounting and the Six-Layer Anneal Trust Ledger (`Rocq`, `CompCert`, `seL4`, `CakeML`)

**Basis:** `documentation`, `derived` (Rocq `8.19.2` / `8.20.1` `Print Assumptions`; CompCert `3.18` `2026-08-31`; seL4 SOSP 2009 and PLDI 2013 binary translation validation; CakeML verified checkers and HOL4 bootstrapping).

Synthesizing small-kernel proof checking (Rocq de Bruijn criterion and theorem-specific `Print Assumptions`), verified compilation vs. verified post-hoc verifiers and extraction (CompCert `3.18`), downward proof-boundary extension via per-binary translation validation (seL4 PLDI 2013), and end-to-end verified checker bootstrapping (CakeML) yields **six orthogonal layers** that every durable Anneal verification record must distinguish:
1. **Logical checker:** Which kernel and evaluation primitives decide validity of the target theorem/certificate?
2. **Theorem-specific assumptions:** Which axioms, admissions, or opaque primitives lie in the final theorem's transitive closure?
3. **Source/model correspondence:** Why do the Lean definitions denote the intended Rust behavior?
4. **Transformation validation:** Which translators are globally verified, which are checked per run by a verified validator, and which remain trusted?
5. **Execution correspondence:** What ties formal target semantics to the deployed binary, runtime, OS/FFI, and hardware?
6. **Artifact identity:** Which exact source, tool, configuration, generated artifact, proof environment, and checker invocation does the evidence cover?

- **Kernel Pin Boundary (PR 14577):** Lean `4.30rc2` (`3dc1a088...`) and `4.31` (`68218e87...`) lack the final nested-inductive parameter check in `environment::add_inductive` added in `a39eab69e1eee9ad38f4efe507907b1026a77808` and present in `4.34.1` (`5045d005...`).
- **Model-Checking, Anti-Circularity, zkVM, and Proof-Producing Frontend Boundaries:** Completed-trace conformance (`CompletedConformance`) and status-erasing `Option` projections (`legacyStatusErasingSuiteCheck`) fail to transfer safety on diverging/faulting prefixes (`completed_conformance_fails_to_transfer_noUB`); zkVM integrity-only or dev/mock verification (`FakeReceipt`, `verify_mock_public_inputs`) and unbound private inputs do not establish soundness of a guest checker; and CairoZero's halting-trace `ensures_ret` does not prove termination.
