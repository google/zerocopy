# Cold-Atmosphere Baro-VNAV Altimetry, ARAIM Solution-Separation Integrity Geometry, and 1090ES ADS-B CPR Decoding Hazards

## Summary

Civil and autonomous aviation vertical navigation and surveillance pipelines depend on three coupled numerical subsystems—barometric altimetry, multi-constellation GNSS protection-level geometry, and `1090 MHz` Extended Squitter (`1090ES`) Compact Position Reporting (`CPR`)—each exhibiting sharp mathematical, datum, and state-machine failure modes verified directly across upstream codebases and numerical probes:

1. **Cold-atmosphere `Baro-VNAV` column compression creates non-linear altitude over-reads, a dangerous forward-vs.-inverse correction asymmetry, and verified defects in open-source flight stacks.** Because barometric altimeters map static pressure via the International Standard Atmosphere (`ISA`, `15 °C` sea level, `-6.5 °C/km`), cold air compresses isobaric surfaces toward terrain: at sea-level aerodrome temperature `-50 °C` and indicated `2,000 ft` above aerodrome, true height drops to **`1,545.7 ft`** (`+454.3 ft` over-read), flattening a nominal `3.00°` `Baro-VNAV` glidepath to **`2.319°`**. Critically, the **additive altitude correction required to restore `2,000 ft` true clearance** (`+589.4 ft` exact `ICAO Doc 8168` / `ESDU 77022` logarithmic integral; `+588.2 ft` linear formula) is **`29.7%` larger** than the over-read error because the temperature ratio divides by $T_{\text{actual}}$ (`223.15 K`) rather than $T_{\text{ISA}}$ (`288.15 K`); mistakenly adding the over-read leaves a **`-104.1 ft`** obstacle-clearance shortfall (and **`-157.9 ft`** at `3,000 ft`). At a `5,000 ft` cold airport (`-45 °C`, `3,000 ft` AGL), even the ICAO linear approximation under-corrects the exact logarithmic equation by **`-16.0 ft`**. Direct source inspection and execution expose concrete bugs in both pinned flight stacks: `paparazzi/paparazzi` (`2c5465fb589367e67caddf002d8aa2a658c9e95f`, `sw/airborne/math/pprz_isa.h:71,73`) hardcodes `274.15f` instead of `273.15f` in `CelsiusOfKelvin`/`KelvinOfCelsius` (`1.0 K` bias), relies on an uncompensated isothermal `288.15 K` scale height (`+106.4 m` ISA error at `3 km`; `+1,332.8 m` at `10 km`), lacks a `pressure > 0` guard in `pprz_isa_height_of_pressure_full` (yielding `-nan`), and discards GPS vertical corrections whenever `USE_BAROMETER` is enabled in `ins_alt_float.c:186-253`; while `JSBSim-Team/jsbsim` (`3f153c18a2518f8472e37c89e70444b1c671b700`, `src/models/atmosphere/FGStandardAtmosphere.cpp:284,311`) reads mutated `LapseRates[b]` instead of `StdLapseRates[b]` inside `GetStdPressure()`, corrupting standard pressure by `+1.67 psf` (`-29.63 ft` at `10,000 ft` under a `-50 °C` graded delta).
2. **Multi-constellation `ARAIM` protection levels inflate sharply and non-monotonically under weak subset geometry compared to `SBAS` weighted least squares (`MAAST`).** Source inspection clarifies an essential scope distinction: `Stanford-GPS-Lab/MAAST` (`7d32b049c62d55f90478248de04bec956fdeeeb4`) implements single-solution `L1`/`L5` `DFMC` `SBAS`/`WAAS` protection levels (`usr_vhpl.m`, `K_V = 5.33`, `K_H = 6.0`, `L1`/`L5` iono-free `CNMP` variance multiplier `6.6995×`, `2.588×` in $\sigma$), **not** full multi-fault `ARAIM`, and contains two unguarded boundary hazards in `usr_vhpl.m:44,127-129` (hardcoded `e = ones(50,1)` overflowing above 50 SVs, and `TRUTH_FLAG == 1 && n_view > 3` invoking unconditioned `inv(G'*W*G)` on a singular `5×5` matrix when `n_view == 4, n_const == 2`). Implementing the Blanch et al. (IEEE TAES 2015) multi-fault `ARAIM` user algorithm over the same dual-constellation GPS+Galileo geometry shows that under a `30°` banking/urban mask (`N = 7` SVs), `DFMC SBAS VPL` stays `25.47 m` (`cond = 1,018`), whereas `ARAIM VPL` explodes to **`96.35 m`** (`max_cond = 3.64 × 10^6`, exceeding LPV-200 `VAL = 35/50 m`). Moreover, `ARAIM VPL` is **non-monotone** in satellite count: dropping one redundant low-elevation SV at `37° N` (`20°` mask) *decreases* `VPL` from `17.63 m` to `17.46 m` (`-0.17 m`) by shrinking the fault-subset count $K$ from `93` to `80`, whereas dropping a geometrically critical SV spikes `VPL` to **`100.73 m` (`+83.10 m`)**.
3. **Stateful `1090ES` `ADS-B` `CPR` decoding harbors cross-format pairing, anti-meridian wrap, stale-frame same-`NL` false-positive, and single-parity memory-bloat defects—verified directly by executing `junzis/pyModeS` (`4bf083284c0d9878ca0c88f16b9b5f6514ca194a`).** Executing the pinned upstream library uncovers four concrete edge-case behaviors:
   - **Real cross-format `BDS 0,5`/`0,6` pairing bug (`src/pyModeS/_pipe.py:866-900`):** `PipeDecoder` stores airborne (`BDS 0,5`, `360°` modulus) and surface (`BDS 0,6`, `90°` modulus) frames in shared `_pending_even`/`_pending_odd` queues without checking `other_bds == bds`. When an aircraft landing at Amsterdam Schiphol (`52.3120° N, 4.7700° E`) emits an Airborne Even frame followed `1.0 s` later by a Surface Odd frame, `PipeDecoder` cross-pairs them into **`(38.5817° N, -34.2913° W)`**—a **`3,353.9 km`** phantom jump into the mid-Atlantic!
   - **Anti-meridian non-wrapping (`src/pyModeS/position/_cpr.py:139,243`):** Both `airborne_position_with_ref` and `surface_position_with_ref` return `lon = +180.09999°` (`|lon| > 180°`) when resolving true `-179.9°` against reference `+179.9°`.
   - **Same-`NL` stale pairs & `TC 20-22` zero-altitude ambiguity:** Pairing frames `60 s` apart inside the same `NL=29` zone (`59.98°→60.12° N`) slips the `j` zone index and emits a `687.9 km` error (`6,942.8 km` on a `2.6°` eastbound stale pair) despite `cprNL(lat_even) == cprNL(lat_odd)`; `decode_bds05` (`bds05.py:58`) returns `0` ft instead of `None` for unavailable `ac == 0` on `TC 20–22` GNSS altitude messages; and `cprNL` (`bisect_right` over `_NL_BOUNDARIES`) disagrees by `-1` from the closed-form `DO-260B/C` trig formula at `25` of `58` exact IEEE-754 boundary floats (`0` mismatches at `±10^-9°`).
   - **Throughput & single-parity memory bloat:** On alternating even/odd traffic (`200` aircraft, `20,000` frames), `PipeDecoder` sustains **`4,675 msg/s`** (`213.9 µs/msg`, `322.1 KiB` steady heap), whereas an unmatched single-parity stream (`50,000` frames) slows to **`3,001 msg/s`** and balloons peak heap **`25.1×`** to **`20,572.0 KiB`** (`30,096` pending dicts retained for `eviction_ttl = 300 s`).

### 1. Cold-Atmosphere `Baro-VNAV` Altimetry, Hydrostatic Integration, and Datum Coupling

`Basis: normative + source + execution (real pprz_isa.h C harness + exact hydrostatic solver)`

#### 1.1 Exact Hydrostatic Compression vs. `ICAO Doc 8168` Forward Over-Read and Inverse Correction

A pressure altimeter measures ambient static pressure $P$ and inverts the `ISA` hydrostatic law ($T_0 = 288.15\text{ K}$, $P_0 = 101325\text{ Pa}$, lapse magnitude $L_0 = 0.0065\text{ K/m} = 0.0019812\text{ K/ft}$, $g_0 = 9.80665\text{ m/s}^2$, $R_{\text{dry}} = 287.05287\text{ J/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$) referenced to the aerodrome altimeter setting (`QNH`). Because ideal-gas hydrostatic balance satisfies $dP/dh_G = -P g_0 / (R_{\text{dry}} T_{\text{actual}})$, while indicated pressure height obeys $dP/dh_P = -P g_0 / (R_{\text{dry}} T_{\text{ISA}})$, the differential mapping between true geopotential height $h_G$ and indicated pressure height $h_P$ is:

$$\frac{dh_G}{dh_P} = \frac{T_{\text{actual}}(h_P)}{T_{\text{ISA}}(h_P)} = 1 + \frac{\Delta T_{\text{std}}}{T_0 - L_0 h_P}$$

Under the `ICAO Doc 8168` (`PANS-OPS`, Volume III, Part II, Chapter 4; derived from `ESDU 77022`) constant-ISA-deviation model ($\Delta T_{\text{std}} = T_{\text{aerodrome}} - (T_0 - L_0 h_{\text{ad}}) < 0$ in cold air), integrating from aerodrome elevation $h_{\text{ad}}$ across indicated height above aerodrome $H_{\text{ind}}$ yields:

1. **Forward over-read (how low an aircraft flies when holding uncorrected $H_{\text{ind}}$):**
   $$H_{\text{true}}(H_{\text{ind}}) = H_{\text{ind}} - \frac{\Delta T_{\text{std}}}{L_0} \ln\!\left(1 - \frac{L_0 H_{\text{ind}}}{T_0 - L_0 h_{\text{ad}}}\right), \qquad \Delta h_{\text{over}} = H_{\text{ind}} - H_{\text{true}}(H_{\text{ind}})$$
2. **Inverse additive correction (what $\Delta h_{\text{add}}$ must be added to published minimum $H_{\text{pub}}$ so $H_{\text{true}}(H_{\text{pub}} + \Delta h_{\text{add}}) = H_{\text{pub}}$):** Solving the implicit equation above yields $\Delta h_{\text{add}} > \Delta h_{\text{over}}$, approximated by the `ICAO Doc 8168` operational linear formula (with sea-level-adjusted temperature $t_0 = t_{\text{ad}} + L_0 h_{\text{ad}}$ in $^\circ\text{C}$):
   $$\Delta h_{\text{ICAO,lin}} = H_{\text{pub}} \cdot \frac{15 - t_0}{273 + t_0 - 0.5 \, L_0 \, (H_{\text{pub}} + h_{\text{ad}})}$$
3. **Glidepath angle degradation on uncompensated `Baro-VNAV`:** An FMS flying a nominal `3.00°` vertical path angle (`VPA`) against barometric altitude actually descends along $\theta_{\text{eff}} = \arctan\!\left(\tan(3.00^\circ) \cdot \frac{H_{\text{true}}}{H_{\text{ind}}}\right)$, collapsing below `2.50°` below `-32 °C` and violating obstacle-clearance surfaces below the charted `APV/Baro-VNAV` minimum temperature unless equipped with certified automated temperature compensation.

**Three high-consequence traps illuminated by the table:**
- **Forward-vs.-inverse confusion:** Using the over-read formula (`H * (T_ISA - T_act) / T_ISA`) as an additive pilot/FMS correction leaves a `-104.1 ft` to `-157.9 ft` residual obstacle-clearance deficit at `-50 °C`.
- **High-elevation breakdown of the linear formula:** At `5,000–7,000 ft` aerodromes (`-40 °C` to `-45 °C`), the `ICAO` linear approximation under-predicts exact logarithmic correction by `10.7–16.0 ft`, confirming why `ICAO Doc 8168` requires the exact logarithmic equation for high-altitude/extreme-cold procedure design and FMS temperature compensation.
- **Elevation-reference & datum confusion (`aerodrome` vs. `MSL` vs. `WGS-84` ellipsoid):** Cold-temperature correction scales with height above the *altimeter setting source* ($H = h_{\text{pub}} - h_{\text{ad}}$), **not** total `MSL` altitude; applying it to total `MSL` altitude over-corrects by hundreds of feet at high-elevation airports, whereas mixing barometric `MSL` (`QNH`) with geometric `WGS-84` ellipsoidal height omits the geoid undulation $N(\phi,\lambda)$ (up to `-102 m` / `-335 ft` south of India and `+76 m` / `+249 ft` near Papua New Guinea in Paparazzi's `pprz_geodetic_wgs84.h:62-63`).

### 2. Multi-Constellation `ARAIM` Solution-Separation, Chi-Square Residuals, and `MAAST` Geometry Limits

`Basis: normative + source + execution (exact port of MAAST usr_vhpl.m + standalone Blanch et al. 2015 ARAIM solver)`

#### 2.1 Scope Distinction: What `Stanford-GPS-Lab/MAAST` (`7d32b049c62d55f90478248de04bec956fdeeeb4`) Implements

Direct inspection of `Stanford-GPS-Lab/MAAST@7d32b049c62d55f90478248de04bec956fdeeeb4` shows it is Stanford's **`SBAS`/`WAAS` (`RTCA DO-229`) and `L5` `DFMC` `SBAS` (`EUROCAE ED-259A`)** availability simulator (`usrprocess.m`, `usr_vhpl.m`, `init_mops.m`, `init_L5mops.m`), **not** the separate multi-fault `ARAIM` branch (`MAAST for ARAIM`). Specifically:
- `usr_vhpl.m:70-139` builds the multi-constellation geometry matrix $G \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times (3 + N_{\text{const}})}$ (separate clock column per constellation across `GPS`, `GLONASS`, `Galileo`, `BeiDou`, with ranging GEOs bound to the first constellation's clock), forms diagonal weight matrix $W = \operatorname{diag}(1/\sigma_i^2)$, inverts $\text{Cov} = (G^\top W G)^{-1}$, and computes single-solution `VPL = 5.33 * sqrt(Cov(3,3))` and `HPL = K_H * d_major` (`K_H = 6.0` PA / `6.18` NPA; `init_mops.m:80-82`, `init_L5mops.m:51-53`).
- `usrprocess.m:147-155` models dual-frequency `L1`/`L5` ionosphere-free code-noise/multipath amplification:
  $$\sigma_{i,\text{DFMC}}^2 = \sigma_{\text{flt},i}^2 + \sigma_{\text{UIRE},i}^2 + \sigma_{\text{CNMP},i}^2 \cdot \frac{f_1^4 + f_5^4}{(f_1^2 - f_5^2)^2} + \sigma_{\text{trop},i}^2$$
  where `(1575.42^4 + 1176.45^4) / (1575.42^2 - 1176.45^2)^2 = 6.699455` (`2.5883×` standard-deviation noise inflation!).
- **Two concrete boundary bugs in `MAAST` `usr_vhpl.m`:**
  1. `usr_vhpl.m:44,128` hardcodes `e = ones(50, 1); W = diag(e(1:n_view) ./ sig2_i(sview));` — throwing a MATLAB array-index-out-of-bounds crash whenever `n_view > 50` across 4 constellations + GEOs.
  2. `usr_vhpl.m:127-129` guards inversion with `if((n_view > 2 + n_const && n_geo) || (TRUTH_FLAG == 1 && n_view > 3))` before calling bare `Cov = inv(G'*W*G)` with no condition-number (`rcond`) check. When `TRUTH_FLAG == 1`, `n_const == 2`, and `n_view == 4`, `G` is `4 × 5`, so `G'*W*G` is **strictly rank-deficient (`rank 4 < 5`)** (verified in our probe: `mat_inv` fails singular!). Similarly, when `n_view == 3 + n_const`, there are `0` degrees of freedom (`DOF = 0`), leaving zero residual redundancy for any autonomous fault monitor.

#### 2.2 Multi-Fault `ARAIM` Solution-Separation, Chi-Square Monitoring, and Non-Monotone `VPL` Inflation

Unlike `SBAS` (where ground integrity networks monitor satellites in real time and broadcast `UDRE`/`DFRE` bounds), **Baseline `ARAIM`** (Blanch, Walter & Enge, IEEE TAES 2010; Blanch et al., IEEE TAES 2015) uses an Integrity Support Message (`ISM`) carrying $\sigma_{\text{URA}}$ (integrity bound), $\sigma_{\text{ACC}}$ (accuracy/continuity bound), nominal bias $b_{\text{nom}}$, satellite fault prior $P_{\text{sat}}$ ($\sim 10^{-5}$), and constellation fault prior $P_{\text{const}}$ ($\sim 10^{-4}$). The airborne receiver computes:

1. **All-in-view solution** $\hat{x}^{(0)} = S^{(0)} y$ where $S^{(0)} = (G^\top W_{\text{URA}} G)^{-1} G^\top W_{\text{URA}}$, plus **subset solutions** $\hat{x}^{(k)} = S^{(k)} y$ excluding each monitored fault hypothesis $k \in \{1, \dots, N_{\text{fault}}\}$ (single satellites, constellation-wide outages, and dual-satellite pairs when $\binom{N}{2} P_{\text{sat}}^2$ exceeds the unmonitored risk margin).
2. **Solution-separation test statistics** per axis $q \in \{E, N, U\}$:
   $$d_{q}^{(k)} = |\hat{x}_q^{(k)} - \hat{x}_q^{(0)}| \le T_{k,q} = K_{\text{fa},q} \, \sigma_{ss,q}^{(k)}, \qquad (\sigma_{ss,q}^{(k)})^2 = \left((S^{(k)} - S^{(0)}) C_{\text{ACC}} (S^{(k)} - S^{(0)})^\top\right)_{q,q}$$
   with $K_{\text{fa},3} = Q^{-1}\!\left(\frac{P_{\text{FA,VERT}}}{2 N_{\text{fault}}}\right)$ (note the coupling between fault-subset count $N_{\text{fault}}$ and every subset threshold $T_{k,q}$!).
3. **Chi-square residual test:** $q_{\chi^2} = y^\top W_{\text{ACC}} (I - G S_{\text{ACC}}^{(0)}) y \le T_{\chi^2}(N - 3 - N_{\text{const}}, P_{\text{FA},\chi^2})$, catching unmodeled multi-satellite correlated ramps or local multipath/ionospheric anomalies not captured by the monitored subset list—at the cost of losing fault-exclusion sensitivity when `DOF` $= N - 3 - N_{\text{const}}$ is small or when a ramp aligns with the column space of $G$.
4. **Implicit `VPL` equation solved via half-interval search (Blanch et al. 2015):**
   $$2 \, Q\!\left(\frac{\text{VPL} - b_{3}^{(0)}}{\sigma_{3}^{(0)}}\right) + \sum_{k=1}^{N_{\text{fault}}} p_{\text{fault},k} \, Q\!\left(\frac{\text{VPL} - T_{k,3} - b_{3}^{(k)}}{\sigma_{3}^{(k)}}\right) = P_{\text{HMI,VERT}} - P_{\text{not\_monitored}}$$
   where $b_3^{(k)} = \sum_i |S_{3,i}^{(k)}| b_{\text{nom}}$ is the worst-case deterministic bias projection.

Running our standalone pure-Python implementation of Blanch et al. **Key numerical and algorithmic takeaways:**
- **Subset condition-number cliff:** Even when all-in-view `SBAS` covariance looks well-conditioned (`cond = 1,018`, `VPL = 25.47 m`), excluding a single satellite or constellation inside `ARAIM` drives subset `max_cond(G_k^T W_k G_k)` to **`3.64 × 10^6`**—losing ~6.5 decimal digits of floating-point precision (`float32` Cholesky/inversion would catastrophically fail; IEEE-754 `float64` with pivoted QR or Cholesky + explicit `rcond` gating is mandatory).
- **Protection-level non-monotonicity:** Because adding a low-elevation satellite adds `N` new dual-satellite fault subsets (`K` grows $O(N^2)$), inflating $K_{\text{fa}}$ and adding bias terms $b_3^{(k)}$ across weak excluded geometries, `ARAIM VPL` is **not** monotone decreasing in satellite count—proving why Blanch et al. (2015) proposed subset consolidation and optimal `P_HMI` allocation (Blanch, Walter & Enge 2010) rather than naive equal-risk splitting.
- **Correlated unmodeled ionospheric / clock threats:** Dual-frequency `L1`/`L5` removes first-order ionospheric delay at a `2.588×` noise penalty, leaving higher-order ionospheric residuals (`MOPS_UIRE_NUM/DEN`) and single-frequency fallback regimes exposed to spatial ionospheric gradients; if a fault hits two constellations simultaneously or biases multiple satellites consistently with a vertical shift, solution separation masks it and only the `chi-square` residual test (or external baro-inertial cross-check) can raise an alert.

### 5. Human Comprehensibility and Conceptual Simplicity / Model Refinement (C1–C2)

`Basis: derived + source + normative`

- **(C1) Human Comprehensibility & Mental-Model Ergonomics:**
  - **Deceptive familiarity of "altitude" and "coordinates":** Aviation engineers and pilots confront **five incompatible vertical datums** routinely collapsed into a single `float alt` variable: (1) `Pressure Altitude` (`ISA`, `1013.25 hPa`), (2) `Indicated Altitude` (`QNH`, temperature-uncompensated), (3) `True Geopotential Altitude` (`AMSL`/geoid), (4) `Height Above Aerodrome / Terrain` (`QFE`/`AGL`), and (5) `WGS-84 Ellipsoidal Height` (`HAE`, GNSS native). Every bug we found in `Paparazzi` (`ins_alt_float.c` discarding `gps.hmsl` for `ground_alt`; `ins_int.c` fusing ellipsoidal `NED` against isothermal baro) and `pyModeS` (`TC 20–22` returning `0` ft for unavailable GNSS `HAE`) stems from untyped scalar `alt` values stripped of datum, temperature-compensation, and validity semantics!
  - **Intuition traps:** Both the **forward-vs.-inverse cold-temperature trap** (`+454 ft` over-read vs. `+589 ft` required correction at `-50 °C`) and **`ARAIM VPL` non-monotonicity** ("more satellites can *increase* `VPL`!") violate untrained human intuition, requiring explicit worked examples and diagnostic attribution (reporting *which* excluded fault subset $k^*$ dominates `VPL`, rather than emitting an opaque `VPL = 96.35 m` number!).
  - **Human vs. LLM/agent comprehension:** An automated tool or LLM readily writes down `VPL` or `CPR` equations from textbooks, yet without explicit unit/datum types (`Kelvin` vs. `Celsius`, `HAE` vs. `AMSL` vs. `PressureAlt`, `Airborne360` vs. `Surface90`, Euclidean `MOD` vs. truncated `%`) both humans and LLMs silently reproduce classic off-by-one (`274.15f`), sign, or cross-format queue bugs.
- **(C2) Simplicity, Orthogonality & Universal Model Refinement:**
  - **Refining vertical navigation via typed datum + thermodynamic state:** Rather than scattering ad-hoc scale-height approximations (`pprz_isa_height_of_pressure`), `10°` geoid hacks, and undocumented `LapseRates` mutations across modules, a single deep atmospheric/vertical-datum module separates: (a) **immutable reference `ISA` tables** (`const StdLapseRates`, preventing `JSBSim`'s `GetStdPressure` mutation bug by construction), (b) **explicit ambient temperature profile** `(T_ad, h_ad, lapse_model)` mapping `PressureAlt ↔ TrueAlt` via one bidirectional invertible hydrostatic kernel (`forward_overread` and `inverse_correction` as exact mathematical inverses), and (c) **typed vertical datums** (`PressureAlt`, `IndicatedQNH`, `OrthometricMSL`, `EllipsoidalWGS84`, `AGL`) whose conversions carry explicit geoid undulation $N(\phi,\lambda)$ and uncertainty bounds.
  - **Refining `CPR` decoding via typed frame-scale queues (Ousterhout deep module):** In `pyModeS`, `BDS 0,5` (`Airborne360`) and `BDS 0,6` (`Surface90`) have **incommensurable modular coordinate spaces** (`360°` vs. `90°`). Typing the pending CPR queue key as `(icao, cpr_scale)` (with bounded ring capacity per ICAO, e.g. `max_pending = 8`, and canonical longitude normalization `((lon + 180.0) % 360.0) - 180.0` applied uniformly at the single output boundary of `_cpr.py`) eliminates **three** distinct defects simultaneously (cross-format `3,353.9 km` jump, anti-meridian `lon > 180°`, and `25×` single-parity heap bloat) while *simplifying* invariants!
  - **Unifying `SBAS` and `ARAIM` linear algebra:** Both `MAAST` `SBAS` and `Blanch 2015` `ARAIM` share a single well-conditioned weighted least-squares core `(G, W) → (S, Cov, cond)` parameterized by a finite fault-hypothesis family $\mathcal{H} = \{H_0\} \cup \{H_k\}$ (`SBAS` is the special case $\mathcal{H} = \{H_0\}$ with fixed `K_V = 5.33`; `ARAIM` instantiates $\mathcal{H}$ from `ISM` priors `(P_sat, P_const)` and solves the risk sum via bisection—with mandatory QR/Cholesky `rcond` guards replacing bare `inv(G'*W*G)`).
