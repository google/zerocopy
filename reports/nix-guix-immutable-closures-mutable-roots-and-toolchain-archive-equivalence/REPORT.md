# Nix/Guix Immutable Closures, Mutable Deployment Roots, `leantar` Archive Formats, and Toolchain Archive Semantic Equivalence

## Summary

This report synthesizes nine primary-source, execution, and methodology investigations covering Nix and GNU Guix functional deployment architecture (`2004–2026`), stopped-container Docker tar ownership and Nix mutable-state preparation, `leantar` (`.ltar`) archive format and cross-version runtime compatibility (`v0.1.16`, `v0.1.19`, `v0.1.20`), layered toolchain-archive semantic equivalence comparison at `google/zerocopy@41f5b37afe7060fd9fe08c00b200672cd76d77b9`, adjacent-version non-generalization across Lean (`v4.30.0-rc2` to `v4.31.0`) and Charon (`0.1.210`), and negative-control replay verification of reference evidence packages.

Across these investigations, five structural conclusions hold:

1. **Immutable Realizations vs. Mutable Roots, Selection, and Activation (`Nix` and `GNU Guix`):**
   - Functional deployment systems separate **constructing an immutable realization** from **deciding which realization is current** and **retaining realizations that are still reachable**. Current Nix (`NixOS/nix@9bc9ab32a57504f068847af4f8013417476cb1ec` and stable tag `2.35.2` peeled to `2c73b59da29606068c0c98db015dd3a66955525d`) does not treat `/nix/store` as a single universal content-addressed namespace: it distinguishes derivation encodings (which are content-addressed), **input-addressed** derivation outputs, **fixed or floating content-addressed** derivation outputs, transitive reference closures, versioned profile symlinks, and garbage-collector roots (`support/evidence-map.json`, `support/matrix.json`).
   - A verification system preparing a toolchain or proof universe must keep at least five identities distinct rather than collapsing them into one digest: (a) **recipe/configuration provenance identity** (input-addressed specification), (b) **admitted upstream-input identity** (such as a fixed-output acquisition hash), (c) **realized prepared-universe identity** (manifest or tree digest), (d) **distribution/archive identity** (if serialized for transport), and (e) **active-generation and lease identity** (which generation is authoritative now and which older generations remain pinned by live sessions).
   - Rollback and concurrent reader safety depend on **retention**, not immutability alone: deleting old profile generations removes their GC roots and makes rollback impossible (`doc/manual/source/package-management/garbage-collection.md`). Furthermore, atomic symlink switching of `/run/current-system` or a profile link is not an atomic live-system migration: both historical NixOS (Dolstra & Hemel, HotOS XI 2007) and current NixOS (`NixOS/nixpkgs@6cb205c37b7c0c39b643163d1b7721b5f9bc0913`, `nixos/modules/system/activation/activation-script.nix`) place mutable host state (`/var`, `/etc/passwd`, running services, and mounts) outside the purely functional model and reconcile it imperatively. For Anneal (`google/zerocopy@cc135f46155b72e4b51188525c2974a3b84acf92`), the transferable design is a small generation/lease manager (`PreparedGeneration`, `CurrentGeneration`, `Lease/Pin`, `Publish`, `Retire`, `Collect`) with claim-relative closure rather than importing a general-purpose derivation evaluator.

2. **Stopped-Container Archive Ownership and Bounded Nix Mutable-State Preparation:**
   - On stopped AArch64 Linux container `5444ba00b8675ccd8ad2d5a55c01b6b88785e06642e6e0e5ac7f459e31a46fd5` (image SHA-256 `71f0139a696ef851a52bebc0b8facfd20e728e0b92f7b0251480979614e24558`, Nix `2.34.8`, `Config.User=0:0`, `CapDrop=[ALL]`, `no-new-privileges`, `overlayfs`), streaming stdin tar via `docker cp --archive - CID:/work` exited `0` yet overwrote requested UID/GID `1000` fixture members to `0:0` (`support/nix-archive-ownership-smoke-20261015/put-1000.json`). Inspection of `docker/cli@9d7ad9ff180b43ae5577d048a7bac1159ce7bacf` and `moby/moby@daa0cb7f23594cdbcce5002e370027d3fd36ffd7` (`support/docker-source-identities.json`) shows that `--archive` sets `CopyUIDGID`, causing Engine `tarCopyOptions` to resolve the container's explicit `Config.User=0:0` into forced `ChownOpts`. 3. **`leantar` (`.ltar`) Archive Format and Cross-Version Runtime Compatibility (`v0.1.16`, `v0.1.19`, `v0.1.20`):**
   - At `google/zerocopy@41f5b37afe7060fd9fe08c00b200672cd76d77b9`, `anneal/flake.nix` builds standalone `leantar` from `digama0/leangz@d251df00e50c98347dea3c95b1ad39a9b0d8db55` (`v0.1.16`) for `packages.mathlib-cache-unpacked`, whereas bundled Lean `v4.30.0-rc2` (`leanprover/lean4@3dc1a088b6d2d8eafe25a7cd7ec7b58d731bd7cc`) builds `digama0/leangz@bc36c619c90ae72572daad8498631d02bf45e488` (`v0.1.19`) and Lean `v4.34.1` (`leanprover/lean4@5045d0056413266e57c625dcd7c365b10e377c52`) bundles `leantar 0.1.20`.
   - In `v0.1.16` and `v0.1.19`, `.ltar` is a custom binary container (`LTAR` V1, `LTR2` V2, `LTR3` V3) with an 8-byte little-endian `depHash` freshness header and six entry codec tags (`0` zstd level 19, `1` custom `lgz` `.olean` pointer-table + zstd dictionary compression, `2` 8-byte LE decimal trace, `3` 8-byte V2 JSON `{depHash}` trace, `4` V3 compact module trace with `schemaVersion: "2025-09-10"`, and `5` grouped `.olean`/`.olean.server`/`.olean.private`), storing relative paths without Unix permissions, ownership, mtimes, symlinks, cryptographic archive digests, or path-traversal containment (`support/leantar-archive-format-v0-1-16-v0-1-19/format-map.json`). While `v0.1.16` and `v0.1.19` share wire formats, `v0.1.19` switches extraction from direct destination writes with rollback (and a `delete_corrupted = true` default that deletes truncated inputs on `UnexpectedEof` and exits `0`) to atomic `NamedTempFile` persistence in the target directory.
   - Controlled Darwin `arm64` execution across `0.1.16`, `0.1.19`, and `0.1.20` (`support/probe.py`, `support/check.py`, `support/leantar-runtime-016-019-020-darwin-arm64/results.json`) shows that without `-s`, all three binaries emit the exact same 101-byte `LTAR` archive (`be5e94042c7f4c245ffeb92c4266c6e15f136539dbd5d3451906e4dd03f94f16`) and all nine producer/consumer pairs unpack identically; with `-s` (strip trace inputs), `0.1.20` emits a new 143-byte `LTR4` format (`f95e6fe5f7a351259f87af14838bebecc33fbd2789bbbb94d4e42971f1f1cd52`) that `0.1.16` and `0.1.19` reject (`bad .ltar file`, exit `1`).

4. **Toolchain Archive Semantic Equivalence Procedure (`google/zerocopy@41f5b37afe7060fd9fe08c00b200672cd76d77b9`):**
   - Because `omnibus-archive` (`zstd -1`) and `omnibus-archive-ci` (`zstd -6`) differ at the compressed byte layer and tar headers carry non-semantic metadata while Exocrate's `--local-archive` reuses `std::env::temp_dir().join("exocrate-archives").join(namespace)` without re-extracting once `.complete` exists, toolchain equivalence must be evaluated through a five-layer fail-closed procedure (`support/toolchain-archive-semantic-equivalence-procedure-main-41f5b37/comparison-procedure.json`): (1) compressed archive bytes, (2) decompressed tar stream, (3) freshly extracted filesystem tree inventory (paths, permissions including stripped write bits, symlinks/hardlinks, and mtimes), (4) proof-critical artifact classes (executables/dylibs, coherent `.olean`/`.olean.server`/`.olean.private` families, recomputed payload hashes independent of 64-bit Lake `.hash` sidecars, and parsed Lake `.trace` / `ConfigTrace` records distinguishing `depHash` and `outputs` from diagnostic `inputs` and `log` alongside Lake's `hashUpToDate` vs. `--old` `mtimeUpToDate` rules), and (5) end-to-end consumer execution probes (`archive-byte-identical`, `payload-identical`, `equivalent-under-declared-consumer-contract`, or `not-established`).

5. **Adjacent-Version Non-Generalization and Reference Evidence Negative Controls:**
   - Version proximity and identical crate strings do not justify transferring technical claims without source re-verification (`support/adjacent-version-non-generalization-examples/examples.json`): between Lean `v4.30.0-rc2` (`3dc1a088b6d2d8eafe25a7cd7ec7b58d731bd7cc`) and `v4.31.0` (`68218e876d2a38b1985b8590fff244a83c321783`), commit `41ecccec6d1244c5f89be2fc76638f22ba37cbc6` (PR `#13683`) moved compiled Lake configuration artifacts (`lakefile.olean`, `.ilean`, `.c`, `libLake_config`, `.o.export`) from package-local `cfg.lakeDir / "config" / <assigned package name>` to workspace-local `cfg.wsDir / defaultLakeDir / "config" / toString cfg.pkgIdx` while retaining `IO.FS.createDirAll cfg.lakeDir`; and across two commits of `AeneasVerif/charon` only 15 commits apart (`a535e914f74db4fd9e6be7048f4233270d8945c0` vs. `0c91ca1a8e002d6bfa8d8f1f452804fce2f92cf1`), both report `Cargo.toml` version `0.1.210` while pinning different Rust nightly toolchains (`nightly` vs. `nightly`).

## Findings

### 1. Nix and Guix Functional Deployment: Immutable Realizations, Mutable Roots, and Deployment Boundaries

#### 1.1 Origin of the Pattern and Separation of Realization from Activation
The 2004 Nix paper (Dolstra, de Jonge, and Visser, LISA 2004) and 2007 NixOS paper (Dolstra and Hemel, HotOS XI 2007) start from the failure modes of ordinary in-place mutation: overwriting `/usr` or shared package directories makes upgrades non-atomic, prevents incompatible versions from coexisting, leaves undeclared dependencies implicit, and destroys prior state needed for rollback. Nix replaces in-place mutation with isolated immutable store objects under `/nix/store/<hash>-<name>`, where construction of a candidate realization never mutates the currently active realization.

Guix explicitly inherited this low-level store and deployment model in 2013 (Courtès, ELS 2013, `arXiv:1305.4584v1`) while replacing the Nix expression language with embedded Scheme (EDSL) and G-expressions (`gexp`) across two-tier staging, maintaining transactional upgrades, rollbacks, per-user profiles, and garbage collection through the current Guix `1.5.0` stable release (`2026-01-23`, tag `v1.5.0` peeled to `230aa373f315f247852ee07dff34146e9b480aec`; see also Courtès & Wurmus, Euro-Par 2015).

#### 1.2 Store Objects, Derivation Addressing Modes, Profiles, and Liveness Roots
Current Nix (`NixOS/nix@9bc9ab32a57504f068847af4f8013417476cb1ec` and `2.35.2` peeled commit `2c73b59da29606068c0c98db015dd3a66955525d`) separates store immutability, output addressing, current selection, and garbage collection (`support/evidence-map.json`, `support/nix-guix-immutable-closures-mutable-roots-2004-2026/evidence-map.json`):
- **Derivation and Output Addressing (`doc/manual/source/store/derivation/outputs/{input-address,content-address}.md`):** While a store derivation specification (`.drv`) is itself content-addressed, derivation outputs are **input-addressed** by default—their store path hash derives from the derivation specification and inputs, not from the realized output bytes. Content-addressed outputs exist only for **fixed-output** derivations (where an expected content hash is declared up front, such as network downloads) or experimental **floating content-addressed** derivations (resolved via `doc/manual/source/store/resolution.md`). Treating `/nix/store` as universally content-addressed is therefore false in current Nix.
- **Profiles and Generations (`doc/manual/source/package-management/profiles.md`, blob `53cf5061f834e160e09db7a3bc2226806e662c41`):** A profile is a mutable indirection symlink (`profile -> profile-N-link -> /nix/store/...-user-environment`) that switches atomically by replacing a symlink targeting a directory. Crucially, the manual warns that the profile symlink must reside on the same filesystem or that its target must be canonicalized when registering a GC root so the collector roots the generation links rather than a dangling cross-filesystem pointer.
- **Garbage Collection and Process Runtime Roots (`garbage-collection.md` blob `29a3b3101490024d77bd0c6f5373d9fdb907e1a8`, `garbage-collector-roots.md` blob `925a3316239ff6ddfeca9787b6adbc2e9ef9f8d4`, `src/nix/unix/store-roots-daemon.md`):** Store objects are retained iff reachable from the transitive closure of explicit GC roots (such as `/nix/var/nix/gcroots` or Guix's `/var/guix/gcroots` and profile generation links) plus discovered runtime roots (running processes and open files scanned via `/proc` or `store-roots-daemon`, preserving the guarantee introduced in Nix 0.10 that running programs are not collected mid-execution). Rollback is available only while old generations remain rooted; deleting old generations precedes effective space reclamation and makes rollback to them impossible.
- **Pinned Source Review (`2.35.2` vs. `9bc9ab32a57504f068847af4f8013417476cb1ec`):** Across the frozen reference cohort (`support/matrix.json`, `support/check_matrix.py`, `support/frozen-cohort.csv`), `NixOS/nix@9bc9ab32a57504f068847af4f8013417476cb1ec` (an installer BusyBox copy change) and the peeled `2.35.2` release commit `2c73b59da29606068c0c98db015dd3a66955525d` (a maintenance backport merge) preserve identical profile symlink and garbage-collection documentation semantics.

#### 1.3 Why Atomic Pointer Replacement Is Not Atomic Live-System Migration
Both historical and current NixOS draw an explicit boundary between immutable static closures and mutable runtime state:
- Dolstra and Hemel (HotOS XI 2007) place mutable state such as `/var` outside the purely functional model and identify `/etc/passwd` as a mixed static/mutable case where activation depends on prior file contents.
- In current NixOS (`NixOS/nixpkgs@6cb205c37b7c0c39b643163d1b7721b5f9bc0913`, `nixos/doc/manual/development/what-happens-during-a-system-switch.chapter.md` blob `2aedb08e2a9746675dfbdc5e91b5e2952cec1629` and `nixos/modules/system/activation/activation-script.nix` blob `338fc1911c01e698eb90eaacad8c5fd0385005b1`), `switch-to-configuration` inspects live host mounts and `systemd` state, executes an ordered imperative reconciliation sequence (`stop` → `activate` → `reload` → `restart` → `start`) whose activation snippets are expected to be idempotent host mutations, and updates `/run/current-system` plus its GC root only as part of that stateful transition (while user services are not automatically restarted).

#### 1.4 Five-Way Identity Decomposition and Small Generation Manager for Anneal
Under Anneal's fail-closed design principles (`google/zerocopy@cc135f46155b72e4b51188525c2974a3b84acf92`, `anneal/PRINCIPLES.md` blob `d5339a95254eae14ac201139d07d9d36d48a19fb` and `anneal/DESIGN.md` blob `0e8170979f3a466f2460c0ef7ec6d9a6b92650b8`), existing Anneal reference evidence (`google/zerocopy` `reference@8fb35253fbc703cb2cd0997f4efcda76cf9062b6`) already shows why distinct stages cannot share a single identity: fixed-output hashes authenticate upstream acquisitions (`reports/anneal-nix-fixed-output-downloads-main-41f5b37/REPORT.md` blob `5f5142381dca9d2c38dbcde41c6d0f77d15b1f7a`), copied Rust/Lean trees retain NAR hashes when relocated outside `/nix/store` on `aarch64-darwin` while `/nix/store` remains mounted (`reports/anneal-nix-toolchain-tree-relocation-aarch64-darwin/REPORT.md` blob `262a141a145a2532745aaad71289896b5b3af1b2`), the final omnibus archive is not established as byte-canonical (`reports/anneal-omnibus-archive-byte-reproducibility-main-41f5b37/REPORT.md` blob `3bf9a589b81b0fdba5648b78a0b7a3dba842ecf9`), and read-only permission bits act as an accidental-write guard rather than continuous tamper validation (`reports/anneal-archive-read-only-behavior-main-41f5b37/REPORT.md`).

| Identity Layer | What It Names | What It Does *Not* Prove |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Recipe / Configuration Identity** | Build/preparation specification, flags, and pinned source revisions (`input-addressed` provenance) | That realized output bytes are canonical across hosts or time |
| **2. Admitted Upstream-Input Identity** | Fixed-output or content hash of fetched upstream tarballs/trees | Publisher authenticity, semantic correctness, or final archive identity |
| **3. Realized Prepared-Universe Identity** | Manifest of realized binaries, `.olean` closure, Charon/Aeneas pins, and claim-relative semantic inputs | That ambient host state (`PATH`, dylibs, env vars, plugins) cannot escape unless fenced |
| **4. Distribution / Archive Identity** | Compressed or uncompressed transport stream digest (if distributed) | That an extracted directory remains unmutated after unpacking without re-validation |
| **5. Active-Generation & Lease Identity** | Authoritative `CurrentGeneration` pointer plus active `Lease/Pin` set held by live proof/LSP sessions | That already-running worker processes automatically migrated to `G+1` on pointer switch |

Rather than adopting a general-purpose Nix/Guix derivation evaluator, Anneal can capture the required deployment semantics with a six-primitive generation manager: `PreparedGeneration` (immutable manifest + artifact roots), `CurrentGeneration` (atomically switched pointer), `Lease/Pin` (per-session/job retention handle), `Publish(candidate)` (fail-closed validation fence before pointer replacement), `Retire(generation)`, and `Collect()` (reclaiming only generations that are neither current nor leased).

### 2. Stopped-Container Docker Archive Ownership and Bounded Nix Mutable-State Preparation

#### 2.1 Why `docker cp --archive` Overwrote UID/GID `1000` with `0:0` While Omitting `--archive` Preserved It
On stopped container `5444ba00b8675ccd8ad2d5a55c01b6b88785e06642e6e0e5ac7f459e31a46fd5` (`Config.User=0:0`, `CapDrop=[ALL]`, `no-new-privileges`, `overlayfs`), an initial smoke probe streamed a stdin tar fixture containing UID/GID `1000` entries via `docker cp --archive - CID:/work`. Both the copy command and raw tar readback exited `0`, yet every extracted fixture member was owned by `0:0` (`support/nix-archive-ownership-smoke-20261015/put-1000.json`).

Source inspection of `docker/cli@9d7ad9ff180b43ae5577d048a7bac1159ce7bacf` (`support/cli-revision-resolution.json`) and `moby/moby@daa0cb7f23594cdbcce5002e370027d3fd36ffd7` (`support/docker-source-identities.json`) explains this behavior:
- The CLI forwards `--archive` (`-a`) as query parameter `CopyUIDGID`.
- In the Engine extraction path, `CopyUIDGID=true` selects `tarCopyOptions`, which inspects the container's configured user (`Config.User=0:0`), resolves UID/GID `0:0`, and populates `ChownOpts` to force all extracted tar entries to `0:0`.
- When `--archive` is omitted (`CopyUIDGID=false`), the Engine uses `defaultTarCopyOptions` with the container's ID map and no forced `ChownOpts`, preserving the tar stream's numeric `uid`/`gid` headers.

In the corrected trial without `--archive` on the 4-member `/work/anneal-ownership-probe-20261015` fixture (`support/nix-archive-ownership-corrected-fixture-20261015/RESULTS.json`), raw readback confirmed UID/GID `1000` across all members, directory mode `0700`, regular/hardlink mode `0600`, symlink mode `0777`, unchanged 7-byte payload, relative symlink target, and in-archive hardlink preservation. A subsequent directory-header-only tar probe (`support/nix-directory-header-fixture-20261015/RESULTS.json`) transitioned the existing directory's owner `1000 → 1001 → 1000` while preserving its child entries, contents, modes, and link topology.

#### 2.2 Capability-Dropped (`CapDrop=[ALL]`) Metadata Observation Failure and Narrowed Census
When the metadata observer briefly started the container's pinned `sleep infinity` command and ran `du --bytes --summarize` as UID `0` across `/nix/var/nix`, `/nix/var/log/nix`, `/homeless-shelter`, and `/work`, the command failed with status `1` (`Permission denied` on `/work/anneal-ownership-probe-20261015`; `support/nix-preparation-metadata-20261015/preparation-tree-sizes.json`, `support/nix-preparation-metadata-20261015/preparation-tree-sizes.stderr`). #### 3.2 Binary Container Structure, Codec Tags, and Unpacking Semantics (`v0.1.16` vs. `v0.1.19`)
Inspection of `digama0/leangz` at `v0.1.16` (`src/ltar.rs` blob `6b6e05f0a3a81bee69555a8a1ad74a1e06a6f381`, `src/tar.rs` blob `866550bff26363763e1a670d1c6410d8868d5e16`) and `v0.1.19` (`src/ltar.rs` blob `fb220b788c9c9ca00440d7cea7a779355f318377`, `src/lib.rs` blob `7cebb1db5eb95ad95c38acf93eed11e1ac55bca7`, `src/tar.rs` blob `4e806a9b0fc491117666b39ca7776164fa217036`) establishes the `.ltar` specification (`support/leantar-archive-format-v0-1-16-v0-1-19/format-map.json`):
- **Header Magic and Trace Version:** 4-byte ASCII magic `LTAR` (`V1`, decimal integer `.trace`), `LTR2` (`V2`, JSON `{"depHash": ...}`), or `LTR3` (`V3`, structured Lake JSON trace with `schemaVersion == "2025-09-10"` and 1-byte base-directory index per entry), followed immediately by an 8-byte little-endian `u64` `depHash` read from the build trace file (`0` in `nobuild` mode). This header `depHash` is **build-input freshness metadata**, not a cryptographic hash over the `.ltar` payload bytes.
- **Section Framing and Six Entry Kinds:** Length-prefixed records (`u64` LE byte length; bit 63 `1 << 63` marks comment records emitted by `-c` and checked via substring match by `-k`). Non-comment entries carry a path string (plus a 1-byte base-directory index in `LTR3`) and a 1-byte kind tag:
  - `0`: Generic file compressed with single-frame `zstd` (level 19).
  - `1`: Single `.olean` file compressed with custom `lgz` (`OLeanGz`: 6-byte `LGZ\x00\x01\x00` header, `u64` base address, pointer/object table transformation, and 32 KiB raw zstd dictionary compression over non-pointer data).
  - `2`: `V1` trace file stored as an 8-byte LE `u64` and rendered on extraction as decimal ASCII text.
  - `3`: `V2` trace file stored as an 8-byte LE `u64` and rendered as JSON `{"depHash":"..."}`.
  - `4`: `V3` compact binary module trace (`inputs`, `depHash`, `outputs`, `log`, `synthetic`) reconstructed on extraction with `schemaVersion: "2025-09-10"`.
  - `5`: Grouped `.olean` + `.olean.server` + `.olean.private` triple (`MultiOLean`) encoded in one shared `lgz` stream using two separator records.
- **Omitted Metadata and Extraction Safety Differences:** `.ltar` stores no Unix file modes, `uid`/`gid`, `mtime`, symlinks, hardlinks, or empty directories. Extraction resolves paths via `basedir[index].join(path)` without rejecting `..` or absolute paths, and allocates `Vec::with_capacity(len)` directly from the untrusted 8-byte record length. Between `v0.1.16` and `v0.1.19`:
  - `v0.1.16` opens destination files directly (`File::create`), records touched paths, and deletes them on error; furthermore, `v0.1.16` initializes `delete_corrupted = true` in `main` (before CLI `-D` parsing), so a truncated archive raising `ErrorKind::UnexpectedEof` triggers `std::fs::remove_file` on the input `.ltar` and returns `Ok(())` (exit `0`).
  - `v0.1.19` writes each extracted payload to a `tempfile::NamedTempFile` in the target directory and atomically persists it (`6d1691e0a44be53e5cffbe113c6618207cfbf425`, `bc36c619c90ae72572daad8498631d02bf45e488`), and fixes `serde` default-field deserialization for `BuildTrace` (`7f12e76ca0384d558f568e9df441b43a747601d8`).

### 4. Toolchain Archive Semantic Equivalence Procedure (`google/zerocopy@41f5b37afe7060fd9fe08c00b200672cd76d77b9`)

At `google/zerocopy@41f5b37afe7060fd9fe08c00b200672cd76d77b9`, `anneal/flake.nix` defines two omnibus archive outputs (`packages.omnibus-archive` at `zstd -1` and `packages.omnibus-archive-ci` at `zstd -6`, lines 437–468) over `omnibus-env` (`toolchain/rust`, `toolchain/lean`, `toolchain/aeneas/bin/{charon,charon-driver,aeneas}`, `mathlib`, and `.anneal-version`). When Exocrate (`anneal/tools/exocrate/src/main.rs` blob `a1f4318886e7b863e70e5e4616c17ef095e50f99`, lines 66–285) unpacks `--local-archive <path>`, it keys `std::env::temp_dir().join("exocrate-archives").join(namespace)` only by `.anneal-version` + platform and skips extraction whenever `.complete` already exists—meaning comparing two archives with the same `.anneal-version` on one machine via default Exocrate silently tests the first archive twice.

To prevent false equivalence and false non-equivalence, `support/toolchain-archive-semantic-equivalence-procedure-main-41f5b37/comparison-procedure.json` defines a five-layer fail-closed procedure:

1. **Layer 1 — Compressed Archive Bytes (`omnibus.tar.zst`):** Compare raw SHA-256 digests. Equality establishes `archive-byte-identical`. Inequality proceeds to Layer 2 because `zstd -1` vs. `zstd -6` produces different compressed streams over identical tar inputs.
2. **Layer 2 — Decompressed Tar Stream (`omnibus.tar`):** Decompress both streams and compare raw tar SHA-256 and raw tar header entries (`uid`/`gid`, `uname`/`gname`, `mtime`, member order, PAX headers, sparse blocks).
3. **Layer 3 — Freshly Extracted Filesystem Graph:** Extract into two fresh temporary directories (bypassing the shared `exocrate-archives` cache directory) and compare relative path sets, entry types, symlink targets, hardlink equivalence classes, permission bits (specifically verifying that `chmod -R u-w` read-only protection on `mathlib` is preserved), and file sizes.
4. **Layer 4 — Proof-Critical Artifact and Metadata Equivalence:**
   - **Source, lockfiles, and executables:** `.anneal-version`, `mathlib/lakefile.lean`, `mathlib/lake-manifest.json`, `mathlib/lean-toolchain`, `toolchain/rust/**`, `toolchain/lean/**`, and `toolchain/aeneas/bin/{charon,charon-driver,aeneas}` must be byte-identical unless an explicit platform loader/signature exception is proven.
   - **Coherent Lean module artifact families (`src/Lean/Setup.lean` blob `c5bb470a9a3d53924bb3c674776c84049fa81054`):** `.olean`, `.olean.server`, `.olean.private`, and `.ilean` files for each module must be compared as a coherent family; mixing `.olean` from one build with `.olean.server` or `.olean.private` from another is invalid.
   - **Lake `.hash` sidecars (`src/lake/Lake/Build/Trace.lean` blob `cb374b761dc9db9fc39c03923ef7c976b744019c`, `src/lake/Lake/Config/Artifact.lean` blob `92df53c5690e7e46c8e8c8116bb7f11358be6b6f`):** Lake `.hash` files store a 64-bit non-cryptographic Lake `Hash`, not SHA-256. The procedure recomputes cryptographic SHA-256 over the actual artifact file and verifies that any present `.hash` sidecar matches Lake's expected 64-bit value.
   - **Lake `.trace` and `ConfigTrace` records (`src/lake/Lake/Build/Trace.lean`, `src/lake/Lake/Load/Config.lean` blob `f1fe9b199f41e00d3cf10725b4dfc6b2d3623ec2`):** Parse JSON `.trace` files under `mathlib/.lake/build/**` and `mathlib/.lake/config/**`. In Lake's `hashUpToDate` check, `depHash` and `outputs` drive rebuild gating, whereas `inputs` (diagnostic captions) and `log` (saved diagnostic messages, which replay during `buildUnlessUpToDate`) do not change `depHash` alone—except when `cargo-anneal` (`anneal/crates/cargo-anneal/src/main.rs` blob `df2aaeb7189fc022c179dc6a1bf44033fb11f404`, lines 777–812) treats warnings or replayed error logs as fatal. Furthermore, if any consumer invokes Lake with `--old` (`mtimeUpToDate`, requiring every trace input's `mtime` to be strictly older than the output's `mtime`), relative `mtime` ordering across extracted files becomes semantically load-bearing.
5. **Layer 5 — Consumer Execution Probes:** Run toolchain version probes, offline `lake build --no-build` freshness verification on `mathlib`, offline `cargo anneal` verification over a known safe fixture (`exit 0`) and known failing fixture (`exit != 0`), and a read-only write-attempt rejection check on `mathlib`. Classify the outcome as `archive-byte-identical`, `payload-identical`, `equivalent-under-declared-consumer-contract`, or `not-established`.
