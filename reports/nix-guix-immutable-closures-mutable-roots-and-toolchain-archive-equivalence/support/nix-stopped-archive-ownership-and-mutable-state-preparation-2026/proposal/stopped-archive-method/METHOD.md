Therefore start **only the verified idle command**, then invoke exact existing trusted metadata/hash utilities explicitly; no shell profile, Nix, target code, daemon or build. Successful rollback means equality of the explicitly captured logical filesystem state, contents and supported metadata, checked by fresh census/hashes. Tar cannot restore original inode numbers or ctime, and ordinary reads/startup may alter atime/runtime metadata. If "complete rollback" requires those identities, or required ACL/xattr/sparse/link properties cannot be represented and verified with available ordinary primitives, **the archive method cannot meet it: stop before mutation**. No full-state preservation claim follows from a plain tar checksum.
