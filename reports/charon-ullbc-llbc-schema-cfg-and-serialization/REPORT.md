# Charon ULLBC and LLBC Schema, Control-Flow Reconstruction, MiniRust and Pass Seams, and Serialization Boundaries

## Summary

Across Charon `0.1.210` (`AeneasVerif/charon@a535e914f74db4fd9e6be7048f4233270d8945c0`, `0c91ca1a8e002d6bfa8d8f1f452804fce2f92cf1`, and `1e08453924610c19d74c245a7308f17c1008e2db`) and later upstream revisions (`9e95cf318e3a2964fcf5e03104cde5b718f98ea5` at `0.1.291`, `1aebab5f03a58067e5dc0ede2fec584117f54964`, and `cf02e3f228f97ee7be3a415be56989053063326d`), Charon's exported unit is a whole translated crate (`TranslatedCrate` inside `CrateData`) rather than a standalone list of function bodies. A `TranslatedCrate` carries target layout parameters, normalized source files and spans, item identity and structured name tables (including items that failed translation), type/function/global/trait declarations and trait implementations, and dependency-reordered declaration groups. Function and global initializer bodies share a single MIR-derived type, place, projection, operand, and rvalue vocabulary and are represented either as **ULLBC** ("Unstructured LLBC", an indexed vector of basic blocks with explicit statements and CFG terminators) or as **LLBC** (nested statement blocks using `Switch`, `Loop`, `Break`, `Continue`, `Call`, `Drop`, `Abort`, and `Return`).

Evaluating Charon and Aeneas (`ac9f1bc5262a5e4ff1e24ca78617121382202727` and `ca282ec2312a96f6a985a4d23a663690816d3f3e`) against direct `rustc_driver` MIR extraction requires distinguishing four architectural boundaries:

1. **Common AST and ULLBC vs. LLBC control-flow reconstruction:** ULLBC and LLBC share typed places (`Local`, `Global`, and projections for dereference, ADT fields, pointer metadata, indexing, and subslicing), operands (`Copy`, `Move`, and constants), rvalues (`Use`, `Ref` with `BorrowKind` and pointer metadata, `RawPtr`, unary/binary/nullary operations, `Discriminant`, `Aggregate`, `Len`, `Repeat`), regions (`Var`, `Static`, `Body`, `Erased`), and body availability markers (`Unstructured`, `Structured`, `TargetDispatch`, `Extern`, `Intrinsic`, `Opaque`, `Missing`, `Error`). Before and after the `ullbc_to_llbc` pass, Charon runs a normalization and resugaring schedule (`52` scheduled semantic pass invocations in `transform/mod.rs` at `cf02e3f228f97ee7be3a415be56989053063326d`) that inserts pointer metadata and missing `StorageLive`, normalizes drops, resolves trait method references, inlines promoted/selected generated functions, folds overflow/division/bounds checks into operation semantics (`reconstruct_fallible_operations`), reconstructs assertions and `vec!` allocations, merges goto chains, duplicates return blocks, and reconstructs enum matches. At `0.1.210` (`1e08453924610c19d74c245a7308f17c1008e2db`), default `ullbc_to_llbc` (`ullbc_to_llbc.rs:731–737`) strips all basic blocks leading to `UnwindResume` (`!is_unwrap_block`), eliminating unwind cleanup blocks (`3 -> 0` in `partial_init_move_cleanup` and `1 -> 0` in `partial_field_reinit_cleanup`), whereas later upstream Charon (`9e95cf318e3a2964fcf5e03104cde5b718f98ea5`) builds the CFG with unwind edges, preserves cleanup blocks across `Call`/`Drop`/`Assert`/`Panic`/`InlineAsm`, rejects irreducible control flow, and supports either code duplication or `ForwardBreak` synthetic one-shot loops (`--no-code-duplication`).
2. **Strongest-flags ULLBC (`0.1.210`) vs. Direct `rustc_driver` MIR and Lean certificate seams:** Invoking Charon `0.1.210` with `--ullbc --precise-drops --extract-opaque-bodies --mir elaborated --rustc-arg=-Zmir-preserve-ub --rustc-arg=-Zalways-encode-mir=yes` skips `ullbc_to_llbc` and preserves `Runtime(PostCleanup)` CFG blocks, compiler-synthesized drop flags (`_20.._23: bool`), field-precise drops (`DropKind::Precise`), unwind cleanup paths, `StorageLive`/`StorageDead`, and `PlaceMention`. On a 3-crate operational benchmark (`stateful_generic_lib`, `client_contract_user`, `client_direct_mir_inspector`), strongest-flags Charon `0.1.210` ULLBC lowers via `support/charon-0-1-210-ullbc-vs-llbc-and-direct-mir-comparative-evaluation/extract_and_link_charon_h3.py` into single-artifact `.amir` v3 closures that satisfy the exact same universal operational theorems (`∀ base : Nat`) in Lean `4.34.1` as direct `rustc_driver` extraction once three adapter obligations are discharged: a `TraitDropAdapter` mapping `core::marker::Destruct` to `<Guard as Drop>::drop`, complete 10-body local-type table validation (`verifyCharonNativeInspectorClosureCompleteC2`), and an independent `rustc` monomorphization completion check (`collect_and_partition_mono_items`). Separately, on the legacy alpha24 bundle (`charon 0.1.210` SHA-256 `51bb6d23...`), `support/charon-fork-storage-certificate-alpha24/ForkModel.lean` and `CapturedCertificate.lean` provide a kernel-checked Lean `4.31.0` certificate relating the captured 4-block ULLBC body of `certificate_probe::choose` to its structured LLBC body and proving removal of reconstruction-boundary `nop` statements across all initial states and fuel budgets `fuel + 3`.
3. **MiniRust operational boundary (`9e95cf318e3a2964fcf5e03104cde5b718f98ea5`):** Charon's `docs/minirust.md` designates MiniRust translation (`charon/src/minirust.rs`) as authoritative for disambiguating statement semantics on its supported route, using `TreeBorrowsMemory<T>`. However, `CliOpts::apply_preset` forces a distinct extraction configuration for MiniRust (`--ullbc`, full monomorphization, at least elaborated MIR, precise desugared drops, byte constants, and `--extract-opaque-bodies`) that rejects multi-target translation, undesugared drops, inline assembly, and reconstructed assertions/panics, and documents known unsound or failing cases (nonzero-offset pointers to statics, missing `track_caller` locations, union padding in `ub/enum_mark_used_bytes.rs`, `panic=abort`, closure marker traits, `char` validity transmutation such as `0xD800 -> 55296`, and atomic Tree Borrows frozen-reference writes).
4. **Library, process, and wire-format boundaries (`0.1.210`):** Charon splits its in-process library (`charon_lib`, which can build on stable Rust with `--no-default-features` and exposes AST types, visitors, transforms, formatting, and deserializers) from its batch subprocess extraction binaries (`charon` and `charon-driver`, which communicate via `RUSTC_WRAPPER` and `CHARON_ARGS`, run one `rustc_driver` callback pass, serialize `CrateData`, and exit without a server/daemon protocol or incremental cache-invalidation contract). On the wire, both ULLBC and LLBC use `export::CrateData { charon_version, translated, has_errors }` in JSON or Postcard with optional stateful `HashConsDedupSerializer` interning (`HashConsId` / `Deduplicated(id)`, disabled by `--no-dedup-serialized-ast`). Both Rust (`CharonVersion::deserialize`) and generated OCaml (`charon-ml/src/CharonVersion.ml`, `OfJson.ml`, `OfPostcard.ml`) enforce **exact string equality** against `CARGO_PKG_VERSION` (`0.1.210`). While `AGENTS.md` and `scripts/ci-check-version-number.sh` couple AST/generated-decoder changes to patch-version bumps (e.g., `0.1.208 -> 0.1.209` for `WithRetag` in `65dcdb566e05472a6bca8074318c05f729e30177`, and `0.1.209 -> 0.1.210` for `ConstantExprKind::TypeId` in `89e2529761404c02ab5a4d0a61a483853e4ee9e6`), nightly release tags (`nightly-YYYY.MM.DD`) do not increment `Cargo.toml`, so distinct commits (`a535e914...` and `0c91ca1a...`, 15 commits apart) share version `0.1.210`. Moreover, convenience loaders (`charon_lib::deserialize_llbc`, `deserialize_llbc_with_format`, `OfJson.crate_of_json`, and `OfPostcard.crate_of_postcard_file`) discard `has_errors`, requiring fail-closed consumers to inspect `CrateData` directly.

## Findings

### 1. Crate-Level Schema, Common AST Vocabulary, and ULLBC vs. LLBC Representation

**Basis:** `source`, `documentation`

#### 1.1 Whole-Crate Serialization Unit and Typed Declaration IDs
In `charon/src/export.rs` and `charon/src/ast/krate.rs` (at `a535e914f74db4fd9e6be7048f4233270d8945c0`), the serialized top-level unit is `CrateData`, which wraps:
- `charon_version: CharonVersion` (the producer's `CARGO_PKG_VERSION` string);
- `translated: TranslatedCrate`;
- `has_errors: bool` (indicating that translation encountered errors and the crate description is partial).

`TranslatedCrate` contains:
- `crate_name` and the `CliOpts` (`options`) used to produce the crate;
- `target_information`, recording pointer size and endianness per target;
- `files`, the normalized source-file table indexed by `FileId` for spans;
- `item_names`, mapping every registered `ItemId` to its structured `Name` (even for items whose translation failed), plus `short_names`;
- indexed declaration vectors/maps for `type_decls` (`TypeDeclId`), `fun_decls` (`FunDeclId`), `global_decls` (`GlobalDeclId`), `trait_decls` (`TraitDeclId`), and `trait_impls` (`TraitImplId`), wrapped by the top-level `ItemId` sum type, plus associated-item IDs (`TraitAssocTyId`, `TraitMethodId`, `TraitAssocConstId`);
- `ordered_decls`, grouping declarations into topological dependency order with explicit mutually recursive declaration groups.

As detailed in [`support/charon-ullbc-llbc-schema/ARCHITECTURE.md`](support/charon-ullbc-llbc-schema/ARCHITECTURE.md), `charon-driver` acquires MIR after rustc type/borrow checking, translates reachable items through a worklist into `TranslatedCrate`, and runs the transformation schedule before serializing `CrateData`.

#### 1.2 Types, Places, Projections, Operands, and Rvalues
In `charon/src/ast/types.rs` and `charon/src/ast/expressions.rs` (`a535e914f74db4fd9e6be7048f4233270d8945c0`) and `charon/src/ast/bodies/{expressions,places}.rs` (`9e95cf318e3a2964fcf5e03104cde5b718f98ea5`):

- **Type declarations (`TypeDecl` / `TypeDeclKind`):** Carry `ItemMeta`, `GenericParams`, source context, `TypeDeclKind` (`Struct`, `Enum`, `Union`, `Opaque`, `Alias`, or `Error`), per-target `Layout` records when computable, and pointer-metadata classification. Although `rustc` normally inlines type aliases at use sites, Charon retains top-level alias declarations. Enum variants carry their semantic `discriminant: Literal` alongside fields, explicitly distinguished from physical in-memory tags.
- **Type vocabulary (`Ty` / `TyKind`):** Includes ADTs and built-in containers (`Box`, arrays, slices, tuples), type variables (`TyVar`), literal primitives (`Integer`, `Float`, `Bool`, `Char`), `Never`, `Ref(Region, Ty, RefKind)` (`Mut` or `Shared`), `RawPtr(Ty, RefKind)`, `TraitType(TraitRef, AssocTyId)`, `DynTrait`, and `FnPtr` / `FnDef` (carrying `is_unsafe: bool`, `Abi` distinguishing `Rust`, `C`, etc., inputs, and output). `GenericArgs` separates `regions`, `types`, `const_generics`, and `trait_refs`.
- **Regions and body typing (`9e95cf318e3a2964fcf5e03104cde5b718f98ea5`):** In `charon/src/ast/type_level/regions.rs`, `Region` has `Var`, `Static`, `Body`, and `Erased` variants. `GExprBody::bound_body_regions` counts existentially bound body regions introduced in place of `rustc`-erased regions. `charon/src/transform/typecheck_and_unify.rs` checks internal type consistency and unifies body lifetimes on a best-effort basis after initial translation and after transformations (unless disabled), skipping already errored items and tolerating type mismatches in copied default trait methods under contradictory where-clause premises.
- **Typed places (`Place`) and projections (`ProjectionElem`):** Every `Place` pairs a `PlaceKind` (`Local(LocalId)`, `Global(GlobalDeclRef)`, or `Projection(Place, ProjectionElem)`) with its `Ty`. Projection elements include `Deref` (for references, raw pointers, and `Box`), `Field(FieldProjKind, FieldId)` (for ADTs and tuples), `PtrMetadata` (modeling wide pointers as address + metadata), `Index`, and `Subslice` (the latter two eliminated in final LLBC by micro-passes).
- **Operands (`Operand`):** `Copy(Place)`, `Move(Place)`, and `Const(ConstantExpr)`. Only literal and variable constant-expression forms remain in final normalized LLBC; intermediate MIR constant forms are desugared into globals or expressions. Constant bytes support abstract provenance: uninitialized bytes, concrete byte values, and pointer bytes with provenance to a global/function or unknown provenance.
- **Rvalues (`Rvalue`):** `Use(Operand)` (with a `WithRetag` flag in `0.1.209+` / `9e95cf318e3a2964fcf5e03104cde5b718f98ea5`), `Ref(Place, BorrowKind, Operand)` (carrying `BorrowKind`—`Shared`, `Mut`, `TwoPhaseMut`, `Shallow`, or `UniqueImmutable`—plus pointer metadata), `RawPtr(Place, RefKind, Operand)`, `UnaryOp`, `BinaryOp`, `NullaryOp`, `Discriminant(Place)`, `Aggregate(AggregateKind, Vec<Operand>)`, `Len`, and `Repeat`.

#### 1.3 Production ULLBC vs. LLBC Body Structure (`a535e914...` and `9e95cf31...`)
Inspecting `ullbc_ast.rs` and `llbc_ast.rs` at `a535e914f74db4fd9e6be7048f4233270d8945c0` alongside `charon/src/ast/bodies.rs`, `bodies/unstructured.rs`, and `bodies/structured.rs` at `9e95cf318e3a2964fcf5e03104cde5b718f98ea5` (`support/charon-llbc-cfg-minirust-9e95cf3/source-index.json` and `support/charon-llbc-cfg-minirust-9e95cf3/coverage.json`) establishes the exact structural split:

| Layer | Representation and Semantic Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| `Body` enum (`bodies.rs`) | `Unstructured(ExprBody)`, `Structured(ExprBody)`, `TargetDispatch(BTreeMap<TargetTriple, FunDeclRef>)`, `Extern(String)`, `Intrinsic(String, Vec<String>)`, `Opaque`, `Missing`, or `Error(Error)`. Only `Unstructured` and `Structured` satisfy `Body::has_contents()`. |
| Shared body data (`GExprBody`) | Span, local declarations (`locals`: index `0` is return local, followed by `arg_count` arguments, then temporaries), existential region count (`bound_body_regions`), and line-numbered source comments. |
| ULLBC control (`unstructured.rs` / `ullbc_ast.rs`) | Indexed vector of `BlockData` (`BlockId = 0` is entry); each block has a statement vector, exactly one `Terminator`, and (at `9e95cf31...`) block kind (`Regular`, `Cleanup`, `Terminate`). |
| ULLBC ordinary statements | `Assign(Place, Rvalue)`, `SetDiscriminant(Place, VariantId)`, `CopyNonOverlapping` (or intrinsic equivalent), `StorageLive(LocalId)`, `StorageDead(LocalId)`, `PlaceMention(Place)`, `Borrowck` (`FakeRead`, variance/ascription, outlives, trait obligations), `Assert { cond, expected, on_failure: AbortKind }`, `Nop`. |
| ULLBC terminators | `Goto { target }`, `Switch { discr, targets }`, `Call { call, target, on_unwind }`, `Drop { kind: DropKind, place, tref, target, on_unwind }`, `InlineAsm`, `Assert`, `Panic` / `Abort(AbortKind)`, `UnwindTerminate`, `UnwindResume`, `Return`, `UndefinedBehavior`. |
| LLBC control (`structured.rs` / `llbc_ast.rs`) | Nested `Block { span, statements }` trees with body-unique `StatementId` handles; replaces CFG edges with `Loop(Block)`, `Switch(Switch)` (`If(Operand, Block, Block)`, `SwitchInt(Operand, IntTy, Vec<(Vec<Literal>, Block)>, Block)`, `Match(Place, Vec<(Vec<VariantId>, Block)>, Option<Block>)`), `Break(usize)`, `Continue(usize)`, and nested cleanup blocks. |
| LLBC statement effects | Collapses the statement/terminator split: includes ordinary statements plus `Call`, `Drop`, `Assert`, `InlineAsm`, `Panic`, and terminal `Return`, `UnwindResume`, `UnwindTerminate`, `UndefinedBehavior`, and `Error`. |

Key semantic invariants of the ULLBC/LLBC body representation at `9e95cf318e3a2964fcf5e03104cde5b718f98ea5`:
- **Storage allocation:** `StorageLive` allocates or reallocates a local; `StorageDead` deallocates it (with an already-dead local treated as a no-op). Non-return locals can be implicitly deallocated at function exit unless `--deallocate-all-locals` requests explicit markers.
- **`PlaceMention` and `Borrowck`:** `PlaceMention` checks place validity without reading its value (invalid projections can trigger UB); MIR translation elides `PlaceMention` on a bare local where no projection UB arises, but retains projected places. `Borrowck` statements are runtime no-ops; coverage counters, const-eval counters, and backward-incompatible drop lint hints are discarded, while `FalseEdge` and `FalseUnwind` select their real runtime target.
- **`AbortKind` and unwinding:** `AbortKind` distinguishes recoverable `Panic`, `UndefinedBehavior` (immediate UB to reach), and `UnwindTerminate` (ABI/double-panic abort). In `translate_unwind_action`, MIR `Continue` maps to a fresh `UnwindResume` block, `Unreachable` to an `UndefinedBehavior` block, `Terminate` to `UnwindTerminate`, and `Cleanup(b)` to its translated cleanup block. `UnwindKind::further_unwind_kind` routes regular blocks to cleanup and cleanup/terminate blocks to terminate.
- **`DropKind::Precise` vs. `DropKind::Conditional`:** `DropKind::Precise` denotes calling `Destruct::drop_glue(&mut place)` and marking the place moved out. `DropKind::Conditional` represents unelaborated drops (which may elaborate to partial, conditional, or async destruction and allow unaligned packed fields at that stage).
- **Dynamic function calls:** `FnOperand::Dynamic` supports function-pointer calls in both translator and exporter arms, overriding a stale historical comment in the ULLBC AST module.

### 2. Control-Flow Reconstruction (`ullbc_to_llbc`), Pre/Post-Reconstruction Passes, and Positional Certificates

**Basis:** `source`, `documentation`, `derived`

#### 2.1 Pre- and Post-Reconstruction Transformation Pipeline (`transform/mod.rs`)
In `charon/src/transform/mod.rs` (inspected at `a535e914f74db4fd9e6be7048f4233270d8945c0` and `cf02e3f228f97ee7be3a415be56989053063326d`), `run_transformation_passes` executes a strictly ordered normalization pipeline before `ullbc_to_llbc`:
1. **Pre-CFG body normalizations:** Pointer-metadata insertion (`insert_ptr_metadata`), explicit unit-return `_0 = ()` assignment insertion (`insert_assign_return_unit`), missing `StorageLive` insertion (`insert_storage_lives`), drop normalization/desugaring (`desugar_drops`, gated by `--desugar-drops`), trait-method reference resolution, dynamic-trait-call transformation, inlining of promoted/inline constants and selected compiler-generated functions (`inline_selected_functions`), removal of trivial drops (`remove_drop_never`), moving dynamic checks into statements, goto-chain merging (`merge_goto_chains`), `reconstruct_fallible_operations` (removing `rustc`-generated overflow, division-by-zero, and bounds-check assert edges because those checks are folded into the semantics of the reconstructed arithmetic/array operations), `vec!` lowering reconstruction (`reconstruct_boxes`), intrinsic recognition, assertion reconstruction (`reconstruct_asserts`), constant simplification, local/index cleanup, return-block duplication (`duplicate_return`), unused-local removal, and unreachable-block filtering.
2. **Control-flow reconstruction (`ullbc_to_llbc`):** Skipped when `--ullbc` is passed; otherwise converts the basic-block CFG into nested `llbc_ast::Block` trees.
3. **Post-reconstruction LLBC passes:** Enum match reconstruction (`reconstruct_matches`), structured CFG prettification (`remove_useless_jump_blocks`), conversion of selected operators/array constructions to function calls (`ops_to_function_calls`), and array/slice indexing to function calls (`index_to_function_calls`).
4. **Shared final passes:** Removal of unused locals and `Nop`s, heuristic body-comment recovery (`recover_body_comments`), allocator-parameter hiding (`hide_allocator`), partial or full monomorphization (`monomorphize`), and topological declaration reordering (`reorder_decls`).

#### 2.2 `ullbc_to_llbc` Algorithm, Unwind Edges, and `ForwardBreak` Mode
In `charon/src/transform/control_flow/ullbc_to_llbc.rs` (at `9e95cf318e3a2964fcf5e03104cde5b718f98ea5`):
- **Dominators, loop headers, and irreducibility:** `CfgInfo::build` constructs both the full CFG from `targets()` (including unwind successors) and the normal-flow graph without unwind edges. Dominators and reverse postorder (RPO) are computed on the full graph. Any edge whose source RPO index is $\ge$ its target RPO index is classified as a back-edge; its target becomes a loop header and **must dominate** the edge source. If dominance fails, `CfgInfo::build` returns `Irreducible`, and `translate_body` registers a nonreducible-control-flow error and panics (caught by surrounding pass recovery).
- **Heuristic exit selection:** `CfgInfo` computes loop membership, merge targets, reachability, shortest paths, and flow weights. Cleanup-only block status is computed using the graph without unwind edges so cleanup paths do not dilute normal-flow exit selection, while unwind-only subgraphs use their own successors. Loop and switch exits are ranked heuristically by reachability, flow weight, and RPO order.
- **Lexical destination stack and `ForwardBreak` (`--no-code-duplication`):** `ReconstructCtx::translate_block_inner` maintains a stack of special destinations. Loop headers push a `Continue` target and an optional `Break` exit; switch exits push `NextBlock` continuations. `translate_jump` resolves jumps from innermost to outermost context as depth-indexed `Continue(depth)`, `Break(depth)`, fallthrough `Nop`, or recursive block translation. Under default code duplication, shared forward continuations may be duplicated across branches. Under `--no-code-duplication` (`ForwardBreak` mode), immediately dominated merge targets are wrapped in synthetic one-shot `loop { ...; break 0 }` blocks with the merge continuation placed after the loop (and explicit `continue` appended to real source loops), followed by `remove_useless_jump_blocks`. As documented in `options.rs`, consumers expecting source-shaped loops (including Aeneas) may reject `ForwardBreak` output.
- **Prior return/cleanup cloning (`duplicate_return.rs`):** Before `ullbc_to_llbc`, `duplicate_return` clones eligible return blocks (containing only `StorageDead` before `Return`) and duplicates unwind cleanup paths (`duplicate_unwind_path`) using a path-local ancestor map to prevent infinite cycle cloning.
- **Unwind cleanup preservation across revisions:** At `0.1.210` (`1e08453924610c19d74c245a7308f17c1008e2db`), `ullbc_to_llbc.rs:731–737` stripped all `!is_unwrap_block` blocks leading to `UnwindResume`. At `9e95cf318e3a2964fcf5e03104cde5b718f98ea5` (`0.1.291`), `translate_terminator` recursively translates cleanup blocks for `Call`, `Drop`, `Assert`, `Panic`, and `InlineAsm`, as preserved in `charon/tests/ui/unwind-llbc.out`.

#### 2.3 Finite Positional Certificate Seam for `ullbc_to_llbc`
Because `ReconstructCtx` emits no correspondence witness, certifying `ullbc_to_llbc` without trusting its heuristic exit discovery requires a finite positional relation between the ULLBC immediately before reconstruction—with positions $(\text{block\_id}, \text{stmt\_idx})$—and the flattened structured LLBC graph immediately after reconstruction—with statement/block IDs plus lexical loop/continuation stacks. An independent checker can verify:
1. Initial position, ordinary statement/value equality, local/region agreement, and copy maps;
2. Observable step and branch-label agreement with the mapped source instruction and normal successor;
3. Unwind successor, panic/UB/termination, and return effect agreement (including cleanup nesting);
4. Lexical resolution of `Break(d)` / `Continue(d)` targets without running dominator/exit-ranking heuristics;
5. Finite administrative stuttering for synthetic loop wrappers, fallthrough `Nop`s, and jump removal, subject to a **well-founded rank (acyclicity) on purely administrative transitions** so target epsilon-cycles cannot introduce silent divergence without source progress.

### 3. MiniRust Operational Boundary (`9e95cf318e3a2964fcf5e03104cde5b718f98ea5`)

**Basis:** `documentation`, `source`

`docs/minirust.md` states that ambiguous Charon statement semantics should be resolved by translation to MiniRust (`charon/src/minirust.rs`, tested by `charon/tests/minirust.rs` against `crates/minirust@c0b555e5b15accc6c1366b1d911a1bfd49c3d9d0`). | --- | --- | --- |

### 6. Library vs. Process Boundaries and Wire Serialization Compatibility (`0.1.210`)

**Basis:** `source`, `documentation`, `derived`

#### 6.1 `charon_lib` In-Process API vs. `charon` / `charon-driver` Subprocess Topology
Inspecting `AeneasVerif/charon@a535e914f74db4fd9e6be7048f4233270d8945c0` (`support/charon-library-process-server-0-1-210/source-map.json` and `support/charon-ullbc-llbc-schema/ARCHITECTURE.md`):
- **`charon_lib` (`charon/Cargo.toml`, `charon/src/lib.rs`, `charon/src/ast/mod.rs`):** Exports the in-memory AST (`TranslatedCrate`, `TypeDecl`, `FunDecl`, `GlobalDecl`, `TraitDecl`, `TraitImpl`), identifiers, errors, `export::CrateData`, name matching, `CliOpts`, pretty-printing, and transformation passes. Although the default `rustc` Cargo feature is enabled for `charon-driver`, `charon/Cargo.toml` supports building `charon-lib` on stable Rust via `--no-default-features` (introduced in commit `b028e082c417eda7cf419e9661ad9fff047ac1ac` without a `Cargo.toml` patch-version bump).
- **Binary-only `rustc` extraction (`charon/src/bin/charon/main.rs`, `cli.rs`, `charon/src/bin/charon-driver/main.rs`, `driver.rs`):** `charon_lib` does **not** export `run_rustc_driver` or `CharonCallbacks`. The `charon` CLI exposes only batch subcommands (`rustc`, `cargo`, `ui_test`, `toolchain-path`, `pretty-print`, `version`). For `charon cargo`, `charon` spawns `cargo` with `RUSTC_WRAPPER=charon-driver` and `CHARON_ARGS=<serialized CliOpts>` and blocks on `spawn().wait()`. For `charon rustc`, it spawns `charon-driver` directly with `CHARON_ARGS`. Each `charon-driver` process runs one `rustc_driver` compilation, executes post-rustc transformations on `TransformCtx`, serializes `CrateData`, and exits. No server/daemon subcommand, RPC protocol, document-version table, or incremental `TranslatedCrate` invalidation contract exists at this pin.

#### 6.2 `CrateData` Wire Format, Exact Version Gate, and Hash-Cons Deduplication
In `charon/src/export.rs`, `charon/src/ast/hash_cons.rs`, `charon-ml/src/CharonVersion.ml`, `charon-ml/src/OfJson.ml`, and `charon-ml/src/OfPostcard.ml` (`a535e914f74db4fd9e6be7048f4233270d8945c0` and `0c91ca1a8e002d6bfa8d8f1f452804fce2f92cf1`):
- **Exact package-version equality gate:** `charon_lib::VERSION` is `env!("CARGO_PKG_VERSION")` (`"0.1.210"`). `CharonVersion::deserialize` compares the file's `charon_version` string against `crate::VERSION` by exact equality and fails with `"Incompatible version of charon"` on any mismatch. In OCaml, `Makefile` generates `charon-ml/src/CharonVersion.ml` (`supported_charon_version = "0.1.210"`) from `charon/Cargo.toml`, and both `OfJson.crate_of_json` and `OfPostcard.crate_of_postcard_file` enforce exact equality before decoding `translated`.
- **Versioning policy vs. nightly release tags:** `AGENTS.md` requires a patch-version bump in `charon/Cargo.toml` for every AST change, and `scripts/ci-check-version-number.sh` fails CI if `charon-ml/src/generated/Generated_*OfJson.ml` changes without updating `CharonVersion.ml`. Preceding commits `65dcdb566e05472a6bca8074318c05f729e30177` (`0.1.208 -> 0.1.209`, adding `WithRetag` to `Rvalue::Use`) and `89e2529761404c02ab5a4d0a61a483853e4ee9e6` (`0.1.209 -> 0.1.210`, adding `ConstantExprKind::TypeId` and shifting subsequent Postcard enum tags) demonstrate this discipline. Conversely, `.github/workflows/release.yml` tags `nightly-YYYY.MM.DD` releases without editing `Cargo.toml`: `a535e914f74db4fd9e6be7048f4233270d8945c0` and `0c91ca1a8e002d6bfa8d8f1f452804fce2f92cf1` are 15 commits apart yet both carry version `0.1.210`.
- **JSON vs. Postcard and `HashConsDedupSerializer`:** `CrateData::serialize_to_file` and `deserialize_from_file` support both JSON (named fields, recursion limit disabled, stack dynamically grown) and Postcard (positional binary encoding, trailing bytes rejected after one `CrateData`). By default, `CrateData::serialize` uses `HashConsDedupSerializer` (`charon/src/ast/hash_cons.rs`), emitting a full tagged value with a process-interning-order `HashConsId` on first traversal and `Deduplicated(id)` on subsequent occurrences; `--no-dedup-serialized-ast` emits untagged inline values. Both Rust and generated OCaml decoders (`Generated_OfJson.ml`, `Generated_OfPostcard.ml`) maintain reconstruction maps and accept either deduplicated or inline representations. Because `HashConsId` values depend on process-global interning and traversal order, they are wire-local reconstruction aids rather than stable semantic keys.
- **Silent discarding of `has_errors` in convenience loaders:** While `CrateData::deserialize_from_file` preserves `has_errors: bool`, Rust's `charon_lib::deserialize_llbc` and `deserialize_llbc_with_format` return only `CrateData.translated` and discard `has_errors`. Similarly, `OfJson.crate_of_json` matches a 2-field `{charon_version, translated}` or 3-field `{charon_version, translated, _}` JSON object and ignores the third field (`has_errors`), and `OfPostcard.crate_of_postcard_file` reads `_has_errors` into a discarded variable. Fail-closed consumers must therefore inspect `CrateData.has_errors` directly rather than relying on convenience loaders.
