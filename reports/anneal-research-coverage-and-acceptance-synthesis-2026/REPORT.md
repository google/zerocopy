## Findings

### F1. Three independent acceptance layers constrain every design

Basis: source and execution in the corpus; derived factorization.

1. Logical validity: the consumer accepts a proof of its independently selected proposition under an explicit axiom/import/computation policy.
2. Semantic adequacy and completeness: that proposition covers the actual configured Rust subject, permitted entries, all relevant operations/calls, and required outcomes—including the first bad source prefix.
3. Artifact and operational binding: the checked result applies to the actual consumed code/model/definitions/dependencies/target, with complete checking and a consumer-controlled publication path.

None implies either of the others. A correct kernel can prove a weakened or producer-selected statement. A correct target compiler can erase an unsafe source behavior. A valid serialized claim can be overwritten by another same-user process. These are demonstrated by the corpus's [acceptance factorization](../anneal-acceptance-frontier-and-trust-reduction-2026-10-14/REPORT.md), [semantic operation and substitution controls](../configured-capture-and-independent-obligation-completeness/REPORT.md) and [real process failure/tampering](../concurrent-acceptance-publication-controls/REPORT.md).

The 24 original O obligations remain the requirements inventory, not 24 discharged implementation theorems. Resource permissions must interpret actual allocation, initialization, aliasing and lifetime authority; a label or address alone is insufficient. Contracts must cover lawful callers and imply the advertised independent goal. Generic/dynamic dispatch and implicit cleanup need semantic evidence for their selected implementations. Allowed failure, UB, divergence, environmental behavior and relational traces require different preservation relations.

### F2. Existing formal methods supply reusable parts of the theorem

Basis: the source/theorem comparisons and primary bibliography.

Iris/RustBelt/RustHornBelt/RefinedRust and VerusBelt provide resource, lifetime, prophecy and semantic-typing mechanisms at explicit modeled-language endpoints. Their model adequacy is valuable independently of whether the current Rust frontend/VC generator is verified. Lean-Iris offers a native Lean foundation, with a separate Rust instantiation and proof/dependency closure to construct. Creusot/Coma and Verus offer authoring/search interfaces whose solver and translation boundaries remain distinct. The inspected Verus encoding thesis contains active admissions; VerusBelt's model theorem is completed source proof and has different exclusions. Neither statement should be generalized to all current Verus execution.

Aeneas/Charon preserve existing pure proof assets and offer practical seams for per-run correspondence evidence. Actual legacy capture/pair/zerocopy-validity experiments seed this work, but their manually imported source/model relation remains visible. hax, Rust-to-C routes, AutoCorres, Cogent/Dargent, VST/Low*/Steel/Pulse and compiler synthesis suggest alternative memory/resource or extraction interfaces, rather than interchangeable Rust safety guarantees.

LCF kernels, dependent-type kernels, reflection, explicit certificate languages, independent checkers and solver certificates supply different acceptance mechanisms. Model checkers, JVM/eBPF verifiers and abstract interpreters often authorize a fragment/policy rather than a foundational arbitrary-source theorem. Proof-producing compilation, translation validation and a proved compiler can be combined: use reusable rules to check untrusted choices, keep target/source semantics distinct, and certify execution only through the actual artifact/loader boundary.

### F3. The same semantics makes a TCB comparison interpretable

Basis: actual owned source/measurements and identified proof/runtime premises; derived comparison (T119).

The finite-heap direct and data-certificate routes are the fair matched pair: both prove the same write/join/frame proposition over the same partial-heap model. They import the identical precompiled Heap module, including its model/checker/soundness library, and share the exact Lean kernel/import/resource interpretation. Both can leave search outside proof validity. The timed routes use `step_sound good (by decide)` versus a direct conjunction proof by decision; neither timing includes JSON loading or witness generation. The separately exercised data pipeline has an explicit grammar and checked soundness/reachability theorem; its actual JSON-to-term selector and caller's subject/result interpretation remain a wrapper boundary. No Rust frontend or kernel was demonstrated to cease being trusted.

| Dimension | Direct finite-heap proof | Data-certificate finite-heap proof | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accepted logical endpoint | Fixed write/join/outside-address frame proposition | Same proposition through proved check/Reach/frame lemmas | Statement and resource meaning are matched, unlike comparisons of unrelated benchmarks. |
| Search/producer authority | Tactic/script may propose proof terms | Producer may propose a finite derivation tree; unknown constructors reject | Neither producer's success flag authorizes a result. |
| Accepted implementation closure | Lean checker/import environment and selected definitions in Heap | Identical loaded Heap/import closure, applying its soundness lemma | The measured pair differs in theorem use, not loaded modules. The separate JSON grammar/quotation wrapper has its own trust accounting. |
| Semantic assumptions | Owned finite heap, exact entry/binding and Rust interpretation if advertised | Same assumptions; rule checker proves only its relation | No Rust semantic premise was removed in this specimen. |
| Privilege | Actual checks run as the user; parent-owned expectations | Same; data generator/checking wrappers are not an OS authority boundary | Separate same-user tamper experiments show why directory ownership alone is insufficient. |
| Checking cost | Five samples, 100 declarations, median about 1,678 ms | Same workload, median about 1,401 ms | Wide overlapping ranges preclude a speed ranking. Original 4.31 observations remain conditional; separate 4.34 replay establishes fixture behavior, not replacement timing. |
| Extension/update burden | New theorem/proof and source/model mapping must be reviewed | New grammar/rule requires interpretation/soundness and binding changes; proof search can change independently | Versioned interfaces limit coupling, but this initiative has not measured production extension frequency. |

Other actual measurements provide complementary rather than matched rankings: safe-byte three-route checking; native Lean-to-C checker timing; proved restricted-Wasm compilation/admission with V8 execution; shared packet-model target receipts; and cvc5/Carcara generation/check/elaboration/recheck on an array-frame goal. Their reports name different overheads and logical/runtime premises. Moving kernel computation offline can add a native runtime, compiler, engine and IO dependency. It is not automatically a smaller whole-system TCB.

The inspected checker update is a concrete maintenance observation: Lean 4.31/4.30rc2 omit a validation path fixed in 4.32.2; 4.34 contains the inspected fix. The pure storage library also has an observed proof-elaboration difference between 4.31 and 4.34. These are one soundness repair and one library migration observation, not an annual extension-rate estimate. Production rates and matched Lean-Iris/RefinedRust authoring/repair costs remain unknown until a chosen pilot runs. The comparison answers which dependencies are retained and which measurements cannot justify an architecture ranking; it does not invent a numerical trust-reduction claim.

### F4. Closed experiments distinguish semantic mismatches from missing implementation

Basis: original checked and compiler/native experiments, detailed in linked report Findings.

The experiment corpus includes complete u8 Boolean domains, byte/write/array laws, safe API guards, actual capture/pass specimens, inlining/failure preservation, source-domain narrowing, modular callee/contract/target/instance substitutions, experimental annotation-owner/alias binding controls, finite ownership/frame derivations, lifetime/pointer leases, native packed/SIMD/union/GAT selections, unwind/drop/growth, two-target packet/layout observations, SMT elaboration and real process failure/concurrency/tamper controls.

The original positive and negative subjects, final statuses, assumptions and material tool identities are preserved. Expected rejection is evidence about the selected policy; an initial tactic/compiler or loader failure is not an unsupported semantic result. A conditional theorem or a source Qed without fresh dependency replay is identified as such. The known Lean kernel defect qualifies historical checks without silently invalidating every owned abstract statement or claiming universal soundness of the newer version.

The matched R095 case checks all four guarded laws in direct/reflected Lean. The selected cvc5/Carcara path accepts the complete byte-view admission law and a write-frame projection, while rejecting the other three whole-law exports for holes or named rule incompatibilities. Five actual SAT mutants are reified into checked Lean refutations. That is a completed comparative experiment with a material negative result, not four successfully checked SMT proofs. The actual layout followup for P130 applies shared PR arithmetic theorems to native/Wasm aggregate observations; it retains compiler/physical interpretation premises.

### F5. A conditional pilot should preserve independent decisions

Basis: derived from the obligations, primary mechanisms and experiments.

First fix source/entry/property/assumption/definition selection and a justified checker configuration. Then certify one existing extraction seam while preserving the trusted import frontier. Compare the same resource task under native Lean-Iris and artifact-pinned RefinedRust, and compare direct proof, a data checker and strict solver certificates for the same independently chosen proposition. Measure authoring, generation, checking, reuse, unsupported cases, artifact/assumption closure and one meaningful dependency/compiler upgrade.

This is the adoption report's reversible stage plan, not a final architecture adoption. Keep existing specifications useful; reject unsupported transformations explicitly; let search and producer implementations evolve without redefining the accepted claim. Choose a cross-logic bridge only when its exact theorem/resource interpretation and compiled assumptions are justified. Retain a trace/outcome interface capable of supporting future noninterference/termination goals even if the first certificate claims only source UB freedom.

Basis: substantive R/T/P audits, exact original questions, and parent integration.

## Boundaries

No report count establishes production Anneal soundness, a globally minimal TCB or an optimal architecture. The corpus preserves incomplete upstream mechanizations, exact source-model/runtime premises, unknown compiled closures, unexamined tools/configurations, bounded citation searches, and deployment authority limitations. Actual named-tool feature parity, complete proof-library builds and future production workload costs require the chosen configuration; the bounded certificate-design comparison does not claim them.

Three stronger prototype outcomes remain unestablished and visibly tracked: P127's initial Rust-source-to-representation adequacy arrow; P131's actual memory-operation-to-storage/initialization interpretation; and R095's complete four-law acceptance by the selected strict SMT certificate route. Their experiments and reports were completed. The crosswalk distinguishes these investigated boundaries from an unperformed experiment, and the original issue boxes remain open for the stronger outcomes. Other limitations and unknowns remain explicit per question; they are not silently collapsed into these three prototype targets.

This synthesis does not certify licensing/redistribution of a combined release. Direct upstream declarations and dependency records are source facts, not legal clearance. Future research and engineering remain possible after the original agenda's bounded questions have reports; research completion cannot mean that every future strengthening is already implemented.
