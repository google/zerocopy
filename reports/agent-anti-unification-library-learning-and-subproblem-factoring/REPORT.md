### 1. Syntactic Anti-Unification (`lgg`), Equational Generalization, and Neuro-Symbolic Library Compression

`Basis: normative + documentation + source + derived` *(clean-room reference implementations of Plotkin `lgg`, `Stitch` branch-and-bound + Eq. 15 DP + `inverse_argument_capture`, and `babble` 2-way vs. $m$-way product-DFTA anti-unification mechanically checked via `support/verify_anti_unification_library_factoring_probes.py`; upstream OCaml/Rust/Python repositories inspected at source without direct binary execution)*

#### 1.1 Generalization Lattices and Complexity Jump from Syntactic to Equational / Higher-Order Anti-Unification

Let $\mathcal{T}(\Sigma, \mathcal{V})$ denote first-order ranked terms over signature $\Sigma$ and countable variable set $\mathcal{V}$, ordered by substitution subsumption: $g \preceq t$ ($g$ is *more general than* $t$) iff $\sigma(g) = t$ for some substitution $\sigma: \mathcal{V} \to \mathcal{T}(\Sigma, \mathcal{V})$. Quotienting by bijective variable renaming $\equiv_\alpha$ and adjoining a bottom element $\bot$ yields **Plotkin's and Reynolds's complete lattice** $(\mathcal{T}(\Sigma, \mathcal{V})/{\equiv_\alpha} \cup \{\bot\},\, \preceq)$ with top $\top = x \in \mathcal{V}$ (`Plotkin 1970`, pp. 153–163; `Reynolds 1970`, pp. 135–151; `Cerna & Kutsia 2023`, `arXiv:2302.00277v5` §2–§4):

1. **First-Order Syntactic Generalization (`FOSG`, Unitary $\mathbf{1}$):** For any finite sequence of $m \ge 2$ non-empty terms $t_1, \dots, t_m$, a unique ($\text{mod }\equiv_\alpha$) **least general generalization** $\text{lgg}(t_1, \dots, t_m)$ exists. Using an injective disagreement map $\phi: \mathcal{T}(\Sigma, \mathcal{V})^m \rightharpoonup \mathcal{V}$:
   $$\text{au}\big(f(s_{1,1}, \dots, s_{1,a}), \dots, f(s_{m,1}, \dots, s_{m,a})\big) = f\big(\text{au}(s_{1,1}, \dots, s_{m,1}), \dots, \text{au}(s_{1,a}, \dots, s_{m,a})\big),$$
   and $\text{au}(u_1, \dots, u_m) = \phi(u_1, \dots, u_m)$ whenever root constructors or arities differ. Two structural invariants govern AST helper extraction (`Probe 1`):
   - **Monotone tree-size contraction:** $|\text{lgg}(t_1, \dots, t_m)| \le \min_{1 \le i \le m} |t_i|$ (strict whenever a disagreement replaces non-leaf subtrees with `#j`), running in $O(\sum_{i=1}^m |t_i|)$ time under hash-consed subterm IDs ($O(|t_1|\cdot|t_2|)$ under naive structural equality).
   - **Repeat-variable relational preservation:** Whenever the *same* $m$-tuple of differing subterms $(u_1, \dots, u_m)$ recurs at multiple AST positions, $\phi$ emits the **same** abstraction variable, preserving intra-term equality constraints such as $\text{lgg}(f(a, g(a, b)),\, f(c, g(c, d))) = f(\#0, g(\#0, \#1))$.
2. **Collapse of Uniqueness Beyond Syntactic Trees (`Cerna & Kutsia 2023` §2–§4, Tables 3 & 6):**
   - **Equational (`FOEG`, Table 3):** Replacing syntactic equality with congruence $=_E$ breaks unitary uniqueness. Associative ($\mathsf{A}$), commutative ($\mathsf{C}$), associative-commutative ($\mathsf{AC}$), and **single-unit-symbol** unital theories ($\mathsf{U}^1, (\mathsf{AU})^1, (\mathsf{CU})^1, (\mathsf{ACU})^1$) are **finitary ($\omega$)** (though $|\mathsf{mcsg}_E|$ can grow exponentially in $\mathsf{AC}$): as verified in `Probe 2`, even under a single commutative binary operator $\mathsf{C}(+)$, $t_1 = \text{pair}(a + b,\, a)$ and $t_2 = \text{pair}(a + b,\, b)$ admit **two $\equiv_{\mathsf{C}}$-incomparable minimal complete generalizations** (`(call:pair (Add var:a var:b) #0)` preserving the ground sum while unlinking the second slot, and `(call:pair (Add #0 #1) #0)` preserving cross-slot linkage while abstracting the summands—represented as `4` syntactic DFTA trees prior to $\equiv_{\mathsf{C}}$ quotienting). By contrast, **idempotent theories ($\mathsf{I}, \mathsf{AI}, \mathsf{CI}$) and unranked hedges with flexible variables are infinitary ($\infty$)**, while **multi-symbol unital theories ($\mathsf{U}^{>1}, (\mathsf{AU})^{>1}, (\mathsf{CU})^{>1}, (\mathsf{ACU})^{>1}$) and $(\mathsf{UI})^{>1}\dots(\mathsf{ACUI})^{>1}$ are nullary ($\mathbf{0}$)**.
   - **Higher-Order ($\lambda$-Terms Modulo $\alpha\beta\eta$, Table 6):** Unrestricted simply-typed $\lambda$-term generalization modulo $\alpha\beta\eta$ is **nullary ($\mathbf{0}$)** (`Cerna & Kutsia 2022`), while dependent-type Calculus of Constructions generalization fails to admit least generalizations (`Pfenning 1991`, `10.1109/LICS.1991.151632`). Unitarity ($\mathbf{1}$) is restored across **top-maximal shallow (`tms`) restricted fragments** (`Cerna & Kutsia 2023` Table 6): linear-time $O(|t_1| + |t_2|)$ on **higher-order patterns** (`p`, where free abstraction variables apply only to distinct bound variables; `Miller 1991`, `10.1093/logcom/1.4.497`; `Baumgartner et al. 2017`, `10.1007/s10817-016-9383-3`) and projective terms (`pr`), and $O((|t_1|+|t_2|)^3)$ on common-subterm (`cs`) and functions-as-constructors (`rfc`/`fc`) fragments.

#### 1.3 `Stitch` Utility Equations, Non-Overlapping DP vs. Greedy Failures, and `babble` Pairwise vs. $m$-Way DFTA (`Probes 1–2`)

In `Bowers et al. (POPL 2023, arXiv:2211.16605v2 §4.1–§4.4)` (`Eqs. 8, 9, 11–12, 14, 15`), let $\text{cost}^*(A)$ assign zero cost to abstraction-variable leaves $\alpha$ ($\text{cost}_{\text{abs}}^* = 0$), whereas $\text{cost}(A)$ charges $\text{cost}_{\text{abs}} = 100$ per parameter reference inside $A$'s body (`cost_prim = 100`, `cost_app = 1`). Rewriting a disjoint match set $R(A)$ yields exact rewrite-free global and local utilities (`Eqs. 11–12`):
$$U_{\mathcal{P},R}(A) = -\text{cost}(A) + \sum_{e \in R(A)} U_{\text{local}}(A, e), \qquad U_{\text{local}}(A, e) = \text{cost}^*(A) - \big(\text{cost}_{t_A} + \text{cost}_{\text{app}} \cdot \text{arity}(A)\big) + \sum_{[\alpha \mapsto e'] \in \text{args}(A, e)} (\text{usages}(\alpha) - 1)\,\text{cost}(e').$$

1. **Admissible Upper Bounds (`Eq. 14` vs. Rust Tighter Bound) and Partial-Pattern Pruning (`Probe 1`):**
   - Paper `Eq. ### 3. Rule-of-Three Amortization ($k^*$), Premature Argument Capture, and Shared-Vocabulary Aliasing

`Basis: documentation + source + derived` *(exact rational `fractions.Fraction` break-even thresholds, Monte Carlo `lgg` capture rates, and exhaustive $2^{r m}$ binary AST enumerations mechanically verified in `support/verify_anti_unification_library_factoring_probes.py`; parameter vectors below are illustrative synthetic calibrations)*

#### 3.1 Closed-Form Reuse Break-Even $k^*$ Incorporating Extraction, Coordination, Faults, and Divergence (`Probe 4`)

Consider $k \ge 1$ call sites requiring functionality of local inline cost $c_{\text{inline}} > 0$. Extending the Cross-Team Project Valuation formula in `InnerSourceCommons/InnerSourcePatterns@126f615297` (`patterns/2-structured/crossteam-project-valuation.md:38-90`, `Brooks 1975` Ch. 1 `3×` reusable-product factor) to incorporate missed-propagation faults and caller divergence, let $c_{\text{extract}} > 0$ be generalization/testing/`AutoDoc` overhead above $c_{\text{inline}}$, $c_{\text{call}} \in (0, c_{\text{inline}})$ per-caller discovery/wiring cost, $c_{\text{sync}} \ge 0$ per-external-caller review/CI/warranty cost (`Capraro & Riehle 2016`, `10.1145/2856821`; `30-day-warranty.md:1-72` PayPal 30-day / GitHub 6-week warranty; `trusted-committer.md:1-156`), $\mu_{\text{sync}} \ge 0$ expected shared-invariant edits (costing $c_{\text{edit}}$ per copy plus missed-propagation fault risk $p_{\text{miss}} p_{\text{fault}\mid\text{UIC}} c_{\text{bug}}$ on $(k-1)$ secondary copies, $p_{\text{fault}\mid\text{UIC}} = 107/203$), and $p_{\text{div}} c_{\text{div}}$ per-external-caller decoupling cost:
$$\begin{aligned}
C_{\text{inline}}(k) &= k\,c_{\text{inline}} + \mu_{\text{sync}}\big[k\,c_{\text{edit}} + (k - 1)\,p_{\text{miss}}\,p_{\text{fault}\mid\text{UIC}}\,c_{\text{bug}}\big], \\
C_{\text{shared}}(k) &= c_{\text{inline}} + c_{\text{extract}} + k\,c_{\text{call}} + (k - 1)\,c_{\text{sync}} + \mu_{\text{sync}}\,c_{\text{edit}} + (k - 1)\,p_{\text{div}}\,c_{\text{div}}.
\end{aligned}$$
Shared extraction satisfies $C_{\text{shared}}(k) \le C_{\text{inline}}(k)$ ($k \ge 2$) iff $(k - 1)g \ge c_{\text{extract}} + c_{\text{call}}$, where the **net marginal savings per additional caller** is:
$$g \triangleq (c_{\text{inline}} - c_{\text{call}}) + \mu_{\text{sync}}\big(c_{\text{edit}} + p_{\text{miss}}\,p_{\text{fault}\mid\text{UIC}}\,c_{\text{bug}}\big) - c_{\text{sync}} - p_{\text{div}}\,c_{\text{div}}.$$

- **Negative-Margin Anti-Extraction Regime ($g \le 0 \implies k^* = +\infty$):** Whenever $c_{\text{sync}} + p_{\text{div}} c_{\text{div}} \ge (c_{\text{inline}} - c_{\text{call}}) + \mu_{\text{sync}}(c_{\text{edit}} + p_{\text{miss}} p_{\text{fault}\mid\text{UIC}} c_{\text{bug}})$, shared extraction loses value at every $k$.
- **Positive-Margin Break-Even ($g > 0$):** $k^* = \max\!\big(2,\, 1 + \lceil (c_{\text{extract}} + c_{\text{call}}) / g \rceil\big)$. On `Probe 4`'s four illustrative synthetic calibrations ($c_{\text{inline}} = 1.0$, $p_{\text{fault}\mid\text{UIC}} = 107/203$): (1) **Security / Protocol Invariant** ($\mu_{\text{sync}}=2.0, c_{\text{bug}}=8.0, p_{\text{div}}=0.10$) gives **$k^* = 2$**; (2) **Standard Intra-Repo Helper** ($c_{\text{extract}}=1.10, \mu_{\text{sync}}=0.5, p_{\text{div}}=0.25$) gives **$k^* = 3$** (Don Roberts's Rule of Three in `Fowler 1999`, Ch. 2); (3) **Cross-Team InnerSource Library** ($c_{\text{extract}}=1.80, c_{\text{sync}}=0.35$) gives **$k^* = 5$**; and (4) **High-Divergence Boilerplate** ($p_{\text{div}}=0.72, \mu_{\text{sync}}=0.1$) gives **$k^* = +\infty$** (`None`).
- **Quadratic Boolean-Flag Entanglement Ceiling ($[k_{\min}^*, k_{\max}^*]$):** If diverging callers accumulate mode flags inside a single shared helper instead of forking (`service-vs-library.md:1-94`), cross-caller interaction adds $\binom{k}{2}\delta_{\text{flag}}$, making net savings $S(k) = -(c_{\text{extract}} + c_{\text{call}}) + (k - 1)g_{\text{base}} - \frac{1}{2}k(k - 1)\delta_{\text{flag}}$ concave with a bounded profitable window (`[3, 12]` in `Probe 4`'s synthetic calibration $c_{\text{extract}} + c_{\text{call}} = 1.25, g_{\text{base}} = 0.85, \delta_{\text{flag}} = 0.12$).

#### 3.2 Premature Argument Capture, Shared-Vocabulary Aliasing, and Distribution-Free Pigeonhole Rule of Three (`Probe 4`)

1. **Disjoint-Alphabet Premature Argument Capture:** Under three explicit preconditions—**(i)** mutually independent witnesses $i \in \{1, \dots, m\}$ and latent variation slots $j \in \{1, \dots, r\}$, **(ii)** **non-degenerate support** $\max_v P_j(v) < 1$ for every $j$ (if $\max_v P_j(v) = 1$, $p_{\text{wrong}}(m) \equiv 1$ for all $m$), and **(iii)** **disjoint per-slot literal alphabets**—syntactic $\text{lgg}(t_1, \dots, t_m)$ misses at least one true parameter iff all $m$ witnesses collide on at least one slot:
   $$p_{\text{wrong}}(m) = 1 - \prod_{j=1}^{r} \left(1 - \sum_{v} P_j(v)^m\right), \qquad \lim_{m \to \infty} p_{\text{wrong}}(m) = 0 \iff \forall j,\; \max_v P_j(v) < 1.$$
   On `Probe 4`'s illustrative 3-axis synthetic calibration ($P_1 = (0.5, 0.5)$, $P_2 = (0.7, 0.3)$, $P_3 = (0.6, 0.25, 0.15)$, `10,000` MC trials), $p_{\text{wrong}}(m)$ drops from **`100.0%` ($m=1$) and `88.34%` (`88.44%` MC, $m=2$) to `63.85%` (`63.99%` MC, $m=3$), `43.03%` ($m=4$), and `28.36%` ($m=5$)** (mirrored syntactically in `Probe 1`, where 2 Python snippets hardcode `var:row` at `arity = 3` before snippet 3 exposes `arity = 4`).
2. **Shared-Vocabulary Parameter Aliasing vs. Distribution-Free Pigeonhole Impossibility:** When $r$ parameters share an **untagged literal domain of size $V$** (such as booleans `{false, true}` or `{0, 1}`, $V = 2$), Plotkin's injective table $\phi$ loses true arity (`arity < r`) whenever **either** a slot is constant across $m$ witnesses ($V$ constant $m$-tuples) **or** two distinct slots draw the *same* non-constant $m$-tuple (aliasing `#j_1 == #j_2`):
   - *Uniform i.i.d. falling-factorial law:* For i.i.d. uniform draws over a size-$V$ domain at $r$ leaf parameter slots (non-uniformity only increases collision/aliasing probability), exact arity-loss probability is:
     $$p_{\text{wrong}}^{\text{shared-vocab}}(m) = 1 - \frac{\prod_{q=0}^{r-1} \max\!\big(0,\; V^m - V - q\big)}{V^{r m}}.$$
     Exhaustive enumeration of all $2^{r m}$ binary AST combinations in `Probe 4` confirms that for $r = 2, V = 2$, cross-slot aliasing raises failure from `75.00%` (constant capture alone) to **`87.50%` (`14/16`, `+12.50 pp`) at $m = 2$**, and from `43.75%` to **`53.125%` (`34/64`, `+9.38 pp`) at $m = 3$** (`76.5625%` vs. `57.8125%` at $r = 3, m = 3$).
   - *Distribution-free Pigeonhole impossibility ($r > V^m - V \implies p_{\text{wrong}}(m) = 1$):* Because only $V^m - V$ non-constant witness $m$-tuples exist over an alphabet of size $V$, **whenever $r > V^m - V$, syntactic `lgg` cannot separate all $r$ parameters under any distribution—including adversarially or oracle-chosen call sites**. In particular, for $V = 2$ at $m = 2$, only $2^2 - 2 = 2$ non-constant patterns `(0, 1)` and `(1, 0)` exist (`max_achievable_arity = 2` verified across all $2^{6} = 64$ outcome pairs in `Probe 4`), so **$r \ge 3$ binary flags fail with probability `100.0%` at $m = 2$**, making **$m = 3$ ($2^3 - 2 = 6 \ge r$) the hard information-theoretic minimum** to separate $3 \le r \le 6$ shared-domain binary parameters.

### 4. Concurrent Helper Scheduling: Symmetric Headcount/$\beta$ Economics and Two-Regime Stochastic Crossovers

Following `Parnas (CACM 15(12):1053–1058, 1972, 10.1145/361598.361623)` and `Krishnan, Eppinger, & Whitney (Manage. Sci. 43(4):437–451, 1997, 10.1287/mnsc.43.4.437)`:

1. **Symmetric Schedule Definitions (`Probe 5`):**

2. **Exact Non-Concave Stochastic Crossover Law (`Probe 5`):**
   Using $X + M_k - \max(X, M_k) \equiv \min(X, M_k)$ for $X = T_{H,\text{shared}}$ and $M_k = \max_{1 \le i \le k} T_{C,i}$, dedicated `Stub-First` beats `Blocking` ($\mathbb{E}[W_{\text{stub}}^{(\text{ded})}] < \mathbb{E}[W_{\text{block}}^{(\text{ded})}]$) **if and only if**:
   $$p_{\text{drift}} < p_{\text{drift}}^{*,\text{stoch}}(k) \triangleq \frac{\mathbb{E}\!\big[\min(T_{H,\text{shared}},\, \max_{1 \le i \le k} T_{C,i})\big] - t_{\text{stub}}}{\mathbb{E}[W_{\text{rework}} \mid \text{drift}]}, \qquad \mathbb{E}[\min(X, M_k)] = \int_0^\infty (1 - F_X(t))\big(1 - F_Y(t)^k\big)\,dt$$
   (where $\mathbb{E}[W_{\text{rework}} \mid \text{drift}] = \mathbb{E}[W_{\text{rework}}]$ when $\mathbb{I}[\text{drift}] \perp W_{\text{rework}}$, and $p_{\text{drift}}^{*,\text{det}} = (\min(\mathbb{E}[X], \mathbb{E}[T_C]) - t_{\text{stub}}) / \mathbb{E}[W_{\text{rework}} \mid \text{drift}]$). Because $(X, Y_1, \dots, Y_k) \mapsto \min(X, \max_{i \le k} Y_i)$ composes concave outer $\min$ with **convex** inner $\max_{i \le k}$, it is **neither globally concave nor globally convex**, splitting stochastic behavior ($\sigma = 0.45, t_{\text{stub}} = 0.12, \mathbb{E}[W_{\text{rework}} \mid \text{drift}] = 2.40$) into two exact regimes:
   - **Regime 1 ($\mathbb{E}[T_C] \ge \mathbb{E}[T_{H,\text{shared}}]$ — Downward Concave-Min Shift):** Since $\mathbb{E}[M_k] > \mathbb{E}[T_C] \ge \mathbb{E}[X]$, Jensen's inequality on $\min$ yields $\mathbb{E}[\min(X, M_k)] \le \min(\mathbb{E}[X], \mathbb{E}[M_k]) = \mathbb{E}[X] = \min(\mathbb{E}[X], \mathbb{E}[T_C])$, keeping $p_{\text{drift}}^{*,\text{stoch}}(k) < p_{\text{drift}}^{*,\text{det}}$ for every finite $k$. On $T_{H,\text{shared}} = 1.50, T_C = 1.60$ ($p_{\text{drift}}^{*,\text{det}} = \mathbf{0.5750}$), exact 1D quadrature gives **`0.4922` ($k=2$), `0.5165` ($k=3$), `0.5382` ($k=5$), `0.5514` ($k=8$), `0.5591` ($k=12$)** (`W_stub_ded = 3.641 > W_block_ded = 3.589` at $k = 2, p_{\text{drift}} = 0.52 \in (0.4922, 0.5750)$); at equal means $T_{H,\text{shared}} = T_C = 1.00$ ($p_{\text{drift}}^{*,\text{det}} = \mathbf{0.3667}$), quadrature gives **`0.3033` ($k=2$) $\to$ `0.3531` ($k=12$)**.
   - **Regime 2 ($\mathbb{E}[T_{H,\text{shared}}] > \mathbb{E}[T_C]$ — Upward Convex-Straggler Shift for $k \ge 3$):** On $T_{H,\text{shared}} = 1.50 > T_C = 1.20$, deterministic theory predicts $p_{\text{drift}}^{*,\text{det}} = (1.20 - 0.12) / 2.40 = \mathbf{0.4500}$, whereas convex growth of $\mathbb{E}[\max_{1 \le i \le k} T_{C,i}]$ pushes exact $p_{\text{drift}}^{*,\text{stoch}}(k)$ from `0.4308` ($k=2$) **strictly above deterministic** to **`0.4626` ($k=3$), `0.4948` ($k=5$), `0.5173` ($k=8$), and `0.5320` ($k=12$)** (`W_stub_ded = 3.641 < W_block_ded = 3.734` at $k = 8, p_{\text{drift}} = 0.48 \in (0.4500, 0.5173)$).
