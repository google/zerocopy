# RISC-V RVC Mid-Instruction Parcel Gadgets, R_RISCV_RELAX vs. SHF_EXCLUDE Span Deletion, CGU Directive Bleed, and fcsr/RVV/STT_TLS Seam Hazards

## Summary

Porting MIR-to-ISA verifiers, self-attesting `core::arch::asm!` span trackers, and binary SFI/CFI validators from `x86-64` and `AArch64` to 64-bit RISC-V (`riscv64gc-unknown-linux-gnu`) exposes four architectural and ELF-linking fractures that do not arise on fixed-length `AArch64` or length-preserving `x86-64` relaxations:

1. **The `R_RISCV_RELAX` / `R_RISCV_ALIGN` $\times$ `SHF_EXCLUDE` Byte-Deletion Catch-22 (`Basis: normative + source + execution + derived`):**
   - Although `rustc` omits `+relax` from `riscv64gc_unknown_linux_gnu` (`0` `R_RISCV_RELAX` across 25 pure-Rust sysroot `.rlib`s), scanning all `27` shipped `riscv64gc-unknown-linux-gnu` sysroot `.rlib`s recovers **`1,877` `R_RISCV_RELAX` (`0x33`) relocations** (`236` in `libcompiler_builtins`—linked into every Rust binary including `#![no_std]`—and `1,641` in `libprofiler_builtins`, both compiled via C/assembly toolchains with `-mrelax`), alongside **`4,426` `R_RISCV_ADD32`/`SUB32`**, **`4,257` `R_RISCV_32_PCREL`**, and **`129` `R_RISCV_SET6`/`SUB6`** unwind/LSDA label-difference pairs.
   - Under default `--relax`, `rust-lld` (`21.1.8` and `23.1.5`) and `ld.mold` (`2.42.1`) delete bytes mid-function—shrinking `call` (`auipc ra, 0; jalr ra`, `8 B` $\to$ `jal ra`, `4 B`), `jump` (`auipc t1, 0; jr t1`, `8 B` $\to$ `c.j`, `2 B`), and `.balign 16` (`R_RISCV_ALIGN`, `16 B` $\to$ `8 B`) to contract a `52 B` function (`victim_with_spans`) to **`34 B` (`-18 B` deleted)**. Meanwhile, `lld/ELF/InputFiles.cpp:786` discards `SHF_EXCLUDE` (`"e"`) metadata sections (`.anneal.asm_sites`) and their `.rela` tables **before** relaxation runs—**even under `--emit-relocs`**—leaving pre-link `[0x1e, 0x26)` spans running **`+4 B` past the end of the `34 B` function** and shifting trailing pure-Rust `HintCert` PC offsets by `-18 B`.
   - **Why `-Wl,--no-relax` alone is unsound—and the two exact solutions:** In `lld/ELF/Writer.cpp:1513` and `lld/ELF/Arch/RISCV.cpp:832, 1012-1028`, `initSymbolAnchors` and `case R_RISCV_ALIGN` execute **unconditionally even when `--no-relax` (`!ctx.arg.relax`) is passed**, contracting `victim_with_spans` from **`52 B` to `40 B` (`-12 B` deleted)** across `rust-lld 21.1.8`, `rust-lld 23.1.5`, and `ld.mold 2.42.1`. Soundness requires either:
     * **Solution (a) (Pre-link `.o` invariance):** Pass `-Wl,--no-relax` **and** enforce a fail-closed `.o` relocation gate rejecting `R_RISCV_ALIGN` (`43`) across all input `.o`/`.rlib` members (which works out-of-the-box with all `27` unmodified `rustc` sysroot `.rlib`s because `libcompiler_builtins` and `libprofiler_builtins` contain `0` `R_RISCV_ALIGN`); or
     * **Solution (b) (Post-link relaxation-aware verification):** Emit `.anneal.asm_sites` without `SHF_EXCLUDE` as a non-`SHF_ALLOC` `"",@progbits` section using `R_RISCV_64` and `R_RISCV_ADD64`/`R_RISCV_SUB64` (zero `PT_LOAD` RAM footprint; relocated in place via post-relaxation `sym.getVA` in `InputSection::relocateNonAlloc`, `InputSection.cpp:1158-1163`), re-verify shrunk spans and `.eh_frame` `ADD32`/`SUB32` (`SET6`/`SUB6`) deltas post-link, and optionally strip the section afterward via `llvm-objcopy --remove-section`.

2. **`RVC` (`Zca`/`Zcd`, `IALIGN = 16`) `pc + 2` Mid-Instruction Gadgets, Trailing `.2byte` `ret`-Swallowing, and CGU `.option`/`.attribute` Bleed (`Basis: normative + source + execution`):**
   - Exhaustive `llvm-mc` disassembly of all `49,152` 16-bit halfwords with `bits[1:0] != 0b11` establishes that **`46,743` (`95.10%`)** are valid non-trapping `RV64GC` (`Zca`+`Zcd`) instructions. Across **`153,678` real `rustc`-compiled `riscv64gc` instructions** in `libcore`, `liballoc`, and `libstd` (`74,512` 16-bit + `79,166` 32-bit), **`72,885 / 79,166` (`92.07%`)** of 32-bit instructions have upper halfwords (`pc + 2`) with `bits[1:0] != 0b11`, and **`53,755` (`67.90%` of all 32-bit instructions)** decode at `pc + 2` as valid non-trapping `RVC` gadgets (`17,438` stores, `8,299` `sp` mutations, `2,716` loads, `1,899` branches/jumps, `23,403` ALU writes), while the remaining `6,281` (`bits[1:0] == 0b11`) fuse across `pc + 4` into `253` `jalr`, `84` `SYSTEM`, `459` load, and `88` store opcodes. Live `qemu-riscv64` execution confirms that jumping to `pc + 2` inside a single benign 4-byte `lui t0, 0xe10c0` (`0xe10c02b7`) or `lui t0, 0x86020` (`0x860202b7`) executes unintended `c.sd a1, 0(a0)` or `c.jr a2`.
   - Under `-C codegen-units=1`, three RISC-V-specific CGU hazards break pure-Rust sibling/caller isolation:
     * **Trailing `.2byte` (`bits[1:0] == 0b11`) `ret`-swallowing & `.byte` odd-PC desynchronization:** An inlined `asm!(".2byte 0x0537")`—even when followed by `.pushsection .anneal.asm_sites ... .popsection`—fuses with the caller's pure-Rust epilogue `ret` (`0x8082` = `c.jr ra`) into `0x80820537` (`lui a0, 0x80820`, sign-extending on RV64 to `0xffff_ffff_8082_0000`), **deleting the caller's `ret` and falling straight into the next function in `.text` (`0xffff_ffff_8082_7777` on `qemu-riscv64`)**, while `.byte 0x01` shifts `ret` onto odd alignment (`0x8201` `c.srli64 a2` + orphaned `0x80` $\to$ `SIGSEGV`, `rc = -11`).
     * **`.attribute` CGU poisoning vs. `.option` streamer split:** Uncalled `asm!(".attribute arch, \"rv64i2p1\"")` overwrites the entire CGU `.o`'s ELF `.riscv.attributes` `Tag_RISCV_arch` **even under default `MCObjectStreamer` (`--emit=obj`)**, whereas `.option norvc` and `.option relax` stay isolated in `MCObjectStreamer` (`AsmPrinter` per-function `STI`) yet bleed into `.s` under **default `MCAsmStreamer` (`--emit=asm`, `IAS=true`)**, disabling `RVC` or injecting `R_RISCV_RELAX` across sibling pure-Rust functions.
     * **`RISCVInstrInfo::getInstSizeInBytes` `±4 KiB` `Bcc` drift:** `RISCVInstrInfo.cpp:1989-1993` delegates `INLINEASM` size estimation to `getInlineAsmLength` (`MaxInstLength = 4 B`), inflating a 10-statement `.pushsection` wrapper around `2 B` `c.addi` to **`40 B` (`20×`)** and prematurely relaxing `c.beqz`/`beqz` into `c.bnez + c.j` at **`N = 105` sites (`228 B` actual $\ll 4,094\text{ B}$)**; a zero-separator per-site macro (`__aw`) restores **100% SHA-256 `.text` parity** (`ab46617346bac5f9` at `N = 105`, `0a1df5048fdc14a7` at `N = 520`).

3. **`options(preserves_flags)` (`~{fflags},~{vtype},~{vl},~{vxsat},~{vxrm}` Without `~{frm}`), `out("v0") _` Gating, and 4-Linker `STT_TLS` Mislinking (`Basis: normative + source + execution`):**
   - Despite RISC-V having no integer `cc` register, `rustc_codegen_llvm/src/asm.rs` (`lines 238-246` in `1.95.0-nightly`, `272-280` in `1.102.0-nightly`, post-commit `5ebdec5ac290`) emits **`~{fflags},~{vtype},~{vl},~{vxsat},~{vxrm}`** when `options(preserves_flags)` is omitted—**yet omits `~{frm}` (`fcsr[7:5]`)** and exposes **none** of `fflags`, `frm`, `fcsr`, `vl`, `vtype`, `vxsat`, or `vxrm` as user-namable `asm!` registers (`error: invalid register`). Because LLVM's default `fadd.d ..., dyn` models zero dependency on `frm`, `fflags`, or memory, `rustc -O` sinks pure-Rust `1.0 + 2^-100` **in both directions** across `fsrmi 3` (`RUP`) and `fsrm` (`RNE`) **even when `options(preserves_flags)` is omitted**, inverting both results live on `qemu-riscv64`!    - Linking non-TLS Rust `extern "C" { static c_tls_var: u64; }` against a `riscv64` `STT_TLS` `.o` (`st_value = 0x30`, `.tdata` init `0x1122334455667788`) **exits with code `0` and zero warnings across all four tested linkers**, exhibiting **three distinct PIE runtime corruptions** on `qemu-riscv64`: `rust-lld 21.1.8` writes static `0x30` into `.got` with zero dynamic relocations (`SIGSEGV`, `rc = -11`); `rust-lld 23.1.1` and `23.1.5` emit `R_RISCV_RELATIVE 0x30` (`isAbsoluteOrTls`, `Relocations.cpp:127, 845`), silently reading the RISC-V ELF64 header **`0x0038004000000005`** (`e_flags = 0x5`); and `ld.mold 2.42.1` resolves `c_tls_var` to the shared `.tdata` initialization template (`0x1122334455667788`), turning thread-local storage into an unsynchronized cross-thread alias.

4. **Lean `v4.34.1` Mechanization ([`support/VerifiedRiscv64ParcelAndRelaxSeam.lean`](support/VerifiedRiscv64ParcelAndRelaxSeam.lean)) (`Basis: execution + derived`):**

## Findings

### 1. The `R_RISCV_RELAX` / `R_RISCV_ALIGN` $\times$ `SHF_EXCLUDE` Byte-Deletion Catch-22

`Basis: normative + source + execution + derived` (`rust-lang/rust@f134bbc78d`: `compiler/rustc_target/src/spec/targets/riscv64gc_unknown_linux_gnu.rs:17-25`; `rust-lang/llvm-project@0a5579eb64`: `llvm/lib/Target/RISCV/MCTargetDesc/RISCVMCAsmInfo.cpp:35-49`, `lld/ELF/InputFiles.cpp:783-805`, `lld/ELF/InputSection.cpp:808-813, 1158-1187`, `lld/ELF/Writer.cpp:1510-1516`, `lld/ELF/Arch/RISCV.cpp:832-923, 1010-1095, 1240-1346`; [`support/probe_riscv64_relax_rvc_and_seams.py`](support/probe_riscv64_relax_rvc_and_seams.py), [`support/probe_riscv64_results.json`](support/probe_riscv64_results.json))

#### 1.1 `riscv64gc-unknown-linux-gnu` Sysroot Relocation Census (`27` `.rlib`s)

In `riscv64gc_unknown_linux_gnu.rs:20`, `rustc` sets `features: "+m,+a,+f,+d,+c,+zicsr,+zifencei"`, omitting `+relax`. Scanning every `.o` member across all `27` sysroot `.rlib`s (`support/probe_riscv64_results.json` $\to$ `relax_and_shf_exclude`) reveals why `R_RISCV_RELAX` and paired label-difference relocations nonetheless appear in standard Rust builds:

- **25 pure-Rust `.rlib`s** (`libcore`, `liballoc`, `libstd`, `libpanic_unwind`, `libgimli`, etc.) emit **`0` `R_RISCV_RELAX`** and **`0` `R_RISCV_ALIGN`**, yet still emit **`4,425` `R_RISCV_ADD32`/`R_RISCV_SUB32` pairs** (`0x23`/`0x27`), **`3,686` `R_RISCV_32_PCREL`** (`0x39`) FDE `initial_location` relocations (`RISCVMCAsmInfo::getExprForFDESymbol`, `RISCVMCAsmInfo.cpp:35-49`), and **`37,927` `R_RISCV_ADD16`/`SUB16`** pairs in `.eh_frame`, `.gcc_except_table`, and `.debug_*` so unwind/LSDA ranges remain valid if linked with relaxed C/assembly objects.
- **`libcompiler_builtins` (linked into every Rust binary, including `#![no_std]`) and `libprofiler_builtins` emit `1,877` `R_RISCV_RELAX` (`0x33`) relocations** (`236` in `libcompiler_builtins` across `240` `R_RISCV_CALL_PLT` sites + `26` `R_RISCV_SET6`/`SUB6` pairs; `1,641` in `libprofiler_builtins` across `607` `R_RISCV_CALL_PLT` sites + `103` `R_RISCV_SET6`/`SUB6` pairs), because their C/assembly sources are compiled via host C toolchains where `-mrelax` is enabled by default. Crucially, **all `27` sysroot `.rlib`s emit `0` `R_RISCV_ALIGN` (`0x2b`) relocations**.

#### 1.3 Architectural Comparison: Solution (a) vs. Solution (b)

1. **Why `--emit-relocs` cannot rescue `SHF_EXCLUDE`:** In `lld/ELF/InputFiles.cpp:786-805`, any non-`-r` link immediately maps `SHF_EXCLUDE` sections (and relocation sections targeting them) to `InputSection::discarded` before `Writer::run` invokes `RISCV::relaxOnce`.
2. **Solution (a) — Linker `-Wl,--no-relax` + Fail-Closed `.o` `R_RISCV_ALIGN` (`43`) Rejection:** Because `Writer.cpp:1513` invokes `RISCV::relaxOnce` unconditionally and `RISCV.cpp:1012-1028` processes `R_RISCV_ALIGN` without checking `ctx.arg.relax` (`relaxable()` gates only `R_RISCV_RELAX` pairs at lines `1034, 1043, 1049, 1054`), `--no-relax` preserves pre-link byte offsets **if and only if zero input `.o` sections contain `R_RISCV_ALIGN` (`43`)**. Rejecting `R_RISCV_ALIGN` (`43`) at `.o` audit time (and forbidding `.option relax` in user `asm!`) makes pre-link `SHF_EXCLUDE` `.anneal.asm_sites` spans and `HintCert` PC offsets 100% post-link invariant while linking unmodified `rustc` sysroot `.rlib`s (`0` `R_RISCV_ALIGN`).
3. **Solution (b) — Non-`SHF_EXCLUDE`, Non-`SHF_ALLOC` Metadata (`"",@progbits`) + Post-Link Span & FDE Re-Verification:** Omitting `"e"` from `.pushsection .anneal.asm_sites,"",@progbits` keeps `0 B` of runtime `PT_LOAD` footprint while causing `RISCV::relax` (`RISCV.cpp:1072-1090`) to adjust every `.L__s*`/`.L__e*` `SymbolAnchor` (`d->value = offset - delta`) and `InputSection::relocateNonAlloc` (`InputSection.cpp:1158-1163`) to resolve `R_RISCV_64` and `R_RISCV_ADD64`/`R_RISCV_SUB64` to exact post-relaxation VAs and byte lengths (`4 B, 8 B, 2 B`). Avoid `R_RISCV_32_PCREL` inside non-`SHF_ALLOC` sections (`InputSection.cpp:1171-1187` warns on PC-relative relocations in non-`ALLOC` sections; note also that writing `.4byte .L__s - .` inside `SHF_ALLOC` `.note` emits `R_RISCV_ADD32`+`R_RISCV_SUB32` rather than `R_RISCV_32_PCREL`). After post-link verification of shrunk spans and `.eh_frame` FDEs, `llvm-objcopy --remove-section=.anneal.asm_sites` produces a clean deployment binary while preserving full `-mrelax` optimization.

#### 2.2 CGU Sibling/Caller Corruption: Trailing `.2byte` `ret`-Swallowing, `.byte`, `.attribute`, `.option`, and `Bcc` Drift

1. **Trailing `.2byte 0x0537` (`bits[1:0] == 0b11`) swallows pure-Rust `ret` (`0x8082`) across `.pushsection`..`.popsection`:** In `caller_corrupted_by_half11(val)` (`val.wrapping_add(1)` after an inlined `asm!(".2byte 0x0537\n.pushsection .anneal.asm_sites,...\n.popsection")`), LLVM schedules `0505` (`c.addi a0, 1`) before the `asm!`, emits `37 05` (`.2byte 0x0537`) into `.text`, switches to `.anneal.asm_sites` and back (`0 B` in `.text`), and emits pure-Rust `82 80` (`ret` = `c.jr ra`) into the **same `MCDataFragment`**. Emitting `.byte 0x01` (`1 B`) shifts `ret` (`82 80`) onto odd alignment (`01 82` = `c.srli64 a2` + orphaned `0x80`), crashing with `SIGSEGV` (`rc = -11`).
2. **CGU `.attribute` poisoning vs. `.option` streamer divergence (`probe_riscv64_results.json` $\to$ `cgu_containment_matrix`):**

   - **`.option norvc` / `.option relax` split between `--emit=obj` and `--emit=asm`:** Under `MCObjectStreamer` (`--emit=obj`), `AsmPrinter` passes `MF->getSubtarget()` per `MCInst`, keeping `z_pure_victim` compressed (`pure_rvc: true`) and unrelaxed (`pure_relax: false`). Under **default `MCAsmStreamer` (`--emit=asm`, even with `IAS=true`)**, `RISCVTargetAsmStreamer` writes `.option norvc` / `.option relax` verbatim to `.s`, **disabling `RVC` (`pure_rvc: false`) or injecting `R_RISCV_RELAX` (`pure_relax: true`) into all subsequent pure-Rust functions**!

3. **`RISCVInstrInfo::getInstSizeInBytes` premature `Bcc` relaxation (`probe_riscv64_results.json` $\to$ `bcc_get_inline_asm_length_drift`):** Because RISC-V `B`-type branches reach only `[-4096, +4094]` B (`1/8` of AArch64 `TBZ` `±32 KiB`) and `getInlineAsmLength` charges `4 B` per statement, a 10-statement `.pushsection` wrapper around `addi {0}, {0}, 1` (`2 B` `c.addi`) inflates estimated size `20×` (`40 B` vs. `2 B`). At **`N = 105` sites** (`228 B` actual `.text`), `multiline_pushsection` estimates `4,200 B > 4,094 B` and relaxes `c.beqz a0, +0xd4` into `c.bnez a0, +4; c.j +0xd6` (`230 B`, `a18d46cd2fd72945` vs. `228 B`, `ab46617346bac5f9`). At **`N = 520` sites** (`1,060 B` actual), `4 B` `beqz` (`40050a63`) relaxes into `2 B + 2 B` `c.bnez + c.j` (`e111 + a909`), **keeping `.text` byte size identical (`1,060 B`) while silently mutating the CFG and SHA-256 (`1e6ad3635924afd5` vs. `0a1df5048fdc14a7`)**! ### 3. `options(preserves_flags)` (`fcsr` / RVV), `out("v0") _` Gating, and 4-Linker `STT_TLS` Mislinking

`Basis: normative + source + execution` (`rust-lang/rust@f134bbc78d` & `3ef0c22cdb`: `compiler/rustc_codegen_llvm/src/asm.rs:238-246` / `272-280`, `compiler/rustc_target/src/asm/riscv.rs:42-54, 135-179`, `compiler/rustc_target/src/asm/mod.rs:1171-1193`, `compiler/rustc_hir_typeck/src/inline_asm.rs:425-458`; `rust-lang/reference@c9cc32825b`: `src/inline-assembly.md:1384-1386`; `rust-lang/llvm-project@0a5579eb64`: `lld/ELF/Relocations.cpp:127-129, 839-902, 924-967`; [`support/probe_riscv64_results.json`](support/probe_riscv64_results.json))

#### 3.1 Exact RISC-V `preserves_flags` Lowering, Bidirectional `frm` Code Motion, and RVV Gating

1. **Five-register default clobber list—missing `~{frm}` and lacking user-namable status registers:** Following `rust-lang/rust@5ebdec5ac290` (*"Adjust RISC-V inline assembly's clobber list"*), omitting `options(preserves_flags)` on `RiscV32 | RiscV64` emits **`"~{fflags},~{vtype},~{vl},~{vxsat},~{vxrm},~{memory}"`** (verified identically in `1.95.0-nightly` and `1.102.0-nightly`), while enabling `options(preserves_flags)` emits **`"~{memory}"`** (zero status-register clobbers). 2. **Live bidirectional `frm` (`fcsr[7:5]`) `fadd.d` code motion—even *without* `options(preserves_flags)` (`qemu-riscv64` `rc = 0`):** Because `rustc` lowers `a + b` (`1.0_f64 + 2^-100`) to `fadd.d fa5, fa0, fa1` (`rm = dyn = 0b111`) under LLVM's default `fpexcept.ignore` environment—which models `fadd.d` as pure with zero dependency on `frm`, `fflags`, or memory—LLVM's instruction scheduler sinks `fadd.d` across `asm!("fsrmi {old}, 3")` (`RUP`) and `asm!("fsrm {old}")` (`RNE`) **even when `options(preserves_flags)` is omitted**:

   - Conversely, in `frm_between_escapes_rup`, `let sum_rup = a + b;` written *between* `fsrmi 3` and `fsrm {old}` (restoring `frm` before exit as required by `r[asm.rules.preserved-registers]`) sinks *past* `fsrm {old}` (`112ca: fsrmi a1, 3; ... 112d0: fsrm a1; 112d4: fadd.d`), returning **`0x3ff0_0000_0000_0000`** instead of RUP `0x3ff0_0000_0000_0001`!

Furthermore, because `RiscVInlineAsmRegClass::vreg` (`riscv.rs:52, 135-166`) is clobber-only (`supported_types() == &[]`) with no `% has_v` predicate and `inline_asm.rs:425` skips feature checks when `op.is_clobber()`, **`out("v0") _` (`~{v0}`) and `clobber_abi("C")` (`~{v0}..~{v31}`) compile cleanly on stable syntax without `#[target_feature(enable = "v")]` or `asm_experimental_reg`**.

## Boundaries

- **Live GNU `ld.bfd` `elf64lriscv` execution (`not examined`):** Host Debian `/usr/bin/ld.bfd 2.47` on this `x86_64` workstation builds only `elf_x86_64`/`i386` emulations; live ELF linking was executed across `rust-lld 21.1.8`, `rust-lld 23.1.1`, `rust-lld 23.1.5`, and `ld.mold 2.42.1`.

- **Hardware Indirect-Branch Landing-Pad (`Zicfilp` `lpad`) enforcement (`not examined` on silicon; `known not to apply` on baseline `RV64GC`):** On cores implementing `Zicfilp`, hardware `ELP` state traps indirect jumps (`jalr`/`c.jr` other than software-guarded returns) that do not land on a 4-byte-aligned `lpad` instruction; on baseline `RV64GC` without `Zicfilp`, `pc + 2` indirect gadgets must be prevented by parcel-start bitmaps or restricted/masked `jalr` targets (`checkRvParcelBitmapSfi_sound`).
- **`c.jal` (`0x2001`) call relaxation on 64-bit RISC-V (`known not to apply`):** `c.jal` exists only in `RV32C` (re-encoded as `c.addiw` in `RV64C`, `RISCV.cpp:912`); on `RV64GC`, `8 B` `call` (`rd = ra`) shrinks at most to `4 B` `jal`, whereas `8 B` tail/local `jump` (`rd = x0`) shrinks to `2 B` `c.j`.
- **Explicit `fflags`, `frm`, `fcsr`, `vl`, `vtype`, `vxsat`, or `vxrm` clobber operands and typed `vreg` inputs/outputs in Rust `asm!` (`unsupported`):** All three examined `rustc` versions (`1.95.0-nightly`, `1.99.1`, `1.102.0-nightly`) reject `out("frm") _` / `out("fflags") _` / `out("vl") _` (`invalid register`) and typed `out("v0") y` (`vreg` is clobber-only). Any `asm!` modifying `frm` must save and restore `frm` internally and bind dependent FP values as explicit `freg` operands so LLVM cannot reorder `fadd.d` across it.
