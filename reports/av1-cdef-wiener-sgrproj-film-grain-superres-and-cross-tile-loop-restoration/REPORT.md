# AV1 Post-Reconstruction Pipeline: Super-Resolution, CDEF, Wiener and SGRPROJ Loop Restoration, AR(3) Film Grain, Warped Motion, and Lock-Free Cross-Tile Ownership

## Summary

In the AOMedia AV1 video codec (`v1.0.0-errata1`, `AOMediaCodec/av1-spec` at `5e04f3f75e73a5898d7616c47c52f032144b8f80`), coding-block reconstruction is strictly tile-disjoint, whereas the post-reconstruction pipeline—Deblocking, Constrained Directional Enhancement Filter (CDEF), horizontal Super-Resolution (`superres`), switchable Wiener and Self-Guided Projection (`SGRPROJ`) Loop Restoration, and out-of-place `AR(3)` Film Grain Synthesis—runs unconditionally **across tile boundaries** while reading mixed pre-filter and post-filter halo neighborhoods.

Cross-repository primary-source analysis of `videolan/dav1d` (`9af5ae4eb5204706c7f381ec62635237179f172e`), `memorysafety/rav1d` (`2e7216c20ac2a75de204d6c8d7268b75a45959b4`), and `imazen/rav1d-safe` (`19327e174e738785cd4155711bbb9ce3d4a467ce`) establishes four foundational engineering results for clean-room, formally verified, memory-safe media decoders:

1. **Dual-frame line-buffer invariants bound hardware and software state without full-frame copies (`Basis: normative`, `Basis: source`):** AV1 specifies Loop Restoration in `64`-luma-row horizontal stripes shifted upward by `8` luma rows (`StripeStartY = (-8 + stripeNum * 64) >> subY`), sourcing interior samples from CDEF-filtered `UpscaledCdefFrame` while clamping out-of-stripe vertical taps (`±3` taps cropped to `±2` rows) to **pre-CDEF deblocked `UpscaledCurrFrame`** (`08.decoding.process.md:6272-6291, 6602-6621`; `dav1d` `src/lr_apply_tmpl.c:36-204`). When `superres` is active (`width[0] != width[1]`), `dav1d` splits CDEF's coded-width border cache (`cdef_lpf_line`) from LR's upscaled-width border cache (`lr_lpf_line`) (`src/cdef_apply_tmpl.c:216-235`).
2. **Isolating `rav1d-safe`'s `4.9x` CDEF / `7x` padding cliff exposes register-fusion vs scratch-widening design gaps (`Basis: documentation`, `Basis: source`):** On single-threaded 4K decode with `DisjointMut` tracking compiled out (`untracked`), `rav1d-safe` spends `9.8 ms/frame` in CDEF against `dav1d`'s `2.0 ms/frame` (`4.9x`), including `3.4 ms` vs `0.5 ms` (`~6.8x–7x`) in `padding_8bpc` (`docs/SAFE_VS_DAV1D.md:38-45`). Inspection of `dav1d`'s `src/x86/cdef_avx2.asm:401-503` reveals that for interior blocks (`edges == 0xf`, `>95%` of 4K blocks), `dav1d` **skips `u8 -> u16` scratch padding altogether** and filters 4 rows (`32` bytes) simultaneously in `ymm` byte lanes (`ACCUMULATE_TAP_BYTE` via `psubusb`/`psignb`/`pmaddubsw`), falling back to `u16` stack padding (`0x8000` dual-sign sentinel) only on border blocks. By contrast, `rav1d-safe` (`src/safe_simd/cdef.rs:322-405, 1274-1395`) unconditionally fills a `12x12` `u16` scratch array (`0xC000` sentinel required so `_mm_abs_epi16(diff)` never hits `i16::MIN` overflow!), widens every row with bounds-checked slices, and filters 1 row at a time in 128-bit `xmm` registers on AVX2.
3. **Every normative division across Warped Motion, Superres, Wiener/SGRPROJ, and Film Grain reduces to exact fixed-point shifts or small ROM tables (`Basis: normative`, `Basis: source`):** Least-squares affine warp determinants (`dav1d_find_affine_int`, `src/warpmv.c:102-111`) use `u64log2` + a 257-entry `div_lut`; `SGRPROJ` (`sgr_calc_row_ab`, `src/looprestoration_tmpl.c:499-517`) replaces $z / (z + 1)$ with 256-byte `dav1d_sgr_x_by_x` and $1/N$ (`N ∈ {9, 25}`) with `{455, 164}` (`>> 12`); Wiener (`wiener_filter_h/v`, `src/looprestoration_tmpl.c:44-191`) splits rounding dynamically (`3+11` bits at 8/10bpc vs `5+9` bits at 12bpc) so horizontal intermediates fit unsigned `15`-bit `uint16_t` without overflow; and Film Grain (`src/filmgrain_tmpl.c:38-408`) combines a 16-bit LFSR PRNG, a strictly causal `24`-tap `AR(3)` IIR filter, and `{27, 17}` / `{23, 22}` (`>> 5`) block-overlap ramps—plus a hidden C `const`-cast input-plane write on odd widths (`src/fg_apply_tmpl.c:196-202`, `src/fg_apply.rs:181-189`) that breaks naive immutable reference-frame borrowing.
4. **`DisjointMut` is a C-porting artifact, not an irreducible AV1 requirement (`Basis: documentation`, `Basis: source`, `Basis: derived`):** `memorysafety/rav1d`'s `DisjointMut` disables overlap checks in release builds and holds `&'a mut V` inside guards (Stacked/Tree Borrows UB under Miri; `rav1d` `src/disjoint_mut.rs:60-107`, `rav1d-safe` `docs/OWNERSHIP_MODELS.md:184-203`). Making `DisjointMut` sound in `rav1d-safe` exposed severe multi-core spin-lock contention (`+27.3%` CPU inflation at `t=8`, `deblock_cols` `2.57x`) and a thread-independent `2.8x–4.0x` filter copy-in/write-back tax (`docs/TILED_SCALING.md:54-83, 366-387`). A clean-room Rust AV1 decoder eliminates `DisjointMut` completely via **phased typestate transitions**: worker-owned column-compact `ReconBand` buffers (`0.315 ms` stitch vs `19.7 ms` tracker, `60:1`), standard `chunks_mut` / `split_at_mut` horizontal strip views over an exclusively owned `PlaneMut` across Deblock/CDEF/Superres/LR, `PlaneMut -> Arc<FrozenPlane>` freeze (after pre-extending odd-width luma right padding), and lock-free `chunks_mut(32 * stride)` out-of-place Film Grain.

## Findings

### 1. Post-Reconstruction Pipeline Topology and Dual-Frame Line-Buffer Invariants

`Basis: normative` (AV1 `v1.0.0-errata1` §§7.14–7.18, `AOMediaCodec/av1-spec` `08.decoding.process.md:5174-7139`) and `Basis: source` (`videolan/dav1d` `src/thread_task.c:646-658, 793-855`, `src/cdef_apply_tmpl.c:41-255`, `src/lr_apply_tmpl.c:36-204`).

After tile-disjoint superblock-row (`sby`) reconstruction completes across all tile columns for row `sby` (`recon_progress`), AV1 executes five ordered post-reconstruction stages per superblock row (`64` or `128` luma lines tall depending on `seq_hdr->sb128`), followed by frame-level or row-dispatched out-of-place Film Grain:

$$\text{Tile Recon } (\text{width}_0) \longrightarrow \text{Deblock Cols} \longrightarrow \text{Deblock Rows} \longrightarrow \text{CDEF } (\text{width}_0) \longrightarrow \text{Superres } (\text{width}_0 \to \text{width}_1) \longrightarrow \text{Loop Restoration } (\text{width}_1) \longrightarrow \text{Ref Update} \longrightarrow \text{Film Grain}$$

Three non-obvious cross-stage data-flow invariants govern correctness and concurrency:

1. **Serialization of `DEBLOCK_ROWS` and the Transform-Height Reach Invariant (`src/thread_task.c:796-813`, `08.decoding.process.md:5364-5394`):**
   In `dav1d`, `DAV1D_TASK_TYPE_DEBLOCK_COLS` (filtering vertical block edges inside `sby`) can run concurrently across consecutive superblock rows once all tiles finish `sby`. However, `DAV1D_TASK_TYPE_DEBLOCK_ROWS` (filtering horizontal edges) calls `ensure_progress(..., &f->frame_thread.deblock_progress)`, **serializing horizontal deblocking strictly in ascending `sby` order**! Why does `DEBLOCK_ROWS(sby)` never race with a concurrent `DEBLOCK_COLS(sby + 1)` below it? At the top edge `y = sby * sb_h`, `DEBLOCK_ROWS(sby)` reaches up to `7` rows upward into `sby - 1` (which has already completed `DEBLOCK_ROWS`). At the bottom-most internal horizontal edge of `sby` (`y = (sby + 1) * sb_h - 4`), no transform block can cross the superblock boundary `(sby + 1) * sb_h`, so the `q`-side transform height is at most `4` pixels; AV1 §7.14.3 bounds filter tap width by `Min(tx_h_p, tx_h_q) <= 4`, selecting the narrow 4-tap filter (`q0, q1` modified, i.e., `y .. y + 2`), **strictly inside `sby`**! Violating this normative reach by unconditionally copying a 7-tap window across `(sby + 1) * sb_h` caused a real multi-threaded overlap panic in `rav1d-safe` (`#494`, `docs/OWNERSHIP_MODELS.md:351-368`).
2. **Pre-CDEF Line Backup (`lr_copy_lpf` / `copy_lpf_progress`) and Split `superres` Buffers (`src/thread_task.c:646-650, 814-827`, `src/cdef_apply_tmpl.c:216-235`):**
   Both CDEF (`±2` vertical taps) and Loop Restoration (`±3` vertical taps cropped to `±2` rows) require **un-CDEF'd, deblocked** pixels across horizontal superblock/stripe boundaries. Immediately after `DEBLOCK_ROWS(sby)` finishes (and before `CDEF(sby)` mutates `sby` in place), `lr_copy_lpf` copies the boundary deblocked rows into dedicated line buffers and sets bit `sby` in `copy_lpf_progress`. Notice `thread_task.c:646-650` and `:818-825`: `CDEF(sby)` (`sby > 0`) **cannot start** until `copy_lpf_progress` bit `sby - 1` is set! Furthermore, when `superres` is disabled (`resize == 0`), `dav1d` shares `f->lf.lr_lpf_line` for both CDEF and LR borders; when `resize != 0`, CDEF runs at `width[0]` (`f->cur`) reading `f->lf.cdef_lpf_line`, after which `filter_sbrow_resize` horizontally upscales both `f->cur -> f->sr_cur` **and** the saved pre-CDEF border lines into `f->lf.lr_lpf_line` at `width[1]`!
3. **Upward-Shifted `64`-Row Loop Restoration Stripes (`08.decoding.process.md:6272-6291, 6587-6621`, `src/lr_apply_tmpl.c:45-98, 169-186`):**
   Loop Restoration partitions the upscaled frame into horizontal stripes indexed by `stripeNum = (lumaY + 8) / 64`, spanning `[StripeStartY, StripeEndY]` where `StripeStartY = (-8 + stripeNum * 64) >> subY` (so stripe `0` is `56` luma rows `0..55`, stripe `1` is `64` luma rows `56..119`, etc.). Why `-8`? Because `DEBLOCK_ROWS(sby)` cannot filter the bottom `8` rows of `sby` (specifically `[ (sby+1)*64 - 8 .. (sby+1)*64 )`) until `sby + 1` arrives to deblock the boundary edge `y = (sby + 1) * 64`! Shifting LR stripes upward by `8` luma lines aligns every stripe boundary `64*k - 8` with the exact frontier of completed deblocking + CDEF for superblock row `sby`! Across any stripe boundary, AV1 §7.17.6 (`get_source_sample`) redirects out-of-stripe taps (`y < StripeStartY` or `y > StripeEndY`) from `UpscaledCdefFrame` to **deblocked, pre-CDEF `UpscaledCurrFrame`**, cropping `y` to `[StripeStartY - 2, StripeEndY + 2]` so the 3rd outer tap reuses the 2nd pre-CDEF line (`wiener_c` `rows[0]` duplication at `src/looprestoration_tmpl.c:269-275`). Horizontally across Restoration Units (`64`, `128`, or `256` px wide), LR reads post-CDEF pixels, so `lr_sbrow` backs up `4` pre-LR columns (`pre_lr_border[2][136][4]`, `backup4xU` at `src/lr_apply_tmpl.c:133, 155`) before overwriting unit `x`.

### 2. Warped Motion Shear Derivation, 8-Tap Two-Pass Filtering, and Signed-Stride Hazards

`Basis: normative` (AV1 §§7.11.3.5–7.11.3.7, `08.decoding.process.md:3216-3632`) and `Basis: source` (`videolan/dav1d` `src/warpmv.c:36-209`, `src/mc_tmpl.c:785-866`; `imazen/rav1d-safe` `src/warpmv.rs`, `docs/MC_WARP_DESTINATION_ROWS.md:1-160`).

AV1 supports both frame-level global affine/rotational/zoom motion (`GLOBALMV`) and block-level local warped motion (`WARPED_CAUSAL`, least-squares fitted from up to 8 neighboring motion-vector sample pairs `pts[np][2][2]`).

1. **Division-Free 64-Bit Least-Squares Estimation (`dav1d_find_affine_int`, `src/warpmv.c:149-209`):**
   Accumulates symmetric $2\times 2$ normal matrix `a[2][2]` and right-hand vectors `bx[2], by[2]` from neighbor coordinates within `|sx - dx| < 256 && |sy - dy| < 256`. Computes `int64_t det = (int64_t)a[0][0] * a[1][1] - (int64_t)a[0][1] * a[0][1]` (if `det == 0`, warp is rejected). Instead of `int64` hardware division, `resolve_divisor_64(|det|, &shift)` (`warpmv.c:102-111`) finds `k = u64log2(|det|)`, normalizes the top 8 fractional mantissa bits `f ∈ [0, 256]`, sets `shift = k + 14 - 16`, and looks up **`div_lut[f] = round(2^14 / (1 + f/256)) ∈ [8192, 16384]`** (`warpmv.c:36-61`). Diagonal parameters `mat[2], mat[5]` (`1.0` nominal = `0x10000`) are clamped to `[0xe001, 0x11fff]` and off-diagonals `mat[3], mat[4]` to `[-0x1fff, 0x1fff]`.
2. **Shear Decomposition and the `15`-Row Window Bound (`dav1d_get_shear_params`, `src/warpmv.c:80-100`):**
   Decomposes the $2\times 2$ affine matrix into two separable 1D shears (`alpha = iclip_wmp(mat[2] - 0x10000)`, `beta = iclip_wmp(mat[3])`, `gamma = iclip_wmp(mat[4] * 0x10000 / mat[2])` via `resolve_divisor_32`, `delta = iclip_wmp(mat[5] - mat[3]*mat[4]/mat[2] - 0x10000)`), where `iclip_wmp` rounds to multiples of `64` (`6` low bits zeroed, `int16_t` range). Crucially, `dav1d_get_shear_params` enforces the normative **shear admissibility check**:
   $$(4|\alpha| + 7|\beta| < 2^{16}) \;\land\; (4|\gamma| + 4|\delta| < 2^{16})$$
   Why `4|alpha| + 7|beta| < 0x10000`? Inside each `8x8` subblock (`warp_affine_8x8_c`, `src/mc_tmpl.c:799-832`), the horizontal pass evaluates `15` rows (`y = -3..+11` relative to block top, `mid[15 * 8]`), stepping horizontal subpel phase by `alpha` (`abcd[0]`) per column `x ∈ [0..7]` and `beta` (`abcd[1]`) per row `y ∈ [0..14]`, selecting 8-tap filters from `dav1d_mc_warp_filter[64 + ((tmx + 512) >> 10)]` (`193` rows total, valid indices `[0..192]`, i.e., `|(tmx + 512) >> 10| <= 64`!). The `4|alpha| + 7|beta| < 65536` bound proves **statically** that `64 + ((tmx + 512) >> 10)` never indexes out of bounds of `dav1d_mc_warp_filter` and vertical shear never reads outside `mid[0..15*8]`!
3. **Two-Pass Fixed-Point Precision (`src/mc_tmpl.c:804-866`):**
   With `intermediate_bits = 4` (`8`-bit and `10`-bit) or `2` (`12`-bit; `bitdepth + intermediate_bits <= 14`), Pass 1 (horizontal) rounds 8-tap products (`sum` of `int8_t` taps × `u8/u16` pixels) by `7 - intermediate_bits` (`3` at 8/10bpc, `5` at 12bpc), fitting signed `int16_t mid[120]`. Pass 2 (vertical) rounds by `7 + intermediate_bits` (`11` at 8/10bpc, `9` at 12bpc) and clips to `[0, (1 << bitdepth) - 1]` (`warp8x8`), or shifts by `7` minus `PREP_BIAS` into `int16_t tmp` for compound blending (`warp8x8t`).
4. **Negative Destination Stride Panic & Repair (`docs/MC_WARP_DESTINATION_ROWS.md:8-27, 125-159`):**
   In C (`dav1d`), negative plane strides (`bottom-up` frame buffers) index negatively from the top-row pointer `dst`. In `rav1d-safe` prior to `54f78fb2`, the safe dispatcher sliced `&mut plane[origin..]` (discarding bytes before `origin`) and cast `y * dst_stride` to `usize`, triggering a wrapped-`usize` bounds panic whenever `dst_stride < 0` took the direct untracked/1-worker path! Passing `(full_bounded_plane_slice, origin_byte_base, signed_stride)` and computing `(origin_byte_base as isize + y * stride) as usize` repairs both warp and standard `put`/`prep` across all 7,768 signed-stride oracle cases (`+0.89%` median 4K 1t cost on warp, noise-neutral `-0.04%` on standard `put`/`prep`).

### 3. Super-Resolution (`superres`) Horizontal Upscaling Arithmetic

`Basis: normative` (AV1 §7.16, `08.decoding.process.md:6132-6222`) and `Basis: source` (`videolan/dav1d` `src/mc_tmpl.c:918-944`, `src/tables.c`).

When `use_superres == 1`, frames are coded and CDEF-filtered at reduced horizontal width `FrameWidth = (UpscaledWidth * 8 + SuperresDenom / 2) / SuperresDenom` (`SuperresDenom ∈ [9..16]`), then upscaled purely horizontally (`1:1` to `2:1`) before Loop Restoration:

- **Exact 14-bit subpel phase stepping (`08.decoding.process.md:6154-6168`):** With `SUPERRES_SCALE_BITS = 14` (`16384`), `SUPERRES_EXTRA_BITS = 8`, and `SUPERRES_SCALE_MASK = 0x3fff`:
  $$\text{stepX} = \left\lfloor \frac{(\text{downW} \ll 14) + \lfloor \text{upW} / 2 \rfloor}{\text{upW}} \right\rfloor, \quad \text{err} = \text{upW} \cdot \text{stepX} - (\text{downW} \ll 14)$$
  $$\text{initialSubpelX} = \left(\left\lfloor \frac{-((\text{upW} - \text{downW}) \ll 13) + \lfloor \text{upW} / 2 \rfloor}{\text{upW}} \right\rfloor + 128 - \left\lfloor \frac{\text{err}}{2} \right\rfloor\right) \mathbin{\&} \text{0x3fff}$$
  Starting from `src_x = -1` and `mx = initialSubpelX` (`resize_c`, `src/mc_tmpl.c:924`), each output pixel `x ∈ [0, dst_w)` selects 6-bit phase `mx >> 8 ∈ [0..63]`, clamps tap coordinates `src_x + k - 3` (`k ∈ 0..7`) to `[0, src_w - 1]`, and advances `mx += dx; src_x += mx >> 14; mx &= 0x3fff`.
- **Negated `int8_t` filter table trick (`src/mc_tmpl.c:926-935`):** In AV1 §7.16 (`08.decoding.process.md:6184, 6215`), `Upscale_Filter[0][3] = +128` and `Upscale_Filter[63][4] = +128`, which **overflows signed `int8_t` (`[-128, 127]`)** while minimum tap value is `-20`! Rather than widening the entire `64 × 8` table to `int16_t` (halving SIMD byte-table density), `dav1d` stores **`-Upscale_Filter` in `int8_t dav1d_resize_filter[64][8]`** (where `-128` is exact in two's-complement `int8_t` and `+20 <= 127`!) and evaluates `iclip_pixel((-(F[0]*s0 + ... + F[7]*s7) + 64) >> 7)`!

### 4. CDEF 8-Direction Variance Search, Dual-Sign Sentinel Algebra, and the `rav1d-safe` `4.9x` / `7x` Cliff

`Basis: normative` (AV1 §7.15, `08.decoding.process.md:5809-6130`; Midtskogen & Valin, `10.1109/ICASSP.2018.8462141`), `Basis: source` (`videolan/dav1d` `src/cdef_tmpl.c:44-265`, `src/x86/cdef_avx2.asm:41-750`; `imazen/rav1d-safe` `src/cdef.rs:419-790`, `src/safe_simd/cdef.rs:46-405, 780-1395`), and `Basis: documentation` (`imazen/rav1d-safe` `docs/SAFE_VS_DAV1D.md:38-45`, `ROADMAP_SIMD_PORTING.md:74-81`, `docs/CDEF_DIRECTION_COST_REVIEW.md:1-59`, `docs/CDEF_PADDING_REVIEW.md:1-40`).

#### Normative Arithmetic and Exact Overflow Bounds

1. **8-Direction Variance Search (`cdef_find_dir`, `08.decoding.process.md:5929-6008`):**
   - For every non-skipped `8x8` luma block, normalizes samples to 8-bit zero-mean signed integers `x = (px >> (BitDepth - 8)) - 128 ∈ [-128, 127]`, partitions the 64 pixels along 8 candidate edge directions `d ∈ {0..7}` (`45°` down-right through horizontal/vertical), squares each directional line sum $S_{d,k}$, and multiplies by $840 / L_{d,k}$ (`Div_Table = {840, 420, 280, 210, 168, 140, 120, 105}`, avoiding all division via $\text{LCM}(1..8) = 840$).
   - **Exact 32-bit non-overflow bound (`docs/CDEF_DIRECTION_COST_REVIEW.md:23-28`):** Because the lines in each direction $d$ partition the 64 pixels into disjoint subsets of sizes $L_{d,k}$ ($\sum_k L_{d,k} = 64$), Cauchy-Schwarz yields $\sum_k S_{d,k}^2 / L_{d,k} \le \sum_{p=1}^{64} x_p^2 \le 64 \times (-128)^2 = 1{,}048{,}576$. Scaling by `840` gives:
     $$0 \le \text{cost}[d] \le 840 \times 64 \times 128^2 = 880{,}803{,}840 < 2^{30} < 2^{31}-1$$
     Every partial sum (`|S| <= 8 * 128 = 1024`, fitting `i16`), squared term, and weighted total fits **signed `i32`** cleanly across all bit depths (`8, 10, 12`), with `var = (best_cost - cost[best_dir ^ 4]) >> 10` scaling luma primary strength via `priStr = (priStr * (4 + min(floor_log2(var >> 6), 12)) + 8) >> 4`.
2. **Nonlinear 12-Tap Filter (`cdef_filter_block`, `src/cdef.rs:420-606`):**
   - Evaluates 4 primary taps along `dir` (`k ∈ {0, 1}`, weights `{4, 2}` for even `pri_str >> (bd-8)` and `{3, 3}` for odd) plus 8 secondary cross taps at `dir ± 2` (`weights {2, 1}`), applying the piecewise tent function:
     $$\text{constrain}(\Delta, S, \text{shift}) = \text{sign}(\Delta) \cdot \min\!\left(|\Delta|, \max\!\left(0, S - (|\Delta| \gg \text{shift})\right)\right)$$
     where `shift = max(0, damping - floor_log2(S))`. Final update rounds symmetrically toward zero for negative sums: `px + ((sum - (sum < 0) + 8) >> 4)` (implemented in AVX2 without shifts via `pcmpgtw` + `paddw` + `pmulhrsw` against `pw_2048 = 2048` since $\lfloor (x \cdot 2048 + 16384) / 32768 \rfloor = (x + 8) \gg 4$!), clamped when `pri_str != 0 && sec_str != 0` to `[min_tap_and_px, max_tap_and_px]`.

#### The Dual-Sign Sentinel Trap (`0x8000` vs `0xC000` vs `+8191`)

Taps falling outside frame boundaries, outside the current tile? (**Note:** CDEF **crosses** tile boundaries, stopping ONLY at frame boundaries or unfiltered `skip` neighbors? Actually CDEF reads across `skip` blocks too — `CdefEdgeFlags` clears `HAVE_LEFT/RIGHT/TOP/BOTTOM` **only at picture edges**!) must be ignored by **both** `constrain()` (`output = 0`) and `min`/`max` clamping.

| Implementation | Sentinel Value | Why It Works (or Silently Corrupts Output) |
|---|---|---|
| **`dav1d` C & AVX2 border path** (`src/cdef_tmpl.c:45`, `src/x86/cdef_avx2.asm:343-373`) | **`0x8000` (`INT16_MIN = -32768` signed, `32768` unsigned)** | `pminuw` (`u16` min) treats `0x8000` as `+32768 > 4095` (never lowers `min`); `pmaxsw` (`i16` max) treats `0x8000` as `-32768 < 0` (never raises `max`). In AVX2 border `constrain`, `psubw` + `packsswb` saturates `-32768 - px` to `-128` (`0x80` after `pabsb`), and for every valid 8bpc `(damping ∈ [3..6], S ∈ [1..63])`, `0x80 >> shift >= S`, so `psubusb` yields `0`! |
| **`rav1d-safe` 16-bit SIMD path** (`src/safe_simd/cdef.rs:48-85, 1021-1028, 1288-1296`) | **`0xC000` (`-16384` signed, `49152` unsigned)** | **Crucial SIMD soundness catch:** `rav1d-safe`'s `constrain_128`/`constrain_avx2` runs in `i16` using `_mm_abs_epi16(diff)` followed by **arithmetic** `_mm_sra_epi16(adiff, shift)`! Had it used `0x8000` (`-32768`), then whenever `px == 0`, `diff = -32768 - 0 = i16::MIN`, and `_mm_abs_epi16(i16::MIN)` overflows signed `i16` to `0x8000` (`-32768` signed!), making `_mm_sra_epi16` **negative** and turning `S - shifted` positive! Choosing `0xC000` (`-16384`) guarantees `|-16384 - px| <= 16384 + 4095 = 20479 < 32768` across 8/10/12-bit while remaining negative in `i16` (`-16384`) and huge in `u16` (`49152`). |
| **Broken early NEON port** (`rav1d-safe` `70c1a70`, `ROADMAP_SIMD_PORTING.md:74-81`) | **`+8191` (`0x1FFF`)** | Large enough to zero `constrain()`, **but positive in signed `i16`**, so `smax(max, 8191) = 8191` silently disabled upper clamping on every frame-border block! Passed a 3-vector smoke test; caught only by full 783-vector differential MD5 testing (`5` corrupted blocks across `4` streams). |

### 5. 7-Tap Separable Wiener and Dual Self-Guided (`SGRPROJ`) Loop Restoration Fixed-Point Divisions

`Basis: normative` (AV1 §7.17, `08.decoding.process.md:6224-6622`; Mukherjee et al., `10.1109/ICIP.2017.8296504`) and `Basis: source` (`videolan/dav1d` `src/looprestoration_tmpl.c:41-900`, `src/lr_apply_tmpl.c:55-84`).

1. **7-Tap Separable Wiener Filter (`wiener_filter_h`, `wiener_filter_v`, `wiener_c`, `src/looprestoration_tmpl.c:44-386`):**
   - Reconstructs symmetric 7-tap horizontal and vertical filters (`5`-tap on chroma where `c0 = 0`) with unit DC gain: `f = [c0, c1, c2, 128 - 2*(c0+c1+c2), c2, c1, c0]` where `c0 ∈ [-5, 3]`, `c1 ∈ [-23, 8]`, `c2 ∈ [-17, 46]`. Note that `128 - 2*(-5 - 23 - 17) = 218 > 127`! In **8-bit SIMD** (`src/lr_apply_tmpl.c:60-65`, `src/looprestoration_tmpl.c:59-61`), `dav1d` omits `+128` from `filter[0][3]` (`-2*(c0+c1+c2) ∈ [-92, +90]`, fitting **signed `int8_t`** for `vpmaddubsw`!) and adds `src[x] * 128` separately into the accumulator.
   - **Dynamic-range-preserving split rounding (`InterRound0` / `InterRound1`):** Total 2D filter gain is $128 \times 128 = 2^{14}$. Had AV1 used a fixed horizontal shift of `3` bits at 12-bit (`max_px = 4095`), horizontal outputs could exceed unsigned `16`-bit `uint16_t`! Instead (`looprestoration_tmpl.c:50-52, 168-170`):
     - **Horizontal pass:** `round_bits_h = (bd == 12) ? 5 : 3`. Adds DC bias `1 << (bd + 6)` (`16384` at 8bpc, `65536` at 10bpc, `262144` at 12bpc), shifts right by `round_bits_h` with rounding, and clips to `[0, (1 << (bd + 8 - round_bits_h)) - 1]` — yielding **`[0, 8191]` (8bpc) or `[0, 32767]` (10bpc and 12bpc)**! Thus the horizontal ring buffer `uint16_t hor[6 * 390]` (`REST_UNIT_STRIDE = 256 * 1.5 + 6 = 390`, covering maximum `384`-wide rightmost RU + padding) **always fits unsigned 15-bit `uint16_t` (`<= 32767`)** (`08.decoding.process.md:6544`)!
     - **Vertical pass:** `round_bits_v = (bd == 12) ? 9 : 11` (`round_bits_h + round_bits_v == 14`), subtracts `round_offset = 1 << (bd + round_bits_v - 1)` to cancel the horizontal bias × unit-DC vertical gain `128`, shifts right by `round_bits_v`, and clips to `[0, (1 << bd) - 1]`.

2. **Dual Self-Guided Projection (`SGRPROJ`, `src/looprestoration_tmpl.c:413-670`):**
   - Approximates local guided image filtering at up to two box radii (`Pass 0`: $r_0 = 2 \implies 5\times 5, n = 25$; `Pass 1`: $r_1 = 1 \implies 3\times 3, n = 9$) selected from 16 `Sgr_Params` rows (`10` dual-pass, `4` $3\times 3$-only, `2` $5\times 5$-only; `src/lr_apply_tmpl.c:75-83` dispatches 3 specialized kernels `sgr_5x5`, `sgr_3x3`, `sgr_mix`).
   - **Three exact fixed-point division eliminations in `sgr_calc_row_ab` (`src/looprestoration_tmpl.c:499-517`, `08.decoding.process.md:6412-6440`):**
     1. *Noise normalization $1 / (n^2 \varepsilon)$:* Precomputed per `(r, eps)` in `dav1d_sgr_params` as $s = \lfloor (2^{20} + \lfloor n^2 \varepsilon / 2 \rfloor) / (n^2 \varepsilon) \rfloor$. Normalizing box sum-of-squares `AA` by `2*(bd-8)` -> `a` and box sum `BB` by `bd-8` -> `b` gives nonnegative variance numerator `p = max(0, a * n - b * b)` (note at 8/10/12bpc after normalization, $a \le 25 \times 255^2 = 1{,}625{,}625$, so `a * 25 <= 40,640,625`, and with max $s = \lfloor 2^{20} / (9^2 \times 4) \rfloor = 3237$, **`p * s` must be evaluated in unsigned 32-bit `uint32_t` (`<= 40.6M` for $n=25, s \le 140$, or $a \cdot 9 \le 5.26\text{M}$ for $n=9$, `p * s < 2^31`)** before `z = (p * s + (1 << 19)) >> 20`!).
     2. *Rational Wiener weight $z / (z + 1)$:* Replaced by clamping `min(z, 255)` into the 256-entry ROM **`dav1d_sgr_x_by_x`**. Notice `dav1d`'s **complement inversion optimization (`looprestoration_tmpl.c:513-515`)**: where AV1 §7.17.3 defines $A = \lfloor (256 z + \lfloor z/2 \rfloor)/(z+1) \rfloor \in [1, 256]$ and $B \propto (256 - A) \cdot \text{sum}$, `dav1d_sgr_x_by_x[z]` stores **`x = 256 - A ∈ [0, 255]`** directly! That shrinks `BB[i] = x` into `[0, 255]` (`uint8_t`/`int16_t`) and turns final filtering from `a * src + b` into `(b - a * src + round) >> shift` (`looprestoration_tmpl.c:561, 581, 591`)!
     3. *Window mean $1 / n$:* Replaced by `sgr_one_by_x = 455` ($n=9$: $\lfloor (4096 + 4)/9 \rfloor = 455$) and `164` ($n=25$: $\lfloor (4096 + 12)/25 \rfloor = 164$), computing `AA[i] = (x * sum_raw * sgr_one_by_x + 2048) >> 12`.
   - **Odd-row subsampling in `Pass 0` (`5x5`, `08.decoding.process.md:6444, 6453-6480`, `looprestoration_tmpl.c:568-595`):** `Pass 0` computes `(A5, B5)` **only on odd row indices `i ∈ {-1, 1, 3, ..., h-1, h+1}`**, halving vertical box-filter and `sgr_calc_row_ab` work (`rotate5_x2`)! Even output rows sum `6` neighbors above/below (`weights {5,6,5; 5,6,5}`, sum `32`, `>> 9`); odd output rows sum `3` horizontal neighbors on the same row (`weights {5,6,5}`, sum `16`, `>> 8`). Finally, `sgr_weighted2` projects `(w0 * flt0 + w2 * flt1 + 1024) >> 11` (`w2 = 128 - w0 - w1` in `dav1d`'s delta formulation since `flt` already subtracted `src`!).

### 6. Bit-Exact `AR(3)` Film Grain Synthesis and the Input-Plane Padding Trap

`Basis: normative` (AV1 §7.18.3, `08.decoding.process.md:6719-7138`) and `Basis: source` (`videolan/dav1d` `src/filmgrain_tmpl.c:38-420`, `src/fg_apply_tmpl.c:41-240`; `imazen/rav1d-safe` `src/fg_apply.rs:16-245`, `docs/FILMGRAIN_CONCURRENCY.md:1-51`).

Film Grain Synthesis runs **outside the coding loop** (never modifying reference frames stored in the DPB), mapping completed `in` planes to display `out` planes in two phases:

1. **Per-Frame `AR(3)` Grain Texture & Piecewise Scaling Generation (`dav1d_prep_grain`, `src/filmgrain_tmpl.c:50-155`, `src/fg_apply_tmpl.c:41-159`):**
   - **16-bit LFSR (`get_random_number`):** Updates `unsigned state` (`16`-bit nonzero seed; `seed ^ 0xb524` for Cb, `seed ^ 0x49d8` for Cr) via `bit = (r ^ (r >> 1) ^ (r >> 3) ^ (r >> 12)) & 1; state = (r >> 1) | (bit << 15)`, returning `(state >> (16 - bits)) & ((1 << bits) - 1)`. Initial white-noise synthesis draws `11`-bit indices into `dav1d_gaussian_sequence[2048]` (`int16_t`), rounded by `shift = 12 - bd + grain_scale_shift` across an **`82 × 73`** luma grid (`44 × 38` for 4:2:0 chroma).
   - **Strictly Causal 2D `AR(3)` Recursion:** Sweeps `y = 3..H-1, x = 3..W-4` over the `24`-tap causal neighborhood `(dy ∈ [-lag, 0], dx ∈ [-lag, +lag])` (`lag ∈ {0..3}`), updating `buf[y][x] = clip(buf[y][x] + round2(sum, ar_coeff_shift), -128 << (bd-8), (128 << (bd-8)) - 1)`. Because `buf[y][x]` depends immediately on `buf[y][x - 1]`, horizontal SIMD vectorization of the `AR(3)` feedback loop requires multi-row wavefront skewing or small-vector lookahead, whereas `generate_grain_uv` additionally couples a `25`th tap (`ar_coeffs_uv[24]`) to the downsampled collocated `LumaGrain` (`LumaGrain` **must** finish before `CbGrain`/`CrGrain` start!).
   - **Piecewise-Linear `scaling` LUT (`generate_scaling`, `fg_apply_tmpl.c:41-97`):** Interpolates up to 14 `(x, y)` control points into a `256` (8bpc), `1024` (10bpc), or `4096` (12bpc) byte table using exact integer reciprocal `delta = dy * ((0x10000 + (dx >> 1)) / dx)` (`dx >= 1` enforced by strict monotonic syntax parsing!).

2. **Row-Independent `32x32` Block Blending (`fgy_32x32xn` / `fguv_32x32xn`) and the Odd-Width `const`-Mutation Bug:**
   - Each `32`-luma-row stripe `row_num` derives its own deterministic PRNG seed `seed ^ (((row_num * 37 + 178) & 0xff) << 8) ^ ((row_num * 173 + 105) & 0xff)` (plus `row_num - 1` when `overlap_flag == 1`, `filmgrain_tmpl.c:192-196`), drawing one `8`-bit random offset `(offx, offy)` per `32x32` block and blending `2`-pixel luma borders with weights `{27, 17}` (`(27*old + 17*new + 16) >> 5`) and `1`-pixel subsampled chroma borders with `{23, 22}` (`>> 5`). Thus **all `32`-row stripes across a frame are 100% data-independent** once `grain_lut` and `scaling` are prepared!
   - **Normative Pre-Grain Luma Invariant & C `const`-Cast Hazard (`08.decoding.process.md:7073-7118`, `src/fg_apply_tmpl.c:174-203`, `src/fg_apply.rs:181-189`):** Chroma grain scaling indexes `scaling[val]` using `averageLuma` computed from **ungrained** luma (`in->data[0]`), NOT grained `out->data[0]`. However, look at `dav1d` `src/fg_apply_tmpl.c:174-202`: despite `const Dav1dPicture *const in`, `dav1d_apply_grain_row` casts `in->data[0]` to non-`const` `pixel *const luma_src` and, whenever `out->p.w & ss_x` (odd frame width in 4:2:0/4:2:2), **mutates `luma_src[out->p.w] = luma_src[out->p.w - 1]` inside the input reference picture** so SIMD `fguv_32x32xn` can load `luma[2*x + 1]` unconditionally! Inherited verbatim into `rav1d-safe` (`src/fg_apply.rs:186`: `let padding = &mut *(luma + (out.p.w as usize - 1)).slice_mut::<BD>(2); padding[1] = padding[0];`), this 8-line C shortcut forces the input reference picture to retain mutable `DisjointMut` interior mutability even after reconstruction and loop restoration have completely finished!

### 7. Eliminating `DisjointMut`: Contention Anatomy and Clean-Room Safe Ownership Architecture

`Basis: source` (`memorysafety/rav1d` `src/disjoint_mut.rs:34-156`; `imazen/rav1d-safe` `crates/rav1d-disjoint-mut/src/lib.rs:1-180`, `src/owned_recon.rs:1-1005`), `Basis: documentation` (`imazen/rav1d-safe` `docs/OWNERSHIP_MODELS.md:1-400`, `docs/TILED_SCALING.md:43-388`, `docs/DECODER_COMPARISON.md:69-84`, `docs/LAYOUT_CDEF.md:198-370`), and `Basis: derived`.

#### Why `DisjointMut` Exists in `rav1d` / `rav1d-safe` and What It Costs

Because `memorysafety/rav1d` mechanically C2Rust-translated `dav1d`'s shared-pointer `Dav1dPicture` architecture—where `'static` pool threads hold shared `&Rav1dFrameData` (`fc.data.try_read()`) while `Arc<Rav1dPictureData>` is simultaneously aliased in reference slots (`sr_cur`, DPB) and the output queue (`docs/OWNERSHIP_MODELS.md:85-91`)—no worker thread could obtain `&mut [u8]` from Rust's borrow checker.

1. **Two Unsoundness Defects in Unchecked / Naive `DisjointMut` (`rav1d` `src/disjoint_mut.rs:60-107`, `docs/OWNERSHIP_MODELS.md:184-203`):**
   - `memorysafety/rav1d` compiles out `DisjointMut` overlap checks in release mode (`#[cfg(debug_assertions)]`), asserting in `src/disjoint_mut.rs:67-72` that because pixel buffers are provenanceless, a release data race *"would only result in wrong results, and cannot result in memory safety."* Under the Rust and LLVM abstract machines, concurrent overlapping `&mut [u8]` / `&[u8]` references violate `noalias` and non-atomic memory-model invariants—**immediate Undefined Behavior**, not benign pixel corruption.
   - Worse, storing `slice: &'a mut V` directly inside `DisjointMutGuard` (`rav1d` `src/disjoint_mut.rs:99`) is **Stacked Borrows and Tree Borrows UB under Miri** whenever a guard is moved by value into a function (`rav1d-safe` PR `#478`): LLVM attaches a function-entry `noalias` protector to `&'a mut V` for the entire call even after `drop(guard)` inside the callee releases the lock and lets another thread retag those bytes! Soundness required storing `NonNull<V>` and materializing `&V`/`&mut V` exclusively inside `Deref`/`DerefMut`, plus exact 1-row or `row_mut(&mut self)` rectangle guards (`#469` proved that recording a 2D rectangle while handing out a contiguous hull `&mut [u8]` over unregistered stride gaps is also UB!).

2. **Measured Multi-Core and Single-Core Costs of Sound Runtime Tracking (`docs/DECODER_COMPARISON.md`, `docs/TILED_SCALING.md`, `docs/OWNERSHIP_MODELS.md`):**
   - **Initial shard-lock collapse:** Naive sharded `DisjointMut` regressed 4K inter decode from `97 ms` (`1` thread) to `178 ms` (`4` threads) under `~3.4M` borrow registrations/frame and `3.1M` contended spin-locks (`CDEF` per-row reads = `43%` of registrations, `84%` of contention).
   - **Residual cost after adaptive row-sharding (`#503`), `ReconBand` (`#482`), and mutable rectangles (`#505/#506`):** Even with borrow registrations halved (`-52%` via `DisjointMutRectGuard`, buying `-3.9%` to `-4.5%` wall at `t=8`, `docs/LAYOUT_CDEF.md:308-369`), `docs/TILED_SCALING.md:54-83, 366-387` isolates **two distinct penalties**:
     1. *Multi-core tracker spin inflation:* From `t=1` to `t=8` on 8-tile `1024x576` (`4K`), total CPU grows **`+27.3%` (`+10.5%`)** with the tracker vs **`+4.4%` (`+3.6%`)** tracker-free (`dav1d`: `+3.2%` / `+3.1%`), driven by `deblock_cols` inflating **`2.57x`** under `TinyLock::lock_slow` spin contention.
     2. *Single-core safe-guard copy-in/write-back tax (`4.0x` at 4K!):* Even with the tracker **compiled out (`untracked`) at `t=1`**, `rav1d-safe`'s filter chain takes **`14.57 ms/frame` vs `dav1d`'s `3.64 ms/frame` (`4.00x`; deblock alone `11.5 ms` vs `2.07 ms` = `5.6x`)** because narrow per-edge safe guards force `LfBlock::open` / `LfBlock::close` compact scratch copy-in and write-back (`60`-byte clearance between concurrent `LfBlock::fill` reads and `loopfilter.rs:887` writes prevents widening contiguous reservations, `docs/OWNERSHIP_MODELS.md:331-342`)!

#### Clean-Room Safe Ownership Architecture Without `DisjointMut`

`Basis: derived` from the verified AV1 normative dependencies (§§1–6) and `rav1d-safe`'s measured `stitch_sbrow` (`0.315 ms/frame` copy vs `19.7 ms/frame` tracker at 4K = **`60:1` net win**, `docs/OWNERSHIP_MODELS.md:171-178`):

A greenfield safe Rust AV1 decoder (`#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` outside optional audited SIMD intrinsics) **never needs `DisjointMut`**. By separating **exclusive mutable frame construction (`PlaneMut`)** from **published immutable reference/output frames (`Arc<FrozenPlane>`)**, Rust's static borrow checker (`&mut [T]` + `split_at_mut` / `chunks_mut`) proves disjointness at **zero runtime cost** across four typestate steps:

| Pipeline Step | Buffer Typestate & Partitioning Primitive | How Cross-Boundary Halo Reads Are Satisfied Safely (`0` Locks, `0` `unsafe`) |
|---|---|---|
| **Step A: Tile Reconstruction (`Intra` + `Inter` + `IntraBC`)** | **Per-tile or per-worker `ReconBand` (`Vec<[u8; 64]>`):** Each active tile worker owns a column-compact buffer (`stride` rounded up to `sb_cols` to absorb right-edge block overhang, `src/owned_recon.rs:922-927`). For `allow_intrabc == 1`, allocate one tile-height compact `TilePlane` per tile (`tiles` are 100% independent in IntraBC!). | Inter MC/Warp/OBMC reads completed reference frames as **`&FrozenPlane` (`&[u8]`)** — zero interior mutability! Intra/OBMC top-of-SB-row edges read the pre-saved immutable `ipred_edge` array (`src/recon.rs:2243`). At SB-row completion (or tile join), `stitch_sbrow` copies live pixels into `PlaneMut` (`0.315 ms/frame` at 4K). |
| **Step B: Deblocking & CDEF (`width[0]`)** | **Exclusive `PlaneMut` partitioned horizontally via `chunks_mut`:**<br>1. `DEBLOCK_COLS`: `plane_mut.rows_mut().chunks_mut(sb_h)` runs all `sby` vertical-edge passes **in parallel** on disjoint `&mut [T]` row bands (enforcing `lf_run_reach` so `y + reach <= (sby+1)*sb_h`).<br>2. `DEBLOCK_ROWS`: Either run sequential `sby = 0..sbh` (matching `dav1d`'s `deblock_progress` serialization while overlapping across frame threads, or parallelize across vertical tile-independent `32x32` transpose blocks before stitching).<br>3. Snapshot `2`-row `cdef_top` + `4`-row `lr_lpf_line` into immutable halo buffers `&HaloLines`.<br>4. `CDEF`: Partition `plane_mut` into **disjoint `&mut` SB-row slices** (`sby = 0..sbh` in parallel!). | Because each `CDEF(sby)` worker holds a contiguous `&mut [u8]` over its entire `(sb_h × stride)` band **plus** immutable `&HaloLines` for `y = -2..-1` and `y = sb_h..sb_h+1`, interior `8x8` blocks (`>95%` of the frame) index `row[y..y+10]` directly in `u8` SIMD **with zero `padding_8bpc` scratch copies and zero `LfBlock` copy-in/write-back**, closing both the `4.9x` CDEF cliff and the `4.0x` filter-chain tax! |
| **Step C: Superres, Loop Restoration (`width[1]`), & Freeze** | 1. If `use_superres`: `upscaled_mut.par_rows_mut()` reads `&plane_mut` (`width[0]`) into disjoint `&mut` output rows (`width[1]`), and upscales `HaloLines -> UpscaledLrHalo`.<br>2. `LOOP_RESTORATION`: Split `upscaled_mut` at `[56, 56+64, 56+128, ...]` (`StripeStartY`) via `split_at_mut` into **100% disjoint `64`-row `&mut` stripes** processed in parallel across all cores!<br>3. Extend odd-width luma right-edge pixel (`row[w] = row[w - 1]`) while `upscaled_mut` is still `&mut`, then freeze **`PlaneMut -> Arc<FrozenPlane>`**! | Each LR stripe worker owns `[StripeStartY .. StripeEndY + 1]` exclusively (`&mut`), maintains its own stack `pre_lr_border[2][136][4]` (`backup4xU`) across horizontal RUs, and reads out-of-stripe `±2` pre-CDEF rows from immutable `&UpscaledLrHalo`! Pre-extending `row[w]` before freezing eliminates the `fg_apply.rs:186` input-plane mutation permanently. |
| **Step D: Out-of-Place Film Grain** | Allocate fresh `OutPlaneMut` (only when `show_frame && film_grain_params_present`), run sequential `prep_grain` (`generate_grain_y` -> `generate_grain_uv`, `~0.05 ms`), and split `out_y`, `out_u`, `out_v` via **`chunks_mut(32 * stride)`** (`(32 >> ss_y) * uv_stride` for chroma) across worker threads! | Every `row_num` worker holds disjoint `&mut` output rows and shared immutable `&FrozenPlane` (`in_y, in_u, in_v`), `&GrainLut`, and `&ScalingLut` — achieving linear multi-core Film Grain scaling with zero locks! |

*Sub-frame low-latency note:* Even when `n_fc == 1` requires sub-frame pipelining (tile workers on `sby + 2` running concurrently with `Deblock`/`CDEF`/`LR` on `sby`), the exact same lock-free `&mut [u8]` strip kernels apply by storing the in-flight frame as a `Vec<Box<[u8]>>` of **`8`-row or `32`-row horizontal slab allocations** owned by a central scheduler channel (`slab` ownership moves linearly: `TileStitch -> DeblockCols -> DeblockRows(pair of slabs) -> Cdef -> Superres -> LrStripe`), copying into a contiguous `FrozenPlane` (or `IoVec` scatter-gather output) only once at `LR` write-out!

### 8. Clean-Room Verification Priority Stack

## Boundaries

1. **Hardware and workload specificity of cited `rav1d-safe` timings (`Basis: documentation`):** The `4.9x` CDEF / `7x` padding breakdown (`9.8 ms` vs `2.0 ms`, `docs/SAFE_VS_DAV1D.md:38-45`) and negative-stride warp cost (`+0.89%`, `docs/MC_WARP_DESTINATION_ROWS.md:127-135`) were measured by upstream `rav1d-safe` on AMD Zen 4 (Linux, Rust `1.98`–`1.99` fat-LTO), while the `t=8` tiled scaling and `4.0x` filter-chain measurements (`docs/TILED_SCALING.md`, `docs/LAYOUT_CDEF.md`) were recorded on Apple M4 Pro (macOS `aarch64`, Rust `1.97.1`, 8-bit 4:2:0). Furthermore, `docs/LAYOUT_CDEF.md:19-31` documents a `±1.37%` single-threaded code-placement ("layout lottery") variance across binary perturbations unless stabilized with `-C llvm-args=-align-all-functions=4` (`0.18%` spread).
2. **External C-FFI custom picture allocators (`dav1d_Context` `Rav1dPicAllocator`):** In `dav1d`'s C ABI (`ffmpeg-c-abi-libav-struct-layout-callbacks-and-rust-ffi-compatibility-layer`), downstream callers may supply raw-pointer picture buffers with arbitrary positive or negative strides and external lifetimes. Zero-`unsafe` `PlaneMut -> Arc<FrozenPlane>` ownership applies natively to Rust-allocated buffers; supporting external C-FFI `alloc_picture_callback` requires an explicit FFI boundary shim that validates buffer byte length, `64`-byte alignment, non-aliasing across planes, and signed stride reach once at allocation time.
3. **Frame threading (`n_fc > 1`) with inter-frame reference progress:** When `n_fc > 1` decodes frame $N+1$'s inter blocks concurrently while frame $N$ is still completing Loop Restoration on lower stripes (`sr_cur.progress[1]`), frame $N$'s plane cannot be frozen as a whole-frame `&[u8]` until frame $N$ completes; sub-frame frame-threading requires **stripe-granular `FrozenStripe` publication** (`OnceLock<Box<[u8]>>` or `Arc` slab array indexed by `sby`, matching `sr_cur.progress[1]`!) rather than whole-plane `Vec<u8>`sharing.
