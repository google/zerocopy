*Authored by an AI agent acting on joshlf's behalf.*

## Summary

This report documents the minimal-TCB verification architecture (`google/zerocopy#4208`) for verifying unmodified `rust-lang/rust` standard library crates (`library/{core,alloc,std}`) and `model-checking/verify-rust-std` targets under Anneal (`google/zerocopy@ccf9caacb6abc54b8b2cfd6db114f02cb87276e5`) with Receipt Schema v3 attestations replayable across independent Lean 4 kernels (`lean4`, `nanoda`, `con-ron`):

1. **Opt-In Stdlib Capture Seams Without 3P TCB Relaxation:** `StdlibTargetConfig` (`--stdlib-crate <core|alloc|std>`, `--allow-local-lang-items`, hashed `--extern`) keeps default third-party `cargo-anneal verify` builds strictly closed while admitting `core`'s `182` `#[lang]` items (`#![no_core]`, validated structurally via `AdtDef`/`layout_of`), `alloc`'s `3` lang items, and `std`'s `6` lang items (`--crate-type rlib`, `STD_ENV_ARCH`), banning `--cfg miri`, staging `library/` sibling mounts (`../../stdarch/...`, `../../portable-simd/...`), classifying `/rustc/<sha>/` diagnostic reads as non-authoritative `NotFound`, and running `--emit=metadata` + selected-closure `required_consts`.
2. **Soundness Across Four Stdlib Semantics Traps:** (i) `#![rustc_preserve_ub_checks]` (`66` sites in `core`, `3` in `alloc`) emits `Operand::RuntimeChecks(UbChecks)` modeled via `ubChecksMode` staging (`"release_only"` in `M0a`/`M0b` $\to$ mandatory demonic `Choice {false, true}` in `M1` matching `lean-miri`'s `Hooks.runtimeChecks`); (ii) `#[rustc_inherit_overflow_checks]` (`55` sites in `core`) maps `AssertKind::is_optional_overflow_check()` to dual-config `∀ overflowChecks : Bool` with condition-read evaluated before `ignored || expected == value` (`ControlFlow.lean:195-200`, `0` patches to `vendor/lean-miri`); (iii) `#[rustc_nounwind]` (`NEVER_UNWIND`) maps `UnwindAction::{Terminate, Unreachable}`; (iv) `-Zcontract-checks=no` residual `core::contracts` `#[ensures]` `Option<{closure}>` locals quarantine unreachable closures as `MirTy.opaqueDead`.
3. **Four-Tier `#[rustc_intrinsic]` Stratification & `const_eval_select`:** Separates pre-`PostCleanup`-lowered ops, native MIR nodes (`BinOp::*Unchecked`, `Assume`, `CopyNonOverlapping`), a closed `CoreIntrinsicOp` sum type (never executing fallback MIR for `must_be_overridden` ops like `is_val_statically_known`), and an allowlist of `#[miri::intrinsic_fallback_is_spec]` pure fallbacks (`cold_path`, `rotate_{left,right}`, `unchecked_funnel_{shl,shr}`, `disjoint_bitor`, `carrying_mul_add`, `select_unpredictable`). `const_eval_select` (`29` non-test sites in `core`/`alloc`) executes `rt_fn` at runtime and `ctfe_fn` in `ConstMirMachine`; scalar validity combines `WrappingRange` with explicit `.charUnicode` (`≤ 0x10FFFF ∧ ¬(0xD800..=0xDFFF)`).
4. **Phased Delivery (`M0a` $\to$ `M0b` $\to$ `M1` $\to$ `M2`) & Settled Policy (`D1`–`D6`):** `M0a` covers call-free scalars (`int_log10::u8_impl`, `midpoint`, `utf8_{first_byte,acc_cont_byte,is_cont_byte}`, `Ordering::{reverse,then}`); `M0b` adds calls + niche ADTs (`verify-rust-std` Challenge 12 `NonZero` and Challenge 30 `ilog`); `M1` gates polymorphic `core::{slice,ptr,mem}` behind universal `<T: ?Sized>` (`#4102`, rejecting bounded-type matrices like `verify-rust-std#734`); `M2` covers `UnsafeCell`, `alloc`, unwind, RC11 atomics, and explicit `tcb_shims[]` OS PAL contracts.

## Findings

### Stdlib Capture Seams (`#![no_core]`, Staging, `RecordingLoader`, Split Metadata)

Basis: `source` (`anneal/trusted/{rustc-capture/src/{capture,extract}.rs,cargo-anneal/src/verify.rs}`, `library/{core,alloc,std}`) + `execution` (`rustc@f8a08b688cbe` `Runtime(PostCleanup)` dumps).

1. **Scoped `StdlibTargetConfig` (`D1-A`):** Default `capture.rs:922-931` refuses local `LangItem` definitions and `capture.rs:463-468` bans `-L` ambient search. Under `--stdlib-crate <core|alloc|std>` / `--allow-local-lang-items`, `extract.rs` validates semantic lang items (`sized`, `manually_drop`, `maybe_uninit`, `unsafe_cell`, `owned_box`, `panic`, `panic_fmt`, `panic_nounwind`, `panic_impl`, `const_panic_fmt`) and diagnostic items (`NonZero` at `nonzero.rs:127`) structurally (`#[repr(transparent)]`, single payload, `is_unsafe_cell()`, `tcx.layout_of`), admits explicit hashed `--extern <name>=<path>` (`(stable_crate_id, crate_hash, file_sha256)` logged in Receipt v3 `target_bindings`), admits `--crate-type rlib` + `--env STD_ENV_ARCH` for `std`, and **forbids `--cfg miri`** (`core/src/ub_checks.rs` suppresses `check_language_ub()` when `cfg!(miri)` holds).
2. **Multi-Root Staging & Diagnostic-Safe `RecordingLoader`:** `library/core/src/lib.rs` mounts `../../stdarch/crates/core_arch/...` and `../../portable-simd/crates/core_simd/...`, whereas `verify.rs:1119-1126` rejects `..` in individual paths. Generalizing `stage_rustc_rust_inputs` (`verify.rs:1139-1174`) with a declared `workspace_root` (`<rust-src>/library`) stages `core/src/**`, `stdarch/**`, `portable-simd/**`, and `std/` hermetically. Meanwhile, `RecordingLoader::read_path` (`capture.rs:147-162`) treats failed diagnostic snippet probes under `/rustc/<sha>/` (`real_rust_source_base_dir`) outside staged sources as non-authoritative `NotFound` without recording `reads.refusals` or polluting `SourceFile` hashes.
3. **Selection-Only `--emit=metadata`:** Replacing `--emit=metadata,obj` (`capture.rs:1309`) with `--emit=metadata` under `Scope::SelectedUnit` and evaluating `required_consts` over the transitive closure of `--select` / `--instantiate` roots avoids unselected LLVM codegen ICEs while preserving `Scope::Crate` full-closure checks (`ir.rs:1057`, `verify.rs:593`).

### Stdlib Semantics Traps: `UbChecks`, Overflow, `NEVER_UNWIND`, and `core::contracts`

Basis: `source` (`vendor/lean-miri/Miri/Interpreter/{OperandMachine,ControlFlow}.lean`, `rustc_codegen_ssa/src/mir/block.rs:708-722`, `rustc_ast_lowering/src/contract.rs`) + `execution` (`rustc@f8a08b688cbe` MIR dumps of `char::convert::from_u32_unchecked`, `precondition_check`, `int_log10::{u8_impl, i128}`, `#[core::contracts::ensures]`).

1. **`Operand::RuntimeChecks(UbChecks)` (`D2-B -> D2-A`):** `core` (`66` sites) and `alloc` (`3` sites) set `#![rustc_preserve_ub_checks]`, keeping `Operand::RuntimeChecks(UbChecks)` even at `-Copt-level=3 -Zub-checks=no`. Evaluating `UbChecks = true` alone yields false safety (failing in `precondition_check` while executing UB at `-Zub-checks=no`). Adding `.runtimeChecks (kind : RuntimeChecksKind)` (`.ubChecks` admitted; `.contractChecks` / `.overflowChecks` fail closed) lets `ubChecksMode = .releaseFalse` (`"release_only"` in M0a/M0b Receipts, pruning the `!= 0` `precondition_check` `&str`/`Arguments::from_str` arm) transition cleanly to mandatory `.demonic` (`Choice {false, true}`, matching `lean-miri`'s `Hooks.runtimeChecks`) in `M1`.
2. **`#[rustc_inherit_overflow_checks]` (`55` sites in `core`, `D3-A`):** Backend codegen (`block.rs:708-722`) deletes `AssertKind::is_optional_overflow_check()` panics in `-Coverflow-checks=no` callers, wrapping arithmetic instead. `ControlFlow.evalTerminator` (`ControlFlow.lean:195-200`) computes `ignored`, **reads and validates `condition` first** (`readAssertCondition` — faulting on uninitialized/invalid `bool`), and then branches on `if ignored || expected == value`. `Trusted.MirSemantics.Step` (`Step.lean:744`) must preserve this exact read-first ordering (`FaultEquivalenceClaim`) with `MirSemantics.Config.overflowChecks : Bool` mapped to `ignoreOptionalOverflowChecks := !overflowChecks` (`0` patches to `vendor/lean-miri`), proving `BodyVerified` for both `false` and `true`.
3. **`#[rustc_nounwind]` (`NEVER_UNWIND`):** Admitted at `extract.rs:898` with `UnwindAction` (`Continue | Unreachable | Terminate | Cleanup(BasicBlock)`). `Terminate` maps unwinding to `.fault .panic`; `Unreachable` requires verified `NoUnwind` (`NEVER_UNWIND` or `M2` `tcb_shims[]`), stepping dynamic unwinds to `.fault .undefined`.
4. **`core::contracts` (`#128044`) & `verify-rust-std` `#[safety::*]`:** Under `-Zcontract-checks=no`, `contract.rs` emits `switchInt(const false)` guarding `contract_check_{requires,ensures}` and allocates `_ret_check: Option<{closure}>` receiving `Option::None` on a reachable block for `#[ensures]`. `Spec.lean` stays authoritative (`core::contracts` closures enforce `C: 'static` `E0597`, rejecting `&T`/`&mut T`, and `verify-rust-std` runtime macros are no-ops). Pruning literal `SwitchInt(const false)` edges maps unconstructed unreachable closure types to `MirTy.opaqueDead` and `#[ensures]` locals to `Option<opaqueDead>`.

### `#[rustc_intrinsic]` Stratification, `WrappingRange` + `.charUnicode`, and `const_eval_select`

Basis: `source` (`vendor/lean-miri/Miri/{Interpreter/{Intrinsics,Call}.lean,ABI/WrappingRange.lean}`, `library/core/src/{intrinsics/mod,num/{niche_types,nonzero},ub_checks}.rs`) + `execution` (`rustc@f8a08b688cbe` MIR dumps).

- **Four-Tier `#[rustc_intrinsic]` (`311 + 1` declarations):**
  1. **Pre-`PostCleanup` lowered (`M0a`):** `wrapping_add`, `add_with_overflow`, `three_way_compare`, `unreachable`, `forget`, `const { size_of / align_of }`, `abort`, `unchecked_div` $\to$ `BinOp::Div`, `unchecked_rem` $\to$ `BinOp::Rem`.
  2. **Dedicated native MIR nodes:** `BinOp::{addUnchecked, subUnchecked, mulUnchecked, shlUnchecked, shrUnchecked}` (`Rvalues.lean:55-60`, `M0a`); `StatementKind::{assume, copyNonOverlapping}`, `Operand::runtimeChecks` (`M0a`/`M0b`); `CastKind::transmute` (`M0b`/`M1`), `Rvalue::Discriminant`, `BinOp::Offset` (`M1`/`M0b`).
  3. **Closed `CoreIntrinsicOp` (never runs fallback MIR):** `M0` pure (`ctlz`, `ctlz_nonzero`, `cttz`, `cttz_nonzero`, `ctpop`, `bswap`, `bitreverse`, `saturating_add`, `saturating_sub`, `exact_div`, `is_val_statically_known` via demonic `Choice` matching `Intrinsics.lean:509-511`, `assert_{inhabited,zero_valid,mem_uninitialized_valid}`); `M1` memory/const (`copy`, `write_bytes`, `compare_bytes`, `raw_eq`, `arith_offset`, `ptr_offset_from`, `ptr_offset_from_unsigned`, `ptr_mask`, `offset_of`, `discriminant_value`, `const_eval_select`); `M2` (`atomic_*`, SIMD). Note: executing `is_val_statically_known`'s fallback body (`false`) would miss bugs on `u32::checked_ilog`'s constant-known path (`E5`).
  4. **Allowlisted `#[miri::intrinsic_fallback_is_spec]` fallbacks (`Call.lean:548-556`):** `cold_path` (`likely`/`unlikely`), `rotate_{left,right}`, `unchecked_funnel_{shl,shr}`, `disjoint_bitor`, `carrying_mul_add`, `select_unpredictable`. Unlisted fallback items (`const_deallocate`) fail closed.
- **`WrappingRange` + `.charUnicode`:** `rustc_abi::WrappingRange` represents `NonZero*Inner` niches (`pattern_type!`) including wrap-around `lo > hi` (`NonZeroI*Inner`), but models `char` as `0..=0x10FFFF` without excluding surrogates `0xD800..=0xDFFF`. `Trusted.MirSemantics.Bytes` retains `.charUnicode` alongside `.wrappingRange (lo hi : BitVec 128)`.
- **`const_eval_select` (`29` non-test sites: `24` `core`, `5` `alloc`, `D4-A`):** Captured as `CoreIntrinsicOp.constEvalSelect(args, ctfeFn, rtFn)`. `MirSemantics` runs `rtFn`, `Trusted.Correspondence.Codec` lowers runtime `constEvalSelect` directly to a `Call` to `rtFn` for `M_ref` (`0` patches to `vendor/lean-miri`), and `ConstMirMachine` runs `ctfeFn`. Requiring `ctfeFn == rtFn` equivalence (`E8`) would reject `ub_checks::check_language_ub` (`ctfe_fn = false` vs. `rt_fn = !cfg!(miri)`).

### Eliminated Alternatives (`E1`–`E8`) & TCB Budget (`M0a`–`M2`)
