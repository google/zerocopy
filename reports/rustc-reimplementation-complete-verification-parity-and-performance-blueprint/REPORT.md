### 2. The 9-Rung Qualification & Parity Gate DAG (`Rung 0` through `Rung 8`)

#### 2.1 Surface Partition ($\mathcal{S}$) and Gate DAG Topology

Let $\mathcal{S} = \mathcal{S}_{\text{FE}} \sqcup \mathcal{S}_{\text{HYB}} \sqcup \mathcal{S}_{\text{BE}} \sqcup \mathcal{S}_{\text{LLVM}}$ partition all observable `rustc` + `rustdoc` behaviors:

#### 3.1 Formal Semantics of the Three `--compat` Modes

A verified compiler faces an apparent tension: compiling a first-party safety-critical crate demands strict adherence to the Rust specification with zero backdoors, whereas compiling that crate's transitive `Cargo.lock` dependency graph from `crates.io` requires accepting historical quirks that `rustc` deliberately tolerates under `--cap-lints=allow`. Factoring compatibility into an explicit, per-invocation CLI flag `--compat=<mode>` resolves this tension cleanly without compromising soundness:

1. **`--compat=strict-spec` (High-Assurance First-Party Verification Mode):**
   - Enforces the normative Rust language specification (`rust-lang/reference` + `rust-lang/fls`) and the modern `NextSolverConfig::Globally` + `Polonius::Next` trait/borrow rules.
   - Escalates all `40` `FutureReleaseError` and `2` `Custom` FCWs to **uncapped hard errors** (even if `--cap-lints=allow` is passed), rejects `RUSTC_BOOTSTRAP` (or marks the verification certificate `TAINTED_UNSTABLE_BOOTSTRAP`), and permits layout randomization (`-Zrandomize-layout`) to prove absence of layout-assumption UB.
2. **`--compat=rustc-fcw` (Default Ecosystem Dependency Mode — Automatic Under `--cap-lints=allow`):**
   - Preserves 100% memory, type, lifetime, and operational-semantics soundness while modeling all **`69` `@future_incompatible` lints** as genuine lints inside `LintStore` rather than hard parser/typeck errors.
   - When Cargo passes `--cap-lints=allow --json=future-incompat` on external dependencies, clamps all `69` FCWs (including the `20` `Deny`-by-default FCWs such as `conflicting_repr_hints`, `ill_formed_attribute_input`, `varargs_without_pattern`, `unstable_imports`, and `no_link_attributes`) to `Level::Allow` (`exit 0`) while emitting `{"$message_type": "future_incompat", ...}` for the `21` lints with `report_in_deps: true`.
3. **`--compat=rustc-hyrum` (Strict Build-Script, Tooling, & Mixed-`.rlib` Interoperability Mode):**
   - Extends `rustc-fcw` by pinning every unspecified Hyrum's-Law representation and CLI output surface scraped by `build.rs` scripts (`rustc_version`, `autocfg`, `cc`), `cargo fix`, profilers, or mixed-compiler static libraries: `rustc`'s exact `#[repr(Rust)]` niche-biased field-sorting algorithm, `(addr, metadata)` wide-pointer order, `SymbolManglingVersion::V0` (`_R...`), 7-line `rustc -vV` stdout grammar, stderr-only `--print=native-static-libs`, `.d` space-only filename escaping, and `proc_macro` `InvisibleOrigin::MetaVar` vs. `InvisibleOrigin::ProcMacro` `Delimiter::None` precedence.

### 4. Master Multi-Dimensional Performance Scorecard Specification (Compiler, Verification, & Generated Code)
