## Remaining gates and decisions

The dominant gap is an **implemented Anneal V2 owner** joining Rust annotations, Charon/Aeneas output, Lake preparation, Lean workers and editor/MCP requests. The component reports intentionally cannot authenticate one end-to-end source/model/obligation identity, prove product-level stale publication fences, establish one batch/live engine, or compare the architectural alternatives in a real service. They do yield concrete acceptance and falsification fixtures for that implementation.

Other boundaries remain explicit in the row ledgers: OCaml/Dune/opam would be needed for same-process Aeneas integration and were not installed; a later compatible Lake/Lean tuple, a real Lean MCP adapter/client, another independent host, and native ext4/Windows/network filesystem subjects are unavailable in this cached setup. Human comprehension requires a frozen interface and consenting participants. The 8-GiB host's guarded full-chain two-worker cell was previously rejected; tiny 1/2/4/8 direct-Lean results cannot substitute for representative concurrent Anneal jobs. Remote execution remains conditional on an actual measured need. These are **gates**, not inferred failures or approvals to install external dependencies.

The 318-row challenge did not identify another *distinct, safely executable cached-only* cell beyond the 14 packaged here that would materially close a current residual. This is a bounded audit conclusion, not a proof that no other experiment can ever be designed. In particular, repeating tiny fixtures with more parameter values does not answer their remaining product, representative-load or platform questions. The row ledgers preserve exact next work rather than classifying component evidence as complete.
