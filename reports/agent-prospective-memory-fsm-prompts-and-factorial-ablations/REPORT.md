# Conditional Progressive-Disclosure Trigger Recall, Executable Finite-State Control Prompts, Sample-Efficient Component Ablations, and Cross-Checkpoint Scaffolding Retirement

## Summary

Primary-source inspection of 28 cognitive-psychology, statistical experimental-design, software-debugging, and language-model agent studies (1946–2026), twelve pinned public repositories (`genglinliu/PMBench`, `KristenZHANG/TriggerBench-Official`, `virgiliojr94/book-to-skill`, `yiranwu0/StateFlow`, `Justherozen/FlowBench`, `zjunlp/WorfBench`, `MadryLab/context-cite`, `uds-se/debuggingbook`, `gostevehoward/confseq`, `strongSoda/prompt-sculpting`, `SWE-agent/mini-swe-agent`, and `OpenAutoCoder/live-swe-agent`), and a deterministic four-probe Python 3.10+ verification suite establishes four engineering invariants governing deferred skill-reference recall, finite-state procedural enforcement, combinatorial prompt/skill screening, and scaffolding retirement across model upgrades:

1. **Event-Based Prospective Memory Collapses Long Before Retrospective Retention, While `Stage 1 → 2 → 3` Progressive Disclosure Is Harness- and Task-Conditioned (`PM-Bench`, `TriggerBench`, `Mittal 2026`, `LoongDoc`):** Across 8 models on `PM-Bench` (`Liu & Gabriel 2026`), clock-scheduled triggers average **`46.4%`** hit rate versus **`3.3%`** on non-clock state/event triggers (`Figure 3`; `1.7%` even with an in-context TODO ledger; `Auto-heartbeat 30m` lifts non-clock hits to `15.8%` while inflating micro false positives `199 → 489` and dropping Macro Set-F1 `60.0% → 57.8%` [`Table 2`] and Micro Set-F1 `59.4% → 51.5%`). On `TriggerBench` (`Zhang et al. 2026`), `GPT-4o` explicit retrospective recall remains **$\ge 98\%$** at `100K` tokens while autonomous prospective trigger execution drops from **`> 90%` to `< 40%`** (`Fig. 4`); making state-tracking cues explicit (`Table 4`) lifts `GPT-4.1` `Pos-Clean` by **`+42.6 pp`** (Base) and **`+49.1 pp`** (`40K`) **at the cost of degrading `Neg-Clean` false-alarm suppression by `-9.3 pp` (`82.4% → 73.2%`) and `-10.2 pp` (`83.3% → 73.2%`)** (`Table 19`), while embedding `RAG` (`51.62%`), `Mem0` (`53.20%`), and `A-MEM` (`41.42%`) trail plain `GPT-4.1` long-context (`60.0%`, `Table 3 & §4.2`). Dual-task interference (`Mittal 2026`) lowers compliance by `-9.8 pp` (`DeepSeek-V3.1`), `-4.8 pp` (`o4-mini`, `3/4` single tasks non-significant), and `-3.2 pp` (`Llama-3.3-70B`) across 4 single foreground tasks (`-10.2 pp`, `-8.9 pp`, and `-6.0 pp` across `Table 8`'s 6-condition `1×–5×` means), hitting **Terminal** (`end_checker`, `-50 pp`), **Structural** (`json_format`, `-50 pp`), and **Counting** (`number_bullet_lists`, `-44 pp`) constraints (`Table 5`; $n = 6$ items/type in `Table 2`). Typed external lifecycle schemas (`PIS`, `82.9%` on `DeepSeek-Chat`, `66.2%` on `Gemma-E2B` exceeding `PM-Bench`'s 8-model `heartbeat-proactive` mean `65.1%` while trailing `GPT-5.4` `79.1%`) and 3-stage `PRO-MEMORY` (`0.7324` 5-backbone mean) restore execution. For `SKILL.md` progressive disclosure, `Stage 1 → 2` lazy loading (`Zhang et al. 2026`) cuts `Qwen3-14B` prompt tokens by `74.4%–81.7%` and avoids `100%` overflow crashes at $N = 100$ (`+0.26 s`, `1.75 s → 2.01 s` latency on **`Qwen3-8B` at $N = 50$**), while `LoongDoc` (`He et al. 2026`, `Tables 1–3`) shows `Stage 1 → 2 → 3` (`flat`: frontmatter $\to$ chunk-summary + entity TOC $\to$ `references/`) wins multi-book English QA at $K = 20$ (`Codex` `0.462` vs. `0.267` `hierarchical` / `0.257` `raw`; `Claude-Code` `0.354` vs. `0.302` / `0.301`) and avoids `hierarchical`'s $K = 1$ `Pi` collapse (`-20` to `-36 pp`), **whereas `hierarchical` beats both `flat` and `raw` on `Claude-Code` `En.MC` $K = 20$ (`0.521` vs. `0.482` / `0.459`) and `Pi` `Zh.QA` $K = 20$ (`0.330` vs. `0.137` `flat` < `0.287` `raw`)**.
2. **Executable Finite-State Control Requires Both State-Entry Guards and Terminal Stop Gates—Plus Per-Instance Binding and Sentinel-Bridged DAG Scoring (`StateFlow`, `FlowBench`, `Sun et al. 2026`, `WorFBench`, `Probe 4`):** Deterministic Python state transitions in `StateFlow` (`Wu et al. 2024`) lift `InterCode-SQL` (`50.68% → 63.73%` `GPT-3.5`, `60.16% → 69.34%` `GPT-4`) and `ALFWorld` (`55.5% → 83.3% / 88.8%`) at `2.5×–5×` lower cost, where deleting the dedicated `Observe` state (`No_Observe`, `Figure 4 & Table 7`) drops SQL accuracy `-5.90 pp` and inflates errors `6.82% → 16.95%` (though source inspection reveals `flow_bash.py:107–113` forces `solve → verify` before `submit` whereas `flow_sql.py:119–122` permits unguarded `select → submit`). Prompt-only `FlowBench` flowcharts (`Xiao et al. 2024`) lift single-turn Tool F1 (`66.3% → 75.5%`) yet stall at `40.1%–42.7%` multi-turn session success. On 200-item unattended batches (`Sun et al. 2026`), `Claude Haiku 4.5` rises from **`27.8%` (`FC = 48.4 ± 52.6`, `Remind-loop`) to `94.8%` (`FC = 139.2 ± 33.8`, `FSM`, `+129.9` over `Plain`, $p = 0.008`) across `8` main seeds (`Table 1`)**, while `3`-seed orthogonal guard ablations (`Table 2`) prove `Stop-only` induces **Goodhart blind fabrication** (`3.7%` compliance, `FC = 5.7`, `108.3` blind decisions, `146.7` decided due to `1/3` session context overflow) and `Gates-only` exhausts the `50`-resumption harness cap (`44.7` stop attempts, `151.7` decided vs. `Remind-loop` `200.0` decided at `44.0%, FC = 76.3` and `FSM` `93.3%, FC = 128.0`), with `3/8` `FSM` sessions still exploiting multi-account batching inside state `s2` (`§4 & Appendix C`). Source audit and `Probe 4` further expose three live evaluator bugs in `zjunlp/WorfBench@38a64555` (`k >= 8` self-match recall capped at `0.8000` on `16.0%` of `alfworld` and `14.9%` of `wikihow`; raw-index edge matching scoring `100%` isomorphic fork DAGs at `F1 = 0.5000`; positive `F1 > 0` on `0`-match graphs) and verify a sentinel-bridge-preserving `t_eval_graph` repair scoring `F1 = 1.0000` on parallel fan-out/fan-in fork-join DAGs and `0.0000` on `0`-match workflows.
3. **Orthogonal Foldover Screening, Memoized `ddmin`, Uniform-Mask Banzhaf Surrogates, and Power-Matched Sequential Stopping Avoid `LOO` Blindness and Peeking Inflation (`Probes 1–4`):** At $N = k + 1 = 12$ runs ($k = 11$ binary clauses), `Plackett–Burman` (`PB12`) reduces half-effect estimator variance by **`6.0×` ($N/2$)** vs. 12-run `LOO` ($\sigma^2/12$ vs. $\sigma^2/2$, $\rho = 0$ vs. `+0.5`) and eliminates `LOO`'s incident-interaction blindness ($A_{j,(jm)} = +1 \implies 0.0$ on redundant `OR` guardrails), while a `24`-run mirror foldover (`Resolution IV`) cancels all `605` two-way aliases identically ($A_{j,(lm)}^{\text{fold}} \equiv 0$, vs. `45` $|A| = 1/3$ aliases in `PB12` and `3–5` $|A| = 1$ aliases in `Sylvester H16`, `Probe 1`). In `Zeller & Hildebrandt (2002)` (`Fig. 5`, `p. 187`), 2-phase `ddmin` guarantees 1-minimality (`Proposition 11`, `p. 188`), $\le 2\log_2 n$ single-defect isolation (`Proposition 13`, `p. 188`), and uncached worst-case complexity $\le n^2 + 3n$ (`Proposition 12`, `p. 188`, `304, 1,120, 4,288` at $n = 16, 32, 64$; exact DP global maximum $n^2 + 3n - 10 = 294, 1,110, 4,278$), while `Fig. 6` query memoization tightens monotone unique oracle evaluations to $\le \frac{n^2 + 3n - 2}{2}$ (`85, 241, 745` vs. uncached `178, 618, 2,266`, `3.04×` cut at $n = 64$), and compensatory non-monotone clauses (`pushy_c1` masked by `guard_c2` until `[guard_c2, bad_c4]` drop together) delete the true defect `bad_c4` (`Probe 2`). In `ContextCite` (`MadryLab/context-cite@c11f8ace`), population uniform-mask ($\alpha_{\text{keep}} = 0.5$) OLS equals **exact Banzhaf values** (`1.0000` vs. `KernelSHAP`/Shapley `1.3333` on 3-way `AND`; `+2.0000` vs. `0.0000` `LOO` on `OR`; `0.0000` on `XOR`), rescuing `OR` pairs in **`98.0%`** of $M = 64$ LASSO runs (`alpha = 0.01` and `0.05`, `Probe 3`). Under continuous monitoring ($n = 5 \dots 200, \alpha = 0.05$), naive $z$-peeking inflates Type-I FPR to **`36.52%`**, whereas Ville's inequality ($\Lambda_n \ge 20$) bounds null FPR to **`2.26%`** (Gaussian `mSPRT`, fixed $\tau^2 = 0.25$) and **`3.00%`** (`0.030043`, exact Beta-Binomial McNemar DP), achieving **`19.0%` (`69.66` vs. `86` at $N_{\max} = 142, 90.28\%$ power)** and **`33.6%` (`23.23` vs. power-matched $m_{\text{fixed}} = 35, 96.17\%$ vs. `96.56%` power; `53.55%` vs. $n_{\max} = 50$ cap)** true power-matched sample savings (`Probe 4`).
4. **Legacy Prompt Heuristics and Heavy Editing Wrappers Compress or Invert Across Model Upgrades (`2024 → 2026`):** Prompt-technique aging is non-monotonic and family-specific (`Rudyk et al. 2026`); rule-heavy `"Sculpting"` on `GPT-5` (`N = 1,317` `GSM8K`) ties `Zero Shot` (`94.00%`) and trails Simple CoT (`96.36%`, `-2.36 pp`; `Khan 2025`); expert personas fail to improve factual accuracy (`Basil et al. 2025`, except `Gemini 2.0 Flash` Engineering/Chemistry and `GPT-4o` Law) and reduce `MMLU` (`71.6% → 68.0%`, `Hu, Rostami, & Thomason 2026`); frozen cross-model prompt transfer (`PromptBridge`, `Wang et al. 2025`) drops `TravelPlanner` `o3` Delivery Rate from `93.33%` to `52.67%` (`100.0%` calibrated) and forfeits `+12.60 pp` (`+37.72%` relative: `33.40%` vs. `46.00%`, `Original 32.00%`) on `SWE-Bench Verified` (`Table 8`); and matched-model `SWE-bench Verified` lift over the minimal single-bash `mini-SWE-agent` loop compresses by **`46%–60%` in one generation** (`SWE-agent` `+9.60 → +4.07 pp`; `OpenHands` `+13.60 → +5.47 pp`; `OpenCode` `~6 → 0.6 pp` on `SWE-bench Pro` with bidirectional `2.89×` vs. `0.63×` prompt-token trade-offs, while a lightweight `NanoHarness` recovers `+6.21` to `+7.37 pp` on `ProgramBench`, `Hu et al. 2026`).

## Findings

### 1. Event-Based Prospective Memory and Conditional `Stage 1 → 2 → 3` Progressive Disclosure

`Basis: documentation + source`

#### 1.1 Multiprocess Prospective Memory Grounding and Multi-Turn Agent Benchmarks

1. **Focal vs. Non-Focal Event Cues (`McDaniel & Einstein, 2000`, *Appl. Cogn. Psychol.* 14(7):S127–S144, `doi:10.1002/acp.775`):** Remembering to execute a delayed intention (`when Y occurs, read/invoke X`) relies on automatic associative retrieval only when ongoing foreground processing directly attends to the **focal cue**. Peripheral, relational, or post-implementation conditions (**non-focal cues**, such as opening `references/<doc>.md` upon touching a cross-module invariant) require continuous strategic monitoring that competes for working attention against foreground reasoning. Whereas humans struggle more on clock-based triggers than focal event triggers, LLM harnesses invert this asymmetry whenever turn headers inject wall-clock timestamps without pushing environmental state changes (`Liu & Gabriel 2026`).
2. **Empirical Prospective-Memory Benchmarks and Intention Stores (`2026`):**
   - **`PM-Bench` (`Liu & Gabriel, COLM 2026`, `arXiv:2607.12385v1`; `genglinliu/PMBench@e1093c47`, `data/synthetic_week_v9.json`, `runs/all_results_v9/experiment_output_comparison_report.md`, `8` models $\times$ `8` setups):** Under `single-baseline`, Macro Set-F1 is **`60.0%`** (Micro `59.4%`), clock trigger hit rate is **`46.4%`**, and aggregate non-clock proactive hit rate is **`3.3%`** (`Figure 3`; Appendix `Table 7` per-channel hit rates: `Bank 0.0%`, `Email 0.0%`, `Waitlist 0.0%`, `Shipment 0.0%`, `Calendar 4.2%`, `Appt. 8.3%`, `Course 12.5%`). Adding a passive in-context TODO ledger (`single-todo-ledger`) raises Macro Set-F1 to `62.8%` by cutting false positives (`199 → 134` FP, precision `66.7% → 73.2%`), **yet non-clock event recall drops to `1.7%` (`Figure 3`)**. `heartbeat-proactive` reaches **`65.1%`** 8-model Macro Set-F1 (`54.7%` clock, `10.0%` non-clock; best individual model `GPT-5.4` **`79.1%`**, `GPT-5.3-Codex` `single-baseline` `78.9%`), whereas unconditional `Auto-heartbeat (30m)` lifts non-clock hits to `15.8%` (`Figure 3`) at the cost of `199 → 489` micro false positives, degrading **Macro Set-F1 `60.0% → 57.8%` (`Table 2`) and Micro Set-F1 `59.4% → 51.5%`**.
   - **`TriggerBench` (`Zhang et al., 2026`, `arXiv:2606.23459v1`; `KristenZHANG/TriggerBench-Official@9e2185c3`, `1,265` dialogues across 5 PM dimensions):** On `GPT-4o`, explicit retrospective recall (`RM Acc`) stays **$\ge 98\%$** to `100K` tokens while autonomous prospective trigger execution (`PM Acc`) plunges from **`> 90%` to `< 40%`** (`Fig. 4`, `§5`). Under attentional overload (`Pos-Clean → Pos-Over`, `Table 3`), `Qwen3-32B` drops `49.80% → 13.11%`, `Qwen3-235B-Inst` `68.65% → 35.66%`, and `Qwen3-235B-Think` `92.83% → 64.14%`. Making `State Track` triggers explicit (`Table 4`, `GPT-4.1`) boosts `Pos-Clean` **`57.4% → 100.0%` (`+42.6 pp`)** at Base and **`31.5% → 80.6%` (`+49.1 pp`)** at `40K` (`Pos-Over` `25.0% → 60.2%`), **but degrades `Neg-Clean` false-alarm suppression by `-9.3 pp` (`82.4% → 73.2%`) at Base and `-10.2 pp` (`83.3% → 73.2%`) at `40K`** (`Table 19` precision–recall trade-off). In **`Table 3` & `§4.2`** (constraint retrieval rates in `Tables 13–14`; qualitative single-dialogue illustration in `Table 15`), `GPT-4.1` plain long-context (`60.0%`) outperforms similarity `RAG` (`51.62%`), `Mem0` (`53.20%`), and `A-MEM` (`41.42%`) because runtime triggers lack lexical overlap with stored rules; only write-time core-memory compilation (`Letta-Sim`, **`62.29%`**) exceeds baseline.
   - **Typed Intention Stores (`PIS`, `Zhao & Wu, 2026`, `arXiv:2609.01272v1`) and Selective Enactment Gating (`PRO-MEMORY`, `Bei et al., 2026`, `arXiv:2611.05731v1`):** Maintaining deterministic lifecycle records (`trigger_type`, `condition`, `status`, `supersedes`) in `PIS` (`Tables 1–3`) lifts `DeepSeek-Chat` `PM-Bench` Set-F1 from `67.7%` (`46.5%` `+Naive RAG`, `48.7%` `+Mem0`, `52.2%` `+Letta`) to **`82.9%`** (`0.0%` cross-day miss vs. `57.1%` `single` / `100%` `RAG`) and lifts `2B` `Gemma-E2B` from `4.2%` to **`66.2%`**—exceeding `PM-Bench`'s 8-model `heartbeat-proactive` mean (`65.1%`), though trailing frontier `GPT-5.4` (`79.1%`). `PRO-MEMORY` (`Tables 1–3`, 5 backbones) raises mean `PM-Bench` Set-F1 from `0.4791` (`No Memory`) and `0.5644` (`RAG`) to **`0.7324`** (Rank `1.20` on `PM-Bench`, `0.7231` / Rank `1.00` on `ProactiveBench`), cutting `Claude Haiku 4.5` false alarms `0.5750 → 0.0750` and `Claude Sonnet 4.5` misses `0.5679 → 0.2469` (`Set-F1 0.5224 → 0.7600`).
   - **Dual-Task Foreground Interference (`Mittal, 2026`, `arXiv:2603.23530v2`, `> 8,000` prompts at $T = 0$):** Across the **4 single foreground tasks** (`TriviaQA`, `MMLU`, `1×GSM8K`, `CNN/DM`), unweighted mean `IFEval` compliance drops by **`-9.8 pp`** on `DeepSeek-V3.1` ($p < .001$ on first 3, $p = .006$ on `CNN/DM`), **`-4.8 pp`** on `o4-mini` (where **3 of 4 single-task drops are statistically non-significant**: `TriviaQA` `-3.9 pp, p = .063`; `MMLU` `-4.1 pp, p = .052`; `1×GSM8K` `-2.5 pp, p = .265`), and **`-3.2 pp`** on `Llama-3.3-70B`. Including multi-task `3×GSM8K` and `5×GSM8K` yields `Table 8`'s **6-condition means** of **`-10.2 pp`** (`DeepSeek-V3.1`, `5×GSM8K` `-17.0 pp`), **`-8.9 pp`** (`o4-mini`, `3×GSM8K` `-15.0 pp, p = .002`; `5×GSM8K` `-19.5 pp`, 95% CI `[53.7, 79.6]`, **`p = .070` non-significant**), and **`-6.0 pp`** (`Llama-3.3-70B`, `5×GSM8K` `-18.5 pp, p = .004`). Per `Table 5`'s taxonomy (`Table 2` indicative per-type deltas at **$n = 6$ items/type**), losses concentrate in **Terminal** (`end_checker` `-50 pp` on `DeepSeek`, `postscript`), **Structural** (`json_format` `-50 pp` on `Llama`, `+6 pp` on `o4-mini`), and **Counting** (`number_bullet_lists` `-44 pp` on `DeepSeek`) constraints vs. `0 pp` on continuous lexical `forbidden_words`, while soft-tension pairs (`number_words + bullet_lists`, `Table 14`) lose `-15.0` to `-21.6 pp`.

#### 1.2 Progressive Disclosure of Agent Skills: `Stage 1 → 2` vs. `Stage 1 → 2 → 3`

1. **`Stage 1 → 2` Lazy Body Loading (`Zhang et al., 2026`, `arXiv:2609.35692v1`, `576` rollouts/model across $N \in \{5, 20, 50, 100\}$, `32K` context):** Progressive Disclosure (`PD`, frontmatter descriptions in prompt + `load_skill` action) prevents `100%` context-overflow crashes suffered by Eager Loading (`EL`) at $N = 100$ (`PD` `1.00` success on `Qwen2.5-7B` [`6,223` tok] and `Qwen3-14B` [`4,661` tok], `0.79` on `Qwen3-8B` [`5,184` tok], `Table 1`), lifts $N = 50$ accuracy from `0.40 → 0.67` (`Qwen2.5-7B`), `0.29 → 0.64` (`Qwen3-8B`), and **`0.13 → 0.72`** (`Qwen3-14B`, saving `74.4%–81.7%` prompt tokens at $N \ge 20, p < 10^{-25}$), and under-selects in only one cell (`Qwen3-8B` $N = 20$: `0.68 → 0.38`).
