# LLM Evolutionary Search, Test-Time Compute Scaling, Multi-Agent Debate Epistemics, and a Variation Taxonomy for Conceptual Refinement

## Summary

Primary-source inspection of 2023–2026 evolutionary coding agents (`FunSearch`, `AlphaEvolve`, `EoH`, `EvoPrompt`, `OpenEvolve`, `ShinkaEvolve`, `GigaEvo`), inference-time compute scaling laws (`Snell et al.`, `Large Language Monkeys`, `Tree of Thoughts`, `Graph of Thoughts`), and formal/empirical analyses of multi-agent debate and self-correction (`Huang et al.`, `Kamoi et al.`, `Liang et al.`, `Estornell and Liu`, `Choi, Zhu, and Li`, `Smit et al.`, `Zheng et al.`, `Zhang et al.`, `Wynn et al.`, `Panickssery et al.`, `Cemri et al.`) establishes five load-bearing invariants for orchestrating iterative conceptual and architectural model refinement:

1. **Verifier Fidelity Is the Master Bifurcation Across Sampling, Evolution, and Debate:** Repeated parallel sampling scales coverage log-linearly ($c(k) \approx \exp(a k^b)$ to $k = 10^4$, lifting `SWE-bench Lite` from `15.9%` to `56%` at $k=250$ under unit-test verification), **yet selection without a truth-correlated automated oracle plateaus by $k \approx 100$** under majority voting and learned reward models (`Brown et al. 2024`; `Probe 3`). Without external verification, intrinsic single-agent self-correction degrades accuracy across every tested frontier model via `Correct -> Incorrect` flips (`Huang et al. 2024`: GPT-4 `95.5% -> 89.0%` on GSM8K; `Snell et al. 2025`: `~38%` regression rate even after dedicated revision fine-tuning), and symmetric peer debate forms a **zero-drift martingale** ($\mathbb{E}[p_{i,t}\mid \mathcal{F}_{t-1}] = p_{i,t-1}$, `Choi, Zhu, and Li 2025`, `Probe 1`) that adds variance without expected progress toward truth unless asymmetric falsification injects positive drift.
2. **Co-Evolving Explicit Conceptual Rationales + Explicit Redundancy Pruning (`EoH M3`) Cuts Sample Complexity by Orders of Magnitude:** Evolving natural-language heuristic thoughts jointly with executable artifacts (`EoH`, `Liu et al. 2024`) matches `FunSearch` online bin packing in **`~2,000` LLM queries vs. `~10^6`**, reduces out-of-distribution `1k C500` gap from `6.75%` (`FunSearch`, overfit to training capacity) to **`2.13%`**, and lowers average gap from `2.57%` (`code-only`) to **`0.66%`**. Pairing `ShinkaEvolve`'s sigmoid-fitness $\times$ offspring-novelty weighting $1/(1+N(P_i))$, embedding novelty rejection ($\eta = 0.95$), and asymmetric UCB1 model-ensemble bandit (`Lange et al. 2025`) reaches `AlphaEvolve` circle-packing SOTA in **`< 150` evaluations**, while `GigaEvo` (`Khrulkov et al. 2025`) demonstrates that **multi-island setups offer zero gain over a single quality-diversity archive on short horizons (`< 200` generations)**.
3. **Difficulty-Dependent Serial $\times$ Parallel Crossover and `Graph of Thoughts` Aggregation:** On easy tasks already inside the right basin, sequential revision wins and aggressive step-level tree search over-optimizes imperfect reward models (`Snell et al. 2025`, Bins 1–2). On medium-hard tasks with framing ambiguity (Bins 3–4), an **interior mix of parallel exploration and bounded sequential refinement** ($\sim \sqrt{N}$ chains $\times \sqrt{N}$ revisions) saves up to **$4\times$ compute**; on capabilities outside base support (Bin 5), test-time compute saturates and larger pretraining wins. Structurally, `Graph of Thoughts` (`Besta et al. 2024`) uniquely achieves **logarithmic latency $\log_k N$ with full causal volume $N$** via $k \to 1$ aggregation (`~62%` lower error at `>31%` lower cost than `ToT`), provided synthesis enforces subtractive concept minimization rather than feature union.
4. **Epistemic Diversity Requires Comparable-Capability Cross-Family Models—Neither Social Personas Nor Capability-Asymmetric Committees:** System-prompt personas across **162 roles and 4 model families** yield near-zero factual lift (`+0.004` max in-domain, `Zheng et al. 2024`). Homogeneous multi-agent debate freezes into an identical-parameter echo chamber as $n \to \infty$ (`Estornell and Liu 2024`, `Thm. 5.1`), exponentially drowns out minority truth under majority misconceptions (`Thm. 5.2`, `Probe 2`), and loses **`>80%` of 36 benchmark scenarios** to simple Chain-of-Thought (`0%` win rate for `Multi-Persona`, `Zhang et al. 2025`). Mixing **comparable-capability distinct model families** (`Heter-MAD`) lifts debate by **`+6.4%` to `+8.2%`**, whereas **capability-asymmetric unmoderated debate degrades accuracy below pre-debate majority voting even when strong models outnumber weak ones** (`-2.0` to `-8.2` pts in `Wynn et al. 2025`; `-19.5` pts in `Probe 4`) because conformity and inter-agent sycophancy (`r = 0.902` in `Yao et al. 2025`) trigger `Correct -> Incorrect` flips.
5. **Cross-Family Clean-Room Evaluation and Lean Phase Contracts Are Mandatory:** Frontier LLMs recognize their own generations out of the box (`GPT-4`: **`73.5%`** accuracy, rising past `90%` after 500 fine-tuning examples), and self-recognition linearly drives causal self-preference bias (`Panickssery, Bowman, and Feng 2024`, Kendall $\tau = 0.41$; corroborated by `Liang et al. 2024` Table 6 `136:52` judge skew). Meanwhile, across `1,642` traces of 7 multi-agent frameworks (`Cemri et al. 2025`, `MAST`), **67.7%** of all failures arise from **System Design/Specification (`44.2%`)** and **Task Verification (`23.5%`)**, confirming that crisp phase contracts and multi-level verification dominate conversational swarm complexity.

### 2. Inference-Time Compute Scaling: Parallel Sampling, Sequential Revision, and Thought Topologies

`Basis: documentation + derived + execution`

#### 2.1 Difficulty-Stratified Compute-Optimal Scaling (`Snell et al., ICLR 2025`, `arXiv:2408.03314v1` & `DVTS`)

Partitioning `MATH` into five model-specific pass@1 quintiles (Bin 1 easiest $\to$ Bin 5 hardest, predicted at test time via mean Process Reward Model score) exposes three sharp crossovers (`Snell et al. 2025`, §§5–7):
1. **Easy Quintiles (Bins 1–2):** Initial drafts already land inside the valid solution basin. **Pure sequential revision outperforms parallel sampling**, whereas step-level **PRM Beam Search ($M=4$) and Lookahead Search actively degrade as budget rises ($64 \to 256$)** due to **PRM reward hacking** (generating repetitive zero-information steps or prematurely truncated 1-step answers that exploit out-of-distribution flaws in the learned verifier).
2. **Medium–Hard Quintiles (Bins 3–4):** Base pass@1 is low enough that global exploration is required before local polishing. **PRM Beam Search beats Best-of-N**, and an **interior sequential $\times$ parallel allocation** (e.g., $\sqrt{N}$ independent chains $\times \sqrt{N}$ sequential revisions) outperforms both pure parallel and pure sequential endpoints—achieving **up to $4\times$ compute savings** (`64` vs. `256` generations). At large $N$, standard beam search suffers path homogenization; **Diverse Verifier Tree Search (`DVTS`, Beeching, Tunstall, and Rush 2024)** splits $N$ beams into $N/M$ independent subtrees to preserve diversity.
3. **Hardest Quintile (Bin 5) & Pretraining FLOPs Exchange:** When required concepts lie outside the base model's support, all inference-time search strategies stall; under total FLOPs parity ($M + 3\frac{D_{\text{pretrain}}}{D_{\text{inference}}}(M-1)$), scaling pretraining parameters ($14\times$) beats test-time compute on Bin 5 and high-query-volume ($R = D_{\text{inf}}/D_{\text{pre}} = 22$) Bin 4 workloads.
4. **Sequential Revision Regression Hazard (`Snell et al.` §6.1, p. 12):** Even with a model *explicitly fine-tuned* on multi-turn error-to-correct trajectories, **`~38%` of already-correct answers regress into incorrect answers** on subsequent revision turns—proving sequential chains must select across *all* iterates $(y_0, \dots, y_T)$ rather than returning final iterate $y_T$ unconditionally.

#### 2.2 Repeated Sampling Coverage vs. The Verifier Selection Gap (`Brown et al. 2024`, `arXiv:2407.21787v3`)

Across `GSM8K`, `MATH`, `MiniF2F-MATH`, `CodeContests`, and `SWE-bench Lite` up to $k = 10{,}000$ samples:
- **Coverage (`pass@k`)** follows an exponentiated power law $\log c(k) \approx a k^b$: with an **exact automated verifier**, `DeepSeek-Coder-V2-Instruct` climbs from **`15.9%` ($k=1$) to `56%` ($k=250$)** on `SWE-bench Lite` (unit tests) and `Gemma-2B` climbs **`>300x`** (`0.02% -> 7.1%`) on `CodeContests`.
- **The Selection Bottleneck Without an Exact Verifier (`Fig. 7` & `Probe 3`):** On `GSM8K` and `MATH`, `Llama-3-8B-Instruct` coverage exceeds **`98%`** at $k = 10^4$ (with manual audit confirming `>90%` of rare correct traces are genuinely faithful reasoning, `Table 2`), **yet Majority Vote, `ArmoRM` Best-of-N, and RM-weighted Majority Vote all plateau around $k \approx 100$**! As verified in `Probe 3` (`p_good = 0.03`, plural misconception `p_misc = 0.22`), Majority Vote converges exponentially to `0.000` accuracy by $k=64$, while Best-of-$k$ under a noisy LLM judge (`mu=0.78` vs. `0.74`, `sigma=0.14`) stalls at `0.217` at $k=512$ because $7.3\times$ more draws from the misconception class generate extreme upper-tail noise outliers (**Goodhart's Winner's Curse**).

#### 2.3 Thought Topologies: `Tree of Thoughts` vs. `Graph of Thoughts` Latency–Volume Law

- **`Tree of Thoughts` (`Yao et al., NeurIPS 2023`, `arXiv:2305.10601v2`):** BFS ($b=5$) lifts GPT-4 `Game of 24` from `4.0%` (`CoT`) and `9.0%` (`CoT-SC@100`) to **`74%`** (`60%` of CoT errors occurred on step 1); DFS with value-threshold pruning and backtracking lifts `5x5 Mini Crosswords` word accuracy from `15.6%` (`CoT`) to **`60%`** (`20%` full games, dropping to `5%` if either pruning or backtracking is ablated).
- **`Graph of Thoughts` (`Besta et al., AAAI 2024`, `arXiv:2308.09687v4`):** Generalizes search to DAGs with **Generation** ($1 \to k$), **Aggregation/Merging** ($k \to 1$), and **Refinement** (self-loops). At fixed $\Theta(N)$ thought budget (`§6 Table 2`), `CoT` has $(\text{Latency } N, \text{Volume } N)$, `CoT-SC` has $(N/k, N/k)$, and $k$-ary `ToT` has $(\log_k N, O(\log_k N))$, whereas a mirrored decompose-and-aggregate **`GoT` DAG uniquely achieves Latency $\log_k N$ AND full causal Volume $N$**—cutting $128$-element sorting error by **`~62%`** at **`>31%` lower cost** than `ToT`.

### 3. Multi-Agent Debate, Self-Refinement, and Epistemic Diversity Failure Modes

`Basis: documentation + derived + execution`

#### 3.1 Collapse of Intrinsic Self-Correction vs. Oracle-Grounded Reflection

- **`Self-Refine` (`Madaan et al. 2023`, `arXiv:2303.17651v2`) vs. `Reflexion` (`Shinn et al. 2023`, `arXiv:2303.11366v4`):** While `Self-Refine` reported $\sim 20\%$ average gains on style/preference tasks (largely attributable to missing instructions in initial prompt $p_{\text{gen}}$ supplied only in $p_{\text{fb}}$, `Kamoi et al. 2024` Tables 9–10), its **`GSM8K` reasoning lift was `0.0%` on GPT-3.5 and `+0.2%` on GPT-4** (`Table 1`). By contrast, `Reflexion` achieved `+22%` (`ALFWorld`) and `+11%` (`HumanEval`, `80% -> 91%`) **strictly by grounding verbal memory in external environment rewards or executed unit tests**.
- **Intrinsic Self-Correction Degrades Reasoning (`Huang et al., ICLR 2024`, `arXiv:2310.01798v2`; `Kamoi et al., TACL 2024`, `arXiv:2406.01297v3`):** Without an external oracle, intrinsic multi-turn self-correction reduces accuracy across every tested model and neutral prompt variant (`GPT-4` `GSM8K`: `95.5% -> 89.0%`, `HotpotQA`: `49.0% -> 43.0%`; `GPT-3.5` `CSQA`: `75.8% -> 41.8%` **`-34.0` pts**; `Llama-2-70B` `GSM8K`: `62.0% -> 36.5%` **`-25.5` pts**). `Kamoi et al.` confirm that prompted self-correction succeeds **only** under: (1) reliable external tool/verifier feedback, (2) atomic "verification-easier-than-generation" decomposition (e.g., factual sub-claim lookup), or (3) $>100\text{K}$-example specialized fine-tuning.

#### 3.2 Formal Impossibility and Martingale Theorems for Multi-Agent Debate (`MAD`)

*(Bibliographic precision note: Estornell and Liu's NeurIPS 2024 paper is titled "Multi-LLM Debate: Framework, Principals, and Interventions", OpenReview `sy7eSEXdPC`, Vol. 37 pp. 28938–28964; `arXiv:2409.16636v1` is Arnesen, Rein, and Michael 2024 on self-play debate.)*

1. **Degeneration-of-Thought (`DoT`) & Same-Family Judge Skew (`Liang et al., EMNLP 2024`, `arXiv:2305.19118v4`):** Once a single LLM commits confidence to an initial stance, self-reflection fails to generate novel alternatives even when wrong (`DoT`). Opposing Affirmative/Negative debaters lift `CIAR` trick-math accuracy on GPT-3.5 from `28.0%` (`CoT`) to `37.0%`, **but `Table 6` exposes severe judge bias**: in `GPT-4` vs. `GPT-3.5` debates judged by `GPT-4`, the `GPT-4` judge selected the `GPT-4` debater **`136:52`** (when Negative) and **`120:77`** (when Affirmative).
2. **Echo Chambers, Tyranny of the Majority, and Misconception Amplification (`Estornell and Liu, NeurIPS 2024`):** Under conditional independence given latent concept $\theta \in \Theta$ (`Lemma 4.2`: $P(z_i^{(t+1)}\mid Z^{(t)}, x, \phi_i) \propto \sum_\theta P(z_i^{(t+1)}, x, \theta\mid \phi_i) \prod_{j=1}^n P(z_j^{(t)}\mid \theta, \phi_i)$):
   - **`Theorem 5.1` (Identical-Model Echo Chamber):** When $\phi_i \equiv \phi$, $\Pr(\theta'^{(t)} = \theta'^{(t+1)}) \to 1$ as $n \to \infty$ (homogeneous debate freezes on its initial mode).
   - **`Theorem 5.2` (Tyranny of the Majority):** When $m$ peers emit MAP concept $\theta'$, likelihood ratios converge exponentially in $m$ to generation under $\theta'$, **drowning out both minority dissenters and the task prompt $x$** (`Probe 2`: even with a `0.55` solo truth prior and 1 truth-telling dissenter, $m = 5$ misconception peers collapse truth posterior from `0.796` to **`0.0115`**).
   - **`Theorem 5.4` (Shared Misconception Amplification):** If $m$ of $n$ agents share misconception $\theta'$, expected final accuracy decreases monotonically in $m$.
   - **`Algorithm 1` Mitigation & Symbolic Embedding Limit:** Composing `QualityPrune` (`Thm. 6.2`) $\to$ `DiversityPrune` (`Thm. 6.1`) $\to$ `MisconceptionRefutation` (`Thm. 6.4`) provably raises truth convergence, **except** that sentence-embedding KL proxies (`ADA-2`) **break down on symbolic/mathematical domains (`MathQ`, §§7–8)** where distinct algebraic derivations share nearly identical surface embeddings.
3. **Unbiased Peer Debate Is a Zero-Drift Martingale (`Choi, Zhu, and Li, NeurIPS 2025 Spotlight`, `arXiv:2508.17536`):**
   - **`Theorem 1`:** 0-round Majority Voting over $N > K/\Delta^2$ independent samples converges exponentially to truth whenever truth holds plurality margin $\Delta = \bar{\theta}_1 - \bar{\theta}_2 > 0$.
   - **`Theorem 2` (Martingale Law):** Modeling peer updates as Dirichlet–Multinomial posterior updates $\alpha_{i,t} = \alpha_{i,t-1} + c_{i,t}$, agent $i$'s expected truth belief $p_{i,t} = \bar{\theta}_{i,t}^{(1)}$ over homogeneous agents or symmetric topologies satisfies **$\mathbb{E}[p_{i,t} \mid \mathcal{F}_{t-1}] = p_{i,t-1}$** (extending to weighted collective intelligence for heterogeneous agents, `App. D.4`). Unbiased debate has **zero expected drift toward truth** while inter-round correlation degrades consensus accuracy below 0-round Majority Vote (`Qwen2.5-7B` across 7 benchmarks: Single `0.7205`, Decentralized MAD $T=5$ `0.7050`, Centralized MAD $T=2$ `0.6551`, **0-round Majority Vote `0.7691`**, vs. truth-biased **`MAD-oracle` `0.8364`**; reproduced in `Probe 1`).

#### 3.3 Personas, Cross-Family Heterogeneity vs. Capability Asymmetry, Self-Preference, and `MAST`

1. **Null Effect of Persona Prompts (`Zheng et al., EMNLP 2024`, `arXiv:2311.10054v3`; `Smit et al., ICML 2024`, `arXiv:2311.17371`):** Across **162 social and domain-expert personas** and 4 model families (`FLAN-T5-XXL`, `Llama-3-8B/70B`, `Mistral-7B`, `Qwen2.5-7B/72B`) on 2,410 MMLU questions, personas fail to beat a no-persona baseline (`Qwen2.5` completely invariant; domain match `+0.004`), and `Multi-Persona` debate (`devil's advocate`) degrades Agent 1's initial accuracy (`Smit et al.` Fig. 4; **`0%` win rate vs. CoT** across 36 scenarios in `Zhang et al. 2025`).
2. **Comparable-Capability Cross-Family Lift vs. Capability-Asymmetric Sycophancy Collapse:**
   - *Positive Regime (`Hegazy 2024`, `arXiv:2410.12853`; `Zhang et al. 2025`, `arXiv:2502.08788v3`):* While homogeneous MAD loses $>80\%$ of 36 benchmark-model scenarios to plain CoT, pairing **comparable-capability distinct model families** (`GPT-4o-mini + Llama-3.1-70B` in `Zhang et al.`; `Gemini-Pro + Mixtral-8x7B + PaLM-2-M` hitting `91%` vs. `82%` homogeneous on GSM8K in `Hegazy`) lifts `Heter-SoM` by **`+6.4%`** and `Heter-EoT` by **`+8.2%`**, with simple `Heter-SoM` beating complex `AgentVerse`/`ChatEval` harnesses.
   - *Negative Regime (`Wynn, Satija, and Hadfield 2025`, `arXiv:2509.05396`; `Yao et al. 2025`, `arXiv:2509.23055`):* Mixing distinct families with **large capability gaps** (`GPT-4o-mini` `82.6%` MMLU, `Llama-3.1-8B` `55.6%`, `Mistral-7B` `34.0%`) **degrades accuracy below pre-debate majority voting even when strong models outnumber weak ones** (`2x GPT-4o-mini + 1x Mistral-7B`: `-2.0` MMLU, `-2.2` CSQA; `2x Llama-3.1-8B + 1x Mistral-7B`: **`-8.2` MMLU, `-8.0` CSQA**; `1x GPT-4o-mini + 2x Llama-3.1-8B`: **`-7.0` MMLU**; reproduced in `Probe 4` at `-19.5` pts). Debater Sycophancy Score correlates **`r = 0.902`** with uncritical Negative Agreement Rate (`Yao et al. 2025`).
3. **Self-Recognition Causally Drives Self-Preference (`Panickssery, Bowman, and Feng, NeurIPS 2024`, `arXiv:2404.13076`; `Arnesen, Rein, and Michael 2024`, `arXiv:2409.16636v1`):** `GPT-4` identifies its own generations at **`73.5%`** accuracy out of the box (`>90%` on `GPT-3.5`/`Llama-2` after 500 fine-tuning examples), and self-recognition linearly drives self-preference ($\tau = 0.41$; reversing author labels flips preference)—requiring **strict author–judge model-family separation** even under blind anonymization. Under evidence-quoted oversight (`Arnesen et al.`), adversarial two-sided debate improves judge accuracy with debater skill, whereas single-sided consultancy exploits sycophantic judges.
4. **Multi-Agent System Failure Taxonomy (`Cemri et al., NeurIPS 2025`, `arXiv:2503.13657v3`):** Auditing `1,642` traces across 7 MAS frameworks ($\kappa = 0.88$) attributes **`44.2%`** of failures to **System Design & Specification** (`15.7%` step repetition, `12.4%` unaware of termination, `11.8%` disobey task spec—tightening role specs alone yielded `+9.4%` on GPT-4o), **`32.4%`** to **Inter-Agent Misalignment** (`13.2%` reasoning-action mismatch, `7.4%` derailment), and **`23.5%`** to **Task Verification** (`9.1%` incorrect verification, `8.2%` incomplete verification, `6.2%` premature termination).

#### 4.2 Three-Tier Evidence Calibration Across the Transfer Boundary

To prevent over-claiming when transferring mathematical optimization and verifiable-code results to conceptual design, every architectural rule falls into one of three explicit evidentiary classes:

1. **Tier I — Representation-Independent Mathematical Theorems (Apply Directly Without Analogy):**
   - **Zero-drift martingale of symmetric peer updates** (`Choi, Zhu, and Li 2025 Thm. 2`) and **echo-chamber / tyranny-of-the-majority skew** (`Estornell and Liu 2024 Thms. 5.1, 5.2, 5.4`);
   - **Non-convex invisibility of linear weighted-sum rubrics** (`Das and Dennis 1997`) and **strict proper Pareto recovery via $\varepsilon$-constraint / Augmented Weighted Tchebycheff** (`Miettinen 1999 Thms. 3.2.2, 3.4.7`), plus **strict Pareto dominance of additive feature-union compromises**;
   - **Expressiveness monotonicity of counterexample overfitting** ($E_1 \subseteq E_2 \implies \Omega(E_1, Z) \le \Omega(E_2, Z)$, `Padhi et al. 2019 Thm. 5`) and **finite-time non-certification of elitist Markov chains** (`Rudolph 1994 Thms. 4, 7`);
   - **Algebraic unidentifiability of $k \le 3$ capture-recapture under $f_1 = 0$ or $f_2 = 0$** (`Burnham and Overton 1978`; `Chao 1987`) and **adaptive holdout max-information bounds** (`Dwork et al. 2015 Thms. 9, 13, 15, 17`: $\sqrt{\ln(TB)/n}$ vs. $\sqrt{T\ln B/n}$ vs. $\Theta(\sqrt{TB/n})$).
2. **Tier II — Controlled Empirical Laws in LLM & Software Systems (Apply Under Matched Conditions):**
   - **Verifier-gated scaling vs. $k \approx 100$ non-oracle selection plateau** (`Brown et al. 2024`), **difficulty-bin parallel-vs-sequential crossover & `38%` revision regression** (`Snell et al. 2025`), and **`GoT` $\log_k N$ latency / $N$ volume gains** (`Besta et al. 2024`);
   - **`~500x` sample-efficiency & OOD generalization of co-evolving thoughts + code + `M3` pruning** (`EoH`, `ShinkaEvolve`, `GigaEvo` single-island short-horizon sufficiency);
   - **Intrinsic self-correction degradation** (`Huang et al. 2024`), **162-persona null result** (`Zheng et al. 2024`), **comparable-capability `Heter-MAD` lift vs. capability-asymmetric sycophancy collapse** (`Zhang et al. 2025`; `Wynn et al. 2025`; `Yao et al. 2025`), **`73.5%` self-recognition $\to$ self-preference bias** (`Panickssery et al. 2024`), **`67.7%` spec+verification share of MAS failures** (`Cemri et al. 2025`), and **`0/12` vs. `12/12` micro-semantic defect resolution** (`single-vs-swarm-model-refinement-empirical-comparison`).
3. **Tier III — Structural Isomorphisms vs. Invalid Analogies (Require Adaptation; Never Cite as Exact Theorems):**
   - *Valid structural adaptations:* `Lässig–Sudholt` protected $\tau$-epoch gestation for multi-step epistatic refactors ($G \ge 3\tau$); `Sudholt` / `Whitley` bounded-depth Baldwinian seam repair; `Katzgraber` $\eta \propto 1/\sqrt{D}$ bottleneck budget concentration; `IC3 MIC` / `BLAST` interface-scoped counterexample generalization; and `Glover` semantic `TABULIST` + Aspiration + Strategic Oscillation + Path Relinking.
   - *Mathematically invalid analogies (`known not to apply`):* Plugging LLM token temperatures and ordinal rubric scores into `Hukushima–Nemoto` $\exp((\beta_i-\beta_j)(E_i-E_j))$ or `Kone–Kofke` `23%` swap targets; using `ADA-2` sentence-embedding cosine distances or `[0,1]^d` `CVT-MAP-Elites` centroids on formal/symbolic specs; and treating `pycma` float64 covariance rules or $2$-critic overlap counts as convergence proofs.

## Boundaries

- **`known not to apply` — Scaling Unverified LLM Generation, Intrinsic Self-Critique, or Homogeneous / Capability-Asymmetric Debate:** Without an asymmetric falsifier or external verifier, `pass@k` gains above $k \approx 100$ are unrecoverable by majority vote or noisy reward models (`Brown et al. 2024`), intrinsic self-critique lowers reasoning accuracy (`Huang et al. 2024`), homogeneous debate is a zero-drift martingale (`Choi et al. 2025`), personas add zero factual diversity (`Zheng et al. 2024`), and capability-asymmetric debate corrupts stronger models via conformity/sycophancy (`Wynn et al. 2025`).
- **`known not to apply` — Multi-Island Overhead on Very Short Budgets ($G \lesssim T_{\text{basin}}$) and Sentence-Embedding Diversity on Symbolic Specs:** `GigaEvo` (`arXiv:2511.17592v1`) found zero empirical benefit from multiple islands over a single MAP-Elites archive on $\le 200$-generation runs, and `Estornell and Liu` (`NeurIPS 2024` §§7–8) proved `ADA-2` sentence embeddings fail to separate valid from invalid mathematical derivations on `MathQ`.
- **`unsupported` — Proprietary `AlphaEvolve` Database Constants and Unimplemented `OpenEvolve` Meta-Prompting:** `AlphaEvolve` (`arXiv:2506.13131v1`) omits internal island counts and MAP-Elites grid dimensions as proprietary; in `algorithmicsuperintelligence/openevolve` @ `4167db43`, `PromptConfig.use_meta_prompting` and `EvaluatorConfig.distributed`/`memory_limit_mb` are marked `# not implemented` in `openevolve/config.py`.
- **`unknown` — Sycophancy Resistance of Future RL-Trained Frontier Verifier Models on Unformalized Design Disputes:** While `Wynn et al. (2025)` and `Yao et al. (2025)` establish severe `Correct -> Incorrect` sycophancy across `GPT-4o-mini`, `Llama-3.1/3.3`, `Mistral`, and `Qwen3`, exact sycophancy flip rates among 2026+ RL-reasoning models specifically on open-ended software-architecture trade-offs remain unmeasured in controlled primary benchmarks.
- **`not examined` — Multi-Agent Fine-Tuning / Co-Training via Reinforcement Learning:** Online multi-agent RL weight updates (`MAPPO`, self-play DPO beyond `Arnesen et al.`'s reading-comprehension oversight benchmark) were outside the inference-time orchestration scope of this report.
