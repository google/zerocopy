# Draft participant introduction and consent checklist

This is a draft for review by the responsible study owner and applicable human-subjects/privacy process before recruitment. It is not an approval or a record of consent.

> We are studying how developers understand freshness and failure messages in a prototype Rust proof workflow. You will see eight synthetic, non-production scenarios and tell us which result they describe and what you would do next. The session is expected to take about 30–40 minutes. This is a test of the interface, not of your ability. You may skip any question, stop at any time without penalty, or decline optional recording. We will use a study slot ID in the research notes and will not put your name, employer code, repository content, or credentials into the report. We will quote responses only after removing identifying details. Please do not paste real source code or secrets into the prototype. Do you agree to participate under the approved study process?

Before starting, the operator must:

1. Obtain the study owner's approved consent/privacy procedure, including whether organizational or institutional review is needed, retention period, and withdrawal/deletion handling.
2. Record the consent decision separately from research data; stop immediately if consent is not given.
3. Ask for optional audio/screen-recording consent separately. Default to no recording. If approved, store media outside the report package with restricted access.
4. Use only synthetic fixture source and mock identities; turn off telemetry and accidental external service access unless specifically approved.
5. Explain that either interface condition may be confusing and that participants can request other views, including generated Lean, but are never required to understand generated Lean internals to succeed.
