Their exact destinations and gates are in [`support/not-run-suggestions-v11.csv`](support/not-run-suggestions-v11.csv).

The seven remaining gate groups are detailed in [`support/gated-work-v11.csv`](support/gated-work-v11.csv): same-process OCaml Aeneas API or approved nonlocal dependency; recruited human participants and a truthful UI; a selected remote target if warranted; additional native filesystems/platforms; real editor and existing MCP adapter clients; an implemented Anneal V2 workspace/editor/MCP/scheduler vertical; and architecture adoption decisions. The component experiments do not remove those gates. In particular, R38's policy-model stale fence, R40's emulated overlayfs counters and R41's synthetic projection cannot establish the corresponding product behavior.
