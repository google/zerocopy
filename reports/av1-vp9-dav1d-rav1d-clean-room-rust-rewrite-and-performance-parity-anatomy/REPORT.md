# AV1 and VP9 Clean-Room Rust Rewrite Anatomy: dav1d, rav1d, rav1d-safe, libvpx, and FFmpeg Parity

## Summary

Modern open-media video decoding splits across two architectural lineages: **VP9**—for which upstream `FFmpeg/FFmpeg` (`7ddf7bc8a482cf28b4fab2e63b7093ba35cb6a80`) maintains a complete first-party C/ASM software decoder (`libavcodec/vp9*.c` plus `27,866` lines of x86/AArch64/RISC-V assembly) alongside FFI wrappers (`libvpxdec.c`, `libvpxenc.c`) to `webmproject/libvpx` (`18656c27cf3bee114562297274b64a84d0a6187c`)—and **AV1** (`AOMediaCodec/av1-spec` v1.0.0-errata1, `5e04f3f75e73a5898d7616c47c52f032144b8f80`), for which `FFmpeg/FFmpeg` has **zero native software pixel decoder** (`libavcodec/av1dec.c:715-720` returns `AVERROR(ENOSYS)` whenever `!avctx->hwaccel`) and delegates CPU decoding to `videolan/dav1d` (`9af5ae4eb5204706c7f381ec62635237179f172e`) via `libavcodec/libdav1d.c`.

Comparing `videolan/dav1d` directly against its two Rust descendants—ISRG Prossimo and Immunant's `memorysafety/rav1d` (`2e7216c20ac2a75de204d6c8d7268b75a45959b4`, `BSD-2-Clause`) and Imazen's safe-SIMD fork `imazen/rav1d-safe` (`19327e174e738785cd4155711bbb9ce3d4a467ce`, `AGPL-3.0-only OR LicenseRef-Imazen-Commercial`)—yields six decisive empirical and verification findings for a clean-room, formally verified Rust media stack:

1. **The Three-Point Safety vs. Throughput Spectrum:**
   - `videolan/dav1d` combines `45,864` lines of C (`86` `.c` files, `12,430` lines of `.h`) with **`267,372` lines of hand-written assembly** (`49` x86 `.asm` files = `163,200` lines; `67` `.S` files across ARM32/ARM64, RISC-V RVV, LoongArch LSX/LASX, and PowerPC VSX = `104,172` lines).
   - `memorysafety/rav1d` replaces the C control/parser/fallback layer with `54,923` lines of Rust across `70` `src/*.rs` modules (`60,053` lines across all `87` `.rs` files), containing **`298` `unsafe` blocks, `170` `unsafe fn` declarations, and `23` `unsafe impl` items** (`632` `unsafe` token occurrences), while **retaining `236,571` lines of verbatim `dav1d` x86/ARM assembly** (`49` `.asm` = `163,265` lines; `45` `.S` = `73,306` lines) and omitting `dav1d`'s RISC-V, LoongArch, and PowerPC assembly backends. Even with identical assembly executing ~50% of total decode cycles plus `lto = "fat"` and `codegen-units = 1`, `rav1d` remained **~5%–6% slower than `dav1d` on multi-threaded `x86_64`** (Ryzen 7700X, 8 threads, Chimera 1080p 8-bit) and **~6.3%–9% slower single-threaded on Apple M3** (`73.914 s` -> `72.182 s` vs. `dav1d`'s `67.912 s`).
   - `imazen/rav1d-safe` compiles its main crate under `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` by default, replacing raw assembly with `57` files (`~104k` lines) under `src/safe_simd/` built on token-gated `archmage` intrinsics and `safe_unaligned_simd` slice loads/stores, plus an externalized `rav1d-disjoint-mut` runtime borrow tracker. On AMD Zen 4 (AVX-512, 1 thread), eliminating hand-written assembly slows decoding to **`1.70x–2.21x` of `dav1d` in `untracked` mode** (`1.55x` on 4K AVIF stills) and **`2.27x–2.76x` in default `checked` mode** (`1.58x` on 1-thread 4K stills, `2.1x` at 4 threads).
2. **Where the Non-ASM Performance Gap Actually Lives:** Leaf-PC profiling of `rav1d-safe` (`untracked`) vs. `dav1d` on real 1080p/4K AV1 footage shows that **74%–78% of the `~2.0x` gap resides inside SIMD DSP kernels**—dominated by Motion Compensation (`3.2x` slower on 4K `aomenc`: `24.1 ms` vs. `7.5 ms` because two-pass 8-tap `i16` mid-buffer filtering loses to `dav1d`'s register-fused AVX-512 6-tap `pmaddubsw` kernels and affine warp) and CDEF (`4.9x` slower: `9.8 ms` vs. `2.0 ms`, including a `7x` penalty in `u8 -> u16` block boundary padding)—while **Daala multi-symbol rANS entropy decoding (`msac` + `decode_coefs`) achieves 1:1 parity (`0.99x–1.07x`)** in pure Rust + safe SIMD.
3. **Why Bounds Checks Were `< 1%` in `c2rust` Yet LLVM Codegen Still Cost `~5%–6%`:** Immunant's controlled compiler ablation—modifying `rustc` to strip **all** slice/array bounds checks and separately testing unchecked signed arithmetic—proved that bounds checks accounted for **`< 1%` of runtime** (and ~2% out of +7% extra retired instructions), while C-style undefined signed overflow had no measurable aggregate effect. Instead, `rav1d`'s residual 5%–6% overhead against `dav1d` stems from stack-frame bloat in large monomorphized functions (`decode_coefs` stack size causing register spills and flipping branchless `cmov`/`csel` idioms into mispredicted conditional branches), redundant stack `memset` of scratch arrays (`Align16([0u16; 200])`), and LLVM's inability to coalesce field-wise `#[derive(PartialEq)]` loads on small structs (`struct Mv { y: i16, x: i16 }`, Rust issue `#140167`) due to uninitialized-field poison/branch-dependence rules unless rewritten via `zerocopy::AsBytes`.
4. **The Soundness Trap of Runtime `DisjointMut` vs. Unchecked `UnsafeCell`:** Because `dav1d`'s tile and frame threads concurrently mutate non-contiguous block/pixel regions inside shared buffers (`Av1Block`, pixel planes, loop-filter masks), `memorysafety/rav1d` introduced `DisjointMut<T>` (`src/disjoint_mut.rs:33-73`), which checks interval disjointness in `#[cfg(debug_assertions)]` builds but **compiles to an unchecked `UnsafeCell<T>` in release builds**—relying on an unverified assumption that `dav1d` is race-free and arguing that races on provenanceless primitives cannot corrupt pointers (even though data races on `UnsafeCell` remain immediate Rust/LLVM UB!). Conversely, `rav1d-safe` turned `DisjointMut` tracking **on** in release builds, incurring **`569,690` borrow registrations per frame** (`4.52 ns -> 9.18 ns -> 19.71 ns/registration` at 2/4/8 workers on Apple M4 Pro from shared cache-line coherence traffic, adding +15% to +25% video decode overhead)—and **still suffered a Miri-confirmed Stacked/Tree Borrows UB bug** in published `rav1d-disjoint-mut = 0.3.1` (`moving_a_mut_guard_into_drop_is_not_ub`), where retiring a slot inside `Drop::drop` while the guard held `&mut [T]` violated LLVM/Tree-Borrows function-entry protector lifetimes before `drop(g)` returned!
5. **`panic = "abort"` and Unchecked Dispatch Surfaces:** `memorysafety/rav1d` sets `panic = "abort"` in `[profile.release]` (`Cargo.toml:48-51`) and initializes `wrap_fn_ptr!` DSP tables with `extern "C" fn default_unimplemented(...) { unimplemented!() }` (`src/wrap_fn_ptr.rs:70-80`), while relying on `Mutex::try_lock().unwrap()` for interior mutability and `assert!` inside `msac.rs`. Any un-discharged panic path on an adversarial bitstream or scheduling edge case terminates the host process (`SIGABRT`).
6. **Implications for a Clean-Room Verified FFmpeg Rewrite:** Neither `c2rust` mechanical transpilation (which locks the Rust codebase into C's shared-mutable pointer graph and `236k` lines of unverified external `.asm`/`.S`) nor runtime `DisjointMut` shard-locking (which pays up to 25% coherence tax and cannot statically guarantee panic-freedom) is optimal. Instead, a formally verified clean rewrite achieves **zero-cost disjoint concurrency** via static spatial permission lenses / ghost partition tokens (`0 ns/borrow`, `0` cache-line writes), **panic-freedom** via range-carrying index types (`Fin<N>`) proved free of `unwrap()`/`assert!()` in MIR, and **100% SIMD parity** by targeting register-fused kernel microarchitectures (either verified ISA assembly or fused target-specific intrinsics proved bit-exact against clean scalar Rust specs).

## Findings

### 1. Ecosystem Architecture: FFmpeg AV1/VP9 Integration vs. `dav1d`, `rav1d`, `rav1d-safe`, and `libvpx`

`Basis: source`

Direct source inspection of `FFmpeg/FFmpeg` (`7ddf7bc8a482cf28b4fab2e63b7093ba35cb6a80`) reveals a sharp structural asymmetry between VP9 and AV1 inside `libavcodec/`:

1. **AV1 in `FFmpeg/FFmpeg` Is Hardware-Only In-Tree (`libavcodec/av1dec.c`):**
   - Although `FFmpeg/FFmpeg` contains `libavcodec/av1dec.c` (`1,623` lines), `av1_parse.c` (`122` lines), `av1_parser.c` (`227` lines), and `cbs_av1.c` + `cbs_av1_syntax_template.c` (`3,550` lines), `av1dec.c` parses OBU sequence/frame/tile-group headers (`AV1RawSequenceHeader`, `AV1RawFrameHeader`) **solely to drive GPU hardware accelerators** (`DXVA2`, `D3D11VA`, `D3D12VA`, `NVDEC`, `VA-API`, `VDPAU`, `VideoToolbox`, `Vulkan Video`, registered at `av1dec.c:1589-1622`).
   - In `get_pixel_format` (`libavcodec/av1dec.c:710-720`), FFmpeg explicitly aborts software fallback:
     ```c
     if (!avctx->hwaccel) {
         av_log(avctx, AV_LOG_ERROR, "Your platform doesn't support"
                " hardware accelerated AV1 decoding.\n");
         avctx->pix_fmt = AV_PIX_FMT_NONE;
         return AVERROR(ENOSYS);
     }
     ```
     And in `av1_receive_frame_internal` (`av1dec.c:1396-1404`), `AV1_OBU_TILE_GROUP` invokes `FF_HW_CALL(avctx, decode_slice, ...)` only under `if (avctx->hwaccel && s->cur_frame.f)`, with no CPU entropy, transform, prediction, or filter code anywhere in `FFmpeg/FFmpeg`.
   - All software AV1 decoding and encoding in FFmpeg routes through external C FFI wrappers: **`libavcodec/libdav1d.c` (`620` lines)** (default preferred software decoder), `libaomdec.c` (`303` lines), `libaomenc.c` (`1,705` lines), `libsvtav1.c` (`871` lines), and `librav1e.c` (`673` lines), alongside GPU hardware encoders (`vaapi_encode_av1.c` `1,115` lines, `vulkan_encode_av1.c` `1,478` lines, `d3d12va_encode_av1.c` `1,618` lines, `amfenc_av1.c` `760` lines, `nvenc_av1.c` `237` lines, `qsvenc_av1.c` `227` lines).
2. **VP9 in `FFmpeg/FFmpeg` vs. `webmproject/libvpx`:**
   - Unlike AV1, `FFmpeg/FFmpeg` ships a **complete, high-speed first-party software VP9 decoder** (`vp9.c` `2,010`L, `vp9block.c` `1,458`L, `vp9data.c` `2,247`L, `vp9dsp_template.c` `2,578`L, `vp9recon.c` `663`L, `vp9mvs.c` `365`L, `vp9prob.c` `272`L, `vp9lpf.c` `203`L, `vp9_mc_template.c` `439`L = `~10.7k` lines of C) backed by **`27,866` lines of hand-written VP9 assembly and init glue** (`18` x86 files = `19,135` lines including SSE2/SSSE3/AVX2/AVX-512 `vp9itxfm`, `vp9intrapred`, `vp9lpf`, `vp9mc`; `12` AArch64 NEON files = `7,778` lines; `5` RISC-V RVI/RVV files = `953` lines)—widely known as `ffvp9`, which historically outperformed `libvpx`'s own VP9 decoder (`vp9/decoder/` `5,052`L C + `vp9/common/` `11,733`L C + `vpx_dsp/` `114,714`L C and `18,215`L ASM in `webmproject/libvpx`).
   - Conversely, FFmpeg has **no native software VP9 encoder** (only `vaapi_encode_vp9.c` `325`L and `qsvenc_vp9.c` `123`L for hardware), delegating software VP9 encoding to `webmproject/libvpx` (`vp9/encoder/`, `75` `.c` files = `66,192` lines) via `libavcodec/libvpxenc.c` (`2,241` lines).

### 3. Quantitative Performance Parity Anatomy: `rav1d` vs. `dav1d` vs. `rav1d-safe`

`Basis: documentation + source`

Primary-source benchmark disclosures from Immunant/Prossimo (`memorysafety.org`, Sep 2024 & May 2025), Ohad Ravid (`2025-05-22`), and `imazen/rav1d-safe` (`README.md:300-339`, `docs/SAFE_VS_DAV1D.md:1-139`, Oct 2026) isolate the exact cost of every architectural decision when porting C+ASM media decoders to Rust:

#### 3.1 `memorysafety/rav1d` (Retaining `dav1d` Assembly) vs. `videolan/dav1d`
Even with all x86/ARM assembly enabled, **Rust code still accounts for ~50% of total AV1 decode wall-clock time** (`rav1d_perf_blog`, Sep 2024: bitstream/symbol parsing, `decode_b`/`decode_coefs`, `refmvs` candidate scanning, `lf_mask` construction, and task dispatch). Measured on Chimera 1080p 8-bit (`Chimera-AV1-8bit-1920x1080-6736kbps.ivf`) against `dav1d` compiled with the exact matching LLVM backend (`Clang 18` vs. `rustc 1.81-nightly` on AMD Ryzen 7700X, 8 threads; and `Clang 20.1.4` vs. `rustc 1.88.0-nightly` LLVM `20.1.2` on Apple M3, 1 thread):
- **Initial `c2rust` Unsafe Transpilation:** `3.8%` slower than `dav1d` C, retiring **`+7%` more dynamic instructions** (~2% from bounds checks on fixed arrays; ~5% from extra integer widening/narrowing casts and suboptimal Rust-frontend LLVM IR).
- **Controlled `rustc` Compiler Ablations (Immunant):**
  - *Removing 100% of slice/array bounds checks inside `rustc`*: Still `3.0%` slower than `dav1d`—proving bounds checks caused **`< 1%` wall-clock overhead** once hot loops were pre-sliced! (Confirmed independently in `rav1d-safe` `README.md:332-338`, where removing bounds checks improved 4K/8K decode by only `187.8 ms -> 179.5 ms` / `1103.2 ms -> 1066.9 ms`, within measurement noise on video.)
  - *Emitting `nsw` (unchecked signed overflow) on Rust `i8`/`i16`/`i32`/`i64` arithmetic inside `rustc`*: Produced isolated instruction cleanups but **zero measurable aggregate speedup** across AV1 decoding, refuting the common hypothesis that C's undefined signed overflow is why C codecs beat Rust!
  - *LLVM 17 -> LLVM 18 Regression*: Both `dav1d` (Clang 18) and `rav1d` regressed by `~3%` relative to Clang 17, demonstrating that backend LLVM version skew alone (`3%`) exceeds all bounds-checking overhead (`<1%`) and must be controlled in parity benchmarks.
- **Peak Safe-Rust Refactoring Regression to Optimized `rav1d`:** Naive safe-Rust conversion peaked at **`~11%` slower** than `dav1d` before targeted optimizations brought `rav1d` to **`~5%–6%` slower on `x86_64`** (prompting Prossimo's May–Dec 2025 `$20,000` performance bounty).
- **Targeted Safe Micro-Optimizations on Apple M3 (1 Thread, Chimera 1080p 8-bit):** Baseline `dav1d 1.5.1` = `67.912 s ± 0.541 s` vs. `rav1d` = `73.914 s ± 0.151 s` (`+8.8%` / `+6.00 s`):
  1. Replacing `Align16([0u16; TMP_LEN])` (`400`-byte `llvm.memset` per $8\times 8$ block in `cdef_filter_neon_erased`, `src/cdef.rs:623`) with `MaybeUninit::<u16>::uninit()` and hoisting `lr_bak` zero-init out of the `by` loop in `rav1d_cdef_brow` cut `cdef_filter_neon_erased` self-samples from `670` to `274` and reduced total decode time to `72.644 s` (`-1.27 s` / **`-1.7%` total runtime**).
  2. Implementing `PartialEq` on `#[repr(C)] pub struct Mv { pub y: i16, pub x: i16 }` (`align 2`, `size 4`) via `zerocopy::AsBytes` (`self.as_bytes() == other.as_bytes()`, lowering to a single 32-bit load + compare matching `dav1d`'s `union mv { struct { int16_t y, x; }; uint32_t n; }` instead of two conditional 16-bit loads blocked by LLVM `#140167`) shaved another `0.46 s` (`72.182 s`, **`-2.3%` cumulative**, closing ~30% of the M3 gap with zero `unsafe`).

#### 3.2 `imazen/rav1d-safe` (Safe Rust SIMD via `archmage`, No `.asm`/`.S`) vs. `videolan/dav1d`
Removing `dav1d`'s `236.6k` lines of hand-scheduled assembly exposes the true gap between compiler-scheduled Rust intrinsics and human-scheduled assembly (`docs/SAFE_VS_DAV1D.md:10-49`, AMD Zen 4 with AVX-512, 1 thread, `dav1d 1.5.3` vs. `rav1d-safe 0.7.0`):

### 4. Concurrency, Interior Mutability, FFI Dispatch, and the `DisjointMut` Soundness Postmortem

`Basis: source + documentation`

Porting `dav1d` exposed four deep friction points between C codec idioms and Rust's aliasing/type rules:

1. **`DisjointMut<T>` — Unchecked Release UB Risk vs. Runtime Coherence Tax (`rav1d/src/disjoint_mut.rs:33-120` & `rav1d-safe/docs/SOUNDNESS_AND_PERFORMANCE.md:15-153`):**
   - In AV1 tile/frame threading, worker threads concurrently mutate disjoint 2D sub-blocks of shared frame-wide allocations (`b: DisjointMut<Vec<Av1Block>>`, `lf_mask`, pixel planes, `cdef` line buffers) whose block sizes ($4\times 4$ up to $128\times 128$) depend dynamically on the decoded partition quadtree and cannot be statically `split_at_mut`-sliced ahead of tile execution.
   - `memorysafety/rav1d` wraps `UnsafeCell<T>` in `DisjointMut<T>`, recording active borrow intervals in `debug::DisjointMutAllBounds` **only under `#[cfg(debug_assertions)]`** (`disjoint_mut.rs:35-36, 60-73`). In release builds, `DisjointMut` performs **zero overlap checks**, relying on manual review of `pub(crate)` callers. Under the Rust / LLVM memory model, if any bug in a malformed bitstream causes two tile threads to form overlapping `&mut` or `&`+`&mut` guards simultaneously—even over provenanceless `u8`/`i16` pixels—the resulting data race and `noalias` violation is **Undefined Behavior**, not merely "wrong pixel values".
   - `imazen/rav1d-safe` keeps sharded slot-based overlap tracking enabled in default release builds (`crates/rav1d-disjoint-mut`). However:
     - **Performance Cliff:** On Apple M4 Pro (`C256_CONTENTION.md` summarized at `SOUNDNESS_AND_PERFORMANCE.md:131-139`), a single frame triggers **`569,690` borrow registrations**, with per-borrow latency escalating from **`4.52 ns` (2 threads) -> `9.18 ns` (4 threads) -> `19.71 ns` (8 threads)** due to cross-core cache-line invalidation on shard locks and `Acquire`/`Release` slot scans!
     - **Subtle Tree-Borrows / Stacked-Borrows UB (`rav1d-disjoint-mut 0.3.1`):** Storing `&'a mut [T]` directly inside `DisjointMutGuard` caused `drop(guard)` (which moves `guard` by value into `core::mem::drop<G>(x: G)`) to execute `Drop::drop(&mut self)`, releasing the tracker slot **before** `core::mem::drop` returned. A second thread could immediately acquire `&mut` to the same slice and write to it while `core::mem::drop`'s active call-frame protector on `x`'s `&mut [T]` field was still live, triggering Miri UB under **both Stacked Borrows and Tree Borrows** (`SOUNDNESS_AND_PERFORMANCE.md:17-31`)! Repairing this required storing raw `NonNull` + `PhantomData` inside guards so no protected Rust `&mut` outlives slot retirement.
2. **`RelaxedAtomic<T>` Without Bus-Locking RMW (`rav1d/src/relaxed_atomic.rs:15-49`):**
   - For shared metadata fields (`CodedBlockInfo` `cbi` bitfields) mutated by a single logical owner thread and read later after synchronization barriers, `rav1d` avoids `Mutex` contention and avoids `Atomic*::fetch_or` (which emits expensive `lock or` instructions on x86 even under `Ordering::Relaxed`!) by wrapping `atomig::Atomic<T>` in `RelaxedAtomic<T>`, exposing only `.get()` (`load(Relaxed)`), `.set()` (`store(Relaxed)`), and non-atomic `.update(|x| ...)` (`set(f(get()))`). Note `rav1d_perf_blog` line 168: an unexpected `.unwrap()` hidden inside `#[derive(Atom)]` bloated `decode_coefs` stack frames from `232` to `296` bytes until replaced by a manual `Atom` impl!
3. **Zero-Cost Hybrid FFI Function-Pointer Dispatch (`rav1d/src/ffi_safe.rs:10-57` & `src/wrap_fn_ptr.rs:41-101`):**
   - Replacing `dav1d`'s indirect DSP function-pointer tables with `match cpu_flags { ... }` enum dispatch **regressed** `rav1d` performance, and adding safe-to-unsafe Rust shims around `.asm` functions added an extra call hop because NASM `.o` objects cannot be LTO-inlined (`rav1d_perf_blog:63-66`).
   - Instead, `rav1d` keeps direct `unsafe extern "C" fn` pointers (`wrap_fn_ptr!`), **appending extra trailing `FFISafe<'a, T>` pointer arguments** to the `extern "C"` signature (`ffi_safe.rs:11-31`). When the function pointer resolves to hand-written `.asm`/`.S`, the assembly routine simply ignores the extra registers/stack slots (valid under System V AMD64 and AAPCS64 caller-cleanup calling conventions); when CPU flags select the pure-Rust fallback (`*_c_erased`), the fallback recovers `&'a T` via `FFISafe::get` without unsafe raw pointer arithmetic!

### 5. Entropy Decoding Anatomy: AV1 Daala 15-Bit Multi-Symbol rANS (`msac`) vs. VP9 8-Bit Binary Bool-Coder

`Basis: normative + source + execution`

A foundational algorithmic advance from VP9 (and H.264/HEVC CABAC) to AV1 is the transition from **bit-serial binary arithmetic coding** to **multi-symbol 15-bit CDF arithmetic coding** (derived from Daala, `AOMediaCodec/av1-spec` Clause 8.2; `rav1d/src/msac.rs:341-429`):

| Feature | VP9 Bool-Coder (`libvpx/vpx_dsp/bitreader.h`, `FFmpeg/libavcodec/vp9prob.c`) | AV1 Multi-Symbol Arithmetic Coder (`dav1d/src/msac.c`, `rav1d/src/msac.rs`) |
|---|---|---|
| Alphabet Cardinality per Step | Binary (`2` outcomes: `0` or `1`; syntax trees binarized manually) | Multi-symbol (`2` to **`16` outcomes** in one coding step; `n_symbols < 16`) |
| Probability Precision | `8`-bit `prob in 1..255` (`P(bit=0) = prob / 256`) | `15`-bit survival CDF `cdf[0..N]` (`Q15`, shifted `>> 6` to 9-bit `r * q` product) |
| Symbol Probability Update | **Frame-end backward adaptation only** (`ff_vp9_adapt_probs`, `vp9prob.c:190-272`) | **Per-symbol in-place SIMD CDF update** (`rate = 4 + (count >> 4) + (n_syms > 2)`) |
| SIMD Vectorizability | Scalar bit-serial (`1` bit per dependent branch/table lookup) | **128/256-bit SIMD across all 16 CDF entries** (`dav1d_msac_decode_symbol_adapt16_{sse2,avx2,neon}`) |
| Cross-Tile / Frame Parallelism | Backward adaptation forces frame $k+1$ entropy decode to wait for **100% of frame $k$ symbol counts** (`s->td[0].counts`)! | Optional frame-end copy of **one** chosen tile's CDF (`primary_ref_frame` / `refresh_frame_context`), never summing counts across tiles! |

Inspecting `rav1d_msac_decode_symbol_adapt_rust` (`rav1d/src/msac.rs:391-429`) alongside our executed exhaustive finite-domain probe (`Basis: execution`) reveals the exact mathematical invariants required to verify an AV1 entropy decoder panic-free and termination-safe:
1. **Normalized Window, Dual-Role `count <= 32` Sentinel, and Strict Positivity (`EC_MIN_PROB = 4`):** `s.rng` is maintained in `[0x8000, 0xFFFF]` (`[32768, 65535]`), so `r = s.rng >> 8` $\in [128, 255]$. For each candidate symbol `val in 0..=n_symbols` (`n_symbols in 1..=15`), `rav1d` computes:
   $$v(\text{val}) = \left\lfloor \frac{r \cdot (\text{cdf}[\text{val}] \gg 6)}{2} \right\rfloor + 4 \cdot (n_{\text{symbols}} - \text{val})$$
   Inspecting `rav1d/src/msac.rs:398-408` reveals a subtle dual-role representation invariant: the `loop` indexes `cdf[val as usize]` *before* testing `!(c < v)`, without an explicit `val < n_symbols` loop guard. In `dav1d`/`rav1d`'s inverted survival-CDF layout (`src/cdf.rs`), `cdf` has length `n_symbols + 1`, where `cdf[0..n_symbols]` stores strictly decreasing non-zero Q15 survival probabilities in `(0, 32768)` and `cdf[n_symbols]` stores the symbol adaptation counter `count in 0..=32` (`assert!(cdf[n_symbols as usize] <= 32)` at `line 398`). When `val` reaches `n_symbols`, `cdf[n_symbols] >> EC_PROB_SHIFT` (`EC_PROB_SHIFT = 6`) evaluates to **`count >> 6 == 0`** (because `count <= 32 < 64`), while `EC_MIN_PROB * (n_symbols - n_symbols) == 0`, forcing **`v(n_symbols) == 0`**. Because `c` is unsigned (`c_uint`), `c < 0` is identically `false`, guaranteeing loop termination at `val <= n_symbols` by overloading `count <= 32` as an in-band zero sentinel.
2. **Sub-Interval Lower and Upper Bounds (`Basis: source + execution + derived`):** Exhaustive evaluation across all $r \in [128, 255]$, $q \in [0, 511]$, and $n_{\text{symbols}} \in [1, 15]$ confirms two companion safety obligations:
   - Even when two adjacent CDF entries quantize to the identical 9-bit value (`(cdf[k-1] >> 6) == (cdf[k] >> 6)`), the `+ 4 * (n_symbols - val)` floor guarantees **`u - v >= 4`** (`ctx_norm`'s `clz(u - v)` never encounters `0`, shifting by at most `13` bits); and
   - The maximum possible `v(0)` occurs at $r = 255, q = 511, n_{\text{symbols}} = 15$, yielding $\lfloor (255 \times 511)/2 \rfloor + 60 = 65,212 \le 65,535$ (and at the minimum normalized window $r = 128$, $\lfloor (128 \times 511)/2 \rfloor + 60 = 32,764 < 32,768 = \text{rng}$), proving that `u <= s.rng` (`line 409`) holds unconditionally whenever `cdf[0] < 32768`.

### 6. AV1 & VP9 Reconstruction and Three-Stage In-Loop Restoration Pipeline

`Basis: normative + source`

Beyond entropy coding, AV1 (`dav1d` / `rav1d`) expands VP9's DSP footprint (`vp9dsp_template.c`: 8-tap regular/smooth/sharp MC, $4\times 4..32\times 32$ DCT/ADST/WHT, 10 intra modes, 16/8/4-wide deblocking) in four major ways that dominate the `236.6k`–`267.4k` lines of SIMD assembly:

1. **16-Kernel 2D Inverse Transforms up to $64\times 64$ (`src/itx.rs`, `src/itx_1d.rs`):** Pairs horizontal and vertical 1D kernels from `{DCT, ADST, FLIPADST, IDTX}` across rectangular and square blocks ($4\times 4$ up to $64\times 64$, with top-left $32\times 32$ non-zero coefficient zeroing on $64\times 64$/$64\times 16$/$16\times 64$ blocks), plus lossless $4\times 4$ Walsh-Hadamard (`WHT_WHT`).
2. **Warped / Compound Motion Compensation (`src/mc.rs`, `src/warpmv.rs`):** Adds local and global $8\times 8$ homography/affine warped motion (8-tap variable-phase per-pixel shear filtering), wedge/difference-modulated/inter-intra compound masks (`src/wedge.rs`), and Overlapped Block Motion Compensation (`OBMC`).
3. **Constrained Directional Enhancement Filter (`CDEF`, `src/cdef.rs`, `arXiv:1602.05975`):** Runs *after* deblocking on $8\times 8$ blocks:
   - First estimates dominant edge orientation `dir in 0..7` by minimizing directional squared residual variance $E_d$ across 8 angles using rapid SIMD shifts/accumulations (`cdef_dir`);
   - Then applies a nonlinear primary (`pri_strength` along `dir`) and secondary (`sec_strength` at $\pm 45^\circ$ to `dir`) 12-tap filter where neighbor differences $\Delta = p_{\text{tap}} - p_{\text{center}}$ pass through the piecewise clamp `constrain(diff, threshold, damping) = sign(diff) * min(|diff|, max(0, threshold - (|diff| >> shift)))`, suppressing ringing mosquito noise without blurring sharp edges.
4. **Loop Restoration (`Wiener` + Self-Guided `SGRPROJ`, `src/looprestoration.rs`, `3,549` lines) and Film Grain Synthesis (`src/filmgrain.rs`, `1,381` lines):**
   - Operates on Loop Restoration Units ($64\times 64$, $128\times 128$, or $256\times 256$), selecting per-unit between bypass, a separable symmetric **7-tap Wiener filter**, or **Self-Guided Restoration (`SGRPROJ`)**—which computes two fast $3\times 3$ and $5\times 5$ local mean/variance guided box filters (`sgr_3x3`, `sgr_5x5`, `sgr_mix`) using reciprocal LUT `dav1d_sgr_x_by_x` (`sgr_one_by_x = 164` for $n=25$, `455` for $n=9$ at `looprestoration.rs:649`), reading saved pre-deblock bottom stripe rows (`lr_bak`) across superblock boundaries to avoid vertical dependency cascades!
   - Finally, **out-of-loop Film Grain Synthesis** (`src/filmgrain.rs`) generates pseudo-random $64\times 64$ luma / $32\times 32$ chroma grain blocks via a bit-exact 16-bit LFSR PRNG and 2D order-3 auto-regressive (`AR`) filter (`generate_grain_y`, `generate_grain_uv`), then scales grain intensity per $32\times 32$ block (`fgy_32x32xn`, `fguv_32x32xn`) via a 14-point piecewise-linear LUT of local luma intensity (and chroma-from-luma `uv_luma_mult` correlation) without altering DPB reference frames.

### 7. Rust Bounds-Check Elimination & LLVM Codegen Lessons for Verified Clean-Room FFmpeg

`Basis: source + documentation + derived`

Synthesizing the empirical record of `memorysafety/rav1d`, `imazen/rav1d-safe`, and `FFmpeg/FFmpeg` across our four-level **Verification Priority Stack** yields a concrete architectural blueprint for an Anneal-verified clean-room rewrite of FFmpeg's VP9, AV1, and broader codec suite:

1. **Priority 1 — UB-Freedom Without Runtime `DisjointMut` Coherence Tax:**
   - **Never copy `rav1d`'s unchecked release `DisjointMut` (`UnsafeCell`) or `rav1d-safe`'s `569k`-registrations/frame lock-sharded tracker:** Instead, architect tile/superblock buffers around **two zero-cost, statically verifiable ownership patterns**:
     1. *Worker-Private Superblock-Row Scratch + Verified Bulk Publish:* Each tile worker reconstructs into an owned, aligned stack/arena buffer (`&mut [Pixel; SB_ROW_CAP]`) using ordinary exclusive `&mut` Rust slices (zero `UnsafeCell`, zero atomic borrows, full LLVM `noalias` vectorization!), then publishes completed superblock rows at tile/row synchronization points; and
     2. *Static Ghost Spatial Partitioning (`PureLift` Disjoint Slice Lenses):* Where in-place plane or `Av1Block` grid mutation across non-overlapping 2D tile rectangles `[tx0..tx1) x [ty0..ty1)` is required, construct a tile-grid view `StridedTileMut<'frame, T>` whose non-overlap proof obligation (`tile_rect(i) ∩ tile_rect(j) = ∅` for `i != j`, using `NonNull<T>` + `PhantomData<&'frame mut [T]>` to avoid the `rav1d-disjoint-mut 0.3.1` Tree-Borrows `Drop` protector bug!) is discharged **once per frame at tile-grid setup in Lean** at **0 ns per block access**.
2. **Priority 2 — Panic-Freedom Without `panic = "abort"` Traps:**
   - Replace `rav1d`'s `Cargo.toml` `panic = "abort"`, `Mutex::try_lock().unwrap()`, `wrap_fn_ptr!` `default_unimplemented`, and `msac.rs` runtime `assert!` calls with **total, non-panicking MIR**:
     - Represent DSP dispatch tables as exhaustive structs of total fn pointers initialized from scalar reference kernels at construction (never `unimplemented!()`).
     - Encode `rav1d`'s bounds-check-elimination patterns (`pre-slicing` `&mut dst[..len]` outside loops, `usize` widening *before* `offset + len` to prevent wraparound lower-bound checks, and `InRange<T, MIN, MAX>`) directly as **verified refinement types** (`Fin<N>`, `ValidatedCdf<N>` maintaining the exact `cdf[0] < 32768 && cdf[N] <= 32` loop-sentinel invariant proved in Finding 5!) without needing `unsafe { assert_unchecked(...) }`.
     - Outline cold error-return formatting (`#[cold] #[inline(never)]`) and avoid hidden panics in derives so hot functions (`decode_coefs`, `decode_b`) maintain compact stack frames (<240 bytes), preventing LLVM register spills and `cmov`/`csel` branch-conversion regressions; use explicit bitmask selection (`(a & mask) | (b & !mask)` or `select_unpredictable`) on unpredictable entropy branches (`rav1d` PR `#1257`).
     - Use `vec![0; len]` (`alloc_zeroed` OS zero-page demand paging) for multi-megabyte DPB frame pools rather than `Vec::resize`, and `MaybeUninit` (with proved write-before-read coverage in Lean) for hot per-block scratch pads like CDEF's 400-byte `tmp_buf`.
     - Implement `PartialEq` on `#[repr(C)]` POD motion-vector / reference-pair structs (`Mv`, `RefMvsPair`) via `zerocopy::IntoBytes` / `AsBytes` byte-slice equality so LLVM emits single-instruction `u32`/`u64` comparisons despite Rust issue `#140167`.
3. **Priority 3 — Behavioral & SIMD-vs-Scalar Equivalence at 100% `dav1d` / `ffvp9` Speed:**
   - The `rav1d-safe` Zen 4 profile proves that **pure safe Rust + `archmage` intrinsics already matches `dav1d` on entropy (`0.99x`), but loses `2.0x` overall unless MC (fused 6-tap/8-tap `pmaddubsw` + warp) and CDEF (SIMD `u8->u16` padding + 256/512-bit 8-direction search) match the exact register-fused algorithms of `dav1d`'s assembly**.
   - Therefore, an Anneal-verified rewrite has a clear two-tier kernel strategy:
     - **Tier A (Verified Fused Intrinsics / `core::arch`):** Implement the exact fused 6-tap/8-tap `vpmaddubsw` / `usdot` / RVV `vwmaccus`, SIMD CDEF padding/direction, Wiener/SGR, and `itx` kernels in Rust target-feature functions (avoiding 2-pass `i16` memory round-trips) and verify each fixed-block kernel (`4x4`..`64x64`) equivalent to the clean scalar reference kernel in Lean. Unlike external `.asm` files, Rust intrinsic kernels participate in LTO inlining and need no `FFISafe` ABI-smuggling pointers!
     - **Tier B (Formalized ISA Assembly Where LLVM Regalloc Lags):** For the handful of extreme register-pressure kernels where LLVM's register allocator still trails hand-scheduled NASM/GAS by >5% (such as scaled/warped MC or 16-bit $32\times 32$/$64\times 64$ `itx` on 32-register AArch64 / AVX-512), lift and verify the assembly routine directly against existing `x86_64` / `aarch64` / `riscv64` ISA formalizations, discharging memory-bounds and calling-convention preconditions at the Rust boundary.
4. **Priority 4 — Full Functional Spec Conformance & Drop-In `FFmpeg` Architecture:**
   - In `FFmpeg`, replace **both** the `libdav1d.c` external dependency and the `ENOSYS` software gap in `av1dec.c` with a single first-party verified Rust AV1 decoder sharing OBU syntax definitions with `cbs_av1` and GPU `hwaccel` slice submission, and unify `ffvp9` (`libavcodec/vp9*.c`) under the same tile-parallel plane-ownership and verified DSP framework (preserving `ffvp9`'s frame-end `adapt_prob` barrier explicitly in the frame-thread state machine). Differential conformance gates run all **803 `dav1d-test-data` vectors** across every SIMD dispatch tier (`Scalar`, `SSE4`/`AVX2`, `AVX-512`, `NEON`, `RVV`) and thread count (`1, 2, 4, 8`) plus FFmpeg FATE `vp9-*` suites.
