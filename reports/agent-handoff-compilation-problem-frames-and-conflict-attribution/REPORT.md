# Sequential Multi-Stage Handoff Compilation, WRSPM/KAOS Problem-Frame Obligations, and Minimal-Conflict Complexity Attribution

## Summary

Primary-source inspection of 22 peer-reviewed requirements-engineering, business-artifact, automated-reasoning, and multi-agent systems studies (1987–2026), eight pinned repository revisions across four open-source systems (`FoundationAgents/MetaGPT`, `OpenBMB/ChatDev`, `liffiton/MARCO`, and `pysathq/pysat`), and a deterministic four-probe Python 3.10+ verification suite establishes three invariants governing sequential multi-stage specification-to-implementation pipelines:

1. **Deliberate-Before-Compile Handoffs Eliminate Both Multi-Hop Prose Drift and Answer-First Schema Suppression (`Mohamed et al. 2025`, `Tam et al. 2024`, `MetaGPT`, `ChatDev`, `GSM`, `Probe 4`):** Passing unconstrained prose across sequential LLM handoffs induces front-loaded concave semantic erosion: in `Mohamed et al. (ACL 2025)` monolingual English rephrasing (`§4.4` & `Appendix D.4` `Table 8`, `News2024`, where `1` iteration = `1` handoff), `BLEURT` drops from `0.945` (hop `0`) to **`0.645` (`Llama`), `0.593` (`Mistral`), `0.614` (`Llama + Mistral`), and `0.587` (`Llama + Mistral + Gemma`) by hop `3`** (`0.602 / 0.562 / 0.572 / 0.531` by hop `10`), while bilingual round-trip chains (`Table 5`, `2` handoffs/iteration) drop from `0.949` to `0.684` (`EN↔FR`) and `0.392` (`EN↔TH`) at iteration `3` (`6` handoffs), with negative `FActScore` gradients across every setting (`-0.004` to `-0.038`/iteration on `News2024`; `-0.040`/iteration on `BookSum`, `Table 2`). Conversely, in `Tam et al. (EMNLP 2024, arXiv:2408.02442v3)`, large single-pass `JSON-mode` reasoning drops on `GSM8K` (`-41.5 pp` `Gemini-1.5-Flash` [`89.3% → 47.78%`], `-46.7 pp` `GPT-3.5-Turbo` [`76.6% → 29.87%`], `-7.62 pp` `gpt-4o-mini` [`94.57% → 86.95%`], `Tables 2 & 11`) arise specifically when schema emission suppresses unconstrained deliberation (`§5.1`: **`100%`** of `GPT-3.5-Turbo` `JSON-mode` outputs emitted `"answer"` before `"reason"`), whereas CFG-backed `JSON-Schema` with structured reasoning fields (`Table 2`) reaches **`91.71%` on `GSM8K` (`-2.86 pp`) and `86.07% > 83.11% NL` on `Last Letter`** (distinct from prompt-only `FRI` drops in `Table 1`, which coincided with `22.75%–60.07%` parse errors in `Table 3`). Separating **unconstrained intra-stage deliberation** from **strictly validated inter-stage compilation** (`MetaGPT` `ActionNode._aask_v1` scoring `3.67/4.0` `SoftwareDev` executability without runtime feedback vs. `2.25/4.0` for dialogue-chained `ChatDev`; `ChatDev` `CDH` + $\tau$-extraction; and Guard-Stage-Milestone `GSM` declarative `PAC`/`PDG` lifecycles enforcing `GSM-1`..`GSM-3` and `Toggle-Once`) avoids both failure modes while preventing silent postprocessor corruption (such as `MetaGPT` `ActionNode.xml_fill` coercing malformed primitives to `0`/`False`/`[]`/`{}`, crashing `create_class()` on required `List[str]`, and silently overwriting `Optional[str]` fields with hardcoded `Field(default=child.example)` prompt templates).
2. **Strengthened `WRSPM` Relative Consistency (`Eq. 5`) and `KAOS` Complete `OR` / Minimal `AND` Refinement Catch Partial-Input Vacuity and Phenomenon Leakage (`Zave & Jackson 1997`, `Gunter et al. 2000`, `van Lamsweerde & Letier 2000`, `Probe 3`):** Partitioning world and machine phenomena into disjoint environment-controlled ($e = e_h \uplus e_v$) and system-controlled ($s = s_h \uplus s_v$) sets restricts designated vocabularies across all five artifacts ($\text{free}(W), \text{free}(R) \subseteq e_h \cup e_v \cup s_v$; $\text{free}(P), \text{free}(M) \subseteq e_v \cup s_v \cup s_h$; and **$\text{free}(S) \subseteq e_v \cup s_v$**) and requires discharging not only adequacy $\forall e, s.\; (W \wedge S) \Rightarrow R$ (`Eq. 4`) and system-side implementability (`Eq. 6`), but **strengthened environment-side relative consistency (`Eq. 5`)**:
   $$\forall e_v.\; (\exists e_h, s.\; W) \Rightarrow \Big((\exists s.\; S) \wedge (\forall s.\; S \Rightarrow \exists e_h.\; W)\Big)$$
   Bare satisfiability $\exists e, s.\; W \wedge S$ and `Eq. (4)` both pass (`True`) when $S$ assumes away fault sensor inputs $e_v$ (`Probe 3`, Case 2), and weak relative consistency (`Eq. 7`) passes (`True`) when $S$ permits $W$-incompatible actuator outputs (`Case 3`); only `Eq. (5)` refutes both defects. Dualizing bridge assumptions in $W$ via `KAOS` (`van Lamsweerde & Letier, IEEE TSE 2000, §3.1–3.3`) requires scoping obstacles to active goal preconditions (e.g., $\lozenge(\texttt{MovingOnRunway} \wedge \neg\texttt{WheelsOut})$ so airborne states do not violate obstruction $\{O, \mathit{Dom}\} \models \neg\mathit{Ass}$) and verifying **complete OR-refinements** ($\{O_i, \mathit{Dom}\} \models O$, $\{O_i, \mathit{Dom}\} \not\models \text{false}$, $\{\bigwedge_i \neg O_i, \mathit{Dom}\} \models \neg O$, exemplified by the A320 braking obstacle tree $\{O_1, O_2, O_3\}$) alongside **minimal AND-refinements** (including Condition 3 irredundancy $\forall i.\; \{\bigwedge_{j \neq i} O_j, \mathit{Dom}\} \not\models O$).
3. **`QuickXplain` Duality (`Propositions 3–4`) and `MARCO+` Cost-Stratum `MCS` Enumeration Expose Multi-Core Complexity Cliffs Masked by Zero Leave-One-Out Shadow Prices (`Junker 2004`, `Liffiton et al. 2016`, `Ignatiev et al. 2019`, `Probes 1–2`):** For non-empty consistent background $B \neq \emptyset$ and $n$ priority-ordered soft constraints $C$, `QuickXplain` isolates the unique $<_{\text{antilex}}$-minimum `MUS` $X^*$ of size $k$ in **$\log_2(n/k) + 2k$ (best case) to $2k\log_2(n/k) + 2k$ (worst case)** consistency checks (`-1` when $B = \emptyset$). By `Junker (AAAI 2004, p. 168)` **Proposition 3**, running `QuickXplain` on reversed priorities under the negated consistency oracle $\neg\text{isConsistent}(B \cup (C \setminus S'))$ isolates the preferred `MCS` $Y^* = C \setminus R^*$ whose complement $R^*$ is the unique $<_{\text{lex}}$-minimum `MSS`, satisfying **Proposition 4** ($\min_<(C \setminus R^*) = \max_<(X^*)$). For global trade-off mapping, `MARCO+` exploits exact minimal hitting-set duality ($\mathcal{C} = \text{MHS}(\mathcal{U}), \mathcal{U} = \text{MHS}(\mathcal{C})$) with `0` `Grow()` calls under both cardinality-maximal (`MinicardMapSolver`) and inclusion-maximal (`MinisatMapSolver.maximize_seed`) seeds. Whenever $\ge 2$ conflict cores co-force a high-cost architectural mechanism (e.g., tier $m_3$ of weight `20` forced by $\{r_1, r_3\}$ and $\{r_2, r_4\}$, full cost `26`), single-requirement leave-one-out shadow prices vanish ($\Delta(r_1) = \Delta(r_2) = \Delta(r_3) = 0$), whereas cost-stratum `MARCO+` `MCS` enumeration paired with `RC2Stratified` `WMaxSAT` exposes the size-2 `MCS` $\{r_1, r_2\}$ saving **`17 > 0 + 0`** (`total cost = 13`).

#### 1.2 `ChatDev`: Phase-Gate Extraction ($\tau$), `<INFO>` Guard Quirks, and `Communicative Dehallucination`

`Basis: source + documentation + derived`

   - In `v2.2.0` (`3c72d860`) and `main` (`464a681b`), `GraphExecutor` (`workflow/graph.py:L53–860` on `v2.2.0` / `L53–862` on `main`: `__init__` `L56–102`, `run` `L260–342`, `_process_edge_output` `L396–444`, `_execute_node` `L549–657`) replaces `<INFO>` string sentinels with explicit `EdgeConfig` flags (`entity/configs/edge/edge.py:L19–62` in `151`-line file: `trigger`, `carry_data`, `keep_message`, `clear_context`, `clear_kept_context`) and `EdgeProcessorConfig` payload transformers (`entity/configs/edge/edge_processor.py:L37–334`).

#### 1.3 Declarative `GSM` Artifact Semantics and the Deliberate-Before-Compile Crossover

`Basis: normative + documentation + execution (standalone Python 3.13 stdlib declarative GSM PAC/PDG engine and parametric calculation in Probe 4) + derived`

1. **Declarative Guard-Stage-Milestone (`GSM`) `PAC` / `PDG` Invariants (`Nigam & Caswell, 2003`; `Hull et al., WS-FM 2010, LNCS 6551, pp. 1–24, 2011`; `Hull et al., DEBS 2011, pp. 51–62`; `Damaggio, Hull, & Vaculín, Information Systems 38(4):561–584, 2013`; `Probe 4`):**
   - An artifact type $(R, x, \mathit{Att}_{\text{data}} \uplus \mathit{Att}_{\text{status}}, \mathit{Typ}, \mathit{Stg}, \mathit{Mst}, \mathit{Lcyc})$ with $\mathit{Lcyc} = (\mathit{Substages}, \mathit{Task}, \mathit{Owns}, \mathit{Guards}, \mathit{Ach}, \mathit{Inv})$ maintains three snapshot invariants over pre-snapshot $\sigma$ (`DEBS 2011`, `§3.2–3.3`, pp. 56–57):

### 2. Requirements Problem Frames ($W, S \vdash R$) and `KAOS` Goal-Obstacle Refinement

#### 2.1 `WRSPM` Four-Phenomenon Partitioning and Strengthened Relative Consistency

`Basis: normative + execution (standalone Python 3.13 stdlib exhaustive 256-state WRSPM model checker in Probe 3) + derived`

1. **Five-Artifact Designated Vocabulary Discipline (`Zave & Jackson, TOSEM 1997`; `Gunter, Gunter, Jackson, & Zave, IEEE Software 17(3):37–43, 2000` [printed byline p. 37; reversed in Crossref]; `Jackson, 2001`; `Probe 3`):**
   - Partitioning phenomena into hidden/visible environment-controlled ($e = e_h \uplus e_v$) and visible/hidden system-controlled ($s = s_h \uplus s_v$, $e \cap s = \emptyset$) enforces five semantic support inclusions (`p. 38`, verified via exact 256-state truth-table support in `Probe 3`):
     $$\text{free}(W), \text{free}(R) \subseteq e_h \cup e_v \cup s_v, \qquad \text{free}(P), \text{free}(M) \subseteq e_v \cup s_v \cup s_h, \qquad \text{free}(S) \subseteq e_v \cup s_v$$
     Referencing $e_h$ (`moving_on_runway`) or $s_h$ (`kalman_register_ok`) inside $S$ (`Case 4`) violates interface observability (`s_vocab_ok = False`), whereas coupling hidden $s_h$ strictly inside platform $M$ (`kalman_register_ok ⇔ wow_airspeed_on`) and program $P$ (`reverse_thrust_cmd ⇔ (wheel_pulse_on ∨ kalman_register_ok)`) realizes sensor-redundant $S$ (`reverse_thrust_cmd ⇔ (wheel_pulse_on ∨ wow_airspeed_on)`) cleanly under bidirectional flight-safe $R$ (`reverse_thrust_cmd ⇔ moving_on_runway`, preventing both runway overrun and mid-air thrust reversal in `Case 5`).
2. **Hierarchy of Consistency and Implementability Obligations (`Gunter et al., 2000`, `Eqs. 2, 4, 5, 6, 7`, pp. 39–40; `Probe 3`):**
   - **Domain Consistency (`Eq. 2`):** $\exists e, s.\; W$.
   - **Adequacy (`Eq. 4`):** $\forall e, s.\; (W \wedge S) \Rightarrow R$.
   - **Weak (`Eq. 7`) vs. Strengthened (`Eq. 5`) Environment-Side Relative Consistency:**
     $$\text{Eq. (7): } \forall e_v.\; (\exists e_h, s.\; W) \Rightarrow (\exists e_h, s.\; W \wedge S), \qquad \text{Eq. (5): } \forall e_v.\; (\exists e_h, s.\; W) \Rightarrow \Big((\exists s.\; S) \wedge (\forall s.\; S \Rightarrow \exists e_h.\; W)\Big)$$
     - *Partial-input vacuity (`Case 2`):* When $S_{\text{partial\_vac}}$ conjoins `wow_airspeed_on ⇒ wheel_pulse_on` (assuming away aquaplaning inside $S$), **both bare satisfiability $\exists e, s.\; W \wedge S$ (`True`) and `Eq. (4)` Adequacy (`True`) pass**, because $W \wedge S$ is satisfied on dry landings and false on wet runways; `Eq. (5)` (`False`) catches the illegal restriction on uncontrollable $e_v$.
     - *Output-compatibility gap (`Case 3`):* Under actuator back-pressure coupling in $W$, permissive $S$ satisfies `Eq. (7)` (`True`, since one safe output exists) while failing `Eq. (5)` (`False`, since $S$ also permits an output $s_v$ contradicting $W$ for every $e_h$).
   - **System-Side Implementability (`Eq. 6`):** $\forall e.\; (\exists s.\; S) \Rightarrow \big((\exists s.\; M \wedge P) \wedge (\forall s.\; (M \wedge P) \Rightarrow S)\big)$ (verified load-bearing in `Case 5`: dropping $M_{\text{platform\_kalman}}$ falsifies `Eq. 6`).

#### 2.2 `KAOS` Obstacle Completeness, Complete `OR` vs. Minimal `AND` Refinement, and LLM4RE

`Basis: normative + documentation + execution (standalone Python 3.13 stdlib KAOS obstacle and AND/OR-refinement checker in Probe 3) + derived`

   - Obstacle $O$ obstructs goal/assumption $G$ in $\mathit{Dom}$ iff $\{O, \mathit{Dom}\} \models \neg G$ (**obstruction**) and $\{O, \mathit{Dom}\} \not\models \text{false}$ (**domain consistency**), and $\{O_1, \dots, O_n\}$ is **domain-complete** w.r.t. $G$ iff $\{\neg O_1 \wedge \dots \wedge \neg O_n, \mathit{Dom}\} \models G$.

   - **Complete `OR`-Refinement (`§3.3`, p. 984):** $\{O_1, \dots, O_n\}$ is a complete `OR`-refinement of parent obstacle $O$ iff (1) $\forall i.\; \{O_i, \mathit{Dom}\} \models O$, (2) $\forall i.\; \{O_i, \mathit{Dom}\} \not\models \text{false}$, and (3) $\{\neg O_1 \wedge \dots \wedge \neg O_n, \mathit{Dom}\} \models \neg O$ (with optional **disjointness** $\forall i \neq j.\; \{O_i \wedge O_j, \mathit{Dom}\} \models \text{false}$).    - **Minimal `AND`-Refinement (`§3.3`, p. 984):** $\{O_1, \dots, O_n\}$ is a minimal `AND`-refinement of $O$ iff (1) $\{O_1 \wedge \dots \wedge O_n, \mathit{Dom}\} \models O$, (2) $\{O_1 \wedge \dots \wedge O_n, \mathit{Dom}\} \not\models \text{false}$, **and (3) minimality / irredundancy**:
     $$\forall i \in \{1, \dots, n\}.\; \Big\{\bigwedge_{j \neq i} O_j,\; \mathit{Dom}\Big\} \not\models O$$
