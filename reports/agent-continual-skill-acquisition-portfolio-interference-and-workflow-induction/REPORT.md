# Continual Skill Acquisition, Toolbox Pruning, Cross-Skill Portfolio Interference, Workflow Induction, Negation Polarity, and Structure-Preserving Skill Optimization

## Summary

Primary-source inspection of 42 peer-reviewed papers and preprints (2023–2026), line-level source audits of 12 pinned public repositories (`MineDojo/Voyager`, `zorazrw/TroVE`, `OSU-NLP-Group/SkillWeaver`, `CharlesQ9/Alita`, `zorazrw/agent-workflow-memory`, `LeapLabTHU/ExpeL`, `suzgunmirac/dynamic-cheatsheet`, `LitaoGuo/How-Self-Evolution-Reshapes-Skills`, `benchflow-ai/skillsbench`, `anishh15/WhoWins`, `allenai/IFBench`, `microsoft/sammo`), and a deterministic four-probe verification suite (`support/verify_continual_skills_interference_sammo_probes.py`) establish five operational invariants for continual agent skill evolution, multi-skill curation, conflict resolution, and prohibition design:

1. **The Three-Gate Law Separating Productive Skill Induction from Self-Generated Collapse:** Online or compile-time skill, tool, and workflow evolution improves out-of-distribution task performance only when three orthogonal gates hold jointly (`[derived]` synthesis across 22 studies): **(G1) Non-parametric external grounding** (environment execution/state verification, held-out unit tests, multi-sample execution agreement, or environment-verified `(failure, success)` contrasts) rather than zero-feedback parametric self-drafting (`SkillsBench v1.1`: `-8.1` to `-11.5 pp`) or unverified self-reflection (`ExpeL w/ Reflexion`: `-10.0 pp`); **(G2) Calibrated non-regression admission gating** (strict held-out validation $s(S') > s(S)$ on $D_{\text{sel}}$ as in `SkillOpt`, swap-order-calibrated pairwise selection at `98.3%` vs. untrained `55.6%` in `EvoGuard`, or 95% Wilson lower-bound multi-family corroboration in `Learning by Doing`); and **(G3) Bounded-capacity pruning plus structural slot isolation** (`TroVE` periodic usage trimming cutting library bloat `74%–90%` and preventing `-7` to `-21 pp` unpruned `INSTANCE` degradation, paired with `SAMMO` AST path-scoped overrides, `SkillOpt` localized $L_t \le 4$ atomic edits, or `SkillDiff` insert-only markers).
2. **Prior Drift Front-Loads Out-of-Prior Knowledge Loss, and Implementation Seams Silently Corrupt Persistent Libraries:** Repeatedly rewriting expert `SKILL.md` files without task feedback (`Guo et al.`, 30 rounds across 6 agents and `8,981` segmented items) degrades `SkillsBench` (`40.2% → 25.7%`, `-14.6 pp`), `LogicBench` (`-14.7 pp`), and `MedCalc-Bench` (`-11.1 pp`): `84.2%` of `10/10` in-prior items survive Round 30 vs. **`23.1%` of `0/10` out-of-prior items (`61.1 pp` gap)**, with **`49.5%` of `SkillsBench` `0/10` items lost in Round 1 alone** (`6.0%` return by Round 30 vs. `30.0%` for `>=1/10`; `Probe 2A`). Direct repository audits uncover concrete silent-failure seams in widely cited implementations: `TroVE`'s `format_toolbox(topk=10)` global frequency sort (`utils/code.py:312–321`) starves `100%` of newly induced `frequency = 1` specialist tools once 10 core tools reach `frequency >= 2`, while `run_trove.py:177` implements a `1.5372×` stricter pruning threshold ($\log_{20} n$ vs. $0.5 \log_{10} n$) (`Probe 1A`); `Dynamic Cheatsheet` (`extractor.py:78–86`) silently overwrites accumulated memory on `max_tokens` mid-tag truncation or `<think>` tag mentions, requiring `<think>`-preamble stripping plus trailing-open-tag rejection (`0/350` corruptions vs. `250/350` pinned and `150/350` unstripped `rfind`; `Probe 3A`); `ExpeL` (`agent/expel.py:683–741, 369–374`) drops multi-sentence `ADD`s and hijacks `EDIT`s via substring matching while mis-indexing non-zero fold histories (`Probe 3B`); and `AWM` (`mind2web/online_induction.py:38–44`) omits `AutoEval` filtering and zips unsorted `os.listdir` entries against ordered test samples (`Table 11` also shows `AWM_off+on` drops `Cross-Task SR` `4.8% → 1.6%` via offline+online workflow interference).
3. **Multi-Skill Portfolio Interference Stems from Competing Sibling Methods and Cross-Modality Distractors:** Across all `87` `SkillsBench v1.1` tasks (`f860ecf02e`, decontaminating `15` `v1.0` container-polluted baseline trials; fleet macro mean `31.6% → 49.2%`, `+17.6 pp`), `66` tasks (`75.9%`) bundle `1–3` skills (`+18.0` to `+19.0 pp`) whereas `21` tasks (`24.1%`) bundle `>= 4` skills (`+10.1 pp`), peaking at `video-silence-remover` (`7` skills) and `multilingual-video-dubbing` (`6` skills). Direct inspection shows `>= 4`-skill bundles inject mutually competing sibling algorithms (`exoplanet-detection-period`: `box-least-squares`, `lomb-scargle-periodogram`, `transit-least-squares` alongside `exoplanet-workflows` and `light-curve-preprocessing`) or cross-modality distractors (`jpg-ocr-stat`: `video-frame-extraction` and `pdf` alongside `image-ocr`, `openai-vision`, and `xlsx`), and even curated skills cause domain regressions up to `-24.0 pp` (`Opus 4.8` Mathematics `57.1% → 33.1%`).
4. **Silent Instruction-Conflict Execution vs. False-Positive Over-Refusal, and Explicit Priority-Tier Contracts:** Reasoning and chat models detect intra-prompt conflicts when asked (`ConInstruct` `DeepSeek-R1` `91.5%` F1) yet **silently pick one side without mentioning conflicts in `90.5%–100%` of normal executions** (`WhoWins` spontaneous flagging `<= 2.92%`). Appending conflict-check guiding prompts (`ConInstruct` `GP`) or forced prohibition self-cues (`DUET` `Qwen3-8B` prefix) triggers `28%–53%` false-positive clarifications on clean prompts and `-40.0 pp` benign utility collapse (`86.0% → 46.0%`), making them net-harmful below high conflict prevalences ($\pi^*(1) = 30.08\%$ on `GPT-4o`, $71.81\%$ on `Qwen3-8B` prefix; `Probe 4A`). In `WhoWins` (`14` open-weight models across `Qwen 3`, `Qwen 3.5`, `gpt-oss`), native `system`/`developer` roles lose `-6.41` to `-48.75 pp` to labelled `user` policies on `tool` and `write` tasks (`26/26` modes), whereas declaring an explicit `Tier 1..6` precedence contract with `thinking=on` raises within-`Tiers` compliance (`Qwen3.5-35B` `67.93% → 93.64%` decided, `Qwen3.5-27B` `93.91% → 98.51%` decided / `82.10% → 87.08%` usable), though conflict-only abstentions still cap usable reliability at `R(0.95) <= 1` tier across all `26/26` modes.
5. **Negation Compliance Bifurcates Across Lexical Output Priming, Runtime Policy Over-Refusal, and Coding Action-Scope Guardrails:** Negative constraints (`"Do not X"`) split into three mechanistically distinct regimes: **(Regime 1) Token-space output prohibitions** backfire under high prior token probability (`Semantic Gravity Wells`, `Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` single-word completions: $p(\text{viol}) = \sigma(-2.40 + 2.27 P_0)$, fitted `10.22%` at $P_0 = 0.10$ to `46.75%` at $P_0 = 1.00$; `87.5%` target-mention attention priming + `12.5%` late-layer FFN override; `Polarity Blindness` increases harm in `8/10` low-reasoning models while `Claude Sonnet 4.5` and `GPT-5 Nano` resist), requiring affirmative replacement schemas or DFA-complement constrained decoding (`NCO`); **(Regime 2) Runtime business/tool prohibitions** suffer uncalibrated CoT over-refusal unless compliance and utility teachers are decoupled (`DUET-8B`: `85.2%` compliance, `91.0%` benign utility, $\pi^* = 0.00\%$); and **(Regime 3) Multi-step coding action-scope guardrails** (`Guardrails Beat Guidance`, `Claude Opus 4.6`, $n = 35$ discriminative SWE-bench tasks) prune out-of-scope file/dependency edits (`3/3` rules with $\Delta > +5\text{ pp}$ are negative scope prohibitions vs. `4/4` rules with $\Delta < -5\text{ pp}$ positive directives, post-hoc Fisher $p = 0.0286$ at $|\Delta| > 3/5\text{ pp}$, though threshold-free Mann–Whitney across all `18` rules is non-significant at $p = 0.1299$). For skill optimization, `SAMMO` guarantees SHA-256 byte invariance on untargeted AST sections **only when mutators use unique `#reference_id` selectors targeting `Section`/`Paragraph` nodes** (a shared `.tunable` class selector silently overwrites 2nd+ matched sections with `matches[0]`'s rewrite at `mutators.py:381–385`, and targeting `InputData` raises `AttributeError` at `L381`; `Probe 3C`), while `SkillOpt` (`51` wins + `1` tie across `52` cells, `11/11` positive transfers) outperforms holistic `TextGrad`/`EvoSkill` rewriters (`-12.2` to `-24.0 pp`).

#### 1.3 `TroVE` Pruning Proof and Five Pinned Source-vs.-Paper Discrepancies (`zorazrw/TroVE@c4d16b6a2e`)

- **Empirical Proof That Unpruned Toolboxes Hurt Both Accuracy and Human Oversight (`2401.12869v1` Tables 2, 3, 5, 8; §5.1, §8.2):**
  - On `CodeLlama-7B-Instruct` (`Table 2`), unpruned per-instance induction (`INSTANCE`) inflates toolboxes to `3,175` (`TabMWP`), `537` (`WTQ`), and `395` (`GQA`) functions and **falls below zero-tool `PRIMITIVE`**: `TabMWP` `0.43 (PRIMITIVE) → 0.36 (INSTANCE, -7 pp) vs. 0.47 (TroVE, 11 tools)`; `WTQ` `0.20 → 0.17 (-3 pp) vs. 0.21 (TroVE, 8 tools)`; `GQA` `0.37 → 0.16 (-21 pp) vs. 0.44 (TroVE, 7 tools, +28 pp over INSTANCE)`; `HiTab` `0.09 → 0.11 vs. 0.18 (TroVE, 6 tools)`.
  - Inside `TroVE` (`Table 8`, evaluated on `MATH_algebra`, `HiTab`, and `GQA`; Fig. 8), disabling periodic trimming inflates libraries `4×–10×` (`74%–90%` reduction lost) and drops `HiTab` `0.18 → 0.15` (`-3 pp`) and `GQA` `0.44 → 0.39` (`-5 pp`). *(Protocol note: §5.1 discloses `TroVE` runs each experiment 5 times and reports best-of-5; randomized stream ordering in Table 7 scores `0.23–0.24` vs. `0.25` on `MATH_algebra`.)*
  - On `WTQ` human trajectory auditing (`Table 5`, `100` trajectories $\times$ `6` annotators), `INSTANCE` increases human verification time **`+20.4%`** (`25.5 s → 30.7 s`, `0.78` accuracy), whereas `TroVE` cuts verification time **`-31.4%` (`17.5 s`, `-43.0%` vs. `INSTANCE`)** and raises human verification accuracy `0.77 → 0.87` (`+10 pp`).
- **Five Audited Code-vs.-Paper Discrepancies in `zorazrw/TroVE@c4d16b6a2e` (`Basis: source + execution`, `Probe 1A`):**
  1. **`53.72%` Stricter Pruning Base (`run_trove.py:177`):** Paper §3.3 specifies $\lambda(n) = 0.5\log_{10} n = \log_{100} n$ ($\lambda(1000) = 1.500$, keeping `frequency >= 2`), whereas `run_trove.py:177` executes `threshold = math.log(n, 20)` ($\frac{\ln 10}{0.5 \ln 20} = 1.5372\times$ higher; $\lambda(1000) = 2.306$, deleting every `frequency == 2` tool).
  2. **Silent `format_toolbox(topk=10)` Specialist Starvation (`utils/code.py:312–321`):** `format_toolbox` sorts `library` globally by `-frequency` and slices `[:10]` when building the `IMPORT` prompt. Once `10` core tools reach `frequency >= 2`, `100%` (`0/90` prompts in `Probe 1A`) of newly induced `frequency == 1` specialist tools are invisible to `IMPORT`, never increment `frequency`, and suffer `100%` (`6/6`) deletion at the next trim boundary.
  3. **Bare-`import` Indented-`return` Parser Bug (`utils/code.py:127–168`):** In `parse_library_functions`, `return tool_dict_list` at `L149` is indented *inside* `for lib in lib_list:` (`L136`) under `if code.startswith("import"):`, returning `['math']` and dropping `2/3` modules on `'import math, numpy, sympy'`, whereas `elif code.startswith("from"):` (`L150–165`) uses a list comprehension that preserves `2/2` functions on `'from toolbox import find_mean, find_std'` (`Probe 1A`).
  4. **Two-Stage Per-Mode Selection & Summed Statement AST-Depth Skips (`run_trove.py:142–165`, `utils/select.py:6–72`):** Rather than pooling all $3K$ candidates across `IMPORT`/`CREATE`/`SKIP` (§3.2), `run_trove.py` picks 1 winner *within* each mode and then votes across at most 3 mode winners (allowing a `1/5` `SKIP` answer to tie `5/5` `IMPORT` agreement and win on lower complexity). While Appendix B (`L947–950`) matches `select.py:22` (`total_depth += depth`) in defining `# ops` as summed per-statement AST depth, `select.py:6–23` additionally skips `ast.FunctionDef` bodies, constant `Assign`, and `df = ...` statements.
  5. **CLI Defaults & Post-Stream Re-Solve (`run_trove.py:208–224, 262, 275–277`):** Defaults set `trim_steps=500` (paper: `200`), `num_return_sequences=1` (paper: `5`), `temperature=0.3` (paper: `0.6`), and run a post-stream final forgetting pass that re-solves every example in `trimmed_indices` using `["import", "skip"]`.
- **Equal-Retrieval Four-Way Streaming Ablation (`Probe 1B`, `[toy model / mechanistic surrogate]`, `N = 2000`, `topk = 10`):** Sharing an identical noisy lexical/semantic retriever (`retrieve_semantic_topk10`, zero exclusive `sim = 1.0` or usage-rate oracle) isolates retrieval from pruning:
  - `Pinned-FreqTopK10` (`format_toolbox` + global $\log_{20} n$): `77.65%` accuracy (`lib = 10`, **`0/12` specialists kept**).
  - `Unpruned-SemTopK10` (equal retrieval, no trim): `82.65%` (`lib = 49`, `27` `oneoff_*` distractors degrading top-10 selection by `-4.15 pp`).
  - `Global-log20(n) SemTopK10` (equal retrieval + global $\log_{20} n$ trim): `86.80%` (`lib = 20`, `0` noise, rescues `6/6` early specialists **but evicts `2/6` valid `frequency == 2` late-shift specialists** at $n = 2000$ where $\log_{20}(2000) = 2.537 > 2$).
  - `Age-log20(age) SemTopK10` (equal retrieval + tool-age $\log_{20}(\max(21, t - t_{\text{born}}))$ trim): `86.80%` (`lib = 22`, **`12/12` specialists including `6/6` late-shift kept**, `0` noise). In the active pure-noise negative control (`962` `oneoff_*` tools generated from non-recurring ephemeral tasks), `Age-trim` evicts **`962/962` (`100%`)** noise tools (`final_lib = 10`) while `Unpruned` balloons to `final_lib = 1085` (`1,075` noise tools).
