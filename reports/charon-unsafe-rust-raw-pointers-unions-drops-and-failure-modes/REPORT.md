# Charon Raw Pointers, Unions, Drop and Destructor Semantics, Intrinsics, Coroutines, and Semantic Failure Modes at 0.1.210

## Summary

At `AeneasVerif/charon@a535e914f74db4fd9e6be7048f4233270d8945c0` (`0.1.210`, embedded Rust toolchain `nightly`, pinned by `AeneasVerif/aeneas@ac9f1bc5262a5e4ff1e24ca78617121382202727` at release `nightly-2026.06.03`), Charon's treatment of unsafe Rust, low-level operations, destructors, intrinsics, coroutines, and error signaling falls into six distinct technical boundaries:

1. **Raw pointers and unsafe-operation preservation (`translate_types.rs`, `translate_bodies.rs`, `insert_ptr_metadata.rs`, `ops_to_function_calls.rs`):** Charon represents raw pointers as `TyKind::RawPtr(Ty, RefKind)` (`RefKind::Mut` for `*mut T`, `RefKind::Shared` for `*const T`), raw borrows as `Rvalue::RawPtr { place, kind, ptr_metadata }`, pointer offsets as `BinOp::Offset`, wide-pointer metadata reads as `ProjectionElem::PtrMetadata`, and raw-pointer construction from parts as `AggregateKind::RawPtr(pointee_ty, RefKind)` (lowered by `--ops-to-function-calls` to `@PtrFromParts` / `@PtrFromPartsShared`). However, source `unsafe { ... }` blocks are already erased before MIR; raw-pointer dereferences (`ProjectionElem::Deref`), unsafe function calls (`Call`), and union field reads (`ProjectionElem::Field`) carry no AST-node unsafety bit and must be identified from operand/callee/base-ADT types. Furthermore, `FunSig::is_unsafe` preserves `unsafe fn`, whereas `TraitDecl` and `TraitImpl` drop `unsafe trait` / `unsafe impl` qualifiers, `GlobalDecl` drops `static mut` (`GlobalKind::Static`), and `CastKind::RawPtr(source_ty, target_ty)` collapses six distinct MIR cast kinds (`PtrToPtr`, `MutToConstPointer`, `ArrayToPointer`, `FnPtrToPtr`, `PointerExposeProvenance`, and `PointerWithExposedProvenance`). Constant pointers preserve a narrower CTFE distinction (`PtrNoProvenance(u128)` vs. borrow/raw-borrow constants and `Byte::Provenance` naming a global, function, or unknown source).
2. **Unions (`TypeDeclKind::Union`, `AggregateKind::Adt`, `translate_types.rs`):** Transparent Rust unions become `TypeDeclKind::Union(fields)`. Runtime union construction records the written field as `AggregateKind::Adt(type_ref, None, Some(field_id))` (`Foo { one: const 42u64 }`), and reads/writes use `ProjectionElem::Field(FieldProjKind::Adt(union_type_id, None), field_id)` (`(one_1).one = const 43u64`, `_two_2 = copy (one_1).two`). Consistent with the Rust Reference (`rust-lang/reference@ad35aca481751a06afeb23820a672b0f3b11a476`), Charon tracks no runtime "active field" tag, and downstream verifiers must supply union-read validity and field-storage overlap rules. In `Layout`, Charon models a union as one internal `VariantLayout` (PR `#1160`, commit `e92460f998cda33aafd8bb3862b70171ea604ab8`) and hardcodes all union field offsets to `0` for `FieldsShape::Union(n)`—an implementation fact of this `rustc`/Charon pair rather than a normative guarantee for default-representation Rust unions. Union constants lack a `FieldId` in `ConstantExprKind::Adt(Option<VariantId>, Vec<ConstantExpr>)` and fall back to `ConstantExprKind::RawMemory(Vec<Byte>)`.
3. **Drop and destructor semantics (`DropKind`, `translate_drops.rs`, `desugar_drops.rs`, `ullbc_to_llbc.rs`):** A Charon `Drop` depends on the extracted MIR phase: `Built`/`Analysis` (including default `Promoted` MIR) maps to `DropKind::Conditional` (pre-elaboration drop whose runtime meaning may involve partial initialization, drop flags, packed fields, or async drop), whereas `Runtime` (`Elaborated`/`Optimized` MIR) maps to `DropKind::Precise` (denoting `<T as Destruct>::drop_in_place(&raw mut place)` plus moving out of `place`). Passing `--precise-drops` raises local extraction to at least `Elaborated` MIR (turning drop flags and partial moves into ordinary Boolean locals, branches, and field drops, as in `conditional-drop.out` and `partial-drop.out`), adds explicit `Destruct` bounds (`explicit-drop-bounds.out`), and synthesizes `Destruct::drop_in_place` bodies for ADTs and closures that invoke user `Drop::drop` followed by field `drop_in_place` calls (`desugar_drops_to_calls.out`). Without `--precise-drops`, polymorphic synthetic `drop_in_place` bodies remain `<missing>` (`manual-drop-impl.out`), and `translate_drops.rs` wraps `rustc`'s `drop_glue_shim` in `catch_unwind` because polymorphic drop glue can trigger `rustc` panics. Trivial `NoopDestruct` drops are eliminated (`filter_trivial_drops.rs`), and `--desugar-drops` rewrites `DropKind::Precise` into `&raw mut place` + a `Call` to `drop_in_place`. Critically, at `a535e914f74db4fd9e6be7048f4233270d8945c0`, while ULLBC retains both `target` and `on_unwind` on `Drop` and `Call` (`drop_after_overflow.out`), `ullbc_to_llbc.rs` (`blob 07322359...`) destructures `on_unwind: _` (`TODO: Have unwinds in the LLBC`) and discards unwind edges for both `Drop` and `Call` even when `--desugar-drops` is enabled.
4. **Intrinsics across six representation categories (`translate_items.rs`, `translate_bodies.rs`, `reconstruct_intrinsics.rs`):** Rust intrinsics cross the `rustc`-to-Charon boundary in six ways (`support/charon-intrinsics-0-1-210/revalidation.json`): (a) **Named intrinsic body** `Body::Intrinsic { name, arg_names }` (`<intrinsic:name>`) for items where `tcx.intrinsic(def_id)` returns a descriptor (`ctpop`, `size_of`, `align_of`, `cold_path`), preserving signature/identity without operational semantics; (b) **Synthetic Charon body** for `core::intrinsics::type_id`, where `build_type_id_body` emits an unstructured body returning `ConstantExprKind::TypeId(T)`; (c) **Resugared dedicated operation** in `resugar::reconstruct_intrinsics`, which rewrites `offset_of` calls with constant ADT/variant/field arguments into `Rvalue::NullaryOp(NullOp::OffsetOf(type_ref, optional_variant, field_id), usize)`; (d) **MIR non-diverging intrinsic statements**, translating `NonDivergingIntrinsic::Assume(op)` to an `Assert` with `on_failure: AbortKind::UndefinedBehavior` and `CopyNonOverlapping { src, dst, count }` to `StatementKind::CopyNonOverlapping`; (e) **Ordinary MIR operations**, mapping `CastKind::Transmute`, `BinOp::Offset`, `NullOp::{UbChecks, OverflowChecks, ContractChecks}`, `ProjectionElem::PtrMetadata`, and `TerminatorKind::Unreachable` (`AbortKind::UndefinedBehavior`); and (f) **Translated library wrappers** (`core::ptr::copy_nonoverlapping`, `core::ptr::const_ptr::offset`) that conditionally run optional `ub_checks` helpers before executing the primitive operation.
5. **Explicit rejection of `async`, generators, and coroutines (`translate_types.rs`, `translate_bodies.rs`, `translate_crate.rs`):** Although `types.rs` includes `Future`, `AsyncFn`, `AsyncFnMut`, `AsyncFnOnce`, and `Coroutine` in its builtin trait taxonomy and Hax exposes `hax::TyKind::Coroutine(ItemRef)`, Charon `0.1.210` rejects coroutine types (`"Coroutine types are not supported yet"`), `AggregateKind::{Coroutine, CoroutineClosure}` (`"Coroutines are not supported"`), `TerminatorKind::{CoroutineDrop, Yield}`, coroutine resumed-state assertions (`ResumedAfterDrop`, `ResumedAfterPanic`, `ResumedAfterReturn`), and `SyntheticCoroutineBody` item registration (`"Cannot register item ..."`). Even `pub async fn f() {}` (`charon/tests/ui/simple/coroutine.{rs,out}`) is a checked-in `known-failure` producing 2 translation errors.
6. **Known semantic hazards, unsupported features, and fail-open error signaling (`options.rs`, `index_to_function_calls.rs`, `expand_associated_types.rs`, `errors.rs`, `OfJson.ml`):**
   - **UB-introducing raw-pointer indexing rewrite (`#583`):** `Preset::Aeneas` (required by Aeneas `src/Main.ml`) sets `index_to_function_calls = true`. In `index_to_function_calls.rs`, `IndexVisitor::transform_place` rewrites index/subslice projections by creating an intermediate `&` or `&mut` borrow of the indexed subplace and calling a builtin indexing function—without checking whether the subplace lies behind a raw-pointer dereference, which `options.rs` explicitly warns `"may introduce UB"`.
   - **Quantified lifetime erasure in associated-type lifting (`#534`):** `Preset::Aeneas` sets `lift_associated_types("*")`, and `expand_associated_types.rs` documents that bound lifetimes in quantified clauses such as `T: for<'a> Trait<'a, Type = &'a ()>` are not tracked properly (erasing `'a` to `&'_ ()`), whereas `#584` (still listed in `docs/limitations.md`) was fixed and closed on `2025-07-02` prior to this pin.
   - **Unwind erasure in `reconstruct_fallible_operations`:** `Preset::Aeneas` enables `reconstruct_fallible_operations`, which replaces check-plus-UB-on-overflow patterns with panic-on-overflow operations and explicitly documents `"This loses unwinding information."`
   - **Fail-open default error continuation and `OfJson.ml` `has_errors` discard:** By default, `ErrorCtx` sets `continue_on_failure = true`, catches item/body translation errors and panics into `TypeDeclKind::Error` / `Body::Error` / `ConstantExprKind::Opaque`, serializes `CrateData` with `has_errors = true`, and exits with status `0` unless `--error-on-warnings` (status `1` after serialization) or `--abort-on-error` (panic on first error) is passed. Because `charon-ml/src/OfJson.ml` ignores the third field (`has_errors`) of the top-level JSON object, an orchestration pipeline that neither passes `--abort-on-error` / `--error-on-warnings` nor inspects `CrateData.has_errors` can silently consume a partial crate.

## Findings

### 1. Raw Pointers and Unsafe-Operation Preservation

**Basis:** `source`, `execution`, `derived`

#### 1.1 Raw-Pointer Types, Raw Borrows, Wide-Pointer Metadata, and Pointer Offsets
In `charon/src/ast/types.rs` (blob `548be29762fdc4f4d1cd652537db6f54065ff6ee`), `charon/src/ast/expressions.rs` (blob `eeb2143c714ee8b429a59c2aaf9f097afb18a940`), `translate_types.rs` (blob `35bef6ea79992c65ea934413765fa95f631274e6`), `translate_bodies.rs` (blob `c03ca8077f131a2ef701e9d27eaa9e69bb617124`), `insert_ptr_metadata.rs` (blob `f4c4ef9437f16a50d574eff2391c31b4a78408be`), and `ops_to_function_calls.rs` (blob `5f8ae571538eeef5fa91fa2c3a914c1ca94f1c52`):
- **Raw-pointer types and borrows:** `TyKind::RawPtr(Ty, RefKind)` uses `RefKind::Mut` for `*mut T` and `RefKind::Shared` for `*const T` (where `Shared` denotes `const` pointer mutability, not shared-reference aliasing). Taking a raw borrow (`&raw const a`, `&raw mut COUNTER`) translates to `Rvalue::RawPtr { place, kind, ptr_metadata }`.
- **Dereferences (`ProjectionElem::Deref`):** Raw-pointer dereference uses the same `ProjectionElem::Deref` as `&T`, `&mut T`, and `Box<T>`. In `charon/tests/ui/unsafe.{rs,out}`, dereferencing `*x_1` prints as `copy (*x_1)` with no unsafe-dereference flag; consumers must inspect the base place's `TyKind::RawPtr` type.
- **Wide-pointer metadata and raw-parts construction:** `ProjectionElem::PtrMetadata` extracts fat-pointer metadata, and `insert_ptr_metadata` populates metadata operands on `Rvalue::RawPtr` and `Rvalue::Ref`. MIR raw-pointer aggregate construction maps to `AggregateKind::RawPtr(pointee_ty, RefKind)` and, under `--ops-to-function-calls`, lowers to `@PtrFromParts` / `@PtrFromPartsShared` (`charon/tests/ui/simple/ptr-from-raw-parts.{rs,out}`).
- **Pointer offset (`BinOp::Offset`):** `BinOp::Offset` scales its right count operand by the pointee size (`charon/tests/ui/ptr-offset.{rs,out}`: `_0 = copy self_1 offset copy count_2`).

#### 1.2 Erased Source Unsafety Qualifiers and Collapsed Raw-Pointer Casts
- **Source `unsafe` blocks, `unsafe fn`, `unsafe trait`/`impl`, and `static mut` (`charon/tests/ui/unsafe.{rs,out}`):**
  - Source `unsafe { ... }` blocks are absent from MIR and LLBC bodies.
  - `FunSig::is_unsafe: bool` preserves `unsafe fn` on function declarations and `FnPtr` types (`unsafe fn core::ptr::read`, `unsafe fn core::intrinsics::assume`), while call statements themselves carry no unsafe flag.
  - `TraitDecl` and `TraitImpl` (`ast/gast.rs`, `translate_items.rs`) have no safety field: `unsafe trait Trait {}` and `unsafe impl Trait for () {}` emit as ordinary `trait Trait<Self>` and `impl` declarations.
  - `GlobalDecl` (`GlobalKind::Static`) has no mutability field: `static mut COUNTER: usize = 0` emits as `static COUNTER: usize`, though body accesses still expose `&raw mut COUNTER`.
- **Collapsed raw-pointer cast reasons (`CastKind::RawPtr`):** In `translate_bodies.rs`, six distinct `rustc` MIR `CastKind` variants all collapse into `CastKind::RawPtr(source_ty, target_ty)`:
  1. `PtrToPtr`
  2. `MutToConstPointer`
  3. `ArrayToPointer`
  4. `FnPtrToPtr`
  5. `PointerExposeProvenance`
  6. `PointerWithExposedProvenance`
  In `charon/tests/ui/ptr_no_provenance.{rs,out}`, null-pointer construction emits `cast<usize, *const T>(0)`. Strict/exposed-provenance cast intent is not recoverable from `CastKind::RawPtr` alone beyond the source and target types.
- **Constant pointer provenance (`translate_constants.rs`, `hax/constant_utils/uneval.rs`):** Constant translation distinguishes dereferenceable pointers (lowered to borrow/raw-borrow constants) from uninterpretable pointer-sized integers (`ConstantExprKind::PtrNoProvenance(u128)`), and `ConstantExprKind::RawMemory` bytes can carry `Byte::Provenance` referencing a global, function, or unknown source.

### 2. Unions, Field Projections, Zero-Offset Layout Convention, and Union Constants

**Basis:** `normative`, `source`, `documentation`, `execution`, `derived`

In `charon/src/ast/types.rs`, `charon/src/ast/expressions.rs`, `charon/src/ast/types_utils.rs` (blob `fbbe1caab444ad99d04aa31a5694a3e76c5204e5`), `translate_types.rs`, `translate_bodies.rs`, `translate_constants.rs`, and `charon/tests/ui/unions.{rs,out}`:
- **Declaration, aggregate construction, and field projections:**
  - `TypeDeclKind::Union(IndexVec<FieldId, Field>)` represents a transparent union declaration (`union Foo { one: u64, two: [u32; 2usize] }`). External unions follow the same visibility gate as external structs: all fields must be public to be translated transparently.
  - Runtime union construction uses `AggregateKind::Adt(type_ref, None, Some(field_id))` (`one_1 = Foo { one: const 42u64 }`), where the third tuple slot (`Option<FieldId>`) identifies which union field is written (note that post-pin issue `#1276` tracks splitting `AggregateKind::Adt` into separate struct/enum/union variants).
  - Union field reads and writes both use `ProjectionElem::Field(FieldProjKind::Adt(union_type_id, None), field_id)` (`(one_1).one = const 43u64` and `_two_2 = copy (one_1).two`) with no union-read unsafe marker.
- **Alignment with normative Rust union semantics (`rust-lang/reference@ad35aca481751a06afeb23820a672b0f3b11a476`, `src/items/unions.md` blob `c330b037...`, `src/types/union.md` blob `3ba187d5...`):** Rust unions have no active field, all fields share storage (so borrowing one field borrows all fields for that lifetime), writes are safe (fields cannot have nontrivial drop glue), and reads are `unsafe` because reading bits that are invalid for the target field type is undefined behavior. Charon's `Some(field_id)` on `AggregateKind::Adt` records only the construction write, not a persistent tag.
- **Single-variant `Layout` and hardcoded zero field offsets (PR `#1160`, commit `e92460f998cda33aafd8bb3862b70171ea604ab8`):** `Layout` models structs and unions as having a single `VariantLayout` at index `0`. When `rustc` reports `FieldsShape::Union(n)`, `translate_layout_data` generates `n` zero byte offsets (`0`) while preserving overall `size`, `align`, and `ReprOptions`. As PR `#1160` and the Rust Reference explicitly state, zero offsets match current `rustc` behavior and `repr(C)` unions, but default-representation Rust unions are not normatively guaranteed to place all fields at offset `0`.
- **Union constants (`ConstantExprKind::RawMemory`):** Unlike runtime `AggregateKind::Adt`, `ConstantExprKind::Adt(Option<VariantId>, Vec<ConstantExpr>)` has no `Option<FieldId>` slot for unions; union constants fall back to `ConstantExprKind::RawMemory(Vec<Byte>)` at `0.1.210` (later tracked in post-pin PR `#1444`).

### 3. Drop and Destructor Semantics, `Destruct::drop_in_place`, and Unwind Edge Loss

**Basis:** `source`, `documentation`, `execution`, `derived`

In `charon/src/options.rs` (blob `f08f8bae08d7fb5f38773c0be038d925fe80cb4c`), `charon/src/ast/gast.rs`, `ullbc_ast.rs` (blob `3c6179f10742b2ee82c8ca07e6d51be6c8dc7694`), `llbc_ast.rs` (blob `40f29f8a4b19f98e929b5fe82b8a009d813120ad`), `translate_bodies.rs`, `translate_drops.rs` (blob `948ac38b6de14f5a8d97036a41eb02ec14df0b98`), `desugar_drops.rs` (blob `b84f1d30fce1a74a21f7b9abd449657e9227954f`), `filter_trivial_drops.rs` (blob `0ca1b0cdcdce96ba0b846f96ed458b3b1fec8d37`), and `ullbc_to_llbc.rs` (blob `07322359e92f92a4d8b55865a67257bedacb0a62`):

| Drop Aspect | Default Extraction (`Promoted` / `Built` / `Analysis` MIR) | `--precise-drops` (`Elaborated` / `Optimized` `Runtime` MIR) | `--precise-drops --desugar-drops` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `DropKind` | `DropKind::Conditional` (pre-elaboration; may involve partial initialization, packed fields, or async drop) | `DropKind::Precise` (invokes `<T as Destruct>::drop_in_place(&raw mut place)` and marks `place` moved out) | Rewrites `DropKind::Precise` into `&raw mut place` + `Call` to `drop_in_place` (`Conditional` drops unchanged) |
| Generic `T: Destruct` bounds | Not added (`add_destruct_bounds = false`) | Explicit `Destruct` clauses added (`explicit-drop-bounds.{rs,out}`) | Explicit `Destruct` clauses added |
| Polymorphic `drop_in_place` body | `Body::Missing` (`<missing>` in `manual-drop-impl.{rs,out}`) | Extracted via `drop_glue_shim` wrapped in `catch_unwind` (can panic in `rustc` on some polymorphic types) | Same as `--precise-drops` |
| Concrete ADT/closure drop glue | Monomorphic glue synthesized if non-generic/synthetic | Full glue synthesized (`impl_Destruct_for_Point::drop_in_place` calls `Drop::drop` then field `drop_in_place`s in `desugar_drops_to_calls.{rs,out}`) | Lowered to explicit `drop_in_place` calls |
| Path-dependent & partial moves | Implicit inside `Conditional` drop | Explicit Boolean drop-flag locals/branches (`conditional-drop.{rs,out}`) and field-only drops (`partial-drop.{rs,out}`) | Explicit Boolean branches and field `drop_in_place` calls |
| `NoopDestruct` scalar drops | Removed by `filter_trivial_drops` (`Drop -> Goto`) | Removed by `filter_trivial_drops` | Removed by `desugar_drops` before call creation |
| Unwind cleanup edges (`on_unwind`) | **Preserved in ULLBC** (`drop_after_overflow.{rs,out}`); **discarded in LLBC** (`ullbc_to_llbc.rs` ignores `on_unwind`) | **Preserved in ULLBC**; **discarded in LLBC** (`Drop` arm drops `on_unwind`) | **Preserved in ULLBC** on `Call`; **discarded in LLBC** (`Call` arm also drops `on_unwind`) |

### 4. Compiler Intrinsics Across Six Representation Categories

**Basis:** `source`, `execution`, `derived`

Inspecting `translate_items.rs`, `translate_bodies.rs`, `ast/gast.rs`, `ast/expressions.rs`, and `charon/src/transform/resugar/reconstruct_intrinsics.rs` (blob `f13ec694f9802a02080abd98cb1155a51387c24d`), together with `support/charon-intrinsics-0-1-210/revalidation.json` and checked-in fixtures (`copy_nonoverlapping.{rs,out}`, `ptr-offset.{rs,out}`, `simple/generic-offset-of.{rs,out}`), establishes six distinct ways intrinsic behavior reaches Charon `0.1.210`:

| Category | Mechanism in Charon `0.1.210` | Concrete Examples & Golden Evidence | Downstream Verification Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Named intrinsic body (`Body::Intrinsic`) | `translate_items.rs` queries `tcx.intrinsic(def_id)` and emits `Body::Intrinsic { name, arg_names }` (`<intrinsic:name>`) | `ctpop`, `size_of`, `align_of` (`copy_nonoverlapping.out`); `cold_path` (`ptr-offset.out`) | Preserves signature, generics, and identity only; consumer must supply operational semantics and UB preconditions. |
| 2. Synthetic Charon body | `build_type_id_body` in `translate_bodies.rs` intercepts `core::intrinsics::type_id` and emits a 1-block ULLBC body assigning `ConstantExprKind::TypeId(T)` | `core::intrinsics::type_id<T>()` | Consumer receives a structured `ConstantExprKind::TypeId(Ty)` value rather than an opaque `<intrinsic:type_id>` body. |
| 3. Resugared dedicated operation | `resugar::reconstruct_intrinsics` matches calls to lang item `offset_of` with an ADT type argument and literal `u32` variant/field IDs, replacing the call with `Rvalue::NullaryOp(NullOp::OffsetOf(type_ref, opt_variant, field_id), usize)` | `std::mem::offset_of!` (`simple/generic-offset-of.{rs,out}`) | Pattern-sensitive rewrite; non-matching calls or `size_of`/`align_of` declarations remain calls unless lowered through `NullOp::{SizeOf, AlignOf}`. |
| 4. MIR non-diverging intrinsic statement | `translate_bodies.rs` maps `mir::StatementKind::Intrinsic(NonDivergingIntrinsic)` directly into ULLBC/LLBC statements | `Assume(op)` $\to$ `Assert { cond: op, expected: true, on_failure: AbortKind::UndefinedBehavior }`; `CopyNonOverlapping { src, dst, count }` $\to$ `StatementKind::CopyNonOverlapping` (`copy_nonoverlapping.out`) | Explicit first-class UB assertion or non-diverging memory-copy statement in both ULLBC and LLBC. |
| 5. Ordinary MIR operation | `rustc` lowers intrinsic-like behavior to MIR casts, binops, nullary ops, projections, or terminators before Charon | `CastKind::Transmute(src_ty, dst_ty)`, `BinOp::Offset`, `NullOp::{UbChecks, OverflowChecks, ContractChecks}`, `ProjectionElem::PtrMetadata`, `TerminatorKind::Unreachable` $\to$ `AbortKind::UndefinedBehavior` | Appears as standard Charon IR rather than `Body::Intrinsic`; intrinsic inventory cannot rely on `Body::Intrinsic` names alone. |
| 6. Translated library wrapper | Public `core` functions translate as ordinary MIR bodies that conditionally call `ub_checks` helpers and then execute Category 1–5 primitives | `core::ptr::copy_nonoverlapping` (`copy_nonoverlapping.out`), `core::ptr::const_ptr::offset` (`ptr-offset.out`) | Conditional `ub_checks` branches are optional debug checks and do not replace proving the primitive operation's UB preconditions. |

### 5. Explicit Rejection of `async`, Generators, and Coroutines

**Basis:** `source`, `execution`, `derived`

At `a535e914f74db4fd9e6be7048f4233270d8945c0` (tracked upstream by open issue `#609`, "Support async"), Charon rejects compiler-lowered coroutine state machines at five distinct points:

### 6. Known Semantic Hazards, Unsupported Rust Features, and Error Signaling

**Basis:** `source`, `documentation`, `derived`

#### 6.1 Active Semantic Hazards Under `Preset::Aeneas`
At `a535e914f74db4fd9e6be7048f4233270d8945c0`, `AeneasVerif/aeneas@ac9f1bc5262a5e4ff1e24ca78617121382202727` (`src/Main.ml`) requires imported LLBC to carry `preset = Some Aeneas`. That preset activates three transformations with source-documented semantic hazards:
1. **UB-introducing raw-pointer indexing rewrite (`--index-to-function-calls`, issue `#583`):** `Preset::Aeneas` sets `index_to_function_calls = true`. In `charon/src/options.rs`, `CliOpts::index_to_function_calls` explicitly warns that converting array/slice indexing into builtin function calls `"may introduce UB"` because `IndexVisitor::transform_place` (`charon/src/transform/simplify_output/index_to_function_calls.rs`) forms a shared (`&`) or mutable (`&mut`) reference to the indexed subplace and dereferences the returned reference—even when the subplace was reached through a raw pointer (`*ptr`). Issue `#583` ("handling of indexing in unsafe code is unsound") was closed on `2025-12-08` by making the flag optional globally, leaving it enabled in `Preset::Aeneas`.
2. **Quantified lifetime erasure in associated-type lifting (`lift_associated_types("*")`, open issue `#534`):** `Preset::Aeneas` adds `"*"` to `lift_associated_types`. `charon/src/transform/normalize/expand_associated_types.rs` documents that bound lifetimes in higher-ranked clauses (such as `T: for<'a> Trait<'a, Type = &'a ()>`) are not tracked properly, erasing `'a` inside the lifted associated-type equality. By contrast, issue `#584` (still listed in `docs/limitations.md` under "Known unsoundnesses") was fixed in Hax and closed on `2025-07-02` before this pin.
3. **Unwind erasure in `reconstruct_fallible_operations`:** `Preset::Aeneas` enables `reconstruct_fallible_operations`, whose option documentation states that replacing check-plus-UB-on-overflow patterns with panic-on-overflow operations `"loses unwinding information."`

#### 6.2 Additional Unsupported MIR Constructs and `rustc-tests.nix` Exclusions
- **Rejected MIR projections, rvalues, and trait-object forms:** `translate_bodies.rs` explicitly rejects `UnwrapUnsafeBinder` projections, `WrapUnsafeBinder` rvalues, reborrow rvalues for `Reborrow` traits, and deep fake borrows for deref patterns (`unimplemented!()`). `translate_trait_objects.rs` rejects `dyn Trait` types whose predicate set contains multiple method-bearing predicates or unsupported virtual-impl shapes. `translate_types.rs` rejects unsupported alias kinds, escaping inference/placeholder types, and Hax `Todo` types. Open pre-pin trackers also record experimental contract annotations (`#646`) and custom smart-pointer unsizing via `CoerceUnsized`/`Unsize` (`#855`).
- **`nix/rustc-tests.nix` harness exclusions and failure taxonomy:** Classifies `generic_const_exprs`, `adt_const_params`, `min_generic_const_args`, `effects`, `transmutability`, `loop_match`, and `default_type_parameter_fallback` as `unsupported-feature`, and categorizes test outcomes into `success`, `unsupported build settings`, `unsupported feature`, `stack overflow/timeout`, `internal compiler error`, `panic`, `failure in rustc`, and `failure in Charon`.
- **Reconciling `docs/limitations.md` against pinned source:** While `docs/limitations.md` lists foreign/std bodies, drops, layout information, and `#584` as missing or broken, at `a535e914f74db4fd9e6be7048f4233270d8945c0` `#584` is already closed, monomorphic `Layout` support is implemented (`#581`), elaborated-MIR drops are available via `--precise-drops` (`#152`, with `#543` closed `2026-06-26`), and std/foreign body availability (`Body::Missing`, `#545`) depends on whether the sysroot/rlibs were built with encoded MIR.

#### 6.3 Default Best-Effort Continuation, Exit Codes, and `OfJson.ml` Fail-Open Hazard
In `charon/src/errors.rs`, `charon/src/export.rs`, `charon/src/bin/charon-driver/main.rs`, and `charon-ml/src/OfJson.ml`:
- **Default continuation (`continue_on_failure = true`):** `ErrorCtx::new` continues after `register_error!` and caught item/body panics, recording `TypeDeclKind::Error`, `Body::Error`, or `ConstantExprKind::Opaque` and setting `CrateData.has_errors = true`.
- **Serialization before exit-policy check and exit codes:** `charon-driver` serializes `CrateData` **before** checking `--error-on-warnings`. Under default flags, a run with translation errors serializes a partial `.llbc`/`.ullbc` file with `has_errors: true`, prints a warning, and exits with status `0` (whereas `--error-on-warnings` exits with status `1` after serialization, `--abort-on-error` panics on the first error, `rustc` failure exits with status `2`, and uncaught outer panic exits with status `101`).
- **Aeneas JSON loader discards `has_errors`:** In `charon-ml/src/OfJson.ml`, `crate_of_json` matches the third top-level JSON field with a wildcard `_` and discards it, never surfacing `has_errors` to Aeneas. Consequently, a pipeline must enforce `--abort-on-error` / `--error-on-warnings` and/or check `CrateData.has_errors` and `Body::Error` / `TypeDeclKind::Error` / `ConstantExprKind::Opaque` nodes directly (as historical `anneal/v1/src/charon.rs` in `google/zerocopy@41f5b37afe7060fd9fe08c00b200672cd76d77b9` did by passing `--preset=aeneas --abort-on-error` and checking structured diagnostics).

## Boundaries

- **`known not to apply`:**
  - `RefKind::Shared` inside `TyKind::RawPtr(Ty, RefKind::Shared)` does **not** imbue `*const T` with shared-reference (`&T`) aliasing or immutability invariants; it represents pointer `const`-ness only.
  - `AggregateKind::Adt(type_ref, None, Some(field_id))` on a union construction does **not** establish a persistent runtime active-field tag, and hardcoded zero union field offsets in `translate_layout_data` (`FieldsShape::Union`) do **not** constitute a language-level guarantee for default-representation Rust unions across all compilers.
  - Passing `--precise-drops --desugar-drops` at `a535e914f74db4fd9e6be7048f4233270d8945c0` does **not** preserve destructor unwind cleanup edges in structured LLBC because `ullbc_to_llbc.rs` drops `on_unwind` on both `Drop` and `Call`.
  - A `Body::Intrinsic { name, arg_names }` declaration or a conditional `ub_checks` branch in a translated `core` wrapper does **not** supply or verify the operational semantics or UB preconditions of the underlying intrinsic.
  - Presence of `Future`, `AsyncFn*`, or `Coroutine` in `ast/types.rs` builtin trait enums does **not** mean Charon `0.1.210` can translate `async fn` or coroutine bodies, and exit status `0` from `charon` under default flags does **not** imply `has_errors == false`.
- **`unsupported`:**
  - Charon `0.1.210` explicitly rejects `hax::TyKind::Coroutine`, `AggregateKind::{Coroutine, CoroutineClosure}`, `TerminatorKind::{Yield, CoroutineDrop}`, coroutine resumed-state assertions (`ResumedAfterDrop`, `ResumedAfterPanic`, `ResumedAfterReturn`), `SyntheticCoroutineBody`, `UnwrapUnsafeBinder`, `WrapUnsafeBinder`, `Reborrow` rvalues, deep fake borrows, and multi-method-predicate `dyn Trait` objects.
  - Source `unsafe { ... }` blocks, `unsafe trait` / `unsafe impl` flags, `static mut` declaration mutability, and distinct `rustc` raw-pointer cast reasons (`PointerExposeProvenance` vs. `PointerWithExposedProvenance` vs. `PtrToPtr`) are not preserved as structural fields in the `0.1.210` AST.
  - Union constants do not preserve a selected `FieldId` in `ConstantExprKind::Adt` and fall back to `ConstantExprKind::RawMemory`.
- **`not examined`:**
  - No fresh `rustc`, Charon, or Aeneas execution was performed in these six source-and-golden studies; checked-in `.out` files are preserved upstream test artifacts.
  - Inline/global assembly (`InlineAsm`), FFI foreign-function calling conventions, and hand-written synchronous `impl Future` types (that do not use compiler-generated coroutine bodies) were not empirically benchmarked here.
- **`unknown`:**
  - Which polymorphic types trigger `rustc` panics inside `drop_glue_shim` under `--precise-drops` (caught by `catch_unwind` in `translate_drops.rs`) is input-dependent and not characterized by a closed type grammar.
