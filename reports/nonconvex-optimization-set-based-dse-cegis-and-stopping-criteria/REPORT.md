# Non-Convex Population Topologies, Set-Based Multi-Fidelity DSE, Counterexample-Guided Overfitting, and Stopping Criteria

## Summary

Across evolutionary optimization, engineering design space exploration (DSE), formal counterexample-guided synthesis, and statistical inspection theory, iterative search over rugged or discrete design spaces is governed by six primary-source-verified mathematical laws and negative impossibility results:

1. **Island Isolation vs. Single-Archive Search Hinges on the Epoch-to-Basin Ratio ($\tau$ vs. $T_{\text{basin}}$):** Semi-isolated island models achieve provable superlinear ($s_\mu = \Theta(3^\mu)$ on Eulerian-cycle plateaus, Lässig and Sudholt 2011) to exponential ($2^{\Omega(z)} \to O(n^2)$ on sequential `LOLZ` traps, Lässig and Sudholt 2010/2013) total-evaluation speedups over panmictic populations and restarts **only when** the migration interval $\tau$ exceeds intra-island basin crystallization time $T_{\text{basin}}$ while total horizon $G \ge 3\tau$. When $\tau \ll T_{\text{basin}}$ or migration broadcasts across dense/scale-free hubs (`Fully Connected`, `Broadcast`, `BA(3,2)`), population-based learners collapse to premature takeover (Ruciński, Izzo, and Biscani 2010: median Kendall rank correlation $\tau_b = -0.19$ between Differential Evolution and Simulated Annealing topology rankings). Conversely, on short horizons ($G \lesssim T_{\text{basin}}$), splitting budget $B$ across islands starves serial refinement depth, and a single shared Quality-Diversity archive with bounded-depth memetic repair dominates.
2. **Memetic Local-Search Depth and Parallel-Tempering Analogies Have Sharp Breakdown Boundaries:** Because standard combinatorial local-search neighborhoods are $\mathsf{PLS}$-complete, unbounded local search ($\delta = \infty$) takes $2^{\Omega(n)}$ steps in the worst case, and misconfiguring depth $|\delta - D| \ge \log^3 n$ or halving frequency $1/\tau \to 1/(2\tau)$ flips runtime from $\operatorname{poly}(n)$ to $2^{\Omega(n^\varepsilon)}$ by under-reaching portals or overshooting into deceptive sinks (Sudholt 2009). Meanwhile, Parallel Tempering's Metropolis swap law $\min(1, \exp((\beta_i - \beta_j)(E_i - E_j)))$ and Kone–Kofke's $23\%$ constant-$C_V$ rule **fail mathematically** in non-MCMC symbolic or LLM search (no Boltzmann stationary distribution, arbitrary ordinal rubric scale, irreversible proposals); only Hamiltonian constraint-relaxation ladders and Katzgraber et al.'s (2006) bottleneck concentration law ($\eta_{\text{opt}}(T) \propto 1/\sqrt{D(T)}$) transfer validly.
3. **Set-Based Asymmetric Falsification and Non-Convex Scalarization Defeat Rework Cascades and "Frankenstein" Compromises:** Set-Based Concurrent Engineering (Sobek, Ward, and Liker 1999; Singer, Doerry, and Buckley 2009; Doerry and Koenig 2019) narrows cross-domain spaces via **asymmetric 3-valued falsification**—one domain's valid refutation eliminates an infeasible region asynchronously without requiring consensus on a scalar utility function. When competing architectures trade off conflicting objectives (e.g., primitive count vs. encoding cost), minimizing convex weighted sums $\sum w_i f_i(x)$ recovers **`0%` of non-convex Pareto-optimal solutions** (Das and Dennis 1997), and naive "compromise" feature union $P_\alpha \cup P_\beta$ produces a **strictly Pareto-dominated** design (`Probe 2`). Non-convex Pareto-optimal unifications are recovered uniquely by **lexicographic / $\varepsilon$-constraint soundness gates** paired with **order-approximating Augmented Weighted Tchebycheff** scalarization $\max_i w_i(f_i(x) - \bar{z}_i) + \rho\sum_i(f_i(x) - \bar{z}_i)$ ($\rho > 0$, Miettinen 1999).
4. **Multi-Fidelity Screening Requires Bounded Bias or $\rho_1^2 > \frac{4r}{(1+r)^2}$ Correlation:** Successive Halving (Jamieson and Talwalkar 2016) reduces uniform worst-case gap scaling $n\max_i \bar{\gamma}^{-1}(\frac{\nu_i-\nu_1}{2})$ to average-gap scaling $\tilde{O}(\sum_i \bar{\gamma}^{-1}(\frac{\nu_i-\nu_1}{2}))$, and Multifidelity Monte Carlo Fusion (Peherstorfer, Willcox, and Gunzburger 2016, 2018) lowers MSE iff $\rho_1^2 > \frac{4r}{(1+r)^2}$ for cost ratio $r = c_{\text{lo}}/c_{\text{hi}}$. Under **crossing learning curves / low-fidelity rank inversion** or underestimated surrogate bias ($f^{(M)}(x_\star) - f^{(m)}(x_\star) > \zeta^{(m)}$ in MF-GP-UCB, Kandasamy et al. 2016), pure **Filtering** permanently discards the true global optimum unless hedged by a full-fidelity bypass bracket ($s = 0$ in Hyperband; $\rho = 1/3$ random floor in BOHB) or online **Adaptation**.
5. **Counterexample Generalization + Stratified Expressiveness (`HEnum`) Prevents Point-Fix Epicycles:** Increasing synthesis grammar expressiveness $E_1 \subseteq E_2$ monotonically inflates the **Potential for Overfitting** $\Omega(S, Z)$—the count of specification-violating expressions matching observed counterexamples $Z$—degrading performance on **65%** of solvable SyGuS pairs via memorizing `ite` epicycles (Padhi et al. 2019; `Probe 4`). Scalable refinement requires projecting concrete counterexamples onto **minimal interface-scoped root-cause invariants** (Craig cut-point interpolants, Henzinger et al. 2004; IC3 minimal inductive subclauses `MIC`, Bradley 2011) and searching in size- and expressiveness-stratified order (`HEnum`, $5.1\times$ speedup; `mincegis`, Jha and Seshia 2017).
6. **Stagnation Windows, Small-Team Capture-Recapture ($k \le 3$), and Scalar Holdout Reuse Cannot Certify Optimality:** Elitist evolutionary convergence as $t \to \infty$ provides **zero finite-time optimality certificate** (Rudolph 1994), and practical stopping rules (`CMA-ES/pycma` v4.4.0's 10 criteria, `OCD`, Kalman-filtered `MGBM`) are purely local-exhaustion or conditioning detectors (`MGBM` failing to stop on `100%` of runs at default $I_{\min} = 10^{-4}$ in Kitamura and Tanabe 2026). In inspection stopping rules ($M_0, M_t, M_h, M_{th}$), all capture-recapture estimators break down below **$k \ge 4$ independent reviewers** (Briand et al. 2000; Petersson et al. 2004): for $k = 2\text{–}3$ critics, zero overlap ($f_2 = 0$) caps $M_h$-Jackknife at $\le 2D$ while LP/Chao1 diverge, and full overlap ($f_1 = 0$) forces **every** estimator to report $\hat{f}_0 = 0$ missed defects despite arbitrarily large shared blind-spot strata (`Probe 5`). Finally, across $T$ serial rounds $\times$ $B$ parallel branches on an $n$-item holdout, releasing full per-branch scalar scores inflates overfitting as $\tilde{\Theta}(\sqrt{TB/n})$ (`+0.338` bias at $n=12, T=3, B=4$ in `Probe 6`), whereas passing only the discrete winner index $i_t^* \in [B]$ restores $O(\sqrt{(T\ln B + \ln(1/\beta))/n})$.

## Findings

### 1. Population Topologies, Memetic Depth, Parallel Tempering, Quality-Diversity, and Anti-Oscillation Memory

#### 1.1 Island Topologies, Takeover Times, and the Four-Regime Speedup Phase Diagram

`Basis: documentation + derived + execution`

In coarse-grained island models, $\mu$ subpopulations of size $N_{\text{island}} = N_{\text{total}}/\mu$ evolve independently for **migration interval** $\tau$ generations before transmitting emigrants along directed graph $G = (V, E)$. Defining algorithmic parallel speedup relative to a single panmictic baseline as $s_\mu := E(T_1^{\text{seq}}) / E(T_\mu^{\text{par}})$ (Alba and Tomassini 2002; Lässig and Sudholt 2014):

- **Migration Policy Takeover Ordering (Cantú-Paz 1999, 2001):** Let takeover time $T^*$ be the expected generations under selection and migration alone for one elite copy to saturate an island. Across $\{\text{Random}, \text{Good}\} \to \{\text{Random}, \text{Bad}\}$ emigrant/replacement policies:
  $$T^*(\text{Good} \to \text{Bad}) < T^*(\text{Good} \to \text{Random}) < T^*(\text{Random} \to \text{Bad}) < T^*(\text{Random} \to \text{Random}),$$
  with emigrant fitness selection (`Good` vs. `Random`) shortening $T^*$ far more sharply than worst-individual replacement.
- **Topology Diameter vs. Intra-Island Solver Inversion (Lässig and Sudholt 2014; Ruciński, Izzo, and Biscani 2010):** Across strongly connected topologies of $\mu$ islands with per-step transmission probability $p$, takeover time scales as $\Theta(\mu)$ on **Rings** (`Thm. 4`: $E(T^{\text{par}}) \le \frac{2}{p^{1/2}}\sum s_i^{-1/2} + \frac{1}{\mu}\sum s_i^{-1}$), $\Theta(\sqrt{\mu})$ on **2D Tori** (`Thm. 6`: $O(p^{-2/3}\sum s_i^{-1/3})$), and $\Theta(\log \mu)$ on **Hypercubes** and **Complete Graphs $K_\mu$** (`Thms. 8, 10, 12`). Crucially, Ruciński, Izzo, and Biscani (2010, benchmarking 14 topologies up to $1{,}024$ islands on global space-trajectory problems) prove that **optimal topologies invert between population-based and single-trajectory solvers** (median Kendall $\tau_b = -0.19$): population-based Differential Evolution peaks on sparse medium-diameter rings (`Ring+1+2`, `Ring+1+2+3`, `Lattice`) and degrades severely on `Fully Connected`, `Broadcast`, and scale-free `BA(3,2)` because hub broadcasts contaminate differential vectors ($x_{r_1} - x_{r_2} \to 0$), whereas memoryless single-trajectory Simulated Annealing peaks on `Hypercube` and `BA(3,2)` ($|\tau_b| = 0.77$ with inverse diameter).
- **Rigorous Multimodal Dichotomies in Migration Interval $\tau$ (Whitley, Rana, and Heckendorn 1999; Skolicki and De Jong 2005; Lässig and Sudholt 2010/2013, 2011):**
  1. *Finite-sample symmetry breaking (Whitley et al. 1999):* On additively separable deceptive traps, when panmixia initializes inside the deceptive attractor's Vose basin ($Y_t$ solve rate) while smaller islands sample into the global basin with probability $X_{t/M}$, $M$ islands beat panmixia whenever $1 - (1 - X_{t/M})^M > Y_t \iff M > \frac{\ln(1-Y_t)}{\ln(1-X_{t/M})}$ (`Probe 1`). Conversely, on nonseparable Rastrigin ($N=5000$ without mutation) and Rana's function ($N=500$), panmixia outperforms fragmented islands.
  2. *Hierarchical Royal Road `LOLZ_{n,z,b,\ell}` (`arXiv:1801.10087` Thms. 11–12):* With $b$ sequential blocks of length $\ell$ containing a `0`-trap of height $z = \ell/4$, panmictic $(\mu+1)$ EAs and $s$ restarts fail with probability $\ge 1 - s e^{-\Omega(z)} - s 2^{-b}$, whereas $\mu = n^{\Theta(1)}$ islands with matched interval $\tau = n^{5/3} = \frac{1}{2}\ell n$ find $1^n$ in **$O(n^2)$ generations** w.h.p.—because $\tau$ lets `1`-branch islands climb past trap height $z$ *and* re-randomizes suffix blocks before migration rescues `0`-trapped islands.
  3. *Eulerian-cycle premature broadcast vs. superlinear speedup (`arXiv:1109.1766` Thms. 4–5):* When $\tau \cdot \operatorname{diam}(T) \cdot \mu = O(m^2)$, the first island reaching decision vertex $v^*$ broadcasts a premature subcycle shortcut across all islands, forcing $\Omega(m^4/\log \mu)$ runtime. When $\tau \ge m^3$, every island reaches $v^*$ independently (failing with probability $(1/3)^\mu$, `Probe 1`), yielding $O(m^3 + 3^{-\mu}m^4)$—a **superlinear speedup $s_\mu = \Theta(m)$ with $\mu = \log_3 m$ islands**.
- **Synthesized Four-Regime Horizon Law (`Basis: derived`):** Let $G = B / (\mu N_{\text{island}})$ be serial generations per island under budget $B$ and $T_{\text{basin}}$ be intra-island trap-escape time:
  - *Regime I ($G \lesssim T_{\text{basin}}$, short horizon):* Panmictic diversity has not yet collapsed; splitting $B$ across islands either triggers premature trap broadcast ($\tau < T_{\text{basin}}$) or starves serial depth ($\tau \ge G$). **Winner:** Single shared novelty/QD archive + bounded memetic repair.
  - *Regime II ($\tau \ll T_{\text{basin}}$ or hub broadcast):* Early deceptive attractors colonize all islands ($s_\mu = O(\log \mu)$).
  - *Regime III ($G \ge 3\tau$ and $T_{\text{basin}} < \tau < T_{\text{stagnation}}$ on sparse `Ring`/`Ring+1+2`):* Islands strictly beat *both* panmixia and single-archive QD—granting multi-step epistatic refactorings a **protected $\tau$-step gestation window** during which fitness temporarily dips before cross-island recombination.
  - *Regime IV ($\tau = \infty$):* Incurs $p_{\text{good}}^b$ sequential multi-trap attrition without rescue.

#### 1.2 Memetic Local-Global Hybridization: Lamarckian vs. Baldwinian Dynamics and Depth Phase Transitions

`Basis: documentation + execution`

- **Lamarckian Write-Back vs. Baldwinian Smoothing (Moscato 1989; Whitley, Gordon, and Mathias 1994; Krasnogor and Smith 2005):** Applying local search $\operatorname{LS}(x) = x^*$, *Lamarckian* evolution overwrites genotype $x \leftarrow x^*$, converging rapidly on aligned funnels but prematurely collapsing allele diversity into wide deceptive basins; *Baldwinian* evolution retains genotype $x$ while scoring $f_{\text{Baldwin}}(x) = f(\operatorname{LS}(x))$, smoothing barriers around narrow global optima without erasing stepping-stone alleles.
- **Complexity Barriers and Exponential Misconfiguration Slowdowns (Sudholt 2009, `DOI: 10.1016/j.tcs.2009.03.003` / `arXiv:1109.6441`; Nguyen and Sudholt 2020, `DOI: 10.1016/j.artint.2020.103345`):**
  1. *$\mathsf{PLS}$-completeness (`Thms. 1–2`):* Unbounded local search ($\delta = \infty$) requires $2^{\Omega(n)}$ pivots on worst-case pseudo-Boolean instances; explicit depth caps $\delta < \infty$ are mandatory.
  2. *Depth phase transition on $f_D$ (`Thm. 3`, `Probe 1`):* On ridge $f_D$ where global portals exist only near depth $D$ while deeper ridge climbing enters a deceptive sink, depth $\delta = D$ succeeds in $\operatorname{poly}(n)$, whereas **$|\delta - D| \ge \log^3 n$ degrades to superpolynomial / $2^{\Omega(n^\varepsilon)}$ runtime** (under-reaching at $\delta < D$ or overshooting into the deceptive sink at $\delta > D$).
  3. *Frequency phase transition (`Thm. 4`):* On twin landscapes $\text{Race}_{\text{con}}$ and $\text{Race}_{\text{uncon}}$, changing local-search period by a factor of $2$ ($\tau \leftrightarrow 2\tau$) flips runtime between $\operatorname{poly}(n)$ and $2^{\Omega(n^\varepsilon)}$ in opposite directions.
  4. *Big-valley `Hurdle` speedup (`Nguyen and Sudholt 2020`, `Thms. 2, 5, 9, 16, 17`):* On hurdle width $w$, restarted LS takes $2^{\Omega(n)}$ and `(1+1)` EA takes $\Theta(n^w)$ (or $\Omega(e^w)$ at optimal $p_m = w/n$), whereas `(1+1)` MA (`FILS`, $\delta \ge w$) takes **$\Theta(n^3 / w^2)$**—getting *faster* as barrier width $w$ grows—and `(2+1)` MA with uniform crossover achieves $O(\frac{n^2 \log(n/w)}{w})$.

#### 1.3 Parallel Tempering Mechanics vs. Non-MCMC Symbolic Transfer Limits

`Basis: documentation + derived`

- **Exact Equilibrium Laws (Swendsen and Wang 1986; Hukushima and Nemoto 1996; Predescu, Predescu, and Ciobanu 2004; Kone and Kofke 2005; Earl and Deem 2005; Katzgraber et al. 2006):** Across $M$ replicas targeting $\prod_m Z(\beta_m)^{-1}\exp(-\beta_m E(X_m))$, partition functions cancel in the Metropolis swap ratio $P_{\text{acc}}(i \leftrightarrow j) = \min\!\big(1, \exp((\beta_i - \beta_j)(E_i - E_j))\big)$ (or Hamiltonian $P_{\text{acc}}^{\text{HPT}}$ over $H_1,\dots,H_M$). Extensive energy fluctuations $\operatorname{Var}_\beta(E) = k_B T^2 C_V \sim O(d)$ require **$M = \Theta(\sqrt{d})$** replicas (`Predescu Cor. 2`: $M(d,p) \ge d^{1/2}\frac{\sqrt{2}\ln(\beta_{\max}/\beta_{\min})}{4\operatorname{erf}^{-1}(1-p)}$, geometric $T_i = T_{\min}R^{i-1}$ when $C_V = \text{const}$). Under constant $C_V$ and uncorrelated walks, Kone and Kofke maximize $(\ln B)^2 \operatorname{erfc}(\frac{1-B}{1+B}C^{1/2})$ at **$\bar{p}_{\text{acc}}^* \approx 0.23$**. Across phase-transition bottlenecks where diffusivity $D(T)$ plummets, Katzgraber et al. label replicas `"up"` / `"down"` from $T_{\min}$ / $T_{\max}$ ($f(T_i) = \frac{n_{\text{up}}}{n_{\text{up}}+n_{\text{down}}}$) and minimize round-trip time $\tau_{\text{rt}}$ via **$\eta_{\text{opt}}(T) \propto 1/\sqrt{D(T)}$**, concentrating replicas *inside* heat-capacity spikes so $f(T_i)$ drops linearly with index $i$.
- **Why Boltzmann Swaps Fail in Symbolic/LLM Search (`Basis: derived`):** Autoregressive decoding temperature $T_m$ scales token logits of a pretrained prior rather than an invariant measure $\propto e^{-E(x)/T_m}$; discrete rubric scores $E(x)$ lack extensive physical scale (multiplying rubric weights by $10$ exponentiates $P_{\text{acc}}^{10}$); constructive refactoring kernels are irreversible ($q(x'\mid x) \neq q(x\mid x')$); and accepting degrading swaps into $T_{\min}$ destroys verified artifacts. Only **constraint-relaxation ladders ($H_1 \dots H_M$)**, **asymmetric elitist/Baldwinian downward injection**, and **Katzgraber bottleneck budget concentration ($\eta \propto 1/\sqrt{D}$)** transfer soundly.

#### 1.4 Quality-Diversity Illumination and Memory-Guided Local Search (`VNS`, `ILS`, `Tabu Search`)

`Basis: documentation + derived`

- **Quality-Diversity & Descriptor Alignment (Lehman and Stanley 2011a,b; Mouret and Clune 2015; Pugh, Soros, and Stanley 2016; Vassiliades, Chatzilygeroudis, and Mouret 2018):**
  - *Novelty Search* ($\rho(x) = \frac{1}{k}\sum_{i=1}^k \operatorname{dist}(b(x), \mu_i)$) solves **39/40** Hard Deceptive Mazes vs. **3/40** for Fitness-NEAT ($2.7\times$ smaller networks), **but collapses to 5/100 in the Unenclosed Maze** when unconstrained behavioral space permits trivial novelty.
  - *`MAP-Elites`* stores one monotone elite per grid cell (with $>90\%$ of niche champions descending across distant stepping-stone cells); *`CVT-MAP-Elites`* replaces $O(b^d)$ grids with $k$ Lloyd Voronoi centroids up to $1000\text{D}$, **failing whenever valid artifacts occupy a sparse/measure-zero submanifold** of $[0,1]^d$ unless centroids are sampled strictly on the feasible manifold (Vassiliades et al. 2018, §VI & fn. 6).
  - *BD Alignment Law (Pugh et al. 2016):* Unaligned single-BC QD (`DirectionBC`) solves **0/20** Symmetric Gauntlet runs (spinning in the start room), while aligned `EndpointBC` solves the maze but under-illuminates target mechanisms; **Multi-BC coupling** (`Aligned Stepping-Stone BD` $\times$ `Orthogonal Mechanism BD`) plus passive diversity slots (`MEPGD`) strictly dominates. For symbolic architectures under small budget $B$, BDs must be **discrete structural tuples** sized to $K \le \lfloor B/5 \rfloor$ rather than dense text embeddings.
- **VNS, ILS, and Tabu Anti-Oscillation Memory (Mladenović and Hansen 1997, 2005; Lourenço, Martin, and Stützle 2003; Glover 1989, 1990):**
  - *`VNS`:* Resets neighborhood $k \leftarrow 1$ on improvement and escalates $k \leftarrow k+1$ otherwise; **Skewed VNS** ($f(x'') - \alpha\rho(x,x'') < f(x)$) jumps from **6/25** (`BVNS`) to **23/25** solved on deceptive `WMAXSAT`.
  - *`ILS`:* Walks on local-optima space $S^*$ using **non-undoable perturbations** orthogonal to local search (e.g., TSP 4-opt double-bridge yields $260\times$ more 3-opt runs in 120s than random restarts on `rl5915`); `Better` wins on single-funnel TSP while `RW`/`Restart` wins on multi-funnel QAP (`0.0%` vs. `2.51%` excess on `kra30a`).
  - *`Tabu Search`:* Combines short-term attribute recency tenure `TABUSTATE(a)` ($t \in [7,20]$ or dynamic `C-SEQUENCE`), **Global/Regional/Attribute Aspiration Criteria** ($c(s(x)) < c(x^*)$, overriding conservative attribute tabus on strict breakthroughs), long-term residence/transition frequency diversification, **Strategic Oscillation** across feasibility/complexity boundaries $g(x) \le B$, and **Path Relinking** across elite symmetric differences $\Delta(x_{\text{init}}, x_{\text{guide}})$.

### 2. Set-Based Concurrent Engineering, Multi-Fidelity Bandits, and Non-Convex Pareto Optimization

#### 2.1 Set-Based Concurrent Engineering (SBCE) and System DSE

`Basis: documentation + derived`

- **Three SBCE Principles vs. Point-Based Rework Cascades (Ward et al. 1995; Sobek, Ward, and Liker 1999; Singer, Doerry, and Buckley 2009; Doerry and Koenig 2019; Toche, Pellerin, and Fortin 2020):** Point-based concurrent engineering iterates critiques around an early point guess without convergence guarantees—each subsystem fix invalidates coupled assumptions elsewhere. Toyota's SBCE enforces: **(1) Map the design space** (range-based checklists, parallel subsystem prototypes, parametric trade-off curves), **(2) Integrate by intersection** (locating $\bigcap_d \mathcal{F}_d$, imposing minimum early constraint such as $\sim 1\text{ cm}$ styling slack and screw-body functional build, and **conceptual robustness** against interfacing design uncertainty), and **(3) Establish feasibility before commitment** (gradual narrowing across integrating gates backed by a conservative fallback + hard cut-off date).
- **Three-Valued Asymmetric Falsification (Doerry and Koenig 2019):** Each domain $d \in \{1,\dots,D\}$ labels regions `Feasible`, `Uncertain`, or `Not Feasible`. Integrated status is `Feasible` iff $\forall d$ `Feasible`, and `Not Feasible` if **$\exists d$ `Not Feasible`**. Because refutation requires **only one domain** while confirmation requires all $D$, fast logic screens asynchronously prune infeasible volume early (eliminating $96\%$ of a naval surface-combatant trade space before high-fidelity CFD/FEA) without subjective utility-weight consensus. *Documented SBCE failure boundaries:* interval-propagation dependency blowup on coupled nonlinear equations (Toche et al. 2020), cross-domain combinatorial explosion without Design Structure Matrix decoupling, schedule procrastination collapsing into subjective Pugh matrices, and fast-feedback modular regimes where point-based iteration is cheaper (Sobek et al. 1999, p. 71; Gries 2004; Pimentel 2017).

#### 2.2 Multi-Fidelity Bandits and Surrogate Model Management

#### 2.3 Non-Convex Pareto Scalarization Pathologies and Why Naive "Compromise Synthesis" Is Strictly Dominated

`Basis: documentation + derived + execution`

- **Two Pathologies of Linear Weighted Sums (Das and Dennis 1997, `DOI: 10.1007/BF01197559`):** Minimizing $\sum_{i=1}^n w_i f_i(x)$ ($w_i \ge 0, \sum w_i = 1$) rotates objective axes (`TLC_theta`) and touches **only supporting-hyperplane points on the convex hull**: (1) **every Pareto-optimal solution in a non-convex inward dent is `100%` invisible for all $w \in \Delta^{n-1}$** (`0/19` interior points recovered on $f_2 = \sqrt{1-f_1^2}$ in `Probe 2`); and (2) on strictly convex fronts, uniform weight steps clump via $\alpha(f_1) = (1 - \partial f_2/\partial f_1)^{-1}$ (`0/19` uniform weights land in $[5.05, 10]$ on $f_2 = 1/f_1$, `Probe 2`).
- **Exact Non-Convex Recovery Without Weak-Pareto Ties (Miettinen 1999; Miettinen, Hakanen, and Podkopaev 2016; Deb et al. 2002, 2014; Zhang and Li 2007):**
  - *$\varepsilon$-Constraint (`Miettinen Thm. 3.2.2`):* $x^*$ is Pareto optimal (convex or non-convex) **iff** it solves $\min_x f_\ell(x)$ s.t. $f_j(x) \le f_j(x^*)$ ($j \neq \ell$) for all $\ell = 1,\dots,k$.
  - *Augmented Weighted Tchebycheff (`Miettinen Thm. 3.4.7`; `MHP16 Thms. 1 & 3`):* Standard Tchebycheff $\max_i w_i|f_i(x) - z_i^{\star\star}|$ admits weakly Pareto-optimal ties along flat L-contours; adding $0 < \rho \ll 1$:
    $$\min_{x \in S} \left\{ \max_{i=1,\dots,k} \big[ w_i (f_i(x) - \bar{z}_i) \big] + \rho \sum_{i=1}^{k} (f_i(x) - \bar{z}_i) \right\}$$
    guarantees **every minimizer is strictly and properly Pareto optimal** and recovers every properly Pareto-optimal point on non-convex or discrete fronts (`14/19` interior points on $f_2 = \sqrt{1-f_1^2}$ in `Probe 2`).
  - *Many-Objective Breakdown ($M \ge 4$):* NSGA-II's $O(MN^2)$ Pareto sorting + crowding distance stalls at $M \ge 4$ as $|F_1| \to N$ (dominance resistance), requiring NSGA-III's $H = \binom{M+p-1}{p}$ Das–Dennis reference rays (Deb and Jain 2014) or MOEA/D's $O(mNT)$ neighborhood Tchebycheff/PBI decomposition (Zhang and Li 2007).
- **Strict Dominance of Naive Multi-Agent Feature-Union Synthesis (`Basis: derived + execution`, `Probe 2`):** Given minimalist candidate $M_\alpha = (P_\alpha, \Sigma_\alpha)$ and defensive-complete candidate $M_\beta = (P_\beta, \Sigma_\beta)$ ($f_{\text{unsound}}(M_\beta) = f_{\text{gap}}(M_\beta) = 0$), an unguided moderator combining features into $M_{\text{union}} = (P_\alpha \cup P_\beta, \Sigma_\alpha \cup \Sigma_\beta \cup \Sigma_{\text{bridge}})$ whenever $P_\alpha \not\subseteq P_\beta$ inflates primitive count $|P_\alpha \cup P_\beta| > |P_\beta|$ and interaction surface $\binom{|P_\alpha \cup P_\beta|}{2} + |\Sigma_{\text{bridge}}| > \binom{|P_\beta|}{2}$ with zero soundness/coverage gain over $M_\beta$—making **$M_{\text{union}}$ strictly Pareto-dominated by $M_\beta$**! And if a refactored unification $M^*$ sits at a non-convex balanced point—e.g., $(f_{\text{prim}}, f_{\text{enc}})$ at $M_\alpha = (2, 9), M_\beta = (9, 2), M^* = (6, 6), M_{\text{union}} = (10, 3)$—linear weighted sums select **only** $M_\alpha$ or $M_\beta$ across all `1,001` weights, whereas **lexicographic soundness gating + subtractive quotient unification + Augmented Tchebycheff** uniquely selects $M^*$ (`Probe 2`).

### 3. Counterexample-Guided Overfitting, Optimization Stopping Rules, Capture-Recapture Breakdowns, and $T \times B$ Holdout Reuse

#### 3.1 CEGAR, Craig Interpolation, IC3/PDR, OGIS, and SyGuS Expressiveness Overfitting

`Basis: documentation + derived + execution`

- **Refinement Complexity and Root-Cause Generalization (Clarke et al. 2003; McMillan 2003; Henzinger et al. 2004; Bradley 2011):**
  - *`CEGAR` (`Clarke et al. 2003`):* `SplitPATH` partitions concrete states in failure class $h^{-1}(\hat{s}_i)$ into dead-end ($S_{D,i}$), bad ($S_{B,i}$), and irrelevant ($S_{I,i}$). When $S_{I,i} = \emptyset$, BDD cofactoring (`PolyRefine`) yields the unique coarsest split in polynomial time (`Cor. 4.20`); when $S_{I,i} \neq \emptyset$, minimal state splitting is **NP-hard** and **inapproximable within $|V|^{1/7-\epsilon}$** (`Thm. 4.17, Rem. 5.8`), and infinite-state loops can diverge forever enumerating $x=0, x=1, \dots$ instead of $x \ge 0$.
  - *Cut-Point Craig Interpolants (`McMillan 2003 Thm. 2`; `Henzinger et al. 2004 Thm. 1`):* Extracting $\psi_i = \operatorname{ITP}(\varphi_i^-, \varphi_i^+)$ in linear time from refutation proofs restricts predicates strictly to **variables live at module/cut-point interfaces** ($\mathcal{L}(\varphi_i^-) \cap \mathcal{L}(\varphi_i^+)$), keeping BLAST to ~8 active predicates per location across 138 KLOC of C.
  - *`IC3/PDR` (`Bradley 2011`):* Rather than blocking a single concrete predecessor state $\neg s$, `MIC` computes a **minimal inductive subclause $c \subseteq \neg s$** ($I \Rightarrow c$ and $F_i \land c \land T \Rightarrow c'$), turning one concrete failure into a high-dimensional forbidden state cube without unrolling $T^k$.
- **CEGIS Divergence, OGIS Occam Learners, and Expressiveness Monotonicity (Solar-Lezama 2008; Jha and Seshia 2017; Padhi et al. 2019):**
  - *Unbounded Divergence & Table Memorization (`Solar-Lezama 2008` §4.2–4.3):* Over unbounded holes/inputs, CEGIS can diverge on unrealizable specs (`(??0 / pow(2,i)) % 2 == i % 2`), and lookup-table holes (`tableBasedAddition`) collapse pruning from ~15 bits/counterexample to 1 table slot ($2^8 = 256$ rounds).
  - *`OGIS` Hierarchy (`Jha and Seshia 2017`, `Thms. 5.1–5.6, 6.1–6.3`):* Minimal-index Occam learners match full CEGIS (`mincegis = cegis`, `Thm. 5.2`) while arbitrary consistent learners diverge; bounded verification is strictly weaker (`cbcegis` $\subsetneq$ `cegis`); positive witnesses are incomparable under finite memory (`pbcegis` $\parallel$ `cegis`), while unbounded-memory learners simulate counterexample exclusion via syntactic `if x == cex then ...` point-fix guards (`Footnote 5`). Optimal teaching sequences are NP-hard (`Thm. 6.1`, Set Cover) and lower-bounded by Teaching Dimension $\ge \operatorname{VC}(\mathcal{C})/\log_2|\mathcal{C}|$.
  - *`SyGuS` Overfitting Theorems (`Padhi et al. 2019`, `arXiv:1905.07457v3`, `Defs. 5–7, Thms. 1–9`):* Define **Potential for Overfitting** $\Omega(\langle\varphi, E\rangle, Z) \triangleq |\{e \in E \mid e \not\models \langle\varphi, E\rangle \land \forall\langle x,y\rangle \in Z: e(x)=y\}|$. Beyond No-Free-Lunch thresholds ($k > \binom{|X|}{m}|Y|^m$ finite `Thm. 3`; $k > \aleph_0$ infinite `Thm. 4`), $\Omega(S, Z) > 0$ for *every* $m$-example set $Z$, and **Expressiveness Monotonicity (`Theorem 5`)** proves $E_1 \subseteq E_2 \implies \Omega(\langle\varphi, E_1\rangle, Z) \le \Omega(\langle\varphi, E_2\rangle, Z)$ (`Probe 4`: on $2x+1 \bmod 16$ at $|Z|=2$, affine $\Omega(E_1)=0$ vs. `ite`-augmented $\Omega(E_2)=96$, causing performance degradation on **65%** of SyGuS benchmark pairs). Interleaving disjoint size-and-grammar strata $U_j^k = \{e \in E_j \setminus E_{j-1} \mid \operatorname{size}(e)=k\}$ by density well-order (`HEnum`, `Thms. 7–9`) achieves a **$5.1\times$ speedup** on SyGuS-Comp Inv (`2,191 s -> 429 s`).

#### 3.2 Black-Box and Evolutionary Stopping Criteria (`Rudolph 1994` and `CMA-ES/pycma` v4.4.0)

`Basis: documentation + source`

- **Markov-Chain Convergence vs. Zero Stopping Certificate (Rudolph 1994, `DOI: 10.1109/72.265964`):** A canonical GA with $p_m \in (0,1)$ and roulette selection has strictly positive primitive transition matrix $P = CMS > 0$, hence is ergodic ($\pi_i > 0\;\forall i$) and **never converges to $f^*$** (`Thm. 4`). Adding elitism makes $P^+$ reducible with $\lim_{t\to\infty}\Pr[Z_t = f^*] = 1$ (`Thm. 7`), **yet carries zero finite-time optimality certificate** (`§6`): finite-window flatness cannot distinguish $f^*$ from a local basin of escape barrier $d$ (waiting time $\Theta(p_m^{-d})$), and worst-case Markov generation bounds exceed $10^{100}$ (Safe et al. 2004).
- **Source-Verified `CMA-ES/pycma` v4.4.0 Termination Suite (`5846f6ea3ac709014f78779182fd01db3c508550`, `cma/options_parameters.py:404-452` & `cma/evolution_strategy.py:3760-3920`; Hansen 2023 App. B.3):**
  1. **`tolfun = 1e-11` / `tolfunhist = 1e-12`:** Range of `es.fit.hist` over window $10 + \lceil 30 N / \lambda \rceil$ (prematurely stops on flat plateaus).
  2. **`tolx = 1e-11` / `tolxstagnation = [1e-9, 20, 0.1]`:** Step-size underflow `all(sigma * sqrt(dC) < tolx)` or mean-vector freeze $\|m_t - m_{t-1}\|/\|m\| < 10^{-9}$ over $\max(20, 0.1 t)$ gens.
  3. **`tolfacupx = 1e3` / `tolupsigma = 1e20`:** Step-size divergence (`sigma * diag(D)_i > 1e3 * sigma_0 * diag(D_0)_i`).
  4. **`noeffectaxis` / `noeffectcoord` / `conditioncov = 1e14`:** IEEE-754 `float64` absorption (`mean == mean + 0.1 * sigma * D[i] * B[:,i]`, `mean == mean + 0.2 * sigma * sqrt(dC)`) or ill-conditioning `(D[-1]/D[0])^2 > 1e14`.
  5. **`tolflatfitness = 1`:** Stops immediately when `fit[0] == fit[min(1 + lambda//4, lambda-2)]` ($\ge 25\%$ tied top fitness)—**catastrophically misfiring on discrete/binary pass-fail evaluators!**
  6. **`tolstagnation = int(100 + 100 * N**1.5 / popsize)`:** Samples `histbest` & `histmedian` every 5th iteration; stops when `median(newest l) >= median(prior l)` ($l = \lfloor \max(\text{tolstagnation}/10, \text{len}/10) \rfloor$)—documented directly in `options_parameters.py:432` to **false-stop early on `ftablet` ($N \ge 50$), `fcigtab` ($N \ge 100$), and integer variables**.
- **DE and Multi-Objective Stopping Failures (Zielinski, Peters, and Laur 2006; Wagner, Trautmann, and Naujoks 2009; Martí et al. 2016; Kitamura and Tanabe 2026):** DE's `ImpBest` misses bursty punctuation jumps while `MaxDist` never terminates on symmetric multimodal peaks; in EMO, `OCD` ($\chi^2$ variance + $t$-slope over $T=10$ gens) misfires on non-monotone NSGA-II crowding oscillations (worst rank `5.00/5` at $\delta=0$ in Kitamura and Tanabe 2026), and Kalman-filtered Mutual Domination Rate `MGBM` **failed to stop on `100%` of runs under published default $I_{\min} = 10^{-4}$**, requiring a $1{,}200\times$ manual retuning to $0.12$.

#### 3.3 Software Inspection Capture-Recapture Breakdowns for Small Critic Teams ($k \le 3$)

`Basis: documentation + derived + execution`

Under Otis et al.'s (1978) closed-population lattice ($M_0$: constant $p$; $M_t$: reviewer $p_j$; $M_h$: defect difficulty $p_i$; $M_{th}$: $a_i b_j$) over $t$ reviewers discovering $D = \sum_{m=1}^t f_m$ distinct defects ($f_m$ = defects caught by exactly $m$ reviewers):
- **Canonical Estimators:** Two-sample Chapman $\hat{N}_{\text{Chapman}} = \frac{(n_1+1)(n_2+1)}{m_2+1} - 1$ (Chapman 1951); Burnham–Overton $M_h$-Jackknife $\hat{N}_{J1} = D + \frac{t-1}{t}f_1$, $\hat{N}_{J2} = D + \frac{2t-3}{t}f_1 - \frac{(t-2)^2}{t(t-1)}f_2$ (orders $1..5$, Burnham and Overton 1978); Chao's Cauchy–Schwarz $M_h$ lower bound $\hat{N}_{\text{Chao1}} = D + \frac{f_1^2}{2f_2}$ and bias-corrected $D + \frac{t-1}{t}\frac{f_1(f_1-1)}{2(f_2+1)}$ (Chao 1987); and $M_{th}$ sample-coverage $\hat{N}_{M_{th}} = \frac{D}{\hat{C}} + \frac{f_1}{\hat{C}}\hat{\gamma}^2$ (Chao, Lee, and Jeng 1992).
- **Empirical Failure Below $k \ge 4$ (Wohlin, Runeson, and Brantestam 1995; Briand et al. 2000; Petersson et al. 2004):** Across all virtual inspector combinations $k = 2..6$ on NASA/GSFC inspections (`Briand et al. 2000`), **every estimator fails at $k = 2, 3$**: at $k = 2$, median relative error is **$-35.8\%$ (`M_0`), $-44.8\%$ (`M_t`), and $-51.7\%$ (`M_h-JE`)**, `M_h-Chao` suffers $+306.7\%$ spikes and $12.8\%\text{–}20.2\%$ $f_2=0$ failures, `M_th-Chao` fails **100%** of the time (dividing by $k - 2 = 0$), and multiplicative debiasing inflates variance by $c^2 = 3.70\times$. Actionable estimation requires **$k \ge 4$ independent inspectors** (`Briand Table 8`; corroborated across 29 studies in `Petersson et al. 2004`).
- **Algebraic Proof That Neither $f_2 = 0$ Nor $f_1 = 0$ Certifies Zero Defects at $k = 2\text{–}3$ (`Probe 5`):**
  1. *Zero Overlap ($f_2 = 0, D > 0$):* $\hat{N}_{\text{LP}} = \hat{N}_{\text{Chao1}} = +\infty$, while $\hat{N}_{J1}(t=2) \equiv \hat{N}_{J2}(t=2) = D + \frac{1}{2}D = \mathbf{1.5D}$ ($\le 2D$ at $t=3$). For $N = 20$ bugs with $p = 0.10$ at $k = 2$, $\Pr[f_2 = 0] = 0.99^{20} = \mathbf{81.79\%}$; at $D=4, f_2=0$, $M_h$-JK outputs $6.0$ while **16 bugs remain missed**.
  2. *Full Overlap ($f_1 = 0, D = f_k$):* Substituting $f_1 = 0$ into **every** Otis–Burnham–Chao estimator yields $\hat{N} = D \implies \hat{f}_0 = 0$ with zero estimated variance! Under a bimodal Wohlin–Runeson / shared-LLM-blind-spot mixture ($N = 20$: $6$ easy bugs at $p = 0.90$, $14$ shared-blind-spot bugs at $p = 0.02$), $k = 2$ critics hit exact full overlap $D=6, f_1=0, f_2=6$ with probability **`16.04%`**, certifying `0` remaining bugs while **14 critical defects (`70%`) survive untouched** (`Probe 5`).

#### 3.4 Serial $\times$ Parallel ($T \times B$) Multi-Branch Amplification of Holdout Overfitting

`Basis: documentation + derived + execution`

Extending `reusable-holdout-thresholdout-ladder-leaderboards-and-clopper-pearson-zero-numerators` via Dwork et al.'s (2015, `arXiv:1506.02629`, `Thms. 9, 13, 15, 17`) $\beta$-approximate max-information $I_\infty^\beta$ and Blum–Hardt / Feldman–Frostig–Hardt boosting lower bounds (`arXiv:1502.04585 Thm. 5.1`, `arXiv:1905.10360 Thms. 1.2, 3.1`) over $T$ adaptive rounds of $B$ parallel branches on an $n$-item holdout $S \sim \mathcal{D}^n$:
1. **Regime (a) — End-Only Single Selection ($T=1$ holdout touch across $K = TB$ open-loop branches):** $|\overline{Y}| = TB \implies \tau_{(a)} = \sqrt{\frac{\ln(2TB/\beta)}{2n}} = O(\sqrt{\ln(TB/\beta)/n})$.
2. **Regime (b) — Per-Round Discrete Winner-Index Only ($i_t^* \in [B]$ passed forward, scores hidden):** Transcript size $|\overline{Y}_T| = B^T$ ($I_\infty \le T\log_2 B$ bits) $\implies \tau_{(b)} = \sqrt{\frac{T\ln B + \ln(2/\beta)}{2n}} = O(\sqrt{(T\ln B + \ln(1/\beta))/n})$ (further compressed if gated by a `Ladder` step margin $\eta$).
3. **Regime (c) — Full Per-Branch Scalar Score Vector Released Each Round ($k = TB$ scalar queries):** Upper bound $\sqrt{\frac{TB\ln(n+1)+\ln(2/\beta)}{2n}}$ matched by 2-round correlation-weighted majority attack bias $\Theta(\min(1, \sqrt{TB/n}))$ (`Probe 6`: on pure coin-flip labels at $n=12, T=3, B=4$, empirical holdout accuracy inflates from `0.500` to **`0.838` (`+0.338` bias)**, and to **`0.905`** at $T=5, B=4$; leaking per-item failure vectors leaks $TBn$ bits for $O(1)$-round memorization!).

## Boundaries

- **`known not to apply` — Parallel Tempering Detailed Balance, Kone–Kofke `23%` Swap Rule, and Continuous `CVT-MAP-Elites` on Symbolic/LLM Artifacts:** Non-reversible generative edits over non-extensive ordinal rubrics lack Boltzmann stationary distributions and partition functions, invalidating $\exp((\beta_i-\beta_j)(E_i-E_j))$; likewise, uniform hypercube sampling in $[0,1]^d$ or text-embedding space places nearly all Voronoi cells off the measure-zero manifold of well-typed symbolic designs.
- **`known not to apply` — Linear Weighted-Sum Rubrics (`\sum w_i f_i`) and `CMA-ES` Continuous Conditioning Rules (`conditioncov`, `tolflatfitness=1`) on Discrete Architectures:** By Das and Dennis (1997), convex weighted sums mathematically cannot select non-convex Pareto optima; `pycma`'s `tolflatfitness = 1` halts immediately on discrete pass/fail gates, and `MGBM`'s default $I_{\min} = 10^{-4}$ fails to stop on `100%` of tested MOEA runs without domain-specific retuning (Kitamura and Tanabe 2026).
- **`known not to apply` — Capture-Recapture Residual-Defect Certification Below $k \ge 4$ Independent Critics or Under Shared Blind Spots:** All Otis–Burnham–Chao estimators fail below $k = 4$ reviewers (Briand et al. 2000) and are unidentifiable on shared blind-spot strata ($p_{\text{hard}} \to 0$) where $f_1 = 0$ yields $\hat{f}_0 = 0$ regardless of missed defect count.
- **`unsupported` — Exact `HEnum` AST-Size Partitioning and Linear-Time Craig Interpolants on Unformalized Prose Specifications:** McMillan's linear-time resolution interpolants (`Thm. 2`) and Padhi et al.'s `Divide` $jk$-unique enumeration (`Alg. 3`) require formal logic / BNF grammars; on natural-language design documents without mechanized models, interface-scoped root-cause generalization and tier-ordered concept parsimony operate as structural discipline rather than exact decision procedures.
- **`unknown` — Independence of $\ge 4$ Distinct Frontier LLM Families Under $M_{th}$ Capture-Recapture:** No controlled primary study measures whether findings from $\ge 4$ distinct-family frontier models satisfy $M_{th}$ conditional independence $p_{ij} = a_i b_j$ on formal specification or architectural review tasks.
- **`not examined` — Non-Stationary Dynamic Optimization Landscapes and Quantum Annealing:** Time-varying objective landscapes and quantum tunneling schedules (`QA`/`SQA`) were outside scope and not examined.
