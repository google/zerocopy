## Findings

### 1. Live `GVN` `0 / x -> 0` `-Zmir-preserve-ub` Bypass vs. Initialization-Preserving Cases

`Basis: source + execution (support/e3_6_gvn_instsimplify_miri_and_zerocopy.json)`

In `compiler/rustc_mir_transform/src/gvn.rs:1520-1531` (`rust-lang/rust@412be32a6a189d9a28922beee178bc0abee9d492`), `GVN::simplify_binary_inner` groups `BinOp::Div` and `BinOp::Rem` with `Mul`, `BitAnd`, `Shl`, and `Shr` under left-zero absorbing elements:

```rust
(
    BinOp::Mul
    | BinOp::MulWithOverflow
    | BinOp::MulUnchecked
    | BinOp::Div
    | BinOp::Rem
    | BinOp::BitAnd
    | BinOp::Shl
    | BinOp::Shr,
    Left(0),
    _,
) => self.insert_scalar(lhs_ty, Scalar::from_uint(0u128, layout.size)),
```

Unlike surface Rust `/` (which lowers in `Analysis` MIR to `TerminatorKind::Assert(rhs != 0)` followed by `Rvalue::BinaryOp(BinOp::Div, ...)`), `Runtime` MIR `BinOp::Div` and `BinOp::Rem` have **immediate UB** on `rhs == 0` (`evalArithOp` returns `.error .undefined`, matching `throw_ub!(DivisionByZero)` in `compiler/rustc_const_eval/src/interpret/operator.rs`). Live `Miri` execution across six `#[custom_mir(dialect = "runtime", phase = "post-cleanup")]` specimens demonstrates the exact behavior:

## Boundaries

1. **Unmodeled `GVN` & `InstSimplify` sub-passes (`not examined` / `known not to apply`).** Upstream `GVN` also performs general SSA value numbering / CSE across arbitrary dominator distances, `CastKind` simplification (`simplify_cast`), aggregate-to-copy (`simplify_aggregate_to_copy`), `PtrMetadata` unsizing extraction, and checked `*WithOverflow` tuple construction (`gvn.rs:1556-1560`, which requires tuple/projection support outside `mirProfileVersion = 1`); upstream `InstSimplify` also folds `&(*a) -> a`, repeated array aggregates, raw pointer aggregates, and intrinsic/clone calls (`instsimplify.rs:75-102, 141-320`). `VrustcFormal.F1_6` scopes strictly to the 6 statement-level algebraic, unary-chain, constant, and enum-discriminant passes formalized.
2. **Scope of `Trusted.MirSemantics` (`mirProfileVersion = 1`, `unsupported`).** Inherited directly from `joshlf/anneal@c131b320eeeb26ef692e5afd5ec8db49ee518613`: 64-bit targets (`pointerWidthBits = 64`), value-only entry parameters (`callerMem = ⟨[]⟩`), unprojected scalar/`bool`/`()`/`!`/fieldless-enum locals, conservative `Operand.move` deinitialization (`deinitMovedPlace = true`), and fail-closed `.fault .unsupported` on `call`, `assert`, `unwindResume`, projected places, floats, pointers, and tuples.
3. **Flow-sensitive abstract certificates (`checkZeroLhsDivRemProgram`, `checkAbsorbingOrSelfCancelProgram`, `checkConstAndEnumDiscrFoldProgram`) are sound sufficient checks, not complete characterizations (`known not to apply`).** The 4-point (`liveNonZero`), 3-point (`liveInit`), and variant (`DiscrAbsSlot`) abstract interpreters join path-insensitively at CFG merges and conservatively reject unmodeled statement/rvalue constructors or correlated multi-variable invariants while guaranteeing exact `FullSemanticEquiv` whenever `== true`.
