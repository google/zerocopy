# HVDC Modular Multilevel Converter Circulating Currents, Sub-3ms Hybrid DC Breaker Commutation, and Sub-Synchronous Fault-Ride-Through Envelopes

## Summary

High-voltage direct current (`HVDC`) transmission stations built around the Lesnicar–Marquardt Modular Multilevel Converter (`MMC`) (`N = 200`–`500` half-bridge or full-bridge submodules per arm switching at `1`–`10 kHz`) couple four tight fast-timescale physical and algorithmic invariants whose violation causes catastrophic valve destruction, DC switchgear arcing, sub-synchronous oscillation (`SSO`), or wide-area AC grid blackouts:

1. **Arm Circulating-Current Polarity and Submodule Sorting Starvation**: Each phase leg carries a differential inner loop current $i_{\text{circ},k} = \frac{1}{2}(i_{u,k} + i_{l,k})$ driven by a negative-sequence second-harmonic ($2\omega_0$) capacitor voltage ripple. Suppressing $i_{\text{circ},k}$ via a double-line-frequency proportional-resonant (`PR`) or synchronous $2\omega_0$ $dq$ controller requires adding $+v_{\text{diff,ref}}/V_{\text{dc}}$ to both upper and lower insertion indices ($n_u, n_l$) so inserted capacitor voltage opposes $2 L_{\text{arm}} \frac{di_{\text{circ}}}{dt}$; inverting that sign creates positive feedback that drives arm currents past semiconductor limits within milliseconds. Simultaneously, reduced-switching-frequency hysteresis-band sorting (`\Delta V_{\text{band}}`) without switching-count fairness pins low-index submodules on during arm-current zero-crossing plateaus, creating `1.86x` IGBT thermal-aging skew (`Basis: normative + execution`).
2. **Sub-3ms Hybrid HVDC Breaker $dI/dt$ Commutation and Counter-Voltage Saturation**: Unlike AC circuit breakers (`DPsim` `EMT::Ph3::Switch` `SwitchingMode::CurrentZero`, which waits indefinitely for natural current zero-crossings that never occur on monotonic DC faults), interrupting a low-inductance pole-to-ground HVDC cable fault requires a proactive hybrid breaker (Load Commutation Switch `LCS` + Ultra-Fast Disconnector `UFD` + main IGBT stack + metal-oxide varistor `MOV` bank). Total mechanical opening latency $t_{\text{open}} = t_{\text{det}} + t_{\text{lcs}} + t_{\text{ufd}}$ (`~2.25 ms`) must satisfy $I_0 + \frac{V_{\text{dc}}}{L_{\text{dc}}} t_{\text{open}} \le I_{\text{SOA,max}}$ (`9.0 kA`), and the `MOV` clamping voltage must maintain strict excess counter-voltage $V_{\text{mov}} \ge (1 + \eta) V_{\text{dc}}$ ($\eta \approx 0.50$). Degrading $V_{\text{mov}}$ from `1.50 pu` to `1.03 pu` stretches fault-clearing time `11.2x` (`8.07 ms -> 90.44 ms`) and multiplies absorbed varistor energy `10.1x` (`11.88 MJ -> 119.71 MJ`) into thermal runaway (`Basis: normative + source + execution`).
3. **Sub-Synchronous $dq$ Admittance Passivity Under Delay and High-Bandwidth PLLs**: Transport and sorting delays ($T_d \approx 150\text{--}300\ \mu\text{s}$) combined with aggressive synchronous-reference-frame phase-locked loops (`SRF-PLL` / `KauraPLL` / `ReducedOrderPLL`) induce asymmetric $q$-axis negative real conductance $\lambda_{\min}\!\left(\frac{1}{2}[\mathbf{Y}_{\text{mmc}}(j\omega) + \mathbf{Y}_{\text{mmc}}^H(j\omega)]\right) < 0$ across $[5, 45]\text{ Hz}$ (`-0.4095 pu` in the unpassivated benchmark), destabilizing weak offshore collector grids ($\text{SCR} < 2.0$) or series-compensated AC lines unless passivated via virtual impedance and bounded PLL bandwidth (`+0.0890 pu > 0`) (`Basis: source + derived + execution`).
4. **Fault-Ride-Through (`FRT`) Active-Power Priority vs. Reactive Current Saturation**: During remote AC transmission lightning dips, uncoordinated reactive-current priority (`PowerSimulationsDynamics.jl` `RECurrentControlB` with `PQ_Flag = 0` and unimplemented `# TODO: Voltage Dip Freeze logic` at `inner_control_models.jl:512`) or poorly damped voltage-control interactions saturate total converter current $I_{\max}$ with $I_q$, collapsing active current headroom $I_{p,\max} = \sqrt{I_{\max}^2 - I_q^2} \to 0$. In the **9 August 2019 Great Britain blackout**, lightning on the Eaton Socon–Wymondley `400 kV` line triggered a `737 MW` Hornsea One offshore wind active-power deloading alongside Little Barford (`641 MW` cumulative) and distributed RoCoF/vector-shift trips, driving grid frequency below `48.80 Hz` and triggering Low Frequency Demand Disconnection (`LFDD`) for `~1.1 million` customers (`Basis: normative + source + execution`).

## Findings

### 1. MMC Arm Differential Dynamics, CCSC Polarity Invariant, and Sorting Starvation

`Basis: normative + derived + execution`

In a three-phase Lesnicar–Marquardt `MMC` with pole-to-pole DC bus voltage $V_{\text{dc}}$, $N$ half-bridge submodules (`SM`s) per arm, submodule capacitance $C_{\text{sm}}$, arm inductance $L_{\text{arm}}$, and parasitic arm resistance $R_{\text{arm}}$, each phase leg $k \in \{a, b, c\}$ splits into an upper arm ($u$) and lower arm ($l$). Defining the output AC phase current $i_{s,k} = i_{u,k} - i_{l,k}$ and the inner common-mode **circulating current** $i_{\text{circ},k} = \frac{1}{2}(i_{u,k} + i_{l,k})$, upper and lower arm currents satisfy:
$$i_{u,k} = i_{\text{circ},k} + \frac{1}{2} i_{s,k}, \qquad i_{l,k} = i_{\text{circ},k} - \frac{1}{2} i_{s,k}$$
Summing Kirchhoff's voltage law around the positive-to-negative DC rails for phase leg $k$ eliminates the AC grid voltage and yields the exact differential circulating-loop equation:
$$2 L_{\text{arm}} \frac{d i_{\text{circ},k}}{dt} + 2 R_{\text{arm}} i_{\text{circ},k} = V_{\text{dc}} - \left(n_{u,k}(t) v_{c,u,k}^{\Sigma}(t) + n_{l,k}(t) v_{c,l,k}^{\Sigma}(t)\right)$$
where $n_{u,k}, n_{l,k} \in [0, 1]$ are continuous insertion indices (realized as integer counts $N_{u,k}, N_{l,k} \in \{0, \dots, N\}$ via nearest-level modulation `NLM` or carrier phase-shifted PWM) and $v_{c,\{u,l\},k}^{\Sigma} = \sum_{j=1}^N v_{c,\{u,l\},k,j}$ are aggregate arm capacitor voltages evolving under $\frac{C_{\text{sm}}}{N} \frac{dv_{c,\{u,l\},k}^{\Sigma}}{dt} = n_{\{u,l\},k} i_{\{u,l\},k}$.

1. **Second-Harmonic ($2\omega_0$) Resonance and Sign-Polarity Trap**: Multiplying fundamental AC modulation $n_{u,k} \approx \frac{1}{2}(1 - m \sin \omega_0 t)$ against fundamental arm current produces a dominant negative-sequence double-line-frequency ($2\omega_0 = 2\pi \cdot 100\text{ Hz}$ on a `50 Hz` grid) power pulsation in $v_{c,u,k}^{\Sigma} + v_{c,l,k}^{\Sigma}$. If $L_{\text{arm}}$ and $C_{\text{eq}} = C_{\text{sm}}/N$ place the series LC resonant frequency $\omega_{\text{res}} = \sqrt{\frac{1 + 2 m^2}{4 L_{\text{arm}} C_{\text{eq}}}}$ near $2\omega_0$, uncontrolled $2\omega_0$ circulating current swells dramatically. In Tu, Xu & Xu's Circulating Current Suppressing Controller (`CCSC`) or an equivalent per-phase proportional-resonant (`PR`) damper regulating $e_{\text{circ},k} = i_{\text{circ},k} - \frac{1}{3} I_{\text{dc}}$:
   $$v_{\text{diff,ref},k}(s) = \left(K_p + \frac{2 K_r \omega_c s}{s^2 + 2 \omega_c s + (2\omega_0)^2}\right) e_{\text{circ},k}(s)$$
   the differential control effort **must enter both insertion indices with positive polarity**:
   $$n_{u,k} = \frac{1}{2}\big(1 - m_k(t)\big) + \frac{v_{\text{diff,ref},k}}{V_{\text{dc}}}, \qquad n_{l,k} = \frac{1}{2}\big(1 + m_k(t)\big) + \frac{v_{\text{diff,ref},k}}{V_{\text{dc}}}$$
   because $n_u v_{c,u}^{\Sigma} + n_l v_{c,l}^{\Sigma}$ appears with a **leading minus sign** on the right-hand side of $2 L_{\text{arm}} \frac{di_{\text{circ}}}{dt}$. Reversing this sign (`- v_diff_ref / V_dc` in `n_u, n_l`, a frequent convention mismatch when porting literature diagrams that define $v_{\text{diff}} = \frac{1}{2}[V_{\text{dc}} - (v_u + v_l)]$) turns $-2 K_p e_{\text{circ}}$ damping into $+2 K_p e_{\text{circ}}$ exponential instability (`19.75 kA` runaway in `< 100 ms`).
2. **Submodule Sorting Starvation and Electro-Thermal Skew**: At `10 kHz` control rate with $N = 400$ submodules/arm (`100 us` hard real-time budget), full comparison sorting costs $O(N \log_2 N) \approx 3{,}458$ comparisons/arm (`20,748` across 6 arms). Reduced-switching-frequency algorithms (`Tu et al. 2011`) hold the active bitmask fixed until $\max_j v_{c,j} - \min_j v_{c,j} > \Delta V_{\text{band}}$ and service small $\Delta N_{\text{on}}$ steps incrementally. If incremental $\Delta N_{\text{on}}$ steps scan submodule indices in fixed physical order (`0..N-1`), low-index submodules experience `1.86x` higher switching transitions than high-index cells (`44.11 V` spread vs. `25.56 V` under switching-count-penalized selection), accelerating bond-wire lift-off in the lowest-numbered IGBT press-packs.

### 2. Solver-Architecture Obstructions Across `DPsim`, `OpenModelica`, and `PowerSimulationsDynamics.jl`

`Basis: source`

Direct source inspection of the three pinned simulation engines exposes distinct structural hazards when modeling switched MMCs, DC circuit breakers, and grid-following PLLs:

1. **`DPsim` MNA Switch Matrix Refactorization vs. AC Zero-Crossing Deadlock (`sogno-platform/dpsim` @ `7fcaac00758cc7f448179edf2a508021dd1374b8`)**:
   - In `dpsim-models/src/EMT/EMT_Ph3_Switch.cpp:123-160,274-309`, `EMT::Ph3::Switch` implements three modes: `SwitchingMode::Ideal`, `SwitchingMode::CurrentZero`, and `SwitchingMode::ExponentialZCSEmulation`. Only `SwitchingMode::Ideal` returns `supportsPrecomputedSystemMatrices() == true` (`L285-287`), allowing `MNASolver` to cache LU factorizations indexed by a switch-configuration bitmask. Representing $6 \times 400 = 2{,}400$ individual MMC half-bridge submodules as `Ideal` switches yields $2^{2400}$ theoretical switch combinations—impossible to precompute—requiring Thévenin-equivalent arm companions (`DOMMEL` companion conductance reduction to 1 scalar stamp per arm per step).
   - Conversely, `SwitchingMode::CurrentZero` (`EMT_Ph3_Switch.cpp:137-146,353-355`) sets `mOpeningRequested = true` and defers pole opening until `updateZeroCrossingState(time)` observes a sign change or tolerance band `mZeroCrossingTolerance` on phase current. Applied naively to an HVDC pole fault without a counter-voltage branch, `CurrentZero` **never opens** because DC fault current rises monotonically ($di/dt = (V_{\text{dc}} - R i)/L_{\text{dc}} > 0$). Furthermore, `dpsim/src/MNASolver.cpp:125-128` throws `CPS::SystemError("System-matrix recomputation is not supported with frequency-parallel MNA.")` if variable-conductance switches (`CurrentZero` or `ExponentialZCSEmulation`) are combined with `mFrequencyParallel`.

### 3. Sub-3ms Hybrid HVDC Breaker Commutation Envelope and MOV Counter-Voltage Law

`Basis: normative + derived + execution`

Following Häfner & Jacobson (`CIGRE Bologna 2011`), a proactive `320 kV` hybrid HVDC circuit breaker parallels:
1. **Normal conduction path**: a low-loss semiconductor **Load Commutation Switch (`LCS`)** ($3 \times 3$ matrix of `4.5 kV` IGBT positions, `< 0.01%` station conduction loss) in series with a mechanical **Ultra-Fast Disconnector (`UFD`)** actuated by a Thomson-coil electrodynamic drive;
2. **Main Breaker (`MB`) semiconductor path**: stacked `4.5 kV` StakPak IGBT cells rated for full pole voltage plus transient overvoltage (`~480 kV`);
3. **Energy absorption path**: parallel banks of zinc-oxide **Metal-Oxide Varistors (`MOV`s)** clamping at counter-voltage $V_{\text{mov}} = (1 + \eta) V_{\text{dc}}$.

For a low-impedance pole-to-ground DC fault at distance $x \to 0$ with pre-fault DC current $I_0$, DC bus voltage $V_{\text{dc}}$, series DC smoothing reactor $L_{\text{dc}}$, and loop resistance $R_{\text{line}}$, the fault current prior to `MB` opening at $t_{\text{open}} = t_{\text{det}} + t_{\text{lcs}} + t_{\text{ufd}}$ satisfies:
$$I_{\text{fault}}(t_{\text{open}}) = \frac{V_{\text{dc}}}{R_{\text{line}}} - \left(\frac{V_{\text{dc}}}{R_{\text{line}}} - I_0\right) e^{-R_{\text{line}} t_{\text{open}} / L_{\text{dc}}} \le I_0 + \frac{V_{\text{dc}}}{L_{\text{dc}}} t_{\text{open}}$$
Two non-negotiable physical safety barriers govern interruption:
- **Barrier B1 (Turn-Off SOA Inductance-Latency Invariant)**: $I_0 + \frac{V_{\text{dc}}}{L_{\text{dc}}} (t_{\text{det}} + t_{\text{lcs}} + t_{\text{ufd}}) \le I_{\text{SOA,max}}$. At $V_{\text{dc}} = 320\text{ kV}$, $I_0 = 2.0\text{ kA}$, $t_{\text{open}} = 0.20 + 0.25 + 1.80 = 2.25\text{ ms}$, and $I_{\text{SOA,max}} = 9.0\text{ kA}$, minimum series smoothing inductance is $L_{\text{dc,min}} = \frac{320\text{ kV} \times 2.25\text{ ms}}{7.0\text{ kA}} = 102.86\text{ mH}$. Undersizing $L_{\text{dc}} = 80\text{ mH}$ yields $I(2.25\text{ ms}) = 10.954\text{ kA}$, destroying the `MB` IGBT stack on turn-off (`soa_9ka_safe = false`); sizing $L_{\text{dc}} = 110\text{ mH}$ bounds peak current at `8.519 kA`.
- **Barrier B2 (Strict Excess Counter-Voltage Margin)**: Once `MB` opens at $t_{\text{open}}$, current decays at $\frac{di}{dt} = -\frac{V_{\text{mov}} - V_{\text{dc}} + R_{\text{line}} i}{L_{\text{dc}}}$, clearing in $\Delta t_{\text{clear}} \approx \frac{L_{\text{dc}} I(t_{\text{open}})}{V_{\text{mov}} - V_{\text{dc}}}$ and depositing varistor energy:
  $$E_{\text{mov}} \approx \frac{1}{2} L_{\text{dc}} I(t_{\text{open}})^2 \cdot \frac{V_{\text{mov}}}{V_{\text{mov}} - V_{\text{dc}}} = \frac{1}{2} L_{\text{dc}} I(t_{\text{open}})^2 \left(1 + \frac{1}{\eta}\right)$$
  As $\eta = (V_{\text{mov}} - V_{\text{dc}})/V_{\text{dc}} \to 0^+$ (counter-voltage saturation from under-tiered MOV columns, parallel HVDC station overvoltage, or thermal knee droop), $(1 + 1/\eta)$ diverges hyperbolically: dropping $V_{\text{mov}}$ from `1.50 pu` ($\eta = 0.50$) to `1.03 pu` ($\eta = 0.03$) inflates clearing time from `8.071 ms` to `90.435 ms` and absorbed energy from `11.876 MJ` to `119.705 MJ` (`Basis: execution`).

### 4. Sub-Synchronous Impedance Passivity and the 9 August 2019 Hornsea One / `48.8 Hz` `LFDD` Cascade

`Basis: normative + derived + execution`

1. **MIMO $dq$ Passivity Criterion**: Small-signal converter-grid stability in the sub-synchronous band $f \in [5, 45]\text{ Hz}$ ($50\text{ Hz}$ synchronous frame) is governed by the $2 \times 2$ $dq$ input admittance matrix $\mathbf{Y}_{\text{mmc}}(j\omega)$ relating grid voltage perturbations $[\Delta v_d, \Delta v_q]^\top$ to converter current $[\Delta i_d, \Delta i_q]^\top$. By network passivity theory (Harnefors; Sun), an interconnect of arbitrary passive inductive-capacitive grid topologies with converter stations is guaranteed free of sub-synchronous resonance (`SSR`) / control interaction (`SSCI`) if every station satisfies Hermitian positive-definiteness across the sub-synchronous interval:
   $$\forall \omega \in [2\pi f_{\min}, 2\pi f_{\max}]: \quad \lambda_{\min}\!\left(\frac{1}{2}\big[\mathbf{Y}_{\text{mmc}}(j\omega) + \mathbf{Y}_{\text{mmc}}^H(j\omega)\big]\right) > 0$$
   Because an `SRF-PLL` rotates only the converter's $q$-axis reference frame proportional to $H_{\text{pll}}(s) = \frac{K_{p,\text{pll}} s + K_{i,\text{pll}}}{s^2 + K_{p,\text{pll}} s + K_{i,\text{pll}}}$ scaled by steady-state active current $I_{d0}$ and transport delay $e^{-s T_d}$, high PLL bandwidth (`Kp = 180, Ki = 9500`, $T_d = 250\ \mu\text{s}$, $I_{d0} = 0.95\text{ pu}$) drives $\operatorname{Re}\{Y_{qq}(j\omega)\}$ down to **`-0.4095 pu`** (negative incremental resistance that pumps energy into weak collector resonances, as observed in NanAo and Zhangbei MMC-HVDC incidents). Shaping PLL bandwidth (`Kp = 32, Ki = 420`) and adding sub-synchronous virtual resistance $R_v = 0.18\text{ pu}$ restores strict passivity (`min_eig = +0.0890 pu > 0`). Crucially, passivity is **additive under parallel composition**: $\mathbf{Y}_{\text{bus}} = \mathbf{Y}_1 + \mathbf{Y}_2 \implies \lambda_{\min}(\mathbf{G}_1 + \mathbf{G}_2) \ge \lambda_{\min}(\mathbf{G}_1) + \lambda_{\min}(\mathbf{G}_2) > 0$, enabling modular station-by-station certification without disclosing proprietary vendor inner-loop firmware!

2. **Anatomy of the 9 August 2019 Great Britain Blackout (`737 MW` Hornsea One + `48.8 Hz` `LFDD`)**:
   Per the National Grid ESO Technical Report and Ofgem Final Investigation Report (`2020-01-03`):
   - At `16:52:33.490 BST`, lightning struck the `400 kV` Eaton Socon–Wymondley overhead transmission circuit, cleared normally inside `~70 ms` by protection relays, producing a transient voltage dip and phase-angle vector shift across eastern England (`~150 MW` immediate vector-shift loss of embedded generation).

### 6. Human Comprehensibility (`C1`) and Conceptual Model Refinement (`C2`)

`Basis: derived + source`

- **`(C1)` Human Comprehensibility & Mental-Model Ergonomics**:
  - *Working-Memory Overload in Legacy Converter Stacks*: Engineers auditing HVDC stations currently juggle **four incompatible formalisms simultaneously**: (i) instantaneous three-phase $abc$ valve switching and sorting masks, (ii) positive/negative-sequence synchronous $dq$ PI loops with inconsistent Park angle conventions ($\theta_{\text{pll}}$ vs. $\theta_{\text{pll}} + \pi/2$ in `PSID` `frequency_estimator_models.jl:50` vs. `DPsim` `PLL.cpp:27-35` where `Omega_cutoff` was mistakenly passed for `omegaNom`!), (iii) discrete protection trip timers (`UV`/`OV`/`RoCoF`/breaker `UFD` delays), and (iv) per-unit vs. SI physical units (`pu` current limits vs. `kA` IGBT SOA and `MJ` varistor energy). Whereas an LLM can mechanically emit 500 lines of $dq$ matrix boilerplate (`PowerControllerVSI.cpp:73-86`), a **human protection engineer cannot eyeball a $6 \times 6$ scalar state-space matrix or a sign flip in `n_u = 0.5*(1 - m*sin) ± v_diff/V_dc`** without clear polarity-typed ports and visual passivity margins.
  - *Diagnostic Legibility*: When an MMC-HVDC collector bus oscillates at `28 Hz`, raw EMT waveforms show coupled `22 Hz` and `78 Hz` phase sidebands ($f_0 \pm f_{\text{dq}}$) across thousands of signals. Projecting failure witnesses directly onto the scalar passivity spectrum $\lambda_{\min}(\mathbf{G}(j\omega))$ and highlighting the exact control loop (`PLL` $q$-axis gain vs. `T_delay`) reduces human root-cause comprehension to a single 1D plot (`< 3` concepts in working memory).
- **`(C2)` Simplicity, Orthogonality & Universal Model Refinement (Felleisen & Ousterhout Tests)**:
  - *Eliminating False Mechanism Splits*: Conventional tools treat (a) inner circulating-current stability, (b) sub-synchronous collector resonance, (c) `FRT` reactive-vs-active current priority, and (d) hybrid DC breaker `MOV` counter-voltage sizing as four unrelated sub-disciplines with ad-hoc patches (`# TODO: Voltage Dip Freeze logic`, `SwitchingMode::CurrentZero` vs. `ExponentialZCSEmulation`).
  - *Deep Minimal Abstraction*: All four phenomena reduce cleanly to **two orthogonal, medium-independent laws** without shifting hidden adapter complexity onto consumers:
    1. **Dissipative Port-Hamiltonian / Passivity Envelopes (`\Delta E / \Delta t \le P_{\text{supply}}`)**: Both `2\omega_0` circulating-current damping (`+v_diff_ref` opposing `di_circ/dt`), `[5, 45] Hz` $dq$ grid passivity ($\mathbf{Y} + \mathbf{Y}^H \succ 0$), and `MOV` DC fault demagnetization ($V_{\text{mov}} - V_{\text{dc}} \ge \eta V_{\text{dc}} > 0$) are instances of **strict energy dissipation over a frequency or state band**. Typing port voltages and currents with explicit causal orientation (`PowerPort<Sign::Load | Sign::Source, Frame::SyncDQ<Hz50>>`) makes the `-v_diff_ref` sign-inversion bug and `DPsim`'s `Omega_cutoff`/`omegaNom` unit-role swap **ill-typed at compile time** (`0` runtime wrapper bytes).
    2. **Lexicographic Hard-Envelope Capability Grading (`SOA_Hard > FRT_ActiveFloor > VoltageSupport_Q`)**: Instead of stateful boolean flag spaghetti (`Q_Flag`, `PQ_Flag`, ad-hoc dip-freeze timers), grading the converter output capability by an explicit priority cone $\mathcal{K}_{\text{frt}} = \{(I_p, I_q) : I_p^2 + I_q^2 \le I_{\max}^2 \;\wedge\; I_p \ge \min(I_{p,\text{pre}}, \sqrt{I_{\max}^2 - I_{q,\text{gridcode}}(V)^2})\}$ paired with back-calculation anti-windup unifies normal dispatch, lightning-dip `FRT`, and semiconductor SOA protection in one closed convex projection.

## Boundaries

1. **Full-Bridge Bipolar Fault-Blocking Submodules and Asymmetric Pole-to-Ground Cable Travelling Waves (`not examined` in micro-probe)**: Our executable probe models half-bridge `MMC` arms (`n_u, n_l \in [0, 1]`) paired with Häfner–Jacobson proactive hybrid DC breakers (`LCS + UFD + MB + MOV`). Full-bridge or clamped-double submodules can insert negative arm voltage ($-V_{\text{dc}}$) to block DC fault currents without mechanical DC breakers (at `~1.7x`–`2.0x` continuous IGBT conduction loss), and distributed-parameter Bergeron/Universal Line Model (`ULM`) subsea cable traveling-wave reflections were **not examined** in our lumped-inductance `RL-MOV` probe.
2. **Proprietary Hornsea One Vendor Firmware Gains (`unknown` / redacted in public regulatory filings)**: While the public National Grid ESO and Ofgem reports (`2020-01-03`) establish the exact `737 MW` Hornsea One active-power loss (`799 MW -> 62 MW`), the Eaton Socon–Wymondley `400 kV` lightning trigger, and the `48.80 Hz` `LFDD` cascade, the exact proprietary inner-loop gain tables inside Hornsea One's turbine/offshore compensation firmware remain vendor-confidential (`unknown` from public sources); our `RECurrentControlB` saturation model (`PowerSimulationsDynamics.jl`) characterizes the structural mechanism class rather than proprietary Ørsted/vendor source code.
3. **AC Zero-Crossing Switch Models Applied to Unassisted DC Interruption (`known not to apply`)**: `DPsim`'s `EMT::Ph3::Switch` in `SwitchingMode::CurrentZero` (`EMT_Ph3_Switch.cpp:137-146`) and conventional AC `SF6` breaker arc models relying on natural `50/60 Hz` current zero crossings are **known not to apply** to HVDC fault interruption unless paired with an active LC resonant injection branch or semiconductor/varistor counter-voltage exceeding $V_{\text{dc}}$.
4. **Frequency-Parallel MNA With Time-Varying Breaker Conductance in `DPsim` (`unsupported`)**: As verified at `dpsim/src/MNASolver.cpp:125-128`, combining `mFrequencyParallel == true` with `mSystemMatrixRecomputationEnabled == true` (required by `CurrentZero` and `ExponentialZCSEmulation` switches) is explicitly **unsupported** and throws `CPS::SystemError` at solver initialization.
