# Deep-Research Literature Synthesis, Inter-Document Conflict Resolution, and Scientific Citation-Chain Distortion

## Summary

Primary-source inspection of 15 peer-reviewed articles and preprints (2003–2026), nine pinned public GitHub repository revisions (`Future-House/paper-qa`, `AkariAsai/OpenScholar`, `AkariAsai/ScholarBench`, `stanford-oval/storm`, `openai/simple-evals`, `Ayanami0730/deep_research_bench`, `zhaochen0110/conflictbank`, `SalesforceAIResearch/FaithEval`, `kevinwu23/StanfordClashEval`), and a preserved deterministic Python 3.10+ standard-library three-probe suite (`support/verify_deep_research_conflicts_citation_drift_probes.py`) establishes five operational invariants for literature-synthesis agents and multi-document attribution pipelines:

1. **Retrieval-Grounded Literature Synthesis Requires Iterative Search Revision, Threshold-Clearing Contextual Summarization, and Post-Hoc Attribution Repair (`PaperQA2`, `OpenScholar`):** Without retrieval, frontier parametric models fabricate **`78.7%`–`94.8%`** of cited scientific papers (per-query macro-averaged `OpenScholar` Table 3 rates: `GPT-4o` `78.7%` CS, `94.8%` Biomedicine; `Llama 3.1 70B` `78.1%` CS, `96.6%` Biomedicine). Decomposing RAG into an agentic tool loop (`PaperQA2`: `paper_search` -> `gather_evidence` with LLM Ranking and Contextual Summarization (`RCS`) -> `gen_answer` -> `complete`) reaches superhuman precision on `LitQA2` (`85.2% ± 1.1%` vs. PhD-level human `73.8% ± 9.6%`, $p = 0.0036$; accuracy `66.0% ± 1.2%` vs. `67.7% ± 11.9%`), cuts "cited and unsupported" statements on 240 gene articles nearly in half vs. human Wikipedia (`WikiCrow` `13.5%` vs. `24.9%`, $p = 0.0075$), and detects `2.34 ± 1.99` contradictions per biology paper (`ContraCrow`, `70%` expert-validated, `1.64`/paper empirical lower bound). However, `RCS` exhibits a hard **comprehension cliff**: substituting weaker models (`GPT-3.5-Turbo` or `Llama-3-70B`) into `RCS` drops accuracy *below* using no `RCS` step at all. Combining specialized retrieval (`peS2o`, `45M` open-access papers), cross-encoder reranking, iterative self-feedback (`<= 3` retrieval-backed critique loops), and post-hoc sentence attribution (`OpenScholar`, *Nature* **650**:857–863, 2026) eliminates fabricated titles (`0.0%`) and lifts `GPT-4o` `Scholar-CS` correctness by `+12.7` points (`45.0 -> 57.7`) at `$0.003–$0.05`/query vs. `$0.30–$2.30`/query for `PaperQA2`.
2. **Multi-Perspective Outline Synthesis Expands Breadth (`+152%` Unique Sources) While Exposing Associative Fabrication and Coverage–Precision Splits (`STORM`, `Co-STORM`, `BrowseComp`, `DeepResearch Bench`):** Pre-writing perspective-guided interviews (`STORM`) expand retrieved reference breadth from `39.56` (`w/o Conversation`) and `54.36` (`w/o Perspective`) to **`99.83`** unique sources/article (`84.83%` citation recall, `85.18%` precision), and adding a discourse `Moderator` over a dynamic hierarchical mind map (`Co-STORM` Table 3 on `WildSeek`) lifts report **`Depth` (`3.77` vs. `3.41`)**, **`Relevance` (`3.78` vs. `3.56`)**, **`Novelty` (`3.05` vs. `2.89`)**, and **`# Unique URLs` per QA turn (`6.04` vs. `5.67`)**. Yet Wikipedia-editor audits show `STORM`'s ~`15%` unsupported claims stem from **web source-bias transfer** and **cross-passage associative hallucination**, not missing search tools. On long-horizon benchmarks, shallow search wrappers collapse and become overconfident (`BrowseComp`, `1,266` tasks: `GPT-4o` `0.6%`, `GPT-4o w/ browsing` `1.9%` with `82%` calibration error, `OpenAI Deep Research` `51.5%` with `91%` calibration error), while `DeepResearch Bench` (`100` tasks, `22` fields) separates prose depth (`RACE`) from citation accuracy (`FACT`: e.g., `Perplexity-Sonar-Reasoning-Pro(high)` scores `40.22` `RACE` but only `39.36%` citation accuracy).
3. **Instruction-Tuned LLMs Exhibit Asymmetric Context Gullibility, Dual-Passage Confirmation Bias, Prompt-Order Recency Collapse, and Conflict-Prompt Sycophancy (`ClashEval`, `ConflictBank`, `FaithEval`):** On single-document parametric-vs.-contextual clash (`ClashEval`, `1,294` prose / `1,278` Table 1 questions), frontier models override their own *correct* prior for corrupted context `31.3%–62.6%` of the time (`Context Bias`: `Claude 3 Opus` `31.3%`, `Claude 3.5 Sonnet` `40.1%`, `Gemini 1.5 Flash` `49.0%`, `Llama-3-8B` `52.9%`, `GPT-4o` `60.8%`, `GPT-3.5-Turbo` `62.6%`; `Prior Bias` `4.1%–7.4%`), yet on strongly entrenched ARC science facts (`FaithEval` `Counterfactual Context`), `GPT-4o` plunges from `96.3%` closed-book (`Rank #1`) to `47.5%` faithfulness (`Rank #17/18`). Across `7,453,853` claim–evidence pairs (`ConflictBank`), models follow a single conflicting passage (`MR < 50%`), flip to **parametric confirmation bias (`MR = 60.21%–77.27%`)** when *both* supporting and conflicting passages appear, and favor the **later-positioned** passage (`77.52%`, rising to `82.08%` under internal parametric conflict) unless explicit temporal/semantic scope qualifiers are added (`Llama-3-70B` `MR: 67.52% -> 8.31%`). On multi-document contradiction (`FaithEval` `Inconsistent Context`, `1.5k` pairs across 10 datasets), open-weight and smaller models silently pick one passage (`Phi-3-mini` `0.0%–8.7%` AVG `2.6%`, `GPT-3.5-Turbo` `4.0%–32.0%` AVG `14.4%`, `Gemma-2-27B` `7.3%–62.0%` AVG `24.9%`, `Llama-3.1-8B` `8.7%–58.7%` AVG `37.2%` vs. `Claude 3.5 Sonnet` `80.0%–99.3%` AVG `94.4%` and `gpt-4-turbo`/`gpt-4o` mean `89.35%`, excluding `gpt-4o-mini` `57.0%`), yet injecting a static `"+conflict"` warning degrades consistent-context accuracy across **`8/10` datasets** on both `Claude 3.5 Sonnet` (`-5 pp`) and `GPT-4o` (`-2 pp`) via sycophantic false positives (`FaithEval` Table 1).
4. **Human Scientific Literature Suffers `70%–90%` Secondary Citation Copying, `25.4%` Quotation Error, and `777×` Review-Lens Distortion ("Woozle Effect"):** Zipf/Yule–Simon misprint-propagation modeling across 12 classic physics papers (`Simkin & Roychowdhury 2003, 2005`, e.g., Kosterlitz & Thouless 1973 `N = 4,301`, `T = 196` misprints, `D = 45` distinct, exact `Eq. 16`/`Eq. 20` `R = 0.1625`, pooled Bayesian median `R = 18%`, KS universal `R = 0.2` $p = 91\%$) shows **`~70%–90%` of scientific citations are copied from secondary lists without reading the primary source**. Manual audits establish **`25.4%` (`95% CI [19.5%, 32.4%]`, $k = 28$, $I^2 = 97\%$)** total quotation error in medicine—**`11.9%` major** and **`11.5%` minor** ($k = 27$ each, `Jergas & Baethge 2015`)—and **`24.2%`** inappropriate citations in marine biology (`Todd et al. 2010`). In biomedical claim networks (`Greenberg 2009`, `242` papers, `675` citations, `220,553` supportive vs. `28` critical paths), **citation bias** (`94%` vs. `6%` primary-data citations, $p = 0.01$), **citation diversion** (`3` erroneous edges spawning `7,848` false paths), **review-lens hubs** (`4` zero-data reviews carrying `95%` of all paths, `777×` growth), **citation transmutation** (`24.3%` dead-end citations), and **back door invention** (laundering unrefereed abstracts) construct consensus from refuted claims, echoed by `Rekdal (2014)`'s **"whisper game"** academic urban legends.
5. **Pinned Deep-Research Evaluation Harnesses Contain Silent Scoring Defects That Mask Attribution and Grading Errors (`Probe 3A`):** Direct source audit uncovers three concrete upstream bugs: (a) `openai/simple-evals` `browsecomp_eval.py:92-109` returns `match.group(0)` (`"correct: yes"` / `"correct: no"`) instead of `match.group(1)`, making **both** `is_correct` and `is_incorrect` evaluate to `False` on 100% of validly formatted grader responses (and lacking `re.IGNORECASE`); (b) `AkariAsai/ScholarBench` `scripts/citation_correctness_eval.py:384-468` skips sentences `< 50` chars, truncates `[0, 1, 2]` to `[0]` via `re.findall(r"\[\d+", sent)`, bleeds `previous_citations` onto uncited sentences, and overwrites `sent_total = total_sents` inside the item loop; and (c) `Ayanami0730/deep_research_bench` `utils/stat.py:21-40` excludes uncited articles, `validate_error != None`, and `'unknown'` (including `"scrape failed: ..."` dead URLs), silently excluding **`1,872 / 4,679` (`40.01%`)** of deduplicated citations in committed `results/fact/claude-3-7-sonnet-latest/validated.jsonl` and inflating `valid_rate` from **`66.31%`** (scrape-inclusive, excluding `983` judge HTTP `429`/`400` API errors, **`-21.00 pp` exact / `-21.01 pp` rounded**) and **`52.38%`** (all-exclusions lower bound, **`-34.93 pp`**) to **`87.32%`**.

## Findings

### 1. Literature-Synthesis Agent Architectures, Attribution Loops, and Evaluation Harness Defects

#### 1.1 `PaperQA2`: Decoupled Search, `RCS` Comprehension Cliff, Retraction Gates, and `ContraCrow`

`Basis: documentation + source + derived`

1. **Agentic Tool Decomposition and Pinned Source (`arXiv:2409.13740v2` §§2–3; `Future-House/paper-qa@c692d0dfeac4a0296599f7fe21a253a58b7ac12a`):**
   - Unlike fixed linear RAG pipelines (`search -> retrieve -> answer`), `PaperQA2` exposes stateful tools over `EnvironmentState` (`src/paperqa/agents/tools.py:47-93`, tracking `Status: Paper Count=(\d+) | Relevant Papers=(\d+) | Current Evidence=(\d+)` at `L65`):
     - `PaperSearch` (`paper_search`, `tools.py:109-211`, `CONCURRENCY_SAFE = True`): converts agent-chosen keywords and optional `[min_year, max_year]` bounds into search queries, parses full-text PDFs (via `Grobid` in the paper; modular readers in the repo), chunks sections/tables, and indexes embeddings via `docs.aadd_texts`.
     - `GatherEvidence` (`gather_evidence`, `tools.py:217-312` -> `Docs.aget_evidence`, `src/paperqa/docs.py:492-586`): retrieves dense top-$k$ chunks (`evidence_k = 30` default in the 2024 paper §3 vs. **`AnswerSettings.evidence_k = 10`** and **`answer_max_sources = 5`** in pinned `src/paperqa/settings.py:98-129`), invokes `summary_llm_model` with `summary_json_system_prompt` (`src/paperqa/prompts.py:108-119`) for **Ranking and Contextual Summarization (`RCS`)** emitting `{"summary": "...", "relevance_score": 0-10}`, and enriches metadata with journal quality (`src/paperqa/clients/journal_quality.py`) plus Crossref/RetractionWatch DOI checks (`RetractionDataPostProcessor._process`, `src/paperqa/clients/retractions.py:75-84`, setting `is_retracted=query.doi in self.doi_set`).
     - `GenerateAnswer` (`gen_answer`, `tools.py:314-386` -> `Docs.aquery`, `docs.py:588-721`): constrains inline references to `pqac-*` context keys (`CITATION_KEY_CONSTRAINTS`, **`src/paperqa/prompts.py:38-50`**; `answer_iteration_prompt_template`, `prompts.py:30-36`, barring reuse of prior-draft keys absent from current context) or outputs `CANNOT_ANSWER_PHRASE = "I cannot answer"` (`prompts.py:28`).
     - `Reset` (`tools.py:389-403`), `Complete` (`tools.py:405-441`, `has_successful_answer: bool`), and optional `ClinicalTrialsSearch` (`tools.py:443-688`; `DEFAULT_TOOL_NAMES` at `tools.py:702-712`). In `arXiv:2409.13740v2` §3, `PaperQA2` also used a `Citation Traversal` tool (`0.46 ± 0.02` traversals and `1.26 ± 0.07` searches per `LitQA2` question), lifting DOI recall across all stages ($t(3) = 3.4, p = 0.022$).
2. **Quantitative Ablations and the `RCS` Model-Capability Cliff (`arXiv:2409.13740v2` §§2–5, §8.4):**
   - **Superhuman `LitQA2` Precision (`N = 248` body-only multiple-choice items with abstention):** `PaperQA2` (`gpt-4-turbo-2024-04-09`, `14.5 ± 0.6` papers parsed/question) achieves **`85.2% ± 1.1%` precision** and **`66.0% ± 1.2%` accuracy** (`21.9% ± 0.9%` abstention), exceeding incentivized PhD researchers given one week and institutional library access in precision (**`73.8% ± 9.6%`**, $n = 9$, $t(8.6) = 3.49, p = 0.0036$) while matching human accuracy (`67.7% ± 11.9%`, $t(8.5) = -0.42, p = 0.66$). Removing agentic iteration (`No Agent`) drops accuracy ($t(3.7) = 3.41, p = 0.015$).
   - **`RCS` Comprehension Threshold:** Frontier `GPT-4-Turbo` `RCS` sharply beats raw chunk insertion (`No RCS Model`: $t(3.92) = 9.29, p < 0.001$; `RCS` depth `1 -> 10`: $t(2.15) = 5.44, p = 0.014$, plateauing `10 -> 30`). Conversely, **running `RCS` with weaker models (`GPT-3.5-Turbo` or `Llama-3-70B`) lowers accuracy *below* using no `RCS` step at all**, proving contextual compression becomes a lossy bottleneck below a domain-comprehension threshold (illustrated analytically in `Probe 3B.1`).
   - **`WikiCrow` (`N = 240` gene articles, `375` blinded expert-graded statements, `$4.48 ± $1.02`/article):** Cuts "cited and unsupported" statements nearly in half vs. human Wikipedia (**`13.5%` vs. `24.9%`**, $\chi^2(1), p = 0.0075$) and uncited omissions `3.9×` (**`3.5%` vs. `13.6%`**, $p < 0.001$). Error decomposition shows significantly fewer reasoning/extrapolation errors (`12` vs. `26`, $p = 0.0144$) but **statistically indistinguishable attribution errors (`10` vs. `16`, $p = 0.21$)**.
   - **`ContraCrow` Literature-Scale Contradiction Detection:** Extracts section-bounded claims (`<= 5,000` chars, quality `>= 8/10`) and queries `PaperQA2` (`Claude 3.5 Sonnet`, `T = 0`, chunk `7,000` chars, overlap `250`) on a `0–10` Likert contradiction scale (threshold `>= 8`). On `ContraDetect`, `ContraCrow` attains **`ROC-AUC = 0.842`**, **`73%` accuracy**, **`88%` precision**, **`7%` FPR**, and **`98%` specificity** on `42` no-evidence claims (neutral score `5`). Across `93` biology papers (`35.16 ± 21.72` claims/paper; printed total `3,180` claims analyzed—preserving the paper's minor discrepancy against `93 × 35.16 ≈ 3,270`), `6.85%` score `>= 8` (`2.89%` score `8`, `3.77%` score `9`, `0.19%` score `10`), yielding **`2.34 ± 1.99` detected contradictions/paper**; `5` domain experts validate **`70%`** of `100` sampled detections, giving an empirical lower bound of **`1.64` contradictions/paper** spanning both direct opposite findings (`LEF1` in colorectal carcinoma) and temporal scientific updates (`GBP` localization).

#### 1.2 `OpenScholar` & `ScholarQABench`: Open-Access Datastore, Iterative Feedback, and Post-Hoc Attribution

1. **Architecture and Pinned Source (`arXiv:2411.14199v1`; *Nature* **650**(8103):857–863, 2026; `AkariAsai/OpenScholar@0e9b8fb912273d3dae39e593da86e4f6d3bf8de1`):**
   - **Datastore (`OpenScholar-DataStore` / `OSDS`):** Indexes **`45 million`** open-access papers (`peS2o v3`, **`~236M` passages** in *Nature* 2026; preprint `arXiv:2411.14199v1` internally prints `237M` in §1, `240M` in Fig. 2, and `234M` in §2.1; benchmark evaluation frozen on `peS2o v2` through Jan 2023), paired with domain-trained `Contriever` bi-encoders and cross-encoder rerankers (`OpenScholar.reranking_passages_cross_encoder`, `src/open_scholar.py:120-141`), optionally augmented with Semantic Scholar API and web search (`src/use_search_apis.py`). In the pinned repo, `src/open_scholar.py:658` comments out `retrieve_pes2o_passages`, so feedback retrieval executes via `self.ss_retriever` (Semantic Scholar API).
   - **Iterative Self-Feedback (`src/open_scholar.py:257-364, 639-701`):** After initial `0`-indexed attributed drafting (`generate_response`, `L173-254`, references `[0], [1], ...` at `L193, L535` and `src/instructions.py:325`), `get_feedback` (`L262-292`) generates up to `3` `(Feedback, Question)` pairs (`process_feedback`, `L257-260`, regex `r'Feedback: (.*?)(?:Question: (.*?))?\n'`).    - **Post-Hoc Attribution (`src/open_scholar.py:527-604, 702-706`; `src/instructions.py:239-301`):** `insert_attributions_posthoc_paragraph_all` (`L527-604`) splits drafts into statements (`>= 10` chars) and attaches `[k]` indices from `final_passages` to citation-worthy supported claims while leaving unsupported sentences unmodified (`instructions.py:241-243`), paired with `posthoc_attribution_with_citations` (`instructions.py:282-301`).

#### 3.2 `Greenberg (2009)`: Network Topology of Unfounded Authority (*BMJ* **339**:b2680, `PMC2714656`, `PMID 19622839`)

  2. **Citation Diversion:** `3` papers misquoted a refuting study (`0` $\beta$-APP in `3/5` vacuolated biopsies) as supportive, branching into **`7,848` false-claim paths** over 10 years.
  3. **Zero-Data "Lens" Review Hubs:** **`63%` (`139,391`)** of all paths ran through a single review paper, **`95%`** through `4` zero-data reviews from one group (vs. `8%` for `4` random papers), and **`97%`** through `8` hubs (`7` from the same group).
