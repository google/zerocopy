# Early Overlap and Higher-Order Conflict Detection Signals, Speculative Merge Cascades, Nogood Sharing, and Dependency-Addressed Shared Findings Ledgers

## Summary

Primary-source inspection of 25 peer-reviewed software-engineering, satisfiability, and agent-systems studies (2006–2026), line-level source audits of 7 immutable GitHub repository revisions (`brunyuriy/crystalvc`, `whystar/MSR2018-DupPR`, `luyaor/INTRUDE`, `OpenBMB/ChatDev` `chatdev1.0` and `2.0`, `zilliztech/GPTCache`, `vcache-project/vCache`), and a five-probe deterministic verification suite (`support/verify_overlap_signals_findings_ledger_probes.py`) establish five architectural invariants for early cross-agent overlap detection and shared findings ledgers over mutable repositories:

1. **VCS Textual Merge Misses `100%` of Cross-File Signature Breakage and `33.3%` of Real Integration Conflicts (`9.3%` of Clean Textual Merges):** Standard check-in merge gates achieve **`0 / 14` (`0.0%`) recall** on cross-file signature conflicts in Java (`Palantír`, `Sarma, Redmiles, & van der Hoek 2012`), whereas continuous workspace diff + signature impact propagation reaches **`14 / 14` (`100%`)** detection and resolution (`37 / 39` early direct-conflict resolution in text tasks, cutting completion time `26.4%`, `9:12` vs. `12:30` min). Across `1,694` automated build/test merges in `Git`, `Perl5`, and `Voldemort` (`Crystal`, `Brun et al. 2011/2013`; `3,562` merges across all `9` repositories), **`133 / 399` (`33.33%`) of all integration conflicts—and `133 / 1,428` (`9.31%`) of textually clean `git merge`s—are higher-order `BUILD⚡` (`24`, `1.42%`) or `TEST⚡` (`109`, `6.43%`) failures** that linger **`9.8` days (`23.2` changesets; median `1.6` days / `3` changesets per developer)** before discovery. Because `brunyuriy/crystalvc@2275e698` restricts `merge --noninteractive` to Mercurial and relies on brittle Git stdout substrings (`"Auto-merging"`, `"Merge made by recursive."`) that fail on disjoint-file and modern `ort` merges (`Probe 3A`), agent brokers should probe textual merges in memory via `git merge-tree --write-tree` (`Git >= 2.38`) before materializing isolated build/test sandboxes.
2. **Historical Logical Co-Change (`FCT`) Socio-Technical Congruence (`STC`) Captures Cross-Language Coupling Missed by Syntactic Call Graphs:** Under `Cataldo & Herbsleb (2013)`, required worker-to-worker coordination $C_R = T_A \, T_D \, T_A^\top \in \mathbb{N}_0^{W \times W}$ compared against actual interaction counts $C_A \in \mathbb{N}_0^{W \times W}$ (binarized via $C_A[i, j] > 0$) shows that defining artifact coupling $T_D$ via historical modification-request co-change (`FCT`) cuts resolution time by **`32%`** (`Cataldo et al. 2008`) and slashes post-release defect odds (`OR = 0.281` structural, `0.209` MR-comment, `0.271` IRC congruence in Project A; `OR = 1.967` and `1.801` per log unit of unsatisfied coordination load), whereas syntactic C/Java call graphs (`CGRAPH`) miss cross-language protobuf, SQL, Python, and TOML invariants (`0 / 4` vs. `6 / 6` in `Probe 1`'s controlled structural counterexample). Because `> 30%` of monthly coordination edges are newly formed (`Cataldo & Herbsleb 2013`), $T_D$ must refresh dynamically from commit history.
3. **Patch/File/Hunk Signals Dominate Text-Only Duplicate Detection, and a 4-Tier Cascade Bounds Pairwise Cost:** On duplicate pull requests (`MSR2018-DupPR`, `2,323` `MSR 2018` pairs refined in `TSE 2022` to `1,751` tuples / `2,032` clique pairs), natural-language title+description similarity achieves only **`54.8%` `recall-rate@20`** (`Li et al. JCST 2021`) because independent contributors describe symptoms vs. mechanisms and touch cold files (`Li et al. TSE 2022`), whereas diff-code similarity alone reaches **`78.2%`** (`Sim_file = 61.0%`) and combined text+file+code reaches **`83.4%` `recall-rate@20`**. Conversely, on cross-fork commit/PR filtering (`INTRUDE`, `Ren et al. SANER 2019`), 9-feature `AdaBoost` prioritizes **`57%–83%` maintainer precision at conservative `10%–22%` recall** (`83%` precision at `11%` recall at default threshold `0.6175`, saving `1.9–3.0` commits per pull request), using a ternary issue-reference polarity feature (`check_pattern` $\in \{-1, 0, +1\}$) to veto adjacent shared-registry edits for disjoint issues—provided four audited regex/type bugs in `luyaor/INTRUDE@87a69cbb` (`#42` 2-digit miss, `#42 + #105` subset `-1`, `HTTP 404` bare-number `+1`, and `comp.py:224`'s `int`-vs-`str` `ab_num` subtraction failure) are repaired (`Probe 2`). Routing active workstreams through a **4-Tier Monotone Cost/Precision Cascade** (`Tier 0` inverted path/symbol + `FCT` `STC` + hardened `check_pattern` $\to$ `Tier 1` `BM25F_ext` + ANN $\to$ `Tier 2` `INTRUDE` diff/hunk $\to$ `Tier 3` `STC`-gated `git merge-tree --write-tree` + build/test) cuts committed-pair speculative runs by **`89.18%` (`11,280 → 1,221`)** and combined uncommitted+`STC` runs by **`93.94%` (`20,160 → 1,221`)** at $N = 64$ while retaining `99.63%` higher-order recall in `Probe 3B`.
4. **Cross-Agent Negative-Result Sharing Requires Both Low-`LBD` + AIMD Selectivity and Machine-Verified Witnesses:** Unfiltered clause/dead-end broadcasting degrades parallel solver throughput via propagation scan bloat; combining `Audemard & Simon (2009)`'s **Literal Block Distance (`LBD <= 2` "glue" nogoods)** with `Hamadi, Jabbour, & Sais (IJCAI 2009)`'s pairwise **AIMD size control** ($e_{j \to i}^{k+1} = e_{j \to i}^k + b / e_{j \to i}^k$ with **$b = 8$** under starvation $|R_i^k| < T$, and $e_{j \to i}^{k+1} = e_{j \to i}^k - a \cdot e_{j \to i}^k$ with **$a = 0.125$** under congestion $|R_i^k| > T$) minimizes literal scan cost (`13,655` BCP scans and `126` imported literals vs. `13,837` / `366` for `AIMD-Size-Only`, `13,948` / `804` for `Share-All`, and `22,300` for `No-Share` in `Probe 4A`, while actively throttling `48` width-8 `LBD = 2` fanout clauses in `Probe 4C`). Unlike deductively sound SAT workers, LLM workers suffer transient/hallucinated failures: accepting **`1` unverified peer nogood** corrupts a satisfiable repair search into `FALSE_UNSAT_CORRUPTION`, whereas gating authoritative nogood admission on a deterministic failure witness quarantines unverified claims as advisory hypotheses and recovers `VERIFIED_SAT_PATCH` (`Probe 4B`). Similarly, in multi-agent coding (`Qian et al. ACL 2024`, `ChatDev` `0.4267 → 0.7304`), `reap_zombie()` dead-end stripping and `valueGain >= 0.9` filtering outperform append-only logs, whereas `ChatDev 2.0`'s `BlackboardMemory.retrieve()` ignores both `query` and `similarity_threshold` to return `self.contents[-top_k:]` (`0 / 3` relevant recall after 3 chatter messages in `Probe 5`).
5. **Pure Semantic Prompt Caches Are Unsound Over Mutable Repositories; Shared Findings Ledgers Must Separate Discovery from Merkle-Cone Validity:** Static-threshold semantic caches (`GPTCache`, `similarity_threshold = 0.8`) suffer both **Mode A static flag/toolchain aliasing** (`2 / 4` false hits at $\cos \in [0.951, 0.962]$ on `--no-default-features` vs. `--all-features` / `rustc-nightly`) and **Mode B `100.0%` post-edit staleness** when files mutate (`H_0 → H_1`). Per-prompt online-calibrated `vCache` (`Schroeder et al. ICLR 2026`, `vcache-project/vCache@50694eeb`) stores zero repository/file/flag digests (`embedding_metadata_obj.py:10–59`), burns **`1` insert + `4` mandatory `EXPLORE` LLM calls = `5` cold calls (`0%` hits across queries `1..5`)** due to `2` seeded priors (`len(obs) < 6`, `verified.py:552`), and fails in two distinct post-mutation regimes at $\cos = 1.000$ (`Probe 5`): (i) in uncalibrated separable `n = 6` state (`B1`, `var_t = 0.035445`), its 50-point `epsilon_grid` yields `tau(1.0) = 0.64878` on query 1 (`35.12%` immediate stale hit risk), and upon the first `EXPLORE`, `verified.py:418–433` appends `(1.0, 0)` (pushing the old entry's `tau(1.0)` to `0.98000`) **and** calls `self.cache.add(...)` without evicting the $H_0$ entry—creating duplicate identical vectors at $s = 1.0$ with undefined `get_knn(k=1)` tie-breaking; (ii) once low-variance calibrated at $H_0$ (`B2`, `var_t = 0.001818`), raw `tau(1.0) = -8.07358 < 0` yields $P(\text{EXPLORE}) = 0.0$ and **`100.0%` (`20 / 20`) permanent silent stale hits**. Decomposing ledger lookup into **Stage 1 semantic/structural candidate discovery** and **Stage 2 deterministic `(scope_key, dep_cone_blobs, verifier_status == VERIFIED)` validation** (`curr_oids.get(p) == recorded_oid`, handling both edits and file deletions cleanly) achieves **`0` Mode A false hits**, **`0` Mode B authoritative stale hits**, and graceful **`STALE_ADVISORY`** demotion on partial-cone drift.

#### 1.1 Direct `Severity` vs. Indirect Signature `Impact` (`Palantír`, `Sarma et al. 2012`)

Standard VCS workflows isolate uncommitted edits until branch push or PR submission, blinding peer workers to both concurrent same-file edits (**direct conflicts**, `DC`) and cross-file API/signature mutations (**indirect conflicts**, `IC`). In `Palantír` (*IEEE TSE* 38(4):889–908, DOI `10.1109/TSE.2011.64`), workspace wrappers emit lifecycle events (`Populated (ChangesInProgress (MetricsChanged | MetricsChanged Impacted)* ChangesCommitted)* UnPopulated`) every `5` seconds or on auto-save (`pp. 892–894`), pairing line-ordered non-comment diff severity
$$\text{Severity}(f) = \frac{|\Delta \text{LOC}_{\text{non-comment}}(f)|}{|\text{LOC}_{\text{non-comment}}(f)|}$$
(propagated upward to directories as a changed-child weighted average, `§5.5, p. 894`; implemented via `difflib.SequenceMatcher.get_opcodes()` in `Probe 1` so line reorderings register `Severity = 1.0000` rather than `0.0`) with incremental `Dependency Finder` signature checks that flag a binary `Impacted` alert whenever workspace $A$ alters or deletes a public signature referenced by workspace $B$ (`p. 894`).

#### 1.3 Socio-Technical Congruence (`STC`): Logical Co-Change vs. Syntactic Call Graphs

Given worker-to-artifact assignment $T_A \in \{0, 1\}^{W \times F}$ and symmetric artifact-to-artifact dependency $T_D \in \mathbb{N}_0^{F \times F}$, `Cataldo & Herbsleb (IEEE TSE 2013`, 39(3):343–360, DOI `10.1109/TSE.2012.32`, `§5, pp. 347–348`; building on `Cataldo et al. CSCW 2006` and `ESEM 2008`) define the **Coordination Requirements matrix** $C_R = T_A \, T_D \, T_A^\top \in \mathbb{N}_0^{W \times W}$ and compare it against observed worker-to-worker interaction counts $C_A \in \mathbb{N}_0^{W \times W}$ (MR comments, IRC messages, or structural/geographical links, binarized by $C_A[i, j] > 0$):
$$\text{STC}(C_R, C_A) = \frac{\left|\{(i, j) : i \neq j \;\land\; C_R[i, j] > 0 \;\land\; C_A[i, j] > 0\}\right|}{\left|\{(i, j) : i \neq j \;\land\; C_R[i, j] > 0\}\right|}, \qquad \text{Gaps} = 1 - \text{STC}(C_R, C_A).$$

- **Logical co-change (`FCT`) outperforms static syntactic call graphs (`CGRAPH`):** Populating $T_D[f_1, f_2]$ from historical modification requests (`FCT`, files committed together) captures cross-language wire schemas, SQL migrations, feature-flag configs, and implicit invariants invisible to single-language AST call graphs (`Cataldo et al. ESEM 2008, pp. 6–9`), cutting MR resolution time by **`32%`** under full four-channel congruence (`CSCW 2006 Table 2, p. 358`: Adjusted $R^2$ rises `0.787 → 0.872` on `N = 809` and `0.756 → 0.854` on `N = 1,983` MRs).
- **Industrial defect and productivity odds ratios (`Cataldo & Herbsleb 2013, Tables 2–4, pp. 349–353`):** Across **Project A** (`3,980` C/Java files, `8,257` MRs / `2,375` multiteam) and **Project B** (`7M` LOC automotive C, `9,074` files, `380` developers / `37` teams, `4,170` resolved MRs / `2,480` in `Table 4`), unsatisfied `Coordination Req. Dep. (log)` inflates post-release file failure odds (**`OR = 1.967`** in `Table 2` / `1.962` in `Table 3` for Project A; **`OR = 1.801`** for Project B, $p < 0.01$), as does `Number Logical Dep. (log)` (**`OR = 2.109`** and **`3.830`**, $p < 0.01$). Conversely, congruence slashes defect odds—in Project A (`Table 3 Model II`, $\Delta\chi^2 = 196.24, p < 0.01$), `Structural Congruence` gives **`OR = 0.281`** (`0.285` in `Table 2`; `-18.6%` failure probability drop), `MR Congruence` **`OR = 0.209`**, and `IRC Congruence` **`OR = 0.271`**; in Project B (`Table 2 Model IV`, $\Delta\chi^2 = 309.07, p < 0.01$), `Structural Congruence` gives **`OR = 0.859`** (`-7.6%` drop) and `Geographical Congruence` **`OR = 0.578`**—while reducing resolution time (`Structural Congruence` $\beta = -0.041^*$ and $-0.059^*$, `Geographical` $\beta = -0.069^*$ and $-0.071^*$; Adjusted $R^2$ `0.656 → 0.697` and `0.462 → 0.527`). Because **`> 30%`** of peak-month coordination edges did not exist the prior month (`p. 353`), static agent partitioning decays without rolling co-change updates.

   - **Precision vs. recall trade-off (must not be conflated with `Li 2021`'s `recall-rate@20`):** `INTRUDE` optimizes low-false-alarm maintainer triage over `AdaBoost` score thresholds `0.5925–0.62`, achieving **`57%–83%` precision at a conservative `10%–22%` recall** (**`83%` precision at `11%` recall** at default threshold `0.6175`, `Ren et al. 2019 §IV, p. 237`), detecting redundant work early enough to save **`1.9` to `3.0` commits per pull request** on average (`pp. 230, 237`).
   - **Audited 9-scalar `sim_to_vet` vector and four `check_pattern` bugs (`comp.py:30–159, 223–341`, `clf.py:30–87, 269`, `git.py:38–57`; `Probe 2`):** `INTRUDE` feeds `AdaBoostClassifier(n_estimators=200, learning_rate=0.1)` (`clf.py:30, 269`) 9 features: (1–2) `title` and `body[:2000]` `LSI`/`TF-IDF` cosines; (3–4) net-added diff-token cosines ($\max(0, c_{\text{add}}(t) - c_{\text{del}}(t))$, `comp.py:87–99`) globally and restricted to overlapping files $F_A \cap F_B$ (`get_pull_on_overlap`, `comp.py:82–85`); (5–6) file-list multiset Jaccard (`comp.py:30–38`) and $|F_A \cap F_B|$; (7–8) added-hunk (`comp.py:67–72`) closed-interval `cross` line-overlap ratio (`comp.py:103–159`) globally and on $F_A \cap F_B$; and (9) ternary `check_pattern` $\in \{-1, 0, +1\}$ (`comp.py:223–240`). Source audit and `Probe 2` uncover **four concrete implementation bugs** in `comp.py:223–240` & `git.py:38–57`:
     1. *2-digit issue miss (`0` instead of `+1`):* `+1` calls `get_numbers` (`len(x) >= 3`), missing shared 1–2 digit issues (`Fixes #42` vs. `Resolves #42` $\to 0$).
     2. *2-digit subset inversion (`-1` instead of `+1`):* `-1` calls `get_pr_and_issue_numbers` (`len(x) > 0`) and tests `a_res != b_res`, returning **`-1`** when PR $A$ cites `#42` and `#105` while PR $B$ cites `#42`.

On `Probe 2`'s 20-pair benchmark, text-only `BM25F_ext` (unigram `avg_u` + bigram `avg_b`) recalls `0 / 8` cold symptom-vs-mechanism duplicates (`42.86%` overall), whereas both the `[linear feature-ablation discriminator]` and out-of-sample 24-stump Real `AdaBoost` classifier (trained on a disjoint 30-pair synthetic split) achieve **`100.0%` (`14 / 14`) recall and `0.0%` (`0 / 6`) shared-registry false positives**—spiking to **`100.0%` (`6 / 6`) false positives** when `check_pattern` is ablated.

#### 2.2 Pre-Edit Duplicate Bug Retrieval (`Runeson 2007`, `Sun et al. 2011 REP`) and the 4-Tier Cascade

Across Eclipse, Mozilla, and OpenOffice (`Tables V–VI & Figs. 3–4, pp. 258–260`), **text-only `BM25F_ext` improves over standard `BM25F` by `3%–13%` relative `Recall@k` ($k = 1 \dots 20$) and `4.20%–10.68%` relative `MAP`**, while full 7-feature **`REP` improves over the authors' ASE 2010 discriminative SVM baseline by `10%–27%` relative `Recall@k` and `17%–23%` (`17.20%–22.99%`) relative `MAP`**.

### 3. Nogood Sharing, Semantic Caching Failure Regimes, and Dependency-Addressed Ledgers

`Basis: documentation + source + execution + derived`

#### 3.1 Parallel CDCL `LBD` + `AIMD` Nogood Sharing and Multi-Agent Experience Distillation

1. **`ManySAT` AIMD Throughput Control (`Hamadi, Jabbour, & Sais, JSAT 2009`, 6(4):245–262, DOI `10.3233/SAT190070`; *IJCAI 2009*, pp. 499–504) and `Glucose` `LBD` (`Audemard & Simon, IJCAI 2009`, pp. 399–404):**
   - Broadcasting all learned conflict clauses across parallel workers degrades Boolean Constraint Propagation (`BCP`) via watched-literal scan overhead. `ManySAT` first bounded static exports to `|C| <= 8` (`JSAT 2009`), then introduced pairwise **`aimdT` throughput control** (`IJCAI 2009, pp. 500–502`, window $\alpha = 10,000$ conflicts, target $T = \alpha / 2 = 5,000$, initial $e^0 = 8$):
     $$e_{j \to i}^{k+1} = \begin{cases} e_{j \to i}^k + \dfrac{b}{e_{j \to i}^k} & \text{if } |R_i^k| < T \quad (\text{starvation; } b = 8), \\[8pt] e_{j \to i}^k - a \cdot e_{j \to i}^k & \text{if } |R_i^k| > T \quad (\text{congestion; } a = 0.125). \end{cases}$$
   - `Audemard & Simon (IJCAI 2009)` proved that **Literal Block Distance `LBD(C)`**—the count of distinct decision levels in $C$—identifies **`LBD <= 2` "glue" clauses** that bind deep conflict subspaces to a single prior decision level. For agent fleets, **cross-agent nogood utility is inversely proportional to local assumption depth (`LBD`)**, **provided** exported nogoods carry a machine-verified failure witness (`Probe 4`):
     - On `Probe 4A`'s 121-variable, 286-clause UNSAT compatibility check across 4 portfolio CDCL workers, **`Glue-AIMD` (`LBD <= 2` + `|C| <= e_lim`) achieves the strictly lowest `13,655` BCP literal scans and `126` imported literals** (`76` clauses), beating `AIMD-Size-Only` (`13,837` scans, `366` literals, `30` clauses rejected by `|C| > e_lim`), `Share-All` (`13,948` scans, `804` literals, `6.38×` higher volume), and `No-Share` (`22,300` scans). On `Probe 4C`'s wide-glue fanout CNF (`144` width-8 `LBD <= 2` candidates under congestion target $T = 1$), `e_lim` contracts to `3.1977` and **actively filters `48` `LBD <= 2` clauses with `|C| > e_lim`** alongside `264` accepted glue imports.
     - On `Probe 4B`'s satisfiable repair instance, injecting **`1` unverified peer unit nogood (`(-1,)`)** from a transient worker fault drives all 4 workers into **`FALSE_UNSAT_CORRUPTION`**, whereas requiring `verifier_status == VERIFIED` quarantines the `3` peer broadcasts as advisory hypotheses and finds **`VERIFIED_SAT_PATCH`** in `2` rounds.
2. **Multi-Agent Coding Experience Compaction vs. Query-Blind FIFO (`ExpeL`, `Zhao et al. AAAI 2024`, `arXiv:2308.10144v3`, DOI `10.1609/aaai.v38i17.29936`; `Qian et al. ACL 2024`, pp. 5628–5640, `arXiv:2312.17025v3`, DOI `10.18653/v1/2024.acl-long.305`; `OpenBMB/ChatDev`):**
   - Contrastive failure-to-success pairing lifts `ExpeL` HotpotQA validation (`mean ± SE` across 4 folds) from `28.0% ± 1.4%` (`ReAct`) and `29.0% ± 0.4%` (insights with uncompacted reflections) to **`39.0% ± 1.7%`**. In `Qian et al. (ACL 2024, Tables 1–2)`, `Experiential Co-Learning` lifts `ChatDev` comprehensive quality from **`0.4267`** (`148.22 s`) to **`0.7304`** (`122.78 s`, `2.31` nodes / `3.01` edges); ablating Assistant coding memory (`(537, 0)`) drops quality **`-27.4%` (`0.7304 → 0.5305`, `4.44` nodes / `5.04` edges)** vs. `0.6840` when ablating Instructor memory (`(0, 537)`).
   - Direct source inspection confirms `OpenBMB/ChatDev@31fd994416` (`chatdev1.0`) strips dead-end loops via `reap_zombie()` (`ecl/experience.py:44–109`), zeroes buggy traces (`compile_weight = 0.0`), and filters `valueGain >= 0.9` (`ecl/post_process/memory_filter.py:4–35`, though `L34` mutates `content` in place and `ecl/memory.py:97–106` `_get_instructionstar` omits updating `max_valueGain`), while retrieving via `FAISS` `IndexFlatL2` (`ecl/memory.py:134–265`) with **zero commit or blob-hash invalidation**. In `OpenBMB/ChatDev@464a681ba7` (`ChatDev 2.0`, `runtime/node/agent/memory/blackboard_memory.py:19–75`), `BlackboardMemory.retrieve()` ignores `agent_role`, `query`, and `similarity_threshold` altogether and returns `list(self.contents[-top_k:])` (`0 / 3` relevant hits after 3 unrelated chatter posts in `Probe 5`).

#### 3.2 Audited Failure Modes of Semantic Prompt Caches (`GPTCache`, `MeanCache`, `vCache`)

1. **`GPTCache` (`Bang 2023` [ACL Anthology]; PDF header byline: `Bang Fu & Di Feng`, *NLP-OSS @ EMNLP 2023*, pp. 212–218, DOI `10.18653/v1/2023.nlposs-1.24`; `zilliztech/GPTCache@a74ac654473f7bf4109118e8576945161616e17a`) and `MeanCache` (`Gill et al. 2024`, `arXiv:2403.02694`):**

     - *Post-mutation Regime `B1` (`n = 6` separable history at $H_0$, `var_t = 0.035445`):* Because `epsilon_grid = linspace(1e-6, 1 - 1e-6, 50)` (`L468`) has `epsilon_grid[1] = 0.02041 > delta = 0.01`, $t'_0 = \text{clip}(\hat{t} + 4.7534\sqrt{\widehat{\text{Var}}(\hat{t})}, 0, 1) = 1.0$ yields **`tau(1.0) = 0.64878`** on the **first** query after `src/framing.rs` mutates ($H_0 \to H_1$, $\cos = 1.000$), risking a **`35.12%` immediate silent stale hit**. ## Boundaries

- **`unknown` — Speculative Build/Test Throughput at `N >= 50` High-Cadence Agents Without Shared Incremental Build Caches:** `Crystal` (`Brun et al. 2011`) and `WeCode` (`Guimarães & Silva 2012`) measured human commit cadences (`15–59` developers), and `Probe 3B` simulates Tier-0 `STC` pair-filtering algebraically (`89.18%` committed-pair reduction) rather than executing `1,221` full compiler/test sandboxes; scaling Tier-3 speculative builds above `50` rapid coding agents depends on pairing `git merge-tree --write-tree` with content-addressed action caching (`bazel-reapi-merkle-action-caches-symlink-sandboxes-and-content-defined-cas`).
