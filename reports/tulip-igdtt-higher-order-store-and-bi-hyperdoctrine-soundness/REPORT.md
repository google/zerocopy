# TULIP, iGDTT higher-order store semantics, and BI-hyperdoctrine weakest-precondition soundness

## Summary

TULIP (Frederik Lerbjerg Aagaard, Jonathan Sterling, and Lars Birkedal, *"A Denotationally-based Program Logic for Higher-order Store"*, MFPS XXXIX / ENTICS Vol. 3, Nov 2023, DOI `10.46298/entics.12232`, `arXiv:2308.02906v2`) is an intuitionistic guarded higher-order separation logic with partial weakest preconditions (`wp e {x. φ}`) built directly over the **denotational equational theory** of System $\mathbf{F}_\mu^{\mathit{ref}}$ in impredicative guarded dependent type theory (`iGDTT`, Sterling, Gratzer, and Birkedal, `arXiv:2210.02169`). Because programs denote into a world-indexed guarded state monad $\mathsf{T} A(w) = \forall_{w' \ge w} \mathsf{tH}_{w'} \to \mathsf{L}(\exists_{w'' \ge w'} \mathsf{tH}_{w''} \times A(w''))$ where heap cells store **delayed** values $\mathcal{H}(w^-, w^+) = \prod_{\ell \in |w^-|} \blacktriangleright[Z \leftarrow w^- \ell].\, Z(w^+)$, TULIP exhibits asymmetric step accounting: **`get` and `unfold` emit one $\vartheta$-step (`wp-get` strips one `▷`), whereas `set`, `new`, `ret`, `fold`, and pure $\beta/\eta$-reductions emit zero steps (`wp-set` and `wp-new` carry no `▷`)**, allowing pure and global-state equations (`set-after-new`, `set-after-set`, `get-after-get` commutativity, `set-after-get`) to rewrite anywhere inside `wp` and under $\lambda$-binders without head-redex focusing (`PureExec`).

Audit and Lean 4 mechanization of TULIP (`arXiv:2308.02906v2`), `iGDTT` (`arXiv:2210.02169`), `jonsterling/coq-sgdt` (`3a69fbc09ef34ae00792e1fd8563d39a957baf7e`), and Polzer–Goncharov's local-store BI-hyperdoctrine (`arXiv:2003.05386`) establish five results:

1. **Heaplet world-variance defect in TULIP's published `wp` forcing clause—and complete mechanized repair (`Basis: source + derived + execution`):** In `arXiv:2308.02906v2` (`paper.tex:1855`), the Kripke–Joyal forcing clause for $w \Vdash (h \models \mathsf{wp}_A\, u\, \phi)$ quantifies over a returned local heap $h' : \mathsf{tH}_{w''} = \mathcal{H}(w'', w'')$ rather than a partial heap at the combined return world $w_r = w'' \cdot |h_f|$, and Lemma 4.22 (`paper.tex:2132`, echoed in Construction 4.13 at `lines 1555–1557`) writes an ill-typed backward pushforward $w''_* h_1$ along $w'' \le w$. Whenever a locally owned cell $\ell \in \|h\|$ holds or writes a reference $\ell_0 \in \|h_f\|$ belonging to the frame, $\mathsf{tH}_{w''}$ cannot represent $\ell_0 \notin |w''|$: in the step-indexed model, the published clause **refutes `wp-set`, `wp-get`, `wp-val`, and even heap-extension monotonicity of `wp`** (`published_wp_set_frame_ref_unsound`, `published_wp_get_frame_ref_unsound`, and `published_wp_not_heap_monotone` in `support/tulip_lean/TulipModel/WP.lean`). Replacing $h' : \mathsf{tH}_{w''}$ by **$h' : \mathsf{pH}\, w_r$ with $|h'| = w''$** (equivalently $\eta_{h'} : \mathcal{H}(w'', w_r)$, total output heap $(w_r)_* h_f \cdot_{w_r} h'$) restores heap/world monotonicity and validates **all** TULIP BI and `wp` rules (`wpRepaired`, `wp_val`, `wp_wand`, `wp_frame`, `wp_bind`, `wp_step`, `wp_get`, `wp_set`, `wp_new`, `wp_rec`, plus closed adequacy `wp_adequacy_runClosed`).
2. **Independence of `▷` well-pointedness from `2308.02906v2` §4.1.2 (`Basis: source + derived + execution`):** `paper.tex:1269–1271` claims well-pointedness $\blacktriangleright(\mathsf{next}_A) = \mathsf{next}_{\blacktriangleright A}$ (needed syntactically for `set-after-get`) is deducible from the delayed-substitution rules on `lines 1180–1264`. The binary-branching presheaf modality $(T X)_0 = 1$, $(T X)_{n+1} = (\mathsf{Bool} \to X_n)$ on $\mathbf{PSh}(\omega)$ satisfies every rule in `lines 1180–1284` (including `gfix`), yet refutes both well-pointedness and `set-after-get` (`support/WellPointedCountermodel.lean`), while all poset models $[\mathbb{O}^{\mathsf{op}}, \mathscr{S}]$ validate well-pointedness semantically.
3. **`jonsterling/coq-sgdt` (`3a69fbc0`) footprint and hidden `dlater` gap (`Basis: source + derived`):** `coq-sgdt` (Coq `8.15.2`) avoids `-impredicative-set` by placing `#[bypass_check(universes = yes)]` solely on `All` (`impredicative.v:6–8`), postulates `proof_irrelevance`, `functional_extensionality_dep`, `propositional_extensionality`, and 13 `Axiom`s in `guarded.v`, and constructs `StorePassingMonad.T` via a 3-step horizontal adjunction chain (`store.v:573–610`, fixing `2210.02169:1206`'s missing `▷` in `World.cat (▷ 𝒯)`). It omits `ret`/`bind`/`get`/`set`/`new`; moreover, `guarded.v:50–53`'s sole `dlater` axiom (`dlater_next_eq : ▷ A = dlater (next A)`) is strictly too weak to define `new` or covariant $\mathsf{pH}$ pushforwards over abstract cell types $A : \blacktriangleright \mathcal{T}$ without adding dependent `dlater` functoriality.
4. **Why Polzer–Goncharov local store breaks `wp` (`arXiv:2003.05386`, `Basis: source + derived`):** Supporting local allocation hiding (`new-discard`, `new-swap`) via Kammar–Levy–Moss–Staton's hiding monad $P$ forces assertions onto coarser partial initializations $\hat{\mathbf{E}}$ (`bi-hyper.tex:1452–1525`), destroying internal PCM structure on $\hat{P}\hat{H}$ and making frame completion to total heaps $H(w')$ **non-invariant under $\sim_{\hat{\mathbf{E}}}$-representatives** (`bi-hyper.tex:2192–2215`). TULIP's global-world poset $(\mathbb{W}, \le)$ sacrifices `new-discard` and `new-swap` (which also remain open in `iGDTTRefLRAT`, `2210.02169` §6.1) specifically to keep $\mathsf{pH}$ and $\mathsf{tH}$ unquotiented over one poset.
5. **Stratification, undecidable conversion, adequacy, and Lean 4 barriers (`Basis: source + derived + execution`):** In $[\mathbb{O}^{\mathsf{op}}, \mathscr{S}]$, assemblies ($\mathsf{Type}_i$), modest sets ($\mathsf{Set}$), and $\neg\neg$-closed propositions ($\mathsf{Prop}$) satisfy $\mathsf{Prop} \subseteq \mathsf{Set} : \mathsf{Type}_0$ with $\mathsf{Prop} \notin \mathsf{Set}$ ($\Omega_{\neg\neg} \cong \nabla 2$ is non-modest), avoiding Coquand's Girard paradox while forcing TULIP's two-sort stratification $\iota \in \{\boldsymbol{p}, \boldsymbol{\ell}\}$. Equality reflection (`paper.tex:521, 2209`) makes judgmental conversion undecidable; neither `2308.02906v2` nor `2210.02169` defines operational semantics or proves operational adequacy; and Lean 4's proof-irrelevant `Prop` + predicative `Type u` preclude direct `iGDTT` embedding while admitting a complete stratified step-indexed encoding (`support/tulip_lean/`).

## Findings

### 1. Realizability Model of `iGDTT`, Universe Stratification, and `jonsterling/coq-sgdt` (`3a69fbc0`)

#### 1.1 Assemblies, modest sets, $\neg\neg$-closed propositions, and TULIP's $\iota \in \{\boldsymbol{p}, \boldsymbol{\ell}\}$ split
`Basis: source + derived`

In `arXiv:2210.02169` §5.1–5.2 (`paper.tex:2370–2664`) and `arXiv:2308.02906v2` §4.1.4 (`paper.tex:1352–1399`), `iGDTT` is interpreted in internal presheaves $\mathscr{E} = [\mathbb{O}^{\mathsf{op}}, \mathscr{S}]$ over a realizability topos $\mathscr{S}$ (such as Hyland's effective topos $\mathbf{Eff}$) and an internal $\neg\neg$-separated intuitionistic well-founded poset $(\mathbb{O}, \le, \prec)$ ($\mathbb{O} = \omega$ in the standard model $[\omega^{\mathsf{op}}, \mathbf{Eff}]$):

- **$\mathsf{Type}_i$ ($\mathbb{V}_i$):** Hofmann–Streicher liftings of $\mathscr{U}_i$-small **assemblies** ($\neg\neg$-separated objects) in $\mathscr{S}$. Restricting $\mathsf{Type}_i$ to assemblies rather than all objects of $\mathscr{S}$ is required both so modest sets are *strongly* complete over $\mathsf{Type}_i$ (Hyland–Robinson–Rosolini 1990, `2210.02169:2468–2476`, interpreting $\forall x : A.\, B(x) : \mathsf{Set}$ for $A : \mathsf{Type}_i$) and so extensional equality $(a =_A b)$ is $\neg\neg$-closed and lands in $\mathsf{Prop}$ (`2210.02169:2437–2441`).
- **$\mathsf{Set}$ ($\mathbb{U} : \mathsf{Type}_0$) and $\mathsf{Prop}$ ($\mathbb{P} : \mathsf{Type}_0$):** $\mathsf{Set}$ lifts the internal category of **modest sets** (PERs), while $\mathsf{Prop}$ lifts the internal poset $\Omega_{\neg\neg}$ of **$\neg\neg$-closed propositions**. Every $P \in \Omega_{\neg\neg}$ is a subsingleton assembly and hence modest ($\mathsf{Prop} \subseteq \mathsf{Set}$), **but the classifier $\Omega_{\neg\neg} \cong \nabla 2$ itself is codiscrete and NOT modest** ($\mathsf{Prop} \notin \mathsf{Set}$, `Prop : Type_0`). Placing $\mathsf{Prop}$ and $\mathsf{Set}$ side-by-side inside $\mathsf{Type}_0$ avoids Coquand's (1986) Girard paradox (`2308.02906v2:181–185, 1140–1142`).
- **Why $\mathsf{Prop} \notin \mathsf{Set}$ forces TULIP's two-flag stratification (`2308.02906v2:178–222, 1456–1483, 1673–1681, 1721–1726`):** Worlds solve $\mathbb{W} = \mathbb{N} \rightharpoonup_{\mathrm{fin}} \blacktriangleright \mathcal{S}_{\boldsymbol{p}}$ in $\mathsf{Type}_0$. Program types ($\iota = \boldsymbol{p}$, $\mathcal{S}_{\boldsymbol{p}} = [\mathbb{W}, \mathsf{Set}]$) must land in impredicative $\mathsf{Set}$ so Kripke exponentials $(A \Rightarrow B)(w) = \forall_{w' \ge w} A(w') \to B(w')$, polymorphic $\forall \alpha : \mathcal{S}_{\boldsymbol{p}}.\, B(\alpha)$, and the world-passing state monad $\mathsf{T} A(w)$ stay $\mathsf{Set}$-small and storable in $\mathbb{W}$. Because $\mathtt{prop}$ denotes $\mathcal{B} = [\mathsf{pH}, \mathsf{Prop}_{\mathbb{W}}]$ and $\mathsf{Prop} \notin \mathsf{Set}$, $\mathtt{prop}$ and predicate spaces $A \to \mathtt{prop}$ cannot live in $\mathcal{S}_{\boldsymbol{p}}$ and must inhabit logical types ($\iota = \boldsymbol{\ell}$, $\mathcal{S}_{\boldsymbol{\ell}} = [\mathbb{W}, \mathsf{Type}_0]$).

### 2. Heaplet World-Variance Defect in TULIP's Published `wp` Forcing Clause and Mechanized Repair

#### 2.1 Diagnosis of ill-typedness and semantic refutation in `arXiv:2308.02906v2`
`Basis: source + derived + execution`

In `2308.02906v2/paper.tex`:
- **Heaplet distributor & partial/total heaps (`lines 1500–1574`):** $\mathcal{H}(w^-, w^+) \triangleq \prod_{\ell \in |w^-|} \blacktriangleright[Z \leftarrow w^- \ell].\, Z(w^+)$ is contravariant in $w^-$ and covariant in $w^+$. A partial heap $h : \mathsf{pH}\, w$ (`Def. 4.11`) pairs $\|h\| \subseteq_{\mathrm{fin}} |w|$ with $\eta_h : \mathcal{H}(|h|, w)$ ($|h| \triangleq w_{\|h\|} \le w$), evaluating cell contents at the **ambient world $w$** so cells in $\|h\|$ may hold references to frame locations in $|w| \setminus \|h\|$; thus $\mathsf{pH} : \mathbb{W} \to \mathsf{Set}$ is **covariant** ($w'_* h = (\|h\|, w'_* \eta_h)$ for $w \le w'$). A total heap $h : \mathsf{tH}_w$ (`Def. 4.14`) satisfies $|h| = w$, i.e. $\eta_h : \mathcal{H}(w, w)$. *(Note first an ill-typed pushforward in Construction 4.13, `paper.tex:1555–1557`: $\eta_{h_1 \cdot_w h_2}\ell$ writes $|h_1 \cdot_w h_2|_* \eta_{h_1}\ell$ along $|h_1 \cdot_w h_2| \le w$, which goes backwards against covariance and should simply be $\eta_{h_1}\ell$ and $\eta_{h_2}\ell$.)*
- **Published forcing clause (`paper.tex:1850–1860`) and `lem:soundness-wp-get` (`line 2132`):**
  $$\begin{aligned}
  w \Vdash (h \models \mathsf{wp}_A\, u\, \phi) \;\Longleftrightarrow\;
  &\forall (w' \ge w)\, (h_f : \mathsf{pH}\, w')\, (h_t : \mathsf{tH}_{w'})\, (h_t = h_f \cdot w'_* h).\, u\, w'\, h_t \Downarrow \lambda p. \\
  &\exists (w'' \ge |h|)\, (w_r = w'' \cdot |h_f|)\, \mathbf{(h' : \mathsf{tH}_{w''})}\, (a : A\, w_r). \\
  &\quad p = \mathsf{pack}(w_r,\, (w_r)_* h_f \cdot \mathbf{(w_r)_* h'},\, a) \;\land\; w_r \Vdash (\mathbf{(w_r)_* h'} \models \phi_{w_r}\, a = \top).
  \end{aligned}$$
  1. **Ill-typedness in `paper.tex:2132`:** In `lem:soundness-wp-get`, $h_1, h_2 : \mathsf{pH}\, w$ and $w'' \triangleq |h_1| \cdot |h_2| \le w$. Writing $h' \triangleq w''_* h_1 \cdot w''_* h_2 : \mathsf{tH}_{w''}$ applies covariant pushforward $w''_*$ backwards along $w'' \le w$.
  2. **Semantic refutation of `wp-set`, `wp-get`, `wp-val`, and heap monotonicity (`support/tulip_lean/TulipModel/WP.lean:216–560`):** Let $w = \{\ell_{\mathrm{frame}} \mapsto \mathsf{next}(\mathbb{N}),\, \ell_{\mathrm{local}} \mapsto \mathsf{next}(\mathsf{ref}\, \mathbb{N})\}$, local heap $h = \{\ell_{\mathrm{local}} \mapsto \mathsf{next}(\ell_{\mathrm{frame}})\} : \mathsf{pH}\, w$, and frame $h_f = \{\ell_{\mathrm{frame}} \mapsto \mathsf{next}(42)\} : \mathsf{pH}\, w$. Neither `set l_local l_frame`, `get l_local`, nor `ret ()` allocates, so $w_r = w = w'' \cdot |h_f|$ with $|h_f| = \{\ell_{\mathrm{frame}}\}$, forcing $\ell_{\mathrm{frame}} \notin |w''|$! Because $\mathsf{ref}\, \mathbb{N}\, w'' = \{i \in |w''| \mid w''(i) = \mathsf{next}(\mathbb{N})\} = \emptyset$, **no $h' : \mathsf{tH}_{w''} = \mathcal{H}(w'', w'')$ can hold $\ell_{\mathrm{frame}}$** (`no_published_totHeap_with_frame_ref`, `WP.lean:272`). Kernel-checked theorems `published_wp_set_frame_ref_unsound` (`WP.lean:335`), `published_wp_get_frame_ref_unsound` (`WP.lean:400`), and `published_wp_not_heap_monotone` (`WP.lean:501`) prove that the antecedents of `wp-set` and `wp-get` hold while `wpPublishedAt` is `False`, and that `wpPublishedAt (M.ret ()) ⊤` holds at $\emptyset : \mathsf{pH}\, w$ yet fails at $h \ge \emptyset$ (so `wpPublishedAt` does not even land in $\mathcal{B}\, w = [\mathsf{pH}, \mathsf{Prop}_{\mathbb{W}}]\, w$).

#### 2.2 Repaired `wp` forcing clause (`wpRepaired`) and mechanized soundness
`Basis: derived + execution`

Replacing $h' : \mathsf{tH}_{w''}$ (and $(w_r)_* h'$) with **$h' : \mathsf{pH}\, w_r$ satisfying $|h'| = w''$** (equivalently $\eta_{h'} : \mathcal{H}(w'', w_r)$, using the two-parameter distributor already defined at `paper.tex:1506`):
$$p = \mathsf{pack}\bigl(w_r,\, (w_r)_* h_f \cdot_{w_r} h',\, a\bigr) \;\land\; w_r \Vdash \bigl(h' \models \phi_{w_r}\, a = \top\bigr)$$
is completely well-typed ($|(w_r)_* h_f \cdot_{w_r} h'| = |h_f| \cdot w'' = w_r$), monotone in stages, worlds $w \le w_1$, and heap extensions $h \le h \cdot_w h_{\mathrm{ext}}$ (`wpRepaired : BIProp n w`, `WP.lean:569`), validates the frame-reference scenario (`repaired_wp_set_and_get_frame_ref_sound`, `WP.lean:1497`), and validates **every** TULIP inference rule (`WP.lean:835–1767`, `Logic.lean:495–656`, `Adequacy.lean:123–162`):
- **BI-hyperdoctrine laws:** `bi_sep_comm`, `bi_sep_assoc`, `bi_sep_top`, `bi_wand_adj`, `bi_persist_dup`, `bi_and_persist_sep`, `bi_later_intro`, `bi_later_sep`, `bi_loeb`, and consistency `bi_consistent` ($\top \not\vdash \bot$).
- **Structural & effect `wp` laws:** `wp_val`, `wp_mono`, `wp_wand`, `wp_frame`, `wp_bind`, `wp_step` (`▷P ⊢ wp step P`), `wp_get` (`(∃ v, [r ↦ v] ∗ ▷([r ↦ v] -∗ Φ v)) ⊢ wp (get r) Φ`), **`wp_set` with zero `▷`** (`(∃ y, [r ↦ y]) ∗ ([r ↦ v] -∗ Φ ()) ⊢ wp (set r v) Φ`), **`wp_new` with zero `▷`** (`(∀ r, [r ↦ v] -∗ Φ r) ⊢ wp (new v) Φ`), guarded recursion `wp_rec`, and closed-program extraction `wp_adequacy` / `wp_adequacy_runClosed`.
