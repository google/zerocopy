# Unified Pure-Lifting Cutpoints, Static-DAG Tarjan SCC `.olean` Sharding under Lean 4 Modules, and Day-2 Proof Maintenance

## Summary

Scaling zero-TCB-growth Lean 4 verification of real Rust crates from isolated single-function slices to multi-hundred-function systems codebases requires resolving three coupled bottlenecks simultaneously: (1) preventing cutpoint and state-machine proliferation across safe, unsafe, interprocedural, and reflective proof libraries; (2) reconciling dynamic post-capture Tarjan strongly connected component (SCC) sharding with static analysis-time build graphs and Lean 4's native `module` system; and (3) sustaining proof durability across routine Rust refactors and compiler upgrades without silent specification weakening.

1. **Minimal Orthogonal Pure-Lifting Core (Strictly Two Execution Levels, Three Zero-`Prop`-Argument Cutpoints, Two Trees, One Lens):** Consolidating `&mut self` call summaries, checked-arithmetic/`?` enum joins, byte-level spatial pinning with Galois codecs, certified MIR pre-simplification (`SimplifyCfg` + dead-local compaction), and batch GMP side-condition reflection onto one orthogonal architecture eliminates duplicate state machines and closure-in-state memory blowups:
   - **Two execution levels:** Pure safe Rust executes in a 4-field `SymState` with a 3-constructor closure-free `LoanPhase` (`activeShared | activeMut | ended`) and one `LawfulLens` algebra, while unsafe byte/borrow reasoning executes in a single Miri-faithful `StackState` over `MemoryState`, `ByteTagMap`, and flat-arena `TbForest` paired with closure-free first-order `CodecMeta` / `SpatialSliceDesc` descriptors.
   - **Three zero-`Prop`-argument `@[irreducible]` barriers:** Because Lean 4's `rw` (`kabstract` + `congrArg`) and `simp` (`simpApp` → `congrArgs`) traverse explicit `Prop` arguments without unfolding the head constant, every cutpoint barrier must accept strictly `Type`-valued data parameters: `StepStageCut` (intra-procedural stage/join), `CallSpecCut` (interprocedural 4-way call/unwind with `StateDisjointFrame`), and `BoolCertCut` (generic reflective `Code → Bool` seal).
   - **Memory-state soundness condition for dead-local compaction:** Extending scalar dead-local compaction (`R3`) to `MemoryState` (`LocalSlot.indirect`) requires `AddressTakenPreserved` (pinning address-taken locals live and preserving their `StorageLive`/`StorageDead` markers so stack `AllocId` sequences remain invariant), alongside `checkDeadBlockStorageSafeProgram` for dead-block elimination.
2. **Dual-View Native Lean 4 `module` Eliminates Binary `.olean` Stubbing and Composes with Depth-1 Static-DAG Sharding:**
   - Converting Lean 4 verification libraries to `module` (`isModule: true`, `public section`, selective `@[expose]` on unfolded `def`s only, never `@[expose]` over `theorem`s) natively exports every `public theorem` as `.axiomInfo` inside `.olean` and moves proof bodies into `.olean.private`, shrinking public `.olean` footprints by **`76.2%`** (`53.76 MiB → 12.81 MiB` across 63 Anneal runtime modules on Lean `v4.33.1` and `v4.34.1`) while making proof-body edits bit-for-bit invisible to downstream `.olean`/`.ir.sig`/`.ir` digests.
   - Destructive binary `.olean` rewriting (`.thmInfo -> .axiomInfo` via `saveModuleData`) breaks cross-region `object_compactor` pointers in `.olean.server`/`.olean.private` (`SIGSEGV`) and fails independent 3-axiom kernels (`lean4export` + `nanoda_lib` with `unpermitted_axiom_hard_error: true`). Instead, one unmodified `module` build serves **two views**: **View A** mounts Stage-1 `.olean` only (`level := .exported` with Stage-2 `importAll := true`, mapping zero bytes of Stage-1 `.olean.private`), while **View B** mounts all parts (`level := .private`) for full end-to-end multi-kernel replay with zero extra axioms. Under `-DElab.async=false`, all 7 output formats are bit-for-bit deterministic across directories, timestamps, and paths (`Elab.async=true` diverges in `.olean.private` via `Lean.extraModUses`).
   - Static-DAG build systems (Bazel `< 9`, GN/Ninja—whose live `gn` binary has zero `dyndep` support—non-IFD Nix, CMake, and Meson) cannot mint dynamic execution-time `Expected/Scc_<id>.lean` actions or wire depth-29 inter-shard edges, and volatile Tarjan ordinals break checked-in proof files on every build system. Emitting proof-free `ShardCert` interface stubs per shard collapses compilation depth from $D = 29$ (`regex-syntax`) to **`D = 1` (embarrassingly parallel)**, unifying **Mode S1** (checked-in nominal `anneal-shards.json` + `lean --deps-json` golden manifest for authored proofs) and **Mode S2** (`dynamic_output` in Buck2, `ParallelDagPlan` in Cargo, `map_directory` in Bazel `9+`, or fixed static $B$-slot bin-packing in GN/Bazel `< 9` for auto-generated proofs), all closed fail-closed by a Merkle `ShardAuditReceiptV2` linker enforcing disjoint ownership, kernel-derived rank monotonicity, canonical $H_{\text{sig}}$, cone-scoped `(axioms, opaques ∨ partial ∨ unsafe)` propagation, and TOCTOU snapshot matching.
3. **Day-2 Proof Maintenance and Zero-TCB Bounded Automated Repair:**
   - Canonical proof-unit hashing (`H_unit`) strips source spans and `:line:col` suffixes, keys by semantic `DefPath` rather than sequential `DefIndex`, applies deterministic reverse-postorder (RPO) locally-nameless temporary/block numbering (eliminating `13.4%–22.4%` raw MIR comment-shift churn), and closes transitively over `Const::Unevaluated` + evaluated `ConstValue` dependencies.
   - Combining top-down `ptreeTabulate` CFG maps, dual-digest `BlockWpCert` backward-cone invalidation (`Cone⁻(G, Δ)`: `93.75%–96.09%` block reuse, `10.85×` speedup on 128-block CFGs), clause-content Merkle `ProofNodeForest` caches, and nominal declarative proof recipes reduces routine refactor maintenance to zero-LLM deterministic regeneration.
   - To prevent verifier-accepted specification weakening (where empirical studies such as `VerusEval` report only `42.3%` semantic-audit pass rates across verifier-accepted LLM artifacts), automated repair enforces a hard read-only specification lock, runs deterministic three-class counterexample triage (`Class Ia` UB, `Class Ib` functional bug, `Class II` non-inductive invariant, `Class III` missing proof rule) before invoking any LLM, escalates through a deterministic Tier 0–2 ladder before bounded Tier 3 agentic repair, and gates acceptance strictly on full kernel replay.

## Findings

### 1. Unifying Pure-Lifting Cutpoints into One Minimal Orthogonal Core

Basis: source + execution + derived

Inspecting `google/zerocopy@d2f63ce1c3` (`anneal/theory/Anneal/{Core,MirTotal,MirWp,PureLift}/`) alongside five independently developed verification-scaling patterns (`&mut self` call summaries, enum/checked-arithmetic join cuts, spatial byte pinning with codecs, certified MIR compaction/simplification, and batch GMP side-condition reflection) identifies four structural redundancies that collapse cleanly into one minimal core:

1. **Strictly Two Execution Levels Plus Stateless First-Order Codecs:**
   - **Level 1 (Pure Safe Layer):** A 4-field symbolic state `SymState LiftedVal` (`locals`, `loans`, `regionGroups`, `regionDeposits`) with a 3-constructor closure-free `LoanPhase` (`activeShared | activeMut | ended`), non-nested binary cons spines (`tupleNil | tupleCons`) for `LiftedVal` (preserving `hasIR = true` without nested-`List` LCNF failures), polymorphic `EvalOutcome (α β : Type)` (`ok | unwound | fail`), and a single lawful bidirectional lens algebra (`Lens` + `LawfulLens`, subsuming `BiLens`). Safe Rust verifies with zero `MemoryState`, `ByteTagMap`, or `TbForest` overhead.
   - **Level 2 (Operational Byte + Borrow Layer):** A single Miri-faithful `StackState` over `MemoryState` (`LocalSlot.indirect`), `ByteTagMap` (`PtrSpan := { tag, fragStart, origWord : BitVec 64 }`), and flat-arena `TbForest` (`RangeMap LocationState` implementing 6-state Miri Tree Borrows with `StrongProtector`, `SIFA`, `LocalAccess`-before-`ForeignAccess`, and forward per-byte protector release).
   - **Closure-Free Codec Registry:** Storing `encode`/`decode` closures inside `LoanPhase` causes severe kernel heap growth when state updates copy closure ASTs. Decoupling codecs into first-order `CodecMeta` + `CodecId : Nat` + `SpatialSliceDesc` (`bufBase`, `bufLen`, `dataPtrOff`, `widthBytes`, `heightRows`, `absStride`, `strideNeg`) resolved through a static 4-law Galois codec registry (`DecodeValid`, `DecodeMono`, `DecodeEncode`, `EncodeDecodeLeDefined` over `PaddingMask`) eliminates closures from state while uniformly handling 1D slices, 2D negative-stride/interlaced planes, padded `#[repr(C)]` structs, and pointer provenance.

2. **Three Orthogonal Zero-`Prop`-Argument `@[irreducible]` Cutpoints:**
   - Direct inspection of Lean `v4.34.1` (`src/Lean/Meta/Tactic/Rewrite.lean:46–82` and `src/Lean/Meta/Tactic/Simp/Main.lean:632–656`) confirms that `rw` (`kabstract` + `congrArg`) and `simp` (`simpApp` → `congrArgs`) descend into explicit `Prop` arguments of any application even when its head constant is `@[irreducible]`. Wrappers of the form `Hold (P : Prop)` or `Firewall (tag : Nat) (P : Prop)` leak under `rw`/`simp`.
   - Every sealing barrier therefore accepts strictly `Type`-valued computational data parameters and zero `Prop` parameters:
     - `StepStageCut {S Err Out : Type} (step : S → StepOutcome S Err Out) (fuel : Nat) (s₀ s₁ : S) : Prop` — seals intra-procedural CFG stages and postdominator joins (collapsing $2^{K+1}-1$ naive WP nodes on $K$ checked-arithmetic/`?` diamonds to $3K + 1$ nodes).
     - `CallSpecCut` — seals 4-way call/unwind transitions (`.cleanup`, `.continue_`, `.terminate`, `.unreachable`) paired with one unified `StateDisjointFrame (fp : ByteFootprint)` (`MemTagsDisjointFrame` + `TbDisjointFrame` modulo monotone `SIFA` weakening), collapsing $(S+1)^M$ symbolic heap `ite` chains across $M$ `&mut self` calls to $3M$ linear steps and supporting safe-caller encapsulation of unsafe callees.
     - `BoolCertCut {Code : Type} (check : Code → Bool) (c : Code) : Prop := check c = true` (with `sealBoolCert`/`openBoolCert` proved prior to `attribute [irreducible] BoolCertCut`) — unifies batch GMP side-condition reflection (`51.72×` faster than `omega`), certified `R3`/`SimplifyCfg` validators, `SepAC` multiset framing, `EnumArithSummary` chains, and Day-2 `BlockWpCert` certificates.

3. **Two Canonical Indexed Trees (Replacing Five Redundant Structures):**
   - **Sparse/Extensional `PTree` with Top-Down `ptreeTabulate`:** Constructing a radix-2 trie via sequential `List.foldl ptreeSetAt .leaf` creates $B$ nested redexes that force all $B$ insertions on the first lookup (stack depth `1,023 > 512` `maxRecDepth` at $B = 512$). Top-down `ptreeTabulate` immediately exposes `.node` in WHNF, forcing only $\lceil \log_2 B \rceil$ spine nodes per lookup (`90.6×` fewer forced nodes at $B = 512$).
   - **Dense Balanced `Chunk8` `BlockTree`:** Packing dense `0`-indexed MIR basic-block, local-slot, and live-mask tables into 8-way leaf chunks keeps AST depth `<= 11` at $N = 4,096$ (`372.5×` shallower than `List.cons`), avoiding `maxRecDepth = 512` and supporting stride-8 $O(L + S \log_2(L/8))$ compaction checking (`5.49×` net speedup over uncompacted `R0` at `K = 32`, scaling cleanly through `K = 128`).

4. **Two Soundness Invariants for `MemoryState` Pre-Simplification and Kernel GMP Reflection:**
   - **`AddressTakenPreserved` for `R3` Dead-Local Compaction on `MemoryState`:** In byte-memory MIR semantics, `.storageLive` and `.storageDead` on an address-taken (`LocalSlot.indirect`) local allocate/deallocate stack `AllocId`s and advance `nextAllocId`. Deleting `.storageLive` on an unused `.indirect` local shifts the `AllocId` of subsequent allocations (`support/probe_scale_sharding_and_day2_repair.py` Probe 1: `base_alloc3=2` vs. `unsound_alloc3=1`). `ValidBodyCompaction` must therefore pin every `.indirect` local live and compact strictly non-address-taken dead temporaries.
   - **`checkDeadBlockStorageSafeProgram` for `SimplifyCfg`:** Because `always_storage_live_locals` (`hasStorageAnnotation`) scans unreachable blocks too, deleting a dead block holding a local's sole `StorageLive`/`StorageDead` marker flips entry liveness from `.dead` to `.live`; reverse VC transport requires checking `hasStorageAnnotation` preservation for surviving referenced locals.
   - **`State.casesOn` Unpacking for Kernel GMP Fast Paths:** In Lean `v4.34.1` (`src/kernel/type_checker.cpp:703`), `type_checker::reduce_nat` aborts GMP reduction whenever `has_fvar(e) == true` before calling `whnf` on arguments. Unpacking state records via `State.casesOn` at step entry and using flat constructor tuples (eliminating $O(3^K)$ `{ s with ... }` tree blowups) restores native GMP reduction in reflective evaluators.

### 2. Dual-View Lean 4 `module` Precompilation and Static-DAG Portable `<300 MiB` Tarjan SCC Sharding

Basis: source + execution + derived

#### 2.1 Why Native Lean 4 `module` Replaces Destructive Binary `.olean` Stubbing

Direct inspection and execution on Lean `v4.33.1` (`819816b2`) and `v4.34.1` (`5045d005`) establish three constraints governing two-stage precompilation (`theory-lock.json`) and independent multi-kernel replay:

1. **Native `module` visibility projection (`readModuleDataParts`):** Under `isModule: true` (`public section` with selective `@[expose]` on unfolded `def`s only, never `@[expose]` over `theorem`s), Lean 4 writes every `public theorem` (tactic-mode, term-mode `:= rfl`, and compiler-generated `.inj`/`.injEq`/`.sizeOf_spec`) and every unexposed `public def` as **`.axiomInfo` (`hasVal = false`) inside `.olean`**, strips `private` declarations from `.olean`, and stores full `.thmInfo`/`.defnInfo` bodies in `.olean.private`. Across all 63 Anneal runtime files (`19` `trusted/lean/` + `44` `theory/`), this shrinks `theory/` public `.olean`s by **`76.2%`** (`53.76 MiB → 12.81 MiB`, moving `37.09 MiB` into `.olean.private`)—exceeding Config 3b binary stubbing (`-54.6%`)—and keeps `.olean`, `.ir.sig`, and `.ir` **bit-for-bit identical** when a theorem proof body is edited.
2. **Incompatibility of binary `.olean` rewriting with `module` and `nanoda_lib`:** Rewriting `.olean` in place via `saveModuleData` corrupts raw absolute cross-region pointers embedded in `.olean.server` (`+0x10000`) and `.olean.private` (`+0x20000`) by `saveModuleDataParts`'s shared `object_compactor`, causing an immediate **`SIGSEGV` (`rc = 245`)** on `readModuleDataParts`. Moreover, feeding `.thmInfo -> .axiomInfo`-stubbed `.olean`s into `lean4export` emits extra axioms that cause `nanoda_lib` (`unpermitted_axiom_hard_error: true`, `src/parser.rs:832–837`) to abort.
3. **Dual-View Zero-Mutation `module` Consumption:** One unmodified `module` build yields `(.olean, .olean.server, .olean.private, .ir.sig, .ir)` per file, consumed in two views:
   - **View A (Fast Compilation & Fast Stage-2 Replay Audit):** Mount Stage-1 `.olean` + `.ir.sig` ONLY (zero bytes of Stage-1 `.olean.private` read or mapped!) plus Stage-2 all 5 parts, loaded via `importModules #[{ module := ProofMod, importAll := true }] {} (trustLevel := 0) (loadExts := false) (level := .exported) (arts := mixedArts)`. Stage-1 theorems load natively as `.axiomInfo`, Stage-1 `@[expose] def`s load as full `.defnInfo`, Stage-2 proofs load as full `.thmInfo`, and `Kernel.Environment.replay` passes cleanly while Stage-1 `(axioms, directFeats, guardedFeats)` summaries are authenticated from `theory-lock.json`.
   - **View B (Full End-to-End Multi-Kernel Replay):** Mount unmodified 5-part artifacts for both Stage-1 and Stage-2 (`level := .private`), exporting zero extra axioms to `lean4export` + `nanoda_lib` / `con-ron`.
4. **Determinism & ABI Factorization:** With `-DElab.async=false` and `-j1`, all 7 outputs (`.olean`, `.olean.server`, `.olean.private`, `.ir.sig`, `.ir`, `.ilean`, `.c`) are **100% bit-for-bit deterministic** across working directories, path forms, and `mtime` shifts with zero embedded filesystem paths (`Elab.async=true` diverges deterministically in `.olean.private` due to `_private.Lean.ExtraModUses.0.Lean.extraModUses`). Because `olean_header.flags` bit 0 encodes `LEAN_USE_GMP` (`module.cpp:493` rejects flag mismatches), `theory-lock.json` must separate its **portable source/semantic lock** (`modules[]` DAG, `source_sha256`, Stage-1 summaries) from its **per-toolchain-ABI artifact digest table** keyed by `(lean_version, githash, use_gmp, elab_async)`.

#### 2.2 Static-DAG vs. Dynamic Tarjan SCC Sharding (`D = 1` `ShardCert` Stubbing)

Real Rust crate call graphs (`rustc --emit=llvm-ir`) exhibit deep condensed DAGs and indivisible multi-node SCCs: `miniz_oxide` (`609` fns, `921` edges, `max_dag_depth = 15`), `regex-syntax` (`2,836` fns, `5,258` edges, `9` multi-node SCCs of sizes `[25, 23, 18, 11, 5, 4, 4, 4, 2]`, `max_dag_depth = 29`). Lean `v4.34.1` deterministically rejects splitting a cyclic SCC across two `.olean` modules in either build order.

Meanwhile, static-DAG build systems cannot read execution-time `expected_manifest.json` to mint $K$ independently cached actions or wire depth-29 inter-shard edges (and live GN binaries contain zero Ninja `dyndep` support), while volatile Tarjan ordinal filenames (`Scc_7.lean`) break checked-in authored proof files on *every* build system.

Decoupling compile-time sharding from audit-time linking via **proof-free `ShardCert` interface stubs** resolves both tensions simultaneously:

- **Depth-1 Compilation Collapse ($D = 29 \to D = 1$):** Each shard's expected file `Expected/Shard/<id>.lean` declares shard `<id>`'s local obligations alongside proof-free interface stubs for its cross-shard callees (`stub_deps`). Consequently, `Proof/Shard/<id>.lean` **never imports another shard's `Proof` `.olean`**. Every shard compiles in parallel at depth `1` (`<300 MiB` peak RSS), and editing a proof body in one shard invalidates zero inputs of any other shard.
- **Two-Mode Build-System Scheduling:**
  - **Mode S1 (Universal Default for Authored Proofs, `> 20` fns):** Checked-in nominal shard plan (`anneal-shards.json`, keyed by stable Rust module/function names, validated fail-closed by `gen-expected` for disjoint coverage and cycle containment) plus checked-in intra-package Lean import graphs generated via `lean --deps-json` (header-only `Lean.Elab.ParseImportsFast`, zero build-time `lake`) and guarded by CI golden tests. Works uniformly on Cargo, Bazel, GN/Ninja, Buck2, Nix, CMake, and Meson.
  - **Mode S2 (Automated Zero-Authored-Proof Batch Pipelines):** Dynamic fan-out via `ctx.actions.dynamic_output_new` (Buck2) or `ParallelDagPlan` (Cargo); flat `D = 1` `ctx.actions.map_directory` over a `TreeArtifact` (Bazel `9+`, zero JSON content reads needed!); or deterministic least-loaded SCC bin-packing into a fixed static slot count $B \in \{4, 8, 16\}$ (`Expected/Slot_0..Slot_{B-1}.lean`, keeping multi-node SCCs atomic, verified in `support/probe_scale_sharding_and_day2_repair.py` Probe 2) on Bazel `< 9`, GN/Ninja, Nix, CMake, and Meson.
- **Cross-Crate `Runtime(PostCleanup)` Sidecar Law:** Direct inspection of `rustc` (`compiler/rustc_metadata/src/rmeta/encoder.rs:1839`, `compiler/rustc_mir_transform/src/lib.rs:686–822`) proves that `-Zalways-encode-mir -Zmir-opt-level=0 --emit=metadata` encodes `tcx.optimized_mir` (`Runtime(Optimized)`, still running `inline::ForceInline`). Cross-crate transparent verification dependencies must therefore hand off an Anneal-owned `Runtime(PostCleanup)` `<crate>.verify.mir` sidecar captured at `mir_drops_elaborated_and_const_checked`, keeping production `.rmeta`/`.rlib` untouched.
- **Merkle `ShardAuditReceiptV2` Global Linker:** Because `CollectAxioms.collect` (`src/Lean/Util/CollectAxioms.lean:62`) omits `.opaqueInfo` and trusts `exportedAxiomsExt` under `loadExts := true`, and because isolated per-shard replay admits split-brain mutual cycles (`S0: B ⊢ A`, `S1: A ⊢ B`), the global linker (`loadExts := false`) must enforce: (1) 4 mandatory kernel edge classes (syntactic refs, mutual inductive cliques, `Quot -> Eq`, implicit `String.ofList`/`Char.ofNat`), (2) disjoint constant ownership, (3) strict topological rank monotonicity ($\tau(\text{owner}(a)) < \tau(S_i)$) computed strictly from kernel-replayed `collectAxioms (allowOpaque := true)`, (4) canonical per-constant $H_{\text{sig}}$ match (binding `(name, kind, levelParams, type, inductMeta, body?)` where `body?` is retained for transparent `DefinitionVal` and non-K `Acc.rec` computational witnesses and omitted for ordinary `TheoremVal`), (5) cone-scoped `(A_trans, O_trans)` propagation rejecting `.opaqueInfo`/`isPartial`/`isUnsafe` inside the claim cone, (6) TOCTOU `.olean`-part and receipt-digest snapshot verification across every shard edge, (7) monotone cross-crate waiver-union semilattice $W(C::f)$, and (8) structural `List.Mem` TCB lookup witnesses (`32.4×` faster than `by decide` / `String.beq` `11.5 ms/cmp`).

### 3. Day-2 Proof Maintenance and Zero-TCB Bounded Automated Repair

Basis: source + execution + documentation + derived

Empirical surgical-edit studies on `miniz_oxide 0.9.1` (`358` `PostCleanup` bodies) and `serde_json 1.0.154` (`1,105` bodies), cross-prover upgrade studies (`Mathlib` `37` bumps/yr and `7,913` `set_option backward.*`, `Rocq` `309` overlays, `F*` retiring `.hints` for lazy `.checked` `prune_decls`, `Dafny` `ProofDependencyManager` + module-based induction), and LLM verification postmortems (`VerusEval` `42.3%` semantic pass rate across `13,716` verifier-accepted artifacts; `KVerus` `80.2% → 51.0%` and `AutoVerus` `56.9% → 4.5%` single-file to cross-file drop; `Baldur`, `PALM`, `Rango`, `LeanHammer`/`HammerRank`) establish a four-layer Day-2 maintenance architecture:

1. **Canonical Proof-Unit Hashing (`H_unit`) & Sound Early Cutoff:**
