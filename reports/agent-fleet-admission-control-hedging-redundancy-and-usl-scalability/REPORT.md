#### 1.2 Classical Systems & Agent Concurrency Foundations

1. **Serialized Admission Gate Queueing (`Little, 1961`; `Stidham, 1985`):** For task-start arrivals at aggregate rate $\Lambda$ entering an `M/G/1` admission gate with service time $S_{\text{chk}}$ (mean $\bar{s}_{\text{chk}}$, squared coefficient of variation $C_s^2 = \text{Var}(S_{\text{chk}})/\bar{s}_{\text{chk}}^2$, utilization $\rho_{\text{gate}} = \Lambda \bar{s}_{\text{chk}} < 1$), Pollaczek–Khinchine mean gate residence time is:
   $$L_{\text{sync}}(\Lambda) = \bar{s}_{\text{chk}}\left(1 + \frac{\rho_{\text{gate}}(1 + C_s^2)}{2(1 - \rho_{\text{gate}})}\right), \qquad N_{\text{blocked}} = \Lambda L_{\text{sync}}(\Lambda)$$
   diverging as $(1 - \rho_{\text{gate}})^{-1}$ and imposing head-of-line blocking on `100%` of disjoint arrivals.
2. **Finite-Resource Inversion & Granular Optimistic Scheduling (`Kung & Robinson, 1981`; `Agrawal, Carey, & Livny, 1987`; `Omega` EuroSys '13, `google/cluster-scheduler-simulator@b882f266`; `Sparrow` SOSP '13, `radlab/sparrow@afb8efad`):**
   - `Agrawal, Carey, & Livny (1987)` prove that while infinite-resource `OCC` scales logarithmically and `2PL` thrashes on blocking chains (Experiment 1), finite physical resources (Experiment 2: `1` CPU + `2` disks) cause unthrottled `OCC` to peak early (`mpl = 10`) and collapse from wasted-work restarts unless multiprogramming admission control caps active concurrency (`2PL` peaks at `mpl = 25`, `92%` useful disk utilization).
   - `Omega` (`OmegaSimulation.scala:L196–265`: `syncCellState` [L198] $\to$ `afterDelay(jobThinkTime)` [L200] $\to$ `scheduleJob` [L205] $\to$ `cellState.commit(claimDeltas, true)` [L220], abandoning jobs after `>100` zero-task or `>1,000` total retries [L262–265]) shows pessimistic `Mesos` resource offers starve concurrent batch schedulers when service decision times hit $1\text{--}100\text{ s}$ (`~200` abandoned jobs, Fig. 7c), whereas optimistic shared state scales to `~3×` with 3 batch schedulers—provided **fine-grained conflict detection** avoids the `2–3×` spurious-conflict inflation of machine-level sequence numbers and **incremental transactions** avoid the `~2×` inflation of gang commits (Figs. 12 & 14).
   - `Sparrow` combines $d = 2$ batch sampling ($d \cdot m$ random workers) with late-bound reservation queues (`NodeMonitor.java`, `TaskScheduler.java`). On a **`10,000`-machine `4`-core simulator** at `80%` load, batch sampling reaches `0.73×` per-task power-of-two (`1.92×` omniscient) and late binding cuts response time a further `45%` (`0.55×` batch sampling, within `5%` / `4 ms` of omniscient at `~2%` RPC cost); on a **`110`-machine EC2 deployment of `8`-core (`m2.4xlarge`) instances** running TPC-H on Spark, median queueing delay stays `<9 ms` (within `12%` of ideal).
3. **One-Way Monotonic Trajectory Pre-Order (`CoAgent`, `Lyu et al., 2026`, `arXiv:2606.15376v1`):**
   - Across `10` contended 2-agent workloads (`100` trials on `deepseek-v4-flash`, `§7.2` Fig. 5), `serial` achieves `98%` pass (`1.00×` speed, `1.00×` cost); `naive` drops to `13%` pass (`1.54×` speed, `1.06×` cost); `2PL-saga` suffers `0.81` deadlocks/trial from startup read amplification (`96%` pass, `1.04×` speed, **`1.45×` cost**); and `OCC-saga` suffers `0.95` aborts/trial (`93%` pass, **`0.93×` speed**, **`1.83×` mean / `2.33×` max cost**). In the `§7.3` single-cell `AIOpsLab` case study, latencies are `50.2 s` (`serial`), `31.8 s` (`naive`), `53.1 s` (`2PL`), `63.7 s` (`OCC`, `~32 s` discarded), and `32.8 s` (`MTPO`, notification at `t = 15.7 s`).
   - Symmetric bilateral notifications (`§5.2` Fig. 2: $x \leftarrow y/2,\; y \leftarrow x/2$) induce cyclic `rw` antidependencies that never reach `GlobalQuiet`. Fixing a strict claim pre-order $\prec_\sigma$ (`MTPO`) so notifications flow **strictly one-way** ($\sigma_i < \sigma_j$) guarantees a DAG, achieving **`1.43×` (`1.4×`) 10-cell speedup**, **`1.15×` token cost**, and **`93%` pass rate** (`5/100` relevance misjudgments = `94.9%` of serial).

#### 1.3 Closed-Form Crossover Equations, Equal-Recall Two-Tier Economics, and Shared Judge Queueing (`Probe 1`)

For standalone task work $W > 0$, destructive-overlap prior $p \in (0, 1)$, merge/reconciliation penalty $(\tau_{\text{merge}}, c_{\text{merge}})$, detector $(\text{TPR}, \text{FPR})$, check cost $c_{\text{chk}}$, async delay $\Delta t_{\text{async}}$ ($\eta = \mathbb{I}(\Delta t_{\text{async}} < W)$), switch cost $(\tau_{\text{sw}}, c_{\text{sw}})$, and advisory triage false-positive cost $\tau_{\text{fp,async}} = \tau_{\text{inspect}} + p_{\text{mis}}(\Delta t_{\text{async}} + \tau_{\text{sw}})$ (with $\psi_{\text{tp}} = \min(W + \tau_{\text{merge}}, \Delta t_{\text{async}} + \tau_{\text{sw}})$ and $\kappa_{\text{tp}} = \min(W + c_{\text{merge}}, \Delta t_{\text{async}} + c_{\text{sw}})$):

$$\mathbb{E}[\Delta T_{\text{sync}}] = L_{\text{sync}}(\Lambda) + p(1 - \text{TPR})(W + \tau_{\text{merge}}) + (1 - p)\,\text{FPR}\,\tau_{\text{fp,sync}}$$
$$\mathbb{E}[\Delta T_{\text{async}}] = p\,\text{TPR}\,\psi_{\text{tp}} + p(1 - \text{TPR})(W + \tau_{\text{merge}}) + (1 - p)\,\text{FPR}\,\eta\,\tau_{\text{fp,async}}$$
$$\mathbb{E}[\Delta C_{\text{sync}}] = c_{\text{chk}} + p(1 - \text{TPR})(W + c_{\text{merge}}) + (1 - p)\,\text{FPR}\,c_{\text{fp,sync}}, \qquad \mathbb{E}[\Delta C_{\text{async}}] = c_{\text{chk}} + p\,\text{TPR}\,\kappa_{\text{tp}} + p(1 - \text{TPR})(W + c_{\text{merge}}) + (1 - p)\,\text{FPR}\,\eta\,c_{\text{fp,async}}$$

- **Single-Tier Latency vs. Token Crossover:** For equal ROC and $\Delta t_{\text{async}} + \tau_{\text{sw}} < W + \tau_{\text{merge}}$, `SyncGate` beats `AsyncAdv` on latency iff $L_{\text{sync}}(\Lambda) < p\,\text{TPR}(\Delta t_{\text{async}} + \tau_{\text{sw}}) + (1-p)\text{FPR}(\tau_{\text{fp,async}} - \tau_{\text{fp,sync}})$. Because external RPC waiting burns `0` worker tokens, minimizing $J(\omega_{\text{tok}}) = \mathbb{E}[\Delta T] + \omega_{\text{tok}}\mathbb{E}[\Delta C]$ flips from `AsyncAdv` to `SyncGate` above $\omega_{\text{tok}}^* = \frac{\mathbb{E}[\Delta T_{\text{sync}}] - \mathbb{E}[\Delta T_{\text{async}}]}{\mathbb{E}[\Delta C_{\text{async}}] - \mathbb{E}[\Delta C_{\text{sync}}]}$.
- **Reconciled Latency vs. Compute Neyman–Pearson Floors:** Letting $S_T = (W + \tau_{\text{merge}}) - \psi_{\text{tp}} > 0$ and $S_C = (W + c_{\text{merge}}) - \kappa_{\text{tp}} > 0$ (when $S_T \le 0$ or $S_C \le 0$, $\text{LR}_+^* = +\infty$), `AsyncAdv` beats `NoCheck` iff $\text{LR}_+ = \text{TPR}/\text{FPR}$ exceeds:
  $$\text{LR}_+^{*,\text{lat}} = \frac{1 - p}{p}\cdot\frac{\eta\,\tau_{\text{fp,async}}}{S_T}, \qquad \text{LR}_+^{*,\text{tok}} = \frac{1 - p}{p}\cdot\frac{\eta\,c_{\text{fp,async}}}{S_C} + \frac{c_{\text{chk}}}{p\,\text{FPR}\,S_C}$$
  where the compute floor adds the second term to amortize `100%`-arrival detector token spend $c_{\text{chk}}$.

#### 2.1 Queueing Crossover $\rho^*$, `JSQ-2` Baseline Separation, and Pure $Z = 0$ `S&X` Instability (`Probe 2A`)

1. **Classical Empirical & Queueing Results:**
   - **Delayed Hedging, Tied Requests, & Backup Tasks (`Dean & Barroso, 2013`, DOI `10.1145/2408776.2408794`; `Dean & Ghemawat, 2004`):** A `100`-server fan-out with leaf $P_{99} = 1\text{ s}$ exceeds `1 s` on $1 - 0.99^{100} = 63\%$ of requests; deferring secondary copies until the `95th`-percentile (`10 ms` BigTable hedge) cuts `1,000`-key $P_{99.9}$ from **`1,800 ms → 74 ms` (`24.3×`)** at `+2%` request overhead, while cross-server cancel-on-start (`Tied` requests) cuts uncached disk-read $P_{99.9}$ by `32%–38%` at `<1%` disk overhead (`Table 2`). MapReduce end-game backup tasks cut `1 TB` TeraSort elapsed time from `1,283 s` to **`891 s` (`-30.6%`)** at a few percent extra compute.
   - **Exact Stability & Suboptimality Regimes (`Vulimiri et al., 2013`; `Gardner et al., 2015, 2017a, 2017b`; `Shah, Lee, & Ramchandran, 2016`):**
     - *Uncancelled $d$-copy replication (`Vulimiri` Theorem 1):* Under `M/M/1` mean-field independence, $\mathbb{E}[T_{\text{rep},d}] < \mathbb{E}[T_1]$ iff $\rho < \rho^*(d) = \frac{1}{d + 1}$ (`1/3` for $d = 2$; verified in `Probe 2A` at $\rho = 0.22$: `0.897 < 1.283` vs. $\rho = 0.42$: `3.052 > 1.750`), destabilizing at $\rho \ge 1/d$.
     - *Instant Cancel-on-Complete (`CoC`):* Under i.i.d. exponential (`IR`) service with zero removal cost ($Z = 0$), `Redundancy-d` preserves server work and stays stable for all $\rho < 1$ (`Gardner et al., 2015, 2017a`, DOI `10.1145/2745844.2745873` / *PER* `43(1)` `10.1145/2796314.2745873`; `Shah et al., 2016` Theorem 1). However, high-load `CoC` becomes suboptimal or unstable under **three distinct mechanisms**: (a) light-everywhere i.i.d. service times (`Shah et al.` **Theorems 4 & 8**), (b) memoryless i.i.d. service with **nonzero removal/setup cost $Z > 0$** (`Shah et al.` **Theorems 5 & 9**), and (c) **coupled inherent job size $R_j = X \cdot S_j$** (`Gardner et al., 2017b`, *IEEE/ACM Trans. Netw.* 25(6):3353–3367, DOI `10.1109/TNET.2017.2744607`; MASCOTS '16 `10.1109/MASCOTS.2016.43`), whose full-synchronization ($d = k$) stability ceiling is $\rho_{\max}^{\text{S\&X}}(d) = \frac{\mathbb{E}[S]}{d\,\mathbb{E}[\min(S_1, \dots, S_d)]}$.

### 3. Universal Scalability Law (`USL`) Knees, Upstream Nose-Curve Sign Audit, and Pod Sharding

`Basis: normative + source + derived + execution`

1. **Rational Capacity Law & Queueing Grounding (`Gunther, 1993, 2007, 2008 arXiv:0808.1431v2`):** For baseline throughput $\lambda_1 = X(1)$, contention $\alpha \equiv \sigma \in [0, 1)$, and pairwise coherency/crosstalk $\beta \equiv \kappa \ge 0$:
   $$C(N) = \frac{X(N)}{\lambda_1} = \frac{N}{1 + \alpha(N - 1) + \beta N(N - 1)}, \qquad N_{\text{opt}} = \sqrt{\frac{1 - \alpha}{\beta}}, \qquad N_{\text{floor}}^* = \left\lfloor \sqrt{\frac{1 - \alpha}{\beta}} \right\rfloor$$
   In `arXiv:0808.1431v2`, synchronous `M/M/1//p` machine-repairman serial fraction $\sigma = \frac{S}{S + Z}$ is established in **Lemma 2 (`§3.2`, Eq. 10)**, while state-dependent `M/G/1//p` service $R(N) = N[S + c(N-1)S]$ equivalence to USL ($\kappa = c\sigma$) is proved in **Theorem 2 (`§4`, Eq. 20–22)**. In `smoeding/usl@4a05e8ee` (`master`, CRAN `v3.0.4`, `R/scalability.R:L106–283`), `peak.scalability()` returns $\sqrt{(1-\alpha)/\beta}$ whereas `optimal.scalability()` returns Amdahl knee $1/\alpha$; in `codahale/usl@513b3f33` (`model.go:L96–98`), `Limitless()` tests `Kappa == 0`.
2. **Algebraic Proof & Source Audit of the Upstream `ThroughputAtLatency` Sign Inversion (`Probe 4`):**
   - By Little's Law $R(N) = N/X(N) = \frac{1 + \sigma(N - 1) + \kappa N(N - 1)}{\lambda_1}$, solving $\kappa N^2 + (\sigma - \kappa)N + (1 - \sigma - \lambda_1 R) = 0$ for $\kappa > 0$ with $\Delta(R) = \sigma^2 + \kappa^2 + 2\kappa(2\lambda_1 R + \sigma - 2)$ yields:
     $$N(R) = \frac{\kappa - \sigma + \sqrt{\Delta(R)}}{2\kappa} \quad \big(\kappa \to 0^+\text{ L'Hôpital limit: } N(R) = \frac{\lambda_1 R - 1 + \sigma}{\sigma}\big), \qquad X_{\text{exact}}(R) = \frac{N(R)}{R} = \frac{\sqrt{\Delta(R)} + \kappa - \sigma}{2\kappa R}$$
   - Direct inspection of `VividCortex/ebooks@969dccbb` (`scalability.tex`, `1,439` lines) shows Eq. 10 (`L878–880`) has the **exact** numerator `\kappa - \sigma + \sqrt{...}`, whereas Eq. 9 (`L677–680`) inverts the sign to **`\sqrt{...} - \kappa + \sigma`**—propagated verbatim into `codahale/usl@513b3f33` (`model.go:L63–66`) and `codahale/usl4j@15ef46d9` (`Model.java:L211–215`).    - For $\kappa > 0$ and $X \in (0, X(N_{\text{opt}}))$, latency is double-valued (`Probe 4` at $X = 4.0, \alpha = 0.04, \beta = 0.006$: sub-knee $R_-(4.0) = 1.2984\text{ s}$ vs. retrograde $R_+(4.0) = 7.7016\text{ s}$). ## Boundaries

- **`known not to apply` — Unconditional Duplicate-Work Suppression, Unbounded $\omega_{\text{tok}}$ Two-Tier Claims, and Unordered Bilateral Advisories:** Suppressing all overlapping claims destroys tail-latency hedging (`hedged_p95_jsq2` `-47.8%` `P99` over `single_jsq2`), 3-version `P99` defect compression (`-93.0%`), and rugged/recombinant pod discovery (`2.88×–2.91×`); intentional redundancy modes require explicit bypass tags. Equal-recall `TwoTier_eq` does **not** dominate across unbounded token-scarcity weights $\omega_{\text{tok}} > 24.11\text{ s/tok}$ (or on sub-second high-recall AST gates at $\omega_{\text{tok}} = 0$), and symmetric two-way conflict notifications without a strict pre-order $\prec_\sigma$ livelock (`CoAgent` `§5.2`, `Probe 1`).
- **`known not to apply` — Extrapolating `IR` Exponential Redundancy Stability or `Random-1` Baselines to Real Agent Fleets:** Zero-cost `Cancel-on-Complete` still blows up at high load when inherent task size $X$ is shared across replicas (`Gardner et al. 2017b` `S&X` `Uniform[1, 2]` at $Z = 0$, `Probe 2A`), static hedge timeouts cause retry storms once queue wait exceeds the timeout, and comparing `RIQ`/`Tied` policies against `Random-1` routing misattributes `JSQ-2` load-balancing gains to task replication.
- **`unsupported` — Unrepaired Upstream `ThroughputAtLatency` for $\sigma \ne \kappa$ and `LatencyAtThroughput` for $\kappa > 0$:** `codahale/usl@513b3f33` (`model.go:L56–66`) and `codahale/usl4j@15ef46d9` (`Model.java:L200–215`) cannot be used unpatched to invert latency-throughput nose curves when $\sigma \ne \kappa$ or $\kappa > 0$, and standard USL rational formulas are invalid outside $0 \le \sigma < 1, \kappa \ge 0, N \ge 1$.
- **`unknown` — Empirical $(\alpha, \beta)$ USL Curves at $N \in [8, 64]$ Concurrent Agents on Production Monorepos:** Published multi-agent repository benchmarks (`CooperBench` up to $N = 4$; `CoAgent` at $N = 2$) have not yet measured empirical $(\hat{\alpha}, \hat{\beta})$ trajectories across $N = 8\text{--}64$ agents under sharded git worktree pods.
- **`not examined` — Post-Detection Merge/Lease Mechanics, Broker Security Cascades, and Byzantine Collusion:** Sunk-progress merge vs. split interventions and clock-skew lease fencing (`agent-collaboration-interventions-fenced-leases-and-concurrency-control`), multi-tier broker prompt-injection cascades (`agent-coordination-topologies-broker-cascades-and-cross-transcript-security`), and Byzantine adversarial voting collusion are outside this report's scope.
