#### 2.2 Axiom footprint, the $\omega$-horizon partial-correctness barrier, and failure of `▷∃` commutation at limit ordinals
`Basis: source + derived + execution`

- **Axiom stratification:** `theories/stepindex.v` (`natI`, lexicographic `pairI`) is purely constructive. `theories/quotient.v:3–7`, `theories/categories/*.v`, and `theories/ordinals/ord_stepindex.v:1–15, 302–322` (Aczel-tree class ordinals `ordI@{i j}` adapted from Transfinite Iris's `set_model.v`) import Rocq's classical and choice axioms: `PropExtensionality`, `FunctionalExtensionality`, `ProofIrrelevance`, `Epsilon` (`constructive_indefinite_description`), and `Classical_Prop` (`classic`), plus `SProp`.
- **Why $\omega$-indexed SGDT/TULIP/GITrees cannot express unbounded-nondeterminism total correctness (`theories/existential_prop/existential_prop.v:87–132`):** Extracting a single global termination step bound $\exists k \in B,\, \forall \alpha \in A,\, P(\alpha, k)$ from horizon-bounded observations $\forall \alpha \in A,\, \exists k \in B,\, P(\alpha, k)$ for downward-closed $P$ is characterized by `Theorem forall_exists_swap` (`lines 87–98`), which requires `(A, R)` to be `regular` **and** `smaller_card B A` ($\neg \exists g : B \to A\text{ surjective}$). #### 3.2 Leibniz OFEs (`OFE.eq_dist'`) as an equational-reasoning bridge
`Basis: source + derived + execution`

In Rocq `iris` and `logsem/gitrees`, `OFE` carries a setoid relation `Equiv` (`≡`) distinct from Coq `=`, requiring pervasive `Proper` instances (`solve_proper`) to rewrite `IT_fold (IT_unfold x) ≡ x` or pure `IT` combinator laws. As verified in `PreOFE.eq_iff_forall_qdist` (`support/TransfiniteAndLeibnizProbe.lean`), porting `gitrees` onto `iris-lean` makes step-0 domain isomorphisms (`IT_fold (IT_unfold x) = x`), pure combinators (`IF True t e = t`), and `reify_vis` native Lean `=` equations rewritable via `rw`/`simp` with **decidable kernel conversion** (`NonExpansive` remains needed only for step-indexed `≡{n}≡`).
