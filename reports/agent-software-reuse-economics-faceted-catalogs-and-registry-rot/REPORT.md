## Findings

### 1. Empirical Root Causes of Reinventing the Wheel: Transaction Costs vs. Attitudinal `NIH` vs. Process Reform

`Basis: documentation + source + execution + derived`

#### 1.1 Group Longevity, Attitudinal `NIH`, and Symmetric Xenophilia

1. **Insular Longevity Decay (`Katz, 1982`, DOI `10.2307/2392547`; `Katz & Allen, 1982`, DOI `10.1111/j.1467-9310.1982.tb00478.x`):** Across `50` R&D project groups (`N = 345` professionals) with independent sociometric communication logs and multi-manager performance ratings, group performance follows an inverted-U over mean group tenure: ramping up below `1.5` years, peaking across `1.5–<5.0` years, and declining past `5.0` years specifically as external technical communication decays in teams lacking active boundary-spanning gatekeepers.
2. **Separating Rational Rejection From `NIH` and Xenophilia (`Antons & Piller, 2015`, *AMP* `29(2):193–217`, DOI `10.5465/amp.2013.0091`; `Hannen et al., 2019`, DOI `10.1016/j.respol.2019.103822`):** True `NIH` is strictly a **suboptimal decision bias** rejecting positive-net-value external assets due to ego-defensive, value-expressive, utilitarian-incentive, heuristic-knowledge, or social-adjustive attitudes; symmetric opposite bias (*Invented Elsewhere* / xenophilia) uncritically adopts high-adaptation-cost external assets that should be built locally. Direct mandates to "stop reinventing" trigger reactance across `4I` learning stages (`Hannen et al., 2019`); effective countermeasures are indirect (boundary rotation, superordinate problem framing, and aligned evaluation criteria, echoed in modular InnerSource portals by `Stol & Fitzgerald, 2015`, *IEEE Software* `32(4):60–67`, DOI `10.1109/MS.2014.77`).

#### 1.2 Cognitive Distance, Conjunctive Reuse Gates, and Parametric Crossover Economics (`Probe P2`)

`Basis: derived + documentation`

1. **Four Cognitive Reuse Tasks & Cost Inequality (`Krueger, 1992`, *ACM CSUR* `24(2):131–183`, DOI `10.1145/130844.130856`; `Mili, Mili, & Mili, 1995`, DOI `10.1109/32.391379`):** Krueger (`pp. 131, 136`) analyzes reuse through *abstraction*, *selection*, *specialization*, and *integration*, arguing that a reuse technique succeeds only when locating, understanding, adapting, and integrating an existing artifact requires less intellectual and operational effort (*cognitive distance*) than writing equivalent software from scratch ($C_{\text{find}} + C_{\text{understand}} + C_{\text{adapt}} + C_{\text{verify}} < C_{\text{scratch}}$). Whenever leaky abstractions force white-box line-by-line inspection of producer code, $C_{\text{understand}} + C_{\text{verify}}$ approaches $C_{\text{scratch}}$.
2. **Seven Sequential Conjunctive Gates & The Awareness Gap (`Frakes & Fox, 1995`, *CACM* `38(6):75–87, 112`, DOI `10.1145/203241.203260`; `Frakes & Fox, 1996`, *IEEE TSE* `22(4):274–279`, DOI `10.1109/32.491652`; `Frakes & Kang, 2005`, DOI `10.1109/TSE.2005.85`; `Ye & Fischer, 2005`, *ASE* `12(2):199–235`, DOI `10.1007/s10515-005-6206-x`):**
   - `Frakes & Fox (1995)` (`113` respondents across `29` organizations) found **no statistically significant effect** of having a reuse repository, CASE tools, or specific programming languages (`Ada`, `C`, `C++`) on code reuse rates.
   - `Frakes & Fox (1996)` model reuse across seven sequential failure gates: (1) *no attempt to reuse*, (2) *part does not exist*, (3) *part is not available*, (4) *part is not found*, (5) *part is not understood*, (6) *part is not valid*, and (7) *part cannot be integrated*. In `Probe P2`, uniform `70%` per-gate survival yields **`0.70^7 = 0.0824` (`8.24%`)** vs. `0.5816` (`58.16%`) at `90%–95%` per gate, and `Gate 1 = 0` collapses survival to `0.0`. `Ye & Fischer (2005, §2.1, p. 202 & p. 204)` formalize why `Gate 1` fails across library space $L_4$ (`2,723` Java 1.4 classes) outside anticipated knowledge $L_3$: *"users cannot ask for help if they are not even aware of the existence of available information."*
3. **Author-Parameterized Expected-Cost & Attitude-Bias Model (`Probe P2`):** Parameterizing search cost $C_{\text{search}}$, availability $p_{\text{avail}}$, context-distance comprehension $C_{\text{und}}(\delta) = c_{\text{und},0}(1 + 3\delta)$, validity $q_{\text{valid}}$, adaptation $C_{\text{adp}}(d_c) = \alpha_{\text{adp}} d_c$, verification $C_{\text{verify}}$, and `[0, 1]`-clamped attitude distortion $\hat{q}_{\text{perc}} = \min(1, \max(0, q_{\text{valid}}/\beta))$ (with $p_{\text{avail}}$ unscaled by $\beta$) yields exact net savings:
   $$\Delta C = C_{\text{scratch}} - C_{\text{obj}} = p_{\text{avail}}\,q_{\text{valid}}\bigl(C_{\text{scratch}} - \alpha_{\text{adp}} d_c - C_{\text{verify}}\bigr) - \bigl(C_{\text{search}} + p_{\text{avail}}\,C_{\text{und}}(\delta)\bigr)$$
   Across `Probe P2`'s five illustrative regimes ($C_{\text{scratch}} = 100$): (i) **raw producer scratchpad** ($\delta = 0.90, p_{\text{avail}} = 0.35, q_{\text{valid}} = 0.50$) has $C_{\text{obj}} = 119.225 > 100.0$ (`net_savings = -19.225`, `deadweight_bias_loss = 0.0`—skipping reuse is objectively optimal); (ii) **sanitized CI-verified card** ($\delta = 0.10, p_{\text{avail}} = 0.85, q_{\text{valid}} = 0.95, d_c = 0.35 < d_c^* = 1.2560$) lowers $C_{\text{obj}}$ to `56.1025` (`56.103`), yielding exact rational savings **`17559/400 = 43.8975`** (`43.898` rounded); (iii) **attitudinal `NIH`** ($\beta = 2.1$) inflates perceived cost to `114.955 > 100.0`, causing suboptimal rejection (`deadweight_bias_loss = 43.897`, differing from `43.898` by a `1`-ULP binary64 tie split around `43.8975`); (iv) **high conceptual distance** ($d_c = 1.40 > d_c^* = 1.2560$) makes scratch build optimal (`106.975 > 100.0`); and (v) **xenophilia** ($\beta = 0.45$ at $d_c = 1.40$) depresses perceived cost to `56.198 < 100.0`, inducing over-adoption (`deadweight_bias_loss = 6.975`).

### 2. Faceted Classification, Intermediary Sanitization, and Federated Catalogs

`Basis: normative + source + documentation + execution + derived`

#### 2.1 Weighted 6-Facet Conceptual Graphs vs. Lexical Drift (`Prieto-Díaz 1987, 1991`, `Probe P4B`)

1. **Overcoming the Vocabulary Problem (`Furnas et al., 1987`, *CACM* `30(11):964–971`, DOI `10.1145/32206.32212`; `Prieto-Díaz & Freeman, 1987`, DOI `10.1109/MS.1987.229789`; `Prieto-Díaz, 1991`, *CACM* `34(5):88–97`, DOI `10.1145/103167.103176`):** Strict single-parent trees fracture on cross-cutting capabilities, while uncontrolled free-text retrieval collapses under producer–consumer synonymy and UI/test/logging lexical false friends. `Prieto-Díaz` indexes each asset by a controlled 6-tuple—internal functionality `(Function, Objects, Medium)` + external environment `(System Type, Functional Area, Setting)`—backed by a synonym thesaurus and a **directed weighted conceptual-distance graph** ($D(Q, C) = \sum_{f=1}^6 \alpha_f d_{\text{graph}}(q_f, c_f)$), achieving a `14%` corporate reuse rate (`38%` active asset utilization) and `\$1.5M` first-year savings in GTE Data Services' Asset Management Program.
2. **Asymmetric Adaptation & Controlled Retrieval Verification (`Probe P4B`):**
   - *Asymmetric 2-hop conceptual distance:* Specializing a general catalog encoder (`deflate -> compress [0.08] -> encode [0.18] = 0.26`) costs strictly less than generalizing an overly specific asset (`encode -> compress [0.34] -> deflate [0.08] = 0.42`).
   - *Lexical parity vs. synonym drift (`12`-entity synthetic catalog: `6` core assets `C01–C06` + `6` UI/mock/reporting distractors `D01–D06`):* On exact-vocabulary queries, `BM25` and `Faceted` tie at `Recall@1 = 1.0000, MRR = 1.0000`. Under synonym drift with surface distractors, `BM25` drops to **`Recall@1 = 0.0000` (`MRR = 0.2146`)** while `Faceted` maintains **`Recall@1 = 1.0000` (`MRR = 1.0000`)**.
   - *Cold-start environment pre-filtering (`12`-entity mechanism reproducer):* When `5`-token queries contain `3` out-of-vocabulary (`OOV`) action/object tokens planted onto cross-`setting` distractors (`D02–D05`) alongside `2` in-vocabulary target tokens, coarse `setting`-facet filtering (retaining $\ge 2$ candidates per bucket) strips cross-environment false friends and restores `Recall@1` from `0.0000` (`pure BM25`) to **`1.0000` (`setting + BM25`)**.

#### 2.2 Producer–Consumer Context Distance and Intermediary Sanitization

### 3. Discretionary-Database Rot, Staleness Quarantine vs. Re-Harvesting, Executable Ratchets, and Broadcast Overload

`Basis: normative + source + documentation + derived + execution`

#### 3.1 Public-Goods Under-Provision vs. Syntactic-Quota `spam` (`Probe P3`)

- **Binary Voluntary Dilemma (`Thorn & Connolly, 1987`, *Comm. Res.* `14(5):512–528`, DOI `10.1177/009365087014005004`; `Connolly & Thorn, 1990`, Sage Ch. 10, `pp. 219–234`, DOI `10.4135/9781483325385.n10`; `Connolly, Thorn, & Heminger, 1992`, in Liebrand, Messick, & Wilke, Eds., *Social Dilemmas*, `pp. 199–208`; `Cabrera & Cabrera, 2002`, DOI `10.1177/0170840602235001`; `Kalman et al., 2002`, DOI `10.1177/0093650202029002002`):** Because decontextualized curation incurs private write cost $c_{\text{curate}} > b_{\text{self}}$ while read benefits $(N-1)b_{\text{peer}}$ are non-excludable, `skip` strictly dominates `curate` (`M1` voluntary equilibrium: `0/12` contributors, welfare `0.0` vs. manual optimum `+492.0` at $N = 12, b_{\text{peer}} = 5.0, c_{\text{curate}} = 14.0$).
- **Derived 3-Action Syntactic-Quota Goodhart Extension (`Basis: derived + execution`, `Probe P3`):** Extending `Connolly & Thorn (1990)` to mandated append-only agent registries (*"every task must append to `REGISTRY.md` or suffer penalty $F = 8.0$"*) introduces low-effort boilerplate `spam` ($c_{\text{spam}} = 1.5 < F < c_{\text{curate}}$, imposing peer read noise $c_{\text{noise}} = 0.8$). Without a semantic CI gate (`M2`), `spam` is **strictly dominant across all $\binom{13}{2} = 78$ peer profiles**, driving fleet welfare to **`-123.6 < 0.0`**. Pairing a semantic CI gate (rejecting `spam`) with selective credit $R = 7.0$ ($R + F = 15.0 > c_{\text{curate}}$, `M3`) restores `curate` dominance (`+492.0` net technical welfare), while **zero-marginal-cost automated CI harvesting** ($c_{\text{post}} = 0, c_{\text{ci}} = 0.4$, `M4`) achieves **`+655.2`**.

#### 3.2 Documentation Drift, Abstraction Bifurcation, and Quarantine vs. Re-Harvesting (`Probe P5B`)

1. **Empirical Staleness Prevalence (`Lethbridge, Singer, & Forward, 2003`, *IEEE Software* `20(6):35–39`, DOI `10.1109/MS.2003.1241364`; `Aghajani et al., 2019`, *ICSE '19*, `pp. 1199–1210`, DOI `10.1109/ICSE.2019.00122`; `Aghajani et al., 2020`, *ICSE '20*, `pp. 590–601`, DOI `10.1145/3377811.3380405`):**
   - Synthesizing `Forward & Lethbridge (2002)`'s `50`-question survey (`N = 48` practitioners) with workplace shadowing (`9` engineers) and usage logs, `Lethbridge et al. (2003)` find that outdated **high-level architectural overviews** remain useful whereas volatile **low-level API/component documentation** is bypassed in favor of source code once stale.
   - From `805,939` mined artifacts across GitHub Issues, PRs, Stack Overflow, and mailing lists, `Aghajani et al. (2019, §III-A.2 & Table II)` set an initial stratified target of `829`, **sampled `49` Stage-1 consensus false positives with replacement** (`829 + 49 = 878` inspected), and **discarded `5` Stage-2 conflict-resolution false positives without replacement** (`829 - 5 = 824` valid multi-labeled artifacts containing `1,548` sentences across `162` leaf types: `485` *What*, `255` *How* [`50%` findability], `134` *Tool Related*, `81` *Process Related*). Within *What*, **`Up-to-dateness` afflicts `190/485 = 39.18%`** of artifacts (`21%` silent runtime divergence), and code examples account for `50%` of *Correctness* (`72` artifacts) defects (`Aghajani et al. 2020` `N = 146 = 78 + 68` practitioners confirming automated CI freshness checks beat manual mandates).
2. **Coupled Two-Cohort `(fresh_live, stale_live)` Belief Cutoff & Catalog-Depletion Proof (`Basis: derived + execution`, `Probe P5B`):** Modeling rational lookup iff belief $b_t > f_{\min} = \frac{c_{\text{lookup}} + c_{\text{stale}}}{v_{\text{fresh}} + c_{\text{stale}}}$ ($c_{\text{lookup}} = 2, v_{\text{fresh}} = 22$):
   - *Manual low-level registry* ($\mu = 0.12, \rho_0 = 0.03, \rho_q = 0.05, c_{\text{stale}} = 18 \implies f_{\min} = 0.5000, \eta_{\text{ci}} = 0$): collapses irreversibly at **`step = 15`** (`14/80` active steps, `final_served_freshness = 0.2000`, `final_trust_belief = 0.4860`), whereas *high-level architectural maps* ($\mu = 0.06, c_{\text{stale}} = 3 \implies f_{\min} = 0.2000$) survive `80/80` steps (`0.5714`).
   - *Pure quarantine without re-harvesting* ($\eta_{\text{ci}} = 0.85, \text{reharvest} = \text{False}$): keeps conditional served freshness at `0.9764 > 0.5000` (`0` trust collapse), **but depletes active catalog mass `fresh_live + stale_live` from `1.0` to `0.000041` (`0.0041%`) by step `80`**, collapsing `Gate 2/3` availability!
   - *Coupled quarantine + automated CI re-harvesting* ($\eta_{\text{ci}} = 0.85, \text{reharvest} = \text{True}$): maintains **`1.000000` (`100%`) active catalog mass** and converges to exact stationary freshness $F^* = 1 - \frac{(1 - \eta)\mu}{1 - (1 - \eta)(1 - \rho - \mu)} = \frac{431}{440} = \mathbf{0.9795}$ (`80/80` steps).

## Boundaries

- **`known not to apply` — Uncalibrated Parametric Thresholds (`Probes P2, P3, P5B`) and Synthetic 12-Item IR Scores (`Probe P4B`):** `Probes P2, P3, and P5B` verify algebraic identities, equilibrium dominance, and two-cohort phase transitions under author-selected functional forms and parameters; numerical thresholds ($d_c^* = 1.2560$, collapse at `step = 15`, peak broadcast at $N = 2$) require calibration to fleet-specific token/defect costs. Likewise, `Probe P4B` (`12` synthetic entities; `OOV` queries retaining `2` in-vocabulary target tokens inside $\ge 2$-item `setting` buckets) is a controlled mechanism reproducer, not a natural-language IR benchmark or a `100%`-OOV zero-lexical-overlap guarantee.
- **`known not to apply` — Assuming Backstage Stitching Prunes Self-Originated Dangling `dependsOn` Edges or Sanitizes `data:` / `vbscript:` URLs:** `Backstage@feeadd4d` `performStitching.ts:103–112` does not filter `relations` by target existence in `refresh_state`, and `performStitching.ts:48–50` rejects only `javascript:` URLs; deployments must layer explicit broken-reference CI checks and stricter URI allowlists.
- **`known not to apply` — Treating `MCP Registry` Publish Validation as Fail-Closed Malware Prevention or Case-Insensitive Tag Blocking:** `modelcontextprotocol/registry@9432a0a9` `validators.go:369, 406–408, 761–783` checks exact lowercase `"latest"`, returns `false` on `""` in `looksLikeVersionRange`, and fails open after `2 s` on OSV lookup timeouts; registries require JSON-schema `minLength` enforcement, case-folded tag policy if desired, and asynchronous post-publish re-scanning.
- **`not examined` — Direct Native Toolchain Execution of Upstream TypeScript/Go/Java/Rego Binaries and Ontology Upfront Cost:** Upstream `Backstage`, `MCP Registry`, `ArchUnit`, and `OpenTelemetry` logic was verified by primary `source` audit and reproduced in Python stdlib (`support/verify_org_reuse_kms_registry_probes.py`) rather than executed under Node.js/PostgreSQL, Go/OSV-API, Maven/JVM, or `opa`/`weaver` binaries. Upfront domain-engineering cost of curating full 6-facet ontologies on tiny short-lived repos ($N \le 2$) and adversarial prompt injection inside poisoned skill manifests (`agent-prompt-injection-tool-security-sandboxing-and-reward-hacking-defenses`) are outside this report's scope.
- **`unknown` — Empirical $f_{\min}$ Abandonment Thresholds Across Frontier Reasoning Models:** While `Lethbridge et al. (2003)` and `Aghajani et al. (2019, 2020)` establish human practitioner staleness avoidance, controlled measurements of the exact stale-hit rate at which LLM tool-use policies stop querying an optional skill/helper catalog remain open.
