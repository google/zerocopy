# Agent Structured Outputs, Constrained Decoding, Programmatic Tool-Calling Schemas, and Computer-Use Grounding

## Summary

Primary-source inspection of 40 research papers (2023–2026, including 17 directly audited primary PDFs), five vendor/benchmark engineering disclosures, eight pinned public repositories (`mlc-ai/xgrammar`, `guidance-ai/llguidance`, `dottxt-ai/outlines`, `ShishirPatil/gorilla`, `xlang-ai/OSWorld`, `browser-use/browser-use`, `microsoft/playwright-mcp`, `microsoft/playwright`), and a deterministic four-probe verification suite establishes five invariants for structured agent outputs, tool-calling interfaces, and computer-use grounding:

1. **Constrained Decoding Degrades Reasoning Only When Scratchpad Tokens, Subword Bridges, or Key Channels Are Confounded:** Catastrophic GSM8K drops under early JSON-schema enforcement (`claude-3-haiku` `86.51% → 23.44%`, `gpt-3.5-turbo` `75.99% → 49.25%`, `Tam et al.`) stem from unordered answer-before-reasoning emission (`100%` of `GPT-3.5-Turbo` `Last Letter` JSON-mode outputs placed `"answer"` before `"reason"`), prompt–schema key wording conflicts (`-12.05 pp` on `Llama-3.2-3B` GSM8K and `-17.80 pp` on `DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B` Math500 when the decoder forces an instructional sentence key against a `"steps"` prompt, vs. `+17.06` / `+22.20 pp` synergy when aligned, `Le 2026`), and naive `k = 0` token-boundary lexing that suppresses **`>95%`** of canonical BPE bridge-token probability mass (`DOMINO`, `Probe 4A`). Enforcing `reasoning`-before-`answer` (`JSONSchemaBench` Table 8: `+3.3` to `+3.7 pp` with `LLGuidance` token healing) or deferring grammar activation behind special-token triggers (`XGrammar` `CompileStructuralTag`; `In-Writing*` intercepting **`<eos>`** for `100%` parse validity, though `CRANE` and vanilla `Constrained` still win specific model–task cells) eliminates reasoning collapse at `< 40 µs/tok` mask latency.
2. **Local Token Masking Distorts Global Posteriors Under Asymmetric Expected Future Grammaticality (`EFG`), While Modern Engines Split on Soundness:** Left-to-right Greedy Constrained Decoding (`GCD`) equals the exact global conditional $Q_{P,G}(w) \propto P(w)\mathbf{1}[w \in L(G)]$ **if and only if** all grammatically alive sibling tokens *with positive LM probability $P(w_i \mid w_{1:i-1}) > 0$* at every reachable prefix share identical EFG $c(w_{1:i})$ (`Park et al.`, `Probe 1C`). Under asymmetric choke points (`Model 1A/1B`), `GCD` oversamples dead-end trap prefixes by `13.5×–4,500.6×` ($D_{\mathrm{KL}} = 0.797\text{–}0.956\text{ nats}$); Sequential Monte Carlo (`SMC`, `Loula et al.`) drives 250-run marginal single-draw $D_{\mathrm{KL}}$ below `0.006 nats` by $M = 16$, **yet single-run particle clouds still suffer support collapse at small $M$** (`Model 1A`: `M = 4` mean single-run `TVD = 0.825` is *worse* than deterministic `GCD`'s `0.550`, and `M = 16` misses at least one positive-support leaf on `100%` of single runs). In production Draft 2020-12 compilation, `mlc-ai/xgrammar@3fe39321` prioritizes non-failing compilation via silent keyword drops and `oneOf → anyOf` relaxation (`38` under-constrained categories in `JSONSchemaBench`), whereas `guidance-ai/llguidance@4b1bdd7e` fails closed on unimplemented keywords and unprovable `oneOf` (`1` under-constrained category).
3. **Programmatic Tool Calling (`CodeAct` / `PTC` / `Code Mode`) Beats Sequential JSON at Multi-Hop and Large-Catalog Scale — Conditional on Return-Shape Stubs and On-Demand Schema Inspection:** Across catalog size $M \in \{42, 200, 2000\}$ (`Rai 2026` Table 6, `n = 10` descriptive), Code Mode cuts valid-regime tokens by **`74.4%–94.8%`** (`4,112` vs. `16,076` at $M = 42$; `7,766` vs. `148,047` at $M = 200$) and sustains `90%` (`11,942` tok) at $M = 2,000$ where Plain MCP overflows (`0%`). Against single-turn parallel JSON (`Patel et al. 2026`), empirical token costs cross over at **`N ≈ 26` calls** (evaluated on BFCL v4 **echo-return stubs**), while parallel JSON recall cliffs are sharply model-specific (`GPT-5` at $N > 13$, `GPT-5.4/5.6` `96.9%–100%` through $N = 48$, `Claude Sonnet 5` at $N = 70\text{–}72$). Critically, omitting author-written **return-shape hints** from stubs drops sealed-split Code Mode by **`-22.7 pp` (`Gemma-4-26B`)** and **`-39.5 pp` (`Nemotron-3-30B`)** (`Rai` Table 9).
4. **Disentangling `I-PTC` Persistence, On-Demand Inspection, and Plain `Bash` Under Equal Turn Budgets:** While stateless monolithic `PTC` exhibits lower per-call tool success (`65.2%` vs. `92.1%` `Bash` on `TheAgentCompany`, `Mak et al. 2026`), `Mao et al.`'s (`2026`) component ablation (`Table 5`) proves that **on-demand schema inspection**—not namespace persistence—drives most of `I-PTC`'s single-query gains (`GPT-5.4 mini`: `Stateless PTC + inspection` reaches **`65.8%` / `40K` raw tokens** vs. `59.1%` / `72K` baseline and `68.0%` / `45K` `Full I-PTC`, whereas **persistent cells alone (`Raw I-PTC`) inflate raw tokens `+79.2%` to `129K`** for `+2.2 pp`). Moreover, **3 of 10 models** (`Claude Opus 4.7`, `Claude Sonnet 4.6`, `Qwen 3.5-397B`) lose accuracy under `Full I-PTC` vs. stateless `PTC`, and plain `Bash` beats `PTC` on score across all 4 `Mak et al.` arms *except* on `GPT-5.5` `TheAgentCompany` cost (`$0.56 / 377k` `Bash` vs. `$0.54 / 242k` `PTC`). Equalizing LLM inference turns (`Probe 2D`, `[toy model]`) shows why: persistent cells beat blind monolithic retries at low per-step reliability ($p = 0.50, T_{\max} = 8$: `+23.3 pp`), whereas strong coders ($p = 0.85, T_{\max} = 6$) finish stateless scripts in `~1.9` turns and outperform multi-cell execution (`98.8%` vs. `92.4%–95.3%`).
5. **High-Resolution GUI Grounding Fails via Head Localization Variance, Sub-Patch Cell Overlap, and Semantic Region Confusion — Resolved by Filtered ARIA IDs on Web DOMs and Native-Scale Cropping / Native RL on Desktops:** On `ScreenSpot-Pro` (`0.07%` mean target area), single-shot `GPT-4o` scores **`0.8%`** (`OS-Atlas-7B` `18.9%`). Exact 2D Gaussian–Uniform convolution geometry (`Probe 3`) proves: (a) `1000`-bin coordinate quantization is *never* the bottleneck (`100.00%` exact uniform hit on `24×24 px` at 4K); (b) coarse generalist collapse (`~2.0%`) is **`97.98%` continuous head localization variance**, whereas even at zero head error ($\sigma_{\text{head}} = 0$), `28 px` merged ViT patches at `1024 px` aspect-preserving width (`105×105 px` 4K cells) hard-cap `24×24 px` uniform overlap at **`5.22%`** (`9.29%` if square-squashed; Gaussian `erf` falsely inflates this `1.72×–3.05×` to `15.96%`); and (c) holding the specialist backbone constant, `1024×1024` native-scale cropping lifts `48×48 px` geometric hit rate from `23.97%` (1440p) / `11.51%` (4K) to `81.08%`, which coarse semantic region-search recall ($R_{\text{sem}} \approx 0.56$) tempers to **`45.40%`** (aligning with `ReGround` `40.2%` and `ScreenSeekeR` `48.1%`). Browser harnesses (`browser-use`, `playwright-mcp`) bypass pixel regression via filtered ARIA/CDP IDs (`17` `CONTROLS` roles, `scale: 'css'`), while desktop SOTA combines Mixture-of-Grounding (`Agent S2` `34.5%` OSWorld) or native multi-turn RL (`UI-TARS-2` `47.5%` GUI-only OSWorld).

## Findings

### 1. Constrained Decoding: Reasoning Expressivity, Distribution Distortion, and Schema Coverage

`Basis: source + derived + execution`

#### 1.1 Resolving the Reasoning-Degradation Paradox (`Tam` vs. `JSONSchemaBench`, `CRANE`, `In-Writing*`, and `Le`)

1. **Answer-First Key Collapse vs. Parse-Error Non-Causality (`Tam et al.`, `2408.02442v3` Tables 1–3, §5.1):**
   - Enforcing schema-constrained JSON on `GSM8K` dropped `claude-3-haiku` from `86.51%` (NL) to **`23.44%`**, `gpt-3.5-turbo` from `75.99%` to **`49.25%`**, and `LLaMA-3-8B` from `75.13%` to **`48.90%`** (`gemini-1.5-flash` stayed flat: `89.33% → 89.21%`). On `gpt-4o-mini` (`Table 2`), OpenAI Structured Outputs (`JSON-Schema`) beat `JSON-Mode` (`91.71%` vs. `86.95%` GSM8K; `86.07%` vs. `76.00%` and `83.11%` NL on `Last Letter`).
   - Crucially, **syntax parse failures did not cause the reasoning drop** (`Table 3`): `LLaMA-3-8B` logged only **`0.148%` JSON parse errors on `Last Letter` despite a `38.15%` accuracy loss** (`GPT-3.5-Turbo` `0.0%` parse error). Instead, in unordered `JSON-mode`, **`100%` of `GPT-3.5-Turbo` `Last Letter` outputs placed `"answer"` before `"reason"`**, eliminating autoregressive scratchpad computation prior to committing to the answer.
2. **Reasoning-First Schemas & Token Healing (`JSONSchemaBench`, `2501.10868v3` §6 Table 8):**
   - When `Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct` is given an ordered schema `{"reasoning": <CoT>, "answer": <type>}` (`reasoning` strictly first), **all four constrained engines (`XGrammar`, `llama.cpp`, `Outlines`, `Guidance`) match or beat unconstrained `LM only`** across all three of `Tam et al.`'s reasoning tasks: `Guidance` (`LLGuidance`) lifts `Last Letters` **`50.7% → 54.0%`**, `Shuffle Objects` **`52.6% → 55.9%`**, and `GSM8K` **`80.1% → 83.8%`** (its `~+3 pp` edge over peer engines stems from subword **token healing** across punctuation boundaries).
3. **Hybrid Unconstrained Reasoning + Triggered Grammar Activation (`CRANE` `2502.09061v4`; `In-Writing` `2601.07525v2` & `Probe 4B`):**
   - **`CRANE` (ICML 2025, Tables 1–2, L326/L415/Listing 2):** Proves via Turing-machine scratchpad simulation that constraining generation from token 0 to a restrictive output grammar $G$ bounds effective Transformer computation depth, whereas interleaving unconstrained reasoning with ASCII `<<` and `>>` delimiters ($r \cdot S_1 \cdot M(x) \cdot S_2$) preserves expressivity (`Qwen2.5-Math-7B` `GSM-Symbolic`: `29% → 38%`, `94%` parse; `DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B`: `13%` Constrained / `21%` CoT $\to$ **`31%`**, `92%` parse; `Llama-3.1-8B` `FOLIO`: `32.02% → 46.31%`, `85.71%` compiles). However, `CRANE` remains vulnerable to (i) **premature delimiter collision** when free-form reasoning emits ASCII `<<` (e.g., bitshifts or quoted templates, `Probe 4B`) and (ii) over-restrictive inner expression grammars (e.g., banning `round()` inside `CRANE` blocks, `In-Writing` App. A.3).
   - **`In-Writing*` (`2601.07525v2` §6.1 L418–423, L456, Tables 3–6, 9, App. A.4.2–A.4.3):** Mitigates premature delimiter triggering by setting **`<eos>` as the unique trigger token**—intercepting the model's natural end-of-response `<eos>` token (NOT `</think>`) before switching into grammar-constrained answer extraction. Because `<eos>` exists on both non-reasoning instruct models (`Llama-3.1-8B`, `Gemma-2-9B`, `Qwen2.5-Coder`) and thinking models and never fires mid-sentence (`Probe 4B`), `In-Writing*` achieves **`100%` parse validity** (`Table 6`) and lifts 8-shot `GSM-Symbolic` (`Table 9`) on `Llama-3.1-8B` (`26%` Constrained / `33%` CRANE $\to$ **`59%`**) and `DeepSeek-R1-Qwen-14B` (`30%` / `38%` $\to$ **`65%`**).
   - **Mandatory `In-Writing*` Boundaries (Tables 3–5, 9 & App. A.4.2–A.4.3):** `In-Writing*` does **not** universally dominate: on `GSM-Symbolic` (`Table 9` L999), **`CRANE` (`31`) beat `In-Writing*` (`30`) on `DeepSeek-R1-Llama-8B`** (`In-Writing*` won 7/8 models); vanilla `Constrained` beat `In-Writing*` on `Qwen3-8B` 0-shot `LastLetter` (`78.4 ± 7.9` vs. `74.0 ± 9.4`, `Table 3` L850), `Qwen3-4B`/`Qwen3.5-2B` `Shuffled Obj` (`81.1 vs. 78.8`, `61.7 vs. 50.5`, `Table 4` L879–881), and `Gemma2-9B` `DDXPlus` (`53.0 vs. 50.1`, `Table 5` L903). Moreover, while `In-Writing` App. A.4.2 (L771–777) notes that `Tam et al.`'s `GPT-4-Turbo` extractor stripped hyphens/spaces on `LastLetter`, **App. A.4.3 (L787–795) confirms `In-Writing*`'s own constrained regex performs the exact same space/hyphen auto-correction**.
4. **Schema-Key Wording as an Independent Non-Additive Instruction Channel (`Le`, AACL-IJCNLP 2026, `2604.14862v3` Eq. 5–7, L225–229, Tables 1–2):**
   - Holding grammar topology fixed, replacing the neutral JSON key `"steps"` with the directive key `"Solve the problem step by step, showing all reasoning and intermediate steps"` acts as a decoder-injected instruction channel $c_s$ that interacts non-additively ($\Delta_{\text{int}} = R_{11} - R_{10} - R_{01} + R_{00}$) with prompt instructions $c_p$:
     - `Qwen2.5-7B` (`GSM8K`): `None (CD)` `81.27%` $\to$ `Key only` **`89.08%`** ($\Delta_{\text{key}} = +7.81^*\text{ pp}$), `Both` `88.10%` ($\Delta_{\text{int}} = -3.87^*\text{ pp}$ redundancy).
     - `Llama-3.2-3B` (`GSM8K`): `None (CD)` `60.50%` $\to$ `Key only` **`48.45%`** (**$\Delta_{\text{key}} = -12.05^*\text{ pp}$** conflict when prompt specifies `"steps"` while decoder forces the sentence key!), `Both` **`68.46%`** ($\Delta_{\text{int}} = +17.06^*\text{ pp}$ synergy).
     - `DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B` (`Math500`): `Key only` crashes **`-17.80^*\text{ pp}`** (`41.80% → 24.00%`), rebounding to **`47.40%`** when `Both` align ($\Delta_{\text{int}} = +22.20^*\text{ pp}$).
   - *Theoretical qualifier (L225–229):* `Le`'s value-level Pinsker bound $|R_{\bar{Q}}(k, x) - R_{\bar{P}}(k, x)| \le \sqrt{\frac{1}{2} D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\bar{Q}_{k,x} \parallel \bar{P}_{k,x})}$ (Eq. 6) is distributional and under greedy decoding serves as a diagnostic rather than a deterministic per-trajectory bound.

#### 1.2 Global Conditional Distortion (`GAD`, `SMC`) and Single-Run Particle Collapse

1. **Exact Zero-KL Necessary & Sufficient Condition (`Park et al.` `2405.21047v4` §3, Fig. 2; `Probe 1`):**
   - For unconstrained LM $P(w)$ and grammar $L(G)$, the true conditional $Q_{P,G}(w_i \mid w_{1:i-1}) \propto P(w_i \mid w_{1:i-1}) \cdot c(w_{1:i})$ weights each next token by its **Expected Future Grammaticality** $c(w_{1:i}) = \sum_{u: w_{1:i}u \in L(G)} P(u \mid w_{1:i})$, whereas greedy local masking (`GCD`) uses $\mathbf{1}[c(w_{1:i}) > 0]$.
   - **Exact Iff Invariant:** $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(Q_{P,G} \parallel P_{\mathrm{GCD}}) = 0$ **if and only if** at every reachable valid prefix, all grammatically alive sibling tokens **with positive LM probability $P(w_i \mid w_{1:i-1}) > 0$** share identical EFG $c(w_{1:i})$ (verified to `< 10^{-12}` in `Probe 1, Model 1C`, which prunes `68%` total dead-end mass *and* includes a $P = 0$ alive sibling with unequal `EFG = 1.0 != 0.40` while maintaining exact `D_KL = 0.000000`).
   - In `Park et al.`'s literal Fig. 2 example (`S ::= 00000 | 1 A_2`, $P(0) = 0.45$/step, $P(\$) = 10^{-8}$), joint mass $P(00000\$) = 0.45^5 \times 10^{-8}$, so prefix `'0'` has EFG $c(0) = 0.45^4 \times 10^{-8} \approx 4.10 \times 10^{-10}$ (and $c(0000) = 0.45 \times 10^{-8}$), yet `GCD` picks `'0'` with `45%+` mass. `ASAp` (`Park et al.`) iteratively refines sound EFG upper bounds across $m = 2{,}000$ samples (`Mistral-7B` SyGuS SSE: `2.259 → 1.242` SLIA, `1.852 → 0.802` INV-BV4, `+38%` geomean correct programs on INV-BV4; `1,588` vs. `12` on `name-combine-4-long`), **but converges slowly and sampled `-73%` fewer correct programs (geomean) on solvable SLIA tasks** where `GCD`'s bias accidentally favored a high-accuracy mode, making `ASAp` unsuitable for single-query serving.
2. **Sequential Monte Carlo (`Loula et al.` `2504.13139v2` Tables 2, 8) vs. Single-Run Particle Support Collapse (`Probe 1`, `[exact analytical / Monte Carlo over stylized prefix trees]`):**
   - `Loula et al.` (`genlm-control`) reweight local `GCD` proposals by incremental survival mass $Z_t = \sum_{a \in \text{valid}} P(a \mid w_{<t})$ with adaptive resampling (`~0.3–31 ms/tok` potential overhead, matching Importance Sampling at `1/10th` the particles and lifting `Llama-3.1-8B` Locally Constrained Decoding from `0.086 → 0.419` Goal Inference, `0.189 → 0.577` Molecular QED, and `0.559 → 0.620` Spider SQL; `0.213 → 0.407` DS-1000 vs. base LM).
   - **Critical Distinction — Marginal Single-Draw Debiasing vs. Single-Run Posterior Support (`Probe 1`):** Evaluating stylized 5-bit grammar `Model 1A` (`|L+(G)| = 17`, `P_true('0') = 0.000122` vs. `P_GCD('0') = 0.550000`, `4,500.6×` oversampling, `D_KL = 0.797260` nats, `TVD = 0.549878`) and union grammar `Model 1B` (`|L+(G)| = 6`, `13.5×` trap oversampling, `D_KL = 0.956466` nats) across `250` SMC replicates exposes a sharp split between *expected single-draw marginal KL* and *single-run particle-cloud fidelity*:

#### 1.3 Subword Bridge-Token Alignment (`DOMINO`) and Production Engine Coverage (`XGrammar` vs. `LLGuidance` vs. `Outlines`)

1. **Byte-Level BPE Bridge-Token Suppression (`DOMINO`, ICML 2024, `2403.06988v1` Tables 2, 4 & `Probe 4A`):**
   - `DOMINO` combines character-level prefix-tree subword alignment (lookahead $k$), opportunistic argmax checking, and grammar-guided speculative decoding ($s = 10$), reaching **`1.77×`** (`Mistral-7B`) and **`1.66×`** (`Llama-2-13B`) throughput *faster than unconstrained decoding* on JSON-schema GSM8K (`Table 2`, where `Llama-2-13B` unconstrained scores `0.262` vs. legacy template-invasive `Guidance`'s `0.152`). In `Table 4`'s 5-shot JSON-schema lookahead ablation (unconstrained baseline `0.155` under that prompt setup), restricting token-boundary lookahead to `k = 0` drops `Mistral-7B` `0.415 → 0.308` and collapses **`Llama-2-13B` `0.155 → 0.0`**, whereas full subword alignment (`k = ∞`) restores **`0.418` / `0.157`**.
   - **True Byte-Level BPE Mechanism (`Probe 4A`, `[exact iterative byte-level BPE + toy model mass check]`):** Training an exact rank-priority byte-level BPE tokenizer (**all 256 single bytes `0..255` preserved intact**, plus 80 learned merges) confirms that **no `0`-token vocabulary deadlock occurs** at JSON punctuation states such as `EXPECT_COLON` because single-byte `':'` (`'}'`, `','`) always survives in the 256-byte base alphabet. Instead, `k = 0` blocks **`8/15` (`53.3%`)** of canonical JSON tool-call BPE tokens (**`100%` of multi-terminal bridges** `{"`, `": "`, `", "`, `": {"`, `"}}`), suppressing **`98.2%` (`>95%`)** of calibrated next-token LM probability mass (`-log Z_t = 4.02` nats OOD split penalty vs. `99.0%` mass retained under `k = ∞`).
2. **Compiler Architecture & Draft 2020-12 Soundness Fault Lines (`JSONSchemaBench` `2501.10868v3` Tables 2–6; pinned source audits):**

#### 2.1 JSON Tool-Loop Failure Taxonomy and Evaluator Quirks (`BFCL`, `ToolSandbox`, `Hammer`)

1. **Multi-Turn State Drift, Parallel Race Hazards & Format Fragility (`BFCL` `PMLR v267:patil25a`; `ToolSandbox` `2408.04682`; `StableToolBench` `2403.07714`; `WorkBench` `2405.00823`; `ToolEmu` `2309.15817`):**
   - Native function-calling (`FC`) modes can degrade parallel calling relative to prompt mode (`BFCL` Table 1: `Claude-3.5-Sonnet-20241022 FC` `3.5%` Parallel AST vs. `70.5%` Prompt; `o1-2024-12-17 FC` `0.0%` vs. `91.5%`), and fine-tuned specialists overfit surface identifiers (`xLAM-7B` perplexity jumps `3.67 → 5.09` on live crowd schemas; `Hammer` `2410.04587v2` mitigates this via randomized identifier masking to reach `83.92%` BFCL v2). On Gorilla's BFCL v4 format-variation study (`[benchmark-blog disclosure]`, 39 models), schema docs consistently rank `JSON > Python > XML` and adding `<TOOLCALL>` wrapper tags collapses overfitted specialists (`CoALM-70B` $\to \sim 0\%$).
   - Conversely, on stateful tools (`ToolSandbox`), larger models (`GPT-4` `69.2%`, `Claude-3-Opus` `74.5%`) score *lower* on `State Dependency` than smaller siblings (`GPT-3.5-Turbo` `82.6%`, `Claude-3-Sonnet` `84.5%`) because frontier models **erroneously emit parallel JSON calls across state-dependent operations**, creating race conditions. Unfiltered 26-tool catalogs in `WorkBench` induce `29%–54%` unwanted DB side effects; `ToolEmu` records `23.9%` severe `GPT-4` safety failures even with safety prompts; and `StableToolBench` shows `55.6%` of 16,464 live RapidAPI endpoints break (`49.2%` unavailable + `6.4%` unauthorized).
2. **Pinned `BFCL` Checker Audit (`ShishirPatil/gorilla@6ea57973`):**
   - `bfcl_eval/eval_checker/ast_eval/ast_checker.py` (646 LOC): normalizes `.` $\to$ `_` for `underscore_to_dot` providers (`L83–90`), enforces 1-level-deep nested type checking (`L93–171`), widens Python `int → float` (`L443–448`), notes a JSON `tuple → list` false-positive caveat (`L436–440`), **strips all spaces and `",./-_*^"` via `standardize_string` (`L174–201`)** (masking delimiter bugs such as `"a_b"` vs. `"a/b"`), and rejects omitted optional parameters unless `""` is explicitly listed in `possible_answer[param]` (`L506–513`).

#### 2.2 Programmatic Tool Calling (`CodeAct` / `Code Mode` / `PTC`): Crossover Regimes and Stub Confounders

1. **Multi-Turn Loops & Production Sandboxing (`CodeAct` `2402.01030v4` Tables 2–3; `CodeTool` `2503.20840v2`; `DynaSaur` `2411.01747v3`; vendor architectures):**
   - On atomic single-tool calls (`API-Bank`), JSON slightly beats `CodeAct` on closed models (`gpt-4-1106-preview`: `82.7%` JSON vs. `76.7%` CodeAct), whereas on multi-step composition (`M3ToolEval`), `CodeAct` wins 12/17 models (`gpt-4-1106-preview`: **`74.4%` in `5.5` turns** vs. `52.4%` JSON in `7.6` turns, `+20.7 pp`; process-supervised `CodeTool` reaches `92.00%` vs. `63.00%` on `RestBench-TMDB`; `DynaSaur` dynamic tool accumulation lifts `GAIA` `29.00% → 38.21%`).
   - `[vendor-reported]` Production deployments isolate LLM-generated orchestration code via **disposable V8 isolates** with `globalOutbound: null` and out-of-band OAuth capability RPC bindings (`Cloudflare Code Mode`) or container-paused async tool dispatch + harness PII tokenization (`Anthropic`: reporting `150K → 2K` MCP tokens via filesystem TS trees, `-37%` tokens on `PTC`, and `72% → 90%` complex parameter accuracy from adding 1–5 `input_examples`).
2. **Parallel Fan-Out Crossover (`N ≈ 26`), Echo-Stub Confounder, and Model Cliffs (`Patel et al.` `2608.06370v1` §§4.2–4.3, §7 & `Probe 2A`):**
   - Evaluating 14 models across $N \in \{7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 20, 30, 48\}$, `Patel et al.` (§4.3 L338) report that **Parallel JSON and PTC token costs cross over empirically at `N ≈ 26` calls** (`N = 30`: `3,380` PTC vs. `3,559` JSON; `N = 48`: `3,535` vs. `5,097`, a `9.9×` flatter post-30 marginal slope of `8.61` vs. `85.44` tok/call; against sequential JSON loops, even an `85%` KV-cache prefix-read discount matching `Mak et al.`'s `80%–94%` cache-read rate already reaches `11,266` cost-equivalent tokens at $N = 26$, `Probe 2A`).
   - **Verbatim Model-Specific Cliffs (NO universal $N > 13$ rule):** Parallel JSON degrades past **`N > 13` on `GPT-5`** (`71.9%` mean vs. `96.9%` PTC across $N = 7 \dots 48$), **holds at `96.9%–100%` through `N = 48` on `GPT-5.4` / `GPT-5.6`**, and only hits its cliff at **`N = 70–72` on `Claude Sonnet 5`** (`100%` at $70 \to 75\%$ at $72 \to 0\%$ at $100$, while PTC holds `100%` at $N = 100$). Conversely, `GPT-4o`, `GPT-4.1`, and `GPT-5.4-mini` drop **`-19.7%` to `-26.9%`** under PTC from emitting literal `\n` escapes in multiline Python, and under 128-schema context flooding, **filesystem-based tool discovery drops `-32.0%`** (vs. `-2.3%` JSON and `+5.5%` PTC).
   - **Critical Methodological Confounder (§7 L490–496, L514–521):** `Patel et al.` evaluate on **BFCL v4 echo-return stubs** (returning call arguments verbatim rather than executing real APIs, with chained values often computed from parametric memory) and note API `tools`-parameter vs. prose system-prompt token-accounting asymmetries—meaning `Patel` **never tests real return-schema parsing**!
3. **Catalog Scaling (`n = 10` Descriptive), Author-Tuned Return-Shape Hints, and Router Failure (`Rai` `2611.19123v1` Tables 6, 9, §4.2, §5.2 & `Probe 2B`):**
   - On `Rai`'s `Table 6` scaling slice (**`Qwen3.8-27B`, `test seed 0, n = 10 tasks, serial — Descriptive only`, L390**): at $M = 42$, Code Mode cuts tokens **`74.4%`** (`4,112` tok / `100%` vs. `16,076` tok / `90%` Plain MCP); at $M = 200$, **`94.8%`** (`7,766` tok / `100%` vs. `148,047` tok / `80%`); at $M = 2,000$, Plain MCP **overflows context (`0%`)** while Code Mode completes **`90%` in `11,942` tokens** (`Probe 2B`, computing ratios strictly on non-overflowing runs).
   - **First-Order Return-Shape Hint Cliff (`Table 9` & §5.2 L460–464):** Removing return-shape hints from code stubs slashed sealed-split Code Mode accuracy by **`-22.7 pp` (`Gemma-4-26B-A4B`)** and **`-39.5 pp` (`Nemotron-3-Nano-30B-A3B`, Holm $p = 0.0003$)**, flipping dev accuracy *below* Plain MCP (`66.4%` vs. `83.1%`; `18.5%` vs. `41.2%`; `Qwen3.8-27B` `-1.7 pp`). **Important qualifier (L460–464):** Those hints were **author-written and tuned on dev data**; how much real MCP servers' `outputSchema` declarations deliver in the wild remains untested. Meanwhile, pre-registered `Router + Code Mode > Code Mode` (`H3`, 238 instances) failed ($p > 0.05$), added `14×` worst-case fallback latency at 2,000 tools, and allowed a single poisoned tool description to hijack prompted-LLM routing.

3. **Equal-Turn-Budget Mechanistic Reconciliation (`Probe 2D`, `[toy model]`):**
   - Enforcing **strict LLM inference turn parity ($T_{\max}$ identical for both Stateless and Stateful)** over a $K = 4$-stage dependent pipeline resolves both sets of observations without hidden turn handicaps:
     - *Low per-step reliability ($p_{\text{step}} = 0.50$, uninspected return schemas, $T_{\max} = 8$):* Blind monolithic scripts fail exponentially ($p^4 = 0.0625$), reaching only **`40.3%`** across 8 full retries (`6.45` turns, `12.1` API calls), whereas checkpointed cells (`4` stage cells + up to `B = 4` retries $\le 8$ turns) reach **`63.7%` (`+23.3 pp`, `6.5` API calls)**. Adding on-demand inspection ($p_{\text{step}}: 0.50 \to 0.82$) lifts *both* to `> 99%` (`99.2%` in `2.19` turns vs. `99.3%` in `4.87` turns).
     - *High per-step reliability ($p_{\text{step}} = 0.85$, strong monolithic coders at tighter turn budget $T_{\max} = 6$):* Stateless `PTC` completes in **`1.89` expected LLM turns** with 5 full-script retries in reserve (**`98.8%`**), whereas decomposing into $K = 4$ sequential cells consumes 4 mandatory turns and leaves only $B = 2$ retry turns, dropping to **`95.3%` (`-3.5 pp`)** even with zero cell drift and **`92.4%` (`-6.4 pp`, `4.70` turns)** under a `3%` per-cell coordination drift—mirroring `Opus 4.7` / `Sonnet 4.6` / `Qwen 3.5-397B` and `Gemini 3.1 Pro`'s latency overhead.

2. **Exact 2D Gaussian–Uniform Convolution Geometry & Honest Variance Attribution (`Probe 3`):**
   - Modeling click offset as independent Gaussian VLM head error $X \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_{\text{head}}^2)$ plus uniform cell rounding $U \sim \mathrm{Uniform}[-\Delta/2, +\Delta/2]$ via the exact closed-form antiderivative $\Phi_{\text{int}}(z) = z\operatorname{erf}(z) + e^{-z^2}/\sqrt{\pi}$ (verified to `< 10^{-7}` against 2,000-point quadrature) isolates three distinct mechanisms:
     1. **Exact Uniform Cell Overlap (`Part 3A`, $\sigma_{\text{head}} = 0$):** On 4K (`3840×2160`) for a `24×24 px` icon, `1000`-bin coordinate quantization ($\Delta = 3.84 \times 2.16\text{ px}$) yields **`100.00%`** deterministic hit (`|err_x| <= 1.92 < 12`).      2. **Generalist Head Variance vs. Sub-Patch Aliasing (`Part 3B`):** For a coarse generalist ($\sigma_{\text{head}} = 0.055 W = 211.2\text{ px}$ on 4K `76.2×76.2 px` `0.07%` target), **head localization error accounts for `97.98%` of total pixel variance** (`2.01%` hit rate vs. `2.05%` if patch stride $\to 0$), whereas patch aliasing (`2.02%` variance share, `52.66%` ceiling on `76.2 px` and `5.22%` on `24 px`) becomes the binding geometric ceiling once specialist training shrinks $\sigma_{\text{head}}$ to sub-patch scales.
     3. **Same-Backbone 1-Stage vs. 2-Stage Crop + Coarse Semantic Recall (`Part 3C`):** Holding a specialist grounder **strictly constant** (`1024 px` aspect-preserving encoder, `14 px` unmerged stride, $\sigma_{\text{enc}} = 14.0\text{ enc-px}$), 1-stage accuracy degrades monotonically across `1080p → 1440p → 4K` on both `24×24 px` icons (`11.51% → 6.65% → 3.02%`) and `48×48 px` widgets (`38.43% → 23.97% → 11.51%`, while hitting **`99.96%`** on `ScreenSpot` v1's `2.01%`-area `204×204 px` negative control). Native `1024×1024` cropping (`1:1` scale) restores geometric hit rate to `34.74%` (`24 px`) and `81.08%` (`48 px`), which coarse Stage-1 semantic region-search recall ($R_{\text{sem}} = 0.56$) tempers to **`19.46%` (`24 px`) and `45.40%` (`48 px`)**—closely tracking `ScreenSeekeR`'s `22.4%` icon and `48.1%` overall scores.
3. **Offline-to-Online Drop, Compositional MoG, and `UI-TARS-2` Action-Space vs. Evaluator Confounders (`Online-Mind2Web` `2504.01382v4`; `Agent S2` `2504.00906v1`; `UI-TARS-2` `2509.02544v2`):**

## Boundaries

- **`unknown` — Wild MCP `outputSchema` Return-Hint Efficacy:** `Rai` (`2611.19123v1` §5.2 L460–464) tuned return-shape hints manually on dev data; how much lift real-world third-party MCP `outputSchema` declarations provide without human curation is unmeasured.
- **`unknown` — `UI-TARS-2` `Online-Mind2Web` Human vs. `WebJudge` Scoring Protocol:** `UI-TARS-2` (`2509.02544v2` Table 1) does not disclose whether `88.2%` (`vs. CUA-o3 71.0%`) was scored by dual human annotators or `WebJudge (GPT-4o)` (`+10.5 pp` inflation in `2504.01382v4` Table 2).
- **`unknown` — Non-Echo Multi-Hop Fan-Out Crossover Under Live APIs:** `Patel et al.` (`2608.06370v1` §7) measured the `N ≈ 26` crossover on BFCL v4 echo-return stubs; exact token crossovers on variable-sized live API payloads depend on response verbosity and harness truncation.
- **`known not to apply` — Naive `0`-Token Byte-BPE Deadlocks, Gaussian `erf` Patch Overlap, and Universal `I-PTC` / `In-Writing*` Dominance:** Byte-level BPE vocabularies retain all 256 single bytes (`':'`, `'}'`, `','`), so `DOMINO` `k = 0` fails via `>95%` canonical-merge probability suppression rather than `0`-token exhaustion (`Probe 4A`); uniform ViT patch quantization follows $\min(1, w/\Delta_x)\min(1, h/\Delta_y)$ (`5.22%` aspect-preserving 4K on `24×24 px`), *not* Gaussian `erf` (`15.96%`, `Probe 3A`); `Full I-PTC` underperforms stateless `PTC` on 3/10 models (`Opus 4.7`, `Sonnet 4.6`, `Qwen 3.5-397B`), and `Raw I-PTC` persistence *without* on-demand inspection increases raw tokens `+79.2%` (`Mao` Table 5); `In-Writing*` triggers on `<eos>` (not `</think>`) and loses specific cells to `CRANE` and vanilla `Constrained` (`2601.07525v2` Tables 3–5, 9).
- **`known not to apply` — Static/Offline Web Scores to Live Websites and `ScreenSpot` v1 to High-Res Desktops:** Static/narrow web suites overstate live `Online-Mind2Web` competence by `23–59 pp`; `ScreenSpot` v1 (`2.01%` mean area) masks high-resolution `0.07%`-area collapse (`99.96%` vs. `3.02%–11.51%`, `Probe 3C`).
- **`unsupported` — Overflow Token-Savings Ratios, Cross-LM `JSONSchemaBench` Table 4 Equivalence, and Vendor Blog Generalization:** Computing percentage token savings at $M = 2,000$ where Plain MCP overflows (`0%`, `Rai` Table 6, `n = 10` descriptive only) is undefined; `JSONSchemaBench` Table 4 open-engine (`Llama-3.2-1B`) vs. `OpenAI`/`Gemini` empirical coverage is non-comparable across differing base LMs; vendor/blog figures (Cloudflare, Anthropic, XLANG `OSWorld-Verified`, Gorilla BFCL v4 blog) are `[vendor-reported]` / `[benchmark-blog disclosure]` without independent third-party re-execution in this package; `Probe 1` (`Model 1A/1B`), `Probe 2D`, and `Probe 4A`'s LM distribution are stylized/parameterized `[toy model]` analyses rather than live LLM runs.
- **`not examined` — Mobile-Only Gesture Trajectories, Audio/Video Real-Time Streaming CUA, and Kernel-Level Container Escape Exploits:** Continuous touch-drag physics on Android/iOS, native audio-video WebRTC agent loops, and Linux kernel namespace breakouts beneath V8/Docker sandboxes were outside scope.
