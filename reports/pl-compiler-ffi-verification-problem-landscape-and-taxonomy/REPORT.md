# Programming Languages, Compilers, Runtimes, FFI, Tooling, and Verification: Unified Problem Landscape and Taxonomy

## Summary

Engineering sound, high-performance, interoperable programming languages, compilers, runtimes, developer tools, and formal verification pipelines confronts two fundamentally distinct classes of difficulty: **historical contingencies** inherited from 1960s–1980s core-RAM, PDP-11/Unix, C psABI, flat-linker, and event-driven Verilog shortcuts, versus **irreducible mathematical, semantic, and physical laws** (undecidability theorems, multi-runtime impossibility triangles, VLSI wire/memory-hierarchy physics, SMT/proof-kernel complexity, and Gödel–Tarski–Löb incompleteness).

This capstone taxonomy maps that landscape across **six orthogonal dimensions**, indexing **38 domain reference packages** and **40 foundational cross-referenced packages** (78 mapped packages in total) in machine-queryable form (`support/taxonomy_ledger.json`, checked and queried by `support/verify_taxonomy_ledger.py`):

1. **Historical contingencies vs. physical & mathematical laws (Dimension 1 — 5 domain packages, 7 cross-refs):** Prose C psABIs, textual `#include` headers, ELF flat symbol interposition, POSIX `fork()` + global signal tables, untagged flat virtual memory, and batch-file-only toolchains are contingent—refuted by Swift witness tables, Multics/Oberon typed linking, Plan 9/Capsicum/Zircon/WASI capability OSes, Smalltalk/Genera/Unison image/content-addressed systems, CHERI ISAv9/Morello/CHERIoT capabilities, and Mill SASOS Turfs/Portals. Register/cache physics, separate-compilation interface contracts, and temporal revocation bandwidth remain irreducible.
2. **Clean-slate PL, type, module, effect, ownership, gradual & metaprogramming hard problems (Dimension 2 — 6 domain packages, 6 cross-refs):** Even without C legacy, designers hit `F_<:` and Java wildcard undecidability, the global-coherence vs. modularity vs. specialization trilemma, multi-shot effect/linearity collisions, borrow-graph vs. value-semantics ergonomics (`Polonius`, `PCG`, `Hylo`, `Swift`, `Austral`, `Pony`/`Verona`), $2^N$ gradual-typing blame cliffs, and hygienic/staged/post-resolution reflection trade-offs (`Racket`, `Lean 4`, `MetaOCaml`, `Zig comptime`, `C++26 P2996`).
3. **Compiler IRs, polymorphism/layout algebras, adaptive JITs, concurrent GCs & async schedulers (Dimension 3 — 5 domain packages, 6 cross-refs):** Mid-level IRs balance graph locality against optimization power (V8's Sea-of-Nodes retreat to `Turboshaft`, hybrid `Graal`, `RVSDG`, `CPS`/join points, `MLIR` erasure cliffs, Cranelift `aegraphs`); generics occupy a monomorphization vs. boxing vs. witness-table triangle complicated by non-injective affine-ceiling DST layout plateaus; dynamic runtimes require sound JIT `FrameState`/virtual-object deopt + OSR, weak-memory-safe GC barriers + derived-pointer stack maps (`gc.statepoint`, `ZGC`, `MMTk`, `Perceus`), and cancel-safe concurrency (`Pin`/`AsyncDrop` vs. Go/Loom/OCaml 5/BEAM fibers).
4. **The four cross-language & cross-runtime impossibility triangles (Dimension 4 — 4 domain packages, 8 cross-refs):** Every polyglot execution and cross-logic verification seam sits inside four irreducible triangles: **(T1) Zero-Copy Memory**, **(T2) Stack & Control Flow**, **(T3) Performance vs. Full-Abstraction & Isolation**, and **(T4) Semantic Unity vs. Verification Diversity** (`DimSum`, `Melocoton`, `Linking Types`, Wasm Component Model, GraalVM Truffle, non-invertible compilation codecs, 2-safety hyperproperties).
5. **Human, specification, diagnostic, debugging, SMT & adversarial kernel hardening walls (Dimension 5 — 6 domain packages, 8 cross-refs):** Scaling is bounded by NP-complete version solving (`PubGrub` vs. `MVS`) and syntactic SemVer unsoundness; `Algorithm W`/`M` blame mislocalization vs. `MUS`/`SHErrLoc`/bidirectional/`Aquascope`; Hennessy's currency problem unifying DWARF, JIT deopt, GC stack maps, and `rr` deterministic replay; `CN`/`Fulminate` runtime separation logic and untrusted counterexample promotion to kernel refutations; `Mariposa` SMT instability and `PUMPKIN Pi` proof repair; and dependent-type kernel cliffs (WHNF spines, `has_fvar` GMP bypasses, unsafe `.olean`/`.vo` deserializers), opaque proof interfaces, and Goodhart-resistant specs + semantic mutation testing.
6. **Exotic compute substrates, accelerators, hardware definition & non-traditional paradigms (Dimension 6 — 12 domain packages, 5 cross-refs):** Mill temporal-SSA belts, EDGE/CGRA/wafer-scale/BSP/Tensix/Groq engines, `CuTe` $(\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{F}_2)$ layout/swizzle algebras, Hopper/Blackwell `TMA`/`mbarrier`/`TMEM` & GPU scoped weak memory (`PTX`/`Vulkan`/`WGSL`/`Descend`), `P4` switches & post-IEEE numerics (`OCP MXFP`, `FP8 E4M3FN`, `IEEE P3109`, `Posit`/`Quire`, stochastic rounding), typed HDLs (`Kôika`/`Filament`/`Calyx`/`CIRCT`/`ACT`) + verified SW↔HW MMIO/DMA/AXI/DPR FFI, Datalog/`egglog`/`DBSP`, reverse-mode AD (`Enzyme`/`Dex`), PPLs (`QBS`/`Hakaru`/`Stan`/`Dice`/`Gen`), choreographies/MPST/CALM, synchronous real-time/WCET (`Esterel`/`Vélus`/`Rattus`), and ZK/`Move`/quantum (`Silq`) conservation laws collapse onto six reusable abstractions.

## Findings

### 1. Dimension 1: Historical Contingencies vs. Irreducible Physical & Mathematical Laws

Basis: normative + documentation + source + derived

Many entrenched pain points in compilers, FFI layers, and OS runtimes stem from freezing 1960s–1980s resource-scarcity conventions—64 KiB core RAM, PDP-11 flat words, textual macro preprocessors, and single-threaded Unix processes—into universal binary contracts. Table 1 distinguishes **refuted historical contingencies** from **irreducible physical or mathematical laws**.

| Layer | Historical Contingency | Refuting Precedents | Irreducible Constraint | Domain Package |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ABI** | Prose C psABIs (SysV AMD64, AAPCS64, Win64), NUL strings, ad-hoc struct classifiers | `Swift` value witness tables, `Wasm` WIT Canonical ABI, `Go` register ABI, executable ABI specs | Register-vs-memory latency, architectural alignment, and a shared binary contract for separate compilation | `c-abi-stack-conventions-and-process-interface-archaeology` |
| **Linking** | Textual `#include` headers, untyped `.o` symbol strings, ELF flat symbol interposition | `Multics` dynamic segments (1968), `Oberon` fingerprinted symbols, `Mach-O` two-level namespaces, `Wasm` components, `Unison` hashes | Typed binary interface descriptors, relocation resolution, and deterministic initialization order across module graphs | `object-files-linkers-loaders-and-separate-compilation-history` |
| **OS boundary** | `fork()` COW cloning, process-global `sigaction` tables, ambient paths, flat unreserved VM | `Plan 9` `rfork`/namespaces, `FreeBSD Capsicum`, `Fuchsia Zircon` handles, `WASI`, completion queues (`io_uring`) | Kernel privilege isolation, explicit address-space reservation, and asynchronous event delivery | `posix-process-signals-fork-and-flat-address-space-contingencies` |
| **Live systems** | Plain-text files + batch CLI compilers as the sole program/state substrate | `Smalltalk-80`/`Pharo Spur`, `Symbolics Genera` (3600), `Mesa`/`Cedar`, `Project Oberon`, `Unison` content-addressed AST store | Persistent live schema evolution, reproducible artifact identity, and cross-ecosystem interop | `image-based-and-single-language-systems-smalltalk-lisp-oberon` |
| **Hardware pointers** | Untagged flat 64-bit integer pointers with coarse 4 KiB page-table protection | `CHERI` ISAv9 (`cheri-architecture.tex`), `Arm Morello`, `CheriABI`, `CHERIoT`, `Mill` SASOS Turfs/Portals/PLBs/WKRs | Parallel-pipeline checks (avoiding `iAPX 432` serial microcode indirection), compressed bounds (`CHERI Concentrate`), and temporal revocation bandwidth (`Cornucopia Reloaded`) | `capability-hardware-tagged-architectures-and-cheri-abi-co-design` |

Key Dimension 1 invariants:

- **Prose C psABIs silently diverge across compilers:** Direct comparison of `rust-lang/rust@b0b600668b` (`compiler/rustc_target/src/callconv/mod.rs`, `compiler/rustc_abi/src/layout.rs`) with Clang, GCC, and Cranelift (`abi-cross-testing-matrix-rustc-llvm-cranelift-gcc-clang`, `tier1-calling-conventions-fnabi-and-llvm-lowering`) documents silent ABI disagreements on `u128`, `f128`, 4-float HFAs, bit-fields, ZSTs, and `bool` zero-extension because LLVM IR omits target-independent ABI types and psABI manuals are ambiguous prose.
- **Flat linking and `fork()` break multi-runtime coexistence:** ELF flat interposition permits duplicate vtables and silent symbol shadowing (`rustc-dynamic-linking-loader-contracts-and-code-signing`), while `IEEE Std 1003.1-2024` (POSIX.1-2024, DOI `10.1109/IEEESTD.2024.10555529`) preserves `fork()` over multi-threaded runtimes—where cloning only one thread wedges mutexes, allocators, and GC threads (Baumann et al., HotOS 2019, DOI `10.1145/3317550.3321435`)—and exposes a single process-wide signal table (`libjsig` chaining fragility; `os-syscall-interfaces-asynchronous-signals-and-environment-models`).
- **Parallel-check law for capability hardware:** Contrasting `Burroughs B5000`, `Symbolics Ivory`, and `Intel iAPX 432` against `CTSRD-CHERI/cheri-specification@845c973538` (`cheri-architecture.tex`), `Arm Morello`, `CHERIoT`, and `Mill` Turfs/Portals establishes that hardware capabilities achieve low single-digit overhead (~4–5 cycle Mill Portal transitions) when bounds/tag checks execute *in parallel* with address generation and cache lookup, failing when indirection sits *serially* on memory critical paths. Spatial bounds compress into 128 bits (`CHERI Concentrate`), leaving **temporal heap revocation** (`Cornucopia Reloaded`) and **cross-domain DMA/IOMMU revocation** as the remaining hardware bottlenecks.

### 2. Dimension 2: Clean-Slate PL, Type, Module, Effect, Ownership, Gradual & Metaprogramming Hard Problems

Basis: normative + source + derived

Even on a greenfield language free of C/POSIX legacy, six core mathematical and feature-interaction walls persist:

1. **Subtyping × Polymorphism × Dependent-Type Decidability Cliffs (`type-system-interactions-subtyping-polymorphism-and-decidability`):** Bounded quantification with contravariant subtyping (`F_<:`) is undecidable (Pierce 1994, DOI `10.1006/inco.1994.1055`), as are Java wildcard generics (Grigore 2017) and higher-order unification. Higher-rank impredicative polymorphism and GADTs destroy principal types under Hindley–Milner inference, forcing constraint heuristics (`OutsideIn(X)` in GHC, DOT approximations in Scala 3) or polar algebraic subtyping (`MLsub`/`Simple-sub`). Quantitative dependent erasure (`Idris 2`) and liquid refinements (`Flux`) separate an expressive surface elaborator from a tiny decidable core checker, as in `leanprover/lean4@0171678607` (`src/kernel/type_checker.cpp`).
2. **Global Coherence vs. Separate Modularity vs. Sound Specialization (`coherence-orphan-instances-and-module-system-compilation`):** Ad-hoc polymorphism faces a strict trilemma among *global coherence* (unique canonical dictionaries preserving `Ord`/`Hash` collection invariants across crate merges), *local modularity* (permitting downstream alternative instances without orphan bans), and *open-world separate compilation*. Rust enforces global coherence via orphan rules; ML modules (`1ML`, applicative vs. generative functors, recursive-module "double vision") choose modularity via explicit module values. Meanwhile, Rust `specialization` fails soundness whenever erased lifetimes affect dispatch, violating parametricity (`verified-rust-frontend-approaches-trait-solver-polonius-and-thir-lowering`).
3. **Algebraic Effects, Handlers, Coeffects, and the Linearity Collision (`algebraic-effects-handlers-coeffects-and-capability-tracking`):** Multi-shot delimited continuations break linear and affine resources: resuming a captured continuation twice duplicates unique handles, locks, or allocations unless shot counts are bounded statically by graded/coeffect types. Systems split across row-polymorphic evidence passing (`Koka`), lexical capability/capture checking (`Effekt`, `Scala 3`), and untyped single-shot fibers (`ocaml/ocaml@d921d1e86c`, `runtime/fiber.c`, which removed continuation cloning in Multicore OCaml and aborts when effects cross foreign C frames). Handler nesting order is observationally non-commutative.
4. **Ownership, Regions, Must-Linearity, and Safe Mutability (`ownership-linearity-regions-and-safe-mutability-design-space`):** Five paradigms trade expressiveness against annotation and aliasing complexity: (i) *lexical stack regions* (`Tofte–Talpin`, `Cyclone`), vulnerable to coarse region retention without storage-mode analysis; (ii) *flow-sensitive borrow graphs* (`Polonius`, Place Capability Graphs `PCG`), strained by self-referential `Pin<P>`, partial borrows, and modular dynamic protectors (`rust-aliasing-models-stacked-tree-minirust-and-llvm-attributes`, `tree-borrows-two-stage-protector-release-and-reborrow-contracts`—Stacked Borrows and Tree Borrows are incomparable and neither yet composes with weak memory); (iii) *Mutable Value Semantics* (`Hylo`, `Swift` `~Copyable`/`~Escapable`), simplifying aliasing by eliminating general references; (iv) *must-linearity* (`Austral`, `Linear Haskell`), confronting the *unwind paradox* where panics must either abort or discharge linear obligations; and (v) *reference capabilities & region isolation* (`Pony`, `Verona` `cowns`).
5. **Gradual Typing Blame Polarity and the $2^N$ Cliff (`gradual-typing-sound-interoperability-and-blame-performance-cliffs`):** Complete monitoring and blame soundness require higher-order proxy wrappers (chaperones/impersonators) that trigger $10\times$–$100\times$ slowdowns across $2^N$ typed/untyped module lattices (Takikawa et al., POPL 2016, DOI `10.1145/2837614.2837630`). Space-efficient coercions, threesomes, and collapsible contracts cap pathological proxy chains, while *transient* (`Reticulated Python`) and *concrete* (`Nom`) semantics trade higher-order blame or structural flexibility for speed—defining the **Soundness vs. Speed vs. Structural Flexibility Triangle**.
6. **Metaprogramming Hygiene, Staging, and Static Reflection (`metaprogramming-hygiene-staging-and-compile-time-execution`):** Inspecting `google/zerocopy@ec133ce4d7` (`zerocopy/zerocopy-derive/src/lib.rs`) alongside `lean-elaboration-hygiene-positions-and-infotree` shows why untyped token-stream proc-macros are structurally unsound for verification-critical derives: attribute-macro ordering lets downstream macros mutate layouts after an `unsafe impl`, re-spelling type tokens fails under nondeterministic macros, raw identifiers (`#[r#repr(packed)]`) bypass token parsers, and missing def-site hygiene causes name collisions. Principled alternatives—**sets-of-scopes hygiene** (`Racket`/`Turnstile`), **typed elaborator reflection** (`Lean 4`, `Idris`), **multi-stage programming** (`MetaML`, `BER MetaOCaml`, `2LTT`), **phase-collapsed CTFE** (`Zig comptime`), and **post-resolution static reflection** (`C++26 P2996`)—must still navigate cross-target CTFE determinism (`scoped-exportlist-invalidation-slicing-and-proc-macro-determinism-sandbox`) and incremental IDE invalidation.
- **Cross-referenced corpus packages (6):** `verified-rust-frontend-approaches-trait-solver-polonius-and-thir-lowering`, `rust-aliasing-models-stacked-tree-minirust-and-llvm-attributes`, `tree-borrows-two-stage-protector-release-and-reborrow-contracts`, `rust-reference-layout-guarantees-target-abi-limits-and-semver-snares`, `scoped-exportlist-invalidation-slicing-and-proc-macro-determinism-sandbox`, `lean-elaboration-hygiene-positions-and-infotree`.

### 3. Dimension 3: Compiler IRs, Polymorphism/Layout Algebras, Adaptive JITs, Concurrent GCs & Async Schedulers

Basis: source + documentation + derived

Lowering language semantics onto dynamic and static execution engines exposes five structural engineering walls:

1. **Compiler IR Design and the Sea-of-Nodes Retreat (`compiler-ir-architectures-sea-of-nodes-rvsdg-cps-and-mlir`):** Click's Sea-of-Nodes unifies data and control graphs (`openjdk/jdk` HotSpot C2; hybrid fixed/floating in `oracle/graal`), yet V8 (`v8/v8@e88118f63d`) migrated from TurboFan's Sea-of-Nodes to **Turboshaft** (block-argument SSA CFG) due to poor graph cache locality, complex effect chains, and late-scheduling brittleness. `RVSDG` encodes structured acyclic regions and explicit state edges at the cost of restructuring irreducible CFGs; GHC Core join points establish CPS/SSA block-argument equivalence; Cranelift **acyclic e-graphs (`aegraphs`)** collapse scalar phase-ordering loops (`equality-saturation-with-proofs-egraphs-aegraphs-and-refinement-rewriting`); and `MLIR` (`llvm/llvm-project@ae38cd5393`, `mlir/docs/LangRef.md`) preserves high-level dialects at the risk of *semantic-erasure cliffs* from unchecked speculatability/memory traits (`mechanized-compiler-ir-semantics-poison-provenance-and-machine-frames`, `verified-and-validated-compiler-optimization-precedents`).
2. **Polymorphism Compilation, Layout Algebras, and Non-Injective Metadata (`polymorphism-compilation-layout-algebras-and-abi-resilience`):** Generic compilation occupies a three-way triangle: **monomorphization** (fast unboxed code, binary bloat, no ABI resilience), **uniform boxing** (separate compilation, heap/cache tax), and **Swift Value Witness Tables / GHC levity polymorphism** (unboxed ABI-resilient generics passing runtime size/stride/copy/destroy witnesses). In systems layout algebras (`google/zerocopy@ec133ce4d7`, `zerocopy/src/layout.rs`), nested packed/aligned trailing-slice DSTs follow $S(n) = C + \mathrm{round\_up}(r + nE, A)$, producing **non-injective plateaus** where multiple counts $n$ share one byte size; naive length-to-metadata inversion yields out-of-bounds slices. Modeling unset layout choices as **reference-admissible demonic nondeterminism** separates portable proofs from target-pinned refinements.
3. **Speculative JITs, Deoptimization, and OSR Soundness (`jit-compilation-speculative-optimization-and-deoptimization-soundness`):** Adaptive JITs (`v8/v8@e88118f63d`, `src/deoptimizer/deoptimizer.cc`; `openjdk/jdk@d65bc4b262`, `src/hotspot/share/runtime/deoptimization.cpp`; `oracle/graal@b5d67b167c`; `PyPy`; `LuaJIT`; `CPython` copy-and-patch) share one soundness protocol: inline-cache guards, global assumption/protector invalidation, **FrameState reconstruction** of inlined interpreter frames, **virtual-object rematerialization** (including cycles) for partial escape analysis, and **OSR** loop entry. Mechanized JIT proofs (`CoreJIT`, `FM-JIT`, eBPF JITs in `ebpf-certificate-verifying-kernels-and-verified-jits-2014-2026`) verify sequential small-language speculation/deopt, far short of concurrent multi-tier protector invalidation (`compiler-correctness-theorem-shapes-and-end-to-end-chains`).
4. **GC Barriers, Safepoints, and Compiler–GC Invariants (`garbage-collection-barriers-safepoints-and-runtime-invariants`):** Moving GCs require precise stack maps tracking *derived interior/exterior pointers* (`llvm/llvm-project@ae38cd5393`, `llvm/lib/Transforms/Scalar/RewriteStatepointsForGC.cpp`) plus bounded **Time-To-Safepoint (TTSP)** via loop strip mining (`HotSpot C2`) or async preemption (`golang/go@b496a3044e`, `src/runtime/proc.go`). Concurrent collectors (`ZGC` colored-pointer self-healing load barriers, `G1` SATB, `Go` hybrid Dijkstra+Yuasa write barrier, `OCaml 5` concurrent major GC, `MMTk`) demand weak-memory-ordered barriers to preserve tri-color invariants. Compile-time reference counting with functional reuse (`Perceus`, Lean 4 LCNF `RC`/`ResetReuse`) achieves pause-free deterministic reclamation on acyclic graphs while requiring fallback handling for cycles and foreign roots.
5. **Async Runtimes, Coroutines, Schedulers, and Cancellation (`async-runtimes-coroutines-schedulers-and-cancellation-semantics`):** Stackless poll state machines (`Rust` `Future`/`Pin`/`!Unpin`, `rustc-statetransform-coroutines-async-gen-and-pin-mir-semantics`) minimize per-task memory at the cost of function coloring and synchronous `Drop` cancellation ("cancel safety" bugs and unresolved `AsyncDrop` unwinding). Stackful movable/segmented tasks (`Go`, `OpenJDK` Loom virtual threads, `OCaml 5` fibers, `BEAM` reductions) avoid coloring yet force OS-thread switching or carrier pinning at FFI seams. Work-stealing deques risk LIFO-slot starvation unless bounded by cooperative budgets (`Tokio`) or preemptive reduction/signal preemption (`BEAM`, `Go`); structured nurseries (`Trio`, `Kotlin`, `Eio`) enforce lexical lifetimes.
- **Cross-referenced corpus packages (6):** `mechanized-compiler-ir-semantics-poison-provenance-and-machine-frames`, `verified-and-validated-compiler-optimization-precedents`, `equality-saturation-with-proofs-egraphs-aegraphs-and-refinement-rewriting`, `compiler-correctness-theorem-shapes-and-end-to-end-chains`, `ebpf-certificate-verifying-kernels-and-verified-jits-2014-2026`, `rustc-statetransform-coroutines-async-gen-and-pin-mir-semantics`.

### 4. Dimension 4: The Four Cross-Language & Cross-Runtime Impossibility Triangles

Basis: normative + source + derived

Cross-language interoperability and cross-logic verification are governed by **four structural impossibility triangles** (Table 2). Every polyglot runtime, bridge generator, or heterogeneous verification framework can satisfy at most two vertices of each triangle and must explicitly budget the cost on the third.

| ID & Triangle | Mutually Incompatible Triple | Structural Mechanism | Concrete System Resolutions | Primary Domain Packages |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **T1: Zero-Copy Memory** | (A) Direct zero-copy raw pointer/slice sharing; (B) Moving/compacting GC or independent non-coherent reclamation; (C) Deadlock-free, handle-free isolation without pinning stalls or cross-heap cycle leaks | Unpinned raw pointers dangle across relocation; pinning causes `GCLocker` stalls, fragmentation, or `Pinner` bounds; isolated GCs leak cross-heap reference cycles | **Wasm Component Model** drops (A) (Canonical ABI copies + handles); **GraalVM Truffle** drops (B) (unified JVM heap); **Panama FFM / HPy / OCaml shadow roots / Go `Pinner`** drop (C); **CHERI / Linear DMA+IOMMU** hardware-enforce bounds & handoff | `multi-runtime-ffi-gc-interop-handles-and-shared-address-clashes`, `garbage-collection-barriers-safepoints-and-runtime-invariants`, `wasm-component-model-truffle-graal-and-polyglot-interop-platforms` |
| **T2: Stack & Control Flow** | (A) Arbitrary synchronous re-entrant calls ($A \to B \to A$); (B) Movable/segmented stacks, stackless async state machines, or multi-shot delimited continuations; (C) Zero-overhead C ABI unwinding (`.eh_frame`/SEH), linear RAII, and signal safety | C frames assume contiguous pinned stacks, linear return/unwind paths, and global `sigaction` dispositions—clashing with stack relocation (`Go`), fiber/effect capture (`OCaml 5`), and personality mismatches (`rust_eh_personality`) | **Go `cgo`** switches to OS `g0` stack (pays handoff cost); **OCaml 5** restricts effects to single-shot and bans crossing C frames; **Swift / Rust `C-unwind`** constrain unwinding or pass typed errors | `c-abi-stack-conventions-and-process-interface-archaeology`, `algebraic-effects-handlers-coeffects-and-capability-tracking`, `async-runtimes-coroutines-schedulers-and-cancellation-semantics`, `multi-runtime-ffi-gc-interop-handles-and-shared-address-clashes` |
| **T3: Performance vs. Full-Abstraction & Isolation** | (A) Native cross-boundary speed (inlining, unboxing, register passing); (B) Sound gradual/contract monitoring + blame or full-abstraction/hyperproperty preservation; (C) Modular isolation across untrusted components | Higher-order proxies trigger $10\times$–$100\times$ Takikawa cliffs; cross-language ThinLTO mixes Rust `noalias`/`noundef` with C TBAA into unsound IR; shared-heap JIT specialization (`Truffle`) collapses isolation | **Typed Racket / Collapsible Contracts** pick (B)+(C); **Truffle / Cross-Language ThinLTO** pick (A) over (C)/(B); **Wasm Components** pick (C); **CHERI / Mill Portals** shift the hardware Pareto frontier for (A)+(C) | `gradual-typing-sound-interoperability-and-blame-performance-cliffs`, `wasm-component-model-truffle-graal-and-polyglot-interop-platforms`, `capability-hardware-tagged-architectures-and-cheri-abi-co-design` |

### 6. Dimension 6: Exotic Compute Substrates, Accelerators, Hardware Definition & Non-Traditional Paradigms

Basis: normative + documentation + source + derived

Across all 12 domains, every exotic substrate and paradigm collapses onto **six orthogonal, composable PL/verification abstractions**: (1) *explicit spatial/memory-hierarchy placement*, (2) *timeline/phase/latency types*, (3) *capability/portal/linear ownership boundaries*, (4) *algebraic layout & indexing morphisms*, (5) *fine-grained provenance & speculative value lattices*, and (6) *deterministic, resource-bounded & bijective execution disciplines*.

- **Non-Von-Neumann CPUs, Dataflow, CGRAs, Tile-BSP & Reversible Engines (`mill-edge-dataflow-and-tile-bsp-compiler-architectures`):** **Mill CPU** replaces register files with a temporal-SSA **Belt** (`conform`/`pick`), inline `None > NaR > Value` metadata, phased `RETIRE`/`smear` loops, hardware spiller/`IZM`, and `genAsm → conAsm` specialization. **EDGE/TRIPS** execute block-atomic hyperblocks; **CGRAs** (`Plasticine`, `Spatial`, `SambaNova RDU`), **Cerebras WSE** (`CSL` wavelets), **Graphcore IPU** (`Poplar` BSP), **Tenstorrent Tensix** (`TT-Metalium` dual NoCs), and **Groq TSP/LPU** (cycle-deterministic VLIW) eliminate coherent caches; **PIM** (`UPMEM`, `HBM-PIM`) computes inside DRAM; and **reversible ISAs** (`Janus`, `Pendulum`/`PISA`) enforce bijective execution.
- **Tensor Layout Algebras, Scratchpad DMA & User-Schedulable Compilers (`tensor-layout-algebras-scratchpad-dma-and-user-schedulable-accelerator-languages`):** `NVIDIA/cutlass@dc6ae46eb5` (`media/docs/cpp/cute/01_layout.md`) and categorical formalizations model **CuTe** hierarchical `(Shape, Stride)` morphisms with exact admissibility preconditions and $\mathbb{F}_2$ XOR-swizzles forming hybrid $(\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{F}_2)$ algebras for algebraic 32-bank SMEM conflict proofs. **Google TPU** (`Pallas`/`Mosaic`) exposes `VMEM` scratchpads and async DMA semaphores; **Triton** compiles block-SPMD via `LinearLayout`; **Futhark** pairs uniqueness types with incremental flattening; and **exocompilation languages** (`Halide`, `ATL`, `Exo`/`Exo 2`, `Rise`/`Elevate`) separate verified algorithm semantics from schedule rewrites.

## Boundaries

Per `FORMAT.md`, negative space is partitioned across four categories:

- **not examined:** This capstone package does not re-execute the 38 individual domain probe scripts or exhaustively survey specialized niche ecosystems tracked in separate corpus reports (e.g., COBOL/MUMPS/Fortran enterprise legacy ABIs, analog/neuromorphic/molecular post-CMOS compilers, optical interconnects, or domain-specific CAD/text-shaping/audio-DSP pipelines). Undocumented proprietary compiler/accelerator internals (closed GPU SASS schedulers, proprietary commercial HLS engines, closed switch ASICs) were not directly inspected.
- **unknown:** All **30 open research problems (`OP-01`–`OP-30`)** in Table 4 and `support/taxonomy_ledger.json` remain genuinely unresolved as of `2026-11-19`. In particular, it is unknown whether one operational memory model can cleanly unify `Tree Borrows` protectors, relaxed weak-memory concurrency (`RC11`/`Promising 2.1`), and hardware capabilities (`OP-09`); whether complete-monitoring gradual typing can eliminate $2^N$ cliffs below $2\times$ worst-case slowdown (`OP-10`); and whether compositional 2-safety hyperproperty preservation scales across heterogeneous open-world FFI links (`OP-20`).
- **known not to apply:** Five widely held generalizations are directly falsified by indexed precedents:
  1. *"C psABIs and flat string-matching linkers are inevitable costs of separate compilation"* — falsified by `Swift` value witness tables, `Wasm` WIT Canonical ABI, `Multics`, `Oberon`, and `Mach-O` two-level namespaces.
  2. *"LLVM IR is a universal neutral compiler spine"* — falsified by Rust provenance/`noalias` miscompilations, `Mill` temporal-SSA belts, `Blackwell` warp specialization, `CuTe` XOR-swizzles, `P4` SALUs, `Filament` timelines, and `Kôika` ORAAT rules.
  3. *"1-safety trace refinement suffices to preserve security properties"* — falsified by 2-safety constant-time and `zeroize` dead-store-elimination counterexamples.
  4. *"Passing positive proof suites certifies TCB operational-semantics soundness"* — falsified by semantic mutation experiments where positive suites pass 100% of single-site guard-weakening mutants without negative canaries.
  5. *"Hardware capability checking inherently causes severe performance collapse (as on Intel iAPX 432)"* — falsified by `Arm Morello`, `CHERIoT`, and `Mill` parallel-pipeline designs.
- **unsupported:** Stock upstream systems explicitly do not support: (i) multi-shot continuations crossing foreign C frames in `OCaml 5` (`runtime/fiber.c`); (ii) memory-safe deserialization of untrusted `.olean` compacted regions in `leanprover/lean4` (`src/runtime/compact.cpp`); (iii) target-independent ABI lowering types in stock LLVM IR (`llvm/llvm-project`); and (iv) general sound specialization over lifetime-parameterized traits in stable `rust-lang/rust`.
