# Agentic Hardware RTL Synthesis, 4-State Testbench Optimism, Formal Equivalence Checking, and PPA Closure

## Summary

Primary-source synthesis across 17 hardware RTL generation and optimization studies (2023–2026), IEEE Std 1364-2005 and IEEE Std 1800-2023, line-level source audits of 6 pinned open-source repositories (`hkust-zhiyao/RTLLM`, `NVlabs/verilog-eval` v1/v2, `NVlabs/cvdp_benchmark`, `YosysHQ/yosys`, `YosysHQ/sby`), and deterministic execution of the preserved Python 3.10+ verification suite (`scripts/verify_hardware_rtl_equivalence.py`) establish three governing invariants for agentic Register-Transfer Level (RTL) synthesis, verification, and Power-Performance-Area (PPA) optimization:

1. **4-State `{0, 1, X, Z}` Testbench Optimism and Harness Bypass Surfaces (`IEEE 1364-2005`, `IEEE 1800-2023`, `RTLLM@51ed553d`, `VerilogEval@4fa0ac4e/@c498220d`, `Probe 1`):** Under IEEE Std 1364-2005 (§5.1.8–5.1.13, §9.4) and IEEE Std 1800-2023 (§6.11, §11.4.5, §11.4.7, §11.4.9, §11.4.11, §12.4), logical equality (`==`, `!=`) and negation (`!`) evaluate unknown (`1'bx`) and high-impedance (`1'bz`) operands to `1'bx`, while procedural `if (cond)` treats `1'bx` and `1'bz` identically to `1'b0` (taking the `else` branch). Across `hkust-zhiyao/RTLLM@51ed553d` (`50` designs), **`34/50` (`68.0%`)** testbenches omit 4-state case equality (`===` / `!==`): **`4/50`** (`comparator_3bit`, `comparator_4bit`, `sequence_detector`, `square_wave`) pass completely empty undriven (`1'bz`) modules in 4-state simulation (`iverilog`, `Synopsys VCS`), while **`26/50`** rely on 4-state `integer` ternary/bitwise bit-tainting (`1'bx ? 32'd0 : 32'd1 == 32'b0...0x`) that collapses to `0` (`pass = True` whenever `ref == 0`) under 2-state `Verilator` or SystemVerilog `int` (`§6.11.2`). In `NVlabs/verilog-eval@c498220d` (`156` designs), **`0/156`** testbenches guard `stats1.clocks > 0` inside `final` blocks—allowing `$finish` at $t = 0$ (`Mismatches: 0 in 0 samples`) to pass both v1 (`execution.py:106`) and v2 (`sv-iv-analyze:287`, alongside sticky `no_mismatch = True` at `L288`)—and the `156/156` asymmetric XOR check `ref === (ref ^ dut ^ ref)` (`Prob001_zero_test.sv:98`) accepts undriven `dut = 1'bz` whenever `ref = 1'bx` while self-failing (`1'b0`) on valid tri-state `ref === dut === 1'bz`.
2. **Formal Equivalence Subtlety in `Yosys` and `SymbiYosys` (`Yosys@11d972c2`, `sby@b21f7783`, `Probes 2–3`):** Formal equivalence checking eliminates finite-vector blindness only when invoked with sound initial-state, flattening, input-definedness, and 3-valued `SatGen` refinement flags:
   - *Sequential initial-state and $k$-induction soundness (`Probe 2`):* `equiv_induct` (`equiv_induct.cc:104–136, 182–196`) checks only weak post-prefix induction (`!ez->solve()` existential satisfiability of `<N>` consistent steps without `satgen.setInitState`); standalone invocation unsoundly proves power-on `initial` / `(* init *)` mismatches equivalent at `step = 1`. Conversely, invariant-strengthened $k$-induction is sound if and only if the $k$-step BMC base case checks *both* output equivalence and the state invariant across $0 \dots k - 1$ and the step proves *both* at step $k$, enabling stuttering re-encoded FSMs (`2`-bit binary vs. `4`-bit one-hot) to close at $k = 3$ with `$onehot` + simple-path uniqueness or $k = 1$ with `-encfile`.
   - *`SatGen` Kleene `{S0, S1, Sx}`, `miter`/`sat` flags, and `State::Sz` (`Probe 3`):* `model_undef` defaults to `false` (`equiv.h:13`, `sat.cc:1108`), mapping literal `RTLIL::State::Sx` under `importSigBit` (`satgen.h:104`) to deterministic `0` (`CONST_FALSE`) so both default `equiv_induct` and `equiv_induct -undef`'s per-cell fallback (`equiv_induct.cc:146–153`) unsoundly equate `gold = 1'b0` with undriven `gate = 1'bx`. Worse, running `miter -equiv -ignore_gold_x -make_assert` + `sat -verify -prove-asserts` fails unflattened (`miter.cc:33, 137–138`) and—even with `-flatten`—leaves `enable_undef = false` (`sat.cc:1108, 1254`), compiling `$eqx(w_gold, State::Sx)` (`miter.cc:193`, `satgen.cc:565, 584`) into `w_gold == 1'b0` and **unsoundly proving `gold = 1'b0` equivalent to `gate = 1'b1` (`SUCCESS!`)**, while bare `-enable_undef` without `-set-def-inputs` injects `X` on primary inputs and falsely rejects valid gate-level `$mux(s, d, d)` decompositions. Sound combinational/state-matched checking requires **`setundef -undef; miter -equiv -flatten -ignore_gold_x -make_assert gold gate miter; sat -verify -prove-asserts -set-def-inputs miter`** (or `setundef -undef` + `equiv_make` + `equiv_simple -undef` + `equiv_status -assert`), where `setundef -undef` (`setundef.cc:105–107, 199–204`) prevents `SatGen` from coercing explicit `1'bz` (`State::Sz`) literals to `0` (`satgen.h:101–104`).
3. **PPA Proxy Anti-Correlation and Finite-Testbench Reward Hacking (`BetterV`, `POET`, `Alpha-RTL`, `Harris 2003`, `Probes 4–5`):** Raw AIG node counts (`proc; aigmap; stat` in `BetterV`, `arXiv:2402.03375` §4.3.1) strictly anti-correlate with timing-closure architectures across $W \in \{8, 16, 32, 64\}$-bit adders (`RCA < Brent-Kung < Sklansky < Kogge-Stone` in `strash` AIG nodes: `576 < 747 < 963 < 1,350` at $W = 64$, vs. `130 > 26 > 18 > 16` in unit-delay depth—penalizing an `8.12×` depth reduction with `+134.4%` AIG nodes, while ignoring `Sklansky`'s `fo = 34` capacitive fanout penalty and `Kogge-Stone`'s `7.11×` wire-span congestion). Even with standard-cell `Yosys + OpenSTA` in the loop (`POET`, `arXiv:2603.19333`; `Alpha-RTL`, `arXiv:2606.05253`), gating PPA search on finite testbenches enables silent functional corruption: at $W = 10$, a Kogge-Stone adder with a `floor(log2(10)) = 3` vs. `ceil(log2(10)) = 4` stage-count bug (`Buggy_KSA10`) achieves **strictly fewer AIG nodes (`132` vs. `138`) at equal critical-path depth (`12 == 12`, area-delay product `1,584 < 1,656`, reward `13.60 > 13.06`)**, passes `100%` of small-integer directed tests and **`70.0%` (`35/50` empirical suites; `74.6%` exact binomial expectation)** of 100-vector random `RTLLM` suites, and hijacks simulation-only PPA selection while corrupting **`6,144 / 2,097,152` (`0.293%`)** inputs (minimal witness `(511, 1, 0)`), whereas an exact $2^{21}$-input combinational miter rejects `100%` of buggy rewrites.

### 5. Synthesized Engineering Rules for Agentic RTL & EDA Flows

`Basis: derived (synthesized from Sections 1–4)`

1. **Harden Simulation Harnesses Before Using Pass/Fail as an Optimization Signal:** Require 4-state `!==` / `===` (under a 4-state simulator such as `iverilog`/`VCS`/`Xcelium`, never 2-state `Verilator` alone, and avoiding `ref ^ dut ^ ref` on tri-state buses); assert `sample_count >= N_min > 0` inside `final` blocks; forbid candidate simulation-control and file-I/O system tasks (`$finish`, `$stop`, `$fatal`, `$fopen`, `$fwrite`) via AST lint while emitting grading verdicts over a harness-private file descriptor/nonce rather than scraping stdout substrings (`"pass"`) or stripping legitimate debug `$display`/SVA `$error` tasks indiscriminately; and gate functional scoring on actual netlist synthesis (`yosys -p "read_verilog -sv ...; hierarchy -check -top ...; proc; check -assert"`).
2. **Gate RTL-to-RTL PPA Optimization on Sound Asymmetric Formal Equivalence:**
   - *Combinational / state-matched rewrites:* Run `setundef -undef` (normalizing any literal `1'bz` `State::Sz` to `State::Sx`, paired with `$tribuf`/`1'bz` lint) followed by **`miter -equiv -flatten -ignore_gold_x -make_assert gold gate miter; sat -verify -prove-asserts -set-def-inputs miter`** (or `equiv_make gold gate equiv; equiv_simple -undef equiv; equiv_status -assert equiv`). Never omit `-flatten` or `-set-def-inputs` on `miter`+`sat`, and never use symmetric `$eqx` when synthesis refines `X` don't-cares.
   - *Sequential rewrites:* Never use `equiv_induct` standalone without an initial-state BMC check; pair `SymbiYosys` `mode: bmc` (`formalff -setundef -clk2ff -ff2anyinit` + `setundef -undriven -anyseq` at depth exceeding maximum counter/pipeline latency) with `mode: prove` (`abc pdr` IC3 or `$onehot`/`-encfile` invariant-strengthened `sat -tempinduct`), or `YosysHQ/eqy`, and switch to latency-aligned transaction miters whenever valid rewrites alter pipeline staging.
3. **Score Post-Mapping Physical Metrics with Delay-Floor Guards and Succinct Repair Context:** Replace raw `proc; aigmap; stat` proxies with technology-mapped `abc` + `OpenSTA` (rejecting `0 ps` constant-wire delay collapse) plus placement/congestion-aware checks (`OpenROAD`) on wide datapaths, and cap compiler/simulation repair history at $\le 5$ succinct most-recent-error turns (`AutoChip` Table 4).
