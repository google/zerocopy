Part A: two independent lognormal factors, median 1, P95 = 10 each (illustrative)
  sigma (log scale) = ln(10)/z95 = 1.3999
  analytic P95 of product = exp(z95*sqrt(2)*sigma) = 25.95  vs product of P95s = 100
  Monte Carlo P95 of product (2e6 draws) = 25.92
  P(product > 100) = 0.00995
  deterministic RfD = 1.65 / 100 = 0.0165 mg/kg-day; 'P95-product' RfD = 0.0636 mg/kg-day
