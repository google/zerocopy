# Mixed-Initiative Agent Collaboration, User-Simulator Fidelity, Preference Elicitation, and Pedagogical Scaffolding

## Summary

Primary-source inspection of 21 peer-reviewed papers and preprints (1999–2026; 18 arXiv records plus 3 non-arXiv publications, with 1 additional abstract-only companion excluded from quantitative claims), seven pinned public GitHub repositories (`sierra-research/tau-bench`, `sierra-research/tau2-bench`, `SALT-NLP/collaborative-gym`, `stellalisy/mediQ`, `eth-nlped/mathdial`, `Wuyxin/collabllm`, and `obastani/GenAICanHarmLearning`), and a deterministic five-pillar Python 3.10+ standard-library verification suite (`scripts/verify_mixed_initiative_usersim_pedagogy.py`) establishes four operational invariants for interactive collaborative agents, simulated-user evaluation harnesses, and pedagogical scaffolding:

1. **Gate Autonomous Action vs. Clarification by Expected Utility, Interruption Cost, and Forward Lookahead (`Horvitz 1999`; `Collaborative Gym`; `CollabLLM`; `Probes 1 & 3A`):** Under `Horvitz (CHI 1999)` Eqs. (1)–(3) and Fig. 6, three-way mixed-initiative control over inaction ($\neg A$), clarifying dialog ($D$), and autonomous action ($A$) is governed by thresholds $p^*_{\neg A, D}$ and $p^*_{D, A}$; when dialog friction collapses the band ($p^*_{\neg A, D} \ge p^*_{D, A}$), clarification is never utility-optimal (`Probe 1`). Single-turn RLHF optimization ($w = 0$) collapses collaborative agents into premature full drafting (`Probe 3A`: `acc = 0.4959`, `tokens = 0.9293`, `E[asks/turns] = 0.000`), whereas forward multi-turn lookahead (`CollabLLM`, ICML 2025; $w \ge 1$) exploits superadditive Value of Information to clarify first (`Probe 3A`: monotonic `w = 0 -> 1 -> 2 -> 3` progression to `acc = 1.0000`, `tokens = 0.7800`, `E[asks/turns] = 0.750`, with a zero-info-`ASK` control strictly preferring `DRAFT` across all states and horizons). In asynchronous shared workspaces (`Collaborative Gym`, ICLR 2026), situational planning lifts `GPT-4o` Initiative Entropy $H_{\text{init}}$ from `0.10–0.42` to `0.40–0.90` (`GPT-4-turbo`: `0.00–0.06` to `0.09–0.43`) and achieves `66%–86%` human win rates over autonomous baselines.
2. **Audit User-Simulator Parsers and Co-Report User Fidelity (`UFS`) Before Trusting Multi-Turn Agent Leaderboards (`tau-bench`; `tau2-bench`; `Collaborative Gym`; `UserBench`; `Flipping the Dialogue`; `UserProxyBench`; `Simulated Customers`; `Probe 2`):** String-based simulator parsers introduce structural failures at pinned upstream revisions—including ReAct `Thought:` leakage and `###STOP###`-in-thought termination (`tau-bench@59a200c6`), premature `###STOP###`/`###TRANSFER###` termination halting the orchestrator before agent write/transfer tools run (`tau2-bench@768f3d8f`, accounting for `8/8 = 100%` of audited telecom user-sim errors), and digit-substring action hijacking (`"1" in plan`, `collaborative-gym@58972c07`). Empirically, prompted assistant user simulators exhibit only `0.04%–3.31%` Pangram human-likeness vs. `77.73%–80.21%` for trained user LMs (`Flipping the Dialogue`, ICLR 2026, where `UserLM-8b` drops fixed `GPT-4o` task accuracy by `~17%`), halve real customer resistance (`25.1% -> 13.5%`, `Simulated Customers`, `arXiv:2606.20708`), swing frozen-agent reward by `15.2` points across proxies while `24.4%` of successful episodes fail `UFS` (`39.7%` of Telecom runs; `UserProxyBench`, `arXiv:2609.38043`), and can strictly invert agent leaderboard rankings (`Probe 2B`).
3. **Decouple Belief State & Abstention Reasoning from Natural-Language Verbalization (`GATE`; `MediQ`; `ClarifyGPT`; `IN3`; `OPEN`; `Probe 3B`):** Frontier models remain severely passive under underspecification (`UserBench` active elicitation `23.85%–26.31%`, Best Exist Rate `0.204–0.260`; `MediQ` `BASIC` asks `0.47–2.47` questions and degrades `Llama-3-8B` by `-19.0` pts). Decoupling structured belief/abstention control from surface generation recovers large gains: `MediQ`'s rationale-calibrated abstention (`RG` ECE `0.286 -> 0.211`) coupled to question generation closes `51.2%` of the Initial-to-Full clinical gap; `IN3` (`Mistral-Interact` 7B) cuts cognitive burden (`1.26` vs. `GPT-4` `2.31` details/round) and lifts `Options Presenting Rate` to `84.08%` vs. `GPT-4` `40.31%` (at `98.70%` vs. `100.0%` `Options Reasonable Rate`); and norm-matched (`||d|| = 1`) Bradley–Terry BOED (`OPEN` Eq. 5, `Probe 3B` with verified `ESS = 317.2/1200`, `min = 154.9`) beats random (`+0.1590` vs. `+0.1056` `TIDA`) and unprioritized axis probing (`+0.0576`) while achieving the lowest posterior entropy (`8.809` bits vs. `9.067` for prior-sorted axes).
4. **Separate Assisted Practice from Unassisted Transfer and Enforce Programmatic Step-Gating + Non-Leakage Metrics in Pedagogical Agents (`Bastani et al. 2025`; `Kestin et al. 2025`; `LearnLM 2024/2025`; `MRBench`; `TutorBench`; `MathDial`; `Probes 4 & 5`):** In `Bastani et al. (PNAS 2025)` (`N = 2,848` complete OLS cases across `G = 44` non-honors classrooms), unconstrained `GPT Base` boosts assisted practice by `+0.1370` (`+48.3%` relative) while harming unassisted exam performance by `-0.0543` (`-16.9%` relative; `t_{43}` 95% CI `[-0.0995, -0.0091]`), whereas prompt-only `GPT Tutor` boosts practice `+0.3613` (`+127.3%`) and neutralizes exam harm (`-0.0043`, `-0.4 pp` raw / `-1.3%` relative, n.s.) without delivering positive unassisted transfer. Achieving positive unassisted transfer (`2.33×` median gain in `81.7%` median time, `0.63–1.3 SD`, `Kestin et al., Sci Rep 2025`) required programmatic sequential step-gating plus verified step-by-step solutions after system prompts alone failed multi-part scaffolding. Optimizing on immediate learner comfort or uncoupled `Success@k` fails because learners fail to perceive cognitive atrophy (`Bastani`), role-played learners rank answer-giving `ChatGPT-4o` equal to expert-preferred `Gemini 2.5 Pro` (`LearnLM Arena`, where experts prefer `Gemini 2.5 Pro` in `73.2%` of non-ties), `GPT-4` leaks answers in `46.88%` of `MRBench` dialogues, and `MathDial`'s `evaluate.py` leaves `Success@k` unpenalized when `Telling@k` is `True` alongside an unsigned numeric regex.

## Findings

### 1. Mixed-Initiative Decision Theory, Shared Workspaces, and Forward-Looking Collaboration

#### 1.1 Expected-Utility Gating, Dialog Collapse, and Dwell Deferral (`Horvitz, CHI 1999`)

`Basis: documentation + execution (Probe 1 exact Eqs. 1–3 identities and stylized numerical sweep) + derived`

- **Three-Way Expected-Utility Partition (`Horvitz, CHI '99`, pp. 159–166, DOI `10.1145/302979.303030`, Eqs. 1–3, Figs. 5–7):** Given inferred probability $p = P(G \mid E)$ that the user desires autonomous goal $G$, expected utility is linear in $p$ for each action $a \in \{\neg A, D, A\}$:
  $$\text{eu}(a \mid E) = p \, u(a, G) + (1 - p)\, u(a, \neg G)$$
  Pairwise intersections yield the binary action threshold $p^* = \frac{u(\neg A, \neg G) - u(A, \neg G)}{[u(A, G) - u(A, \neg G)] - [u(\neg A, G) - u(\neg A, \neg G)]}$ and the three-way thresholds $p^*_{\neg A, D}$ and $p^*_{D, A}$ (Fig. 6; note p. 7 prose contains an editorial typo referring to "Figure 5" when describing Fig. 6). In LookOut, Horvitz trained a Platt linear SVM on `500` relevant + `500` irrelevant emails (pp. 4–5) and gated pop-up timing via an empirical dwell-time sigmoid curve over message lengths `0–2500` characters (Fig. 7).
- **Verified Identities & Sensitivity Directions (`Probe 1`):**
  1. *Baseline three-way ordering* (`u(A,G)=1.00, u(A,~G)=0.00, u(~A,G)=0.20, u(~A,~G)=0.80, u(D,G)=0.82, u(D,~G)=0.62`): exact intersections satisfy $p^*_{\neg A, D} = 0.2250 < p^* = 0.5000 < p^*_{D, A} = 0.7750$ (`dialog_band = 0.5500`).
  2. *Dialog-collapse condition:* Raising dialog friction (`u(D,G)=0.60, u(D,~G)=0.35`) pushes $p^*_{\neg A, D} = 0.5294 \ge p^*_{D, A} = 0.4667$, eliminating `DIALOG` across all `201` probability grid points on $[0, 1]$ (`dialog_band = 0.0000`). Clarification is optimal only when its expected net gain exceeds both inaction and direct action at $p^*$.
  3. *Bidirectional threshold shifts (Horvitz Fig. 5 vs. attention-cost prose):* Horvitz Fig. 5 specifically illustrates *increasing* $u(A, \neg G)$ (lower false-positive cost), shifting $p^*$ **leftward** (`u(A,~G) = +0.00 / +0.15 / +0.30` $\implies$ `p* = 0.5000 / 0.4483 / 0.3846`). Conversely, per Horvitz's attention-cost discussion, deeper user focus *decreases* $u(A, \neg G)$ and $u(D, \neg G)$ (`pen = 0.00 -> 0.45` in Probe 1's stylized sweep), shifting both $p^*$ (`0.5000 -> 0.5429 -> 0.5789 -> 0.6098`) and $p^*_{D, A}$ (`0.7750 -> 0.7994 -> 0.8191 -> 0.8352`) **rightward** while strictly increasing the expected-utility regret of a `+0.20` overconfident agent (`0.0310 -> 0.0352 -> 0.0386 -> 0.0403`).
  4. *Stylized LookOut dwell deferral:* Probe 1 illustrates Horvitz Fig. 7's qualitative monotone dwell-vs.-length relationship using a stylized sigmoid (`d_min=2.0, d_max=18.0, k=0.015, midpoint=250.0`, yielding `2.76 s / 10.00 s / 17.63 s` at `L = 50 / 250 / 500`; Horvitz Fig. 7 plots an empirical curve without publishing closed-form parameter literals).

#### 1.2 Asynchronous Shared Workspaces & Initiative Entropy (`Collaborative Gym`, ICLR 2026)

`Basis: documentation + source`

- **Asynchronous Environment & Initiative Metric (`Shao, Samuel, Jiang, Yang, & Yang, ICLR 2026`, `arXiv:2412.15701` v6; `SALT-NLP/collaborative-gym@58972c07`):** Models mixed-initiative work via an asynchronous 5-tuple step interface with private/public visibility (`collaborative_gym/core.py:44–95`, `CoEnv.step` carrying `private: bool`) and `7` collaboration acts over an asynchronous tick clock (`collaborative_gym/nodes/task_env.py:122–140`). Balanced initiative is quantified by **Initiative Entropy** $H_{\text{init}}$ (Eq. 1, p. 5) evaluated over `collaborative` and `succeeded` trajectories via `"Yes" in result["initiative"]` (`collaborative_gym/eval/initiative_analysis.py:58, 88–98`).
- **Model-Matched Collapse vs. Situational Planning Recovery (Table 3 & Table 4):**
  - *Simulated evaluation (Table 3, 4 models × 3 tasks):* Basic Collaborative Agents span $H_{\text{init}} \in [0.00, 0.48]$ overall, with **`GPT-4o`** spanning **`0.10–0.42`** (`0.42` Travel, `0.16` Related Work, `0.10` Tabular) and **`GPT-4-turbo`** collapsing to **`0.00–0.06`**. Adding **Situational Planning** lifts **`GPT-4o`** $H_{\text{init}}$ to **`0.40–0.90`** (`0.09–0.90` across all 4 models; **`GPT-4-turbo`**: `0.09–0.43`) and raises Travel task performance above autonomous execution (`Auto 0.591 -> Situational Collab 0.667*` for `GPT-4o` at $H_{\text{init}} = 0.90$; `0.707*` for `Llama-3.1-70B`).
  - *Real-human evaluation (`N = 50` per task, Table 4):* Situational collaborative agents achieve `1.00` Delivery Rate and beat autonomous baselines across all three domains: Travel **`86%`** (`[0.764, 0.956]`), Tabular **`74%`** (`[0.618, 0.862]`), Related Work **`66%`** (`[0.529, 0.791]`).
  - *Sim-vs.-Real error divergence (Table 5):* Overall error frequencies correlate at Spearman $\rho = 0.803$ ($p = 7.94 \times 10^{-7}$) and human–simulator distinguishability is `56.9%` ($p = 0.0898$ n.s.), yet error shares diverge materially by category: Communication failures represent `65%` real vs. `80%` simulated, while Environment Awareness failures represent `28%` real vs. `13%` simulated.

#### 1.3 Forward Multi-Turn Rollouts vs. Myopic Single-Turn Collapse (`CollabLLM`, ICML 2025)

`Basis: documentation + source + execution (Probe 3A unified belief-MDP and zero-info-ASK control) + derived`

- **Multiturn-Aware Reward (`Wu et al., ICML 2025 Outstanding Paper`, `arXiv:2502.00640`; `Wuyxin/collabllm@7378aab4`):** `collabllm/reward.py:70–137` implements `multiturn_aware_reward` (`max_metric_workers: int = 16` at `L79`), simulating forward conversation rollouts of window $w$ via `ChatSessionSimulator().run_chat_simulation(...)` (`L94–99`) and scoring metrics concurrently in a `ThreadPoolExecutor` loop (`L118–137`). Across Table 1 (Online DPO vs. Proactive Base), forward rollouts improve both accuracy and efficiency: `MediumDocEdit` BLEU `35.0 -> 36.8` (`PPO 38.5`), tokens `2.18k -> 2.00k`, `ITR 62.0 -> 92.0`; `BigCodeBench` `11.0 -> 13.0` (`PPO 14.0`), `ITR 33.7 -> 52.0`; `MATH-Chat` `12.5 -> 16.5`, tokens `2.90k -> 2.37k`, `ITR 46.0 -> 60.0` (overall `+18.5%` performance, `+46.3%` interactivity; 201-judge human study: `+17.6%` satisfaction, `-10.4%` time, `63.8%` highly engaging; Fig. 4 confirms $w \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ outperforms $w = 0$).
- **Exact Unified Belief-MDP Mechanism & Anti-Vacuity Proof (`Probe 3A`):** Using a single coherent finite-horizon belief-MDP (`H = 4`, 3 conjunctive binary intent bits $k \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$, exact-match score $S_{\text{term}}(k) = 2^{-(3-k)}$, strictly negative `ASK` step reward `-(0.25*0.10 + 0.11) = -0.135`, `DRAFT` token cost `-0.25*0.48 = -0.120`, miss drop-off `p_drop = 0.35`, with true terminal states `turns_left == 1 or k == 3` strictly preceding `depth == 0` truncation and zero reward discontinuity), `Probe 3A` demonstrates that superadditive Value of Information ($0.125 \to 0.25 \to 0.50 \to 1.00$) drives monotonic improvement with lookahead $w$ without any interactivity reward bonus:

| Lookahead Window $w$ | First Action at $k=0$ | Expected Accuracy | Expected Reading Tokens | `E[asks]` | `ITR = E[asks/turns]` | Zero-Info `ASK` Control (`gain=0`) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **`w = 0`** (myopic) | `DRAFT` | `0.4959` | `0.9293` | `0.000` | `0.000` | `ASK < DRAFT` at all `(k, t)` (`acc = 0.4959`) |
| **`w = 1`** | `DRAFT` | `0.5445` | `0.9138` | `0.277` | `0.069` | `ASK < DRAFT` at all `(k, t)` (`acc = 0.4959`) |
| **`w = 2`** | `DRAFT` | `0.6937` | `0.8667` | `1.137` | `0.284` | `ASK < DRAFT` at all `(k, t)` (`acc = 0.4959`) |
| **`w = 3`** (full horizon) | `ASK` | `1.0000` | `0.7800` | `3.000` | `0.750` | `ASK < DRAFT` at all `(k, t)` (`acc = 0.4959`) |

  Because `ASK` incurs strictly negative immediate reward and receives zero interactivity bonus, a zero-information-gain `ASK` (`gain = 0`) never beats `DRAFT` at any `(k, t)` state or any lookahead $w \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$—confirming that clarification emerges strictly from multi-step Value of Information.

### 2. User-Simulator Instrumentation Forensics & Multi-Proxy Benchmark Distortion

#### 2.1 Pinned Upstream Parser, Orchestrator, & Cost-Accounting Defects

`Basis: source + execution (Probe 2A verbatim parser/dispatch reproductions)`

1. **`sierra-research/tau-bench` @ `59a200c6d575d595120f1cb70fea53cef0632f6b` (`2026-03-18`):**
   - *ReAct parser ordering (`tau_bench/envs/user.py:136–146`, `Probe 2A`):* `ReactUserSimulationEnv.parse_response` checks `if "###STOP###" in response:` first (so mentioning `"must NOT say ###STOP###"` inside `Thought:` returns `"###STOP###"` and sets `done = "###STOP###" in observation` at `tau_bench/envs/base.py:99`), then checks `elif "Thought:" in response:` *before* `elif "User Response:" in response:` and calls `_, user_response = response.split("Thought:")`. Compliant ReAct output leaks private `Thought:` contents verbatim to the agent, and a second `"Thought:"` raises `ValueError`.
   - *Cost overwrite & prompt typo (`tau_bench/envs/user.py:52, 66, 105, 123, 171`):* Assigns `self.total_cost = cost` instead of accumulating (`+=`), overwriting cumulative spend with the final turn (`0.009` vs. `0.054` in `Probe 2A`'s illustrative 4-turn pattern); system prompts also spell `"satisified"` (`L66, L105`).
   - *Default-argument OOB (`tau_bench/envs/base.py:69, 80`):* Calls `random.randint(0, len(tasks))` (inclusive upper bound, hitting `IndexError` `85/600` times for `N = 5` in `Probe 2A`). Reachability boundary: `tau_bench/run.py:73, 80` passes `task_index=idx` explicitly, so this OOB triggers only on direct callers omitting `task_index`. Reward combines DB SHA-256 hash and output substring checks (`base.py:38–41, 124–164`).
2. **`sierra-research/tau2-bench` @ `768f3d8fe8528b4f32a5096f9ed008ae50f48ead` (`2026-11-21`):**
   - *Premature sentinel termination before agent tool execution (`src/tau2/user/user_simulator_base.py:51–53`, `user_simulator.py:68–71, 142–155, 194–225`, `src/tau2/orchestrator/orchestrator.py:293–296, 857–874, 892–915`, `Probe 2A`):* `is_stop` returns `True` whenever `###STOP###`, `###TRANSFER###`, or `###OUT-OF-SCOPE###` appears as a substring of any non-tool user message (`tool_calls` returns `False`). In `orchestrator.py:857–874`, `is_stop` sets `self.done = True` immediately on the user turn *before* handing control to `Role.AGENT`—aborting pending write/transfer actions (`"Yes, I confirm modification #W5061109. Please proceed!###STOP###"`, Appendix E.4; `"###TRANSFER###"`, Appendix E.3). Bundled terminating tool calls are rejected (`L892–915`).
3. **`SALT-NLP/collaborative-gym` @ `58972c0702412f293e303c3e49b6cc896db2467a` (`2026-08-20`):**
   - *Digit-substring dispatch collision (`collaborative_gym/nodes/simulated_user.py:232–245, 263–322`, `Probe 2A`):* Prompts specify `"lazy user"` and anti-debugging behavior (`L232–245`), then branch sequentially via `if "1" in plan:` $\to$ `elif "2" in plan:` $\to$ `elif "3" in plan:` $\to$ `elif "4" in plan:`. Any natural-language plan containing `"1"`—such as `"4. Finish the task (phase 1 is complete)"` or `"Do not pick 1; choose 2. Offer feedback"`—is hijacked to `"answer_question"`.

## Boundaries

- **`unknown` — Causal Separation of Step-Gating vs. Population, Verification Shortcuts, and Retention Delay (`Bastani 2025` vs. `Kestin 2025`):** `Bastani et al.` (`-0.0043` n.s. on delayed closed-book exams in mandatory high-school math) and `Kestin et al.` (`+0.63–1.3 SD` on immediate crossover post-tests in Harvard undergraduate physics) differ simultaneously across four confounded dimensions—programmatic step-gating vs. prompt-only guardrails, learner age/motivation, answer-verification shortcuts during practice, and immediate vs. multi-session delayed transfer. No factorial RCT holding population and delay constant was available to isolate their individual effect sizes.
- **`not examined` — Live Execution of Upstream Multi-Turn LLM Pipelines & Post-2026 Model Releases:** The six interactive benchmark repositories (`tau-bench`, `tau2-bench`, `collaborative-gym`, `mediQ`, `mathdial`, `collabllm`) were audited at pinned source code and published tables rather than executed against live LLM APIs; only `scripts/verify_mixed_initiative_usersim_pedagogy.py` was executed directly. Complementary preprint `arXiv:2405.00981` (Austin, Korikov, Toroghi, & Sanner, *"Bayesian Optimization with LLM-Based Acquisition Functions for Natural Language Preference Elicitation"*) was inspected at abstract/metadata level only and excluded from quantitative claims.
- **`known not to apply` — Treating `ClarifyGPT` Table 2 as Unassisted End-User Performance:** Because `ClarifyGPT` §5.1 supplied ground-truth unit test cases to human participants as well as the automated simulator, neither Table 2 (`+15.35%`) nor Table 3 (`+11.52%–15.07%`) measures clarification gains with users who lack an executable test oracle.
- **`known not to apply` — Treating `MathDial`'s 0-Indexed `all_match` as an Unintended `k + 1` Off-by-One Bug:** Upstream callers (`gpt_baseline.py:57–65`) and unit tests (`tests/test_metrics.py:18–72`) deliberately reserve index `0` for the mandatory Round-0 pre-tutoring walkthrough; a `k`-shift occurs only when `evaluate_at_k` is reused on dialogues omitting that Round-0 exchange.
- **`known not to apply` — Extrapolating Stylized Probe Simulations to Unseen Human Effect Sizes:** `Probes 1` (stylized attention-cost/sigmoid sweeps), `2B` (hidden-constraint rank inversion), `3A` (3-bit belief-MDP), `3B` (synthetic 8-D Bradley–Terry BOED), and `5` (stylized cognitive-offloading dynamics with explicit structural `step_gated` bonuses) establish mathematical or mechanistic sufficiency under transparent assumptions—not empirical effect-size estimates on unseen human cohorts or live frontier models.
- **`known not to apply` — Unconditional Reachability of `tau-bench` `base.py:69,80` OOB via `tau_bench/run.py`:** `tau_bench/run.py:73,80` passes `task_index=idx` explicitly; `random.randint(0, len(tasks))` OOB triggers only on direct environment callers omitting `task_index`.
