# Agent Context Compaction, Externalized Memory, Multi-Agent Coordination Scaling, Technical Retrieval, and Evaluation Hygiene

## Summary

Primary-source inspection of 27 peer-reviewed papers and technical reports (2023–2026), four production engineering reports and rebuttal analyses (Manus, Anthropic, Factory.ai, Zep), 13 pinned commits across eight public repositories (`SWE-agent/SWE-agent`, `OpenHands/software-agent-sdk`, `OpenHands/OpenHands`, `letta-ai/letta-code`, `letta-ai/letta`, `getzep/graphiti`, `getzep/zep-papers`, `mem0ai/mem0`), and a deterministic three-probe verification suite establishes five operational invariants for long-horizon agent systems:

1. **Prompt-Cache Prefix Stability Flips Optimal Compaction Economics, and Text Summarizers Fail Artifact-Trail Tracking:** In software-engineering trajectories, tool observations account for **`≈84%`** of turn tokens (`JetBrains Complexity Trap`), allowing simple **Observation Masking** (`M = 10`) to match or beat LLM Summarization on `SWE-bench Verified` while halving cost (`-52.7%` on `Qwen3-Coder 480B`, `-56.1%` on `Gemini 2.5 Flash`) and avoiding LLM-Summary's `+13%–15%` trajectory elongation on those models (though `Qwen3-32B` reverses this pattern: Observation Masking elongates `+13%` vs. Raw). However, exact prefix-cache modeling (`Probe 2`, `[exact analytical / mechanistic surrogate]`) proves that moving from `1×` (no cache discount) to `4×` or `10×` cached-read pricing (`$3.00` vs. `$0.30/MTok`, Manus) **flips per-turn rolling masking (`polling = 1`) from `#1` cheapest to `#7` dead last** at `T = 60` and `T = 120` (`$3.5274` and `$7.9835` at `10×`, rising to `$4.2813` and `$9.6385` under a `1.25×` cache-write surcharge). Respecting JetBrains §5.3's rule of passing **unmasked** context to hybrid summarization (`sum_in = 201.3k` at `T = 120`), batched observation masking (`mask_poll10`, `$3.4952`) **beats** deferred hybrid (`$3.8461`) at `T = 120` under `10×` caching; deferred hybrid crosses permanently below `mask_poll10` only at long horizons (`T* = 181` onward) or when hard context limits force compaction. Across all evaluated text compressors (`Factory.ai`, >36k messages), **Artifact Trail Integrity** is the worst failure dimension (`2.19–2.45 / 5.0`), driving production harnesses like `letta-ai/letta-code` to externalize persistent state into Git-backed Markdown filesystems (`MemFS v2`) with pre-commit validators and token-fenced conflict repair.
2. **Externalized Memory Benchmarks Are Dominated by Evaluation Confounders and Extraction Blind Spots:** Offline full-context stuffing suffers `30%–60%` relative accuracy degradation at `~115k` tokens (`LongMemEval_S`) and collapses adversarial abstention (`LoCoMo` `GPT-3.5-16K`: `13.1 → 2.1` F1). Structured memory systems trade off verbatim recall against temporal synthesis: `LongMemEval_M` shows fact-augmented key expansion (`K = V + fact`) raises `GPT-4o` Top-10 QA from `0.670 → 0.720` whereas summary-only keys collapse to `0.252`; bi-temporal graphs (`Zep/Graphiti`) lift `LongMemEval_S` `gpt-4o` accuracy `60.2% → 71.2%` (`-91%` latency) while **degrading `single-session-assistant` recall by `-17.7%`** (`94.6% → 80.4%`). Forensic diffing of the May 2025 `Zep` vs. `Mem0` `LoCoMo` controversy (`84% → 75.14%` vs. `58.44%` / `65.99%`) isolates **six code-level confounders**—a Category-5 numerator/denominator bug (`~+8.9 pp`), event-time vs. mention-time double temporal subtraction (`16.7 pp`), a silent `'blip_captions'` vs. `'blip_caption'` key mismatch dropping all `1,226` image captions, serial vs. `asyncio.gather` search latency, verdict-first/label-only judge schemas (neither harness enforces reasoning-before-verdict), and cyclic post-hoc category-label permutation.
3. **Multi-Turn Compounding Reliability (`pass^k`) Collapses Under Heterogeneity and Dual Control, While Multi-Agent Scaling Is Architecture-Gated:** On `τ-bench`, `gpt-4o` drops from `61.2%` `pass^1` (`τ-retail`) to `< 25%` `pass^8`; `Probe 1A` (`[exact analytical / derived fit]`) confirms per-task heterogeneity (`Beta(0.9988, 0.6332)`, `M = 1.632` at interior point `0.226`) explains why `E[p^8]` exceeds naive homogeneous `0.612^8 = 0.0197` by `11.48×`, yet sustaining `pass^8 >= 0.80` across `m = 5` stateful writes demands homogeneous single-step reliability `q >= 0.99444` (`<= 0.556%` step error) and still leaves `18%–19%` brittle tasks (`p_i^8 < 0.5`) unless concentration `M` rises (`Probe 1B`). Shifting to dual-control user coordination (`τ²-bench`, ICML 2026) drops `pass^1` by `-18 pp` (`gpt-4.1`: `0.52 → 0.34`) and `-25 pp` (`o4-mini`: `0.67 → 0.42`), while parallel calls over stateful tools invert model size rankings (`ToolSandbox`). Across 260 multi-agent configurations (`Kim et al.`), the aggregate MAS effect is near zero (`-0.3%`, 95% CI `[-58.7%, +77.2%]`): centralized/decentralized topologies gain `+74.5%` to `+80.8%` on parallelizable `Finance-Agent` but collapse `-39.1%` to `-70.0%` on sequential `PlanCraft` and regress `-2.1%` to `-14.9%` above the `~45%` single-agent saturation threshold (`SWE-bench Verified`). Crucially, Kim et al.'s `A_e^trace` (`1.0×–17.2×`) measures execution-trace extra work with **no significant main effect** on task success ($\hat{\beta} = 0.014, p = 0.658$), distinct from task-level error ratio `A_e^task` (`0.98×–1.18×`).
4. **Dense Bi-Encoders Fail Negation, Exclusion, Reasoning, and Call Graphs; Lexical+Graph Traversal + Reranking Dominate Technical Retrieval:** On `NevIR` (random `25%`), every bi-encoder scores **`< 12%`** (`6.8%–11.1%`) and even `ColBERTv2` scores `13.0%`; cross-encoders cross random (`MonoT5-3B` `50.6%`). On `ExcluIR`, HotpotQA-trained retrievers sit near `50%` random (`BM25` `53.48`, `DPR` `54.02`). On `BRIGHT`, MTEB leader `SFR-Embedding-Mistral` drops **`59.0 → 18.3`** nDCG@10 (Table 2 max `Qwen 22.5`; CoT query expansion adds up to `+12.2`). On `SWE-bench` code localization, `LocAgent` (BM25 + dependency graph) reaches `94.16% File@5 / 77.37% Func@10` (`Claude-3.5`, `$0.66`) and `92.70% / 77.01%` (`Qwen2.5-32B-ft`, `$0.09`, **`9.6×`** relative cost-efficiency vs. `OpenHands`), with removing BM25 causing the largest ablation drop (`-19.34 pp` File@5). `Probe 3` (`[toy model / mechanistic surrogate]`) demonstrates why naive lexical negation cue lists (`"longer"`, `"fails"`, `"drops"`) misfire on `12/12` adversarial affirmative technical queries (`AdvAffirm` crashes `100.0% → 25.0%`), whereas fixing leaky cues restores `AdvAffirm` (`100.0%`) while exposing an honest recall ceiling on implicit lexical negations (`75.0%` vs. `100.0%` explicit).
5. **Autonomous Research Agents and Ecosystem Sweeps Require Hard Sandboxes, Structured Verification, and Explicit Attrition Audits:** Citation recall falls below `50%` on open-ended synthesis (`ALCE` `ELI5`: `GPT-4` `44.0%–48.5%`); unsandboxed research agents evade timeouts and spawn runaway processes/checkpoints (`AI Scientist v1`), while `AI Scientist-v2` had `1/3` blind workshop submissions pass review (`6.33/10`, `2/3` rejected) with residual citation hallucinations; and on 20 ICML replications (`PaperBench`), top 12h `BasicAgent` (`Claude 3.5 Sonnet` `21.0%`) and 48h `o1` (`26.6%`) trail ML PhDs (`41.4%`). Meanwhile, benchmark and GitHub-sweep audits (`UTBoost`, `SWE-Bench+`, `ABC`, `AIDev`, `Liu et al.`) prove regex test-log parsing corrupted `54.7%` of SWE-bench Lite / `54.2%` of Verified (`40.9%` / `24.4%` leaderboard changes), `63.75%` of audited GPT-4 SWE-bench passes involved leakage or weak tests, writable test archives allow trivial `100%` reward hacking (`SWE-Lancer`), live web drift broke `28.3%` (`13/46`) of OSWorld Chrome tasks, and explicit Git-metadata attribution (`99.0%` precision) misses unattributed AI commits while file deletions bias survival estimates.

  - **Why the Task-Beta tail stays `18.19%`:** Holding task-level concentration fixed at `M = 1.632` (`μ = 0.93486`, `β = 0.1063 << 1`, J-shaped density) fixes the distribution of `p_i` identically while taking `q = μ^(1/m)`. ## Boundaries

- **`unknown` — Empirical Artifact-Trail Impact of `letta-code` `MemFS v2` Under Multi-Compaction Horizons:** Source inspection proves deterministic structural/read-only enforcement and CAS repair on committed memory files, but no controlled benchmark examined here measures whether `MemFS v2` + `MEMORY.md` closes Factory.ai's `2.19–2.45 / 5.0` artifact-trail ceiling or measures agent stall rates under pre-commit hook rejections over 100+ turns.
- **`unknown` — Effect Size of Judge Field Order (`is_correct` Before `reasoning`) and Length Caps on `LoCoMo`:** Neither `mem0ai/mem0@2b58775c17` (`json_object` reading only `['label']`) nor `getzep/zep-papers@d7401e8932` (`Grade(is_correct, reasoning)`) enforces reasoning-before-verdict, and the isolated percentage-point effect of field ordering vs. removing the 5–6 word cap has not been ablated independently.
- **`unknown` — Dynamic/Semantic Compaction Triggers and TTL Expiry Interaction:** JetBrains (`2508.21433v3`), `SWE-agent`, `OpenHands`, and `Letta V1` all rely on fixed turn, event, or token thresholds; optimal semantic trigger policies and `5m` ephemeral KV-cache TTL expiry during slow build/test turns remain unmeasured in the examined corpus.
- **`unknown` — Unattributed AI Code Share & Defect Survival Differential:** `Liu et al.` (`2603.28592v3`) verify `99.0%` precision on Git-attributed AI commits, but the prevalence and defect-survival rate of unattributed AI-generated commits in public repositories remain unknown.
- **`known not to apply` — `Kim et al.` `A_e^trace` as a Direct Task-Failure Exponent:** `A_e^trace` (`1.0×–17.2×`) measures execution-trace extra work and has no significant main effect on task success (`p = 0.658`); task-level error ratios `A_e^task` span `0.98×–1.18×`, with `Decentralized` (`0.477`) slightly outperforming `SAS` (`0.466`) in Table 5. `Probe 1C` is therefore an illustrative multiplicative-exposure stress model over abstract `K`, not a Kim et al. calibration.
- **`known not to apply` — `jetbrains_hybrid` Winning at `T = 120` Under `10×` Caching or `Observation Masking` Always Avoiding Elongation:** Under JetBrains §5.3's unmasked summarization billing rule and `10×` caching, `mask_poll10` (`$3.4952`) beats `jetbrains_hybrid` (`$3.8461`) at `T = 120` (hybrid stays permanently cheaper only from `T* = 181` onward); on `Qwen3-32B` (§4.4), Observation Masking elongated trajectories `+13%` vs. Raw; and uncalibrated `M = 10` breaks when transferred from `SWE-agent` to `OpenHands` (requiring `M = 58`).
- **`known not to apply` — Multi-Agent Gains on Sequential or Saturated Tasks and Dense Retrieval on Negation/Code Graphs:** MAS topologies degrade sequential `PlanCraft` (`-39.1%` to `-70.0%`) and saturated `SWE-bench Verified` (`-2.1%` to `-14.9%`); bare dense bi-encoders fail below random on `NevIR` (`< 12%`) and drop from `59.0 → 18.3` on `BRIGHT`.
- **`unsupported` — Unqualified `LoCoMo` SOTA Tables, `BRIGHT` `24.3` Model Variant, Independent-Retry Optimism, and Toy Surrogates as Live LLM Measurements:** Unreplicated vendor `LoCoMo` leaderboards without prompt/timestamp/category-5/judge parity are unsupported (`Factory.ai` is likewise a single-judge vendor self-eval without released transcripts); `BRIGHT` v4's `24.3` prose figure has no corresponding Table 2 row (`Qwen 22.5` is max); `Probe 1C`'s conditionally independent retries are an upper bound over real correlated retries; and `Probe 3`'s synthetic polarity gate and deterministic 2-hop graph reachability do not substitute for empirical `MonoT5-3B` (`50.6%`) or `LocAgent` (`77.37%`) measurements.
- **`not examined` — Multi-Agent Failure Taxonomies (`MAST`), Evolutionary Code Search (`FunSearch`/`AlphaEvolve`), Multi-Agent Debate Dynamics, and 2026 Provider-Native Compaction APIs:** Excluded to keep scope distinct from adjacent corpus topics and because controlled multi-model benchmarks on 2026 provider-native compaction endpoints were unavailable in the examined literature.
