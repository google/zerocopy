# Agent Interruption Cost, Subtask-Boundary Digest Batching, KV-Cache Prefix Preservation, and Transactive Memory Routing

## Summary

Primary-source inspection of 32 peer-reviewed cognitive science, human-computer interaction, software engineering, queueing, and language-model systems studies (1975–2026), nine pinned public repositories (`prometheus/alertmanager`, `tokio-rs/tokio`, `sgl-project/sglang`, `langchain-ai/langgraph`, `temporalio/sdk-python`, `aserg-ufmg/Truck-Factor`, `erikbern/git-of-theseus`, `hmarr/codeowners`, and `RaunoArike/goal-drift-evals`), and a deterministic five-probe Python verification suite establishes five invariants governing **when**, **in what representation**, and **to whom** workspace awareness updates should be delivered across concurrent coding-agent teams:

1. **Coarse Subtask-Boundary Tail Appends Preserve Both Goal-Memory Retrieval and Paged KV-Cache Prefixes, Whereas System-Prefix Banners Multiply Prefill Cost by `3.75×–6.42×` (`Altmann & Trafton 2002`, `Iqbal & Bailey 2006, 2008`, `sglang`, `Probe 1`):** Mid-task interruptions double human primary-task error rates (`0.22` vs. `0.11`, $F(1, 48) = 67.30, p < 0.001$; `Bailey & Konstan 2006`) and impose a **`3.67×` fine-over-coarse resumption lag** (`1,702 ms` vs. `464 ms`, $F(2, 131) = 25.23, p < 0.0001$; `Iqbal & Bailey 2006`), while even *same-context* mid-task interruptions elevate stress (`+36.7%`) and truncate output (`Mark, Gudith, & Klocke 2008`). Under ACT-R goal activation ($A_i = \ln(n/\sqrt{T}) + \sum_j W_j S_{ji} + \epsilon_i$, threshold $\tau = 4.0$, source weight $W = 1.0$; `Altmann & Trafton 2002`), suspending `depth = 3` vs. `depth = 1` active subgoals increases retrieval failure by **`2.741×` (`0.6168` vs. `0.2250`)** in a single-factor controlled ablation and **`4.441×` (`0.9992`)** without pre-suspension strengthening. In LLM inference engines (`sgl-project/sglang@448764da` `RadixCache`), mutating a `128`-token dynamic workspace banner inside a `4,096`-token system prompt at offset `2,048` invalidates every downstream KV page, inflating `40`-turn prefill cost by **`3.769×` (`page_size = 16`) / `3.751×` (`page_size = 64`)** when updated every `2` turns (`53.94%` raw historical token hit rate) and **`6.417×` / `6.386×`** when updated every turn (`10.19%` raw hit rate). Appending coalesced digests strictly to the transcript **tail** preserves `100.0%` of page-aligned historical prefixes (`100.0%` raw historical tokens at `page_size = 16` for `16`-token-aligned coarse digests; **`99.8499%` = `798,144 / 799,344`** raw historical tokens at `page_size = 64` under `820`-token micro-turns due to `820 mod 64 = 52` unaligned trailing tokens).
2. **First-Arrival `GroupWait` Batching Has Positive Optimal Delay $W^* > 0$ If and Only If $\lambda K > h$, While Heavy-Tailed Subtasks Require Timeline-Preserving Checkpoint Caps and Root-Cause Inhibition (`Alertmanager`, `Scholl & Kleinrock 1983`, `Probe 2`):** Modeling ephemeral advisory batching with Poisson arrival rate $\lambda$, digest setup/interruption cost $K$, and per-advisory staleness holding cost $h$ yields long-run cost rate $C_{\text{GW}}(W) = \frac{\lambda K - h/2}{1 + \lambda W} + \frac{h}{2}(1 + \lambda W)$ ($u = 1 + \lambda W \ge 1$), whose boundary derivative at $W = 0$ is $\left.\frac{dC_{\text{GW}}}{du}\right|_{u=1} = h - \lambda K$. Thus **$W^* = 0$ across the entire regime $0 < \lambda K \le h$ ($2\lambda K/h \le 2.0$)**—including $\frac{h}{2} < \lambda K \le h$ (`0.75 < 0.8693` at $\lambda = 0.75, K = 1, h = 1$) where the unconstrained stationary point would be negative—and **$W^* = \frac{1}{\lambda}\!\left(\sqrt{\frac{2\lambda K}{h} - 1} - 1\right) > 0$ strictly when $\lambda K > h$** (`-72.0%` cost rate `25.0 → 7.0` at $W^* = 12.0\text{ s}$ for $\lambda = 0.5, K = 50, h = 1$). In `prometheus/alertmanager@5dea4ded`, `GroupWait` fires *only once* on group creation (`dispatch/dispatch.go:771`), transitioning persistent groups to periodic `GroupInterval` ticks (`L965`, mean follow-on arrival wait `28.46 s` at $I = 60\text{ s}$) with unchanged sets muted by `RepeatInterval` (`notify/dedup_stage.go:89`), while parent-to-child `InhibitRule` (`inhibit/inhibit.go:168–203, 299–303`) suppresses `100%` (`960 → 0`) of cascading compile symptoms while preserving `120/120` root breaks and preventing mutual self-mute via `excludeTwoSidedMatch` (`2/2` two-sided errors preserved vs. `0/2` unguarded). Under `Scholl & Kleinrock (1983)` rest-period queueing, bimodal stalled subtasks (`95%` `5 s`, `5%` `305 s`, mean `20 s`) inflate residual boundary wait from `10.0 s` to **`116.875 s`**; partitioning subtasks at a `30 s` checkpoint cap (conserving all `2,000 s` of work) bounds residual wait to **`11.875 s` (`9.84×` reduction)**.
3. **Transactive Memory Directories (`CODEOWNERS` + Cohort-Survival-Weighted `DOA`) Collapse $\Theta(N^2)$ Broadcast Distractors to $\Theta(kN)$ Without Stale-Creator Lock-In (`Wegner 1987`, `Truck-Factor`, `git-of-theseus`, `codeowners`, `Probe 3`):** Broadcasting $2N$ workspace events across $N$ peers generates $2N(N-1)$ deliveries, injecting in-domain lexical distractors that collapse uninstructed LLM macro reasoning accuracy on `GSM-IC` from `95.0%` to `6.0%` (`Shi et al. 2023`) and accelerate `>100k`-token trajectory goal drift (`Arike et al. 2025`). Routing via gitignore-anchored `CODEOWNERS` (`hmarr/codeowners@11d3ff26`) and expertise directories (`k = 2` specialists) cuts total deliveries by **`93.55%` at $N = 32$** (`128` vs. `1,984`) and **`96.83%` at $N = 64$** (`256` vs. `8,064`), eliminating `100%` (`1,856` and `7,808`) of non-owner distractor injections. Static Degree-of-Authorship (`aserg-ufmg/Truck-Factor@614600ed`, `AuthorshipInfo.java:38`) exempts `firstAuthor` and `secondaryAuthor` from its `3.293` absolute floor, misrouting **`100%` (`120/120`)** of refactored 2-year files to departed creators (`a_departed_creator_*`), whereas weighting authorship and deliveries by pure `git-of-theseus` exponential cohort survival $e^{-\kappa \Delta t}$ ($T_{1/2} = 180\text{ d}$, with static-`DOA` fallback on long-dormant files) restores **`100%` (`120/120`)** active-maintainer top-1 routing (`z_active_owner_*`).
4. **Superstep Snapshot Isolation Eliminates Torn Tool Chains, Yet Multi-Writer Determinism Requires Semilattice Reduction or Path Sorting Beyond `tokio::sync::watch` (`Tokio`, `LangGraph`, `Temporal`, `Probe 4`):** `tokio::task::coop::Budget` (`coop/mod.rs:115, 260`) bounds tight ready-poll starvation to **`128` polls** (`RestoreOnPending` preserving budget on `Pending`), while `tokio::sync::watch::Sender::send_replace` (`watch.rs:1247`) performs unconditional `mem::swap` overwrite—coalescing `500` ordered single-writer updates across `8` artifacts into `8` wakeups (`98.4%` digest reduction) and, when read at superstep boundaries, eliminating mid-tool-chain tearing (`0/500` vs. `500/500` preemptive). However, under out-of-order multi-writer network jitter, raw `watch::send_replace` still yields **`500/500` jittered final states**; achieving **`1` deterministic state across all `500` permutations** requires either a commutative semilattice channel (`LangGraph` `BinaryOperatorAggregate`, `channels/binop.py:66–149`) or deterministic producer-path sorting (`pregel/_algo.py:256–257`), complemented in `Temporal` (`_workflow_instance.py:521–560`) by 4-bucket `job_sets` (`patch` $\prec$ `signal`) under `_single_batch_activation` (`500/500` consistent patch visibility vs. `242/500` unbucketed).
5. **Coarse-Boundary Deferral Alone Still Loses Utility (`-1.8943`) Without Calibrated 3-Tier Value-of-Information Gating (`Horvitz 1999`, `Iqbal & Bailey 2008`, `HandRaiser 2026`, `Probe 5`):** Because off-topic interruptions incur a `4.968×` resumption penalty (`23.10 s` vs. `4.65 s`; `Iqbal & Bailey 2008`) and uncalibrated LLM self-interruptions degrade task success (`0.743 → 0.500`) while inflating tokens (`346 → 461`; `Wang et al. 2026`), deferring *all* updates to coarse boundaries cuts mixed-stream loss by `85.5%` versus immediate interruption (`-1.8943` vs. `-13.0232`) **yet remains strictly worse than `never_notify` (`0.0000`)**. A 3-tier VoI gate (`PREEMPT_NOW`, `DEFER_COARSE_BOUNDARY`, `PULL_LEDGER_MUTE`) driven by noisy relevance estimates $\hat{p}_{\text{rel}}$ under prior stakes achieves **`+4.7156` at $\sigma_{\text{cal}} = 0.10$** and **`+3.6692` at $\sigma_{\text{cal}} = 0.20$** (oracle upper bound `+4.8809`), maintaining positive net utility up to Gaussian noise **$\sigma_{\text{cal}} < 0.65$** (`-0.6016` at `0.65`) and systematic overconfidence bias **$\beta_{\text{bias}} < +0.25$** (`+2.4978` at `+0.20` vs. `-0.6002` at `+0.25` and `-1.7162` at `+0.45`).

### 1. Cognitive Interruption Cost, Subtask-Boundary Granularity, and Paged KV-Cache Prefix Preservation

`Basis: documentation + source + derived`

#### 1.1 Workspace Awareness Display Trade-Offs and Empirical Human Interruption Penalties

1. **Present vs. Historical Workspace Awareness (`Gutwin & Greenberg, 1998`, *CSCW '98*, pp. 207–216, `doi:10.1145/289444.289495`; `Gutwin & Greenberg, 2002`, *CSCW* 11(3–4):411–446, `doi:10.1023/A:1021271517844`):**
   - Mixed-focus collaboration alternates between individual execution and shared coordination. `Table I` (`2002`, p. 421) categorizes *present* awareness (*Who*: Presence, Identity, Authorship; *What*: Action, Intention, Artifact; *Where*: Location, Gaze, View, Reach), while `Table II` (p. 422) defines *past/historical* awareness (*How*: Action/Artifact history; *When*: Event history; *Who/Where/What* histories) supporting five coordination uses (`Table III`, p. 432: coupling management, communication simplification, action coordination, anticipation, and assistance) gathered via consequential communication, artifact feedthrough, and intentional communication (`§6`, pp. 422–425).
   - In `§8.1` (`Table IV`, pp. 433–434), `situated-literal` feedthrough works naturally when collaborators share the same viewport, but becomes **invisible across unseen files in relaxed-WYSIWIS workspaces** (such as multi-file codebases), while streaming literal peer activity inline during individual focus induces perceptual distraction (`Gutwin & Greenberg, 1998`), motivating **separate, symbolic displays** and `Table II` change histories inspected at coupling transitions.
2. **ACT-R Goal Decay, Interference Clutter, and Stack-Depth Resumption Scaling (`Altmann & Trafton, 2002`, *Cogn. Sci.* 26(1):39–83, `doi:10.1016/S0364-0213(01)00058-1`; `Probe 1`):**
   - Suspended goals do not sit on a noise-free LIFO stack; base-level activation decays with lifetime $T$ and sample count $n$ (`Eq. 1`, **pp. 46, 71**, ACT-R decay $d = 0.5$, omitting the $\ln 2 \approx 0.69315$ midpoint-integral shift [`0.67153` at $n = 200$ in `Probe 1`]):
     $$m = \ln\!\left(\frac{n}{\sqrt{T}}\right), \qquad m_a = \ln(n) - \frac{1}{2}\ln(t) - s\ln\!\left(\frac{1}{2s} - 1\right)$$
     where `Eq. (A5)` (`Appendix A`, p. 71; `1.8567` closed-form vs. `1.9602` series at $s = 0.3, t = 7.0\text{ s}, n = 15$) gives the closed-form mental-clutter interference floor of old goals under logistic noise $s = 0.3$ ($\sigma = \pi s/\sqrt{3}$).
   - In `Appendix B.4` (`pp. 78–79`), total activation $A_i = m_i + \sum_j W_j S_{ji} + \epsilon_i$ uses default goal source weight **$W = 1.0$** and total-activation retrieval threshold **$\tau = 4.0$** (`100 ms/sample` strengthening rate, p. 72). Holding strengthening (`+10` samples = `1.0 s`) and externalized workspace cue strength ($S_{ji} = 2.7, W = 1.0, \tau = 4.0$) strictly constant across `4,000` Monte Carlo trials (`Probe 1`), suspending **`depth = 3` vs. `depth = 1`** active subgoals raises episode retrieval failure from **`0.2250` to `0.6168` (`2.741×` pure stack-depth penalty)**, and to **`0.9992` (`4.441×`)** under unprepared mid-subtask preemption (`+0` strengthening samples, $S_{ji} = 2.15$).
3. **Mid-Task vs. Boundary Empirical Penalties and Same-Context Interference (`Bailey & Konstan, 2006`; `Iqbal & Bailey, 2005, 2006, 2008`; `Mark, Gudith, & Klocke, 2008`; `Parnin & Rugaber, 2009, 2011`):**
   - **`Bailey & Konstan (2006)` (`doi:10.1016/j.chb.2005.12.009`, $N = 50$, `Control` [coarse boundary] vs. `Experimental` [mid-task] across `6` task types):** Between-group mid-task interruption **doubled primary-task error rates** (`0.22` vs. `0.11`, **`F(1, 48) = 67.30, p < 0.001`**), increased annoyance by `+31%`–`+106%`, and nearly doubled STAI anxiety (`Session × Timing` $F(1, 48) = 3.90, p < 0.05$; $\mu_\Delta = 11.72$ vs. $6.28, p < 0.028$). While the between-subjects `Timing` main effect on completion time was non-significant (`F(1, 47) = 0.16, p < 0.69` due to inter-participant baseline speed variance), `Peripheral Task × Timing` was significant (**`F(2, 94) = 4.79, p < 0.01`**), and *within* the Experimental group mid-task interruptions slowed completion by **`+3%` to `+27%` vs. uninterrupted trials** (**`F(2, 46) = 15.69, p < 0.001`**: registration `+3%`, reading `+7%`, image `+10%`, selection `+16%`, adding `+24%`, counting `+27%`), with no corresponding slowdown in the boundary Control group.
   - **`Iqbal & Bailey (2005, 2006, 2008)`:** At pupillary low-workload subtask boundaries (`CHI '05`, `doi:10.1145/1056808.1056948`, $N = 12$), `Timing` significantly reduced resumption lag (**`F(2, 22) = 6.866, p < 0.005`**: `Best` $\mu = 2.06\text{ s}$ vs. `Worst` $6.69\text{ s}$ and `Random` $5.66\text{ s}$) and annoyance ($F(3, 33) = 7.414, p < 0.001$) while raising system respect ($F(3, 33) = 12.105, p < 0.001$). In `CHI '06` (`doi:10.1145/1124772.1124882`, $N = 134$ probes, $\chi^2(4) = 39.96, p < 0.0001$), GOMS cost-of-interruption classes separated log-millisecond resumption lags (**`F(2, 131) = 25.23, p < 0.0001`**): coarse low-carryover **`COIL` = `6.14 ln(ms)` (`SD = 0.97`) = `464 ms`**, medium **`COIM` = `6.92 ln(ms)` (`SD = 0.66`) = `1,012 ms`**, and fine high-carryover **`COIH` = `7.44 ln(ms)` (`SD = 0.92`) = `1,702 ms` (`3.67×` `COIL`)**. In `OASIS` (`CHI '08`, `doi:10.1145/1357054.1357070`), breakpoint deferral (`90 s` mean wait) cut frustration (`Policy` $F(3, 109) = 5.4, p < 0.002$; Coarse $\mu_C = 3.6$ and Medium $\mu_M = 2.6$ vs. Fine $\mu_F = 5.5$, $F(3, 52) = 6.2, p < 0.001$), while off-topic notifications slowed Diagram Editing resumption by **`4.97×`** (`23.1 s [SD=41.4]` vs. task-relevant `4.65 s [SD=4.4]`; `Content × Activity` $F(1, 109) = 7.75, p < 0.006$, Diagram simple effect **`F(1, 54) = 5.91, p < 0.018`**).
   - **`Mark, Gudith, & Klocke (CHI '08)` (`doi:10.1145/1357054.1357072`, $N = 48$):** Same-context (`S`) and different-context (`D`) mid-task interruptions caused statistically indistinguishable output truncation (`29.17` and `30.16` vs. `31.49` baseline words, $F(2, 92) = 3.34, p < 0.04$), speed compensation (`20.31` and `20.60` vs. `22.77 min`, $F(2, 77.98) = 3.36, p < 0.05$), and NASA-TLX stress spikes (`9.46` [`+36.7%`] and `9.13` [`+31.9%`] vs. `6.92`, $F(2, 92) = 12.15, p < 0.001$) via associative similarity interference.
   - **`Parnin & Rugaber (ICPC '09 & SQJ 2011)` (`doi:10.1109/ICPC.2009.5090030`, `85` devs; `doi:10.1007/s11219-010-9104-9`, `86` devs across `9,899` raw / `5,175` edit sessions + `414` surveyed, reporting `N = 371` Microsoft survey responses in `Tables 15–16`):** Across all edit sessions, only `10%` resume editing in `< 1 min` while `~30%` exceed `30 min`. Within the **`12`-developer (`1,213`-edit-session) Visual Studio subset** (`§5.1–5.2`, pp. 20–21, `Tables 5–7, 15`), only `7.5%` (`91/1,213`) edit immediately without prior navigation (`83%` navigate to new locations across `2–12` [mean `7`] locations vs. `15–150` [mean `135`] selection events in the `74`-developer Eclipse log; `58%` self-report navigate-to-remember on `N = 371`), and `< 1 min` resumption drops from **`35%` (`Table 5`, same method)** to **`16%` (`Table 7`, cross-location navigation)**. Respondents rated compact **Instant Diff (`3.9/5`)** and **Change Summary (`3.4/5`)** above heavyweight **Task Sketches (`2.9/5`)** (`Table 16`).

### 2. Queueing-Theoretic Digest Batching, Alertmanager Three-Timer State Machines, and Root-Cause Inhibition

`Basis: normative + source + derived + documentation`

1. **Exact Renewal-Reward `GroupWait` Dichotomy (`Heyman, 1977`, `doi:10.1287/mnsc.23.7.775`; `Levy & Yechiali, 1975`, `doi:10.1287/mnsc.22.2.202`; `Probe 2`):**
   - For ephemeral advisory groups triggered by the first Poisson($\lambda$) arrival and flushed after timer $W \ge 0$ (cycle duration $\frac{1}{\lambda} + W$, fixed setup/interruption cost $K > 0$, linear holding/staleness cost $h > 0$ per advisory-second), substituting $u = 1 + \lambda W \ge 1$ gives:
     $$C_{\text{GW}}(W) = \frac{\lambda(K + hW + \frac{1}{2}h\lambda W^2)}{1 + \lambda W} = \frac{\lambda K - h/2}{u} + \frac{h}{2}u, \qquad \left.\frac{dC_{\text{GW}}}{du}\right|_{u=1} = h - \lambda K$$
   - **Global Constrained Optimum on $W \ge 0$:**
     - **Urgent / Low-Rate Regime ($0 < \lambda K \le h$, i.e., $\frac{2\lambda K}{h} \le 2$):** $C_{\text{GW}}'(0) \ge 0$ and $C_{\text{GW}}(W)$ is strictly increasing for all $W > 0$ (including the band $\frac{h}{2} < \lambda K \le h$, where $C_{\text{GW}}''(u) > 0$ places the unconstrained stationary point at $u_{\text{stat}} \in (0, 1] \iff W < 0$). ### 5. Three-Tier Value-of-Information (`VoI`) Interruption Gating Under Calibration Noise

`Basis: normative + documentation + derived`

1. **Exact Expected-Utility Routing Conditions (`Horvitz, Jacobs, & Hovel, UAI 1999`, pp. 305–313, `arXiv:1301.6707v1`; `Wang et al., 2026`, *HandRaiser*, `arXiv:2604.06452v2`; `Probe 5`):**
   - Prompting raw LLMs to self-interrupt (`Wang et al. 2026`, `Table 1`, Llama-8B) degrades Pictionary success from `0.743` (`346` tokens) to **`0.500` (`461` tokens)** and Meeting Scheduling from `0.273` (`1,361`) to **`0.200` (`1,778`)**, whereas cost-calibrated RL (`HANDRAISER`) preserves/improves success (`0.743 / 0.307 / 0.583`) while cutting tokens by `24.3%–48.9%` (`262 / 1,042 / 782`).
   - Given estimated relevance $\hat{p}_{\text{rel}} \in [0, 1]$, remaining time to next coarse boundary $\Delta t_{\text{rem}}$, expected waste rate $\hat{w}$ while working, post-boundary value $\hat{B}_{\text{post}}$, mid-task interruption cost $C_{\text{mid}}(\hat{p}_{\text{rel}}) = \hat{p}_{\text{rel}} c_{\text{rel,mid}} + (1 - \hat{p}_{\text{rel}}) c_{\text{irrel,mid}}$ (`4.65 s` vs. `23.10 s`), and coarse-boundary cost $C_{\text{bnd}}(\hat{p}_{\text{rel}}) = \gamma_{\text{bnd}} C_{\text{mid}}(\hat{p}_{\text{rel}})$ ($\gamma_{\text{bnd}} = 0.464 / 1.702 = 0.2726$), let $\hat{u}_{\text{pre}} = \hat{p}_{\text{rel}}(\hat{w}\Delta t_{\text{rem}} + \hat{B}_{\text{post}}) - C_{\text{mid}}(\hat{p}_{\text{rel}})$ and $\hat{u}_{\text{def}} = \hat{p}_{\text{rel}}\hat{B}_{\text{post}} - C_{\text{bnd}}(\hat{p}_{\text{rel}})$:
     - **Tier 0 (`PREEMPT_NOW`):** $\hat{p}_{\text{rel}}\hat{w}\Delta t_{\text{rem}} > C_{\text{mid}}(\hat{p}_{\text{rel}}) - C_{\text{bnd}}(\hat{p}_{\text{rel}}) \;\land\; \hat{u}_{\text{pre}} > 0$ (hard write/contract invalidation on active target).
     - **Tier 1 (`DEFER_COARSE_BOUNDARY`):** $\hat{u}_{\text{def}} > 0 \;\land\; \hat{p}_{\text{rel}}\hat{w}\Delta t_{\text{rem}} \le C_{\text{mid}}(\hat{p}_{\text{rel}}) - C_{\text{bnd}}(\hat{p}_{\text{rel}})$ (coalesced into next boundary tail digest).
     - **Tier 2 (`PULL_LEDGER_MUTE`):** $\max(\hat{u}_{\text{pre}}, \hat{u}_{\text{def}}) \le 0$ (recorded only in pull-based workspace history).
2. **Baseline Comparison and Calibration-Error Breakdown Thresholds (`Probe 5`, `2,000` mixed advisories: `5%` hard collisions [`100` Tier-0], `25%` moderate coupling [`500` Tier-1], `70%` chatter [`1,400` Tier-2], online controller using prior stakes $\hat{w} = 0.80, \hat{B}_{\text{post}} = 20.0$ without oracle `B_post` leakage):**
   - **Static & uncalibrated policies all fail or collapse:** `interrupt_immediately_all = -13.0232` (`-20.6129` on low-relevance stream), `uncalibrated_overconfident_prompting (+0.45) = -7.6233`, `random_50pct = -6.8503`, **`always_defer_coarse_boundary = -1.8943`** (`-5.3759` low-relevance—proving boundary deferral alone still loses to `never_notify = 0.0000`!), and **`uncalibrated_overconfident_3tier (+0.45) = -1.7162`**.
   - **Noisy-calibrated performance & breakdown:** 3-tier VoI achieves **`+4.7156` at $\sigma_{\text{cal}} = 0.10$** and **`+3.6692` at $\sigma_{\text{cal}} = 0.20$** (vs. `+4.8809` oracle upper bound), remaining $> 0$ across zero-mean Gaussian noise up to **$\sigma_{\text{cal}} = 0.50$ (`+0.9125`, crossing `< 0` at $\sigma_{\text{cal}} = 0.65$: `-0.6016`)** and systematic overconfidence bias up to **$\beta_{\text{bias}} = +0.20$ (`+2.4978`, crossing `< 0` at $\beta_{\text{bias}} = +0.25$: `-0.6002`)**.

- **`not examined` — Live Binary Compilation/Execution of Upstream Repositories and Redirected Web-Agent Preprints:** Upstream `prometheus/alertmanager`, `tokio-rs/tokio`, `langchain-ai/langgraph`, `temporalio/sdk-python` (and `temporalio/sdk-core`), `sgl-project/sglang` (and `vllm-project/vllm`), `aserg-ufmg/Truck-Factor`, `erikbern/git-of-theseus`, `hmarr/codeowners`, and `RaunoArike/goal-drift-evals` were inspected at source (`Basis: source`) rather than compiled or executed directly; only the standalone Python 3.10+ verification suite `support/verify_interruption_batching_tms_probes.py` was executed (`Basis: execution`). User goal-revision web benchmarks (`Zou et al., 2026`, *InterruptBench*, `arXiv:2604.00892v1`) were not examined beyond abstract metadata, and paywalled organizational `TMS` texts (`Wegner 1987`, `Liang et al. 1995`, `Faraj & Sproull 2000`, `Lewis 2003`, `Brandon & Hollingshead 2004`) are bounded strictly to verified DOI/PubMed abstracts and open synthesis (`Argote & Ren 2012`).
- **`known not to apply` — Unconditional `GroupWait > 0` for $\lambda K \le h$, Permutation-Invariant Multi-Writer `tokio::sync::watch`, and Unfiltered Boundary Deferral:** Setting `GroupWait > 0` whenever $\lambda K \le h$ (including $\frac{h}{2} < \lambda K \le h$) strictly increases total cost (`Probe 2`); `tokio::sync::watch::Sender::send_replace` is unconditional last-arrival-wins and does **not** converge deterministically across out-of-order multi-writer permutations without a commutative semilattice join or deterministic path sort (`Probe 4`); and deferring every broadcast to coarse boundaries without `TMS`/`VoI` relevance gating still yields negative net utility (`-1.8943 < 0`, `Probe 5`), as does feeding `+0.25` overconfident LLM relevance probabilities into the 3-tier gate (`-0.6002`).
- **`known not to apply` — Treating Same-Topic Peer Chatter or System-Prompt Live Dashboards as Low-Cost:** Same-context mid-task interruptions degrade output length and spike stress just as severely as off-topic interruptions (`Mark et al. 2008`), and mutating dynamic status blocks inside system prefixes destroys downstream paged KV caches (`3.75×–6.42×` prefill cost, `Probe 1`).
- **`unknown` — Cold-Start `DOA` Accuracy on Green-Field Swarms Without Declarative Footprint Claims:** On newly created repositories with zero commit history, VCS-mined `DOA` and `git-of-theseus` survival weights are empty until combined with declarative `CODEOWNERS` rules or live task-footprint registrations.
