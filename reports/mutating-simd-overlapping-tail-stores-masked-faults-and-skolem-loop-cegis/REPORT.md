# Mutating SIMD Overlapping Tail Stores, Write-Side Masked-Store Fault Asymmetries, and Skolemized Loop CEGIS in Lean 4

## Summary

Read-only SIMD search routines (`memchr`, `strlen`) can safely over-read past slice bounds within an aligned hardware protection granule (`DemonicGranuleLoad`), and accumulator-reducing SIMD loops (`ByteSum`, `DotMaddubs`, `PipelinedMac`) admit scalar `CutBox` invariants without quantifying over memory. Neither technique transfers unchanged to **mutating SIMD and `asm!` byte-buffer routines** (`xor_stream`, `ascii_tolower`, `vpshufb` nibble-LUT codecs, `rep movsb`, masked vector tails). Direct execution on Intel Xeon Ice Lake-SP silicon, `qemu-aarch64 11.1.2`, Miri (`-Zmiri-tree-borrows` and Stacked Borrows), and `prelude` + `Init.*`-only mechanizations in Lean `v4.34.1` (`trustLevel := 0`, private-level `toKernelEnv.replay`, `0` `._unsafe_rec` constants, and bundled `CaDiCaL 2.1.2` with zero external SMT solvers) establish four primary findings:

1. **Write-side hardware masked-store fault asymmetry, LLVM `InstCombine` masking, and `+avx2` 2-safety leakage (`Basis: execution + source + normative + derived`):**
   - On live Intel Xeon Ice Lake-SP silicon (`[real measurement]`, `38` `SA_SIGINFO` page-boundary cases in [`support/write_masked_faults_and_miri_results.json`](support/write_masked_faults_and_miri_results.json)), AVX2 `VPMASKMOVD`/`VPMASKMOVQ` (`_mm256_maskstore_epi32`/`epi64`) and AVX-512 EVEX `VMOVDQU8`/`VMOVDQU16 {%k1}` suppress **both** `#PF` (`PROT_READ` `si_code = 2`, `PROT_NONE` `si_code = 2`, `munmap` `si_code = 1`) **and** `#GP(0)` (`SI_KERNEL` `si_code = 128` on non-canonical `0xdead_0000_0000_0000`) on disabled lanes; EVEX `VMOVDQA32 [ptr] {%k1}` with `k1 = 0` even suppresses the 32-byte alignment `#GP(0)` fault on `ptr = rw + 1`, creating an exact ISA-versus-Rust/LLVM refinement asymmetry against `simd_masked_store<..., SimdAlign::Vector>`, which unconditionally checks pointer alignment before inspecting the mask (`Miri/Interpreter/IntrinsicSimd.lean:445`, `rust_simd_masked_store_vs_evex_k0_asymmetry`).
   - By contrast, SSE2 `_mm_maskmoveu_si128` (`MASKMOVDQU`, `66 0F F7`, implicit `[RDI]`) and VEX `VMASKMOVDQU` raise **`SIGSEGV` even when `mask == [0; 16]`** across `PROT_READ` (`si_code = 2`, faulting at `RDI + 8` because Ice Lake-SP probes the upper qword first), `PROT_NONE` (`si_code = 2`, `RDI + 8`), `munmap` (`si_code = 1`, `RDI + 8`), and non-canonical addresses (`si_code = 128`), and trap at page boundary `4096` when straddling `[RW][PROT_READ]` at `RW + 4088` with trailing mask bytes `8..15` equal to `0`. Under `-O` (`rustc 1.99.1`), LLVM `InstCombine` folds `_mm_maskmoveu_si128(src, _mm_setzero_si128(), ptr)` with a compile-time constant zero mask to a no-op—hiding the crash in constant-mask unit tests while runtime-zero masks (`black_box`) emit `vmaskmovdqu` and fault (`test_bug03_maskmovdqu_all_zero_mask_fault`). Miri (`3ef0c22cdb`) leaves `llvm.x86.sse2.maskmov.dqu` unsupported; delegating it to `simd_masked_store` would be unsound (`naive_maskmovdqu_miri_shim_unsound`).
   - Under `-C opt-level=3 -C target-feature=+avx2` (both `rustc 1.99.1` and `1.102.0-nightly`), portable `simd_masked_store` lowers `u32x8`/`u64x4` to branchless `vpmaskmovd`/`vpmaskmovq` (`0` branches), but scalarizes `u8x16` into `vpmovmskb` + **`31` `test` + `31` `jcc` + `16` `vpextrb`**, `u8x32` into **`63` `test` + `63` `jcc` + `32` `vpextrb`**, and `u16x16` into **`31` `test` + `31` `jcc`**, violating constant-time 2-safety `LeakTrace` on secret masks (`avx2_scalarized_masked_store_1safety_vs_2safety`); adding `+avx512vl,+avx512bw` collapses all five to single branchless `vmovdqu{8,16,32,64} {%k1}`.
2. **Categorical unsoundness of write-side aligned RMW slack-writeback (`Basis: execution + source + derived`):**
   - Unlike read-only `DemonicGranuleLoad`, replacing a sub-vector tail write with an aligned 32-byte read-modify-write (`vmovdqa` load $\to$ `vpblendvb` $\to$ `vmovdqa` store) is **categorically unsound** across three independent layers (`[kernel-checked proof]` in [`support/lean/WriteMaskedFaultsAndTailTrichotomy.lean`](support/lean/WriteMaskedFaultsAndTailTrichotomy.lean) + `[real measurement]`):
     1. **Rust `Tree Borrows` (`-Zmiri-tree-borrows`):** While a foreign read preserves `.Frozen` even under an active `FnEntry` `StrongProtector`, a foreign write transitions `.Frozen -> .Disabled` (`Miri/TreeBorrows/Permissions.lean:93-100`), triggering immediate `ProtectedDisabled` UB (`write access through <tag> at allocN[0x10] is forbidden`) under `StrongProtector` and poisoning unprotected siblings so their next read fails with `ChildAccessForbidden` (`read_overread_sound_but_rmw_writeback_ub_under_tree_borrows`).
     2. **Concurrent lost updates on silicon:** On Ice Lake-SP, `buggy_rmw_slack_writeback` on bytes `[0..16)` wiped out **`197,797 / 200,000` (`98.9%`)** concurrent `AtomicU64::fetch_add(1, SeqCst)` increments on an adjacent sibling field in `[16..24)` (`rmw_slack_writeback_causes_concurrent_lost_update`).
     3. **Protection and 16-byte MTE tag granules:** 32-byte `stp q0, q1` across adjacent 16-byte Armv8.5-A MTE granules raises synchronous `SEGV_MTESERR` (`si_code = 9`, `rel_addr = 16`, `ptr_tag = 1` under `qemu-aarch64 -cpu max`, `rmw_slack_writeback_faults_on_mte_16b_granule`), and faults with `SIGSEGV` (`si_code = 2`) when slack bytes abut `.rodata`.
3. **The overlapping unaligned tail-store (`[len - W .. len)`) soundness trichotomy (`Basis: derived + execution`):**
   - Production SIMD kernels for `len >= W` (`W ∈ {16, 32}`) execute `strictSteps(W, len) = (len - 1) / W` non-overlapping `W`-byte chunks (`cursor + W < len`) followed by one branchless unaligned tail load-transform-store at `[len - W .. len)` (overlapping `[len - W .. W * strictSteps)` whenever `len > W` and `len % W != 0`). Mechanized in [`support/lean/WriteMaskedFaultsAndTailTrichotomy.lean`](support/lean/WriteMaskedFaultsAndTailTrichotomy.lean) (`528` constants cold-replayed in `719.89 ms`, `0` `._unsafe_rec`):
     1. **Disjoint `(dst: &mut [u8], src: &[u8])` (`disjoint_overlap_tail_universal_soundness`):** Universally sound for **every** pure lane function `F` (`src` remains unmodified inside the overlap window).
     2. **In-place `buf: &mut [u8]` with unbuffered tail read-after-loop (`inplace_unbuffered_overlap_tail_sound_iff_idempotent`):** Sound for all `len >= W` ($W \ge 2$) **if and only if** `F` is byte-idempotent (`∀ b, F (F b) = F b`, e.g., `ascii_tolower`, `clamp`, bitmask-clear). When `F` is non-idempotent (`xor_stream` with `key != 0`, byte-swap, LUT remap, wrapping add), **`len = W` (`32`) silently passes** (`strictSteps(32, 32) = 0`, `len_eq_W_hides_nonidempotent_bug`) while **`len = W + 1` (`33`) is the sharp minimal counterexample** (`len_W_plus_1_minimal_counterexample_nonidempotent`), double-applying `F(F(b))` across all `31` overlap bytes `[buf + 1 .. buf + 32)`.
     3. **In-place `buf: &mut [u8]` with preloaded tail register (`inplace_preloaded_overlap_tail_universal_soundness`):** Hoisting `tail_in := load(buf[len - W .. len])` into a vector register *before* the overlapping main-loop store restores universal soundness for arbitrary non-idempotent `F`.
4. **Skolemized single-address (`q : BitVec 64`) zero-SMT `CaDiCaL` loop CEGIS + `3.5x–64.1x` factored `Pass1GmpLrat` certificates (`0` manual invariant LOC) (`Basis: execution + derived`):**
   - Introducing a universally quantified **Skolem probe address `q`** tracking the 3-way memory partition (`written_prefix`: `buf <= q < buf + cursor`, `unwritten_inplace_suffix`: `buf + cursor <= q < buf + len`, and `frame_outside`: `q < buf ∨ buf + len <= q`) reduces mutating byte-slice loop equivalence to quantifier-free bit-vector SAT without array-theory SMT solvers (`Z3`/`cvc5`). Both `CaDiCaL exit 10` (`182.37 ms`) and theorem `refute_omitting_unwritten_inplace_suffix` prove that dropping `unwritten_inplace_suffix` renders even idempotent `Avx2AsciiTolowerInplace` non-inductive.
   - Leaving `dst`/`src` unnormalized at 64 bits causes `CaDiCaL 2.1.2 --no-elim` to time out (`> 20,000 ms`) on 2-slice disjoint kernels; normalizing to slice-relative `q_rel = q - base : BitVec 64` solves in `17.33–641.40 ms` yet still emits **`925–21,851` RUP steps** on overlapping-tail loops due to the $O(w^2)$ 64-bit ripple-borrow transitivity diamond between `q_rel < cur`, `cur + 32 >= len`, and `len - 32 <= q_rel < len`. Factoring out a **`36`-step 1-bit ripple-borrow order transducer** (or branch-split scalar/SIMD VCs on `ScalarEpilogueShortDispatch`) plus condensed `8`-bit spatial + exact `8`-bit SIMD lane certificates compresses proofs to **`264–451` total RUP steps (`3.50x–64.08x` compression)**.
   - [`support/synth_skolem_mutating_cegis.py`](support/synth_skolem_mutating_cegis.py) automatically synthesizes **`6 / 6`** `AutoLoop_*.lean` modules (`36.7–67.7 KB`, `0` manual invariant LOC, `type_hints == 0`, `0` `._unsafe_rec`, `by rfl` compile `6.26–12.52 s`, private kernel replay `2.62–4.99 s`) and **`6 / 6`** dual bug refutations in [`support/lean/RefuteMutatingSimd6.lean`](support/lean/RefuteMutatingSimd6.lean) (`6.95 ms` replay, `['propext']` only) + [`support/generated_mutating_bug_repros.rs`](support/generated_mutating_bug_repros.rs) (`6 passed; 0 failed` in `9.76 ms` on silicon).

### 1. Write-Side Hardware Masked-Store Fault Asymmetry, `InstCombine` Folding, and `+avx2` `LeakTrace` Leakage

#### 1.2 Three Subtle Verification & Compiler Seams on Masked Stores

1. **ISA-vs-Rust/LLVM alignment refinement asymmetry (`rust_simd_masked_store_vs_evex_k0_asymmetry`):** In `proofs-rs/lean-miri@c92d1d9754c9dd585b6b9d6949ae67e5e4665d6a` (`Miri/Interpreter/IntrinsicSimd.lean:444-446`), Miri executes `checkPointerAlignment h choice ptr vectorLayout alignment` **before** iterating over `mask` lanes. Confirming this in live `cargo +nightly miri test` (`ub_simd_masked_store_misaligned_mask_zero`), `simd_masked_store::<_, _, _, { SimdAlign::Vector }>(mask_all_zero, mis_ptr, vals)` triggers immediate UB (`accessing memory with alignment 1, but alignment 16 is required`), whereas hardware EVEX `VMOVDQA32 [mis_ptr] {%k1}` with `k1 = 0` is a architectural no-op. Lowering Rust `simd_masked_store` to EVEX is sound (target refines source), but lifting raw `asm!` `vmovdqa32 {%k1}` into `simd_masked_store` without proving `k1 != 0 ∨ aligned(ptr, 32)` introduces false UB.
2. **LLVM `InstCombine` constant-zero mask elision on `_mm_maskmoveu_si128`:** When `zero_mask = _mm_setzero_si128()` is visible to LLVM `-O` (`rustc 1.99.1`), `InstCombine` folds `llvm.x86.sse2.maskmov.dqu(src, zeroinitializer, ro_page)` to a no-op (`elided.is_none()` in `test_bug03_maskmovdqu_all_zero_mask_fault`), masking the hardware `SIGSEGV` in constant-mask tests while opaque runtime zero masks (`core::hint::black_box(_mm_setzero_si128())`) emit `vmaskmovdqu` and fault (`si_code = 2` at `ro_page + 8`). Furthermore, Miri rejects `llvm.x86.sse2.maskmov.dqu` as unsupported (`miri_unsupported_maskmovdqu_mask0`); theorem `naive_maskmovdqu_miri_shim_unsound` proves that shimming it via `rustSimdMaskedStore` would miss read-only/guard-page crashes whenever `mask == 0`.
3. **`rustc +avx2` byte/word `simd_masked_store` scalarization & 2-safety `LeakTrace` violation:** Because AVX2 provides `VPMASKMOVD`/`VPMASKMOVQ` (`32`/`64`-bit lanes) without byte/word masked stores, LLVM's `ScalarizeMaskedMemIntrin` pass under `-C opt-level=3 -C target-feature=+avx2` lowers `simd_masked_store` on `u8x16`, `u8x32`, and `u16x16` to `vpmovmskb` followed by a per-lane diamond of `test` + conditional jump (`jcc`) + `vpextrb`/`vpextrw` scalar stores (`31` branches on `u8x16`/`u16x16`; **`63` branches on `u8x32`**). Theorem `avx2_scalarized_masked_store_1safety_vs_2safety` proves that while this lowering is 1-safety memory-equivalent to EVEX `vmovdqu8 {%k1}`, its branch/address `LeakTrace` leaks the entire mask bitmask on `+avx2` whenever the mask depends on secret data (e.g., cryptographic padding or conditional byte redaction).

### 2. Why Write-Side Aligned RMW Slack-Writeback Is Categorically Unsound

`Basis: execution + source + derived`

A tempting write-side analogue of read-only aligned granule over-reads (`DemonicGranuleLoad`) is to update an unpadded `&mut [u8]` tail by executing an aligned 32-byte load `old := vmovdqa [algn]`, blending active slice lanes via `vpblendvb`, and writing all 32 bytes back via `vmovdqa [algn], blended`. Both Miri execution (`write_masked_faults_and_miri_results.json`) and [`support/lean/WriteMaskedFaultsAndTailTrichotomy.lean`](support/lean/WriteMaskedFaultsAndTailTrichotomy.lean) prove this idiom is **categorically unsound**:

- **Tree Borrows vs. Stacked Borrows asymmetry (`Miri/TreeBorrows/Permissions.lean:62-112`):**
  - On an exact 16-byte allocation (`Box::new([0u8; 16])`), a 32-byte RMW immediately fails bounds checking in both models (`attempting to access 32 bytes, but got allocN which is only 16 bytes from the end`).
  - Inside a shared 32-byte allocation (`#[repr(C, align(32))] struct Pair([u8; 16], [u8; 16])`), `foreignRead` maps `.Frozen => .Transition .Frozen` (`Permissions.lean:70`), so read-only `DemonicGranuleLoad` over-reading `right: &[u8; 16]` is sound even under an active function-entry `StrongProtector`. By contrast, `foreignWrite` maps `_ => .Transition .Disabled` (`Permissions.lean:99`—only `.Cell` / unprotected `.ReservedIM` survive). Consequently:
    - When `right: &[u8; 16]` or `&mut [u8; 16]` carries a `StrongProtector` (`fn callee(left: &mut [u8; 16], right: &[u8; 16])` where `right` was read), RMW slack-writeback triggers immediate `ProtectedDisabled` UB (`ub_same_alloc_sibling_strong_protector`, `rc = 1`).
    - Even without a protector, RMW slack-writeback transitions `right`'s tag on `[0x10..0x20)` to `.Disabled`, so the caller's next actual MIR read (`let val = r[0];`—note that bare `let _ = r[0];` compiles to `PlaceMention` without reading memory!) triggers `ChildAccessForbidden` UB (`sibling_frozen_read_after_rmw` and `sibling_active_mut_read_after_rmw`, `rc = 1`; mechanized in `read_overread_sound_but_rmw_writeback_ub_under_tree_borrows`).
    - *Subtle Tree Borrows vs. Stacked Borrows edge case:* On `#[repr(C, align(32))] struct Tiny16([u8; 16])` with **zero** sibling references, tail padding `[0x10..0x20)` is initialized in Tree Borrows with `accessed = false` and no foreign protector, so `padded_32b_subslice_reborrow` passes `-Zmiri-tree-borrows` (`rc = 0`) while failing Stacked Borrows (`rc = 1`, `tag does not exist in the borrow stack`). Relying on uncontested tail padding is non-compositional and breaks the moment any struct field or allocator block occupies `[0x10..0x20)`.
- **Non-atomic concurrent lost updates (`rmw_slack_writeback_causes_concurrent_lost_update`):** When Thread 1 runs `vmovdqa` RMW on `left: &mut [u8; 16]` while Thread 2 runs `AtomicU64::fetch_add(1, SeqCst)` (`lock xaddq`) on an adjacent field in `[16..24)`, Thread 1's store overwrites Thread 2's committed increments with stale snapshot bytes—losing **`197,797 / 200,000` (`98.9%`)** atomic increments and producing `70,711` non-monotonic counter regressions in `13.73 ms` on Ice Lake-SP.
- **Armv8.5-A 16-byte MTE tag granules (`rmw_slack_writeback_faults_on_mte_16b_granule`):** Under `qemu-aarch64 -cpu max` with `PR_MTE_TCF_SYNC`, tagging `[0..16)` with tag `1` and `[16..32)` with tag `2` causes 32-byte `stp q0, q1` to fault synchronously (`SIGSEGV`, `SEGV_MTESERR` `si_code = 9`, `rel_addr = 16`, `ptr_tag = 1`).

### 3. The Overlapping Unaligned Tail-Store (`[len - W .. len)`) Trichotomy

`Basis: derived + execution + source`

Because hardware masked byte stores (`VMOVDQU8 {%k1}`) require AVX-512BW and RMW slack-writeback is unsound, standard AVX2/NEON mutating kernels (including `core::slice::ascii::make_ascii_lowercase` in `rust-lang/rust` and SIMD codecs) guard `len < W` with a short scalar loop and handle `len >= W` via:

$$\text{for } k = 0, 1, \dots, \lfloor(\text{len}-1)/W\rfloor - 1:\ \text{store}_W(\text{dst} + Wk,\ F(\text{load}_W(\text{src} + Wk))); \quad \text{store}_W(\text{dst} + \text{len} - W,\ F(\text{tail\_src})).$$

Notice the strict loop guard `cursor + W < len` (`strictSteps W len = (len - 1) / W`), which guarantees `len - W <= W * strictSteps W len < len` (`strictSteps_bounds`): the main loop leaves `1..W` unwritten bytes at the end, and the final unaligned `W`-byte store at `len - W` stays strictly inside `[0, len)` while overlapping `[len - W .. W * strictSteps)` whenever `len > W` and `len % W != 0`. [`support/lean/WriteMaskedFaultsAndTailTrichotomy.lean`](support/lean/WriteMaskedFaultsAndTailTrichotomy.lean) establishes the complete three-way classification (validated dynamically under `-Zmiri-tree-borrows` in `trichotomy_all_modes_under_tree_borrows`, `rc = 0`):

| Tail-Store Idiom | Aliasing Regime | Soundness Condition on `F` | Key Lean `v4.34.1` Theorem | Minimal Failure Witness when Violated |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Disjoint overlap** (`src + len <= dst ∨ dst + len <= src`) | `(dst: &mut [u8], src: &[u8])` | **Unconditional** (arbitrary pure `F`) | `disjoint_overlap_tail_universal_soundness` | None (`src` bytes in `[len - W .. W * steps)` are never mutated by `dst` writes) |
| **2. In-place unbuffered overlap** (`tail_src := load(buf[len - W .. len])` *after* loop) | `buf: &mut [u8]` | **Sound $\iff \forall b,\ F(F(b)) = F(b)$** (idempotent `F`: `ascii_tolower`, `clamp`, `and_mask`) | `inplace_unbuffered_overlap_tail_sound_iff_idempotent` | **`len = W` (`32`) passes** (`len_eq_W_hides_nonidempotent_bug`); **`len = W + 1` (`33`) fails** on all `q ∈ [buf+1 .. buf+32)` (`0 ^ 1 ^ 1 = 0 != 1`, `avx2_xor_inplace_unbuffered_len32_ok_len33_fails`) |
| **3. In-place preloaded overlap** (`tail_in := load(buf[len - W .. len])` hoisted *before* overlapping store) | `buf: &mut [u8]` | **Unconditional** (arbitrary non-idempotent `F`: `xor_stream`, `bswap`, LUT, `add`) | `inplace_preloaded_overlap_tail_universal_soundness` | None (register `tail_in` snapshots original `mem0[buf + len - W + j]` prior to overwrite) |

### 4. Skolemized Single-Address (`q : BitVec 64`) Loop CEGIS, the 64-Bit Ripple-Borrow RUP Cliff, and Dual Bug Refutation

`Basis: execution + derived`

#### 4.1 Three-Way Skolem Memory Partition and Necessity of `unwritten_inplace_suffix`

To verify mutating byte-buffer loops in a zero-SMT pipeline (`bin/cadical 2.1.2` + `Pass1GmpLrat`), [`support/synth_skolem_mutating_cegis.py`](support/synth_skolem_mutating_cegis.py) Skolemizes the universal postcondition $\forall q,\ \text{mem}_{\text{final}}[q] = \text{spec}(q)$ with a single symbolic byte probe `q`, tracking three pairwise-disjoint spatial implications at loop header `cursor = 32 * k`:

1. **`written_prefix`:** `buf <= q < buf + cursor -> mem[q] == F_spec(orig[q])`
2. **`unwritten_inplace_suffix`:** `buf + cursor <= q < buf + len -> mem[q] == mem0[q]`
3. **`frame_outside`:** `(q < buf || buf + len <= q) -> mem[q] == mem0[q]` (plus `in_tail -> tailReg[q - (buf + len - 32)] == mem0[q]` when preloaded, and `in_slice -> perm(q) == true` when fault-checked).

In read-only or disjoint `(dst, src)` loops, `dst[cursor .. len)` is never read by subsequent iterations. For **in-place** mutating loops, however, iteration `k` computes `F_hw(mem[buf + 32*k + j])`: without `unwritten_inplace_suffix`, the inductive hypothesis permits `mem[q]` in `[buf + cursor .. buf + len)` to hold arbitrary garbage! Both 64-bit `CaDiCaL` (`exit 10` in `182.37 ms`, witness `buf = 0, len = 33, q = 0, mem0_q = 0, corrupted_mem_q = 1`) and kernel theorem `RefuteMutatingSimd6.refute_omitting_unwritten_inplace_suffix` prove that omitting `unwritten_inplace_suffix` breaks inductiveness even on idempotent `Avx2AsciiTolowerInplace`.

#### 4.3 Dual Counterexample Synthesis Across Six Real Mutating-SIMD Bugs

[`support/synth_skolem_mutating_cegis.py`](support/synth_skolem_mutating_cegis.py) synthesizes minimal witnesses via `CaDiCaL exit 10` + two-tier coupled assumption shrinking (`6.03–176.57 ms`), emitting both `[propext]`-only Lean refutation theorems ([`support/lean/RefuteMutatingSimd6.lean`](support/lean/RefuteMutatingSimd6.lean), `479.69 ms` compile, `6.95 ms` replay) and executable silicon tests ([`support/generated_mutating_bug_repros.rs`](support/generated_mutating_bug_repros.rs), `6 passed; 0 failed` in `9.76 ms`):

1. **`bug01_inplace_xor_unbuffered_overlap`:** `CaDiCaL` proves `len = 32` is `UNSAT` (`2.05 ms`, aligned test blindness!) and shrinks `len >= 32` to **`len = 33, buf = 0, q = 1, key = 1, mem0[1] = 0`** (`17` SAT calls, `48.14 ms`), catching byte `1` double-XORed back to `0`.
2. **`bug02_unguarded_len_lt_32_wraparound_fault`:** Shrinks missing `len < 32` guard on `(len - 32) mod 2^64` (`len >= 1`) to **`len = 1, tail_off = 0xffff_ffff_ffff_ffe1` (`-31 B`)** (`7` SAT calls, `46.34 ms`), confirmed by pre-guard `PROT_NONE` `SIGSEGV` (`si_code = 2`, `addr = rw - 31`).
3. **`bug03_maskmovdqu_zero_mask_fault`:** Shrinks `MASKMOVDQU` permission failure to **`active_bytes = 0` (`mask == 0`), `offset = 0`** (`3` SAT calls, `6.03 ms`), confirmed on `PROT_READ` silicon (`si_code = 2`, `addr = ro_page + 8` under `black_box`).

5. **`bug05_aligned_rmw_slack_writeback_clobber`:** Shrinks 32B aligned RMW slack-writeback clobber to **`slice_len = 1, clobbered_offset = 1, sibling_delta = 1`** (`12` SAT calls, `27.02 ms`), overwriting an interleaved volatile/sibling write back to `0`.

## Boundaries

- **QEMU `11.1.2` `linux-user` omits MTE tag checking on SVE `st1b` (`known not to apply` as a hardware MTE oracle for SVE):** In [`support/write_masked_faults_and_miri_results.json`](support/write_masked_faults_and_miri_results.json), `neon_32b_stp_mte_granule_mismatch` raises synchronous `SEGV_MTESERR` (`si_code = 9`, `rel_addr = 16`, `ptr_tag = 1`), whereas `sve_st1b_mte_tag_mismatch_qemu_user_gap` with `ptr_tag = 1` vs. allocation tag `2` completes with `faulted = false` because QEMU `11.1.2` `linux-user` SVE store helpers bypass MTE tag checks. Do not treat `qemu-aarch64` user-mode execution as proof of SVE-plus-MTE hardware tag behavior; test on physical Armv9 MTE+SVE2 silicon or full-system emulation.
- **Miri does not implement `llvm.x86.sse2.maskmov.dqu` (`unsupported`):** `cargo +nightly miri` (`3ef0c22cdb`) halts with `unsupported operation: can't call LLVM intrinsic llvm.x86.sse2.maskmov.dqu on architecture x86_64`. Passing Miri on fallback paths does not validate `MASKMOVDQU` callers.
- **Uncontested tail padding in Tree Borrows vs. Stacked Borrows (`known not to apply` to general `&mut [u8]` slices):** While `padded_32b_subslice_reborrow` passes `-Zmiri-tree-borrows` when `[0x10..0x20)` is unaccessed struct padding with zero sibling references, it fails Stacked Borrows and fails Tree Borrows whenever any sibling borrow or allocator metadata shares the 32-byte granule. Sound library verification must never emit write-side RMW slack-writeback on general `&mut [u8]` inputs.
- **Weak memory models and non-temporal `SFENCE` ordering (`not examined`):** `VMOVNTDQ` fault behavior (`#GP(0)` on misalignment, `#PF` on `PROT_READ`/`PROT_NONE`) was measured sequentially; weakly ordered write-combining visibility across threads without `_mm_sfence()` and AArch64 `stnp` release/acquire ordering were not examined.
- **AMD Zen 3/4/5 microarchitectural fault-offset ordering on `MASKMOVDQU` (`unknown`):** Ice Lake-SP consistently reports `CR2 = RDI + 8` (high qword first) when `MASKMOVDQU` faults with `mask == 0` on a page-aligned `PROT_READ`/`PROT_NONE`/`munmap` address; whether AMD Zen microarchitectures report `RDI + 0` or `RDI + 8` was not tested (`WriteMaskedFaultsAndTailTrichotomy.lean` conservatively models `RDI` byte-range writability without hardcoding `RDI + 8`).
